## **Visual Design Elements**

### **Box Styles by Function:**

- **Rounded Rectangles:** Main classifications, states
- **Diamonds:** Decision points (Biology, Symphonia)
- **Circles:** The six fundamental forces
- **Pills:** Techniques and abilities
- **Parallelograms:** Titles and special designations

### **Border Styles:**

- **Solid:** Standard elements and confirmed relationships
- **Dashed:** Conditional paths (Symphonia → Manifestation)
- **Dotted:** Theoretical elements (Primoton)
- **Invisible:** Background groupings and organizational clusters

### **Line Types:**

- **Direct + Solid:** Core requirements and primary flow
- **Bezier + Dashed:** Conditional and branching paths
- **Square + Dotted:** Theoretical connections
- **Direct + Long-Dashed:** Enhancement and modification paths

### **Color Coding:**

- **Pyrenikon Family:** Red/Orange spectrum
- **Elektromagneton Family:** Blue/Purple spectrum
- **Baryton:** Dark purple/black
- **Psioniton:** Green spectrum
- **Primoton:** Gold/yellow spectrum
- **Conqueror Elements:** Silver/platinum accents
- **Standard Elements:** Neutral earth tones
