# Sorcery Division - Students and Origins

> *"I have a proclivity for taking in broken things, no?"*
> *"What does that say about you then, Master?"*
> *"That we're a ragtag band of experiments and runaways, my dear."*

## The Re-Emergence of Magic

### The Context

- Magic was sealed by the Divine Mandate Against Sorcery (DMAS) after the Atlantean incident
- Battra was exiled for 12,000 years
- The DMAS weakened, allowing Primus to "leak" into receptive individuals
- Powers awakened chaotically in 2020

### Battra's Revelation

Battra was **completely unaware** human sorcerers existed again:
- Too focused on Godzilla and Mothra during the Xilien Invasion
- Only discovered it during a Reincarnation Egg post-2025
- His reaction: *"There are human sorcerers again?!"*

### Battra's Redemption Mission

Hunting down and teaching these sorcerers became his path to redemption:
- Part of his reintegration deal with Godzilla and Mothra
- He pleaded with Mothra to allow him to prove the Atlantean tragedy wasn't his fault
- Found them using:
  - Camouflage techniques
  - Tracking magical "footprints"
  - Telepathy (easier method)

## The Eight Core Sorcery Students

*(Ages listed are as of 2025; subtract 5-6 years for 2019-2020)*

### 1. Andrew Julius Ford - The Displaced Protector

**Origin**: American, Kansas
- Lived with crippled single father (military deployment)
- Father died late 2019, post-Ghidorah
- Powers activated 2020: accidentally teleported to NYC
- Couldn't teleport back; survived place to place hiding his powers

**Theme**: Loss and survival
**Arc**: Finding a new family to protect
**Specialty**: Protective magic

---

### 2. Elric Parcs - The Caged Bird

**Origin**: American, North Carolina
- Orphan, adopted at age 3
- Adoptive mother into "New Age stuff"
- Powers activated 2020: mother locked him in house
- Forced to create materials for her "hobby" under physical/verbal abuse

**Theme**: Exploitation and abuse
**Arc**: Using power for liberation, not others' gain
**Specialty**: Elemental magic (air and earth)

---

### 3. Mathias "Mat" Faust - The Betrayed Son

**Origin**: German, migrated to America
- Peaceful life until parents went into debt late 2019
- Powers awakened 2020; disclosed to parents
- **Sold to a gang** in exchange for money

**Theme**: Betrayal, corruption of desperation
**Arc**: Trust issues, learning power isn't just transactional
**Specialty**: Manipulating speed of processes
**Note**: Closely monitored by Battra due to dangerous ability

---

### 4. Maria Sierra Balagtas

**Origin**: Filipino-American
- Known by Monarch since 2020
- Story detailed elsewhere
- One of two with institutional connections

**Theme**: Bridge between worlds
**Arc**: Balancing dual heritage and responsibilities

---

### 5. Agrippa "Egg" Gideon - The Bridge

**Origin**: Italian
- Kidnapped after showing friends his powers in 2020
- Friends spread the word; connected people took him
- **Same facility as Psionic Division students**
- Misclassified as "weird psychic" until Battra confirmed sorcery post-2025

**Theme**: Misunderstanding and hidden potential
**Arc**: Ambassador between Sorcery and Psionic Divisions
**Importance**: Key connection between both divisions; knew psychics during captivity

---

### 6. Kira Solomon - The Golden Cage

**Origin**: Unknown
- Normal life until powers awakened
- Parents in finance/trading
- Revealed powers to struggling parents
- Became parents' "cheat code" for 6 years
- No friends, no freedom, constant supervision
- Eventually disappeared on her own

**Theme**: Burden of a gift
**Arc**: Learning to use sight for her own purposes
**Specialty**: Divination (ironic—never allowed any distance from family)

---

### 7. Felicia "Feli" Lucia - The Rescuer

**Origin**: Unknown
- Parents abroad; lived with grandparents
- Hid her powers when they emerged
- Learned neighborhood friend (Egg) disappeared
- Went to investigate; captured by illegal weapons gang

**Theme**: Heroism and consequence
**Arc**: Using skills beyond survival
**Specialty**: Enchantment (fits desire to help/empower others)
**Connection**: Natural partner for Egg (the transmuter)

---

### 8. Dorianne "Anne" LeFay - The Ghost

**Origin**: Unknown
- Orphaned during events of 2014 (first MonsterVerse movie)
- Believed dead; actually hid and survived place to place
- Powers became ultimate survival tool
- Illusions so potent they confused psychic-holding institutions
- **Last to be found** (main students)

**Theme**: True survival
**Arc**: Moving from survival to belonging
**Specialty**: Illusion magic
**Battra's Admiration**: Her skill in fooling even institutions holding psionics impressed him

## Dorianne's Recruitment Scene

A showcase of Battra's character and methods:

### The Hunt
- Battra tracked her for at least a week
- Used multiple disguises (old man with green eyes, various others)
- Her decoy illusion was in **America**; she was actually in **Europe**
- Had to use a counter-spell to finally locate her

### The Confrontation
Key moments:
1. He dismantled her reality piece by piece
2. Demonstrated overwhelming power *gently applied*
3. Questions her technique with genuine intellectual curiosity
4. Final reveal: *"Your new teacher, my dear."*

### What It Reveals About Battra
- **Theatricality as weapon**: Puts on a performance, tests her, admires her work
- **Intellectual curiosity**: Genuinely fascinated by her intuitive skill
- **Underlying empathy**: Sees a fellow outcast, recognizes a kindred spirit
- **Not recruiting soldiers**: Gathering fellow strays

## The 2020-2024 Incubation Period

Before Keep Charlie:
- Students lived in Castle Bravo
- Helped around, witnessed Titans adapting to human forms
- Mothra attempted to teach them

### Mothra's Teaching Challenge
- Great teacher, but couldn't relate to humans
- Taught "like a god, not a person"
- Could only reach **intuitive learners**
- This is where her arc truly begins

### Godzilla's Unexpected Role
- Can't teach technique, barely understands human world
- BUT knows abandonment, feeling outcast, helplessness
- Offers **emotional safety** (not ethereal/divine safety like Mothra)
- Beautiful twist: the "amoral" one provides visceral emotional connection

> *"Nowhere has the King of the Monsters ever been more vulnerable emotionally than now. The students and Godzilla are kindred."*

## World-Building Implications

### Scarcity of Sorcerers
- These 8 (+ 2 juniors) are the **only living sorcerers**
- The fate of magic on Earth rests on their shoulders

### The DMAS Still Matters
- Powers activated in traumatic, chaotic ways
- No widespread, easy access to magic
- Primus "leaked" into receptive individuals—not a floodgate

### Multiple Threats
- Shadow Organization (held psychics AND Egg)
- Illegal weapons gangs (took Feli)
- Other factions exist in the supernatural underworld
