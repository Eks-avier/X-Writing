# Psionics - Core Mechanics

> *"The mind is incredibly fragile and intimate. There is nothing more intimate than inviting oneself into the mind of others."* — Mothra

## Fundamental Concepts

### The Psionic Dyad

Psionics is founded on **two fundamental abilities** (the "Twin Faculties"):

1. **Telepathy** - The ability to interact with minds
2. **Psychokinesis (PK)** - The ability to influence matter/energy with the mind

Everything in Psionics derives from these two pillars.

### The Production of Psienergy

```
Brain + Psiforce → Psienergy → Telepathy / Psychokinesis
```

- All humans can interact with Psiforce (Homo sapiens is an Unnon species)
- Most lack the brain architecture to produce *enough* Psienergy for actual abilities
- This explains both ancient human psychics and modern psionic inquiry

---

## Psychokinesis Hierarchy

| Level | Ability | Definition |
|-------|---------|------------|
| **Pillar** | Psychokinesis (PK) | Fundamental ability to influence matter/energy with the mind |
| **Discipline** | Telekinesis (TK) | **Unconstrained** - versatile, can affect any physical object/energy |
| **Discipline** | Constrained Kinesis | **Biologically limited** to specific element/phenomenon |

### Constrained Kinesis Examples

- **Pyrokinesis** (fire)
- **Hydrokinesis** (water)
- **Cryokinesis** (ice)
- **Aerokinesis** (air)
- **Lithokinesis** (rock/earth)

**Key Distinction**: Constrained Kinesis users have biology acting as a "filter"—they can only manipulate certain things.

---

## True Psychic Definition

A **True Psychic** is any Unnon who can **actively and independently project their will to influence either the environment or another sapient mind.**

**Requirements** (at least one):
1. Active Telepathy
2. Telekinesis (Unconstrained PK)

### Classification Examples

| Character | Abilities | True Psychic? |
|-----------|-----------|---------------|
| Gerome Marcel | Active Telepathy | **Yes** |
| Suki Takao | Active Telepathy + Telekinesis | **Yes** |
| Rodan | Passive Telepathy + Constrained Kinesis | **No** |
| All Psionic Division | Various Active abilities | **Yes** |
| Most elemental Titans | Constrained Kinesis only | **No** |

## The Realm of Consciousness

### Definition

A non-physical realm generated by the brain's interaction with Psiforce, where the consciousness resides.

- **Not the Soul** (according to Magic)—but scientists believed it was
- **Envelops and permeates** the physical body
- **Direct interface** to the brain—provides "read/write privileges"

### How It Works

Accessing someone's Realm of Consciousness = Accessing their brain itself

This explains how **pure telepaths** (without Psychokinesis) can perform mental manipulation:
- They access the "software" (Realm of Consciousness)
- Rather than the "hardware" (physical brain)

### The Two Paths to Mind Manipulation

| Method | Practitioner | Approach | Analogy |
|--------|--------------|----------|---------|
| **Telepathic** | Gerome (pure telepath) | Accesses Realm of Consciousness | Software hacking |
| **Psychokinetic** | Suki (has both) | Directly manipulates brain matter | Hardware modification |

**Axiological Dilemma**: Which is "better"? Is manipulating intangible consciousness more or less of a violation than manipulating physical brain?

## The Five Telepathic Disciplines

### 1. Mental Communication (The Lingua Franca)

**Core Function**: Exchange of thoughts, emotions, concepts between minds

**Skills**:
- T2T Linking (Telepath-to-Telepath) - Foundation for Telepathic Web
- T2N Linking (Telepath-to-Non) - Advanced, requires immense precision
- Surface Skimming - Involuntary/passive reading
- Deep Reading - Active, consent-based probing

**Specialist**: Samuel Johns

---

### 2. Mental Fortification (The Aegis)

**Core Function**: Defense of one's own mind and others' from external influence

**Skills**:
- Mental Shielding - Barriers against intrusion
- Thought Obfuscation - Camouflaging surface thoughts
- Interference Filtering - Creating mental boundaries in "soup"
- Anchoring - Reinforcing ally's mind

**Specialist**: Isabella Vance

---

### 3. Mental Incursion (The Scalpel)

**Core Function**: Active, offensive manipulation of another's mind *(Most ethically fraught)*

**Skills**:
- Persuasion/Suggestion - Subtle influence ("Jedi mind trick")
- Illusion Casting - Projecting false sensory information
- Memory Manipulation:
  - Extraction (Battra's preferred method)
  - Alteration/Erasure (most dangerous)
- Psychic Assault - Brute-force consciousness attack

**Specialists**: Nathan Draper (persuasion), Gerome Marcel (extraction)

---

### 4. Mental Projection (The Surveyor)

**Core Function**: Extending consciousness beyond physical body

**Skills**:
- Remote Viewing (Clairvoyance) - Perceiving distant locations
- Psychic/Astral Projection - Sending consciousness as intangible observer
- Dreamwalking - Projecting into another's subconscious

**Specialists**: Stella Balagtas (Dreamwalking), Gerome Marcel (Projection)

**Clarification**: "Astral Projection" is moving Consciousness to physical world *from* Realm of Consciousness—not to another plane.

---

### 5. Extrasensory Perception (The Antenna)

**Core Function**: Gathering information beyond normal five senses

**Skills**:
- Empathy - Sensing emotional states
- Psychometry - Reading psychic imprints on objects/locations
- Precognition/Danger Sense - Glimpses of immediate future

**Specialists**: Julia Simmons (Empathy), Marcus Hale (Psychometry), Ethan Caldwell (Precognition)

## Active vs Passive Telepathy

| Type | Function | Capability |
|------|----------|------------|
| **Active** | Can project influence | Full telepathic abilities |
| **Passive** | Can only receive | Limited to sensing/receiving |

**Sensitivity**: Even within these categories, telepaths vary in sensitivity (like sensitivity to sounds/smells). Telepathy is as involuntary as it is voluntary.

### Surface Skimming

What is allowed (involuntary and reflexive):
- Brushing the surface of another's mind
- Getting glimpses/flashes/impressions—not the whole picture
- The stronger the telepath, the more vivid these flashes

**Mothra's Burden**: As the strongest psionic, she must constantly hold back her telepathy.

---

## Mothra's Ethical Stance

> *"Never use telepathy, not even casually, on the Non, most especially fellow men."*

The first principle taught to the Psionic Division:
- The mind is powerful but incredibly fragile and intimate
- Even skimming is meaningful to her
- Contrasts typical fictional telepaths who use powers casually

### The Battra Contrast

Battra cares not for minds when using telepathy:
- Views the mind as a "public library"
- Will rip memories out to satisfy his thirst for knowledge
- Believes being graced by his telepathy is an "honor"
- Mothra constantly reminds him to set an example for students

## The "Tainting" of Psionics

### The Problem

Psionics has been "tainted by science and technology":
- Shadow organizations viewed psychic abilities as quantifiable resources
- Forced, unethical development in laboratory environments
- Focus on *what* it can do, not *who* the user is

### The Reclamation

A core narrative conflict:
- **Original nature**: Art, music, dance, martial arts
- **Corrupted nature**: Weapon, tool, measurable resource
- **Goal**: Reclaim Psionics as an art form and expression of self

---

## Cosmological Note: No Astral Plane

The ATM Universe has **no separate astral realm**:

- What feels like "astral communion" = linking Realms of Consciousness together
- Two telepaths connecting aren't in another plane
- They're networking their individual Realms

This maintains the unified cosmology: **One universe, no parallel dimensions.**
