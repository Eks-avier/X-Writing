## The Triad of Presence: Aura, Field, and Excess

Each power system has a unique "footprint" on reality.

### I. Krátos: The Aura (The Sovereign's Territory)

**Theme**: Ontology (Being)
**Linguistic Mode**: Ancient Greek

- **Nature**: A **Territorial Volume** - not reaching out, but enlarging the world that they are
- **Perception**: Physical (heat, pressure, Submission effect)
- **Function**: Passive Shield and Debuff
- **Strategic Effect**: Regardless of knowledge, you must endure their presence

### II. Psionics: The Field (The Empath's Network)

**Theme**: Axiology (Values/Feeling)
**Linguistic Mode**: Modern English (Scientific)

- **Nature**: The **Operational Range of the Mind** - like Wi-Fi or radio broadcast
- **Neural Ignition vs. Field**: Psienergy exists within; Field is where it can be projected
- **Network Effect**: Multiple Fields intermingle, creating "mental soup"
- **Mothra's Unique Field**: Planetary - she IS the network, always "online"
- **Perception**: Felt as a "vibe" or emotional frequency

### III. Magic: The Excess (The Scholar's Signature)

**Theme**: Epistemology (Knowledge)
**Linguistic Mode**: Latin (Academic)

- **Nature**: **Metaphysical Waste** - residual information from Soul-stabilized energy
- **Signature**: Every spell, artifact, and Engram leaks information carrying the sorcerer's signature
- **Repertoire Hum**: Master sorcerers "hear" how many spells opponents carry by Excess quality
- **Battra's Stealth**: 99.9999% efficient - produces ZERO noise; only detectable by watching Articulation Components
- **Perception**: Like a scent trail or data leak

### Comparison Table

| Feature | Krátonic Aura | Psionic Field | Magical Excess |
|---------|--------------|---------------|----------------|
| **Primary Interaction** | Submission/Assertion | Resonance/Connection | Detection/Analysis |
| **Active/Passive?** | Always Active (1st Layer) | Active Focus Required | Passive Leakage |
| **Range** | Fixed Radius (Short-Med) | Broadcast Range (Variable) | Dissipating Scent (Med-Long) |
| **Information Provided** | Power Level & Will | Emotion & Intent | Identity & Methodology |
| **Counterplay** | Match the Will | Shield the Mind | Master the Knowledge |
