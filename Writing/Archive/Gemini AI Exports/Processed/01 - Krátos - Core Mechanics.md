# Krátos - Core Mechanics

> *"Krátos is pure being. They sear and burn; electrify and pin down; scorch and pierce. They're visceral, they're the here, and they're the now."*

## Fundamental Concepts

### What is Krátos?

Krátos is the **ontological** power system—concerned with Being itself. Unlike Magic (knowledge-based) or Psionics (values-based), Krátos is the direct expression of a being's **will to exist and impose that existence upon reality**.

### The Two Practitioner Types

| Type                | Greek Term                  | Definition                                                               | Manifestation               |
| ------------------- | --------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ | --------------------------- |
| **Koinon**          | κοινός (Common)             | Born with a fixed "Themelion" (Will + Resolve)                           | Stable, reliable, inherited |
| **Krator/Krateira** | κράτωρ/κράτειρα (Conqueror) | Must discover their "Horme" (Desire) and achieve "Symphonia" (Alignment) | Volatile, powerful, earned  |

**Plural Forms (Hellenic):**
- Kráton → Kratones
- Koinon → Koinones
- Krator → Kratores (masc.) / Krateirai (fem.)

## Aura - The Passive Emission of Manifestation

### Definition

Aura is the **latent, casual expression** of a Kráton's Manifestation—a passive emission that saturates the environment with their unique phenomena.

### Key Properties

- **Irrepressible**: As long as "the will to act" exists, Aura is produced (though at variable rates)
- **Spherical**: Emanates as a 3D field around the Kráton
- **Biological Integration**: Krátos and biology are intertwined—Godzilla's radioactive emissions *are* his Aura

### Individual Thresholds

Each Kráton's Aura depends on:
1. Physical Fitness
2. Physical Constitution
3. Mentality
4. Willpower

---

## Krator's Two-Layered Aura

### First Layer - The Mark of a True Conqueror

- **Invisible** passive field that induces fear, uneasiness, and dread
- **Available at birth** - unconscious, perpetual, irrepressible
- **Never turns off** - remains active even in unconsciousness, Latency, Misalignment, or Repose
- **Source**: The most basic "will to exist and defend oneself"—almost reflexive

### Second Layer - The Active Manifestation

- **Visible** phenomena (radiation, electricity, cold, etc.)
- Requires **conscious effort** and **Alignment** with one's Horme
- **Interruptible** via Misalignment
- **Can be suppressed** (to a point) via training
- **Turns off** in Repose or Latency
- **Source**: Conscious will to act *in alignment with one's Desire*

## Aura Manipulation - The Two Axes

### Axis One: Intensity Management

| Ability | Hellenic Term | Effect | Energy Cost |
|---------|---------------|--------|-------------|
| **Flaring** | Anapsis (ἄναψις - "Kindling") | Increases potency 1.5x-2x | Exponential drain; Krator more efficient |
| **Suppression** | Kryptis (κρύψις - "Hiding") | Decreases potency | Free for Koinon; steep learning curve for Krator |

**Flaring Side Effect**: Slight unintentional expansion of Aura radius

**Suppression Mastery**: Allows separation of phenomena (e.g., Ghidorah using gravity to fly without shocking effects)

### Axis Two: Spatial Management

| Ability | Hellenic Term | Effect | Energy Cost |
|---------|---------------|--------|-------------|
| **Expansion** | Ektasis (ἔκτασις - "Expanse") | Increases radius | Exponential, diminishing returns |
| **Reduction** | Systole (συστολή - "Contraction") | Decreases radius | Low (requires focus) |

**Expansion Consequence**: "Thinning"—effects spread over larger area become less potent at edges

**Reduction Consequence**: "Condensing"—latent energy concentrated into smaller volume, slight intensification

## Coating - Epichrisis (ἐπίχρισις)

**Definition**: Taking one's Aura and actively applying it as a "sheath" or "cladding" to oneself or an object.

- Separate from passive territorial field management
- Aura *actualized* for a specific, tangible purpose

### Biological vs Krátonic Expression

**Example - Godzilla's Atomic Breath:**
- Without Coating: Expression of *biology* (bio-atomic nature)
- With Coating: Expression of *Krátos* (gains penetrative/neutralizing effects)

This explains why attacks sometimes "work when they shouldn't have"—instinctive Coating application.

## Sensory Abilities

### External Willpower Awareness

- **Universal** to all Kráton
- Ability to sense, perceive, and gauge the willpower of other sentient beings
- **Krator Privilege**: Can identify fellow Conquerors by sensing the First Layer

### Sensory Extension

By saturating the environment with Krátonic Manifestation, Aura extends pre-existing biological senses.

**Requirement**: Must possess sensory organs capable of processing the feedback

**Examples:**
- **Ghidorah**: Everything affected by electricity/gravity can be sensed; electrocution highlights victims
- **Godzilla**: Everything exposed to radiation can be mapped and tracked
- **Scylla**: Everything touching ice is immediately sensed; synergizes with hydrokinetic senses

**Counter-example**: A theoretical human Pyroton couldn't process flame feedback—humans lack the capacity.

## Learning Curves - Inverted Progression

### Koinon Path: Activation → Control

1. Born with "traces" of Aura (e.g., feverishly warm Pyroton at birth)
2. **Must train** to activate full Manifestation via Aura
3. Flaring and Expansion are essential early skills
4. Journey: Learning to *become* their power

### Krator Path: Suppression → Refinement

1. First Layer always active from birth
2. Second Layer active upon achieving Symphonia
3. **Must train** to suppress overwhelming presence
4. Journey: Learning to *contain* their power

## Willpower Dependency

Aura effects depend on willpower, skill, and emotional state.

### Godzilla as Example:

| State | Aura Effect |
|-------|-------------|
| Wants to avoid harming others | Unconsciously reduces strength |
| Stressed | Dangerously intensifies (even unconsciously) |
| At rest/sleeping | Weakens, returns to baseline upon waking |
| Unconscious | Dissipates completely |

### Training Implications

For *Titanus gojira* specifically:
- Unless you're a *female* gojira, beneficial radiation emissions require rigorous training
- Post-transformation (2020), Godzilla learned conscious control to allow indefinite safe human contact

---

## Ghidorah's "Primitive" Understanding

Despite his power, Ghidorah only understood there was a "strong relationship" between willpower and abilities:
- Recognized his powers worked on things they normally wouldn't
- Knew he was special, but not *why* or the implications
- Battra considered this framework "primitive"

**Implication**: Even ancient, powerful beings operate on instinct. Battra (the epistemological master) is the one who will formally classify Krátos.
