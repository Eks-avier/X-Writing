# Monster Island

> *The Headquarters of Monarch is Castle Bravo, and the Headquarters of the Titans is the Palace at Monster Island.*

## Overview

Monster Island is the sovereign territory of the Titans—a roaming landmass in the Pacific Ocean that serves as their primary gathering place, political center, and sanctuary. It predates the formal Titan hierarchy, having been used for Titan assemblies since before the Palace was constructed or court protocol was established.

**Size:** 50,000 sq. km
**Location:** Mobile (roaming Pacific Ocean)
**Status:** Titan sovereign territory
**Access:** Restricted to approved visitors with strict time limits

---

## Geography and Environment

### The Living Anachronism

Monster Island exists in a state of **temporal stasis**—time does not progress biologically on the island. This is maintained by Mothra's reflective scales, which literally reflect the effects of time away from the island's boundaries.

**Environmental Features:**
- Flora and fauna from the Mesozoic and early Cenozoic eras
- Living dinosaurs (treated by Titans as roughly equivalent to "cattle")
- Man-eating plants and other prehistoric dangers
- Climate controlled by the elemental abilities of resident Titans

**Health Hazards:**
- Drinking water or inhaling air outside the Villa/Palace boundaries may be inadvisable
- Prehistoric atmospheric composition differs from modern Earth
- Aquatic prehistoric animals exist only within the island's immediate boundaries

### The Temporal Mechanics

Mothra's power maintains the temporal reflection, which requires her to be at full power. To compensate for having split her soul, she has placed one of her soul fragments (a Reincarnation Egg) on the island, maintaining the scattered scales' power.

**Battra's Domains:** Within the temporal stasis, Battra creates "Rooms" or "Domains" where time proceeds normally in parallel with Earth's temporal flow. These exist in:
- The Villa (permanent)
- Accommodation areas for invited researchers (created as needed)

The island still experiences day and night cycles based on its current location—it simply doesn't move biologically forward in time.

## Location and Movement

### Pre-Battra (Historical)

- Stationary location at **Point Nemo**
- Hidden by Mothra's reflective scales since human prehistory
- Scales' concealment persisted even after her 2020 transformation

### Post-Battra (Current)

The island is now a **roaming island** that Battra constantly teleports around the Pacific Ocean. It remains hidden through:
- Mothra's reflective scales
- Battra's enchantments
- Complete invisibility and undetectability regardless of either twin's presence

**Strategic Positioning:** The island typically resides opposite from Castle Bravo's location:
- If Monster Island is in the western hemisphere → Castle Bravo is in the eastern hemisphere
- This ensures global connectivity for the Titan-Monarch network

Battra has also installed portal points at every known Monarch outpost, earning him the joking title "Lord of Logistics" in addition to Lord of the Mystic Arts.

## The Palace

The Palace serves as the formal seat of Titan governance, fully completed after the Xilien Invasion Arc at the end of the Keystone Arc.

**Integration:** The Palace interacts seamlessly with the island's prehistoric environment:
- Dinosaurs roam freely in certain areas
- Flora and fauna integrated into yards and gardens
- Managed by the Warden of the West, the former Warden of the North, and (post-introduction) Biollante

---

## Human Access Rules

### The One-Month Law

No human may stay on Monster Island for more than one month, regardless of status. This includes:
- Madison Russell
- Mark Russell
- Jia

**Enforcement:** The law is enforced magically through pocket watches given to visitors that display remaining days. When time expires:
1. The watch alerts the visitor
2. They must use the portal room in the Villa to return to the nearest Monarch Outpost
3. Attempting to re-enter redirects them to another outpost
4. The island becomes imperceptible to people in "cooldown"

### Cooldown Period

Humans must wait one month before entering the island again.

**Exceptions:**
- Madison, Mark, and Jia receive reduced cooldown (one week only)
- Keep Charlie students have unlimited stay (they need to learn all human-facing systems)
- Sorcery Division students handle human-facing systems instead of Battra

### Visitor Capacity

Maximum 250 humans present at any time. This cap will not change for centuries.

### The Philosophy

> *The spaces for Titans must remain for them only: a sovereign space.*

The rules have nothing to do with magic or the island's sanctity—they exist to preserve Titan autonomy. The Iwi maintain similar restrictions on their lands, which Jia herself used as precedent when the exemption classes were established.

## The Villa

A small, high-end resort located beside the shores of Monster Island.

**Features:**
- Integrated within the island's environment
- Protected by Battra's magic
- Managed by service bots from Monarch (similar to Keep Charlie's)
- Portal access to the Palace exterior for convenience
- Buggy rides available for landscape tours

**Travel Time:** 30 minutes on foot from the Villa to the Palace (without powers)

**Access Rule:** No human may enter or walk within the Palace unless explicitly permitted by a Titan of at least Warden rank.

---

## Safety and Enforcement

### Visitor Rules

1. **No unauthorized technology** beyond what exists in the Palace and Villa
2. **No touching or interacting** with flora or fauna without explicit permission
3. **No extended stays** beyond the one-month limit

### Enforcement Progression

| Offense | Consequence |
|---------|-------------|
| First | Immediate teleportation off the island |
| Second | Restrained by the flora |
| Third | Fatal means (man-eating plants, dinosaurs) |

### Security Whitelist

Madison, Mark, and Jia are whitelisted in security protocols involving fatal means—they cannot be killed by the island's defenses, though they still face non-fatal enforcement.

## Student Residence

After the Pacific Rim arc, the question of student housing was resolved:

**Options Considered:**
- Battra proposed a student residence area
- Mothra offered the Palace itself (rejected by both Godric and Madison)
- Godric's objection: Humans shouldn't be in Titan spaces
- Madison's objection: Students are already privileged enough

**Resolution:** The Student Dorm was built beside the Villa.

## Cultural Significance

### To the Titans

Monster Island represents their only truly sovereign space—a place where they are not guests in the human world or remnants of an ancient one, but masters of their own domain.

### To the Nordson Children

Junior and Leo grew up believing dinosaurs exist everywhere. When Mark or Madison visit and tell them otherwise, they're fascinated by the concept of a world without living dinosaurs.
