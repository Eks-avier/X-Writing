# Hiding the Colossal

> *The most successful predators aren't those that appear obviously dangerous, but those that blend seamlessly into their environment.*

## Overview

Following the Antitheriomorphosis (2020), the transformed Titans faced an unprecedented challenge: how do beings of immense cosmic power hide in plain sight among the very species they've protected and terrified for millennia?

The answer required coordination between Monarch, the Alpha Tier, and every Titan on Earth—a global conspiracy of silence that would last until the Palace was revealed.

---

## The Great Secret (2020-2024)

### The Core Directive

Godzilla himself **ordered**—not suggested, not recommended—that all Titans hide themselves regardless of whether they were human or not (which they were still trying to figure out). This directive carried the full weight of his authority as Alpha Paramount.

**Japan Exception:** The sole exception was Japan, whose top officials were made aware of Gojira being in a human body, given the historical and cultural significance of the relationship.

### The Cover Story

To the public, the Titans were simply "settling into their ecological roles as giant monsters." All observable Titan activity was attributed to their original colossal forms, maintaining the illusion that humanity was dealing with predictable, powerful animals—not a secret society of super-powered beings living among them.

Dr. Sam Coleman's department became the most important in Monarch—responsible for translating godlike activity into mundane explanations that satisfied governments, media, and the scientific community.

## The Proximity Problem

### Uncanny Recognition

Humans who encounter the transformed Titans experience something unsettling. The Titans appear conventionally attractive, well-proportioned, even perfect—but something triggers instinctual alarm bells in the human psyche.

**The Proximity Effect:**
1. **Distant:** Perceived as exceptionally attractive individuals
2. **Approaching:** Undefined discomfort begins
3. **Close proximity:** Fight-or-flight responses without conscious understanding
4. **Adaptation:** Extended exposure can build tolerance in some

This creates a natural social buffer most humans never consciously acknowledge—they simply create distance without understanding why.

### Physical Tells

Subtle details that trained observers might notice:
- **Density paradox:** Appearing lighter than actual weight
- **Perfect symmetry:** Unnaturally bilateral precision
- **Effortless movement:** No wasted energy or unnecessary motion
- **Thermal anomalies:** Body temperatures running hot or cold
- **Presence mismatch:** Affecting spaces disproportionate to apparent size

## Life in the Shadows

### The Adjustment Period

The Castle Bravo Four (Godzilla, Mothra, Rodan, Anguirus) and the Wardens were still adapting to their new forms when the hiding began. They had to learn:
- Social calibration (appearing approachable)
- Behavioral mimicry (human mannerisms)
- Emotional expression (readable to humans)
- Movement moderation (human speeds and limitations)

### Madison's Tours

Despite the secrecy mandate, Madison Russell loved bringing the Titans to various places on Monarch's budget. The rest of the world might see glimpses of infuriatingly attractive people—sometimes with weird scientists or some random young woman—but they would never know a Titan was in their midst.

Only their primal senses would.

---

## The Duoarchy's Burden

### Mark Russell's Challenge

The Director of Monarch didn't struggle with managing the Titans themselves—he struggled with:
1. How staff interacted with beings who could level cities
2. Ensuring governments (UN, USA, EU) didn't discover the truth
3. Maintaining budgets, supply chains, and cover stories globally
4. Translating Titan directives into actionable human policy

### Godric's Position

Godric rarely overrode Mark's directives, actually enjoying how he ran operations. When he did intervene, it was only for Titan-specific business. The two operated as parallel monarchs—one ruling Titans, one ruling the human apparatus that kept their secret.

---

## The Reveal

The Great Secret officially ended with the completion and public acknowledgment of Monster Island Palace (2025), though the transition was gradual. The world learned that their colossal protectors had been walking among them for years—an unsettling revelation that redefined humanity's relationship with the Titans.
