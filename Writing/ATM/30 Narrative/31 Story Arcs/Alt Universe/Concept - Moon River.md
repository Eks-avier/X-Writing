The night is quiet.
Not silent—**never silent, not with them.**
But it’s a stillness that belongs to them alone.

The moon hangs low, casting its pale glow across the sea.
The waves lap at the shore, gentle, unhurried.
The stars blink like distant memories, old and endless.

And Mothra sings.

---

### **Mothra’s Perspective** (*"Why does this feel so familiar?"*)

It started as something **small**—a human song she picked up, a melody that resonated deep inside her. It was from some film she spotted Madison watch. She didn't know the young one was into films that looked... old, or so at least she explained it.

**The people of old used to sing to her.** 
Soft, whispered hymns on Infant Island. 
Songs that carried their love and prayers to the sky, to her.

But this?

This was different.

*"Moon river, wider than a mile..."*
*"I'm crossing you in style someday..."*

She lets the words drift into the air, her voice weaving them like silk.
The notes float across the sea, **reaching something unseen.**

She thought she was alone.

But then—

She hears him.

**A deep, low hum joins her.**
A voice **like the rolling tide, ancient and vast.**

She turns—
And there he is.

Standing in the moonlight.

*Him*. He is something **closer, something softer,** even if he would never admit it.

She **shouldn’t be surprised**—he’s sung before—but here, with no one to hear them except the stars—

**Why does it feel different?**

He steps beside her, voice low, unshaken.

*"Oh, dream maker..."* The words leave her lips too easily.
*"You heartbreaker..."*  She slips in. Where did he learn this song?
*"Wherever you're goin'..."* He follows. His voice felt like a gentle hand on her shoulder.
*"I'm goin' your way..."* She continues.

Their voices **blend too easily.**

Like something **well-practiced.**
Like something **that has happened before.**
Like something **that was meant to happen.**

She watches the way **his golden eyes soften** under the moonlight.
Not glowing, not burning—just **watching.**

Like he knows something she doesn’t.

Like he **remembers something she has forgotten.**

And suddenly, the song feels **too heavy.**

---

### **Godzilla’s Perspective** (*"Why does this feel so familiar?"*)

He didn’t mean to join in.
Not at first.

He was just watching.
Listening.

She sings **so easily.**

Like it’s **a part of her.**
Like it’s something **woven into her soul.**

And he—
He **has always been drawn to the things that feel old.**

So he sings with her.
Not because of the song.
Not because of the moment.

But because it feels like something **he should do.**

*"Two drifters off to see the world..."*
*"There's such a lot of world to see..."*

Her voice is **gentle, delicate, careful.**
His voice is **deep, steady, constant.**

They follow the same melody—
But it **feels like more than that.**

Like they’re following **a thread.**
Like they’re following **a memory.**

Like they’ve done this before.

---

### **The Final Verse – The Moment Everything Changes**

She turns slightly, her breath hitching for just a moment.
He notices.

She’s watching him—**really watching.**
Like she’s seeing him **for the first time.**

And suddenly, the words **hit too close.**

*"We're after the same rainbow's end..."*

A pause.

*"Waitin' 'round the bend..."*

Another pause.

*"My huckleberry friend..."*

She doesn’t realize it, but her fingers have curled into her palm.

She doesn’t realize it, but **he’s watching the way her throat tightens when she swallows.**

*"Moon river... and me."*

The last note lingers.

The wind carries it away.

And suddenly—
The night is **too quiet.**

---

### **The Silence That Follows**

Mothra blinks, **like waking from a dream.**

Godzilla exhales slowly, as if grounding himself.

Neither of them move.
Neither of them **speak.**

The waves crash softly against the shore.

Mothra **looks down at her hands.**
She doesn’t know why they feel **empty.**

Godzilla looks up at the sky.
He doesn’t know why the moon **feels farther away than before.**

Finally—
She forces a smile.

*"That was nice."*

He nods, slow and unreadable.

*"Yes."*

A pause.

A beat too long.

A silence that says **too much.**

---

### **The Unspoken Realization**

It was just a song.
Just a melody.
Just something **to fill the night.**

And yet—

Why did it feel like something **more?**

Why did it feel like **a memory waiting to be remembered?**

Why did it feel like **the end of something… or the beginning?**

Mothra shakes her head, brushing the thought away.
Godzilla lets the wind carry his away.

And yet—
Neither of them forget.

Not that night.
Not ever.

Because some songs **aren’t just songs.**
Some moments **aren’t just moments.**

And some stories?
**Have already been written in the stars.**
