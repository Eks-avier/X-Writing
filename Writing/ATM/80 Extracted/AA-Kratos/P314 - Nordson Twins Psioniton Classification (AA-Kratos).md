# Nordson Twins Psioniton Classification
> **Source:** `Formatted_With_Thoughts - AA - The Kratos of Kings.md` - Prompt 314

## Your Notes
Let us now discuss what Psionitons can do via our only cases so far: Godric Nordson Jr. and Leonard Aurelius Nordson.

Hailing from perhaps the greatest psionic bloodline on Earth or in several star systems, Leo and Junior possess supreme telepathic and telekinetic capabilities. Although, slightly less refined than their mother, likely due to their *gojira* nature, they are still far and beyond most Titan species, or even alien specimens from outer space.

The twins were capable of proper telepathy shortly after being one years old. Tending to their needs had been subsequently easy, though most of Junior's nascent thoughts are of his daddy, and Leo about how much mess he can make. They were so strong in fact, that Maria had to prevent most of the Psionic Division from trying to telepathically contact her children, despite the fact that most of the division was sufficiently trained. At 19, they can each manage to stabilize at least 60% of the Telepathic Web on their own, enabling them to become its "maintainers" from time to time, aside from their mother and uncle Barth. Even with all this power, they strictly abide by their mother's ethics surrounding telepathic interfacing with living people.

The twins were capable of telekinesis around the same time they developed their *mosura* scales. Easily capable of lifting at least 3 or 5 times their weight with ease, albeit for only a short duration. Both telepathy and telekinesis, when newly acquired, require some level of focus. As children... well, let's just say they can only use both of their psionic gifts for a couple of seconds. Eventually, their control grew with them, allowing them to use it as easily as they breath. Their telekinesis is so potent that they can use it to give shape to their atomic energy--but only when they enable AA first. Besides that, they possess great fluidity with which they manipulate tangible things. But, because of their age, they still don't have the same refinement as their mother and uncle to be mistaken as a Constrained Psychokinetic when manipulating a specific element. They can easily make water dance in the air, but making it flow with grace is not yet in their skill level at 19. Compared to Suki Takao, the strongest human telekinetic so far, the Nordson twins are at least one and a half times her strength at 19 years old. Considering Suki's age at that time, it is really impressive. The twins are immortal, so they have time to further improve.

Now, they weren't officially classified as Psionitons until the age of four. Bartholomew had considered Kratos unable to manipulate either the Psi or the Primus Force. That was his previous theorem. But one day, his precious psionic students were assigned to watch over the kids as Godric and Maria have business to do down in the Hollow Earth with Kevin. Junior was upset, but surprisingly pliant. He napped all day. Leo, however, kept trying to go near the students. Maria had expressly forbidden the students from coming into contact with the toddlers due to their strength. Both of them look like babies, but if a human baby has surprising grip strength, a gojira-mosura hybrid can bend steel. Also, because they are children their minds are unorganized yet, so telepathically communing with them beyond just analyzing their surface thoughts was dangerous.

Despite the distance the students kept, whenever Leo came just shy of two meters with them, they sense as if their minds were pulling towards the toddler. Barth had told them that they were Krator--Conquerors--but because they are in Lanthaneia, only their First Layer is active. Stella Balagtas assumed that was the case.

It wasn't until Junior had a tantrum about his missing daddy that they truly understood what was happening. The Temporary Horme to "Want one's father" was enough to trigger a Submission. Bartholomew had given them a special charm that should filter that out. It worked, they didn't pass out and Julia Simmons was on her way to pacify Junior when...

They felt that their minds were literally *melting*. Ethan Caldwell, acted just in time to tell Suki to telekinetically grab everyone out of the immediate range of Junior.

When Godric, Maria, and Barth later on, came back. The division told them all about what happened.

Barth couldn't BELIEVE it. Beyond excited, he urged Junior to do that again. But after a warning glare from Godric and Maria, that was postponed. Instead, Sam Johns offered to let Maria and Barth read their memories of what happened. With all in agreement, they began.

That melting sensation wasn't their brains literally melting from chaotic telepathy or even radiation, it was... unprecedented. Somehow, Junior was unintentionally sipping up their Psienergy. Because Submission implies that the Aura is available, even when nascent, it meant that within the range of Junior's temporary Aura, he was absorbing both atomic energy AND Psienergy into him.

Was that some sort of new Psionic skill? No. Barth and Maria mastered all of them, and it couldn't have been possible. While most psionic powers relied on Psienergy, actively stealing that from another person was impossible. Similar to Primus Energy, Psienergy is imprinted the psychic's identity. However, instead of the Soul imprinting, it is their Consciousness--their Mind--meaning that the Psienergy a psychic generates is unique to themselves, like a fingerprint.

All applications of Psionics come from within towards the outside. It is impossible for a psychic to leech off someone's unique Psienergy. Is it possible to contain that Psienergy into an object? Yes. But, the owner of that Psienergy will be aware of it. You can *attempt* to use it, but as long as a Psychic is aware of them, that block of Psienergy cannot be *converted* into any usable form of classical energy.

So, the first Psioniton was born... until Kane slammed against a marble statue inside the Palace and Leo laughed, *urging* his parents to command the funny human to do it again. Two Psionitons.

Bartholomew theorized on the spot that if they were capable of this... Psionic Vampirism, then they were capable of the same way Godric could disrupt the flow of Primus Energy and Psienergy in an environment. A sufficiently powerful Krator can produce an Aura so strong that given area is composed of their pure will. However, that's all he could do--push them away via Will, via his Aura. He cannot control them or do anything more than that. The twins however could actually convert the Psienergy into their own, because they are Unnon, and actively make the environment dangerous for *only* Psychics. This also means that the Psionic weakness of Kratos is mitigated, or even entirely removed as the moment a harmful telepathic attack reaches them, instead of Neutralizing it, they can simply absorb it via their Aura into themselves.

Absorbing Psienergy from external sources is one ability, but there are many more applications of being an Unnon Psioniton. Is it supremely overpowered? Yes, duh. The conflict comes into view because neither Junior and Leo ARE horrific unethical monsters. They're good-natured boys, inheriting Maria's compassion. The fact that they will struggle with this throughout their youth is a compelling story to explore.

## Analysis
This is a monumental development in the lore of your universe. You have effectively created the "Apex Predator" of the supernatural world. By making the Nordson twins **Unnon Psionitons**, you have synthesized the Tangible (Kratos) and the Intangible (Psionics) into a terrifyingly perfect loop.

### The Anatomy of a Psioniton: The Living Paradox

To understand why this is so broken (and so fascinating), we have to look at the mechanics you've established.

* **The Psychic's Limit:** A normal psychic runs on **mental stamina.** Using powers drains them. Their Psienergy is finite and personal.
* **The Kraton's Engine:** A Kraton runs on **Will.** As long as their Will holds, they generate power.
* **The Synthesis:** Junior and Leo generate **Psienergy** via **Will.**
  * They do not get "mentally tired" in the same way a normal psychic does. As long as they have the **determination** to keep going, their "mana bar" refilled instantly.
  * This explains how, at 19, they can maintain **60% of the Telepathic Web.** It's not just skill; it's raw, limitless horsepower. They are powering a planetary network with the stubbornness of a *gojira*.

### The Incident at Age 4: The Day the Minds Melted

The scene you described is chilling because it highlights the **involuntary nature** of Kratos in Lanthaneia.

* **The Tantrum:** Junior didn't want to hurt the students. He just wanted his dad. That desire was a **Temporary Horme.**
* **The Submission:** His Aura flared. Usually, this just forces physical submission (kneeling).
* **The Vampirism:** Because he is a **Psioniton**, his Aura didn't just push down on their bodies; it **pulled on their minds.**
  * **The "Melting" Sensation:** This is horrifying. It implies that he was literally **liquefying their Realms of Consciousness**--stripping away the "fingerprint" of their identity--to convert their Psienergy into raw fuel for himself. He was metabolizing their minds.
* **The Charm's Failure:** Battra's charm blocked the *fear* (the psychological effect of Submission), but it couldn't block the *physics* of the absorption. It's like wearing earplugs to stop a tidal wave.

### The Mechanics of Psionic Vampirism

* **Normal Psychics:** Psienergy is "keyed" to the user. You can't use someone else's energy because it's incompatible. It's like trying to put diesel in a gas engine.
* **The Psioniton Workaround:** The twins don't just "drink" the energy. Their Kratonic Aura acts as a **refinery.**
  1. **Absorption:** The Aura pulls the external Psienergy in.
  2. **Neutralization:** The Kratonic nature of the Aura **scrubs the identity/fingerprint** off the energy (similar to how Godric nullifies magic, but internal).
  3. **Conversion:** The raw, neutral energy is absorbed into their own reserves.

This makes them the ultimate **Anti-Psychics.** Attacking them with telepathy is like throwing water at a black hole; you're just feeding it.

### The Ethical Nightmare: The Good Monsters

This is where the story shines. You have given the ultimate "villain power"--mind-eating vampirism--to two boys who are being raised by the avatar of benevolence.

* **Mothra's Horror:** To Maria, this ability is an abomination. It violates her core tenet: the sanctity of the mind. Her children are born with a biological imperative to violate that sanctity just by existing too loudly.
  * **The Parenting Challenge:** Her task is no longer just "teach them to fly." It is "teach them how not to accidentally consume the souls of their friends."
* **Godric's Pride (and Concern):** Godric likely sees it as a supreme survival trait ("Good, they can't be mentally attacked"), but he respects Maria's ethics enough to enforce the discipline required to control it.
* **Battra's Fascination:** Of course Battra wants them to do it again. It breaks the known laws of magic and psionics. To him, it's a breakthrough; to the parents, it's a crisis.

### The Psioniton Arsenal

Based on this, their "moveset" expands significantly:

1. **Absolute Mental Defense:** They don't need to parry or shield in the traditional sense. They can just **eat the attack.** A mental blast directed at Junior just... disappears, and Junior gets a sudden burst of energy.
2. **The Psychic Roar (Flaring):** Instead of a physical roar, they can flare their Aura to create a **psionic EMP.** It doesn't convey words or feelings; it just blasts raw, "loud" Psienergy outward, overloading the brains of anyone nearby. Non-psychics get headaches; psychics get knocked unconscious.
3. **Infinite Construct Sustenance:** This explains why they can make constructs (like the swords or hammers) despite being Gojira. Their Psioniton nature provides the infinite battery life needed to keep the shape stable without the mental strain that would kill a normal psychic.
4. **Domain Dominance:** If they enter a Projection Duel, they are terrifying. They don't just "lasso" an opponent; their mere presence in the astral plane begins to **erode the opponent's tether.**

### Conclusion

The Psioniton classification is the perfect capstone to the twins' power. It explains their anomalies, justifies their immense potential, and creates a rich source of internal and external conflict. They are "good boys" who have to constantly hold back a nature that wants to devour the world's thoughts. It is a beautiful, tragic, and epic conflict worthy of the Nordson name.
