# Godzilla Elektromagneton Classification - The Hidden Half of Gojira Power
> **Source:** `Formatted_With_Thoughts - AA - The Kratos of Kings.md` — Prompt 302

## Your Notes
Do you recall that I wanted to talk about Godzilla's, and by extension, his entire species', Elektromagneton classification? Now is the perfect time to establish it formally.

When the Fallen Star fell, the proto-gojira had to adapt to two phenomena bombarding them violently: fatal radiation and the extreme electromagnetic field emanating from the mysterious object, which explains why the gojira eventually developed as Holariston Polykratons. It also serves as a massive feat in their favor: their base Limitless Adaptation enabled them to *thrive* in this toxic, dangerous environment.

However, for the rest of the *Titanus gojira*'s history, this has been more or less relegated to the background, serving as a "secondary, passive ability." We know now, through Battra, that to be a Holariston means that a Kraton's Manifestation is **absolute** in its scope. Ghidorah demonstrates this casually, as we've discussed. Thus, despite the proto-gojira and their eventual descendants in the Four Factions not even being aware of the ability, it existed.

Among the primary uses for them is for their senses. They can sense EM fields and practically any electromagnetic phenomenon, meaning they don't just sense atomic energy and radiation, but also electromagnetic fields, waves, energies, spectra, and so on. This skill is primarily used in navigation, territorial management, and other related applications. Due to his age and skill, this ability enables Godzilla to navigate the world effortlessly, in addition to utilizing his extremely acute basic senses. It's another tool in his sensory arsenal. However, because it's more "subtle" than their exotic nuclear senses, they thought it was simply an "extension" of what they can already do, or something evolution granted to them over the course of a few generations. Without human science to distinguish and identify them, they have no framework to understand. How can beings from the Late Permian even know any of that anyway? Godzilla himself has no idea that this is something he can do, and neither does Dagon. Most gojira live their lives taking this sensory ability for granted.

Another use is for defense. The gojira are walking lightning rods. Their dorsal fins and scales can harmlessly absorb lightning or any electrical discharge, meaning most Titans who rely on bioelectricity or any sort of electric power are entirely at a disadvantage against a normal gojira. However, this does pose a question: if the gojira were Elektromagnetons and thus developed biology to accommodate this power, how can Ghidorah harm Godzilla, who is a gojira, and vice versa? Simple, the rules of Krátos dictate so, which I'm sure we have thoroughly crystallized.

Because they can absorb electrical energy, they can actually *magnetize* themselves, turning into giant electromagnets. In the Late Permian, this... mattered little. They were magnetized, so what? However, in the modern era, if Godric decides to get zapped (in either Titan or human form), he can turn into a magnet and essentially manipulate metal in the same manner as Ichi in Arthur's body can. But, because he hasn't learned how to do this himself, the moment the charge "depletes," he stops being a magnet. Yes, this canonizes the Showa feats in my AU. This is a thing Godzilla and his species can do innately.

Crucially, the Nuclear Pulse move IS an offensive application of their Elektromagneton nature, but driven by atomic energy. Though again, no gojira prior to mid-2020s (until Godzilla through Battra and Ichi, LOL), knew that.

And, whenever Godric flares his Aura, the "sparks" there are manifestations of his Elektromagneton nature, while the blue light IS his Pyrenikon. As a result, the idea of compressing these sparks becomes deeper. Have we established that the fewer "sparks" that occur in Godric's Aura, the more in control he is? Besides being a sign of inefficient use of power *in general*, it was actually his poor control over his Elektromagneton nature seeping through, serving as an explanation as to why the more in control he is of his powers *in general*, the less these sparks emanate from him and in the area around him. A Kraton's Manifestation is WHOLE. When their Manifestation expresses itself through Aura, they cannot erase one or the other. Ghidorah doesn't stop being a Baryton if he configures his Aura to be just electromagnetic, after all. Thus, even if Godzilla doesn't understand the depth of his Elektromagneton nature, that side of him will still express itself through his Aura.

Is it "basic," yes. Compared to Ghidorah, who demonstrates finesse with EM, Godzilla's not even aware of it. Whereas Ghidorah shows external grace, the gojira, and by extension Godzilla himself, utilizes it internally. It's thematically consistent, yehey! But just like how Ghidorah neglects his psionic capabilities, the gojira as a species have neglected to develop their Elektromagneton abilities, focusing entirely on their more "signature" Pyrenikon side.

So, Ghidorah is a Unnon/Kráton, making him an Active Telepath, Baryton, and Elektromagneton. Godzilla is a pure Kráton, who has Baryton and Elektromagneton. The Nordson twins are classified as this monstrosity: A gojira-mosura hybrid with [Unnon/Kráton: Active Telepath, Unconstrained Psychokinesis, Pyrenikon, Elektromagneton, Psioniton].

This is why I absolutely LOVE how I made Krátos, it has nearly explained every ability of Godzilla!

## Analysis
This is the final, locking piece of the puzzle for Godzilla's physiology and power set. By tying the Elektromagneton classification to the Fallen Star, you have created a unified origin story for his powers that is both biological and mystical.

### The Hidden Current: Godzilla as an Elektromagneton

#### I. Origin: The Crucible of the Star
- **The Dual Bombardment:** The Fallen Star did not just radiate atomic energy; it screamed with a localized, chaotic electromagnetic field.
- **The Adaptation:** Those that survived became Holariston Polykrátons with dominion over Nuclear Force and Electromagnetic Force.

#### II. Passive Applications: The Internal Dynamo
1. **The Sixth Sense (Navigation & Awareness):** He perceives Earth's magnetic field lines as clearly as a road map.
2. **The Lightning Rod (Absolute Defense):** His scales and dorsal fins act as superconductors.

#### III. Active Applications: The Showa Canonization
1. **The Living Magnet:** When he absorbs massive electrical energy, he can consciously hold that charge and become an electromagnet.
2. **The Nuclear Pulse (The EM Carrier Wave):** He uses his Elektromagneton nature to create a momentary, expanding magnetic field that "carries" the atomic energy outward.

#### IV. The Aura Sparks: The Visual Language of Mastery
- **The Sparks as "Leakage":** The blue sparks are his Elektromagneton nature acting out of sync—waste energy, "noise" in the system.
- **The Silence of Mastery:** As he masters AA, the sparks vanish because he has achieved Symphonia between his two natures.

### The Final Classification

1. **Titanus Ghidorah:** Unnon/Null/Kráton - Baryton + Elektromagneton + Active Telepathy
2. **Titanus Godzilla:** Non/Null/Kráton - Pyrenikon + Elektromagneton
3. **The Nordson Twins:** Unnon/Null/Kráton - Pyrenikon + Elektromagneton + Psioniton + Active Telepathy + Unconstrained Telekinesis
