# Fight Me Sexual Tension Eras
> **Source:** `Formatted_With_Thoughts - AA - The Kratos of Kings.md` — Prompt 335

## Your Notes
A huge source of Godric and Maria's sexual tension, even before they start dating, and way before they're married, is their stances on fighting/sparring.

As professional friends, Godric would show off more of his Dagon-side humor to tease her and goad her into fighting him. He will use every trick in the book, even using the mockery the lower Titans will use. She knows what he's doing, and refuses it gently.

As her boyfriend, he'll try and bribe her with wholesome affection: a kiss, a dinner later, a walk along the beach, and so on.

As her husband... sex. Lots of it. Godric has been really, really good at it. Maria still says no, though. It disappoints him a bit. He just wants to connect; she knows that and often feels guilty for rejecting him, rewarding him instead with a quick spar. Hilariously, this makes him horny enough, which is Maria's only goal out of this "fighting debate." If he's horny, he can do his magic on her, which she craves, while not giving him a real fight.

## Analysis
This is a hilariously steamy and incredibly character-driven evolution of their dynamic. You've turned their fundamental philosophical disagreement (Violence vs. Pacifism) into a form of **erotic negotiation and foreplay.**

It brilliantly reinforces their natures: Godric is the relentless pursuer (The Hunter), and Maria is the graceful deflector who ultimately controls the outcome (The Queen).

Let's break down this **Ritual of Refusal** across the eras.

### **The Eras of "Fight Me"**

#### **1. The Era of Denial (Friends/Colleagues): The Tease**
*   **The Tactic:** Godric channels his inner Dagon. He mocks her. "Come on, *Alpha Divine*. Are you scared you'll break a nail? The other Titans say you're getting soft up there in the clouds."
*   **The Response:** Maria rolls her eyes. She sees right through the "Titan mockery." She knows he's just bored and lonely for a peer. She dismisses him with a wave of her hand and a witty retort, keeping the wall of "professionalism" up, even though she finds his childishness secretly charming.

#### **2. The Era of Courtship (Boyfriend/Girlfriend): The Barter**
*   **The Tactic:** Godric learns that honey catches more flies (or moths). He offers **service.** "One serious spar, and I'll take you to that garden in Kyoto you like. I'll even carry your bags."
*   **The Response:** Maria softens. She feels the guilt of denying him, but her principles hold firm. She refuses the fight, but accepts the date anyway, maybe giving him a small display of telekinesis as a consolation prize.

#### **3. The Era of Union (Marriage): The Seduction**
*   **The Tactic:** Godric pulls out the big guns. He weaponizes his stamina and his skill in the bedroom. "Fight me for ten minutes, full power, and I promise you won't sleep tonight. I'll do *that thing* with the tongue."
*   **The Response:** The "Compromise." Maria knows exactly what he's doing, and she wants the reward just as much as he wants the fight.

### **The "Quick Spar": The Ultimate Foreplay**

This specific interaction you described—the "quick spar" that leads to sex—is a fascinating psychological loop for them.

1.  **Godric's Trigger:** Combat is his love language. Seeing Maria move, seeing her block his strikes, seeing the flash of her power—it hits his primal *Gojira* brain. It proves she is his equal. It proves she can handle him. **Violence (controlled) turns him on.**
2.  **Maria's Strategy:** She knows this. She refuses the "Serious Fight" (World War III) but grants the "Quick Spar" (The Dance). She engages just enough to rev his engine, to make him feel that "King and Queen" connection.
3.  **The Pivot:** The moment she feels his arousal spike (which she can sense psionically and physically), she ends the spar. She has successfully transmuted his **Battle Lust** into **Sexual Lust.**

It's a brilliant manipulation on her part. She gets the "magic" she craves, and he gets the physical release and connection he needs, even if it wasn't the *type* of physical release he originally asked for.

### **Scene: The Negotiation**

**(Setting: The Training Room. Godric is shirtless, sweating, radiating heat. Maria is sitting on a bench, reading a book, looking devastatingly elegant.)**

"Five minutes," Godric pleaded, wiping his brow. "Just five minutes. No beams. Just CQC. I need to test this new grapple."

Maria didn't look up from her page. "No, dear."

He walked over, looming over her, casting a shadow. He dropped his voice to that low, gravelly register that he *knew* made her shiver. "Come on, Mari. I'm restless. If you spar with me... I'll make it worth your while." He leaned down, whispering a very specific, very explicit promise into her ear involving his endurance and her pleasure.

Maria paused. Her page stayed unturned. Her cheeks flushed a delightful shade of pink.

"That is... bribery," she said, her voice attempting to remain stern but failing. "And it is beneath the dignity of a King."

"I'm a commoner, remember?" He grinned, the Dagon mischief in full force. "I have no dignity. Fight me."

She sighed, closing the book with a snap. She stood up, and for a split second, her eyes flashed sapphire, and the air in the room grew heavy. "Fine. Three minutes. No atomic energy. And if you ruin this dress, the deal is off."

Godric's grin widened into a predatory smile. "Deal."

**[Two Minutes Later]**

He lunged. She sidestepped, her movement a blur, and used his own momentum to flip him onto the mat. Before he could recover, she was straddling his waist, her hands pinning his wrists to the floor with telekinetic force.

She wasn't using her full strength. She wasn't trying to hurt him. But the sight of her above him, fierce, dominant, and breathing hard...

The "fight" was over immediately.

"You lose," she whispered, leaning down.

"I think," Godric growled, reaching up to pull her down for a kiss, "I just won."
