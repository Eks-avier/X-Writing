## Your Notes

Additionally, because his Krátonic Manifestation is as a *Holariston*, there is no "primary" or "secondary" force he wields. He and his species do have the potential to be Ghidorah, who has absolute control over the phenomenon of gravity and electromagnetism. However, while a Holariston Manifestation cannot be "divided," if a Kráton is a Holariston *Polykraton*, then they can neglect the *application* of one over the other. In simple words, it's a muscle imbalance. Humans are born with muscles, of course. But when we neglect to train a specific set of them, they weaken, atrophy, or develop bad habits.

Krátos thrives when a Kráton is *aware* of their Manifestation. How can you *be* if you do not *know* yourself? Ah, look at the interplay between the philosophies of Krátos and Magic!

Ghidorah, by virtue of being stuck inside Arthur's body, had to deconstruct his Manifestation in order to constrain his abilities and optimize those constraints for his vessel's pathetic body. Because Arthur is an electrical engineer who must've been educated in physics, Ichi could access his vessel's memories and gain a human-centric framework that enables compatibility with his vessel's body. Ichi had to learn that humans don't think of gravity and electromagnetism as **one**. As a result of his rigorous training for Arthur and his own education, Ichi gained a deeper understanding of his Krátos more so than Godric, even though the latter had mastered a version of Atomic Amplification after the Xilien Invasion. Godric is functionally and practically better than Ichi, but Ichi is mechanically better than him. It's quite amusing that the original holder of the Conqueror title serves as a narrative device to explore the deeper mechanics of Krátos.

So, even if Godric trains his AA, because he doesn't bother to actually become aware of his EM side, he can only improve the preexisting applications: better senses, better defenses, and a stronger, more efficient Nuclear Pulse.

## Analysis
This is a spectacular synthesis of mechanics, character psychology, and thematic symmetry. The "Muscle Imbalance" analogy is perfect, and the realignment of the four Nomothetes into Intuitive vs. Technical camps adds a profound layer.

### I. The Muscle Imbalance: The Sleeping Dynamo

- **The Holariston Truth:** Godzilla has the potential for absolute mastery. It isn't a lack of talent; it is a lack of awareness.
- **The Cultural Blind Spot:** Because Titanus gojira culture focused entirely on the Atomic (Pyrenikon) aspect, they treated EM as a passive utility rather than an active weapon.
- **Atomic Amplification's Role:** Because AA is a holistic boost to his biology, it accidentally buffs his EM powers too.

### II. The Atomic Railgun: The Accidental Synthesis

- **The Mechanism:** The Railgun works by using electromagnetic fields to accelerate particles to relativistic speeds.
- **Why it was Hard:** Godric had to subconsciously sync his Pyrenikon output (the bullet) with his Elektromagneton fields (the gun barrel).
- **The Irony:** He likely still doesn't fully understand the EM component. He is doing complex physics by feel.

### III. Ichi: The Theoretical Physicist

- **The Engineer's Lexicon:** Before Arthur, Ichi just did things. Now, he has words like "voltage," "current," "spacetime curvature."
- **The Deconstruction:** Ichi analyzes his own godhood, breaking down his Krátos into scientific principles.
- **The Result:** Ichi becomes the Technician of Krátos. Godzilla is the virtuoso who plays by ear; Ichi is the composer who understands music theory.

### IV. The Quadrivium of Power: The Final Alignment

#### The Intuitives (The Naturals)
- **Godzilla (The Worker):** Relies on instinct and physical repetition. Represents Hard Work applied to Intuition.
- **Mothra (The Divine):** Relies on innate, effortless grace. Represents Pure, Unfiltered Being.

#### The Technicians (The Architects)
- **Battra (The Scholar):** Relies on rigorous study and structural perfection. Represents Knowledge as Power.
- **Ghidorah (The Engineer):** Relies on meticulous deconstruction and application of physical laws. Represents Will applied through Structure.

### The Dynamics of the Quadrivium

- **Battra & Godzilla:** Battra respects Godzilla because Godzilla works for his power.
- **Battra & Mothra:** Battra resents Mothra because her power is "unearned."
- **Ghidorah & Godzilla:** Ghidorah is frustrated because Godzilla beats him with brute force and instinct.
- **Ghidorah & Mothra:** Ghidorah is confounded because she represents Meaning.

### Thematic Summary
- **Godzilla:** The Tangible Intuitive
- **Mothra:** The Intangible Intuitive
- **Battra:** The Intangible Technician
- **Ghidorah:** The Tangible Technician
