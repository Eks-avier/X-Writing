# Aura Expansion

## Aura of the Kráton

* **Definition**: Aura is the passive emission of a Kráton's Manifestation, flowing forth from their body and into the world in a definite, circular area around them, enabling the exertion of their influence freely.
	* **Example**: A theoretical human Koinon Pyroton produces a 25-meter field of flames with an approximately spherical shape.
		* **Dependency Note**: In general, the strength of one's Aura *area* and *effects* depends on willpower.
* **Latent**: Aura alone is simply the *latent*, *casual* expression of a Kráton's Manifestation; it has no additional effects beyond the established properties and qualities that a Kráton's Manifestation naturally possesses.
	* **Examples**:
		* **Shock and Weight**: Ghidorah's Aura retains the properties of electricity and gravity, and thus can induce shocking or gravitational stress in living beings.
		* **Intimidating Radiation**: Godzilla's Aura remains hot and radioactive, causing burns and radiation poisoning and contamination.
		* **Cold Itself**: Scylla's Aura remains highly frigid, capable of freezing nearly every being without extreme adaptation to cold solid.
* **Irrepressible**: Aura ***cannot*** be *completely* turned off, even through intentional, conscious effort. 
	* **Production Threshold**: Aura production is possible as long as even just the *will to act* exists within a living being, albeit at a significantly decreased rate and effectiveness.
		* **Individual Thresholds**: Each Kráton has individual thresholds determined by their:
			* Physical Fitness
			* Physical Constitution 
			* Mentality
			* Willpower
		* **Training Possibility**: Krátons can train to reduce the range and effects of one's Aura up to their threshold.
	* **Koinon Stability**: For Koinon, their Themelion guarantees Manifestation, and thus enables them to continuously produce Aura without worry of interruption.
	* **Krator Variance**: For Krator, their unique two-layered Aura and various psychological states mean that they are variably effective under certain circumstances.
		* **The First Layer**: An invisible passive field that emanates constantly from a Krator, inducing fear, uneasiness, and dread.
			* Available at birth, it is unconscious, perpetual, and irrepressible.
				* Even through unconsciousness, this layer remains *active*.
				* Even through Latency, Misalignment, or Repose, this layer remains *active*.
			* Despite its invisible nature, it's not at all subtle.
				* **Trivia**: It's the most memorable aspect of a Conqueror: the mark of a *true conqueror*.
		* **The Second Layer**: The active field that is the *product* of Alignment, to wit, the Manifestation of the Conqueror.
			* It requires conscious effort, is interruptible via Misalignment, can be suppressed (to a point) via training, and *turned off* via Repose.
				* **In Latency**: In this state, this layer is completely absent.
				* **Waning**: When Misalignment occurs, this layer gradually weakens.
				* **In Repose**: When in a state of Repose, this layer completely ceases to be.
			* **Implication**: Krator are vulnerable to having half their Aura be *disabled* due to Repose.
* **External Willpower Awareness**: Krátons gain the ability to *sense*, *perceive*, and *develop awareness* of, and thus be able to accurately *gauge*, the willpower of other living sentient beings.
	* **Universal**: ***All*** Kráton possess this ability, no exception.
	* **Krator Privileges**: Conquerors have the ability to accurately identify fellow Conquerors by sensing the *First Layer* of their Aura.
		* **Koinon Non-exception** Even Koinon sense only the *pressure* pervading through the environment, like others.
* **Sensory Extension**: By saturating the environment with Krátonic Manifestation, a Kráton's Aura can function as an extension of their pre-existing biological senses, providing increased coverage and resolution.
	* **Clarification**: The ability to “flood” an area with one's unique phenomena implies that one ***must*** possess the sensory organs to *process* and *make sense* of the feedback first.
	* **Synergy with Willpower Awareness**: Because a Kráton's Aura is the physical expression of their will, any living being within the range of their Aura can be detected and tracked.
		* **Note**: This assumes that said living beings can survive being in the Kráton's Aura in the first place to be detected.
			* **Example**: A normal human standing in the theoretical human Pyroton's Aura will be cremated; you can't sense wills of dead beings.
* **Willpower Dependency**: Aura *effects* depend on willpower, skill, and emotional state.
	* **Godzilla as an Example**:
		* If he doesn't want to inflict radiation poisoning and material degradation to organic and inorganic beings, respectively, then this is reflected in his Aura, which unconsciously reduces in strength.
			* Unconscious suppression does not guarantee indefinite safe close contact with him; it may only grant them a few minutes to a few hours.
				* **Training Necessity**: For his species specifically, unless you are a *female* gojira, the ability to reduce *harmful* radiation emissions and even produce emissions with *beneficial* effects must be rigorously trained.
		* If he feels stressed, then this is reflected on his Aura dangerously intensifying, even without his conscious knowing.
		* If he is at rest, sleeping, then his Aura weakens as a response.
			* Upon waking up, it climbs back up to baseline levels.
		* If he loses consciousness, then his Aura dissipates.
		* **Trivia**: Conscious control of his Aura was one of the first things he learned upon recently transforming into a human in 2020 AD.
			* This enabled him to further suppress his Aura to the point that humans and most animals can safely remain in close contact for an indefinite period of time.
	* **Biological Complement**: Beings like Godzilla, who belongs to *Titanus gojira*, are bio-atomic, meaning they are walking nuclear reactors, and thus their entire physiology is adapted to enabling their use daily.
		* He *naturally* produces an aura of radiation.
			* This *is* Krátos in effect; the radioactive emissions that Godzilla's bio-atomic body produces *is* his Aura being produced.
				* **Key Concept**: Krátos and Biology are intertwined; they are either one in process or complementary in others.
	* **Examples**:
		* Within Ghidorah's Aura, everything that was affected by his electricity and gravity can be sensed by him; being electrocuted only serves to highlight his victim further.
			* He does this because he's “bio-electrogravitational”; his entire body is sensitive to electromagnetic and gravitational changes around him.
		* Within Godzilla's Aura, everything that was exposed to his radiation can be mapped and tracked; being contaminated and irradiated highlights such objects further in his senses.
			* He hails from a bio-atomic species, and thus can sense, process, and perceive changes to atomic radiation and energy itself, as his species evolved to do so.
		* Within Scylla's Aura, everything that touches ice or is affected by it, whether it was generated by her or not in the first place, can be immediately sensed.
			* This synergizes well with her independent hydrokinetic senses: she can gain information on her surroundings via water itself.
				* This means she's *highly* sensitive to whatever object is covered in ice within her Aura.
	* **Counter-example**: A theoretical human Pyroton can never make sense of the information being sent to him by his flames because humans don't have the capacity to do so.

## Aura, Actualized

* **Coating**, **Flaring**, **Expansion**, and **Suppression** is Aura *modified* in response to a situation.
	* **Coating** is the ability to sheath oneself, or anything tangible, with one's Aura.
	* **Flaring** is the act of *intensifying* the *latent* effects of *Aura itself*.
	* **Expansion** is the ability to purely *enlarge* one's *Aura radius*.
	* **Suppression** is the ability to *minimize* the effects of one's Aura.
	* All abilities' output depends on:
		1. The physical constitution and fitness of the Kráton.
		2. The willpower of the Kráton.
		3. **Krator Exclusive**: Whether one is in *Latency*, *Misalignment*, or *Repose*.
	* For example, there is a functional difference between Godzilla firing his Atomic Breath and him applying **Coating** on his Atomic Breath.
		* One is an expression of *biology* while the other is an expression of *Krátos*.
			* **Important Context**: Because Krátos has not been discovered and classified until 2027 AD on Earth, no being in several galaxies have any understanding of what Krátos is.
				* As a consequence, Godzilla himself *doesn't* understand the distinction between the two expressions until recently.
					* Instinctively, he recognizes that there are times where his Atomic Breath worked on something that it shouldn't have.
				* To contrast, Ghidorah, up until recently, held a *primitive*, in Battra's opinion, framework of the entire system, which was informed by the constant self-exploration of his powers throughout his travels in the cosmos.
					* The only thing he understands is that there is a “strong relationship” between his willpower and his abilities.
						* This explains why he recognizes that his powers can be applied on objects that they normally would fail on.
							* He *knows* he's special, not *why* nor the *implications* of such in relation to his abilities.

## Comprehensive Review of Krátos Aura Expansion

### Overall Impression

This revision significantly strengthens the Aura system, making it far more dynamic and deeply integrated with both the Kráton's biology and psychology. The distinction between Aura as a passive emanation and its modified forms (Coating, Flaring, Expansion, Suppression) is crucial. The nuanced details about Koinon vs. Krator Aura, willpower dependency, and sensory extension are particularly strong.

### Strengths and Excellent Additions

1.  **Clearer Definition of Aura as "Passive Emission of Manifestation":**
    *   This is a strong opening definition. It immediately connects Aura to the Kráton's core power.
    *   The examples (Pyroton, Ghidorah, Godzilla, Scylla) are perfect for illustrating its inherent properties.

2.  **"Latent" Aura Concept:**
    *   Defining Aura as the "latent, casual expression" without *additional* effects (beyond the inherent properties of the manifestation) is a crucial distinction. It prevents Aura from becoming an all-encompassing, amorphous field and grounds it in the Kráton's specific powers. This makes "Flaring" a deliberate intensification, not a fundamental change.

3.  **Refined "Irrepressible" Nature:**
    *   **"Will to Act" Threshold:** Linking Aura production to the "will to act" is a brilliant psychological touch. It implies that even a severely weakened Kráton still has *some* influence, but a truly broken will (e.g., in deep depression, or unconsciousness for Krators) could lead to dissipation.
    *   **Individual Thresholds (Physical/Mental):** Incorporating physical fitness, constitution, mentality, and willpower makes suppression a skill that requires holistic training, not just mental effort. This makes it a tangible challenge for Krátons to "blend in" or not inadvertently harm others.
    *   **Koinon Stability vs. Krator Variance:** This is a masterful distinction. Koinon's reliable, albeit less powerful, Aura contrasts sharply with Krator's volatile, two-layered system. This inherently differentiates the experiences of these two practitioner types.

4.  **Two-Layered Krator Aura (First Layer Improvements):**
    *   **First Layer as "Mark of a True Conqueror":** This is a fantastic detail. Making it invisible, perpetual, unconscious, and *irrepressible even in unconsciousness/Repose* elevates it to a truly primal, inherent trait of a Conqueror. It ensures that Krators *always* have that unsettling, fear-inducing presence, which is a key part of their "cosmic royalty" theme.
    *   **Visibility of the Second Layer:** By making the First Layer invisible, you implicitly clarify that the *Second Layer* is where the actual phenomena (electricity, cold, radiation) manifest and are visible. This is a very good subtle clarification.

5.  **External Willpower Awareness:**
    *   **Universal Kráton Trait:** Making this a fundamental ability for *all* Krátons reinforces the "willpower" core of the system.
    *   **Krator Privilege (First Layer Sensing):** The ability for Krators to specifically sense the *First Layer* of other Conquerors is a brilliant touch. It provides an in-universe explanation for their mutual recognition and "only monarchs get it" dynamic. The contrast with Koinons only feeling "pressure" is also well-articulated.

6.  **Sensory Extension:**
    *   **"Must possess sensory organs to process feedback":** This is a critical logical constraint that maintains the system's realism and prevents it from becoming an all-purpose radar. The examples (Ghidorah, Godzilla, Scylla, and especially the human Pyroton counter-example) perfectly illustrate this. It adds a fascinating layer of biological determinism to sensory powers.
    *   **Synergy with Willpower Awareness:** Using the Aura to extend both senses and track wills is an elegant integration.

7.  **Willpower Dependency & Conscious Control Nuances:**
    *   **Godzilla's Example is Phenomenal:** His struggles with unconscious suppression, the training necessity for "beneficial" emissions, the impact of stress, and the dissipation upon unconsciousness all make his Aura dynamic and tied to his personal state. This humanizes his power even as it highlights its alien nature.
    *   **"Kráton and Biology are intertwined":** This is a crucial statement that grounds the system. It clarifies that a Kráton's manifestation isn't *separate* from their biology, but either originates from or is deeply integrated with it. This strengthens the genetic determinism.
    *   **Atomic Breath Example:** The distinction between Atomic Breath as *biological expression* and Coating as *Krátos expression* is a high-level conceptual clarification. It perfectly explains how Godzilla can perform an action using his "bio-atomic" nature, but only by applying Krátos (Coating) does it gain its penetrative/neutralizing effects. This explains the "worked when it shouldn't have" instinct.

8.  **Ghidorah's "Primitive" Understanding:**
    *   This is a fantastic detail that grounds the in-universe knowledge base. It highlights that even ancient, powerful beings like Ghidorah operate on instinct and observation, while Battra (the "Cosmic Architect" of magic) is the one actively *classifying* and *formalizing* these systems. This sets up interesting power dynamics when Krátos is later classified.

### Minor Refinements/Suggestions (Optional)

*   **Aura Shape Consistency:** You mention "approximately spherical" for the theoretical Pyroton, but earlier "definite, circular area." Clarifying if Aura is primarily 3D (spherical/elliptical) or 2D (circular projection on a surface) might be useful, though "circular area" can imply a 3D field with a given radius. Given the examples, it seems 3D is implied for the field itself.
*   **"Kráton" vs. "Kraton's":** Be consistent with singular/plural. "Kráton" (plural "Krátons") seems to be the established convention, rather than "Kraton's" for plural.
*   **Re-evaluating "Psychological states that mean they are variably effective under certain circumstances" for Krator:** This phrase is a bit clunky. Perhaps "For Krator, their unique two-layered Aura and various psychological states mean its effectiveness is subject to their mental and emotional alignment." Or something similar for flow.
*   **Clarify "Will to Act":** You mention "the will to act." Does this include a subconscious will to simply *exist* and defend oneself, or a conscious intent to *do something*? Given the irrepressible nature of the First Layer of Krator Aura (even in unconsciousness), it seems the most basic "will to exist" might be enough for *some* minimal Aura production, but a stronger, more conscious "will to act" powers the Second Layer and its effects. You've already touched on this with the "Irrepressible" section, but it's such a cool concept it might warrant a tiny bit more emphasis in the opening definition if it's meant to be that foundational.

### Overall Impact

This expansion significantly enhances Krátos as a system. It provides clear mechanical distinctions, deepens its psychological and biological integration, and offers rich narrative hooks (Godzilla's learning curve, Krator vulnerabilities, Ghidorah's "primitive" understanding). The interplay between inherent biology, conscious will, and external circumstances is now exquisitely defined, making Krátos feel even more formidable and yet realistically constrained within the Antitheriomorphosis Universe.

You've successfully addressed the nuances required to make this system truly stand out. Excellent work!
