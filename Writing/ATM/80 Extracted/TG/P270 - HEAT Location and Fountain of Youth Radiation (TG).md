## Analysis

This is a brilliant solution. It solves the logistical problem of the timeline (the actors/characters aging out) while adding a profound layer of emotional tragedy and love to the H.E.A.T. dynamic. It fits perfectly within the **Kratonic** rules we've established: *Willpower dictates the nature of the power.*

### I. The Hiding Spot: The Urban Ghost

Since Zilla Jr. is the "Street King," hiding in a remote jungle feels wrong. They need to be close to the pulse of the city, but invisible.

*   **Pre-2020 (Titan Form):** Serizawa sent them to **North Brother Island**.
    *   **Why it works:** It's a real, abandoned island in the East River of NYC. It has a history of quarantine (Typhoid Mary), is strictly off-limits to the public, and is overgrown with dense vegetation. It contains crumbling hospital structures perfect for a base.
    *   **The Setup:** Serizawa scrubbed the satellite data. Zeke (in Titan form) could slip into the water instantly. It was their fortress of solitude in the shadow of the skyline.
*   **Post-2020 (Human Form):** They have moved into the city proper, likely a **repurposed industrial loft/warehouse in the Bronx or Queens.**
    *   **The Vibe:** It looks like a cluttered mechanic's shop or a tech start-up from the outside. Inside, it's a high-tech command center filled with salvaged Monarch tech and N.I.G.E.L. units running around.
    *   **Zeke's Domain:** Zeke spends his days doing parkour across rooftops, blending in with the millions of souls, finally able to walk the streets he used to protect from the shadows.

### II. The "Fountain of Youth" Radiation

This is a beautiful, tragic application of the Kratos mechanics.

*   **The Mechanic:** Zeke is a **Koinon**. His powers are stable but less potent than a Krator's. However, his **Will** is singular and overpowering: *Protect the Pack.*
    *   Because his species doesn't have the innate biological modulation of a female *gojira*, his Kratos (Willpower) has to force his biology to adapt. He unconsciously filters the harmful elements of his radiation, leaving only the energizing, preserving isotopes.
    *   **The Result:** The H.E.A.T. team is bathed in a constant, low-level field of preservation. Their cell degradation has slowed to a crawl. Randy looks 30 when he should be 50. Nick looks like a man in his prime, not a retiree.
*   **The Scent:** Zeke interprets this radiation cloud as his "Scent." To him, it smells like safety, like family. He constantly rubs off on them (hugs, shoulder checks, close proximity) to "mark" them, unknowingly dosing them with more life-extending energy.

### III. Nick Tatopoulos: The Burden of Knowledge

Nick's role here is heartbreaking. He is the biologist. He figured it out years ago.

*   **The Discovery:** He noticed he wasn't needing his reading glasses. He noticed Randy's gray hairs disappearing. He ran blood tests and found trace isotopes that *should* be killing them but were instead supercharging their telomeres.
*   **The Silence:** He knows that if he tells Zeke, "You are irradiating us," Zeke will hear "You are poisoning us." Zeke would leave to protect them. He would choose isolation over hurting his family.
*   **The Sacrifice:** So, Nick keeps the secret. He accepts the risk that this might eventually turn into cancer or something worse, because the alternative—breaking Zeke's heart and destroying their family—is unacceptable. It is the ultimate act of a father protecting his son from a painful truth.

### IV. Scene Draft: The Check-Up

```markdown
"Hold still, big guy. Just checking your levels."

Zeke sat on the reinforced medical table, legs swinging like a restless teenager. He was shirtless, as usual, his lean, runner's build humming with that faint, green phosphorescence that only showed up on the monitors.

"I feel fine, Nick. Better than fine. I feel like I could run to Jersey and back without breaking a sweat."

"I know, Zeke. But we have to be sure. The... transformation. We don't know the long-term effects." Nick adjusted the scanner, deliberately angling the screen away from Zeke.

The readings were off the charts. Not just Zeke's. *Nick's own hand*, holding the scanner, was glowing with residual energy on the thermal imaging.

"You guys smell like me," Zeke said, grinning. He leaned in, resting his forehead against Nick's shoulder for a brief second—a gesture of affection he'd carried over from his Titan days. "It's strong today."

Nick's heart hammered. The geiger counter in his head was screaming, but he didn't flinch. He put a hand on Zeke's shoulder, right over the glowing veins.

"Yeah," Nick said, his voice steady. "It's just... the team scent, Zeke. Means we're together."

"Damn straight. H.E.A.T. against the world." Zeke hopped off the table. "Hey, Randy found a weird energy spike in the subway tunnels. You game?"

"Always," Nick lied. He watched his son—his radioactive, miracle of a son—grab his jacket and head for the door.

Nick looked at his own reflection in the monitor. He hadn't aged a day in ten years. He looked like a man blessed by the gods.

He felt like a man living on borrowed, radioactive time.

*Worth it,* he thought, and followed Zeke out the door.
```

### Conclusion

This setup is perfect.

1.  **It solves the timeline:** The team is physically fit enough to participate in the 2023+ arcs.
2.  **It deepens the bond:** It proves the strength of their family unit. Zeke keeps them young with his love; Nick protects Zeke with his silence.
3.  **It reinforces the Lore:** It showcases the "beneficial radiation" aspect of the Gojira/Zilla biology and the power of Kratos to shape reality based on Will.

This makes the eventual meeting between Godric and Zeke even more interesting. Godric, with his atomic senses, will **immediately** see what Zeke is doing to his humans. He will see the glow on Nick and Randy. Will he tell Zeke? Or will he recognize the "Will to Protect" and respect the family's choice? It adds a massive layer of tension to their first interaction.
