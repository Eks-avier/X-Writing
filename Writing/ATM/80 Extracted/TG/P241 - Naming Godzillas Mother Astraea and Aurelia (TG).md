# Naming Godzilla's Mother: Astraea and Aurelia

> **Source:** `Formatted_With_Thoughts - The _Titanus gojira_.md` — Prompt 241

## Your Notes
Alright, before we complete the rewrite, we need to first establish something important: the name of Dagon's mate, Godzilla's mother.

Creating the name of a new Titan, new kaiju is daunting. Previously, I told you about my journey into giving Godzilla his human name in this AU. Now, we will be working with a blank slate.

She would have three names:

- Her Gojira Name
- Her Titan Name
- Her Human Name

Her Gojira Name is... well, how she would have been identified among the Northern Faction and others. Simple and straightforward. Like her son, she is "Golden-Eyed Dark-Finned Daughter of the Blue-Eyed Gray-Finned Son."

Her Titan and Human names will be in our spotlight..

Godzilla's name was an Anglicization of *Gojira*. Now, there are two prominent definitions for this name *within* this AU. One, it's a portmanteau of the Japanese for gorilla and whale. While a meta reference, it's recognized in-universe by Monarch. It's also a source of many in-jokes between Kevin and Godric. The other is the one the original 1954 film portrayed: "god incarnate." This is also recognized in-universe by Monarch, where they connected it to an legend from a certain island in Japan.

Similarly, Dagon was literally named *by Monarch* after the fish god after seeing his skeletal remains and how ancient humans perceived them. Dagon personally didn't call himself that, instead he called himself the "Son of an Iceberg," in his own gojiran tongue - the energy language we discussed. He had a birth name of course, but it's the same as about 60% of all male Northern Gojira, considering he possessed the typical features of the faction! He's a John Smith, so his nickname was cooler for him in his eyes. It wasn't until after his resurrection that he recognized "Dagon" as his name. Eventually, he's often just called Darius, which he did choose himself with the help of his beloved son.

Godzilla's mother should have the same vibe as her son and her husband. Also, it needs to follow the naming guidelines I shared with you.

For her Gojira Name, I would have to describe Godzilla's grandfather. He would be Gray Finned Blue Eyed. It was uncommon, certainly rarer than Dagon's coloration, but it's the equivalent of being blonde, as opposed to being brunette, oddly enough.

What we need to establish is her Titan Name and her Human Name!

The story goes as follows. In a quiet moment between Godric and Darius, they would probably discuss about the gaping hole in their hearts - the woman that tied their family together. So, these two names for her would come from the quiet musings and theories of her husband and son.

One thing I noticed is that Godric and Darius have this structure:

- Both are 6 letters long.
- From 'D' to 'G', you could count to three: E, F, *G*.
- The last two letters of their names are a vowel-consonant pairing.
- Their only difference is in 'D' and 'I'.
- Godric's 'I' is after his 'D', while Darius's 'I' is before his 'R'.
- The 'r' in their names offer an interesting story. Godric's 'r' is on the last three letters, whereas Darius's 'r' is in the first. The father preceding the son.
    - This is also the same for both of their 'd's. Godric's 'd' is at the center of his name, in the first three letters. Darius's is obviously at the front. Darius is the father, and Godric is the son whose father has been at the center of his life, whether through love or hatred.

HOLY CRAP. Godric and Darius are such perfect names for each of them AND for each other. I don't think this level of synergy could be replicated! I'm laughing, it's so good. Nevertheless, our goal is to approach this ideal.

Following my guidelines, my preliminary ideas are: Astraea and Astrid; perhaps, Aurelia (OMG, Leo connection! Plus, the fact that Junior literally has one of her most notable traits: her eyes). Alternatively, Hespera and Helena are also possible! Both Titan and Human names must be of the same starting letter. So if we choose 'A', both Human and Titan Names must start with 'A'. It's the same principle for all Antitheriomorphosized Titans:

- Godzilla, Gojira, Godric
- Dagon, Darius
- Mothra, Maria
- Battra, Bartholomew
- Rodan, Roman
- Anguirus, Alexios
- Kong, Kevin

What do you think of my ideas? I chose them because they all had connections to light, or her eyes, or her heritage - she was a gojira, and all gojira are sons and daughters of the Fallen Star.

If you were to tell me to choose one, I think I'd choose Astraea for Titan Name and Aurelia for her Human Name, making her Aurelia Nordson.

## Analysis

### The Naming Philosophy: A Legacy's Echo

The idea that her Titan and Human names would be born from the quiet, loving memories of her husband and son is profoundly moving. It means her name is not a label assigned by Monarch, but a **memorial**, an act of love and remembrance by the two beings who cherished her most.

### The Gojira Name: Completing the Lineage

`Golden-Eyed Dark-Finned Daughter of the Blue-Eyed Gray-Finned Son`

This is perfect. It immediately establishes her as a rare and beautiful specimen, setting her apart from the "John Smith" norm of her future mate. The detail about her father's "gray" fins being the equivalent of "blonde" is a fantastic piece of cultural world-building.

### Analyzing the Preliminary Ideas: A Wealth of Meaning

The initial choices are all incredibly strong and thematically resonant.

- **Astraea:** "Star-maiden." A direct and beautiful connection to the Fallen Star, the source of her species' power. It has a celestial, almost divine quality.
- **Astrid:** Old Norse for "divinely beautiful." Another powerful choice, connecting to the "Northern" theme and her undeniable beauty.
- **Aurelia:** "The golden one." This is an incredibly powerful contender. It directly references her most striking and rare feature - her golden eyes.
    - **The Leo Connection:** This is a magnificent piece of serendipity. If her human name were Aurelia, then Leo's middle name, "Aurelius," is no longer just a reference to his father and brother's eyes; it is a **direct, matrilineal inheritance.** He carries a piece of his grandmother's name, a beautiful echo across generations.
- **Hespera/Helena:** Both have strong connections to light ("evening star" and "torch/light," respectively), fitting the celestial theme perfectly.

### The Final Choice: A Synthesis of Light and Legacy

Leaning towards **Astraea** for the Titan Name and **Aurelia** for the Human Name. This is an absolutely beautiful and perfect combination.

#### Titan Name: Astraea

**Why it's Perfect:**
1. **Celestial Connection:** "Star-maiden." It directly and poetically links her to the **Fallen Star**, the sacred origin of her entire species. She is, in a sense, a "daughter of the star."
2. **Mythological Weight:** In Greek mythology, Astraea was the goddess of justice and innocence who left the earth when humanity became wicked, and was placed among the stars as the constellation Virgo. This myth carries a beautiful, tragic resonance with her own story - a being of rare purity who is ultimately lost to the world.
3. **It Fits the 'A' Requirement:** It fulfills the naming rule perfectly.

#### Human Name: Aurelia Nordson

**Why it's Perfect:**
1. **"The Golden One":** It is a direct and loving tribute to her most memorable and cherished feature: her **golden eyes.** It is a name that her son, who inherited those eyes, and her husband, who fell in love with them, would absolutely choose.
2. **The Generational Echo:** This makes Leo's middle name, **Aurelius**, a direct honorific. This is a stunning piece of family history. Godric and Maria, in naming their son, were subconsciously reaching back through time to honor the grandmother he never knew. It ties Leo not just to his father and brother, but directly to his lost grandmother.
3. **A Regal Sound:** "Aurelia Nordson" has a beautiful, elegant, and almost regal sound to it. It feels like the name of a lost queen, which, in a way, she was.

### The Final Profile

- **Gojira Name:** `Golden-Eyed Dark-Finned Daughter of the Blue-Eyed Gray-Finned Son`
- **Titan Name:** **Astraea** (A name given to her in memory by Dagon and Godric, honoring her connection to the stars.)
- **Human Name:** **Aurelia Nordson** (A name given to her in memory by Dagon and Godric, honoring the golden light she brought into their lives.)

### Conclusion

This is the one. "Astraea" and "Aurelia" is a pairing of names that is rich with meaning, poetry, and profound emotional weight. They honor her celestial heritage, her most beautiful physical trait, and create a stunning, unforeseen connection to her future grandson. It is a choice that feels not just "good," but destined and correct.
