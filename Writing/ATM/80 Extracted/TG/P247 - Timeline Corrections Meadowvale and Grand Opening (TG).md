## Analysis

A critical structural integrity issue has been identified: the **Timeline vs. Reputation Paradox.**

If the world knows "Godric Nordson is Godzilla" in early 2025 (Grand Opening), the Meadowvale Arc (where he needs to be an undercover teacher) breaks completely.

### The Solution: The "Soft Launch" vs. The "Hard Launch"

To make Meadowvale work, the *nature* of the "Grand Opening" and the status of Godric's identity must be adjusted.

#### 1. The "Grand Opening" (Early-Mid 2025): The Soft Launch
- **What is revealed:** Monarch reveals the **Monster Island Palace** to the world governments and the public. They reveal that the Titans have a "headquarters" and a "civilization."
- **What is kept secret:** The **Human Forms.** The public is told that the Titans reside there. They are *not* told that the Titans look like handsome humans.
- **The Keep Charlie Role:** The students act as the bridge. They are the ones presented as the "human liaisons" living on the island. This protects the Titans' privacy while giving the students the spotlight.
- **Godric's Status:** He acts as a "Monarch Consultant" in the background. To the public, Godric Nordson is just a high-ranking, mysterious Monarch agent.

#### 2. Meadowvale (Mid 2025 - Mid 2027): The Deep Cover
- Because the public doesn't know Godric Nordson = Godzilla, the cover works.
- **The Tension:** The risk isn't just "people finding out they are Titans"; it's "people finding out the truth about the Antitheriomorphosis."

#### 3. The New York Incident (Mid 2027): The Hard Launch
- This is the moment the dam breaks. Godric uses his powers publicly in human form to save the city/Arthur.
- **The Aftermath:** The secret is out. This forces the "official" public reveal of the Titans' human identities, segueing perfectly into the Dagon arc where Godric is now dealing with global fame *and* his father's return.

Here is the reorganized structure, accommodating the 2-year Meadowvale mission and the correct order of Dagon and Biollante.
