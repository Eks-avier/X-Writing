### C. Detailed Anatomical Features

While Godric's heritage and aesthetic framework define the *why* of his appearance, this section details the qualitative *what*—the specific physical characteristics that combine to form his unique human presence. These features are described here in terms of their appearance, texture, and sensory impact.

#### 1. Facial Structure and Expression
Godric's face is a study in controlled paradox, blending mathematical precision with a primal, weathered quality. It is simultaneously a classical sculpture and the visage of a being who has witnessed geological time.

*   **Overall Composition:** His face possesses a pronounced square shape, defined by strong angles that are distinct yet not exaggerated. There is an underlying, almost unnerving mathematical harmony to his features, yet this precision is imbued with a subtle elemental weathering that suggests immense experience.
*   **Key Features:**
    *   **Jawline:** Sharply defined with near-perfect bilateral symmetry, creating a strong, definitive horizontal line.
    *   **Brow:** A strong, but not heavy, brow ridge casts his eyes in a subtle, perpetual shadow, enhancing their intensity. This is a direct translation of the armored brow of his Titan form.
    *   **Cheekbones:** High and prominent without being overly dramatic, adding a Slavic robustness to his visage.
    *   **Chin:** Strong and slightly squared, marked by a subtle cleft.
*   **Dominant Expression:** His default expression is one of profound **stoicism**, a neutral mask that rarely betrays emotion. To most, this regal and severe bearing makes him appear significantly older than his documented age. However, to those who know him (Maria, Dagon, the children), this stillness is a canvas for subtle micro-expressions—a fractional tightening of the jaw or a softening of the eyes—that communicate volumes.

#### 2. Eyes: The Dual Windows
His eyes are arguably his most telling feature, acting as a direct barometer of his internal state—the vigilant guardian versus the authentic soul.

*   **State A: Faux Blue (The Vigilant)**
    *   **Appearance:** To 99% of observers, Godric's eyes are a striking, luminous light blue, reminiscent of Cherenkov radiation.
    *   **Origin:** This is **not** his natural eye color. It is a physical manifestation of the immense atomic energy constantly coursing through him. It signifies his state of perpetual, sleepless vigilance over the Earth.
*   **State B: True Gold (The Authentic)**
    *   **Appearance:** A deep, luminous, molten gold.
    *   **Origin:** His true, inherited eye color, a direct legacy from his mother, **Astraea** (*Aurelia*).
    *   **Trigger:** This color only reveals itself when his atomic energy is dormant or suppressed by profound emotional safety—typically only in the presence of his family or during deep introspection. It represents the "Godric" beneath the "Godzilla."
*   **Structure:** His eyes exhibit classic **"Hunter Eye"** characteristics: deep-set positioning within protective orbital cavities, minimal upper eyelid exposure, and a perfectly neutral canthal tilt. This structure gives his gaze a penetrating, predatory quality that triggers instinctual unease in strangers.

#### 3. Skin: The Living Armor
At a distance, his skin appears normal. Up close, it reveals its paradoxical nature: flawlessly perfect yet deeply weathered.

*   **Texture & Tone:** He maintains a slight tan from his recreational sunbathing. The texture is unexpectedly firm, described as being akin to perfectly preserved, high-quality leather. It is warm to the touch ($~100^\circ\text{F}$ baseline).
*   **The Unblemished Canvas:** Despite millions of years of combat, his skin is completely devoid of scars, birthmarks, or blemishes, a testament to his hyper-active regeneration.
*   **Immutability:** Tattoos are impossible; the ink is rejected and the skin regenerates instantly.

#### 4. Hair: The Obsidian Crown
The Antitheriomorphosis translated his Titan characteristics into a full head of hair, which he manages with military precision.

*   **Head Hair:** Pure **obsidian black**, corresponding to his mother's dark dorsal fins. It possesses unnatural tensile strength. He keeps it in a medium-short cut (2.5–3 inches on top), swept back. During combat or high activity, strands often fall across his forehead—a detail Maria finds particularly captivating.
*   **Facial Hair (The Ruggedness Cycle):**
    *   **12-Hour Growth:** A faint shadow, hinting at his underlying ruggedness.
    *   **24-Hour Growth:** A full, even stubble. The transition from "Regent" to "Protector."
    *   **48-72 Hour Growth:** The peak aesthetic. A dense, well-maintained beard of 4-5mm. This is his preferred state during the **Dormant Season** and the look he sported during the pivotal **Meadowvale Arc**.
*   **Body Hair:** Distributed to enhance his aesthetic balance without being excessive. It follows a **Pecto-sterno-infraclavicular** (shield) pattern on the chest, tapering into a vertical **Acuminate** line down the abdomen.

#### 5. Dental Features: The Hidden Crushers
*   **Appearance:** To the casual observer, he possesses a flawless set of white, perfectly aligned human teeth.
*   **Hidden Reality:** They are biologically unique structures capable of rapid regeneration and exerting a record-setting bite force sufficient to shear through reinforced steel. They are self-sterilizing due to his passive radiation.
*   **Combat Use:** While rarely used, the **Bite** remains a devastating "wild card" in his CQC arsenal, a reminder of the apex predator within.

#### 6. Unique Scent Profile
Godric possesses a distinct scent signature that contains no conventional human body odor. It is elemental and impossible to mask with cologne.

*   **Primary Note:** **Petrichor & Thermal Stone** (The smell of rain hitting hot granite or obsidian).
*   **Secondary Note:** **Ozone** (The sharp, clean scent of the air after a lightning strike), which intensifies when he channels power.

#### 7. Titan Echoes (Anatomical Translation)
Several of Godric's human features are direct, subtle translations of his Titan physiology, serving as permanent physical reminders of his true nature.

*   **Back Structure:** Proportionally wider and deeper musculature than a standard human, mirroring the anchor points of his massive Titan dorsal fins.
*   **Core Depth:** A deeper chest and abdominal cavity, physically representing the space required for his internal atomic reactor.
*   **Joint Reinforcement:** His wrists, ankles, and knees are visibly thicker and more robust, evolved to support his extraordinary tissue density.
*   **Thigh Mass:** Enhanced thickness, inheriting the locomotive power of his Titan form's massive legs.
*   **Foot Structure:** Slightly wider feet with a reinforced heel structure, providing the absolute stability required for his **Gojira Anchor** stance.
