## III. Core Biology and Species Abilities

This section details the intrinsic nature of Godric's being, exploring the biological foundations of his power, his species' unique evolutionary history, and how his existence interfaces with the fundamental forces of the universe.

### A. Species Heritage (Summary)

The *Titanus gojira* were not born as kings. Their journey to the pinnacle of the Titan hierarchy was an unprecedented evolutionary ascent, marked by a singular cosmic event and a subsequent, tragic decline.

#### 1. Proto-Gojira: The Unremarkable Ancestors
Long before the arrival of the Fallen Star, the ancient ancestors of the *Titanus gojira* were a physically unremarkable species.

*   **Classification:** **Emergent / Inferior.** In an era dominated by the established Ascendant lineages like the *Titanus mosura*, the proto-gojira were considered trivial.
*   **Temporal Context:** They inhabited the **Late Permian** period. To humans, they would resemble large saurian predators—cousins of the *Tyrannosaurus rex*—but within the Titan world, they were nothing more than "unremarkable lizards."
*   **The "Pest" Reputation:** Because they lacked the overt power or elemental mastery of other Titans, they were often viewed as "pests" or the prehistoric equivalent of **cockroaches.** They were common, seemingly lucky in their survival, and largely ignored by the great powers of the age.
*   **The Hidden Edge (Limitless Adaptation):** Even in this Emergent state, they possessed their first and most vital ability: **Limitless Adaptation.** This was a subtle power; it didn't allow them to conquer, but it allowed them to **thrive.** They were the ultimate extremophiles, capable of adjusting their biology to survive in any environment, from volcanic vents to glacial plains.
*   **Meta-Inspiration:** This stage of their history draws from the Heisei-era origin (e.g., the *Godzillasaurus*), framing them as a natural species that was later "boosted" into godhood.
