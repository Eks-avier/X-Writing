### E. Seasonal Physique Variation

Unlike other organisms whose seasonal changes are biological mandates for survival, Godric's cycle is a **purely voluntary adaptation** he established post-Antitheriomorphosis. It is a conscious, strategic choice to manage his energy, maintain his cover, and adapt the circadian rhythms of an immortal Titan to the demanding schedule of human society.

#### 1. The Voluntary Cycle: Energy Management vs. Hibernation
In his Titan form, Godric followed a specific rhythm: resting during the warm months (Spring–Fall) and becoming fully active during the Winter, his species' preferred "vacation" season. His human life creates a conflict: he must work and be active during the warm months to maintain his cover (e.g., teaching, Monarch operations).
*   **The Winter Crash:** By the time Autumn arrives, he has spent nearly a year fighting his natural cycle. The "Dormant Season" (September–November) is his biological compromise. He forces his body into a state of recovery to prevent burnout.
*   **Metabolic Shift:** This is the only time his metabolism shifts from being primarily radiation-based to relying on **conventional nutrition**. To maintain his mass while his internal reactor is in "low power mode," he must significantly increase his caloric intake.
    *   **Result:** He gains mass (approx. $9$ kg), his definition softens, and he achieves the "Heraclean" bulk.

#### 2. The Winter Camouflage Strategy
Godric leverages human seasonal expectations to mask the physical changes of his dormancy. He turns his fatigue into a disguise.
*   **Layering:** As his bulk increases, he adopts a layered wardrobe. The undershirt becomes mandatory, often paired with waistcoats or thick sweaters. This hides the loss of his "statue-like" definition while emphasizing his sheer size.
*   **Performance of Frailty:** He engages in a calculated performance of "feeling cold"—rubbing his hands together, gravitating toward heat sources, or drinking hot beverages. To a human observer, he is just a big man sensitive to the chill. To a Titan observer, it is a hilarious irony, as he is immune to cold.

#### 3. The "Hibernation Misunderstanding" as Operational Cover
This cycle has generated a persistent misunderstanding within Monarch, which Godric deliberately cultivates as an asset.
*   **The Monarch Theory:** Staff believe he is entering a biological hibernation state common to reptiles, slowing down to conserve energy during the cold.
*   **The Reality:** He is physically tired but mentally sharper than ever. He uses the excuse of "hibernation" to retreat from daily duties. It is not a time of unconsciousness, but of **Strategic Planning.** He spends these months in his quarters or his truck, undistracted, formulating long-term strategies for the coming year.
*   **The Benefit:** He finds no reason to correct a misconception that grants him three months of relative solitude and administrative immunity.

#### 4. Psychological State: The Grumpy King
While useful, this season takes a toll on his mood.
*   **The Missed Vacation:** He is grumpy because he is missing his favorite time of year. Biologically, he *wants* to be swimming in the freezing Arctic waters or hunting in the Hollow Earth. Instead, he is forced to rest because his human life exhausted him during the summer.
*   **Lethargy:** He genuinely feels a heavy, physical lethargy during this time, a sensation he detests. This grumpiness often manifests as increased taciturnity and a shorter fuse for nonsense, leading Monarch staff to tread very lightly around the "sleeping bear."
