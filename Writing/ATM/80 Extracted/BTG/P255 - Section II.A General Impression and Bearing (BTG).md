## II. Physical Profile (Human Form)

Godric's human form is a meticulously crafted manifestation of his ancient Titan essence. It is a physique of unsettling perfection, designed to navigate the human world while subtly betraying the extraordinary nature of the being within.

### A. General Impression and Bearing

Godric's physical presence is a masterclass in controlled contradiction. At a distance, he appears to be an exceptionally well-built and conventionally handsome man. However, closer inspection reveals a series of uncannily precise traits that signal something fundamentally inhuman.

#### 1. Uncanny Bilateral Symmetry
Godric's body possesses a near-perfect **98.7% bilateral symmetry**. While aesthetically striking, this characteristic is the primary trigger of subconscious unease in human observers. The human brain is conditioned to expect minor asymmetries; Godric's near-flawless mirroring—from facial structure to musculature, down to the patterns of his fingerprints—creates an instinctual sense of artificiality. He registers less as a biological organism and more as a divine statue or a perfectly engineered effigy, contributing to the sensation that he is "too perfect to be real."

#### 2. The Proximity Effect
Humans experience a predictable, layered physiological response to Godric's presence, directly correlated with their distance from him. This effect serves as a natural, subconscious social buffer, activating ancient survival circuits in the human limbic system.

| Distance | Conscious Perception | Subconscious/Physiological Response |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **> 15 Meters** | Conventional attraction. | Normal social interest; admiration of physique. |
| **5 – 15 Meters** | Recognition of "unusual" perfection. | Growing unease; attraction becomes intimidating or "off." Heart rate elevates slightly. |
| **< 5 Meters** | Awareness of commanding authority. | Activation of primal fight-or-flight circuits. An instinctual urge to defer, submit, or flee, regardless of social status. |

#### 3. Perpetual Readiness
A key physical trait telegraphing his constant vigilance is the minimal variance between his relaxed and flexed muscular states. Even at rest, his muscles remain in a state of partial engagement, ready for explosive, supersonic movement.

*   **Upper Arm Expansion (Flexed):** +6.00% (Active Season)
*   **Chest Expansion (Flexed):** +3.35% (Active Season)

This state of physical tension is the somatic parallel to his **Faux Blue** eyes. Just as his true golden eye color is masked by the blue of his atomic vigilance, his body's capacity for true relaxation is veiled by a state of constant, controlled readiness.

#### 4. Movement, Gait, and Posture
*   **The Predator's Gait:** His walk is economical and tactical. He does not bounce or sway; he glides with a heavy, inexorable momentum. Every step is placed with the precision of a creature that cannot afford to waste energy.
*   **The Recalibrated Balance:** In the early months of 2020 (Adaptation Period I), his movement was marked by a subtle backwards lean, a subconscious attempt to counterbalance a tail that no longer existed. By 2021, this was perfectly recalibrated into a posture of absolute, vertical stability.
*   **The Immovable Object:** He maintains an effortless military bearing. Whether standing or sitting, his spine is perfectly aligned, projecting an aura of unshakable stability. He does not fidget.

#### 5. The Voice of the King: A Linguistic Profile

Godric's vocal quality is a **deep, resonant baritone** described as "gravel wrapped in velvet"—possessing a weathered, roughness akin to ancient stone, smoothed over by a rich, commanding timbre. However, his accent and vocabulary shift depending on his psychological state and social context.

*   **Mode A: General American (The Voice of the Crown)**
    *   **Context:** Public speaking, Monarch briefings, international diplomacy.
    *   **Characteristics:** "Polished Steel." Crisp, precise, and authoritative. Flattened vowels and hard 'r's. Devoid of regional idiosyncrasies to ensure absolute clarity and command. This is the voice of **Godzilla**.
*   **Mode B: Northern English/Scottish (The Voice of the Soul)**
    *   **Context:** Private moments, family interactions, times of deep comfort or authenticity.
    *   **Characteristics:** "The North Resounding." A subtle blend of Northern English and Scottish inflections. Features gently rolled 'r's, deeper vowels, and earthy vocabulary (*aye, nay, lad, lass, bairn, dour*). This is the voice he gravitates toward instinctually, connecting him to his "Northern" heritage. This is the voice of **Godric**.
*   **Mode C: Japanese - Bōsō Dialect (The Voice of the Heart)**
    *   **Context:** Intimate family moments, interactions with the Yamamoto family, or while in Japan.
    *   **Characteristics:** Highly specific to the coastal Chiba region. Warm, rustic, and imbued with the cadence of fishermen. It is the sound of his human rebirth and his first experience of unconditional love.
*   **Mode D: Southern US Drawl (The Voice of Mischief)**
    *   **Context:** Teasing Madison, disarming tense situations, or signaling relaxation to close staff.
    *   **Characteristics:** A conscious affectation learned from **Dr. Rick Stanton**. Used as a tool of humor or to feign simplicity.

**Speech Structure:**
Regardless of accent, his sentence structure tends to be **imperative and taciturn**. He favors asyndetic phrasing (omitting conjunctions) and often drops articles or subjects for efficiency.
*   *Example:* "Go. To location. Now."
*   **The Royal "We":** When speaking with **Maria** in official capacities, they often unconsciously adopt the *pluralis majestatis*, reinforcing their unified sovereignty.
