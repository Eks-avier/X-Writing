# Alright Nebulans Bilusaludo And The Exif
> **Source:** `Formatted_With_Thoughts - The Antitheriomorphosis AU.md` — Prompt 249

## Your Notes

ALRIGHT! Nebulans, Bilusaludo, and the Exif all have solid ties to the characters now. 

We can now solidly return to the Precursors and the Pacific Rim arc. 

Remember that the events of the *New Empire* run in parallel? We need an inciting incident for the characters of the PPDC. 

The Kaiju don't appear on Earth from the Breach (which is now from the Moon) until 2030! This means there is a substantial amount of time between the establishment of the PPDC and the Kaiju's appearance. *The PPDC wasn't created in response to the Kaiju, meaning we have to change the motivations and back stories of the  Pacific Rim characters whose inciting incident was the Kaiju*.

I wonder if we can root this in an incident between 2027–2030 or from an earlier event, like the Xilien Invasion? Which one is better? 

Since we're not done establishing them, shall we begin?

## Analysis

This is a fantastic and crucial question. You've correctly identified the central challenge and the greatest opportunity in adapting the Pacific Rim characters to the ATM Universe: **their motivations must be pre-Kaiju.** This is a brilliant narrative puzzle.

The answer is, we don't have to choose between a recent incident and an earlier one. The most compelling solution is to **root their motivations in the accumulated trauma of the *entire post-2019 era*.** The Kaiju are not the *inciting incident*; they are the horrifying **validation** of a fear that has been building for over a decade.

Let's begin by setting this foundation.

---

### **The PPDC: A Child of a Traumatized Decade**

The psyche of the PPDC and its core members is not forged by the Kaiju. It is forged by the relentless, escalating, and often incomprehensible series of "monster" events that have plagued humanity since Ghidorah's awakening.

**The Timeline of Trauma (2019-2030):**

1.  **The Mass Awakening (2019):** Dozens of Titans erupt from the earth, causing untold destruction on a global scale. Cities are flattened. Millions die. **This is Trespasser-level trauma, years before Trespasser.**
2.  **The Antitheriomorphosis (2020):** The monsters don't disappear. They *transform* and start walking among us, a profound and terrifying paradigm shift.
3.  **The Age of Instability (2020-2024):** This is a period of constant low-grade terror and spectacular incidents: Godric's initial rampages, Biollante's emergence, the Kiryu incident. The "Titans are now humans" narrative is undermined by repeated instances of their immense destructive power.
4.  **The Xilien Invasion (2024):** Not just a monster attack, but a full-scale **alien invasion,** with orbital bombardments, advanced fighters, and the deployment of new, terrifying creatures like Gigan. This is a planetary-scale war.
5.  **The Great Deception (2025 onwards):** Monarch reveals the humanized Titans, but for a growing segment of the population, this isn't a relief; it's a terrifying confirmation that the world is run by hidden, non-human entities. The "Trained Animal" theory takes root.

**Conclusion:** By the time the PPDC is formally established around 2027, humanity is not waiting for a threat. They are a deeply traumatized species that has already endured multiple apocalypses. The PPDC is not a "just in case" project; it is a **"never again" project.** They are building the Jaegers not to fight a future war, but to finally give humanity a say in the war that has been raging for their entire adult lives.

This framework is much stronger because it means the core characters of Pacific Rim—Raleigh, Yancy, Mako, Stacker—don't need the Kaiju to be motivated. They are already survivors of a decade of horror.

---

With this foundation set, let's begin to build our core characters.

### **The Becket Brothers: Raleigh and Yancy**

*   **Core Archetype:** The Hotshot Aces.
*   **ATM Universe Backstory:** Instead of being civilian construction workers, let's make them **US Air Force pilots**—the best of the best.
    *   **The Inciting Incident (The Xilien Invasion, 2024):** Their story begins in the skies over a major city during the Xilien Invasion. They are flying advanced fighter jets against Xilien ships. They are incredibly skilled, but they are hopelessly outmatched by the alien technology and the sheer scale of the conflict.
    *   **The Moment of Helplessness:** During the battle, they witness **Rodan**—a being they've only known as a "Titan"—engage a squadron of Xilien fighters. They watch as this single, human-looking being incinerates an entire wing of alien ships with a casual wave of his hand. They see firsthand the immense, god-like power that Monarch is "allied" with.
    *   **Yancy's Conviction:** For Yancy, this is a moment of profound revelation. He sees the Titans not as saviors, but as an uncontrollable variable in an already chaotic equation. His motivation is born here: *"We were the best pilots on Earth, and we were useless. We can't rely on them. We have to be able to fight these battles ourselves."* He becomes a true believer in the PPDC's humanist cause.
    *   **Raleigh's Motivation:** Raleigh's motivation is simpler and more personal: **he follows his brother.** He shares Yancy's conviction, but his core drive is their unbreakable bond. Where Yancy goes, he goes.

By the time the Jaeger program is recruiting its first pilots, the Becket brothers are legendary Air Force aces who have seen the worst of an alien war and are desperate for a way to give humanity a fighting chance. They don't need a Kaiju to motivate them; the memory of their own powerlessness during the Xilien War is all the motivation they need.

What do you think of this as a starting point for their characters?
