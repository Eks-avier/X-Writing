# Excellent Now How Do We Setup
> **Source:** `Formatted_With_Thoughts - The Antitheriomorphosis AU.md` — Prompt 258

## Your Notes

Excellent! Now, how do we setup Monarch's reaction to the creation of the PPDC, since they would be made *in response* to the anti-Titan sentiment and not M-Day.

## Analysis

This is a fantastic question, and the answer lies in a reaction that is complex, conflicted, and deeply frustrating for Monarch. Their response to the PPDC's creation would not be one of simple opposition, but a painful mix of **begrudging understanding, professional jealousy, and profound, Cassandra-like dread.**

---

### **Monarch's Reaction: A Cautious Welcome for a Trojan Horse**

The formation of the PPDC, well before M-Day, would be a major political event that Monarch has to navigate with extreme delicacy. Here's how their reaction would play out.

#### **Phase 1: The Public Stance - "A Welcome Partner" (Gritted Teeth)**

On the world stage, Monarch would have no choice but to publicly welcome the PPDC.

*   **The Political Reality:** The anti-Titan sentiment is a powerful political force. Openly opposing a popular, well-funded "human self-defense" initiative would be political suicide. It would paint Monarch as arrogant, elitist "monster-lovers" who want to maintain a monopoly on global security.
*   **Mark Russell's Official Line:** In public, Mark would issue carefully worded statements about "valuing diverse approaches to planetary defense" and "looking forward to a collaborative relationship with our new partners at the PPDC." It would be a masterpiece of diplomatic doublespeak, and every word would taste like ash in his mouth.
*   **A veneer of Cooperation:** Monarch would be forced to share some of its non-classified data on Titan biology and behavior. This would be a painful process, with Monarch scientists (like Rick Stanton) likely redacting information and providing the bare minimum required, all while accusing the PPDC of being reckless amateurs.

#### **Phase 2: The Internal Reaction - "This is a Disaster."**

Behind the closed doors of Castle Bravo, the mood would be one of deep anxiety and frustration.

*   **Mark Russell's True Fear:** He would see the PPDC not as a partner, but as the **successful rebranding of the Shadow Organization's core philosophy.** He knows the stench. He recognizes the playbook. He sees them successfully weaponizing public fear to achieve the very thing he's been fighting against for years: the idea that Titans are a problem to be "managed" and "controlled" with human technology.
*   **The Scientific Snobbery (Rick Stanton & Team):** Monarch's scientific staff would be deeply offended by the PPDC's approach.
    *   They would see the Jaeger program as crude, brutish, and fundamentally misunderstanding the nature of Titans. "You don't fight a hurricane with a giant boxing robot," Rick might quip. "You study the weather patterns."
    *   They would view the PPDC's "human-centric" approach as arrogant and dangerously simplistic, ignoring decades of Monarch's own research into the complex ecological and psychological nature of the Titans.
*   **The Ethical Alarm Bells (The Chen Twins & Psionic Division):** The announcement of the Drift program would send a shockwave of horror through Monarch's psionic and mythological experts.
    *   They would see it as a grotesque and unethical weaponization of psionic potential, a crude attempt to force a "neural handshake" with a machine that is the polar opposite of the organic, empathetic connection they study.
    *   This is where the Psionic Division's ethical debate would begin in earnest, years before the Kaiju arrive. Is it ever ethical to use a human mind as the "wetware" for a weapon of war?

#### **Phase 3: The Titan Response - A Collective Shrug**

When Mark brings his profound concerns to the Titans, their reaction would be utterly maddening for him.

*   **Godric's Pragmatism:** *"So they build their own guardians. Good. It shows they are learning. It is a sign of a species' maturity."* He would see it as a positive step, a return to the "Golden Age" when humans could defend themselves. He would have zero interest in the petty human politics or Mark's "conspiracy theories."
*   **Mothra's Cautious Optimism:** *"Perhaps it is a way for them to feel secure, Mark. A sense of security may lead to greater understanding, not greater conflict."* She would see the potential for it to be a bridge, a way for humanity to engage with Titan-scale concepts on their own terms.
*   **The Result:** Mark would be completely stonewalled. The beings with the power to actually *do* something about the PPDC see it as a non-issue or even a positive development, leaving him completely isolated with his fears.

**Conclusion: The Lonely Watchman**

Monarch's position becomes incredibly tragic. They are forced to publicly smile and shake hands with an organization they secretly believe to be a puppet of their greatest enemy. They have to watch as this new, popular institution builds weapons based on a philosophy they know is dangerously flawed. And their most powerful allies are completely indifferent to the threat.

Mark Russell is not just the director of Monarch; he is the **lonely watchman on the wall,** desperately trying to warn a sleeping city that the beautiful, impressive gift horse they just wheeled inside is filled with enemy soldiers. His story, in this arc, becomes a tense political thriller about trying to expose a conspiracy that no one else is willing to see.
