### **Fleshed Out Scene: The Prince's Inheritance**

**(Setting: The Nordson's Alpha Wing in the Palace. A quiet afternoon. Leo is sprawled across his bed, expertly flipping a newly forged knife—a birthday gift from Junior—end over end. The door between their rooms, usually ajar, is firmly closed.)**

Junior had been secretive for years about it. Every anniversary of that fateful day he first saw the photograph, he would make a quiet pilgrimage through the Palace's portal room to Castle Bravo. There, under the sworn secrecy of a delighted and co-conspiring Aunt Madison, he would access the secure Monarch archives. His project: to study the visual history of his father, the man known as Godric Nordson from 2021 to 2029. He had memorized every detail: the length of the hair, the clean-shaven jaw, the severe but powerful cut of the suits.

Today was his and Leo's 16th birthday. He was nearly as tall as his father now, and the time felt right. After a long, nerve-wracking hour in his own bathroom with a pair of shears, he took a deep breath and opened the door to Leo's room.

Leo, mid-flip with the knife, froze. He looked up, his green eyes widening in genuine, comical confusion.

"Whoa?! Who are you, sir?"

Junior let out a slow, nervous breath, but a small smile touched his lips. He ran a hand through his newly shorn, pristine white hair. It was short, precisely cut, and swept back, a perfect replica of the style in the archives. His jaw was smooth, the first traces of his adolescent beard completely gone.

"How do I look, Lee?"

Leo sat up, the knife forgotten on his comforter. His eyes darted back and forth, mapping this new face onto the one he'd known his whole life. "You look like... like Daddy, in those old photos." His mind, quick as lightning, connected the dots. "Wait... Did you...?"

"I just copied his style from that one picture Aunt Maddie left—"

"NO!" Leo leapt off the bed, his voice a triumphant shout. "I heard Uncle Barth yelling at Daddy about it! He said you've been using the portals to Castle Bravo every year, on a specific day!"

Junior felt a flush of panic. "*Uh...*"

"You had Aunt Madison help you with this!" Leo declared, pointing an accusatory finger.

"Yeah," Junior sighed, immensely relieved that Leo had landed on the slightly less obsessive-sounding version of the truth. He'd actually compiled a whole portfolio. "Yeah, I did."

Leo circled him, a grin spreading across his face. "You really do look like him. But... you know, skinnier. And with white hair."

"Wow. I'm touched, Lee," Junior said, his voice dripping with sarcasm. "Now, get off my bed. I'm going to—"

"We GOTTA show Daddy!" Leo's excitement was a physical force, an energy that filled the room. "No—we gotta show him *and* Papa! They're gonna be SO confused! This is gonna be hilarious!"

A wave of pure, unadulterated shyness washed over Junior. This had felt like a good idea in the privacy of his bathroom. Now... not so much. "Uh... Maybe later, Lee? It's not a big deal."

"NOPE!" In a blur of motion, Leo's hand shot out, his grip like a vise on Junior's wrist. He took off at a run, dragging his older, now significantly less confident, brother behind him. "NOW!"

"LEONARD AURELIUS NORDSON, LET GO OF ME!"

"No way, bro! This is too good! DAD! PAPA! YOU GOTTA SEE THIS!" Leo's voice boomed through the grand halls of the Alpha wing.

They burst onto the main terrace where Godric and Darius were enjoying a quiet moment, looking out at the sea. Both men turned, their expressions of serene calm shifting instantly. Dagon's icy-blue eyes widened in confusion. Godric's golden eyes went from amused to startled to something soft and unreadable in the span of a single heartbeat.

Junior stood there, mortified, his white hair a perfect copy of his father's old style, his face flushed red. He looked exactly like a teenage version of the formidable, clean-shaven King who had ruled before him.

Darius looked at Godric, then back at Junior, then back at his son. A slow, deeply amused grin spread across his face. "Son," he said to Godric, a rare note of pure mischief in his voice. "I believe you have a visitor. From the past."

Godric didn't laugh. He simply looked at his son, his golden eyes filled with a profound, almost overwhelming mix of pride, nostalgia, and a love so deep it was startling. He saw not just a haircut, but an act of profound honor and devotion. He saw a son who, on his 16th birthday, chose to pay tribute to the man he admired most in the universe.

"It suits you, son," Godric said, his voice thick with an emotion he didn't try to hide. "It suits you perfectly." And in that moment, all of Junior's embarrassment melted away, replaced by a warm, radiant pride. He had made his father proud. That was the only gift he had wanted, after all.
