# Im Considering Making A Different Kind
> **Source:** `Formatted_With_Thoughts - The Antitheriomorphosis AU.md` — Prompt 271

## Your Notes

I'm considering making a different kind of ranking for the Titans that the PPDC have. But first, a more whimsical addition to the lore: Monarch graciously unveiled a consistent power ranking for the Titans. They didn't pull it out of their ass, it's the joke Power Hierarchy of the Titans themselves. 

Public reception of the release was really positive! Lots of kids really liked it and it's been a topic of many social media posts. It's like power-scaling debates, basically, and for the humans of the ATM, it's real life power scaling. The Titans are treated like sports celebrities and video game characters. It's a masterful piece of PR that Monarch uses to battle the anti-Titan sentiment.

Monarch hoped it would illustrate how dangerous it was to confront the Titans back in 2027 to the PPDC. That was its second purpose, but it only took them in the middle of the Kaiju War in 2030 to realize that Battra was ranked 3rd for a reason.

During 2027, Dagon and Biollante haven't really been established yet. Nevertheless, this is the power ranking for the Titans:

1. Godzilla
2. Mothra
3. Battra
4. Scylla*
5. Rodan*
6. Leviathan*
7. Baragon
8. Manda
9. Behemoth

## Analysis

This is a stroke of PR genius from Monarch (specifically Sam Coleman, no doubt) and a fantastic narrative device. By gamifying the Titan hierarchy, Monarch achieves two things:

1.  **Normalization:** They turn terrifying eldritch gods into "sports stars." It makes the Titans feel manageable, familiar, and culturally integrated.
2.  **Misdirection:** It focuses the public's attention on *raw power output* (flashy beams and explosions) rather than the deeper, more complex political and social realities of the Titan world (the Standing Hierarchy, the Vow, the true nature of the Wardens).

It’s meta-commentary on fandom power-scaling debates turned into an in-universe cultural phenomenon. Let’s explore the fallout of **The Monarch Titan Power Index (2027 Edition).**

---

### **I. The Public Face: The "Monsters & Mana" Phenomenon**

The release of the list turns Titanology into pop culture.

*   **Social Media Wars:** Hashtags like `#TeamGodzilla`, `#MothraQueen`, and `#JusticeForRodan` trend globally. Kids argue in schoolyards about whether Scylla’s ice beam beats Rodan’s sonic clap.
*   **The "Big Three" Branding:** Godzilla, Mothra, and Battra are solidified as the untouchable triumvirate.
    *   **Godzilla:** The undisputed G.O.A.T. The "John Cena" or "Michael Jordan" of the Titan world.
    *   **Mothra:** The beloved, benevolent powerhouse. Her #2 spot validates her worshippers and fans who argue she’s not just a "support" class.
    *   **Battra:** The edgy anti-hero. His #3 spot makes him the favorite of the "cool" kids who like the darker aesthetic.
*   **The Betting Pools:** Gambling on the "Mid-Card" (Scylla, Rodan, Leviathan) becomes a massive underground industry. Since their ranks fluctuate, people bet on who will take the #4 spot in the next quarterly update.

### **II. The Internal Fallout: Ego and Apathy**

Inside the Palace and the Outposts, the list is a source of comedy and contention.

*   **Scylla's "Official" Validation:** Scylla pretends not to care, but she absolutely frames a copy of the list in her outpost. It is objective, scientific proof that she is superior to "The Bird."
*   **Rodan's Campaign:** Rodan takes to the internet (anonymously, or so he thinks) to argue that speed and mobility should be weighted higher in the algorithm. He starts performing increasingly flashy feats near Monarch sensors just to try and spike his numbers for the next update.
*   **The Absent Kings (Kong & Anguirus):** Their exclusion is a point of pride for them.
    *   **Kong:** "Let them measure their beams. I ruled a kingdom with my bare hands." It reinforces his identity as the *technical* fighter, the one who wins through skill, not just output.
    *   **Anguirus:** "They can't measure endurance." He knows he can tank hits from anyone on that list (except maybe the top 2), so he’s content to be the unseen backbone.

### **III. Ichi's Indignation: The Dragon's Gym**

This is the best character beat. For Ichi, a being of infinite ego, being *omitted* is worse than being ranked last.

This pettiness becomes the driving force for Arthur's physical training. Ichi pushes him to peak human condition not just for survival, but because he wants to be ready to reclaim his spot on the leaderboard the moment he reveals himself.

### **IV. The PPDC's Blind Spot: The Danger of Data**

The PPDC looks at this list and sees **target prioritization**, not a warning.

*   **Misinterpreting #3:** They see Battra at #3 and think, "Okay, he's high energy, but he's below the Moth and the Lizard. We can handle him."
*   **The Fallacy of "Output":** The list measures *Output* (Energy projection), not *Lethality* (Skill + Hacks).
    *   They don't realize Battra's #3 ranking is based on raw magical discharge, not his ability to *dismantle reality*.
    *   They don't realize that **Kong** (unranked) could likely tear a Jaeger apart with his bare hands because he knows leverage and martial arts.
    *   They don't realize that **Scylla** (#4) isn't just an ice beam; she's a bacterial generator and a political mastermind.

The list reinforces their reliance on "hard data" while blinding them to the qualitative reality of the Titans. It sets them up perfectly for the shock of the Shatterdome incident, where Battra proves that "Power Output" is meaningless if your opponent can just unscrew your bolts with his mind.

This "Power Index" is a fantastic narrative tool. It adds texture to the world, motivates the characters, and deepens the central themes of perception vs. reality.
