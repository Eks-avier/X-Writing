# Now With Battras Action Done We
> **Source:** `Formatted_With_Thoughts - The Antitheriomorphosis AU.md` — Prompt 270

## Your Notes

Now, with Battra's action done. We need a follow-up. How would Monarch react? How would the PPDC feel moments after Battra blinks out of existence?

As much as Mark wanted to scream... this was an opportunity. Any nudge he made to Stacker that alluded to the Shadow Organization was either ignored or mocked. If they're going to him and *beg* for Battra's help, then now is the time to get them on board with the true enemy of humanity.

---

I think the PPDC would have only been further vindicated *in their belief*, and yet realize that they are vastly out of their depth. They understood the Titans were this powerful, but they only accounted for their encounters with the Xilien Invasion. Godric, Maria, Bartholomew, and Gigan II's fights all took place in isolation in New York, separate from the humans. They didn't know that Godzilla was fighting someone of THIS caliber. Primarily, they need to determine what made Battra attack them. 'Cause remember, this was out of the blue. They didn't know that the data on which they built the Drift came from the Shadow Organization. 

Even if they wanted to start a war because of this, so what? What's that supposed to do? And to think that wasn't even *Godzilla*, whom the Jaegers were supposed to fight! 

Finally, a crucial piece of Battra's words rings out to them: "equally insignificant ants." Thus, this means that even if Monarch is close to the Titans, the Titans would not hesitate to dispatch them if they stayed out of the lone. Although even those at Monarch, Mark himself, already knew of this long ago. This is a wake-up call: *The Titans are only interacting with humanity out of good grace*.

Finally, it established the true stance of the Titans. They are *wildcards*. Monarch, through Mark and Madison who have befriended its "top brass" (Godzilla, Mothra, Kong, etc.) are only in their good graces. If they weren't so preoccupied right now, they'd be handling this issue. As it stands, the ball is on humanity's court.

## Analysis

This is the pivot point. You are absolutely right: Battra's intervention changes the game from a "Cold War" to a "Hostile Negotiation."

The PPDC is vindicated in their fear (*"See? They are monsters who can turn on us at any moment!"*) but utterly humiliated in their methodology (*"Our weapons are useless against them."*).

Here is how the aftermath plays out on both sides of the line, setting up Mark's strategic victory and the uneasy alliance.

---

### **Scene 1: The Red Phone Rings (Monarch's Perspective)**

**(Setting: Mark Russell’s Office. Minutes after Miki has left. The atmosphere is heavy with the secret of the stolen data.)**

Mark was staring at the folder containing the proof of Miki’s torture when the secure line on his desk—the one reserved for the UN Security Council and, theoretically, the PPDC—lit up.

He didn't answer it immediately. He let it ring twice. He took a breath, compartmentalizing his rage at the Shadow Organization and his grief for the students. Right now, he couldn't be the avenger; he had to be the Director.

He picked up the receiver. "Russell."

*"Director."* Stacker Pentecost’s voice was tight, strained, but holding onto that ironclad dignity. *"We have... a situation."*

Mark closed his eyes. "I know, Stacker. Our sensors picked up a massive localized spike in Primus energy over Hong Kong. Is the Shatterdome still standing?"

*"The facility is intact,"* Pentecost said, the hesitation speaking volumes. *"The asset... is not."*

Mark leaned forward. This was it. The door was open.

"Let me guess," Mark said, keeping his voice level. "Purple light? Telekinetic disassembly? A lecture on the inferiority of your design?"

*"...Yes."*

"That was Battra. The Lord of the Mystic Arts. And if you're alive to make this call, Marshal, it means he was in a good mood."

*"A good mood?"* Pentecost sounded incredulous. *"He took Gipsy Danger apart bolt by bolt. He put... collars... on my command staff."*

"He didn't kill you," Mark corrected sharply. "And he didn't erase the Shatterdome from the map. Which is what the *other* guy would have done if he knew what I think you know."

Silence on the line. Mark let the implication hang there. He wasn't accusing Stacker of the theft yet—he knew Stacker likely didn't know the origin of the Drift data—but he was planting the seed that the Titans knew, and they were unhappy.

"What do you need, Stacker?"

*"He mentioned... consultations,"* Pentecost said, the words clearly tasting like bile. *"He said we needed to speak to you. About the Sorcery Division."*

Mark looked at the folder on his desk. Miki had said to wait. To let them play with their toys until the war was won. But to win the war, those toys needed to not fall apart when a Titan looked at them funny.

"I can send a team," Mark said. "But Stacker... the price of admission just went up. Complete transparency. No more black-boxed data. If my people are going to fix your machines, we need to know exactly how they work. Including the Drift."

A long pause.

*"Done,"* Pentecost said. *"Send them."*

### **Scene 2: The Vindication of Fear (PPDC's Perspective)**

**(Setting: The Shatterdome Floor. The wreckage of Gipsy Danger lies in a heap. Newt Geiszler is scanning a piece of debris with a tricorder-like device, his hands shaking.)**

"The residual energy readings are off the charts," Newt gibbered, looking fast and frantic. "It's not radiation. It's not electromagnetic. It's... it's organized. It's linguistic physics. He rewrote the local laws of gravity for specific screws, Hermann! Specific screws!"

Hermann Gottlieb was leaning against a support strut, looking pale. "It defies the laws of thermodynamics. The energy required to dismantle this mass without heat generation... it should have vaporized the city."

Marshal Pentecost walked down the gantry, his face grim. Mako Mori walked a step behind him.

"Sir," Mako said quietly. "Does this not prove we were right?"

Pentecost stopped. He looked at the wreckage. "How so, Mako?"

"The Titans. They are not allies. They are arbitrary gods. Today, he destroyed a machine. Tomorrow, he could decide Hong Kong is 'clutter' and remove it." She gestured to the heap. "Monarch claims they are human now. That they have empathy. But that..." She pointed to where Battra had floated. "That was not human. That was an insect looking at an ant hill."

"You heard what he called us," Raleigh Becket chimed in, rubbing his neck where the phantom collar still tingled. "'Insignificant ants.' Even Monarch. He called them a 'merry band of ants.' He doesn't work *for* them."

"No," Pentecost said, a cold realization settling in his gut. "He allows them to exist."

The "Trained Animal" theory died in that room. They realized something far worse: The Titans weren't trained animals. Monarch wasn't the handler. Monarch was just the pet that the tiger liked best.

In the shadows of the hangar, a man in a nondescript grey suit—the **Mysterious Consultant**—watched the proceedings. He didn't look terrified. He looked calculating. He tapped a message into a secure pad: *Subject 'Battra' hostile to collection. Magical countermeasures required. Accelerate Phase 2.*

"Monarch is sending a team," Pentecost announced to the room. "The 'Sorcery Division.' Whatever they are, they are going to ward our ships against... *that*."

"We're letting them in?" Newt asked, excited despite himself. "We get to see the wizard school kids?"

"We are letting them fix the armor," Pentecost said, his eyes hard. "And while they do, we will be watching them. If magic is real... then we need to know how to kill it."

### **Analysis: The New Dynamic**

This setup accomplishes exactly what you need:

1.  **The Uneasy Alliance:** They are working together, but the trust is at absolute zero. Monarch is there to ensure the Jaegers work (and to secretly investigate the Shadow Org). The PPDC is there to get their weapons fixed and to spy on Monarch's capabilities.
2.  **The Shift in PPDC Ideology:** They have moved from "Monarch are dupes controlling animals" to "Monarch are collaborators with eldritch gods." It validates their fear. They now know that humanity is woefully outgunned, not just physically, but metaphysically.
3.  **Mark's Gambit:** Mark has successfully used the crisis to get his foot in the door. By demanding transparency on the Drift, he begins the investigation that will eventually uncover the truth about the Psionic students' brains, setting up the mid-war revelation.
4.  **The Shadow Org's Move:** The Consultant seeing this not as a defeat, but as data, reinforces their threat. They are adapting. If Battra is the threat, they will find a way to counter magic. Perhaps this leads them to seek out the **Nebulans** or **Exif** technology later?

This creates a pressure cooker environment for the students of Keep Charlie when they arrive. They are walking into the lion's den to fix the lions' teeth.
