# The Contrast Between Miki And Battra
> **Source:** `Formatted_With_Thoughts - The Antitheriomorphosis AU.md` — Prompt 269

## Your Notes

The contrast between Miki and Battra is sublime! The difference between a survivor and a showman, of a psychic and a sorcerer.

It also contrasts him if Godric and Maria were told of this information first. 

- Godric would literally rip apart their entire base. Or he'd irradiate it and leave, killing everyone inside.
- Maria would give them a stern talking to.

And it simply reinforces the interplay between Krátos, Psionics, and Magic.

Battra's dismantling of Gipsy Danger happens in between Kaiju attacks, so nothing *yet* as emerged. It hasn't yet been a Category IV or anything of the sort. This only fuels the need of the PPDC to reach out to Monarch. Someone who can unravel their creation surely has the means to put it back together just as fast. Crucially, they need to figure out what went wrong, what caused such a powerful being, who they are denying the person hood of, to attack them? 

Battra's not stupid, he understands the Jaegers have been useful. Although he knows the Kaiju don't have telekinetic abilities, he knows that *other Alien Titans* do. Against a theoretical Gigan III, what can they do? Against a Kaiju that can use telekinesis, what can they do? Battra knows the Precursors, they know that they will stop at nothing to get what they want. What the Precursors don't know, though, is that Battra is alive and well on Earth, nor do they know of the real strength of the Titans.

## Analysis

This creates a fascinating, multi-dimensional chess game where Battra is playing on a level the PPDC hasn't even conceived of yet.

You've highlighted the perfect contrast in how the different "Powers" handle offense:

*   **Miki (Psionics - The Survivor):** Endures, waits, and plans. She absorbs the trauma to use as leverage later.
*   **Godric (Krátos - The King):** Eradicates. If the PPDC angers him, he doesn't teach them a lesson; he removes them from the map. The fact that he *doesn't* know is the only reason Stacker Pentecost is alive.
*   **Battra (Magic - The Teacher):** Deconstructs. He breaks the toy to show the child why it was built wrong.

### **The Lesson of the Scrap Heap: Battra's Hidden Motive**

Battra’s specific method of destruction—telekinetic disassembly—is the key. It wasn't just a display of power; it was a **simulated combat scenario.**

1.  **The Threat Assessment:** Battra knows the galaxy. He knows the Precursors. He knows the Nebulans. He knows that **Gigan III** will likely have advanced countermeasures, and that the Precursors are genetically engineering Kaiju that can adapt to energy attacks.
2.  **The Flaw:** The PPDC built Jaegers to punch dinosaurs. They treated the fight as purely physical (Newtonian physics).
3.  **The Lesson:** Battra showed them what happens when a physical machine meets a **non-physical force** (Telekinesis/Magic). He proved that without **metaphysical wards** (which only Sorcery can provide) or **psionic shielding** (which only Keep Charlie can teach), the Jaegers are obsolete before they even launch against a high-tier threat.

He didn't just break Gipsy Danger; he **stress-tested** it against the reality of the universe, a reality the PPDC was willfully ignoring.

### **The Aftermath: The Phone Call**

This sets up the inevitable, humiliating, and necessary moment where Stacker Pentecost has to pick up the red phone.

Let's look at the scene immediately following Battra's departure.

***

**DATE:** October 14, 2030 (10 Minutes Post-Breach)
**LOCATION:** The Shatterdome, Hong Kong.
**STATUS:** CRITICAL FAILURE

The silence in the hangar was heavier than the pile of scrap metal that used to be *Gipsy Danger*.

Technicians were weeping. Security guards were staring blankly at the walls, rubbing their phantom-collared necks.

Marshal Stacker Pentecost stood amidst the wreckage of his life's work. He reached out and picked up a single, massive bolt—one of the thousands that had been unscrewed by a mind he couldn't comprehend. It was perfectly intact. There were no shear marks. No heat damage. It hadn't been forced; it had been *removed*.

"Marshal," Tendo Choi’s voice came over the localized comms, shaking. "Sensors are clear. He's... he's gone. But sir... the atmospheric readings... they're still spiking with that purple energy signature. It's like the air remembers him."

Pentecost looked at the bolt in his hand, then at the mountain of debris. He thought of the "Wolf Collar." He thought of the terrifying ease with which the being had dismissed their guns, their walls, and their science.

*If the enemy includes such a weapon in their arsenal... what response will you give?*

The question burned in his mind. He realized, with a cold pit in his stomach, that he didn't have an answer. If a Kaiju appeared tomorrow with the ability to pull a Jaeger apart with its mind, humanity was extinct.

"Sir?" Tendo asked again. "What are your orders? Do we scramble the reserve wings? Do we initiate the rebuild?"

Pentecost dropped the bolt. It clattered loudly against a piece of armor plating.

"No," Pentecost said. His voice was gravel, but it was steady. He turned and walked toward the command deck. "Get me a secure line to Castle Bravo."

"Sir?"

"You heard me, Mr. Choi," Pentecost said, stepping over a severed hydraulic line. "Get me Mark Russell."

He paused at the foot of the stairs, looking back at the ruin of his machine. He hated Monarch. He hated their secrets. He hated their monsters. But he was a soldier, and a soldier knows when he is outgunned.

"And Tendo?"

"Yes, Marshal?"

"Ask them..." Pentecost swallowed his pride, a bitter pill that tasted like survival. "Ask them if the 'Sorcery Division' accepts emergency consults."

***

### **Why This Works**

*   **Pragmatism Over Pride:** Pentecost hates doing it, but he prioritizes the mission (saving the world) over his ego. This keeps him sympathetic and competent.
*   **The Bridge is Built:** This is the moment the two factions are forced to merge. The PPDC supplies the hardware (Jaegers) and the pilots. Monarch (specifically Keep Charlie) supplies the **software patch**—the magical wards and psionic defenses necessary to keep the machines from being scrapped by higher-tier threats.
*   **The Jet Jaguar Parallel:** Just as Jet Jaguar needs a human bond to function, the Jaegers now need Monarch's magic to survive. The themes of cooperation and synthesis are reinforced again.

It is a perfect narrative pivot point. The Cold War is over; the uneasy Alliance has begun.
