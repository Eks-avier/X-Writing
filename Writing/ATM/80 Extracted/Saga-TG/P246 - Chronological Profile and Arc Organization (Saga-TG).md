# Chronological Profile and Arc Organization

> **Source:** `Formatted_With_Thoughts - The Saga of the _Titanus gojira_.md` — Prompt 246

## Your Notes

I think there are some details that are dependent on the timeline of the story. Like, for example, Godric's relationship with Maria. They only started officially dating after Meadowvale. Yet, during that mission, Maria was technically his *pretend* wife. Then, after 2029, they officially married. There are other details like this woven into his character, which is why I wanted a sort of chronological profile rewrite.

I'm thinking it's time to section of the overarching narrative. I believe our development of Godzilla as a character has shone a light on a crucial part of building this project: organization. It's time to organize the arcs. This helps not just this character, but every character in this AU, as well as myself.

But before this, let's have a definitive list of ALL the arcs we have currently discussed.

## Analysis

This is the smartest way to proceed. Organizing the narrative into defined eras and arcs provides the scaffolding we need. It allows us to track the evolution of characters, relationships, and powers without getting lost in the sheer density of the lore.

Here is the **Definitive Chronological Arc List of the Antitheriomorphosis Universe**, organized by timeline and major narrative focus.

### Era I: The Primordial & Pre-Transformation (Pre-2020)
*The foundational history that shapes the present.*

*   **Arc 0: The Saga of the Fallen Star (Ancient Pre-History)**
    *   **Events:** The crash of the Blue Star; the Ascension of the *Titanus gojira*; the founding of the Kingdom of Kong; the Bio-Cultural War waged by Mother Prime; the tragic romance of Dagon and Astraea (Aurelia); Dagon's sacrifice; the Exile of Battra.
    *   **Godzilla's State:** A hatchling/juvenile, orphaned and traumatized, developing his "survival" *Horme*.

*   **Arc 0.5: The Modern Titan Wars (2014 - 2019)**
    *   **Events:** The re-emergence of the MUTOs (2014); the awakening of Ghidorah and the Mass Awakening (2019); Mothra's sacrifice; Godzilla's first (assisted) Burning Form; the crowning of the Alpha Paramount.
    *   **Godzilla's State:** The Weary Warden. His fighting spirit is doused by the grim reality of Ghidorah.

### Era II: The Awakening & Adaptation (2020 - 2022)
*The immediate aftermath of the humanization and the struggle to adjust.*

*   **Arc 1: The Antitheriomorphosis (Early 2020)**
    *   **Events:** The global transformation event.
    *   **Godzilla's State:** **First Repose.** Catastrophic misalignment due to biological shock. Powers are dormant.
    *   **Key Narrative:** The **Fishing Family Arc**. Godric washes ashore in Chiba, meets the Yamamotos, learns Japanese, and rediscovers the joy of learning. He breaks Repose to protect them.

*   **Arc 2: The Gathering & The Outposts (Mid 2020 - 2021)**
    *   **Events:** Rodan found in Fuji; Anguirus discovered; Mothra returns/reincarnates on Monster Island (2021). The Wardens settle into their outposts.
    *   **Godzilla's State:** **The Adjusting Human.** He moves to Castle Bravo. The "Southern Drawl" incident. He begins his physical training to overcome his clumsiness.
    *   **Key Narrative:** The awkward reunion of the Castle Bravo Four.

*   **Arc 3: The Duel of Kings (2022)**
    *   **Events:** Skull Island collapses. Godric seeks the Kong survivors and fights Kong.
    *   **Godzilla's State:** **The Contamination.** He enters the fight with his "Refined Titan" style, is forced to adapt to Kong's martial arts, and leaves with a "contaminated" hybrid style.
    *   **Key Narrative:** Kong is named Alpha Sovereign. Anguirus feels replaced. Godric begins the psychological process of grieving Dagon.

### Era III: The War for Earth (2023 - 2024)
*The first major threat to the new world order.*

*   **Arc 4: The Training Interlude (2022 - 2023)**
    *   **Events:** Godric trains with Kong to refine his contaminated style.
    *   **Godzilla's State:** Developing **Atomic Amplification.** Moving from self-loathing to self-acceptance.

*   **Arc 5: The Xilien Invasion (2023 - 2024)**
    *   **Events:** Xiliens invade. Battra returns as an antagonist with Gigan. The Wardens defend the globe.
    *   **Godzilla's State:** **The Uncaged Sun.** He fights Battra, enters "The Zone," and achieves the **Burning Form** solo for the first time.
    *   **Key Narrative:** Battra is defeated and "leashed." Mothra kills Gigan. Humanity (Monarch/Psionics) proves their worth.

### Era IV: The Keystone & Domestic Era (2025 - 2026)
*The golden age of relationship building and "slice of life" with high stakes.*

*   **Arc 6: The Keystone Arc (Early 2025)**
    *   **Events:** Construction of the Monster Island Palace. Battra reintegrates. Keep Charlie is founded. Anguirus and Kong resolve their tension.
    *   **Godzilla's State:** **The Innovator.** He is refining his arsenal (Atomic Railgun).
    *   **Key Narrative:** The formalization of the new Titan governance structure.

*   **Arc 7: Blue House at Maple Street (Mid 2025 - 2026)**
    *   **Events:** Godric and Maria go undercover in Meadowvale. They meet Arthur Calloway (host of Ichi).
    *   **Godzilla's State:** **The Lover.** He develops the **Atomic Stride** and **Atomic Mantle**. His relationship with Maria shifts from "partners" to "in love."
    *   **Key Narrative:** The New York Incident (Arthur ingests the fragment). Godric and Ichi make their Vow. Godric and Maria return and become "boyfriend and girlfriend."

### Era V: The Legacy Era (2026 - 2029)
*The expansion of the family and the healing of the past.*

*   **Arc 8: The Resurrection (Late 2026)**
    *   **Events:** The Bilusaludo steal Dagon's bones (Project Kiryu). Dagon is resurrected in human form.
    *   **Godzilla's State:** **The Son.** He finally heals his abandonment trauma.
    *   **Key Narrative:** Father and son reunited.

*   **Arc 9: The Rose of the North (2027)**
    *   **Events:** The creation of Biollante (Erika Rose Nordson).
    *   **Godzilla's State:** **The Brother.** He accepts a new sibling and expands the family unit.
    *   **Key Narrative:** Rose's integration into the family; her relationship with Rodan begins.

*   **Arc 10: The Road to Marriage (2028 - 2029)**
    *   **Events:** Godric proposes (Nov 2028). Rodan confesses to Godric. Mothra and Battra fully reconcile.
    *   **Godzilla's State:** **Second Repose.** Upon learning of Maria's pregnancy, his *Horme* shifts from "Survival" to "Family," causing a power drop.
    *   **Key Narrative:** The Wedding (April 2029). The Birth of Junior and Leo (Dec 2029).

### Era VI: The Convergence / Pacific Rim Era (2030)
*The global crisis where the parents must trust the world to survive without them.*

*   **Arc 11: The Breach (Surface)**
    *   **Events:** Precursors invade with Kaiju. PPDC and Jaegers deploy. Psionic Division and Sorcery Division mobilize.
    *   **Godzilla's State:** **The Present Father.** He refuses to leave his wife and newborns. He trusts his allies to handle the threat.

*   **Arc 12: The Hollow Throne (Below)**
    *   **Events:** Skar King and Shimo rise. Kong defends his kingdom alone.
    *   **Key Narrative:** Kong's ultimate victory and validation as Sovereign.

*   **Arc 13: The Red Planet (Space)**
    *   **Events:** Ichi and Arthur hunt the Precursors on the Moon and Mars.
    *   **Key Narrative:** Ichi terraforms Mars and completes his redemption.

### Era VII: The Next Generation (2030 - Future)
*The story of the children.*

*   **Arc 14: The Growing Years**
    *   **Events:** Lora is born. The children manifest powers (Scales at 7, Atomic Breath at 10-12).
    *   **Key Narrative:** The "family pile," the smithy, the training.

*   **Arc 15: The Star-Crossed Pilgrimage (Approx. 2047)**
    *   **Events:** Godric is poisoned by his own energy. The siblings travel to the parallel Earth.
    *   **Key Narrative:** Junior's tragic romance. Leo's glimpse of the Rainbow. Lora's sacrifice of immortality for Magic. Godric is saved.

This list covers every major beat we have discussed. It provides a perfect chronological map to organize the profiles and the wiki.
