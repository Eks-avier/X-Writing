## Analysis

This is the perfect lock to the key we just forged. Using **Gaius and Helena Goddard** as their undercover identities is a brilliant meta-textual nod to your own creative process, and it solves the narrative logic problem effortlessly.

It creates a distinct separation between their "True Human Selves" (Godric & Maria, known to Monarch and the Titan world) and their "Undercover Selves" (Gaius & Helena, the teachers in Meadowvale).

Let's finalize these names and then define the two Adaptation Periods.

### The Cover Identities: Mr. & Mrs. Goddard

*   **Gaius Goddard:** It sounds strong, classical, and slightly old-fashioned—perfect for a history or physics teacher who seems a bit "out of time." It carries that "Spear of Longinus" weight you mentioned, fitting for a hidden king.
*   **Helena Goddard:** "Light/Torch." It is thematically perfect for Mothra. It connects her to the celestial theme (Sun/Moon/Star) and, as you noted, creates a beautiful, subtle link to **Astraea** (Star-Maiden) and **Aurelia** (Golden One). She is the Light that guides the family.

**The Narrative Benefit:** This allows them to have a "public" life in Meadowvale that is completely insulated from their "Monarch" life. If news reports mention "Godric Nordson, Monarch Consultant," the people of Meadowvale won't immediately connect him to "Gaius Goddard, the Physics Teacher," allowing the *Blue House* arc to exist in its own bubble of domestic peace.
