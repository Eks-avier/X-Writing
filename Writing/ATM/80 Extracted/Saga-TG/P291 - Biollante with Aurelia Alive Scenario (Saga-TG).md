# Biollante with Aurelia Alive Scenario
> **Source:** `Formatted_With_Thoughts - The Saga of the _Titanus gojira_.md` — Prompt 291

## Your Notes

I wonder what changes with the Biollante event with Aurelia alive instead of Darius, and that is: Biollante is still Dagon's clone.

Dr. Genichiro would not retrieve genetic material from the remains of Kiryu. In this scenario, Monarch prevented any sort of access to the site. Instead, on the black market tracing from the Philippines, Dr. Genichiro would receive a sample from Dagon's bones long before access to that skeleton was locked down as Dagon's remains would be moved to Monster Island.

With preparations in place, Erika Shiragami's DNA is spliced with that of a rose's and... Dagon's.

I can imagine Aurelia being furious that her husband's memory would be tarnished like this, but I think she can at least empathize with why Dr. Shiragami wanted to do this, insane as it is. She understands the desperation of a parent better than anyone else.

And, with Genichiro dead at the end, Erika as Biollante would be alone.... an orphan. Naturally, Aurelia would have a soft spot for them, knowing her husband's past. Godric still gets a sister in Rose, though, and their relationship is much the same as the canon timeline. But, the relationship between Aurelia and Rose is different.

With Darius, at least he can sort of vibe his way with Junior, Leo, and Lora's dynamic. He can manage the external world. With Aurelia, she deals with the internal world, and I think that's beautiful.

## Analysis

This is a devastatingly poetic twist on the Biollante dynamic. It transforms the relationship from a "Father/Daughter" bond into something far more complex and spiritual: **The Widow and the Echo.**

If Aurelia is the one who survives, her relationship with Rose becomes the emotional anchor of the family, and it fits perfectly with the Northern Gojira tradition of the female managing the **Internal Domain.**

### I. The Widow and the Echo

The key emotional hook: **Rose is the last living piece of Dagon that Aurelia can interact with.**

*   **The Physical Absence:** Unlike Darius, who got to resurrect and reconnect, Aurelia never got to say goodbye.
*   **The Genetic Miracle:** When she looks at Rose, she doesn't just see a hybrid monster. She sees **Dagon's cells, alive, breathing, thinking.** Rose's very existence is proof that some part of him endures.
*   **The Complicated Grief:** Aurelia's relationship with Rose would be more intense and complicated than Darius's. She might initially struggle to separate the "daughter" from the "relic." Is she nurturing Rose, or is she mourning Dagon through Rose?

### II. The Empathy of the Desperate Parent

Aurelia's reaction to Dr. Shiragami's crime is nuanced in a way Dagon's wasn't.

*   **The Fury:** Her first instinct is rage. He violated her husband's remains. He desecrated the body of the only man she ever loved.
*   **The Mirror:** But then she remembers what *she* did. She collapsed a mountain on herself to give her son a chance. How is that any different from a father breaking the laws of nature to save his daughter?
*   **The Verdict:** Aurelia cannot condemn Shiragami without condemning herself. Her final judgment would likely be: *"He was a fool. He was an arrogant, desperate fool. And I understand him perfectly."*

### III. The Internal Domain: Aurelia and Rose

Where Darius manages the "external world" (teaching the kids to fight, wrestling with Leo, being the fun grandpa), Aurelia would manage the "internal world" (emotional healing, therapy, guiding the heart).

*   **Rose's Human Side:** Rose still carries the memories and emotions of Erika Shiragami. She has human anxieties, human insecurities, and human grief for her father. Aurelia, the Queen of the Internal Domain, is the perfect person to help her process this.
    *   *Aurelia:* "You feel torn between two selves. The human girl who died. The Titan bloom who was born. I understand this better than you know."
    *   *Rose:* "How?"
    *   *Aurelia:* "Because I, too, am a woman who died and was reborn. Astraea collapsed. Aurelia emerged. The body changed. The memories remained. You are not a monster wearing a girl's skin. You are a new kind of being, and that is a blessing, not a curse."

*   **The Flower and the Florist:** Aurelia, with her "Internal Domain," would likely help Rose cultivate her powers—not for combat, but for beauty. They would spend hours in a greenhouse, growing impossible plants, using Rose's hybrid biology to create things that shouldn't exist.

### IV. The Role Reversal

In the "Darius Timeline," he is the protector and Rose is the protected. In the "Aurelia Timeline," Rose might become **Aurelia's protector.**

*   **The Frail Queen:** Aurelia cannot regenerate well. Her heart condition limits her. Rose, with her overwhelming Titan vitality, could become her mother's shield.
*   **The Dynamic:** Rose would accompany Aurelia everywhere, not as a daughter, but as a living suit of armor. She would be fiercely protective, channeling her confusion about her own identity into a singular purpose: **"I will keep her safe. I am all that's left of him. I won't let her be alone again."**

This creates a beautiful, melancholic bond: the Widow who lost her love and the Echo who carries his cells. Together, they are the Mourners of Dagon.
