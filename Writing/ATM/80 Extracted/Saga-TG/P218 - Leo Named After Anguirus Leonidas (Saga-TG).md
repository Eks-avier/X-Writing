### **Leonard "Leo" Aurelius Nordson: A Name Forged in Loyalty**

This single connection elevates Leo's name from a simple reference into a powerful, multi-generational statement of love, respect, and enduring brotherhood.

#### **1. The First Layer: A Father's Tribute**

The most immediate and powerful meaning is that Godric named his son in honor of his most steadfast and loyal friend.

*   **A Lion's Heart:** "Leonidas" is the name of a king, a warrior, a figure of legendary courage and self-sacrifice. For Godric to bestow a version of this name ("Leo") upon his own son is the **highest possible honor** he could pay to Anguirus.
*   **A Brother in All But Blood:** It is a public and permanent declaration of Alexios's status. He is not just a subordinate or an ally; he is **family.** Godric sees him as a brother, and by naming his son "Leo," he is making him an uncle in the truest sense of the word.
*   **The Unspoken Apology:** On a deeper level, it can also be seen as Godric's quiet, almost unconscious way of trying to mend the rift that had grown between them since Kong's arrival. While Godric might not fully comprehend the depth of Alexios's insecurity, this act is a profound reaffirmation of Anguirus's irreplaceable place in his life. He is saying, "No matter who else enters my life, you are so important to me that I have written your name into the future of my lineage."

#### **2. The Second Layer: A Grandfather's Echo**

The name "Aurelius" is an equally perfect choice, creating a beautiful connection to the other side of the family.

*   **The Golden One:** "Aurelius" means "the golden one." This is a direct and beautiful reference to the rare, golden eyes that are a signature of Godric's maternal line—the eyes of his lost mother, his own true eyes, and the eyes of his firstborn son, Junior.
*   **A Bridge Between Brothers:** It subtly links Leo back to Junior. While Leo may have inherited his mother's hair and mysterious green eyes, his middle name is a constant reminder of the "golden" heritage he shares with his twin.

#### **3. The Third Layer: Leo's Own Destiny**

And finally, the name connects to Leo's own character and destiny.

*   **"Leo" the Lion:** A lion is a symbol of courage, strength, and wild, untamed power. This is a perfect fit for Leo's "berserker" nature and his immense physical prowess.
*   **The Heisei Legacy:** It still retains the meta-reference to Mothra Leo, but now that reference is grounded in a deep, in-universe emotional truth.
