### **The Unified Field Doctrine: Electrogravitational Warfare**

As you said, for Ichi, electromagnetism and gravity are two sides of the same Kratonic coin. This allows him to apply his "tagging" system with a terrifying, multi-layered versatility that is completely unpredictable. Once an object or person is "tagged," they are not just electromagnetically charged; they are now a **node in his personal gravitational grid.**

#### **I. Offensive Synergy: The Art of the Inescapable Blow**

His ability to combine both forces on a single target makes his attacks utterly devastating.

*   **The Gravitational Slingshot:** Your example is perfect. This is his signature one-shot finisher. It's a multi-stage symphony of physics abuse:
    1.  **Tag:** A low-energy electromagnetic tag is applied to the opponent.
    2.  **Reinforce:** EGO Fortifies Arthur's arm to withstand the coming forces.
    3.  **Attract:** Ichi gives Arthur's electrogravitational knuckles an opposite charge, pulling the opponent in.
    4.  **Accelerate:** Simultaneously, he increases the gravitational pull *on the opponent,* multiplying their acceleration toward the fist.
    5.  **Amplify:** As the punch is thrown, he increases the gravitational mass of the knuckles themselves, turning a simple punch into an impact with the force of a falling meteorite.
    *   **The Inversion:** And as you said, he can reverse the entire methodology. He can make Arthur's fist a gravity well that pulls the enemy in, using electromagnetism simply to guide them. The end result is the same; the physics are completely different. An opponent who figures out how to counter one method is completely unprepared for the other.

#### **II. Crowd Control Synergy: The Puppet Master's Grid**

This is where his battlefield control becomes absolute.

*   **The Pin and Isolate:** He tags multiple opponents, applies a repulsive electromagnetic charge to keep them scattered, and then simultaneously applies a high-gravity field on all of them, pinning them to the ground. They are conscious, helpless, and forced to watch as he deals with their isolated leader.
*   **The Wall Magnet:** A truly brilliant and terrifying tactic. He can tag a large metal surface (a wall, a truck) and magnetize it. Then, he can tag multiple enemies (especially if they have metal armor or weapons) and simply pull them to the wall, sticking them there like helpless fridge magnets while he deals with the primary threat.
*   **The Gravity Well Trap:** His most insidious area-denial technique. He can tag a random object on the battlefield—a piece of rubble, a car—and turn it into a temporary, high-powered gravity well. Any other tagged enemies in the vicinity will be violently pulled and slammed into that object, effectively taking themselves out of the fight.

#### **III. Z-Axis Warfare: The Unfair Advantage**

His ability to fly is a given, but his ability to make *others* fly is what makes him a master of three-dimensional combat.

*   **Forced Aerial Combat:** An opponent is a master of grounded martial arts? No problem. Ichi can tag them and simply make them weightless, sending them floating helplessly into the air where their skills are useless. The battle is now fought in *his* preferred domain: the open sky.
*   **The Human Missile:** In a team fight, he could tag a strong but non-mobile ally (perhaps a future human character) and use his gravity control to launch them at an enemy like a living cannonball.
*   **Environmental Havoc:** He can tag every piece of loose debris in an area—cars, rocks, lampposts—and make them all float, creating a chaotic, zero-gravity kill box where only he understands the rules of movement.

**Conclusion:**

This is a complete and utterly terrifying combat doctrine. He is a true master of the **"electric universe,"** a being whose understanding of electromagnetism and gravity is so profound that he wields them as a single, unified, and inescapable weapon.

The synergy is perfect.
*   **Electromagnetism** provides the **targeting system** (the tags, attraction/repulsion).
*   **Gravity** provides the **overwhelming force** (mass amplification, acceleration).

This reinforces the core distinction between him and Godzilla. Godzilla is the Sculptor, perfecting the one, ultimate weapon: himself. Ichi is the Painter, using his vast and complex palette of physical laws to turn the entire battlefield into his personal, destructive masterpiece. It's a fantastic and deeply compelling contrast.
