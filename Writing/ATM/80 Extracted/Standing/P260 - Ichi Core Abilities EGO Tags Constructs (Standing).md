### **The Arsenal of the Fallen King: Ichi-in-Arthur's `Techni`**

Ichi's combat doctrine within Arthur's body is not a reflection of his ancient, overwhelming power, but a testament to his forced adaptation and cruel ingenuity. It is a system built on layers of control, externalization, and the meticulous manipulation of his opponents' physical state. All of his active combat abilities are powered by his Kratonic Manifestation of **Electromagnetism and Gravity.**

#### **I. The Core Engine: Electrogravitational Overclock (EGO)**

This is the foundational state, the "power on" switch for Ichi's entire arsenal. It is his imperfect, self-damaging analogue to Atomic Amplification.

*   **Function:** Applies **Fortification** throughout Arthur's body to reinforce it against the strain of Ichi's power. Any physical attacks made in this state are automatically imbued with **Penetration.**
*   **Critical Limitation:** It is an incomplete imitation. It **lacks an internal Neutralization component,** leaving Arthur's internal systems vulnerable to backlash and strain. Mastering the uniform distribution needed for Neutralization is a complex hurdle that Ichi, a master of *external* manipulation, has yet to overcome. He envies Godric's mastery of this balanced, holistic state.

#### **II. The Central Tactic: The "Tagging" System**

Since his Aura is severely diminished, Tagging is the cornerstone of his combat philosophy. It is his method of projecting his will and extending his control across the battlefield.

*   **Mechanism:** Any direct application of Ichi's Kratonic power—be it an EGO-powered punch, a Graviton effect, an EM jolt, or an Injection—leaves a residual "Tag" of his will on the target. Low-energy jolts are simply the most efficient and safest method for application.
*   **The Debuff:** A Tag is a multi-purpose debuff. A tagged opponent becomes a node in Ichi's personal electrogravitational grid, subject to his manipulation.
    *   **Direct Manipulation:** Tagged enemies can be pinned by gravity, shocked by electricity, or subjected to magnetic attraction/repulsion.
    *   **Proxy Kratonics:** A tagged enemy can be affected by Kratonic techniques like **Submission** from a distance, without needing to be in his immediate Aura.
*   **The Chaining Property:** Tags enable his arcing and chaining attacks. An attack on one tagged target can be made to arc to all other tagged targets. A single tagged target can even spread the tag to nearby allies.
*   **Durability and Counterplay:**
    *   **Proximity:** Tags last longer the closer they are to Ichi and are permanent within his immediate Aura.
    *   **Willpower Contest:** Tags are a contest of wills. A weaker Kraton will be unable to remove the Tag. An equal can fight it off over time. A superior Kraton (like Godric) can purge it instantly. This makes the Tagging system a perfect litmus test for the Kratonic power hierarchy.

#### **III. The Primary Interface: Energy Constructs**

Because Arthur's body is an inefficient and fragile conduit, energy constructs are Ichi's primary and safest method for engaging in combat. They are the "mittens" that protect the vessel from the power of the god.

*   **Mechanism:** EGO is activated, but instead of the power being contained within Arthur, it is channeled *through* him and into a stable, external form (a spear, a knuckle, a shield). The construct becomes the primary **conduit and capacitor** for Ichi's power, drastically reducing the strain on Arthur's body.
*   **The Symbiotic Limitation:** The constructs must remain in physical contact with Arthur's body. This is a reflection of their imperfect **Harmonia** (Will Synergy). They are not yet in sync enough for Ichi to create a fully independent, self-sustaining object. Disarming them is a critical vulnerability.
*   **The Tactical Flow:** The combat flow is therefore logical and layered:
    1.  Activate **EGO** to power the system.
    2.  Manifest a **Construct** to safely channel the power.
    3.  Use the Construct to apply a **Tag.**
    4.  Use the Tag to control the battlefield and create an opening for a finishing blow.

This is a complete, well-defined, and strategically rich skill set. It gives Ichi-in-Arthur a unique identity as a fighter—not a brute-force brawler, but a meticulous, tactical "combat engineer" who dismantles his opponents by rewriting the physical laws that govern them.
