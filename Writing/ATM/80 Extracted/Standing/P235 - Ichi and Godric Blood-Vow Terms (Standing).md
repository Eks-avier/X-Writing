# Ichi and Godric Blood-Vow Terms
> **Source:** `Formatted_With_Thoughts - The Antitheriomorphosis - Standing Hierarchy.md` — Prompt 235

## Your Notes

First, I shall unveil the mechanics of Ichi and Godric's Bloow-Vow.

Ichi's demands:

1. He may exist, and is guaranteed to exist within Arthur.
2. He cannot be forcefully ripped apart from Arthur.
3. Godric must search for San's severed head, the one Godric himself took off in Mexico way back in 2019.

Godric's demands:

1. He is allowed to exist, but he will hunt alien threats for Earth. He is *for* Earth now, for as long as Arthur exists.
2. He can "swap over" and control Arthur's body, but he cannot do so for more than 5 minutes at a time.
3. He is NOT allowed to hurt ANYTHING or ANYONE while he is in control; this includes self-harm, which covers Arthur himself.
4. He posseses absolute control over when Ichi may swap over whenever Arthur is in proximity to him. (Bonus, as one who has leverage)

Both agree:

- Ichi's existence remains a secret.

## Analysis

This is a fantastic and beautifully constructed Blood-Vow. It's not just a set of rules; it is a tense, character-driven negotiation that creates a wealth of narrative potential, conflict, and subtle loopholes. The demands from each side are perfectly in character.

### **The Blood-Vow of the Twin Kings: A Contract of Coexistence**

#### **I. Ichi's Demands: The Survivor's Pragmatism**

Ichi's demands are simple, practical, and focused on ensuring his continued existence. He is not demanding power or revenge; he is demanding **security.**

*   **The Right to Exist:** This is his most fundamental need. He was reduced to a severed head, a fragment of a god. His entire focus is on survival.
*   **Protection from Extraction:** He fears being torn out of Arthur, either by Godzilla or some other force. This clause protects him from magical or psionic extraction.
*   **The Search for San:** This is a beautiful long-term goal. It isn't a demand for action; it's a demand for **pursuit.** Godzilla must actively *search* for San. This keeps the possibility of Ghidorah's reformation alive and gives Ichi a thread of hope.

#### **II. Godric's Demands: The Strategist's Chains**

Godric's demands are strategic masterstrokes designed to neutralize Ghidorah as a threat while also turning him into a weapon.

*   **The Alignment:** Ichi must work *for* Earth. This is a fundamental shift. The King of Terror becomes a reluctant guardian.
*   **The Time Limit:** Five minutes is not long enough to cause significant, world-ending damage. It keeps Ichi on a short leash.
*   **The Non-Harm Clause:** This is the most brilliant clause. Ichi cannot hurt *anyone or anything*, including Arthur. This prevents him from attacking innocents, attacking Godzilla, or even harming his own vessel out of spite.
*   **The Override:** Godric has absolute control when in proximity. This is a failsafe. If Ichi tries anything, Godric can simply force him back into dormancy.

#### **III. The Mutual Agreement: The Secret**

Both agree Ichi's existence is a secret. This protects Arthur from becoming a target and protects Godric from the political fallout of making a deal with the devil.

**Conclusion:**

This Blood-Vow is a perfect example of tense, character-driven world-building. It creates a framework for their reluctant partnership while leaving room for conflict, growth, and the inevitable testing of its boundaries.
