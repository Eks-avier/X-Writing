# Standing vs Rank Clarification
> **Source:** `Formatted_With_Thoughts - The Antitheriomorphosis - Standing Hierarchy.md` — Prompt 280

## Your Notes
I feel like we need to "spring clean" the Standing Hierarchy.

- What is a Rank?
- What is a Standing?
- What makes a Standing unique?
- What is the functional role of a certain Rank?
- How does a Rank of a certain Standing interact with the other Ranks?

It's currently very messy. Let me propose this schema:

A *STANDING* is the TITLE of a Titan, whether it's an official title or a common name. For example, Godzilla's official title is the Alpha Paramount. However, that title falls under the Alpha RANK.

- ALPHA is the Rank.
- ALPHA PARAMOUNT is the Standing.
- These two can be mutually exclusive. For example, the Alpha Sovereign IS an Alpha, while Rodan the Lord of the Skies is a Beta.

Mothra's official titles would then be the Alpha Divine but has the colloquial name of Queen of the Monsters. Her Standing is Alpha Divine, but her colloquial name, like the King of the Monsters, is not her Standing.

- The Wardens is not a Standing, it is a Rank. Their respective Standings are either CARDINAL or INTERCARDINAL.
- Rodan's Rank is Beta, and his Standing is Lord of the Skies.
- Anguirus's Rank is Beta, and his Standing is Stalwart Vanguard.

Regional Titans may or may not have a Standing. In which case, their Standing is simply whatever name their Regional title may be. However, if they're commonly known, they're given Standings, i.e., official titles.

This is... complicated. Is it good, though? You might have a better schema.

## Analysis
Your instinct to "spring clean" is absolutely correct, and your proposed schema is excellent. It is logically consistent and provides the necessary separation between the "box" and the "title" that has been missing.

Here is my analysis and refinement of your proposal to make it even cleaner.

---

### **The Spring Cleaned Standing Hierarchy: A Two-Axis System**

The Titan political structure is defined by two distinct but interrelated concepts:

1.  **Rank:** The **Administrative Category** or "Box."
2.  **Standing:** The **Specific, Named Title** within that Rank.

Think of it like a company.
*   **Rank:** "Vice President"
*   **Standing:** "Vice President of Marketing" or "Vice President of Engineering"

#### **Axis 1: Rank (The "What You Are")**

**Rank** is the level of a Titan's authority within the hierarchy. It defines their administrative category and their **functional relationship to the other Ranks.** Ranks are primarily **earned** through strength, territory, and achievement, though some (Alpha, Beta, Warden) also require **appointment** by the Paramount.

| Rank | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Alpha** | The Sovereigns. The global rulers. |
| **Beta** | The Direct Lieutenants. Extensions of the Paramount's will. |
| **Warden** | The Hemispheric Governors. Managers of vast regions. |
| **Regional** | The Natural Lords. Highest "natural" rank achieved through strength/territory. |
| **Territorial** | The Local Chieftains. Managers of small, specific areas. |
| **Primal** | The Non-Sapient. Managed, not governing. |

#### **Axis 2: Standing (The "Who You Are")**

**Standing** is the specific, named title a Titan holds within their Rank. It is their **political identity.** A Standing can be an official, appointed title or a colloquial one earned through reputation.

| Rank | Standing(s) |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Alpha** | Alpha Paramount (Godzilla), Alpha Divine (Mothra), Alpha Sovereign (Kong) |
| **Beta** | Lord of the Skies (Rodan), Stalwart Vanguard (Anguirus) |
| **Warden** | Cardinal Warden of the South (Scylla), Intercardinal Warden of the Northeast (Manda), etc. |
| **Regional** | Guardian of [Location Name] (e.g., Guardian of the Sunda Trench) |
| **Territorial** | Protector of [Location Name] |
| **Primal** | N/A (No Standing, as they lack the sapience to hold one) |

---

### **Key Rules of the System**

1.  **All Standings Require a Rank, But Not All Ranks Require a Standing.**
    *   Mothra is an **Alpha** (Rank), and her Standing is **Alpha Divine.**
    *   A random powerful serpent in the Mariana Trench might be a **Regional** (Rank), but if it has no official title, its Standing is simply "Guardian of the Mariana Trench" or "the Mariana Serpent."

2.  **Colloquial Names are NOT Standings.**
    *   "King of the Monsters" is a human epithet for Godzilla. His **official Standing** is **Alpha Paramount.**
    *   "Queen of the Monsters" is a colloquial name for Mothra. Her **official Standing** is **Alpha Divine.**

3.  **Standings Can Be Unique or Categorical.**
    *   **Unique Standings:** There is only one "Alpha Paramount," one "Lord of the Skies," etc.
    *   **Categorical Standings:** There are multiple "Cardinal Wardens." The full Standing would be "Cardinal Warden of the [Direction]."

4.  **The Warden Sub-Ranks (Cardinal/Intercardinal) are NOT Standings; they are Sub-Categories of the Warden RANK.**
    *   Scylla's **Rank** is Warden.
    *   Her **Sub-Rank** is Cardinal.
    *   Her **Standing** is "Warden of the South."

---

### **Summary: The Clean Schema**

*   **Rank** = The Level (e.g., Alpha, Beta, Warden).
*   **Standing** = The Title (e.g., Alpha Paramount, Lord of the Skies, Cardinal Warden of the South).

This schema is clean, consistent, and will hold up as you continue to expand the world. It clearly separates the "administrative function" from the "political identity," which is exactly what was needed.
