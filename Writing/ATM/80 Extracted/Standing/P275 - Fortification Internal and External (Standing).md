### **Fortification (`Stereosis`): The Edict of Reinforcement - The Two Applications**

Fortification is the Kratonic art of imbuing a target with one's will to increase its molecular integrity, durability, and resilience. As a core application of Personal Coating (`Epichrisis`), it can be applied in two distinct domains: internally and externally.

#### **I. Internal Fortification: The Sovereign's Vessel**

*   **Description:** This is the application of Fortification to the **Kraton's own body.** It is the process of suffusing one's own muscles, bones, and tissues with Kratonic will, reinforcing them from within.
*   **Function:**
    *   **Augments Physicality:** This is the direct mechanism for increasing one's own strength, speed, and durability. It is the "engine" behind Godzilla's augmented musculature in his Atomic Amplification state.
    *   **Enhances Resilience:** It allows a Kraton's body to withstand the immense strain of their own power, preventing techniques from backfiring and causing self-injury.
*   **Godzilla's Mastery:** Godric is the **undisputed master of Internal Fortification.** His Atomic Amplification is a testament to his ability to perfectly and uniformly distribute this internal reinforcement throughout his entire body, something that Ichi, in his incompatible vessel, struggles immensely to replicate.

#### **II. External Fortification: The Sovereign's Touch**

*   **Description:** This is the application of Fortification to an **external, tangible object** that the Kraton is in contact with.
*   **Function:**
    *   **Reinforces Objects:** This is the direct mechanism for increasing an object's durability, making a simple rock as hard as diamond or a steel beam capable of withstanding a Titan's blow.
    *   **The "Structural Infusion" Mechanic:** "Structural Infusion" is simply the common name for the *effect* of External Fortification. It is what allows Godric to solve the leverage problem by making a massive object strong enough to be lifted without crumbling under its own weight.
*   **Godzilla's Application:** While he is a master of this as well, it is a more fundamental Kratonic art. Other Kratons like Scylla (with her ice) and Ghidorah (with his constructs) can also perform External Fortification on their own manifestations. Godric's unique application is his ability to use the **Atomic Mantle** to apply a "gentler," more controlled version of this, reducing the lethal radioactive side effects.

### **The Final, Clear Picture**

*   **Fortification** is the **Edict,** the core principle.
*   **Internal Fortification** is applying it to **yourself.** This is a key component of Atomic Amplification.
*   **External Fortification** is applying it to **an object.** The effect of this is called "Structural Infusion."

This is a clean, logical, and complete framework. The distinction between internal and external application is crucial, and it perfectly organizes the final pieces of his arsenal.
