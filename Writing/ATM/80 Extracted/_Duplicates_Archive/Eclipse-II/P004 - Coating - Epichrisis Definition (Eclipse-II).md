# Coating - Epichrisis Definition

> **Source:** `Eclipse II` - Prompt 4 (Lines 585-778)

## Your Notes

While the four techniques above manage the Aura field itself, **Coating** remains a separate, distinct application.

## Analysis

### The Missing Piece: Coating

#### **Coating (Epichrisis - "Anointing" or "Coating Upon")**

* **Definition:** The act of taking one's Aura and actively applying it as a "sheath" or "cladding" to oneself or an object. This is separate from managing the passive territorial field. It is Aura *actualized* for a specific, tangible purpose.

Coating stands apart from the Intensity and Spatial Management axes because it represents the *active application* of Aura rather than the *passive management* of the Aura field. While Flaring, Suppression, Expansion, and Reduction modify the ambient field around a Kraton, Coating takes that Aura energy and deliberately focuses it onto a target--whether the Kraton's own body, a weapon, or another object.

This distinction is crucial: the four management techniques adjust the *environment* created by the Aura, while Coating is a *technique* performed with the Aura.
