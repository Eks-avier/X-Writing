# You Can Imagine How Horrified Darius
> **Source:** `Formatted_With_Thoughts - Branch of The Saga of the _Titanus gojira_.md` — Prompt 371

## Your Notes

You can imagine how horrified Darius is with Leo's current condition. With Godric, it reminds him of his own youth. Leo would be hyperactive one moment, then flopped down the next. It was probably enough to give Godric a heart attack the first time it happened. Luckily, Leo immediately recovers, suggesting it has nothing to do with his father and grandmother's heart condition. It spurs on the mystery. If it doesn't seem to be the heart condition, then it probably isn't from his `gojira` side. Though, that doesn't clear Leo of any suspiscion, because Astraea and Godzilla were mutants of their species! Astraea had her overactive heart which lead to her frail body. Godzilla had an overactive heart, a frail body, and a perfectly retentive memory. 

Let me clarify something, synesthesia is natural for a `gojira` to have. In fact, `mosura` have this, too, and likely more vivd due to their telepathy. This means that all three hybrid children are highly synesthesic, highly perceptive, and highly intelligent "out of the box," it's just their biology. Again, both `gojira` and `mosura` specimens have powerfule cognitive functions. For the `gojira`, it was necessary for managing huge swathes of territory. For the `mosura`, it was just a byproduct of their powerful psychic heritage. For Godzilla`, it's the combination of hyperthymesia, eidetic memory, and photgraphic memory, and the naturally powerful baseline cognitive functions that normal `gojira` have that made it overkill.

## Analysis

This creates a fascinating atmosphere of **High-Stakes Parenting** in the Palace. It means that for the first few years of Leo's life, Darius and Godric were likely living in a state of constant, low-grade panic, haunted by the ghosts of their medical history.

Let’s break down the nuance of **Leo’s "Crash"** and the **Cognitive Baseline** of the family.

---

### **I. Leo’s "Crash": The False Positive**

To Darius, seeing Leo zoom around at mach speed only to suddenly flop face-down and motionless on the floor is the ultimate PTSD trigger.

*   **Darius’s Perspective:** He sees Astraea. He remembers the days she would try to walk the territory and simply collapse, her energy spent, her chest heaving. When he sees Leo go down, his heart stops. He thinks, *"Not again. Not another one."*
*   **Godric’s Perspective:** He sees himself. He remembers the days Dagon forced him to march, and how his legs would just give out. He rushes over, expecting to feel the dangerous heat of a fever or the erratic pulse of a struggling heart.
*   **The Relief (The Quick Recharge):** But then, Leo pops up three minutes later, energized and asking for a snack.
    *   **The realization:** It’s not a *defect* (like the Heart Condition); it’s an *inefficiency*. Leo isn't sick; he's just a sports car with a small gas tank.
    *   **The Mystery:** This keeps them on edge. They know it's not the Heart... but what is it? Is it the Mosura side? Is it a new mutation? It keeps the "Mystery of the Nordsons" alive.

### **II. The Cognitive Baseline: A World of Technicolor**

Your clarification on Synesthesia is vital. It normalizes the "Super-Senses" for the species while highlighting Godric's specific burden.

#### **1. The Standard High-End Model (Darius, Junior, Leo, Lora)**
*   **Synesthesia as a Tool:** For them, "seeing sounds" or "tasting energy" isn't a disorder; it's a UI (User Interface).
    *   **Darius:** Used it to map territories. He could "smell" the boundaries of a rival's domain like a color line in the air.
    *   **The Children:** They use it for creativity and combat.
        *   **Leo:** Plays the violin because he likes the "colors" the notes make.
        *   **Junior:** Strategies combat because he can "see" the rhythm of an opponent like a geometric shape.
        *   **Lora:** Enjoys fashion because fabrics have a "sound" or "texture" to her extra senses.
*   **Status:** They are high-functioning geniuses by human standards, but "Normal" by Titan standards. They can turn it down or tune it out.

#### **2. The Godric Anomaly (The "Always-On" Recorder)**
This distinguishes Godric from everyone else.
*   **The Difference:** His brain doesn't just process this high-fidelity input; it **archives** it permanently.
*   **The Overload:** A normal *gojira* sees the "color" of the wind and uses that info, then discards it. Godric sees it, and his brain stamps it with a timestamp, emotional context, and saves it to a hard drive that cannot be erased.
*   **The Tragedy:** This is why his childhood was so much harder than his children's. He wasn't just dealing with the input; he was dealing with the inability to *filter* the retention of that input.

### **III. The "Mutant" Paranoia**

You raised a great point: **The Nordsons are a family of Mutants.**

*   **Astraea:** Heart Mutation (Defect/Power Source).
*   **Godzilla:** Heart Mutation + Memory Mutation.
*   **Leo:** Pigmentation Mutation (Green Eyes/Hair) + Scale Mutation (Rainbow).
*   **Darius:** The "Control Group" (Standard Northern Gojira).

This explains why Darius is the "Anchor." He is the only one who knows what "Normal" is supposed to look like for their species. He is the baseline against which all the anomalies are measured. When Darius says, "That's odd," the family listens, because he's the only one who isn't a genetic outlier.

### **Summary**

The Nordson household is a place where:
1.  **Senses are Supercharged:** The air is thick with information that only they can perceive.
2.  **Health is Obsessively Monitored:** Every nap is checked to ensure it's just a nap, not a coma.
3.  **Genius is Default:** Being a prodigy is just the baseline requirement for sitting at the dinner table.

It creates an environment of intense, loving scrutiny. Junior, Leo, and Lora are growing up in the safest, most monitored, and most high-pressure nursery in the universe, guarded by a father and grandfather who are determined to ensure that biology does not betray them this time.
