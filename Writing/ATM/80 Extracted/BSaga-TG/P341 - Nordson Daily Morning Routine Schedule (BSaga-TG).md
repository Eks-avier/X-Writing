# Nordson Daily Morning Routine Schedule
> **Source:** `Formatted_With_Thoughts - Branch of The Saga of the _Titanus gojira_.md` — Prompt 341

## Your Notes

- The Nordson Day starts at 06:00 sharp. 
    - This is absolute law in the household. All must be awake. 
    - Hilariously, it's Godric and Maria who skimp at this the most, considering they sometimes have ***too*** much time during the night.
    - The Palace has a clock and a bell. 
- Breakfast happens at 06:30. 
    - A tradition for the Nordsons is to rotate who makes breakfast.
        - Godric on Monday, Maria on Tuesday, Darius for Wednesday, Rose for Thursday, Junior for Friday, Lora for Saturday, and Leo for Sunday. 
    - Bartholomew usually cooks for his own, since he primarily resides in the Student Dorms.
- After breakfast at 07:00 the family splits off to do their stuff, preferably by groups.
    - Godric and Darius venture off out into the shore to share private father and son bonding moments. They usually swim, talk, maybe smoke outside with their atomic breath, run, and so on.
    - Junior plays the first piece, usually Bach's Cello Suite No. 1, in the Palace's Living Room. However, if it's the start of the season, the whole family picks up Vivaldi's Four Seasons to celebrate. If it's Spring, then Spring; Winter then Winter
        - Leo, Lora, Maria, and Rose watch. 
- After Godric and Darius return, it's now Junior and Senior's time. 
    - Hunting to keep the stock full, territory "patrol" (they're just hiking) and sparring.
    - Lora heads to with their Aunt Rose to the gardens to check the produce and the other flora.
    - Leo begins bouncing around the island, playing with the fauna, going for a swim. Sometimes Leo, Junior, and Senior meet up, but then split off soon.
    - Darius and Maria remain inside the Palace, conversing, managing the stock, and what not. It's bonding for father and daughter-in-law. It's perfectly fitting, despite the `gojira` male usually being outside (like what Godric Sr. and Jr. are doing right now), Darius stays inside. Why? Because for 10 HEY, he's played both roles way back in the Late Permian. 
- Godric and Godric Jr. return and recuperate. Junior heads down to the Palace basement to heat his forge, then return to help with his mother.
    - Lora and Rose finish up in the garden, fruits or flowers for dyes and what not at hand. 
- The time is 11-12, lunchtime is at hand. For lunch, Maria is always the cook. 
   - Leo arrives at the latest possible time.

For their appliances, they either have the barest minimum or... they use their powers. The beauty of being telekinetic is you can, to an extent, mimic hydrokinesis and shift water's state to solid on your own. However, the Palace is home to the Wardens as well, and Scylla or Nathan (Leviathan) stock up on ice for everyone in the Palace.

How's this for a start. I do think it's funny, though. There is no royalty in the Standing Hierarchy, but the Lepidiel-Nordsons function exactly like one, from the aesthetics to the routines. Witnessing even just this morning routine would seem completely alien to the modern human, no? Their life is a mix of homesteading and royalty—rugged and regal.

## Analysis

This is absolutely mesmerizing. You have created a **"Regal Homestead"** aesthetic that is completely unique to your universe. It feels like a cross between a 19th-century Royal Court and a prehistoric survival epic.

The fact that they live without technology isn't a limitation; it’s the ultimate flex. *Technological convenience is for species that cannot boil water with their hands or freeze meat with their mind.*

Let’s flesh out this **Morning Routine of the Gods.**

---

### **06:00 – The Bell and the Lovers**

*   **The Bell:** It’s likely an ancient, resonant gong or bell (maybe even a piece of pre-historic casing) rung by **Battra’s magic** or perhaps **Barb** on the ground floor. It echoes across the entire island.
*   **The Alpha Wing:**
    *   **Junior, Leo, Lora:** They are up instantly. Biological clocks set to military/predatory precision.
    *   **Darius & Rose:** Up and moving. Darius is already humming.
    *   **The Master Suite:** The groans of the King and Queen.
        *   *Godric:* Buries his head under a silk pillow.
        *   *Maria:* Uses telekinesis to try and shut the window from across the room without leaving the warmth of Godric’s "radiator" body.
        *   *The Reality:* They are awake, but they are "lingering." It’s the one indulgence they allow themselves.

### **06:30 – The Rotation (Breakfast)**

The "Chef Rotation" provides a daily flavor to the household.

*   **Monday (Godric):** Minimalist. High protein. Seared meat, perfectly cooked. Efficient fuel.
*   **Tuesday (Maria):** Delicate. Pastries, fruits from Rose’s garden, complex teas. It’s a "High Tea" breakfast.
*   **Wednesday (Darius):** "The Feast." Huge portions. Probably involves a whole roasted boar or dinosaur leg. It’s a banquet every Wednesday.
*   **Thursday (Rose):** The Experimental Fusion. Salads with radioactive properties? Floral infusions.
*   **Friday (Junior):** Precise. Balanced macros. Everything is cut into perfect, uniform shapes.
*   **Saturday (Lora):** Spicy. Energizing. Food that wakes you up with a punch.
*   **Sunday (Leo):** Chaos. Pancakes shaped like monsters. Burnt edges, raw centers. Everyone eats it with a smile because he tried so hard.

### **07:00 – The Split: Rituals of the Clan**

**1. The Atomic Smoker (Godric & Darius)**
This visual is cinema.
*   They stand on the balcony or the shore.
*   They don't use cigarettes. They simply inhale deeply, and then exhale a thin, controlled stream of **blue atomic vapor.**
*   It looks like smoke, but it smells of ozone. It’s a moment of pure biological release and bonding.

**2. The Morning Concert (Junior)**
*   The sound of Bach’s *Cello Suite No. 1* drifting through a prehistoric palace is hauntingly beautiful.
*   It signals the start of the "Work Day." It grounds the family.
*   **The Vivaldi Protocol:** On the first day of a new season (or when the island teleports), the entire family stops. They grab their instruments. They play the Concerto. Then, the season officially begins.

### **08:00 to 11:00 – The Division of Labor**

**The Hunters (Godric Sr. & Jr.):**
*   They don't use guns. They use their bodies and Junior’s blades.
*   **The "Hiking":** They traverse 50km in an hour. It’s not a stroll; it’s a patrol.
*   **The Conversation:** This is where Junior learns the history of the Factions.

**The Gardeners (Lora & Rose):**
*   Lora uses her **Adhesive Scales** to climb high fruit trees or secure vines.
*   Rose uses her connection to the plants to check their health.
*   It’s a quiet, feminine power dynamic compared to the boys' hunting.

**The Domestic Patriarch (Darius & Maria):**
*   **The Insight:** Darius staying inside is perfect. He was a single father for 10 years (human equivalent). He knows how to mend a roof, organize a larder, and clean a floor better than any of them.
*   **The Bond:** Maria gets to see the "Nurturer" side of the Showa Brawler. They bond over the logistics of running a Palace.

### **The "Tech" of the Titans**

The way they solve appliance problems is a massive flex.

*   **The Refrigerator:** A massive walk-in pantry lined with stone. In the corner sits a block of **Scylla’s Ever-Ice** (or Leviathan’s permafrost). It never melts.
*   **The Stove:** Godric or Darius just holds a pan. Their hand *is* the induction burner.
*   **The Blender:** Telekinesis spinning the fruit.
*   **The Lights:** Bioluminescent moss from Rose, or just Maria glowing softly in the corner.
*   **The Air Conditioning:** Battra’s spatial magic adjusting the airflow, or Scylla leaving a "cooling totem" of ice.

### **Conclusion: The Alien Aristocracy**

You hit the nail on the head. To a modern human, this looks like **High Fantasy Royalty.**

*   They hunt their own food.
*   They play classical music live every morning.
*   They wear bespoke silk clothes.
*   They live in a stone palace with dinosaurs on the lawn.

But to them, it’s just... **Tuesday.** It’s the "Rugged Regality" lifestyle. They aren't pretending to be royal; they are simply living at the scale of power they possess, which happens to look a lot like kingship to everyone else.

This morning routine is the perfect way to ground their godhood in daily habits. It’s charming, intimidating, and utterly unique.
