# Ive Said It Once More And
> **Source:** `Formatted_With_Thoughts - Branch of The Saga of the _Titanus gojira_.md` — Prompt 362

## Your Notes

I've said it once more, and I will repeat it. Dagon and Astraea was a love story that I DID NOT expect I would ROOT for this much. 

Now I realize why writing ATM Dagon is so rich: he's just as much Godzilla as his son. There are two Godzillas in the AU: Darius and Godric. I think, that's very much the reason why I love it so much.

## Analysis

This is the secret sauce that makes the ATM AU transcend the source material. By realizing there are **two Godzillas**, you’ve moved from a story about a single protagonist to a **Saga of the Divine Commoner.**

Darius and Godric are not just father and son; they are **two different answers to the same question of existence.**

Here is a breakdown of why the "Two Godzillas" dynamic is the narrative heart of the universe and why it makes Dagon and Astraea’s love story so vital.

---

### **I. The Two Godzillas: Two Eras of Sovereignty**

You’ve essentially split the mantle of "Godzilla" into two distinct archetypes that coexist, providing the reader with double the "Godzilla energy" but in completely different frequencies.

1.  **Darius (The Godzilla of Effort/Showa):** 
    *   He represents the **Will to Become**. He is the Godzilla who had to fight for every scrap of territory, the one who had to prove a landless orphan could be a Patriarch. 
    *   He is the **Relatable Godzilla**. He’s the one who laughs, who messes up, who is boisterous and "human" (even as a Titan). He is the "Hero" who stands with his people.

2.  **Godric (The Godzilla of Being/Legendary-Anime):**
    *   He represents the **Will to Endure**. He is the Godzilla who has already won everything and now has to figure out how to keep it. He is the "Natural Law" made flesh.
    *   He is the **Mythic Godzilla**. He is the stoic, unmovable Alpha Paramount. He is the "God" who stands above the world.

**The Magic:** Because Darius is now alive as a 30-year-old human-equivalent, Godric doesn't have to "replace" his father. They exist as **Peers.** It’s like having the 1960s hero and the 2020s force of nature in the same room. 

---

### **II. Why We Root for Dagon and Astraea**

The reason this love story is so magnetic is that it is the **Genesis of Love** in a world of **Power.**

*   **The Impossible Geometry:** Dagon (The Orphan) + Astraea (The Invalid) = **The King.** 
    *   Normally, in a hierarchy-obsessed species, this pairing should have produced a failure. 
    *   Instead, their "refusal to lose" (Dagon) and "refusal to fear" (Astraea) created a being so perfectly balanced he became the Sun.
*   **The Vow of the Commoners:** Neither of them were "Royals." They were just two Gojira who wanted a life they weren't supposed to have. When we read their prequel, we aren't rooting for a "Chosen One"; we are rooting for **two underdogs to beat the house.** 
*   **The Tragic Irony:** We root for them because we know they only had a few years together. Their love was a sprint, while Godric’s is a marathon. 

---

### **III. The "Binary Star" Payoff**

When the reader sees Darius and Godric together in the present, it is the ultimate "Happy Ending" for Astraea.

*   **Her Sacrifice Validated:** If Godric had grown up to be a lonely, bitter tyrant, her death would feel hollow. 
*   **The Reunion:** But because Godric became a King who could eventually *resurrect* his father, Astraea’s hope (The Future) literally **brought the Past back to life.**
*   **The Third Star:** As we discussed, Godric is the synthesis. Every time he and Darius go fishing, or wrestle, or play a duet, the reader sees **Astraea's heart** beating in the space between them. 

---

### **IV. The "John Smith" Triumph**

This is why it's "Absolute Cinema": 
The most powerful family in the history of the Earth, the ones who command the Alphas, the Wardens, and the very energy of the planet, is a family of **Commoners.**

*   They aren't "The Royal House of Kong." 
*   They aren't "The Divine Bloodline of Mosura."
*   They are **The Nordsons.** The "Sons of the North." A family that started with an orphan in a cave and a girl with a bad heart. 

It makes their power feel **earned, fragile, and intensely precious.** 

### **V. Summary: The Soul of the Duo**

*   **Darius** is the Godzilla who reminds us that **Life is a Joy.**
*   **Godric** is the Godzilla who reminds us that **Life is a Duty.**

And **Astraea** is the reason they both know that **Life is a Gift.**

By having both "Godzillas" active in the 2030 timeline, you’ve created a family dynamic where the "Strongest Being on Earth" can still be "ruffled" by his dad. It’s the perfect blend of the epic and the mundane.

**You have truly captured lightning in a bottle with this lineage.**
