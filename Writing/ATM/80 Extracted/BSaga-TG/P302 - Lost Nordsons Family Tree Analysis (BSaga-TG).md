## Analysis

This is a fascinating archaeological dig into the "Pre-Historic" era of your own creative process! Seeing "Mrs. Nordson" as a placeholder compared to the rich, tragic history of **Aurelia/Astraea** we just built is a testament to how far this universe has evolved.

The tag **"Genetic Offspring / Half-Siblings"** is the Rosetta Stone here. If these are beings derived from Godric's genetic material (like Rose/Biollante), then these "Lost Nordsons" must be the other iconic **Godzilla-Cell Mutants/Clones** from the franchise.

Here are my guesses for the Lost Siblings:

### **1. Millen Nordson**
**Identity:** **Orga (The Millennian)**
*   **The Logic:** This one feels the most definitive. Orga was originally a **Millennian** alien who absorbed Godzilla's "Organizer G-1" cells in the film *Godzilla 2000: Millennium*.
*   **The Name:** "Millen" is a direct nod to the **Millennium** era or the Millennian alien itself. It fits the pattern perfectly. He would have been the "Alien Half-Brother" who tried to become Godzilla but failed.

### **2. Megara Nordson**
**Identity:** **Megaguirus**
*   **The Logic:** Megaguirus is the queen of the Meganulon, who became a Titan-level threat by stealing Godzilla's energy. While not a direct clone like Biollante, she is defined by her parasitic siphoning of his specific power.
*   **The Name:** "Megara" is a near-perfect phonetic match for **Mega**guirus. Plus, Megara is a Greek name (Hercules' wife), fitting the classical naming conventions you sometimes use, but the "Mega" prefix is the giveaway. She would have been the "Parasitic Half-Sister."

### **3. Simon Nordson**
**Identity:** **SpaceGodzilla**
*   **The Logic:** If we have Biollante (Plant-Hybrid) and Orga (Alien-Hybrid), the only major "Genetic Offspring" left in the pantheon is SpaceGodzilla (Crystal-Hybrid). He is canonically formed from Godzilla cells cast into space (sometimes theorized to be carried by Biollante or Mothra). He is the ultimate "Evil Twin/Brother."
*   **The Name:** This is the trickiest one!
    *   It could be phonetic: **S**pace -> **S**imon.
    *   It could be a play on **Psi**onics (since SpaceGodzilla is a heavy telekinetic) -> **Si**mon.
    *   Or, it might be a nod to the fanon name "Xenilla" (X -> S sound?).
    *   Regardless, in a lineup of "Godzilla's Genetic Offspring," SpaceGodzilla is the missing piece of the trinity.
