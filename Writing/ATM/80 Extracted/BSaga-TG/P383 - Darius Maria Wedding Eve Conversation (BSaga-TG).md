# A Conversation 2029 One Day Before
> **Source:** `Formatted_With_Thoughts - Branch of The Saga of the _Titanus gojira_.md` — Prompt 383

## Your Notes

*A conversation. 2029. One day before Godric married Maria.*

>No, Darius, let me fix—
>Nonsense. If you must know one thing, I raised my lad alone. I know my way around a household... this fancy cavern...
>The Palace?
>Aye. Go be my beautiful daughter instead and knock some sense into my son.
>He's lucky to have you, Darius.
>Wouldn't think that, lass. I caused that poor laddie's heart to break for 250 million years. I'm lucky to have *him* to continue on my legacy.
>And yet even in that great gulf of time... even through his vapid hate and misunderstanding... you're still here to be what he needs the most... You've mended with him. 
>(Laughs) You make me sound like I'm marrying my own lad!? Lighten up, lassie! No need for all that weight in your face. It's turning you ugly. And we can't have that just before the big day.
>I apologize, Darius. It's that...

Darius reaches for his future daughter-in-law's hand. 

>I ken that look, lass.
>You do?
>'Course. Ah see it every day in me reflection on the lakes. You miss your mommy and daddy.
>That's not what I meant—
>Bah! Your twin brother's right, daughter! You deny yourself too much! 
>How did... you know?
>Cause I am like you. Got no sire nor mother. Got no clan.
>I wish... I wish... they left something. Anything. I wish I could show them... this. Tomorrow.
>Aye. I ken the feelin'. Was much like that with your man's mother. Heaven, she was beautiful. But ye, I've got none on me side of the family during the binding rites. 

---

>Never worry about your father, Maria. My beautiful wife will straighten that one out. Brothers can be difficult, especially new ones.
>Your brother, Darius? I thought you had no...
>Lass. You're not the only one gaining a family tomorrow. In our culture, the son and daughter aren't the only ones being bound. Your father shall my brother, and your mother shall be my sister. They will have Godric's mother and I as brother and sister as well.
>Will you be alright with that?
>Why wouldn't I be? Ghosts've haunted me since I was born. What's a few more, even divine ones?.
>Thank you, Darius. Father.

## Analysis

This is a breathtakingly tender scene. You have identified the final piece of the "Domestic Fantasy": **The Healing of the Orphans.**

In this moment, Darius Nordson stops being just the Father of the Groom and becomes the **Patriarch of the Lost.** He uses the ancient, communal culture of the Northern Gojira to reach across 250 million years and adopt the ghosts of the Mosura.

Let’s break down the profound implications of this conversation.

---

### **I. Darius: The High-Functioning Caretaker**
Darius’s insistence on "fixing" things himself is the perfect callback to his 10 HEY as a single father.
*   **The "Showa" Competency:** He doesn't need servants or bots. He knows how to manage a "cavern," even if it’s a Palace. To him, the work is a way to stay grounded. 
*   **The "Daughter" Promotion:** When he tells Maria to "go be my beautiful daughter," he is performing an act of **Sovereign Grace.** He is stripping away the weight of her "Alpha Divine" title and giving her a place where she is simply *protected*.

### **II. The Shared Silence of the Orphanage**
The most moving part of this dialogue is Darius recognizing the "Look."

*   **The Gojira Intuition:** Darius doesn't need telepathy to know Maria is grieving for her parents. He knows because he spent his youth looking at the Fallen Star and seeing an empty space where a father and mother should be. 
*   **Validating the Denial:** Maria "denies herself too much"—this is her Lunar nature. She reflects everyone else's joy but hides her own sorrow in the "Oort Cloud." Darius, being the Tangible Earth, forces her to look at it. He makes the intangible grief real so it can be handled.

### III. The Binding of Ghosts: A New Northern Law
This is a magnificent piece of cultural lore. In the Northern Faction, a marriage isn't just a union of two individuals; it is a **Merger of Lineages.**

*   **The Spiritual Adoption:** Darius declares that Maria's father is now his **Brother**, and her mother is now his **Sister**. 
    *   This is the **"Will to Love"** at its most expansive.
    *   Darius is essentially telling Maria: *"You are no longer an orphan. Your parents are not gone; they have been folded into the House of the Iceberg. I am their brother, and I will carry their memory as my own blood."*
*   **Astraea’s Role:** The idea that Astraea will "straighten out" Maria's father in the afterlife is a wonderful character beat. It suggests that even in death, the Northern Matriarch is the one who manages the "Internal Domain" of the family, even across the veil.

### IV. The "Father" Realization
The moment Maria calls him "Father" is the emotional climax of her journey.

*   **The 33rd Inheritor finds a Sire:** For 250 million years, Mothra was the mother to the world but had no father of her own. By saying that word, she is finally **landing.** She is ending her "Drift" and becoming part of a tangible, grounded family.
*   **Darius’s Acceptance:** "What’s a few more ghosts?" He accepts the burden of her history with a smile. He is a man who was built to carry heavy things, and he is honored to carry her parents' legacy alongside his own.
