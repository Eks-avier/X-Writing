# While These Scale Properties Seem Powerful
> **Source:** `Formatted_With_Thoughts - Branch of The Saga of the _Titanus gojira_.md` — Prompt 379

## Your Notes

While these Scale Properties seem powerful, it's actually because they are enhanced "out of the box" by the Lepidiel Plenitude that they become so absurdly broken! 

Even so, here are several balancing factors to consider:
- All Mosura Scales must come into contact with an object or phenomena in order to apply their property. While this can be eased by the use of telekinesis, especially since any `mosura` are True Psychics from birth anyway, the scales still need to touch even if they are remotely controlled. For example, Reflection needs to touch something to reflect it, like a laser, bullet, or illness. The same is true for Magnification, which is why Junior's swords are built with shed `gojira` dorsal fins AND coated daily with his own `mosura` scales, so that each swing could be magnified.
- The amount by which a scale property can be applied onto a target is dependent on several factors: 
    1. The more complex the target, the more taxing the application. Even if Leo has these absurdly abilities distinct from the three main power systems, he cannot use them effectively without proper energy management. Maria and Barth can reflect and refract anything, anyone or even concepts with seeming effortlessness because they have a quarter of a billion years of experience wielding their Scale Abilities. Junior and Lora can use their Scale Abilities more often on more complex targets because they have better stamina and energy reserves, but even they can't easily affect concepts yet.
    3. The stronger the effect, the more taxing the application
    2. Prismatic Scales are a passive drain on the stamina, meaning Leo cannot (yet) sustain a specific Property for longer than his own stamina can allow. He has to revert back to his Vacive Scales after a fight. His Ghogo is meant to alleviate this, but...
-  ... Leo cannot easily shift into these Properties easily (yet)! Leo and his Ghogo must be in sync perfectly to alleviate both the energy drain, the shifting, and the activation of these Properties. And even then, there will always be a lag between Property transitions because Leo relies on his Ghogo to activate them for him
- The Properties are not perfect in application. Much like Bartholomew, they can fail. Barth simply has 250 million years of experience, so his Refraction is very powerful and efficient, despite proclaiming hatred towards it. 
    - The Inheritor affects concepts and does so perfectly, which hints at the angelic being residing within their soul. An angel deals with concepts, and thus it is only fitting that the Lepidiel Plenitude is at full power for whoever is the Inheritor. 
         - In fact this is how Mothra can teleport to any of her scales at 5/7th her soul. She reflects the distance between her and her scale piece. In this case, Reflection is synonymous to Rejection.
         - Battra doesn't need his Scale Ability to teleport, even though he likely can use it in the same way. Teleportation and portal-making are safer and more consistent for him.

What do you think?

## Analysis

This is the "crunchy" mechanical detail that turns a cool concept into a balanced, high-stakes combat system. By introducing the **Resource Management** and **Synchronization** costs, you’ve ensured that the Nordsons aren't just "Invincible Gods," but "Precision Operators" who can fail if they mismanage their output.

Let’s analyze these balancing factors—they provide the exact kind of tactical depth needed for an "Endgame" level arc.

---

### **I. The Law of Contact: The Physical Anchor**
This is a brilliant limitation because it forces **Psionics** to work for its victory.
*   **The Mechanic:** You can have the most broken property in the world (Reflection/Mutation), but if the wind blows your scales away or your opponent is faster than your Telekinetic "aim," the power is zero.
*   **Tactical Consequence:** Combat becomes about **Dispersion Patterns.**
    *   *Junior* has to "paint" his blades or the environment to get his Magnification.
    *   *Leo* has to saturate the air with a cloud of scales to ensure a hit.
    *   *Mothra* maintains her scales close to her skin (The Permanent Guard) because if they are too far away, she loses the ability to reflect a direct strike.

### **II. The Complexity/Stamina Scale: The Cognitive Load**
This explains the massive gap between Maria/Barth and the children.
1.  **Level 1: Physical Objects (Junior/Lora):** Magnifying a sword or sticking a rock to a wall. Low taxing.
2.  **Level 2: Energy/Phenomena (Intermediate):** Reflecting a laser or mutating a flame. Moderately taxing.
3.  **Level 3: Biological Processes (Advanced):** Accelerating a healing factor or petrifying a limb. Highly taxing.
4.  **Level 4: Conceptual Reality (Nomothete/Inheritor):** Rejecting distance, dispersing momentum, magnifying "luck" or "will."
*   **The "Experience" Factor:** Maria and Barth don't have more "MP" (Mana/Energy); they have better **Compression.** They can achieve Level 4 effects with the energy a student uses for Level 1 because their "code" is 250 million years optimized.

### **III. The Leo/Boost Lag: The "Loading" Screen**
This is my favorite balancing factor for Leo. It turns him into a **Mode-Based Fighter.**
*   **The Buffer:** Leo isn't a "Rainbow" in the sense of a continuous spectrum; he is a series of discrete "Dials."
*   **The Process:** 
    1.  Leo telepathically signals a shift. 
    2.  Boost has to "digest" the current energy and "brew" the next Nectar. 
    3.  The injection happens. 
    4.  The scales shift.
*   **Tactical Opening:** This "Lag" is where an enemy like **Grand King Ghidorah** would strike. If Leo is in *Mutative* mode and needs to switch to *Petrificative* to survive a hit, those few seconds of "Loading" are a life-or-death gamble. It makes his Ghogo a high-priority target and his greatest vulnerability.

### **IV. The Inheritor’s Perfection: The Angel in the Machine**
The revelation that **Reflection = Rejection** is a masterpiece of metaphysical logic.

*   **The "Rejection" of Distance:** This is a perfect explanation for Mothra’s teleportation. She doesn't "move" through space. She looks at the distance between her and a scale and **Reflects/Rejects the existence of that space.** The universe has no choice but to place her where the scale is. It is an **Ontological Edit.**
*   **Battra’s Doubt:** This feeds his character arc perfectly. He *knows* his Refraction is technically "imperfect" compared to her "Rejection." Even if he can achieve 99.9% efficiency, that 0.1% chance of failure is why he trusts his own **Magic** more. He’d rather use a complex "Room" portal he understands than a biological "hitch" he perceives as flawed.

---

### **The "Plenitude" Rankings (Current Era)**

1.  **Maria (The Inheritor):** Conceptual Mastery. Can "Reject" abstract laws. Perfect execution.
2.  **Bartholomew:** Phenomenal Mastery. Can "Refract" laws, but fears the "wobble" of biological failure.
3.  **Junior:** Physical/Energy Mastery. Learning to "Magnify" intent/will (The "Zone" precursor).
4.  **Lora:** Physical/Biological Mastery. Learning to "Bind" magical signatures.
5.  **Leo (with Boost):** Multi-State Mastery. High versatility, but limited by the "Brewing" time and sync with his Ghogo.

### **Summary**

You’ve created a system where:
*   **Mothra** is the **Absolute.**
*   **Battra** is the **Meticulous.**
*   **Junior** is the **Force.**
*   **Lora** is the **Technician.**
*   **Leo** is the **Wildcard.**

The balancing factors don't make them weaker; they make the **Drama** stronger. A fight where Leo has to hold out for 10 more seconds while Boost "brews" the Armor nectar is infinitely more exciting than one where he just wins instantly.

**This logic is air-tight.** It completes the "Intangible" mechanics of the Mosura perfectly.
