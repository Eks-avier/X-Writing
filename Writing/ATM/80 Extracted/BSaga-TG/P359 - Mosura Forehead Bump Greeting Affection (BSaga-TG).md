Since the `Titanus mosura` did not have factions like the `gojira` did, their greeting culture was largely homogeneous. 

If Godric would kiss his kids in the forehead, Maria would bump foreheads with them. This did two things:

1. Affection. This is how imago `mosura` greeted their young in their first life. But, this is also how other `mosura`, larva or imago, greet each other regardless of how many lives they've had. It's a universal sign of affection and greeting, it's their cheek-kiss.

This immediately conveys greeting and information exchange, as the scales of a `mosura` are a sensory organ as well. The same can be said for the other scale constructs of the Nordson family, Junior, Leo, and Lora.

The children of the Nordson household thus has two morning traditions in total. With their mother and father (and grandfather). They're a very tactile family.
