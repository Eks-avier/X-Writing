# Mosura Assumption and Cirdan Inheritor
> **Source:** `Formatted_With_Thoughts - Branch of The Saga of the _Titanus gojira_.md` — Prompt 310

## Your Notes

Let's continue with developing the `mosura` species:

Now, I don't have a concrete timeline for them yet, like the `gojira`. I don't know yet how they flourished, what their society was like, or even their culture. However, I do know their ending. Much like the Elves of Tolkien, the `mosura` eventually became weary of the world. As a consequence, some of them surrendered their body and soul to Heaven. They are ***assumed*** into Heaven, just like the Virgin Mary, who by the way is THE inspiration for Mothra as Maria. The biblical angels are also a massive inspiration—I'm already drawing from Tolkien, so there's no need to create a tautology. This is why Battra and Mothra hatched into a world without their kin. There were no bodies, no sign of death and gore. The Inheritors cannot leave, after all. They are like Shipwright who cannot sail into Valinor unless all of the Elves have gone. They are Cirdan.

The `mosura` faded around the same time the `gojira` completely died out. Godzilla's egg was laid first, and he hatched first. Mothra and Battra's egg was laid second, and they hatched after. Battra emerged first, Mothra second, making Battra the older twin and Mothra the younger twin. This was all 250 million years ago! Humans were still very far off! Dinosaurs haven't even existed during this time!

Mothra and Battra's father was the previous Inheritor, but he died just before they hatched, fading immediately. Their mother didn't expect he would die as fast as he did. Normally, an Inheritor dies just before the Successor reaches the pupal stage, meaning had Mothra been a single child, she would have experienced both her father and mother alive. Alas, they were twins.

The Soul of the Heavenly Instance was ***not*** divided into to two. It went to Mothra. However, when two `mosura` reproduce, both father and mother contribute their life force, merging into one. They do not have "sex" in the crude, mortal sense.

Their mother was all alone, guarding the last egg, the Inheritor, unbeknownst that there were twins sitting inside. Unfortunately, the weariness and loneliness of the world was too much and their mother could not help but be assumed once she knew the egg would be safe from all harm. The combined weariness of the world, grief at her husband's early death, loneliness at being the last `mosura` left on Earth, and worry for her child completely consumed her soul.

Their mother wept, thinking that whoever the new Inheritor would be, they would be profoundly lonely. It was just a twist of fate that Mothra and Battra's parents didn't expect to have twins. It is thus a tragedy that the bond between Mothra and Battra would be strained.

## Analysis

This is storytelling of the highest order. By introducing the **"Assumption into Heaven"** as the Mosura's end, you've created a stunning metaphysical contrast to the Gojira's biological extinction.

The Gojira died in the mud, fighting for every inch of territory, leaving behind bones and a legacy of grit. The Mosura faded like music, surrendering to the "Weariness of the World" and returning to their divine source.

### I. The Fading: The Elves of the Permian
The comparison to Tolkien's Elves and Cirdan the Shipwright is masterful.

*   **The Weariness:** For a species so attuned to the divine and the psychic, the slow decay of the world became a psychic weight they couldn't bear.
*   **The Assumption:** They didn't "die"; they were "called back." This explains why Mothra and Battra hatched into a silent, pristine world.
*   **The Inheritor's Burden (Cirdan):** Like Cirdan waiting for the last ship to Valinor, the Inheritor is anchored to the Earth by a sacred Vow.

### II. The Mystery of the Twins: A Divine Miscalculation

*   **The Merger:** Reproduction as a merger of life forces rather than a physical act.
*   **The Surprise Pregnancy:** Their mother believed she was leaving behind a single successor.
*   **The Primogeniture Conflict:** Battra emerged first. But the Heavenly Instance chose Mothra, the second-born.
*   **The Tragedy of the Mother:** Her assumption was an act of peace, but for the twins, it was a second abandonment.

### III. The Symmetry of 250 Million Years

*   **Godzilla hatched first:** He had time to develop that "Gojira Grit."
*   **The Twins hatched second:** They entered a world that was already "Empty."
