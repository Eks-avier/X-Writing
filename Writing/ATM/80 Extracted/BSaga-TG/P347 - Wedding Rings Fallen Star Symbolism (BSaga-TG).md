# Ah Now I Know What Was
> **Source:** `Formatted_With_Thoughts - Branch of The Saga of the _Titanus gojira_.md` — Prompt 347

## Your Notes

AH. Now I know what was missing! It was their wedding rings. 

Godric and Maria had three pairs:
- One pair for Gaius and Helena 
- One pair for fiancées.
- One pair for Godric and Maria

Bartholomew made the first one. They're part of the disguise that Godric and Maria eventually put away when they decided to start anew after Meadowvale. The second one is a collaboration between Bartholomew, Godric, and Kevin. They forged it together, serving as future foreshadowing of Godric's prowess at forging. Bartholomew added the flourish, Godric provided the scale-silk composite material that he and Maria used on their clothes, and Kevin helped him find the Fallen Star (yes, any scene where we have Godric and Kevin in them before the Fallen Star is contextualized by the ring-making).

The engagement ring possessed a piece of the Fallen Star, while their wedding rings are bands rather than rings (because they had practicality in mind). Instead of having the piece of the Fallen Star as a gem, it was embedded into the bands themselves. 

In all the rings, Godric's was as black as his dorsal fins, with white vines of Mothra's chitin, which she has many of in her lairs scattered across the world, etched in the crevices of the scaly finish. Likewise, Maria's was as white as her fluff and chitin, with black veins of Godzilla's shed scales embedded in its finish. They wear these rings daily.

The rings during Meadowvale currently sit inside their wardrobe. Maria's engagement ring has been turned into a necklace. Their wedding rings are worn daily.

## Analysis

This is the final, physical seal on their union. It ties **history, family, magic, and biology** into three simple circles of metal and material.

The revelation that the **Fallen Star** search was catalyzed by Godric wanting to make a ring is *perfect*. It means his journey to discover his parents' "Will to Love" wasn't just an archaeological trip; it was a quest to fuel his *own* love. He went to find the source of his species' power to forge a promise to his wife.

Let’s break down the **Trilogy of Rings.**

---

### **I. The Disguise Set: The Rings of "Gaius and Helena"**
*   **Maker:** Bartholomew Lepidiel.
*   **Material:** Simple silver and gold, enchanted to look worn and unassuming.
*   **Significance:** These are the **"Rings of Pretend."** They represent the safety of the mask.
*   **Current Status:** Stored in the wardrobe.
*   **Why Keep Them?** Because "Gaius and Helena" were the people who fell in love. Those rings are the souvenir of the couple they were allowed to be when the world wasn't watching. Godric probably looks at them when he misses the simplicity of Meadowvale.

---

### **II. The Engagement Set: The Ring of the Star**
*   **Makers:** The "Groomsmen" Trinity (Godric, Kevin, Battra).
*   **The Quest:** This re-contextualizes the Hollow Earth trip. Godric didn't just want a rock; he wanted the **Heart of his People.** He wanted to bind his promise with the same energy that created his lineage.
*   **The Material:** A fragment of the **Fallen Star.**
    *   **The Symbolism:** By using the Star, he is literally proposing with the **"Will to Love" of Dagon and Astraea.** He is saying, "My parents died for love; I will live for it."
*   **Current Status:** Worn as a necklace by Maria. It hangs close to her heart, a radiant gem of cosmic history.

---

### **III. The Wedding Bands: The Rings of the Eclipse**
*   **Design Philosophy:** **Practicality and Inversion.** These are rings for hands that fight, build, and garden. They cannot be ostentatious; they must be indestructible.
*   **The "Star-Dust" Infusion:** Instead of a gem, the Fallen Star fragment was pulverized and mixed into the alloy. The rings *hum* with the faint, collective will of the Gojira ancestors, singing a song of protection.

#### **Godric’s Band: The Moon in the Night**
*   **Base:** **Black** (His dorsal fin/scales).
*   **Inlay:** **White Vines** (Maria’s Chitin).
*   **Visual:** It looks like lightning striking through a dark sky, or white veins in black marble.
*   **Meaning:** He carries her fragility and her grace embedded in his unyielding strength. It is the Moon’s light breaking through the Void.

#### **Maria’s Band: The Shadow in the Light**
*   **Base:** **White** (Her fluff/Chitin/Silk).
*   **Inlay:** **Black Veins** (Godric’s Shed Scales).
*   **Visual:** It looks like cracks in porcelain, or ink diffusing in milk.
*   **Meaning:** She carries his density and his gravity embedded in her ethereal form. It is the Sun’s gravity holding the Light together.

### **The "Wearing Each Other" Conclusion**

This completes the visual motif we established with their clothes.
*   **Clothes:** Wrapped in each other's protection (Silk/Scales).
*   **Rings:** Pierced by each other's essence (White veins in Black / Black veins in White).

The rings are the **microcosm of the Eclipse.** They are the physical proof that the Tangible (Godzilla) and the Intangible (Mothra) have not just met, but have become inseparable alloys of one another.

And the fact that **Kevin (Kong)** helped find the material and **Bartholomew (Battra)** helped forge the structure means the rings are also a testament to the **Found Family** that made the marriage possible.

**We are ready.** The Profile for Godric Nordson will be the culmination of all this work. It will be a document that breathes with history, tragedy, and triumph.
