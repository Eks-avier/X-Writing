## Analysis

This clarification adds a vital layer of "Alien Morality" to the Nordsons. It prevents them from becoming too "Disney-fied" or purely heroic in a human sense.

By grounding Dagon's acceptance in **genetic tribalism** rather than universal benevolence, you reinforce the fact that they are **Apex Predators** from a species on the brink of extinction. Their love is fierce, but it is **exclusionary**.

### **I. The Gojira Imperative: "We Are Few"**

The principle that **"Any *gojira*... always seeks their own"** is the most powerful biological law in your series.

*   **The Evolutionary Logic:** For a species with such low birth rates and such powerful enemies (the MUTOs), unconditional loyalty to the bloodline is a survival necessity.
*   **Dagon's Perspective:** He isn't adopting Millen, Rose, Simon, and Megara because he's a "nice guy." He is adopting them because his instincts are screaming, **"These are Kin. Kin must survive."**
*   **Maria's Contrast:** This highlights just how special Maria is. She is the **only** one who loves outside her species. It makes her role as the "Heart" of the family even more crucial.

### **II. Simon Nordson (SpaceGodzilla): The Twisted Preservationist**

Your take on SpaceGodzilla is psychologically fascinating. He is the **Dark Mirror** of the Gojira instinct.

*   **Twisted Krátos:** His crystals are his Will made manifest. But where Godric uses Will to *protect* or *destroy*, Simon uses Will to **possess**.
*   **The Conflict:** He comes to Earth to conquer. He senses Dagon and Godric. The "Gojira Imperative" hits him like a truck.
    *   *His Logic:* "I cannot destroy my kin. But I must rule. Therefore, I will **crystallize** them."

### **III. Megara Nordson (Megaguirus): The Conflicted Predator**

Megara represents the war between **Hunger** and **Heritage**.

*   **The Parasite's Nature:** She *needs* to drain energy to evolve. Godric is the biggest battery in the universe.
*   **The Gojira Block:** But her Dagon-cells revolt at the idea. Draining Godric feels like auto-cannibalism.
*   **The Result:** She becomes the **"Sibling Rivalry" Incarnate.**

### **IV. Identity in Hybridity: The Core Theme**

Two main struggle tracks for the children:

1.  **The Villain Track (Nature vs. Goal):** Simon and Megara—goals sabotaged by biology.
2.  **The Hero Track (Identity vs. Parts):** Rose, Millen, Junior, Leo, Lora—figuring out *who* they are.

**Blood is Thicker than Ambition.** Even a space tyrant and a parasite queen cannot overcome the fundamental Gojira imperative: **The Lineage Must Survive.**
