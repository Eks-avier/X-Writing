Junior is the **Alchemist of the Gojira Line.**

*   **The Equation:**
    *   **Dagon (Iron):** Raw, unrefined, enduring ore. The foundation of the earth.
    *   **Astraea (Fire):** The heat required to forge.
    *   **Godric (The Smith):** The one who hammers the iron.

*   **The Transformation:** Junior takes the "Commoner" strength of his father’s line—which was rough, brutal, and survivalist—and refines it with **Mosura Grace.**

### **II. Leo: The Will to Explore (The Maternal Perfection)**

Leo is the **Anchor of the Mosura Line.**

*   **The Equation:**
    *   **The 32nd Inheritor (The Rainbow):** Infinite potential, but ethereal and fleeting. A dream that fades.

    *   **The Gojira Blood:** **The Anchor.** The density, the regeneration, the stubborn refusal to die.
*   **The Transformation:** Leo takes the "Intangible" magic of his maternal grandfather and gives it **Mass.**
