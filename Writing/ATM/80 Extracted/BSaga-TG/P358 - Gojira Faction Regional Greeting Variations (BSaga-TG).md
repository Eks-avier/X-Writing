# Its Perfect And Indeed Different Factions
> **Source:** `Formatted_With_Thoughts - Branch of The Saga of the _Titanus gojira_.md` — Prompt 358

## Your Notes

It's perfect. And indeed, different factions and different regions WITHIN those factions do the same ritual differently. 

Towards the East, Northeast, and Southeast, the play-bow is the adult's choice of greeting. Even between the mentioned three, the ritual means different things. For the Northeast, it's play. For the Southeast, it's a test of reflexes. For the East, it's an actual greeting followed by a sniff towards the neck. 

Towards the West, the Factions begin doing a tail-clasp. This is obviously the equivalent of a hand-shake or a hand-clasp. 

You can imagine what these greetings would translate to human rituals if we had different `gojira` races still alive! 

As an orphan Northerner who lived with fellow Northern orphans from various regions in the Northern Faction, he knows most if not all of the greetings in his Faction. 

However, because he was used to watching the trials at the young age by the Fallen Star where the other `gojira` factions congregate, he had also observed many of the other factions greet each other. He did his best to share what he remembered to Godric (because Darius only taught him the basics long ago), Junior, Leo, and Lora. Sometimes he gets it wrong, but then again, there's no other `gojira` left but them left so someone has to keep them alive.

## Analysis

This is social world-building at its most evocative. You’ve created a **Linguistic of the Body** that maps the geographic and cultural diversity of a lost species onto human social cues.

The fact that Darius is the "Professor of Ettiquette" for a dead world is a beautiful, bittersweet role. He isn't just teaching them how to move; he’s giving them the **social DNA** of the Gojira. If the "Fallen Star" is their soul, these greetings are their **civilization.**

Let’s map out the **Gojira Protocol** and how it translates to their modern lives.

---

### **The Gojira Protocol: A Factional Map of Human Touch**

#### **1. The Western Faction: The Clasp of Peers**
*   **Original (Titan):** **Tail-clasp.** A slow, rhythmic entangling of tails while facing each other. It required trust (exposing the back) and was used to gauge the other’s strength and temperature.
*   **Human Translation:** **The Warrior’s Handshake.** A firm, forearm-to-forearm clasp.
*   **The Vibe:** Respect and equality. This is how Godric greets Kong. It’s an acknowledgment of a fellow "Pioneer" and warrior.

#### **2. The Eastern Faction: The Liturgy of Order**
*   **Original (Titan):** **The Formal Bow + Neck Sniff.** A deep lowering of the head, followed by a slow, methodical scent-sampling of the neck.
*   **Human Translation:** **The Deep Bow.** A silent, respectful bow, followed by standing close enough to sense the other’s energy signature.
*   **The Vibe:** Tradition and Purity. This is the most "Royal" greeting. Junior uses this most often in formal settings, reflecting his meticulous nature.

#### **3. The Southeastern Faction: The Spark of Reflex**
*   **Original (Titan):** **The Reflex Bow.** A sudden, low-to-the-ground crouch meant to trigger a "fight or flight" response in the other. If the other Gojira didn't flinch, they were accepted as competent.
*   **Human Translation:** **The High-Five / Hand-Slap.** A sharp, fast physical exchange that tests coordination.
*   **The Vibe:** Competitive Camaraerie. Leo loves this. It’s a "Yo, what’s up?" that doubles as a "Are you ready to move?"

#### **4. The Northeastern Faction: The Invitation to Joy**
*   **Original (Titan):** **The Play-Bow.** Lowering the chest to the ground while the tail is held high and wagging. 
*   **Human Translation:** **The Shoulder-Shove / Roughhousing.** A playful, heavy nudge or a quick "tackle" that leads to a brief wrestling match.
*   **The Vibe:** The Showa Soul. This is Darius’s favorite. It’s the "Frat Boy" greeting that says, *"I love you, now let's see if you can take me down."*

---

### **Darius: The Last Librarian of Rites**

The role of Darius here is profoundly important. 

*   **The "Orphanage" Advantage:** Because he grew up in a communal setting with orphans from across the North, he speaks more "dialects" of touch than a Gojira raised in a single clan would. He is a **Generalist of Affection.**
*   **The Pilgrimage Memories:** Watching the inter-factional meetings at the Fallen Star gave him the "Foreign Language" versions. He remembers the way the Southerners would practically swarm each other (The "Family Pile" origin) and the way the Westerners were always showing off new tail-grips.
*   **The "Correction" Dynamic:** 
    *   *Darius:* "No, no, Leo! The Southeastern bow requires a quicker snap of the wrist! You’re greeting a hunter, not a napping turtle!"
    *   *Godric:* (Sighing) "Dad, the humans are staring at us. We are in a supermarket."
    *   *Darius:* "Let them stare! They’re witnessing *culture*, son!"

---

### **The "New" Correct: The Nordson Synthesis**

There is a beautiful, accidental truth in Darius getting some things wrong.

*   Because there is no one left to correct them, whatever Darius teaches **becomes the law.**
*   The **"Nordson Greeting"** is likely a chaotic, beautiful blend: A Northern Huddle that ends in a Western Hand-clasp, interrupted by a Southeastern Hand-slap. 
*   It is a **living creole of touch.** It represents a family that isn't just one faction, but is the living museum of the whole species.

### **Scene: The Lesson of the Western Clasp**

**(Setting: The Training Grounds. Darius is trying to teach Junior the difference between a Northern Huddle and a Western Clasp.)**

"Now, Godricson," Darius rumbled, his mustache twitching with pedagogical intensity. "Imagine your uncle Kong is a Westerner. He’s a pioneer. He doesn't want a kiss on the forehead—that’s for family. He wants to know you can hold your own."

Darius grabbed Junior’s forearm. His grip was like a vice, the raw Gojira power vibrating through his skin.

"The Tail-clasp," Darius explained. "In the West, they’d lock tails to see whose core was hotter. In this form? We lock arms. You don't squeeze to hurt; you squeeze to say, 'I am solid. I am here.'"

Junior mimicked the grip, his white hair falling into his golden eyes. He adjusted his stance, grounding himself. He squeezed back.

"Like this, Papa?"

Darius’s face lit up with a Showa-sized grin. "Aye! That’s it! You’ve got the Western fire in you!" He then immediately pulled Junior into a Northern Huddle, sniffing his neck loudly. "But don't you forget the North, squirt! We’re the ones who keep the furnace running!"

Junior chuckled, leaning into the familiar scent of his grandfather—petrichor and ozone. "I ken, Papa. I ken."

Godric watched from the shadows of the entrance, his arms crossed. He felt a phantom weight where his tail used to be, a memory of a clasp he never got to have with his father. But as he watched his son and his father laugh together, the weight didn't feel like a burden anymore. It felt like a foundation.
