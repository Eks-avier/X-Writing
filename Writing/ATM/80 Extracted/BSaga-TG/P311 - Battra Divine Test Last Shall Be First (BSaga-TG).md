# Battra Divine Test Last Shall Be First
> **Source:** `Formatted_With_Thoughts - Branch of The Saga of the _Titanus gojira_.md` — Prompt 311

## Your Notes

Before I dive into the dynamic between the `gojira` and `mosura`. I would like to point out that below is why Mothra and Battra's dynamic is the way it is:

>So the last will be the first, and the first will be last.

Battra was a "bonus" from the Creator. He wasn't intended, but he was still there so that Mothra, the Last Inheritor, would never feel lonely. Unlike Godzilla, who would definitely be the last of his kind, Mothra and Battra shall have each other for eternity. However, for Battra, it was a test of humility, which he... consistently failed throughout the ages. Battra wasn't aware of the Soul or that Mothra had been chosen at first. He functioned exactly as intended during their youth: the protective older brother. It's only after their first imago form that Battra realized there was something... off about his sister, and simultaneously, he felt as though he was "lacking" something. Due to his hubris, he had rendered not just his sister lonely, but himself alone as well. It is a powerful lesson in how pride and envy can inflict pain on the people we love.

## Analysis

This is a profound theological and psychological layering that elevates Battra from a simple "evil twin" into a figure of **Biblical tragedy.** You have effectively written him as the **Prodigal Son** who never left home, but instead burned the house down because he wasn't given the fatted calf.

### **I. The Design of the Creator: The Gift of Brotherhood**

This re-contextualizes Battra's entire existence. He was not an accident; he was a **mercy.**

*   **Godzilla's Fate:** To be the lonely King. A singular point of gravity.
*   **Mothra's Fate:** To be the Inheritor. A heavy, singular burden of divinity.
*   **Battra's Purpose:** To be the **Brother.**
    *   God saw that the Inheritor would be lonely, so He created a twin. Battra's sole cosmic function was to ensure she never had to bear the weight alone.
    *   **The Tragedy:** Being "The Brother" was a noble, essential calling. But Battra viewed it as being "The Spare." He rejected the gift of companionship because he coveted the gift of authority.

### **II. The Metamorphosis: The Moment of Division**

*   **Larval Stage (Ignorance is Bliss):** As larvae, they were equals. Battra could be the Big Brother. He fulfilled his purpose perfectly.
*   **Imago Stage (The Revelation of the Soul):** When they emerged from their cocoons, the **Divine Soul** within Mothra "woke up."
    *   **The "Off" Feeling:** Battra looked at his twin and saw a light that blinded him. He looked inside himself and saw... just Battra.

### **III. Sorcery as Overcompensation**

*   **Filling the Void:** If Heaven wouldn't fill his soul with Divine Light, he would fill it himself with **Primus.**
*   **The Tower of Babel:** His pursuit of sorcery is a desperate, 250-million-year attempt to build a tower tall enough to look his sister in the eye.

### **IV. The Ultimate Irony: The Lonely Family**

*   **Godzilla:** Destined to be alone, yet ends up surrounded by a massive, loving family.
*   **Battra:** Destined to have a companion, yet ends up exiled in space, utterly alone.

By failing the test of humility, Battra inverted his own destiny.
