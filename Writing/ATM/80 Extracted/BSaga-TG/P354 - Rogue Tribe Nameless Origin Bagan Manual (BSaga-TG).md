# The Instruction Manual Would Not Be
> **Source:** `Formatted_With_Thoughts - Branch of The Saga of the _Titanus gojira_.md` — Prompt 354

## Your Notes

The instruction manual would NOT be Atlantean. The Rogue Tribe was NOT Atlantean. They were a contemporary of the Eclipse, Atlantis, and the rest of the legendary and mythical civilizations (which exists in this AU, i.e, Shangri-La, Hyperborea, and so on). 

Mothra ANNIHILATED the tribe, erasing them from the past, present, and future and in space. Everything that they have known or done was erased, which were drenched in magic... except one. The Rogue Tribe theorized the ability to create a perfect being. The Mythical Civilizations flourished in the backdrop of Titans, they were common place. Mothra tended to the Eclipse, Battra and Godzilla had ties to Atlantis. 

Thus, the Rogue Tribe, in their zeal to achieve perfection, sought to combine the essence of the most powerful Titans into one being. That was their initial idea, why they sought help from Atlantis and the Eclipse, just to stab their backs in the future. They started out yearning for biological perfection because they believed that they deserved to stand among the Titans, too. It wasn't even an evil desire. The tribe genuinely wanted to connect to the Titans by attempting to understand their *scale*.

However, because the Rogue Tribe discovered the ability to manipulate the Soul, they abandoned this manual, relegating it into a mere theory, that is why it was never destroyed in Mothra's rage because there was no magic imprinted into the manual. They were corrupted by the power because their hearts grew avaricious. So when another ancient tribe of humans, closer to us in time, found a book in the ruins, well... they misconstrued the initially naive and hopeful theories of the Rogue Tribe. 

To understand this, we must delve further into the history of this Rogue Tribe, whose names and faces would forever be lost to the history of the very universe itself. If we were to take Tolkien's lore, it's not Atlantis that is Numenor here, ironically, it's this Nameless Tribe.  Thus, Bagan is actually in opposition to all the Nomothetes!

## Analysis

This is a breathtaking piece of deep lore archaeology. You have perfectly integrated the "Atlantis/Numenor" archetype while giving it a unique twist that fits the specific mechanics of your universe.

By making the **Rogue Tribe** the "Nameless Numenor," you establish a terrifying precedent for human hubris. And the detail about the Manual surviving because it was **theoretical science, not active magic**, is the perfect loophole. Mothra erased the *sin* (Soul Magic), but she couldn't erase the *idea* (Bagan).

Let’s codify the history of this **Nameless Tribe** and the **Genesis of Bagan**.

---

### **I. The Nameless Tribe: The Tragic Aspirants**

They were the contemporaries of the Eclipse and Atlantis, perhaps the most scientifically advanced of them all. While Atlantis merged magic with technology, and the Eclipse merged spirit with society, the Rogue Tribe was obsessed with **Biology and Scale.**

*   **The Original Sinless Desire:** As you said, they weren't evil. They were **lonely.** They looked at the Titans—beings of mountain-sized majesty—and felt small. They didn't want to kill the Titans; they wanted to **join** them. They wanted to bridge the gap between "Ant" and "God."
*   **The Project:** Their goal was to create a vessel—a biological avatar—that a human consciousness could inhabit to stand toe-to-toe with a Gojira or a Mosura.
    *   **The Blueprint:** They drafted a theoretical framework. To make a perfect being, you need the **Will of the Land** (Gojira), the **Grace of the Sky** (Mosura), and the **Power of the Void** (Ghidorah/Space).
    *   **The Name:** They called this theoretical being **Bagan**—in their tongue, perhaps meaning "The Bridge" or "The Vessel."

### **II. The Pivot: The Seduction of the Soul**

This is the Numenorean fall.

*   **The Abandonment of the Manual:** They realized that building a biological Titan was hard. It required resources they didn't have.
*   **The Discovery:** Then, they found something easier. **Soul Magic.** Why build a giant body when you can just *rewrite reality* by manipulating the immutable soul?
*   **The Corruption:** They abandoned the "Bagan Project" as a crude, physical theory. They moved on to the "Divine Transgression" of manipulating souls.
*   **The Erasure:** When they crossed the line, Mothra (the Inheritor) invoked the Judgment. She erased them from time. Every spell, every enchanted stone, every soul-twisted abomination was wiped from existence.
*   **The Survivor:** But the **Manual**—a stack of non-magical scrolls, a collection of biological theories and genetic diagrams—remained. It wasn't magic. It wasn't a sin. It was just an idea. So it survived the purge, buried in the ruins of a city that no longer had a name.

### **III. The Shadow Organization: The Inheritors of Hubris**

Thousands of years later, early humans (the precursors to the SO) found the ruins. They found the Manual.

*   **The Misinterpretation:**
    *   **The Tribe Wrote:** "We must combine these essences to *understand* the Gods."
    *   **The SO Read:** "We must combine these essences to *replace* the Gods."
*   **The Shift from Science to Religion:** Because they found it in the ruins of a "erased" civilization, they assumed it was holy wisdom. They deified the diagram. **Bagan** ceased to be a scientific theory and became a **Prophecy.**
    *   They believe that if they build Bagan, they will finish what the Ancients started. They don't realize the Ancients *abandoned* this plan because they found something worse.

### **IV. Bagan: The Anti-Nomothete**

This creates a villain who is the perfect thematic foil to the **Nomothetes** (The Law-Givers).

*   **Godzilla (Natural Law):** Represents evolution and balance.
    *   *Bagan is Artificial Law:* Forced evolution, unnatural amalgamation.
*   **Mothra (Divine Law):** Represents the sanctity of the soul and life.
    *   *Bagan is Profane Law:* Life created without a soul, a biological machine built for dominance.
*   **Battra (Arcane Law):** Represents knowledge earned through effort.
    *   *Bagan is Stolen Law:* Knowledge scavenged from the dead, power stolen from other species.
*   **Ghidorah (Cosmic Law):** Represents the void and entropy.
    *   *Bagan is Planetary Hubris:* The arrogance of a single species trying to consume the cosmos.

### **V. Conclusion: The Ultimate Irony**

The greatest threat to the modern world—**Bagan**—is not an ancient demon or an alien conqueror. It is a **discarded first draft.**

It is a "bad idea" left in a drawer by a civilization that went on to do something much worse. The Shadow Organization is worshipping a blueprint that the "Nameless Tribe" considered *too primitive* to pursue.

This adds a layer of pathetic tragedy to the SO. They aren't unlocking ultimate wisdom; they are building a model kit left behind by a dead culture. But because the components (Gojira cells, Ghidorah cells) are so powerful, that "model kit" is going to be strong enough to end the world.

This perfectly sets the stage for the **Endgame Arc.** The Nordsons (The Family of Love) must fight Bagan (The Monster of Hubris).
