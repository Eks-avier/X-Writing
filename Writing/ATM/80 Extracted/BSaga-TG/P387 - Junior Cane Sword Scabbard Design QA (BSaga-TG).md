# Here Are Some Expansions That Might
> **Source:** `Formatted_With_Thoughts - Branch of The Saga of the _Titanus gojira_.md` — Prompt 387

## Your Notes

Here are some expansions that might need some input: 

Q: Does Junior uses an entirely different sword for his cane-sword?
A: No. LOL. He designed a different cane for each of his daily drivers. They're basically custom-designed scabbards, built to bash people on its own and house his chosen weapon at the same time..

Q: Why would Junior design several different—
A: It's Junior. He's a nerd. Why would he refuse to use the best tool for the job? He made several swords for several different situations. Why the hell would he use a generic sword for his cane?

Q: Would Junior use his larger swords as a cane? Does his greatswords have their own cane-scabbards?
A: Yes. But, at that point, if he brought them out, then hiding them in a cane is pointless. Still, he made canes for them anyway. It's for symmetry, of course. Would he use them in public? No. No he won't.

Q: Are his scabbards just canes?
A: No. LOL. He has the proper scabbards, and he has the cane-scabbards. Yes, he has multiple scabbards for the same sword.

Q: How does Junior explain why he needs a cane?
A: No. He just handwaves it off as the fact that he's a prince and that he's old fashioned. It makes him look like someone from the 17th-19th centuries. He's just being a gentleman.

## Analysis

This is a fantastic expansion that leans perfectly into Junior's **"Warrior-Scholar"** and **"Perfectionist Craftsman"** archetypes. It adds a layer of obsessive detail to his character that is both impressive and slightly hilarious.

It turns out Junior isn't just a master swordsman; he is a **Gear-Head.**

Here is how these details flesh out his profile and the logic of his arsenal.

### **1. The Completionist Craftsman: "Because I Can"**

The fact that he made a cane-scabbard for the **Zweihänder ("Sirius")** even though he will *never* use it in public is the ultimate insight into his psychology.

*   **The Logic of Symmetry:** To Junior, a collection isn't complete unless every piece has the full kit. Leaving "Sirius" without a cane mode would feel like an itch he couldn't scratch. It is **Compulsive Craftsmanship.**
*   **The Visual:** Imagine a "cane" that is essentially a 6-foot-tall, leather-wrapped pillar. It sits in the corner of his room, a monument to his stubborn thoroughness.
    *   *Leo:* "Bro, that's a tree trunk."
    *   *Junior:* "It is a concealable scabbard for a two-handed anti-material blade. It is *elegant*."

### **2. The "Bash-First" Doctrine: The Scabbard as a Weapon**

Since the canes are custom-designed "to bash people on their own," Junior effectively has **two weapons** in his hand at all times.

*   **The "Peaceful" Option:** He doesn't have to draw the blade to win a fight. He can use the sheathed cane as a **bo staff, a club, or a escrima stick.**
*   **The Material:** These scabbards aren't wood; they are likely high-density polymer or hollowed-out Titan-bone wrapped in **hardened white silk.** They are indestructible. A strike from the scabbard alone (especially with **Magnification Scales** active) would shatter a normal weapon.
*   **The Reveal:** Drawing the sword becomes an escalation. If the steel comes out, it means the "Gentleman" has left the building and the "Vanguard" has arrived.

### **3. The Gentleman's Excuse: Weaponized Charisma**

His refusal to fake a limp is perfect. Faking an injury would be inauthentic. Instead, he uses **Social Engineering.**

*   **The "Prince" Card:** By simply saying, "I prefer the aesthetic," or "It suits my mood," he shuts down inquiry. He relies on the **Uncanny Perfection** and his family's reputation.
*   **The Perception:** People see a handsome, wealthy, slightly eccentric young elite carrying a walking stick. They don't think "Weapon"; they think "Fashion Statement." He hides the danger in plain sight by wrapping it in **Style.**

### **4. The Tactical Wardrobe**

This implies that when Junior gets dressed for an event, he is making a tactical loadout choice based on the "Cane" he grabs.

*   **Gala Event?** He takes the **"Antares" (Rapier) Cane.** Slim, elegant, black lacquer with silver accents. Matches the tux.
*   **Business Meeting in a Rough Area?** He takes the **"Polaris" (Arming Sword) Cane.** Thicker, sturdier, looks like a heavy walking stick. Good for parrying.
*   **Visiting a Foreign Dignitary?** He takes the **"Altair" (Jian) Cane.** Ornate, diplomatic, culturally respectful.

It’s a brilliant way to integrate his swordsmanship into his daily life without breaking the "Masquerade." He is never unarmed, but he is always impeccable.
