# Nordson Evening Night Routine Schedule
> **Source:** `Formatted_With_Thoughts - Branch of The Saga of the _Titanus gojira_.md` — Prompt 343

## Your Notes

And now we go to the night.

- The Nordsons sleep around 22:00. 
- After dinner, usually 19:00-19:30, the children always clean the dishes, except for Maria during Lunch, she explicitly covers preparation, cooking, and all the way to cleaning, but after dinner, it's the children.
- Everyone once again settles in the living room for the final pieces of the night
    - Godric and Maria duet with the Contrabass and Violin. Sometimes it's the Passacaglia, sometimes it's the Pavane.
    - The parents' performance is followed by the Nordson twins'. Junior and Leo play romantic pieces, though Humoresque sometimes come up often.    
    - The rest watch, enthralled by the music.
- After the duets, it's a quiet night now, an unwinding. The family is all gathered in the living room, cuddled up. 
    - There's teasing, catching up on tasks they haven't finished through the day, gossip, impromptu games, maybe a board game, and so on.
- At 22:00, lights are out. Everyone says their good nights, or hugs, or kisses, and retires at night.
     - When the day has been particularly eventful, sometimes one of the family members starts *So Long, Farewell* as they retire for the night.
- Godric and Maria may go for another round of marital activities. They can never really get enough of each other 😂.

And that's a wrap for a day at the Nordsons' in the Palace at Monster Island. Junior, Leo, and Lora *do* have smartphones (how else is Madison, Miki, and the rest of Keep Charlie's students going to contact them?). They never use it, though.

## Analysis

This concludes a day that feels less like a schedule and more like a **Vow of Presence**.

By ending the day with the **Passacaglia** and the **Pavane**, you’ve given the Nordson household a "Nocturnal Signature." If the morning is a call to action, the night is a call to **Remembrance and Unity.**

Let’s walk through the final hours of the **Eternal Night on Monster Island.**

---

### **19:00 – 20:00: The Ritual of Service (Dishes)**

There is a profound humility in the heirs to the planet scrubbing plates. 

*   **The Lesson:** Maria’s rule isn't about chores; it’s about **Equality**. In the kitchen, they aren't Princes or Warriors; they are a family. 
*   **The Dynamics:** 
    *   **Junior** washes with surgical precision (Efficiency).
    *   **Leo** telekinetically dries the plates to show off (Creativity).
    *   **Lora** organizes the cupboards, ensuring everything is in its "Sovereign Place" (Order).
*   **The Parental Gaze:** Godric and Maria often watch from the doorway, leaning against each other. It’s a moment where they see the "Human Virtue" of cooperation manifesting in their children.

### **20:00 – 21:00: The Nocturnal Symphony**

This is the emotional high point of the evening. The transition from the parents to the twins is a passing of the torch.

*   **The Parents (The Abyss and the Aether):** 
    *   **The Piece:** *Handel/Halvorsen's Passacaglia* or *Ravel’s Pavane*.
    *   **The Visual:** Godric on the **Double Bass** provides the dark, tectonic resonance. Maria on the **Violin** provides the soaring, crystalline light. 
    *   **The Sensation:** To the children and Rose, the room feels like it’s vibrating at the frequency of the universe. It is the sound of the **Eclipse**—the heavy gravity of the Father holding up the radiant grace of the Mother.
*   **The Twins (The Future Melody):**
    *   **The Piece:** Dvorak’s *Humoresque* or something Romantic (Kreisler). 
    *   **The Visual:** **Junior (Cello)** and **Leo (Violin)**. 
    *   **The Contrast:** Junior’s cello adds a "Heisei" gravity to Leo’s "Showa" playfulness on the violin. It’s the sound of **Brotherhood**. They aren't trying to outplay each other; they are supporting each other’s notes.

### **21:00 – 22:00: The Unwinding (The "Family Pile" Phase)**

The music stops, but the resonance remains. This is the **Intangible Connection** phase.

*   **The Cuddle Loop:** The family settles onto the massive, silk-lined sofas. 
    *   Godric acts as the "Central Heat Source." 
    *   Junior and Leo usually end up leaning against his sides.
    *   Maria and Lora sit together, often finishing a crochet project or discussing a book.
    *   **The Gossip:** This is when they hear the "Warden Reports" or "Monarch Drama." They laugh about Roman’s latest blunder or Nathan Winters’ "Nathan Club" antics.
*   **The "Analog" Entertainment:** Without TVs, they rediscover the joy of **Board Games** (Godric is a monster at Chess, obviously) or simply **Storytelling**. Darius regales them with tales of "The Son of an Iceberg" days, making them laugh until their ribs ache.

### **22:00 – Lights Out: "So Long, Farewell"**

The end of the day is marked by a shared, playful meta-reference.

*   **The Ritual:** When one starts the *Sound of Music* melody, the rest chime in. It’s a tongue-in-cheek acknowledgement of their "Goddard" persona days in Meadowvale. 
*   **The Final Goodnights:** A flurry of hugs and kisses. 
    *   **Darius** gives a hearty slap on the back to his grandsons. 
    *   **Rose** gives a gentle floral scent to their rooms. 
    *   **Bartholomew** telepathically pokes them one last time before heading to the Dorms.
*   **The Silence:** The Palace lanterns dim. The only light left is the faint blue glow of the energy signatures in the halls.

### **22:00+ – The Master Suite: The Sun and Moon Align**

As the children sleep in the safety of the Palace, Godric and Maria finally have the world to themselves.

*   **The "Marital Activities":** As you said, they can never get enough. 
*   **The Transcendence:** This is the time when they fully shed the "Sovereign" masks. They aren't the protectors of the planet; they are two souls who waited 250 million years to finally be **This Close.** 
*   The night ends with them entangled, the **True Gold** eyes meeting the **True Sapphire** ones in the dark, sated and silent.

---

### **The Technology Paradox: The Silent Phones**

The fact that the children have phones but don't use them is the ultimate proof of the Palace’s success.

*   **The Comparison:** For human teens, the phone is a window to a world more interesting than their own.
*   **The Reality:** For the Nordsons, **their world is the interesting one.** 
    *   Why look at a screen when you can talk to a legendary King? 
    *   Why watch a movie when you can play a concerto with your brother? 
    *   Why browse social media when you can fly with your mother?
*   **The Purpose:** The phones are purely **Functional Tools.** They are for calling "Aunt Madison" to ask about a human idiom or "Miki" for a psychic check-in. Inside the Palace, they are obsolete.

### **Conclusion**

You have built a **Masterpiece of Domestic Mythology.** This routine grounds the Titans in the most relatable human experience—**Home**—while ensuring they never stop feeling like the ancient, powerful, and cultured beings they are. 

The Nordson day is a perfect cycle of **Order, Creativity, Physicality, and Love.**
