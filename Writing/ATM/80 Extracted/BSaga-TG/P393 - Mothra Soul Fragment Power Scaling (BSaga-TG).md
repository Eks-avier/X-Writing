## Analysis

This scaling is **crucial**. It establishes the "Power Ceiling" of your universe and validates the title of **Nomothete.**

The fact that Scylla or Rodan (the absolute peak of the "Normal" Titans) can only realistically threaten Mothra when she is operating at nearly **half-capacity (4/7th)** proves just how terrifying the gap is between the **Law-Givers (Nomothetes)** and the **Law-Abiders (Wardens/Betas).**

Let’s codify this **"Ladder of Grace"** and the terrifying nature of the **Heavenly Instance.**

---

### **I. The Scaling of the Soul: The Variable Goddess**

Mothra’s power isn't static; it’s a **Resource Management Game.** She is constantly debating: *"Do I recall a fragment to win this fight easily, or do I leave it there to protect the island/timeline?"*

#### **1. The Contested Zone (4/7th Soul)**
*   **Status:** **The Baseline Deity.**
*   **The Dynamic:** She is the "2nd Strongest," but the gap is bridgeable by skill or elemental advantage.
    *   **Scylla/Rodan/Leviathan:** They can push her. They can hurt her. In a straight brawl, if she isn't careful, a focused Scylla or a raging Rodan *could* take the win (Mid-Diff).
    *   **Why she stays here:** This is likely her standard "War Mode" because it leaves 3 fragments active (Monster Island, Birth Island, and perhaps one other contingency). It balances personal safety with global responsibility.

#### **2. The Untouchable Zone (5/7th Soul)**
*   **Status:** **The Ascendant Queen.**
*   **The Dynamic:** The gap widens. The Wardens go from "Rivals" to "Nuisances."
    *   **The Fight:** It would take a coordinated effort of *multiple* Wardens (e.g., The Serpent Club combined) to push her to Extreme-Diff. A single Warden loses 10/10 times.
    *   **The Capability:** This is where her Advanced Scale Properties (Intangibility/Teleportation) become fluid and spammable.

#### **3. The "I Am That I Am" Zone (6/7th Soul)**
*   **Status:** **The Event Horizon.**
*   **The Dynamic:** **Neg-Diff.** This is the "Lore Accurate" Mothra.
    *   **The Fight:** It stops being a battle and becomes an execution. She can silence Rodan’s volcano, shatter Scylla’s ice, and pin Anguirus to the floor with a thought. 99% of the cast cannot even perceive her movements.
    *   **The Context:** She only goes here for **Existential Threats** (like Gigan).

#### **4. The Solar Equal (7/7th Soul)**
*   **Status:** **The Peer of the King.**
*   **The Dynamic:** **Godzilla = Mothra.**
    *   She is one of the only three beings in existence (alongside Battra and Ghidorah) who can kill Godzilla.
    *   **Stalemate:** Their fight would destroy the planet before a winner was decided. This explains why Godzilla respects her "Veto." He knows that if she recalls all her souls, she isn't a subordinate; she is his mirror.

---

### **II. The Heavenly Instance: The Override Switch**

This distinction is vital. **7/7th Mothra is Maria.** She is a person, a wife, a mother.

**The Heavenly Instance is a Cosmic Protocol.**

*   **Not a Power Level:** It isn't "8/7th." It is a fundamental shift in the nature of reality.
*   **The Trigger:** The "End of the Plan." The total destruction of Earth/Humanity.
*   **The Agency:** Maria isn't driving anymore. The Angel is.
*   **The Power:** It ignores "Stats." It opens Gates to Heaven. It rewrites laws. It is the "Admin Console" of the universe.
    *   *Godzilla vs. The Instance:* Godzilla might be the Strongest Organism, but the Instance is the **Author of the Environment.** You can't punch a miracle.

---

### **III. The Hierarchy of Threats: The "Big Four"**

You’ve solidified the **Nomothete Class.**

1.  **Godzilla:** The Physical Absolute. (Cannot be broken).
2.  **Mothra:** The Spiritual Absolute. (Cannot be silenced).
3.  **Battra:** The Intellectual Absolute. (Cannot be outsmarted).
4.  **Ghidorah:** The Void Absolute. (Cannot be filled).

**Everyone else—Kong, Scylla, Rodan—is fighting for 5th place.**

This explains the awe and terror the Wardens feel. They are the strongest *creatures* on Earth, but the Nomothetes are the *Forces* that govern them.

**This scale is perfect.** It justifies the politics (Godzilla listening to Mothra), the stakes (Battra needing a Black Hole to win), and the tragedy (Mothra having to weaken herself to protect the world).

We are now fully equipped to finalize the profiles. The power scaling is locked.
