### **I. The Grandparents: The Tragic Mutants**

They were the anomalies of their time, beings who possessed power that their bodies or destinies could barely contain.

*   **Astraea (The Nuclear Mutant):**
    *   **The Mutation:** A hyperactive heart (The Biological Reactor).
    *   **The Power:** **Nuclear Fusion.** The crushing of atoms to create heat and light. It is **Internal, Physical, and Volatile.**
    *   **The Sacrifice:** She gave her life to save the *vessel* of the future (Godzilla).
*   **The 32nd Inheritor (The Prismatic Mutant):**
    *   **The Mutation:** Prismatic Scales + Divine Vessel (The Metaphysical Prism).
    *   **The Power:** **Conceptual Fusion (Integration).** The blending of realities to create wholeness. It is **External, Spiritual, and Harmonious.**
    *   **The Sacrifice:** He gave his life to pass on the *soul* of the future (Mothra).

### **II. Junior: The Burning Vanguard**

Junior inherits the **Astraea Line.** But because he has Dagon’s durability and Godric’s discipline, he perfects what killed his grandmother.

*   **Burning Atomic Amplification:**
    *   **The Mechanic:** Instead of letting the Burning Form explode outward (like Godric’s 2019 Pulse), Junior uses his **Magnification** and **AA** to keep the fusion reaction *tightly contained* against his skin or within his blade.
    *   **The "White-Hot" Knight:** He doesn't turn into a walking bomb; he turns into a **Star compressed into the shape of a man.** His "Royal Armor" becomes a layer of nuclear plasma.
    *   **Thematic Resolution:** He proves that the "Overactive Heart" is not a curse of death, but a limitless engine of protection. He achieves **Stable Fusion.**

### **III. Leo: The Eternal Adventurer**

Leo inherits the **32nd Inheritor Line.** But because he has Gojira grit and the "Will to Explore," he grounds the ethereal power of the Eternal into the physical world.

*   **Integrative Scales (The Eternal State):**

    *   **The "Rainbow" Bridge:** If he uses Integration on himself and his brother, he creates the ultimate tag-team attack. He fuses their energies into a single, unstoppable force.
    *   **Thematic Resolution:** He proves that the "Divine Soul" doesn't have to leave the earth to find perfection. He brings perfection *down* to the mud and the blood of the fight. He achieves **Applied Eternity.**

When Junior (Burning AA) and Leo (Integrative Scales) fight together, they are re-enacting the creation of the universe.

*   **Junior provides the Energy (Fusion).**
*   **Leo provides the Unity (Integration).**
