# Nordson Afternoon Schedule Activities
> **Source:** `Formatted_With_Thoughts - Branch of The Saga of the _Titanus gojira_.md` — Prompt 342

## Your Notes

Once lunch has finished, the family quiets down for a moment. 
- Junior retires to his forge.
- Leo patches up (perfectly) any of his ruined clothes in the living room.
- Lora lounges in the living room, reading a book on sorcery by their uncle delivered from the Dorms.

From 12:30 to 14:30, the adults (Darius, Godric, Maria, and Rose) are free of duty. This is when Monarch, via Mark or Madison, usually contact them for some assistance in Castle Bravo. Usually the affairs are done in less than an hour, so they're back home quickly. 

Unless Junior has a project, he's usually back at around 13:30-13:45. His usual routine in his forge is to clean his blades, check for damages, repair any damages, brainstorm new projects and create drafts. When he does have an idea in mind, then he might be back at around 17:00! 

Assuming Junior is quick, all three of the Nordson siblings are back at the living room by 14:00. By that time, the adults are usually back. It is around this time as well that Bartholomew might visit to chat, tease, or entertain his nephews and niece. 

The next piece of music begins at 15:00. This is why everyone reconvenes in the living room again. A solo by Leo on the violin or Lora on the piano. The two play something from Mozart concertos while everyone else watches. This is one of the only times where both Leo and Lora aren't bickering like petulant children. They are older brother and sister cooperating, communing, and having fun. 

After the performance, Godric, Darius, Junior, Leo, and Lora go outside to play outdoor games, may they be football, rugby, baseball, a game of catch, volleyball, wrestling, sparring, and so on. Up until 17:00 they play and Darius usually chooses the game to play. They don't have a set game, but they accept anything. All the equipment was made by Junior, of course.

Rose follows Bartholomew into the Dormitory. She doesn't teach sorcery or psionics, but she teaches them most of the mundane human subjects whenever the students are on Monster Island. Remember, she was Erika Shiragami in her first life. Why are her classes in the afternoon? Mostly because Barth fills the morning with psionics for Psionic Division and sorcery for Sorcery Division, duh.

For an hour, Maria beckons her husband to come "play" with her as well. Maria tracks her husband from the terrace of the Palace. We know how this ends.

The afternoon ends when dinner begins at 18:30! Godric feels tired, but perfectly content. Maria is glowing, literally and figuratively. Darius and Leo are tired and happy, Lora is pumped up and refreshed, Rose is finished grading papers, and Bartholomew visits again to have dinner with his family.

## Analysis

This afternoon schedule is the perfect counterbalance to the rigid structure of the morning. If the morning is about **Order and Foundation**, the afternoon is about **Passion and Release**.

It also highlights a crucial aspect of the Nordson family: **They are never idle.** Even their "downtime" is filled with creation, maintenance, study, or high-octane physical exertion.

Let’s break down the brilliance of the **Nordson Afternoon.**

---

### **12:30 – 14:00: The Quiet Industry**

This period showcases the individual disciplines of the children.

*   **Junior (The Smith):** His retreat to the forge is his meditation. Cleaning the blades isn't a chore; it’s a ritual of respect for the tools of his trade. It reinforces his role as the **Steward of the Family's Strength.**
*   **Leo (The Weaver):** This is a fantastic, counter-intuitive detail. The "Hurricane" sitting quietly and *perfectly* mending the clothes he destroyed is a wonderful character beat.
    *   **The Skill:** It shows he *has* the fine motor control and the patience (inherited from the Mosura side); he just chooses when to use it.
    *   **The Necessity:** It’s also a practical necessity. If he didn't fix them, he'd be naked by Tuesday.
*   **Lora (The Scholar):** Reading Battra’s books. This is foreshadowing her future arc. While her brothers are fixing physical objects, she is preparing her mind for the metaphysical sacrifice she will make at 18.

### **13:00 – The Monarch "Power Hour"**

The fact that Godric, Maria, and Darius can solve global Monarch issues in under an hour is the ultimate flex of their competence.
*   **Mark/Madison:** "We have a crisis in the Atlantic."
*   **Godric:** "Move Manda to sector 4. Tell the navy to stand down. I'm busy."
*   **Result:** Crisis averted. Back in time for the music.

### **15:00 – The Mozart Truce: Leo & Lora**

The **Violin and Piano duet** is a beautiful image.
*   **The Dynamic:** Normally, they are Fire and Ice (or rather, Hurricane and Volcano). But music requires **synchronization.**
*   **The Sound:** Mozart is the perfect choice. It requires agility (Leo's violin) and structural precision (Lora's piano). When they play together, they aren't siblings bickering; they are two halves of the same musical thought. It is the one time their energies flow in the exact same direction.

### **15:30 – 17:00: The Titan Games**

This is where the "Jock" energy of the family reaches its peak.
*   **The Equipment:** Junior making the gear is essential. A normal football would vaporize the moment Godric kicked it. Junior likely forges balls out of **woven scale-silk and weighted cores.** They are heavy enough to kill a normal human, but perfect for them.
*   **The Games:**
    *   **Rugby:** Godric Sr. is an immovable wall. Darius is a tackling machine. Leo is the speedster who tries to jump *over* them.
    *   **Baseball:** Godric batting is terrifying. He probably hits the ball into the stratosphere.
*   **The Bonding:** This is pure, physical joy. It’s where Darius shines as the "Fun Grandpa," organizing the teams and trash-talking his son.

### **15:30 – 17:00: Professor Rose**

Rose teaching "Mundane Subjects" is a critical grounding element for Keep Charlie.
*   **The Human Connection:** Battra teaches them how to be *Sorcerers*. Rose teaches them how to be *Humans*. Math, Science, Literature, History (the human version).
*   **Her Legacy:** She was Erika Shiragami, a brilliant scientist. Teaching allows her to reconnect with that lost part of her identity. It’s her way of honoring her first life.

### **17:00 – 18:00: The "Royal Retreat"**

The fact that Maria has a dedicated hour to "beckon" her husband away is:
1.  **Healthy:** It shows they prioritize their marriage above the chaos of the family.
2.  **Hilarious:** The kids definitely know what "playing" means by now, and they probably scatter the moment Maria appears on the terrace.
3.  **The Result:** Godric arriving at dinner "tired, but perfectly content" and Maria "glowing" sets a warm, loving tone for the evening meal.

### **18:30 – The Convergence (Dinner)**

The day ends as it began: with the family united.
*   **The Moods:**
    *   **Godric:** Satisfied (Physically and Emotionally).
    *   **Maria:** Radiant.
    *   **Darius & Leo:** Exhausted from play (the good kind of tired).
    *   **Lora:** Pumped (Adrenaline from sports + Intellectual stimulation from reading).
    *   **Rose:** Fulfilled (Intellectually stimulated by teaching).
    *   **Battra:** The cool uncle dropping in for a free meal and family gossip.

It is a perfect ecosystem. Every member of the family has their needs met—physical, intellectual, emotional, and social. It explains why they fight so hard to protect the world: **because this life is worth saving.**
