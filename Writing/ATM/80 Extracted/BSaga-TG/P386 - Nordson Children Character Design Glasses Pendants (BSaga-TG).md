## Your Notes

Let's pull it back now, and discuss some character designs.

1. Junior would wear glasses, with quite the strong tint. Does he need them? NO. 
    - He has all of the `gojira` and `mosura` sensory capabilities we've discussed. He needs these glasses because no human has naturally golden irises. Unlike his beloved father, who has mastered suppressing his true eye color with a low-level energy current perpetually, Junior would rather not do it. Yes, he can replicate the feat itself, easily so with his energy efficiency, but he finds it emotionally draining to be inauthentic. He has not yet compartmentalized his identity, which Godric is proud of. 
    - Whenever he needs to remove his glasses, though, that's the time he makes an effort to shift to his blue eyes, especially in places where he's trying to blend in more.
    - When ever he needs a sword, he either brings a knife (though not the one he was gifted for his 18th birthday) or uses a cane that acts as a scabbard to hide one! It's less of a cane and more of a walking stick
2. Green eyes are not an odd human eye color. Rare, yes, but not impossible. Thus Leo does not need to cover his eyes. 
    - He does wear a pendant, which is Starfall minimized, but that's after his 18th birthday, though.
    - He also has his Ghogo who rides his back, but you're not bringing a furry dog-seal creature into a human city! So, prior to his 18th, he either wore a backpack. It makes him look like an adventurer with his pet!
3. Lora has sapphire-blue irises. She is the spitting image of her own mother. Thus, her eyes require no hiding. So, she freely wears nothing on her face. You'd think Lora would be all about that jewelry, but she actually just lets her silk dresses do the talking. She only has a headband made out of from pieces of Mothra's chitin that they found laying around in one of her layers. It serves as her tiara without looking like one. A princess, through and through.

All accessories, items, and trinkets are made of their own silk, their father's shed scales, their mother's shed chitin, and other  sourced materials from Monster Island or the Hollow Earth, like unique metals, produce from Titans, and so on.

## Analysis

This is the final polish on their visual identities. You’ve taken their psychological archetypes and translated them into **functional fashion accessories.**

These choices perfectly reinforce the "Social Camouflage" theme while highlighting their individual approaches to their heritage.

Let’s codify the **Nordson Accessories.**

---

### **1. Godric Jr. (Junior): The Scholar’s Shield**

*   **The Accessory:** **Heavily Tinted Glasses.**
    *   **The Design:** Likely aviators or a sharp, rectangular cut. The frames are undoubtedly forged by him from **Hollow Earth metal** or **Godric's shed dorsal material** (lightweight, indestructible).
    *   **The Psychology:** This is a profound character beat. He refuses to use the "Energy Mask" (turning eyes blue) because he values **Authenticity**. He would rather wear a physical mask (glasses) than alter his biological reality to fit in. It’s a quiet rebellion against the "performance" of humanity.
    *   **The Aesthetic:** It leans into the "Clark Kent" / "Incognito Prince" vibe. It adds a layer of mystery and separation between him and the world.
*   **The Weapon:** **The Cane-Sword (Shikomizue).**
    *   **The Design:** A gentleman’s walking stick. The shaft is likely made of **Ironwood from the Hollow Earth** or wrapped in a dense weave of his own **White Silk** (hardened).
    *   **The Function:** It fits his "Prince/Diplomat" aesthetic perfectly. It allows him to carry a blade into a gala or a boardroom without raising an alarm. It implies he is a "gentleman," masking the fact that he is the most dangerous swordsman on the planet.

### **2. Leo (Leonard): The Pack Mule of Adventure**

*   **The Accessory:** **The "Hiker's" Backpack.**
    *   **The Design:** Not a store-bought bag. This is woven from his own **Green Silk**, making it waterproof, fireproof, and virtually indestructible. It looks like a high-end travel pack, but it’s military-grade biological gear.
    *   **The Function:** It is the mobile home for **Boost (The Ghogo).** It has a special compartment for the creature, likely lined with lead or density-shifted silk to mask Boost’s energy signature.
    *   **The Aesthetic:** It sells the "Student on a Gap Year" or "Urban Ranger" look. It tells the world, *"I'm just passing through,"* which fits his nomadic soul perfectly.
*   **The Eyes:** The fact that he *doesn't* need to hide his green eyes is his "Free Pass." It reinforces his role as the one who moves most easily between worlds. He doesn't have the "burden of gold."

### **3. Lora (Rose): The Crown of Chitin**

*   **The Accessory:** **The Chitin Headband.**
    *   **The Material:** This is metal as hell. She is wearing a piece of her mother's **Titan exoskeleton.** It is organic, iridescent, and harder than diamond. To a human, it looks like an exquisite, avant-garde tiara made of mother-of-pearl or opal.
    *   **The Symbolism:** It is a literal **Crown.** She is the only one who wears her heritage on her head. It fits her "Warrior-Princess" motif—it is a piece of armor worn as jewelry.
*   **The Aesthetic:** No necklaces, no rings (except maybe eventually), no earrings. Just the dress and the crown. She relies on the **"Lepidiel Silhouette"**—the flow of fabric and the authority of her presence. She doesn't need to sparkle; she *is* the event.

---

### **Summary: The "Made" Family**

The fact that **everything** they wear is sourced from their own bodies or their parents' bodies (scales, silk, chitin, fins) adds a layer of extreme intimacy to their fashion.

*   **They are self-contained.** They don't rely on human manufacturing.
*   **They are armored.** Even in a tuxedo or a sundress, they are wearing bulletproof, energy-resistant biological plating.
*   **They are connected.** Junior wearing his father's fin-metal frames, Lora wearing her mother's chitin, Leo carrying the ancient symbiote in his own silk.

They walk through the human city of 2045 looking like high-society elites, but in reality, they are **Tribal Warriors wearing the spoils of their own biology.** It is the ultimate "Wolf in Sheep's Clothing"—or rather, **Gods in Gucci (but better).**

**This completes the visual design.** You have the lore, the looks, the psychology, and the history. You are ready to write the definitive profiles.
