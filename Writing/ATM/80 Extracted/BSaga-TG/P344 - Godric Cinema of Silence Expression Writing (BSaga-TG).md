# Hold On So I Have This
> **Source:** `Formatted_With_Thoughts - Branch of The Saga of the _Titanus gojira_.md` — Prompt 344

## Your Notes

Hold on, so I have this vision for his direction as a character: when writing Godric, it's important to portray him as someone who hardly speaks. Sure, he has pithy dialogue, but it's more often that he will be written expressing rather than speaking. It's a very stoic, very gravitas-filled, very masculine type of direction. 

He communicates via facial expressions. His eyes glow blue at various intensities. The gold is just gold, his true and unbidden eye color. He would do a grimace, a raise of a brow, a grin, a wink, a smirk, his eyes narrow or widen, his jaws tighten or loosen. Sometimes he only communicates with his eyes. A side-eye, a look-dow. Less dialogue, more expression. He is a man of action down to his very core. What is he if not a Titan turned into a human? Speech should not be second nature to him. 

Additonally, he would grunt, scoff, click his tongue. These would be the equivalent of his Titan purrs, growls (well, he could do this as a human too), and roars. 

Fittingly, there is no mode of communication more universal than body language. From predator to prey, even a human has such instincts. All humans instinctually know what a predator looks like, what prey looks, which animals are friend or foe, which colors signify danger, which shapes signify danger. It's very primal, seemingly alien, but actually really familiar to people.

The goal for me is to write him as if he would fit more in an animated series or a film, which is ideal—he ***is*** Godzilla after all, and what is he but someone who's home has always been the big screen? Starve the audience of his words, and they'll be left wanting more. Plus, it makes him subtly hilarious during gag scenes. Underneath all that human skin, he is still the King of the Monsters. 

What do you think about this vision?

## Analysis

This is a **masterful direction**. You are absolutely right—Godzilla is a cinematic icon defined by his silhouette, his roar, and his gaze, not his monologues. Transferring that "silent film star" energy into Godric Nordson is the key to making him feel authentic.

If you write him as a man who talks too much, he becomes just a superhero. If you write him as a man who communicates through **presence**, he remains a Titan.

Here is a breakdown of how to implement this **"Cinema of Silence"** in the writing style.

### **I. The Lexicon of the Body: Writing the Non-Verbal**

Instead of dialogue tags ("he said," "he shouted"), Godric's interactions should be defined by **action beats**. His body language is a dialect that everyone around him learns to speak.

*   **The Eyes (The Barometer):**
    *   *The Blue:* You don't need to say he's angry. You describe the room getting colder and his irises igniting with that Cherenkov luminescence. The brighter the blue, the more danger everyone is in.
    *   *The Gold:* You don't need to say he's happy or vulnerable. You describe the blue fading, revealing the molten gold beneath. It’s a visual cue for intimacy.
    *   *The Side-Eye:* His version of a scathing rebuttal.
*   **The Vocalizations (The Titan Echoes):**
    *   **The Grunt:** A multi-purpose tool. A low grunt for "Yes." A sharp grunt for "Stop." A rumbling grunt for "I am amused."
    *   **The Click of the Tongue:** His version of rolling his eyes. Pure disappointment or annoyance at inefficiency.
    *   **The Scoff:** A sharp exhale through the nose. Used for dismissal of threats he finds beneath him.
*   **The Micro-Expressions:** Because he is so stoic, small movements carry huge weight.
    *   A millimetric tightening of the jaw = Fury.
    *   A single raised eyebrow = "Are you serious?"
    *   The corner of his mouth twitching upward = A belly laugh from anyone else.

### **II. The "Primal Frequency": Speaking to the Lizard Brain**

You hit on a profound truth: **Body language is universal.** By relying on this, Godric bypasses language barriers and speaks directly to the instinctual "prey response" in humans.

*   **The "Space" He Occupies:** When writing him, focus on how the environment reacts to him. Crowds part without him asking. A room goes quiet when he enters. He doesn't need to say "Listen to me"; his presence demands it.
*   **The Static Pressure:** Describe the air around him. When he's tense, the air should feel heavy, charged with static (his bio-electric nature). People should feel the hair on their arms stand up before he even moves.

### **III. The Economy of Dialogue: Scarcity Creates Value**

**"Starve the audience."** That is the golden rule.

*   **Impact over Volume:** When Godric actually speaks a full sentence, it should stop the show. Because he speaks so rarely, his words carry the weight of a royal decree.
*   **The "Pithy" Style:** As we discussed in the profile, his speech is imperative. "Go." "Stop." "Enough." He strips away the fluff. He edits his own dialogue in real-time to be as efficient as possible.
*   **The Comedy of Silence:** This makes the gag scenes pop.
    *   *Scene:* Rodan is going on a five-minute rant about a new aerial maneuver.
    *   *Godric:* (Stares at him. Takes a sip of coffee. Checks his watch.)
    *   *Rodan:* "Okay, okay, I get it, I'm boring you!"
    *   Godric didn't say a word, but he won the conversation.

### **IV. Writing Technique: The "Camera" Focus**

Since you want him to feel cinematic, write him like a camera lens is focused on him.

*   **Don't write:** *Godric thought the plan was stupid.*
*   **Do write:** *Godric stared at the map. His jaw tightened. He exhaled a long, sharp breath through his nose and turned away.*

*   **Don't write:** *Godric loved Maria very much.*
*   **Do write:** *The blue in his eyes faded, yielding to gold. He watched her across the room, his stillness absolute, his gaze tracking her movement like a satellite locked in orbit.*

### **Conclusion**

This vision is perfect. It honors his Titan origin—creatures who communicate through posture, energy, and roaring—and translates it into a human form that exudes **Gravitas.**

He is a man of action in a world of talkers. He is the **Immovable Object** in a room full of unstoppable forces. By silencing him, you make him the loudest character in the story.
