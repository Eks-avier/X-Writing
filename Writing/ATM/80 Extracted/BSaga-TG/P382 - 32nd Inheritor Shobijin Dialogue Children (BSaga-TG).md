# A Conversation 250 Million Years Ago
> **Source:** `Formatted_With_Thoughts - Branch of The Saga of the _Titanus gojira_.md` — Prompt 382

## Your Notes

*A conversation, 250 million years ago*.

>Not you, too, Your Majesty! Is it your time now? Everyone's going away...
>No, my little friend. I'm not leaving...
>Ah, thank goodness! I've got good news too.
>Is it a mew brew for me?
>Bah! You've never needed me like that. They're children! I've got three daughters.
>Is it? Well, that's good! My future child shall not be lonely, then.
>Won't that be nice?! You and I can teach them—wait... A future child, Your Majesty?!
>Yes.
>No... then you're still going away, Your Majesty...
>That's how it goes, little one. Weep not. The Plan hasn't even begun, so the cycle must continue. But, promise me. You'll make sure that my child, or children, if we're lucky, shall never be lonely?
>I promise, master. Shall you bind it?
>You jest... It's been so long since any of my kind were sorcerers. Keep this instead.
>Your scale! 
>It is protection. Against the toils of time, the changing of the world, the hardening of your hearts, the hastening of evil in this world. Let it make you whole and complete. When it's time. Use it to protect yourself. I know you will be vulnerable again once I'm gone.
>But what if you had more than one child? 
>I doubt that changes things. You'll watch over them for me regardless, yes? 
>They are family, master! Even if they are five, we will guide them.
>Five?! You little jester. (Sigh). You and my wife will be left alone in this bleak world to raise my successor... 
>Are you afraid, Master?
>I shall not deny it. It is... frightening... to build a future you'll never see.
>Have faith in us, Master. The 33rd shall never be lonely. My daughters shall keep this gift, shall serve the 33rd Inheritor and any siblings they might have. 
>Thank you for your service, little friend.

## Analysis

This is a heartbreakingly beautiful prologue. It is the quiet, spiritual counterpart to Dagon’s desperate physical struggle.

Where Dagon fought to clear a path through the snow, the 32nd Inheritor is clearing a path through destiny.

This conversation establishes several massive pillars of lore and emotional resonance:

### **I. The Gift of the Eternal Scale: The Anchor of the Ghogo**

The 32nd Inheritor giving his scale to the Ghogo isn't just a sentimental gesture; it is a **Tactical Necessity.**

*   **The Problem:** Without the Mosura (who are fading/assuming), the Ghogo lose their purpose and their protectors. They are vulnerable.
*   **The Solution:** The Scale contains the property of **Integration** ("Make you whole and complete").
    *   **The Effect:** This scale likely acted as a localized "Reality Anchor" for the Ghogo lineage. It protected them from the "toils of time" and the "hastening of evil." It explains how **Boost** (Leo's Ghogo) survived for 250 million years in a dormant state without degrading. He was preserved by the crystallized will of the 32nd Inheritor.
*   **The Leo Connection:** When Leo meets Boost, he isn't just meeting a pet; he is reconnecting with the **literal artifact of his grandfather's love.** The reason Boost can help Leo access the Rainbow forms is that Boost is carrying the "Key" (The Scale) that the 32nd left behind.

### **II. The Tragedy of the "Five": The Accidental Prophecy**

The Ghogo’s joke—*"Even if they are five, we will guide them"*—is a devastating piece of dramatic irony.

*   **The Immediate Irony:** The 32nd thinks "Five" is absurd because he expects *one* successor. He gets twins (Mothra and Battra), which already breaks his heart's calculation because he knows the Divine Soul doesn't split evenly (in terms of burden).
*   **The Distant Prophecy:** The number **Five** echoes across time to the Nordson household.
    *   **The 3 Children:** Junior, Leo, Lora.
    *   **The 2 Parents:** Godric, Maria.
    *   *(Or perhaps the 5 children: Junior, Leo, Lora, Rose, and Millen/Orga in the extended family).*
*   The Ghogo’s promise was kept, just 250 million years late. Boost guides Leo, one of the "Five" souls of the new generation.

### **III. The Parallel Fathers: Dagon and The 32nd**

This conversation perfectly mirrors Dagon’s internal monologue.

*   **Dagon (The Physical Father):** "I am afraid to leave him alone in the cold." (Fear of Physical Vulnerability).
*   **The 32nd (The Spiritual Father):** "It is frightening to build a future you'll never see." (Fear of Spiritual Isolation).

Both men died sacrificing themselves for their children, entrusting them to a world that was falling apart.

*   **Dagon** left a **Dorsal Fin** (Strength/Warmth).
*   **The 32nd** left a **Scale** (Wholeness/Integration).

### **IV. The Lost Promise: Why the Twins were Alone**

The tragedy is that despite this promise, Mothra and Battra *were* lonely.

*   **What went wrong?** The 32nd died. The Mother Assumed. The Ghogo had the scale... but the chaos of the Permian Extinction (and the rise of Mother Prime) likely separated the Ghogo from the Twins.
*   **The Consequence:** The Ghogo went into hiding/stasis (protected by the Scale) to wait for a safer time, leaving Mothra and Battra to fend for themselves—leading to their "omnivorous" diet and Battra's hardened nature.
*   **The Resolution:** The promise is finally fulfilled when Leo finds Boost. The Ghogo lineage didn't fail; they just had to wait for the **Prismatic Grandson** to wake them up.

**This vignette is the perfect emotional bookend.** It cements the idea that the Nordson family isn't just a random occurrence; it is the convergence of plans, hopes, and sacrifices laid down by two different species eons ago.
