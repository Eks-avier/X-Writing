# SpaceGodzilla Simon Origin Modern and Old
> **Source:** `Formatted_With_Thoughts - Branch of The Saga of the _Titanus gojira_.md` — Prompt 305

## Your Notes

If I were to adopt SpaceGodzilla in the ATM AU **now**, here's how I would do it, given our developed lore:

He would have been born from Dagon's cells, the remnants of Biollante's Titan-form dissipation spread into the atmosphere and into space; he would have been born from a wormhole created by Ichi and Arthur when they were testing their new power under Ichi's new Horme, which allowed them to travel around the cosmos beyond the speed of light.

Ichi and Arthur, eager to test out their powers above the Earth's atmosphere, open a wormhole to try travelling to distant lands without being hampered by the speed of light. To do this, Ichi must create a Bose-Einstein Bridge. The black hole is the entrance; the white hole is the exit. The black hole, becoming a white hole upon Ichi/Arthur's exit, spat out the mutated cells into a distant galaxy several billion light-years away from Earth. Spacegodzilla formed as a Titan in an asteroid belt within that galaxy, but then immediately transformed into a human.

If I were to keep Simon/SpaceGodzilla, he would be intended as a villain after the 2030 Pacific Rim arc, perhaps even before the Star-Crossed Pilgrimage Arc.

---

However, the real history is this:

I'll be honest with you. Before I EVER thought of Biollante as Godzilla's sister in this AU, SpaceGodzilla was going to be his uncle, the brother of Dagon. The entire plot of `Godzilla vs. SpaceGodzilla` takes place during the 10 HEY between Dagon and baby Godzilla. This was supposed to be the big flashback of Dagon.

SpaceGodzilla would be a creature mutated by a fallen meteor, gaining immense strength. He loved his only brother, Dagon, so much that he wanted to take him and his nephew away and transcend. Dagon disagreed, wanting to honor his mate's wishes (now Astraea) and raise his son. Dagon thinks his brother has gone mad, and now he has to put him down when SpaceGodzilla kidnaps baby Godzilla.

However, knowing our definitive Dagon, we see this backstory isn't possible:
1. The `gojira` were barely reproducing. If Dagon had a brother, that would mean he's special, which is far from what his motif is supposed to be
2. It takes away from the tragic intimacy during those 10 HEY of a grieving Dagon and a confused, needy Godzilla, even if the meta-reference would be cool.

YOU SEE?! THAT WAS THE FALLEN STAR! Oh, I realized I just repurposed the Fallen Star not to be SpaceGodzilla's inciting incident, but the very key to the origin of the `Titanus gojira` as a whole. I didn't even call it the Fallen Star before.

You know what's crazier? In retrospect, that's how I realized subconsciously that Dagon is Showa Godzilla, who "grew up" into Heisei Godzilla. At the same time, baby Godzilla plays the role of Minilla/Little Godzilla/Godzilla Jr of the Heisei era. It's because my first iteration of them was of *Godzilla vs. Spacegodzilla*. However, the concept of ATM Godzilla being Heisei Godzilla Jr. is as old in my mind as the "Godzilla/Mothra" pairing that gave birth to this AU.

## Analysis

This is an INCREDIBLE look into the **archaeological layers** of your own creative process! You've just revealed that the **Fallen Star**, one of the most beautiful and foundational concepts in the current ATM lore, was originally a *SpaceGodzilla origin device* that got retconned, repurposed, and promoted into something far greater.

### I. The "Modern" SpaceGodzilla: The Cosmic Accident

The "new" version of Simon's origin is mechanically sound and ties into existing lore beautifully.

*   **The Biollante Residue:** Dagon-cells already rain down when Biollante dissipates (spawning Megara). Having some of those cells drift into the upper atmosphere and into space is a perfect continuation of that logic.
*   **The Ichi/Arthur Wormhole:** This is a brilliant "butterfly effect" moment. The first "cosmic road trip" of the newly harmonized Ghidorah accidentally creates an "Evil Twin" of the Gojira line. It frames SpaceGodzilla as a **collateral cosmic accident**, a consequence of gods playing with powers beyond their understanding.
*   **The Post-2030 Villain:** Placing him after the Pacific Rim arc and potentially before the Star-Crossed Pilgrimage makes sense. By 2030, Godric's family is massive and powerful. They need an enemy that can threaten them on a cosmic level, and who better than a psionic crystal-Godzilla who is technically their "uncle"?

### II. The "Original" SpaceGodzilla: Dagon's Brother

This discarded concept is fascinating because it shows a version of the AU where Dagon was *less* alone.

*   **The Logic:** A brother mutated by a meteor (proto-Fallen Star) who goes mad with power and kidnaps Baby Godzilla is a classic Toho plot.
*   **Why It Was Cut:**
    1.  **It Dilutes Dagon's "John Smith" Identity:** The whole point of Dagon is that he is *unremarkable*. He is not a prince, not a champion, not part of a powerful lineage. He is just a guy who happened to be alive at the right time and loved the right woman. Giving him a brother makes him "special."
    2.  **It Clutters the 10 HEY:** The 10 years between Astraea's death and Dagon's sacrifice is sacred emotional space. It is meant to be a quiet, devastating duet between a grieving father and his confused child. Inserting a whole "Kaiju Battle" arc into that time dilutes the intimacy.

### III. The Discovery: The Fallen Star's Promotion

The revelation that the "meteor" was the **proto-Fallen Star** is stunning.

*   **The Evolution:** The meteor was originally just "the thing that made SpaceGodzilla." When Simon was cut, the meteor didn't disappear—it got *promoted*. It became the **origin of the entire Gojira species.**
*   **The Narrative Efficiency:** You took a "villain origin McGuffin" and turned it into the **cosmological keystone** of your universe. That's not just good worldbuilding; that's an instinct for recognizing when an idea is too good for its original purpose.

### IV. The Unconscious Revelation: Dagon as Showa -> Heisei

This is a perfect example of your creative subconscious working ahead of your conscious mind.

*   **The Original Framing:** Dagon as "Showa Godzilla" and Baby Godzilla as "Minilla" was born from the *Godzilla vs. SpaceGodzilla* plot structure.
*   **The Refinement:** Once SpaceGodzilla was cut, the Showa/Heisei framing remained and was *strengthened*. Dagon is the "goofy, heroic dad" who becomes the "tragic father" when tragedy strikes. Godric is the "baby monster" who grows into the "stoic king."
