# Edict Allocation Points System

> **Source:** `Formatted_With_Thoughts - The Kratos of Kings.md` — Prompt 220

## Your Notes

The strength of each Edict scales with the amount of energy poured into applying it. This is the only thing that's universal to all Kratonic species.

---

Let's have our Godric as an example:

A 10% AA Godzilla has 10% Fortification, Neutralization, and Penetration at once for a total of 30 "allocation points". AA is a holistic boost, no? It's part of the reason why it was so mechanically difficult to obtain, besides the emotional journey to achieve it. AA gives 10 "points" for each Edict. This is its *uniform nature* in effect, and what Godric fought hard to have.

However, because all three Edicts are a *product* of Aura, who in turn is a product of Manifestation, which is in turn fueled by a Krator's Symphonia, which in turn is composed of his Thelesis and Horme, managing them becomes a complex game of resource management. There's only one Conqueror's Will in a given Conqueror, after all.

---

>No Kraton pulls power of their ass.

This is a fundamental element of the power system.

---

Godzilla needs to reallocate his resources to get the desired boost. Let's have three motivating examples. Godzilla might want 15% Penetration for an attack, but he can't pull them out of his ass. Let's see how he can achieve it:

- He might set Fortification at 5%,  Neutralization remains at 10%, but it allows him to obtain 10% Penetration.
- He can have 8% Fortification with 7% Neutralization and then the rest goes to Penetration for 15% in total. This is a balanced mix, keeping Neutralization and Fortification nearly even.
- He can "min-max" and dump all of his points in Neutralization and Fortification to Penetration to achieve a whopping 30% Penetration at 10% AA.

There's an element of Knowledge and Strategy in Kratos, which is ironic because it's usually straightforward. It shares this with Magic, making them thematically tied. However, despite this, only strategic beings make use of them. You'd expect Junior to strategize. Leo probably dumps everything into Penetration and Fortification, then calls it a day. Lora would likely be close to her eldest brother; she's meticulous with her usage, but likely gravitates to a "balanced preset". Leo would only use Neutralization for Injection. While one average a Kraton's utilization of Kratos is straightforward, strategic minds might capitalize on the depth laying underneath it. It is not *necessary* to be strategic in Kratos, unlike in Magic where it is a hard requirement.

Know that this precise set of mechanics is for AA, which is for *Titanus gojira* specimens ONLY. Scylla and Ghidorah have their own resource management game.

## Analysis

### The Law of Conservation of Will: "No Kraton pulls power out of their ass."

This is the perfect, characterful, in-universe law that governs the entire system. It establishes a fundamental truth: **a Kraton's Will is a finite resource.** Even for a being as powerful as Godzilla, there is a limit to how much "will" he can consciously apply at any given moment. This is the bedrock of the resource management system.

#### I. Atomic Amplification as a "Power Budget"

The "allocation points" model is a perfect way to visualize this. Atomic Amplification isn't just a flat buff; it is a **total power budget** that Godric can then distribute across the three personal Edicts.

- **The Uniform Nature (The Default Setting):** A standard 10% AA provides a balanced, 10-10-10 split across Fortification, Neutralization, and Penetration. This is the "perfect harmony" he fought so hard to achieve. It's stable, versatile, and requires the least amount of active thought to maintain. This is his default combat stance.
- **The Total "Budget":** A 10% AA state grants him a total of **30 "allocation points"** to work with. He can choose to keep the balanced 10/10/10 distribution, or he can actively and consciously **reallocate** that budget to suit his immediate tactical needs.

#### II. Tactical Reallocation: The Ironic Strategy of a Straightforward System

This is where the magic happens. A layer of strategic depth that is wonderfully ironic for the system of "directness."

- **The Thinking Person's Brute:** While the core of Kratos is straightforward, mastering its application at the highest level requires immense tactical acumen. Godzilla doesn't just "get stronger"; he has to actively decide *how* he wants to be stronger in any given split second.
- **Examples:**
  1. **Sustained Defense, Piercing Strike:** Sacrificing a bit of Fortification (5/10/15) to ensure his attack pierces a heavily armored foe while maintaining his core defenses. A calculated, offensive move.
  2. **The Balanced Gambit:** A slight, balanced shift (8/7/15) to get the edge he needs without creating a major defensive vulnerability.
  3. **The "Glass Cannon" One-Shot (The Leo Special):** This is the ultimate all-or-nothing move. He can "min-max," sacrificing all of his active defense (0/0/30) for a single, overwhelmingly powerful Sceptered Strike guaranteed to pierce *anything*. This is an incredibly risky but potentially fight-ending gambit.

#### III. Personality Through Playstyle: A Window into the Soul

*How* a Gojira allocates their points is a direct reflection of their personality and combat philosophy.

- **Godric Sr. (The Grandmaster):** He is the master of dynamic reallocation. His 250 million years of experience and his hyper-analytical mind allow him to shift his points around mid-combo, moving from a defensive stance to an offensive one in the blink of an eye. He plays the system like a virtuoso.
- **Junior (The Strategist):** He would be a master of the **balanced reallocation.** He would never go for the "all-in" gambit. His fights would be a series of small, calculated shifts, always maintaining a strong defensive core while looking for the most efficient way to apply his offensive power.
- **Lora (The Preset Duelist):** She is a meticulous fighter. She would likely develop a series of preferred "balanced presets" for different situations and opponents. A preset for fighting a brute, a preset for fighting a speedster, etc. She wouldn't be as dynamically flexible as her father, but her preparation and execution would be flawless.
- **Leo (The Min-Maxer):** Leo would see this system and his first and only thought would be, "How much can I dump into Fortification and Penetration?" He lives for the "all-in" one-shot. His style would be to take a massive hit (relying on his base Gojira durability), and then deliver a single, 30-point, curse-enhanced Starfall slam to end the fight. His only use for Neutralization would be to fuel an equally all-or-nothing Injection.

This is a fantastic and satisfying final layer to the mechanical definition of Kratos. It adds strategic depth, allows for profound character expression, and reinforces the thematic kinship between Magic (knowledge/strategy) and Kratos (will/determination).
