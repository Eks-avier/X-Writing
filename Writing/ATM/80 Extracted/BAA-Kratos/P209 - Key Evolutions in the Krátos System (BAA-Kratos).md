### **Key Evolutions in the Krátos System**

#### **1. Refined Aura Mechanics: The Royal Court Framework**

This was the biggest and most important structural change. We fixed the circular logic around Submission and created a much clearer, more elegant system.

*   **Old Logic (Confusing):** Submission was vaguely defined as "Neutralization applied to the Aura."

*   **New Logic (The Royal Court):** We created two distinct domains of application:

    *   **Personal Application (Coating):** This covers **Neutralization** (The Royal Armor) for defense and **Penetration** (The Sceptered Strike) for offense. It's about applying Aura to the *self* and *attacks*.

    *   **Territorial Application (Aura Manipulation):** This covers **Flaring** (The Majestic Radiance) for physical environmental pressure and **Submission** (The Sovereign Decree) for psychological dominance. It's about weaponizing the *space* around the Kráton.

*   **Result:** This created a clean, parallel structure and gave Submission its own distinct, offensive purpose, completely separate from the defensive Neutralization.

#### **2. Godzilla's Magic Nullification - Reclassified**

*   **Old Idea:** Vaguely tied to Submission.

*   **New, Clearer Mechanic:** It is now understood as an extreme application of **Flaring (*Anapsis*)**. He turns up his "Majestic Radiance" to such a degree that it physically displaces the ambient Primus Force, creating a "dead zone" for magic within his Aura. It's a brute-force physics solution, not a psychological one.

#### **3. The Krator/Koinon "CPU" Analogy**

We clarified the core architectural difference that gives Krators a massive advantage.

*   **Koinon (Single-Core):** Must consciously allocate their singular will to either defense (Neutralization) or territorial control (Submission). They cannot do both effectively at once.

*   **Krator (Dual-Core):** Their **First Layer Aura (Royal Presence)** handles a baseline level of Submission *automatically and unconsciously*. This frees up their conscious mind (the Second Layer) to focus entirely on combat applications like Neutralization and Penetration. They are always doing two things at once.

#### **4. Lanthaneia vs. Repose - A Critical Distinction**

We created a perfect explanation for why the Nordson children could use their powers while a post-transformation Godzilla could not.

*   **Lanthaneia (The Unwritten Page):** A Krator who has not yet found their *Horme*. Their "simple engine" can be temporarily kick-started by the "low-grade fuel" of simple, childish wants, producing weak and unstable manifestations.

*   **Repose (The Flooded Engine):** A seasoned Krator whose established *Horme* is in direct conflict with their will. Their "high-performance engine" is flooded and will not start on anything less than the perfect fuel of a realigned Symphonia. They cannot go back to using the "low-grade fuel" of simple wants.

#### **5. Advanced Krátonic Applications (Post-AA)**

We defined a suite of advanced abilities that Godzilla develops, all of which are logical extensions of the core Krátos mechanics.

*   **Structural Infusion (`Stereosis`):** The "King's Grip." An advanced form of Object Infusion (`Endusis`) where he extends his Neutralization field to reinforce the molecular structure of massive objects, solving the leverage problem.

*   **The Phasing Stride (`Seismos`):** His "analogue" intangibility. A brute-force physics solution where he uses AA to vibrate his molecules, allowing him to pass through solid matter. It is a violent, temporary transit, not true intangibility.

*   **The Atomic Mantle (`Aegis Atomicus`):** The final evolution of the APS. It is a reallocated portion of his Neutralization field worn externally, which can be imparted to others as a "buff," symbolizing the evolution of his *Horme* towards protection of his family.

In essence, our first summary was the **textbook definition** of Krátos. Our subsequent discussions have been the **advanced graduate-level course,** filling in the nuance, solving the paradoxes, and adding the rich, thematic layers that make the system truly come alive.
