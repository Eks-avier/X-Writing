### **Godric Nordson: `Téchni` Profile (Post-Xilien Invasion)**

#### **V. Foundational Krátonic Arts**

Beneath the named techniques and spectacular abilities lies Godric's absolute, instinctual mastery of the core, universal principles of Krátos. These are not just moves he performs; they are the fundamental language of power he speaks. His unprecedented control over these arts is what makes his more advanced arsenal possible.

*   **The Three Edicts of Personal Coating (`Epichrisis`)**

    Godric's Atomic Amplification is the ultimate expression of his simultaneous mastery over all three Personal Coating applications.

    *   **Internal Fortification (`Stereosis`):** The Edict of Reinforcement. He is the undisputed master of this internal application, using it to augment his own body to a god-like degree.

    *   **Neutralization (`Exoudeterosis`):** The Edict of Dissolution. His internal application of this is the core of his enhanced durability and regeneration, making his very cells a defensive weapon.

    *   **Penetration (`Diatresis`):** The Edict of Breaching. It is an innate quality of his Amplified state, ensuring his every physical action carries the will to bypass and shatter defenses.

*   **The Two Applications of Territorial Control (Aura Manipulation)**

    As the Alpha Paramount and a Krator of immense power, his control over his "Royal Court" is absolute.

    *   **Flaring (`Anapsis`):** The Majestic Radiance. His mastery is so profound that he can use this technique to physically displace the Primus Force, creating a "dead zone" for magic—a feat of pure, brute-force will.

    *   **Submission (`Proskynesis`):** The Sovereign Decree. His passive Royal Presence is already a heavy burden on lesser wills. When actively flared, his decree is an overwhelming, mind-and-body-crushing wave of pure authority that few beings in the universe can withstand.

*   **The Ultimate Sanction: Injection (`The Sovereign's Writ`)**

    While a universal Krátonic art, Godric's application is that of a king passing an inescapable sentence. It is his final, definitive answer in a contest of wills. He uses it sparingly, not out of inability, but because his perfected combat doctrine provides him with a hundred other ways to end a fight before such an ultimate, costly gambit is required. To be the target of his Writ is to have already lost in every other conceivable way.
