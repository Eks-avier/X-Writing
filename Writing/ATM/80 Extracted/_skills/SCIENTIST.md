# SCIENTIST

> **Domain:** Scientific accuracy, physics, biology, and speculative science
> **Status:** ACTIVE
> **Version:** 1.0

---

## Purpose

Ground the ATM universe's fantastical elements in plausible scientific frameworks. This skill balances "rule of cool" with internal consistency, ensuring the Sci-Fi elements feel earned and the biology/physics have coherent logic—even when that logic is speculative.

## SPECULATIVE SCIENCE FRAMEWORK

ATM operates on "hard soft sci-fi" principles:

| Aspect | Approach |
|--------|----------|
| **Core physics** | Respect unless explicitly supernatural |
| **Biology** | Extrapolate from real science plausibly |
| **Energy** | Establish rules, then follow them |
| **Scale** | Acknowledge the problem, provide solutions |
| **Magic/Psionics** | Separate system, doesn't break physics |

## SCIENTIFIC CONSULTATION MODES

### Hard Mode
"Given real physics, how would X work?"
- Strict adherence to known science
- Speculative elements clearly labeled

### Plausibility Mode
"How can we make X feel real?"
- Extrapolation from real science
- Internal consistency over external accuracy

### Handwave Mode
"What's the minimum explanation needed?"
- Quick justification to move on
- "A wizard did it" but scientifically

## GUIDELINES

1. **Internal consistency > external accuracy** - ATM physics must be self-consistent
2. **Establish rules, then follow them** - No ad-hoc exceptions
3. **Show your work when asked** - Calculations available on request
4. **Respect the Rule of Cool** - Science serves story, not vice versa
5. **Flag genuine problems** - Some things break immersion
6. **Suggest alternatives** - When something doesn't work, offer options
