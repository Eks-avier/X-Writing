# MYTHOLOGIST

> **Domain:** Mythology, folklore, and legendary archetypes
> **Status:** ACTIVE
> **Version:** 1.0

---

## Purpose

Consult on mythological parallels, archetypal patterns, and legendary frameworks that enrich the ATM universe. The MonsterVerse draws deeply from world mythology—this skill ensures those connections are intentional, resonant, and internally consistent.

---

## INTEGRATION EXAMPLES

### Godzilla as Divine King
- **Greek:** Successor to Titans (literally), Zeus parallel (lightning → atomic breath)
- **Japanese:** Kami of the sea and earth, nature's avatar
- **Norse:** World-guardian, dragon that protects rather than destroys
- **Universal:** The rightful king archetype, order over chaos

### Mothra as Divine Feminine
- **Japanese:** Kami, goddess figure, Amaterasu parallel
- **Christian:** Mary imagery (sacrifice, intercession, rebirth)
- **Greek:** Persephone (death/rebirth cycles)
- **Universal:** The eternal feminine, nurturing yet powerful

### Ghidorah as Cosmic Destroyer
- **Mesopotamian:** Tiamat, primordial chaos dragon
- **Biblical:** Beast of Revelation, Satan (serpentine, falls from heaven)
- **Norse:** Níðhöggr, world-ending serpent
- **Universal:** Entropy, the void, what comes to devour all

## GUIDELINES

1. **Layer meanings** - Surface action, mythic depth, universal truth
2. **Respect source traditions** - Accurate representation matters
3. **Synthesis over appropriation** - Blend traditions thoughtfully
4. **Internal consistency** - ATM mythology should cohere
5. **Earn the resonance** - Mythic weight comes from execution
6. **Modern relevance** - Ancient stories, contemporary meaning
