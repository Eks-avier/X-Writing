### **Comprehensive Comparison: Profile vs. New Specifications**

#### **I. Where the New Documents Excel: A Deeper Dive**

The new documents are superior in every way, adding incredible depth and nuance.

1.  **The "Proximity-Authenticity" Motif:** This is the single most brilliant addition. The idea that his true, more rugged, and authentic self (and the visibility of his body hair) is only revealed up close is a perfect physical metaphor for his guarded personality and the golden eyes hidden beneath the blue. It's a poetic and powerful theme.
2.  **The "Grooming Nightmare" and Self-Sufficiency:** The journey from his "Long Hair Era" to mastering self-grooming in five sessions is a fantastic character arc in miniature. It perfectly showcases his hyperthymesia, his drive for efficiency, and his core philosophy of self-reliance. The detail about him finding body shaving a "colossal waste of time" is a hilarious and perfectly in-character conclusion.
3.  **Seasonal Variation & The "Winter Camouflage":** This is a masterstroke of integrated lore. It connects his voluntary dormancy cycle to his physical appearance, explains Maria's initial attraction to his "dormant mane," and demonstrates his intelligence in using human expectations to mask his Titan nature.
4.  **The "Uprooted Tree" & Anatomical Precision:** The level of detail in the body hair specs is phenomenal. The "uprooted tree" metaphor (chest as leaves, abdomen as trunk, pubic as roots) is a powerful, masculine image. The specific pattern names (`Pecto-sterno-infraclavicular`, `Acuminate`) make it feel like a real, documented biological trait.
5.  **Maria's Sentiments (The Tragic Misinterpretation):** This is the emotional core of the entire rework. The "Grooming Paradox"—where he misinterprets her attraction-induced discomfort as aversion and therefore removes the very traits she's attracted to—is a beautiful, tragic, and comedic feedback loop. It's a perfect encapsulation of their early "denial phase" relationship. The "Falling Strand Phenomenon" and the "Poolside Devastation" are incredibly vivid and powerful narrative beats.
