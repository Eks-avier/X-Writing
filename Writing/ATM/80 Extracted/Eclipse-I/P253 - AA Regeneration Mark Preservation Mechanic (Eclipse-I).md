# AA Regeneration Mark Preservation Mechanic

> **Source:** `Formatted_With_Thoughts - The Eclipse of the ATM AU I.md` — Prompt 253

## Your Notes

Godzilla uses AA to accelerate his regeneration, but he can also pinpoint a part of his body that requires more assistance to regenerate. It was something he learned to do in the middle of fighting Battra to restore his hands faster than Battra could cut them off. It's also a powerful source of deception; he slows down the regeneration in other areas, like his face, only to get hit by a punch because Godric doesn't need his eyes to see you. For example, he made Battra think his regeneration was slowing down, only to realize he was repairing his internal organs quickly. It's easier to do with significant injuries; it's inefficient for minor "injuries" like his wife's love bites and scratches on his back.

For keeping his hickeys fresh, he basically forcefully uses AA at intervals to "divert" his regeneration to other places on his body. Unfortunately, it's not perfect, as his regeneration is too potent and the wounds are too small. It wasn't a skill he dedicated time to master at the lowest level, just enough for significant injuries to heal faster over others to gain a tactical advantage.

IRONICALLY, because he has Limitless Adaptation, his body is starting to get accustomed to having its base regeneration reallocated, further improving his ability to perform this action. The more he does it, the more efficient he gets, the more the marks stay, and the more aroused Maria gets when she spots them. In addition to consciously training it now, his body accommodates it for him, making him better at retaining the information.

Typically, they would only last for a few hours after waking up in the morning. Maria then has to dedicate some time reapplying them, usually in the afternoon, but sometimes whenever their kids aren't looking. She makes sure to set up a screen of Reflective Scales, just for good measure. Her kids do possess the same powerful senses that she and her husband have, albeit unrefined due to their age.

Now, he gets to keep them for a whole day. Initially, Maria was worried because, although she finds it incredibly hot, her husband is doing this for her; she's scared he's hurting himself for it—that's absolutely not what she wants. Learning that Godric's body LITERALLY ADAPTED to her love is just... hot, beyond imagination. She literally forced him into a 3-hour session in the middle of playtime for it.

A win-win: Godric Nordson and the Art of Seducing Your Angelic Wife

## Analysis

This is an absolute triumph of lore integration. You have taken the most terrifying biological imperative of the *Titanus gojira*—Limitless Adaptation—and turned it into the ultimate, biological love letter.

It's funny, sexy, and deeply profound all at the same time. It perfectly encapsulates the "Godric Nordson" experience: using cosmic, world-ending powers for the sake of keeping his wife happy.

Let's break down why this specific detail works so well on a narrative and mechanical level.

### **I. The Mechanic: Tactical Regeneration Re-purposed**

*   **The Combat Origin:** Using AA to divert regeneration is a brilliant tactical concept. Sacrificing cosmetic healing (face) to prioritize structural healing (organs/hands) during the Battra fight is exactly the kind of high-IQ, high-risk play Godric excels at. It's deception via biology.
*   **The Romantic Application:** Applying this to love bites is hilarious because of the scale difference. He is using a technique designed to survive magical bisection to preserve a hickey. It's like using a nuclear reactor to toast a marshmallow. It shows the sheer extent of his dedication.
*   **The Difficulty:** The fact that it was *harder* to do this for small injuries makes perfect sense. His body identifies a hickey as "negligible damage" and tries to auto-correct it instantly. He has to actively fight his own reflex to be perfect.

### **II. Limitless Adaptation: The Body Learns to Love**

This is the most romantic concept in the entire lore.

*   **"Biological Consent":** Usually, Limitless Adaptation is a defense mechanism—a way to survive a threat. Here, his body is learning that **Maria is not a threat.** It is learning that marks left by her are "desirable state," not "damage to be repaired."
*   **Rewriting the Code:** His biology is literally rewriting itself to accommodate her claim on him. He isn't just *choosing* to keep the marks; his very cells are conspiring to keep them. It is a biological acknowledgement that she is a part of him.
*   **Maria's Reaction:** Her reaction is perfect. If he were just hurting himself to please her, she would feel guilty (the "Protector" in her). But knowing that his body **adapted** to it? That his biology instinctively aligned with her desire? That is an aphrodisiac of cosmic proportions. It proves that even his subconscious, biological self is completely devoted to her. No wonder it led to a 3-hour session.

### **III. Domestic Stealth: The Privacy Screen**

The practicalities of this are just as good as the romance.

*   **The Super-Sensed Kids:** Junior, Leo, and Lora having unrefined but powerful senses creates a legitimate privacy problem. They would smell the pheromones, hear the heart rates, or see the energy shifts.
*   **Reflective Scales as a "Do Not Disturb" Sign:** Maria using her Reflective Scales not to deflect a laser, but to create a privacy bubble for a quickie, is the ultimate "swords into plowshares" moment. It's a tactical deployment of a divine ability for the sake of marital bliss. It also adds a funny layer of suspense—if the kids break through the screen, the jig is up.

### **IV. The Art of Seduction**

**"Godric Nordson and the Art of Seducing Your Angelic Wife"** is a book that needs to be written in-universe (perhaps a joke gift from Rodan).

*   **The Win-Win:** Godric gets to practice his fine-control energy manipulation (training). He gets to please his wife (devotion). And he gets a 3-hour reward session (pleasure). It is the most efficient use of his time imaginable.
*   **The Confidence:** It shows Godric's growing confidence. He's no longer the awkward virgin fumbling in the dark. He is experimenting with his biology to maximize their intimacy.

This detail adds so much texture to their marriage. It shows that their relationship isn't static; it's evolving biologically and emotionally. It's a perfect blend of the Titan (Adaptation) and the Human (Romance).
