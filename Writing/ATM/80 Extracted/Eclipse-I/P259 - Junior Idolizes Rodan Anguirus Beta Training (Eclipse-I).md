# Junior Idolizes Rodan and Anguirus: Secret Beta Training

> **Source:** `Formatted_With_Thoughts - The Eclipse of the ATM AU I.md` — Prompt 259

## Your Notes

The only other people that Gricky idolizes other than his father are... Roman and Alexios.

Why? They have faithfully served his father since the Mesozoic. For millions of years, they have been the left and right hand of the king. Gricky wants to become *like* them above all else.

There's a difference between Gricky's idolization of his father and his Betas. He wants to *become* like Roman and Alexios, but he *loves* his father. He *aspires* to be like the Betas, but *adores* his father.

He eagerly takes practical advice from Alexios—learns the duty, responsibilities, and response. He learns the "how" from Alexios. Meanwhile, he hears the sentiment between being a Beta from Roman. Roman focuses on experience and camaraderie. He learns more about his father through their perspectives.

However, it is crucial that they do it in secret or in passing. Gricky doesn't want his dad to know, otherwise, he'll shut it down. He WILL help his father. That's the only outcome. It's high time for a third Beta; it WILL be him. He wills it to happen.

It is funny. Even in his "rebellious teenage phase," Junior only ever wants to help his father. He really, really loves his Dad.

## Analysis

**The "Loyalty Rebellion":**
- Usually teenage rebellion is about rejecting the father's legacy—Junior's rebellion is embracing it too hard
- **Godric's Command:** "Be free. Be happy. Be my son."
- **Junior's Rebellion:** "No. I will serve. I will burden myself. I will be your soldier."
- It is rebellion born of excessive love

**The Curriculum of the Betas:**
- **Alexios (The Textbook):** Teaches mechanics of duty—logistics, territory management, threat assessment
- **Roman (The Biography):** Teaches heart of the King—stories, the reckless days, moments of vulnerability
- By learning from both, Junior prepares to be the Perfect Beta

**The "Third Beta" Ambition:**
- Aligns with his Kratoros Thelesis and *Horme* ("To Magnify")
- "He wills it to happen"—manifesting through sheer willpower
- A Triad of Betas mirrors Trinity of Alphas: Anguirus (Defense), Rodan (Speed), Junior (Strategy/Synthesis)

**The Secret Society of the Shield:**
- Roman and Alexios respect Junior's will, see fire in him
- The inevitable explosion when Godric finds out will be massive emotional climax
