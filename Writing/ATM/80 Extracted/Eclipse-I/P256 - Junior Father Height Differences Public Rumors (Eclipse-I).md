# Junior's Affection with Father and Public Height Misconceptions

> **Source:** `Formatted_With_Thoughts - The Eclipse of the ATM AU I.md` — Prompt 256

## Your Notes

Gricky really does love his role models. His quiet persona drops the moment he's in his father or grandfather's presence. He's almost soft and rather expressive. Even after his experience off-planet at 18, it never quite disappears!

Godric would poke him, tickle him at the sides, or even kiss his temple occasionally. Junior used to always hold hands with his father, though now he has grown out of it. Instead, his father now just wraps an arm around his shoulders, and pulls him closer. All of these fluster Gricky, but he loves it a lot. And to Godric, a son that feels incredibly safe satisfies his paternal instincts: he won and that's all that counts.

It really is no wonder that he gets mistaken as his father's romantic partner. That fact is endlessly laughed about by Maria, and something she deeply appreciates.

**Height differences:**

The Nordsons:
- Godric Sr. is 6'5
- Darius is 6'3
- Rose is 5'7

The Lepidiels:
- Bartholomew & Maria are 5'8 (twinning, even in height)
- Battra is the shortest male in their extended family!

The Nordson siblings:
- Godric Jr. is 6'4 (almost 6'5)
- Leo is 6'3
- Lora is 5'11

When Maria is escorted by her son, it sells the rumors further. A small woman with a tall escort? Must be something going around here.

Maria plays into social misconceptions. She'd even publicly sit on her husband's lap, which flusters Junior because he's the one fretting about PR. BUT, even with Gricky, he and Maria are mistaken as a couple. Unlike with his father, Gricky gets upset—he doesn't like that his mother is being the subject of such rumors.

## Analysis

**The "Safe Harbor" Triumph:**
- Every time Junior shows vulnerability, it is a victory over 250 million years of trauma for Godric
- Godric using tickles and pokes to strip away Junior's "adult self-awareness" shows he's embracing his Dagon-inherited goofball side

**The Height Hierarchy:**
- Maria and Battra being 5'8" is a "twin" detail—makes Maria tall for a woman but "tiny" in the Nordson household
- Battra being the shortest male adds another needle to his inferiority complex
- Rose at 5'7" fits right under Godric's protective wing

**The "Bodyguard Son" Dynamic:**
- Junior (6'4", lean, shredded) escorting Maria (5'8", ethereal, youthful) looks like a "Power Couple" to humans
- Junior's reaction to rumors about his mother: fine being teased about "dating" his dad, but takes sanctity of his mother seriously
- Maria's response: "I raised a strong, handsome boy that will protect me"—pure parental bliss
