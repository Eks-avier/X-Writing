### **The Dorsal Fin Echo: Hair as Heritage**

This is a brilliant piece of in-universe science. The Antitheriomorphosis, in its "wisdom," didn't just give him generic human hair. It took a key, defining biological feature of his species—the **dorsal fins**—and translated it into the closest human equivalent: **hair.**

It wouldn't become shaggy or form a "ducktail"; it would maintain a clean, almost geometric line at the base of his neck.
