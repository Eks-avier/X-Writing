# Godric's True Voice: Northern English/Scottish Accent

> **Source:** `Formatted_With_Thoughts - The Eclipse of the ATM AU I.md` — Prompt 209

## Your Notes

Yes, exactly! His drawl is a sign he's quite relaxed and feeling mischievous.

But honestly, with how sticky his learning is, I imagine he actually has an accent he's most comfortable with, which he slips into whenever he's in a calmer mood.

Notice how he says 'Aye' and 'Nay' all the time? He'd have an accent somewhere along Northern England or Scotland. I guess he'd feel a sort of solidarity with them, knowing they live in the colder regions, even though the continents have shifted, and even if what is north then is south now. Godric would take a profound, instinctual liking to human cultures who were sons and daughters of the cold, just as he is. Though his accent isn't really a caricature, much like his Japanese accent, it's something he came about because he truly resonated with the human experience there.

It is *full* with a velvety, subtle, and *gravelly* nature; it comes from his lower throat and chest. One of the things Maria first found attractive about his human form, besides his face and physique obviously, was his voice. She found him utterly handsome, and that voice was added nothing but even more trouble for her nerves. Its sheer masculinity is something she deeply appreciates, especially whenever he's being gentle. He'd make a quiet, little joke when alone, only for each other, and she can't help but resist the urge to swoon at it. This is a staple of their dynamic when they were pretending they didn't have feelings for each other early 2021-2025. Godric pretends NOT to know this, for his sake and hers, 'cause he's in DEEP denial, too.

Finally, this cements the accent as an adherent of his motifs. His General American accent was something he learned out of obligation, this accent was, like his Japanese one, something he gravitated towards naturally. Hence, it is the Western counterpart to his Eastern first language.

## Analysis

If his Japanese is the sound of his rebirth and his American English is the language of his duty (and mischief), his **"Northern" English accent** is the sound of his soul.

**The Origin: A Call of the Blood**
- Not an accent learned from a single person; he gravitated towards it instinctually
- Drawn to cultures that are "sons and daughters of the cold"—recognizes a similar ethos of pragmatic resilience and connection to harsh but beautiful landscape

**The Description: "Gravel Wrapped in Velvet"**
- **Pitch and Register:** Deep, resonant baritone produced from throat and chest, giving it physical weight
- **Northern English/Scottish Inflection:**
  - Slightly flattened 'a' sounds
  - Deeper, rounded 'o' sounds
  - Gently rolled or tapped 'r's—a subtle "burr"
  - Melodic, lyrical cadence that rises and falls
- **Signature Qualities:**
  - **Gravel:** Underlying roughness, weathered by eons of existence
  - **Velvet:** Incredibly rich and pleasing smoothness
  - Combined effect: "Gravel wrapped in velvet"—simultaneously comforting and intimidating

**The Triad of Voices:**
1. **Japanese (Bōsō Dialect):** The Voice of the Past—rebirth, humility, vulnerability
2. **Northern English/Scottish:** The Voice of the Present—core identity, heritage, intimacy (the voice of Godric Nordson)
3. **General American/Southern Drawl:** The Voice of the Role—public facing, mischief
