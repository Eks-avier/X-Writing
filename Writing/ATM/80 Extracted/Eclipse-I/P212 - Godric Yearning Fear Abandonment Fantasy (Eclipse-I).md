# Godric's Yearning, Fantasy, and Fear of Abandonment

> **Source:** `Formatted_With_Thoughts - The Eclipse of the ATM AU I.md` — Prompt 212

## Your Notes

Godzilla is not ignorant of the way their human bodies perfectly complement each other. He's not stupid.

The clothes he wears bear her mark, a piece of *her* physical form—her silk. He's not ignorant of the way his own shed scales synergize with her silk.

He's not oblivious to the way Maria looks at him with darkened sapphire eyes when he cracks a quiet, dry joke, looking like she wants to slam him into a wall and demand that he make her his with his mouth.

He absolutely adores her hair, a river of moonlight. He's incapacitated by her sapphire eyes, the color of the ocean he has called his home before even the continents have settled in their current places.

With her, the "Rodan" in him is always on display. Just look at their conversations after Godric's battle with Kong: he's being supremely, intentionally difficult *for* her. She would nag at his recklessness and he would be the wisecrack reversing her words. It's a comfortable dynamic, a friendly one. But, with these human bodies, they become... more complicated, more charged.

When he stands before her, it's easy to imagine enveloping her with his tall frame. It would be easy to wrap his arms around her. And though Maria always wears very modest dresses, he does have an idea of what's underneath it. Only an idea—her clothes are lined with her scales, her skin are lined with her scales. Godzilla can technically *see* through people and their clothes, their skin. But, he never does it with Mothra, because he can't... and he wants the mystery. He wants to fantasize, to *imagine*.

For the first time, the instincts he thought had fully faded away were alive, strong, and beating in his human form. Sometimes, in his pool, after trying to fix his memories of Dagon, he would close his eyes and *fantasize* about certain activities with Maria. It's utterly boyish, but it has been the FIRST time he had ever allowed himself to do that. Unlike his instincts as a Titan, this was deliberate and intentional. He wants to think about her.

She drives him crazy just as he does her. He yearns, but is afraid. It's easy to keep the familiarity of their banter than to address that his hand fits perfectly on hers. It's easy to laugh at her scolding than to realize he finds her utterly stunning when she's mad. It's easy to be friends than to be hurt if she left.

He could gamble his body for the world he lives in. He could not gamble his heart still so thoroughly wounded from the loss of his parents.

## Analysis

**The King in Denial: A Conscious Ignorance**
- He is not oblivious; he is in active, profound denial
- He catalogs every data point with perfect memory then deliberately chooses to misinterpret them
- His adoration of her "river of moonlight" hair and "ocean sapphire" eyes is an ancient appreciation newly amplified

**The "Rodan" Persona:**
- He leans into his goofball, wisecracking persona with her as a defense mechanism
- Their old, comfortable dynamic is a safe harbor

**The Rebirth of Desire:**
- First fantasies in his life—a monumental event
- He *wants* to think about her—deliberate, conscious desire
- His refusal to use "x-ray" senses on her is profound romanticism—he chooses the beautiful uncertainty of desire

**The Core of His Fear: Abandonment**
- "It's easy to be friends than to be hurt if she left"—the Rosetta Stone of his emotional state
- The Cycle of Loss: lost mother before birth, believed abandoned by father, watched Maria die repeatedly
- **"He can gamble his body for the world. He cannot gamble his heart."**
