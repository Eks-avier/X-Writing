# Godric's Virgin Status and Physical Attraction Awakening

> **Source:** `Formatted_With_Thoughts - The Eclipse of the ATM AU I.md` — Prompt 211

## Your Notes

Maria is *physically* attracted to Godric. The same applies to the man himself.

The sensation is intoxicating in a way that is confusing. He's a virgin! He has NEVER engaged with the primal need for reproduction. His father has been the one and *only* Titanus gojira he has ever seen. He *knows* the sensation; had sought ways to pleasure himself during the first millenia of his life to *no* avail. Eventually, as the last, living member of his faction, of his species, the instinct *faded away*.

With Mothra then, they were two different species. They were simply, genuinely, with no ulterior feelings, *friends*. They were *close friends*, but friends nonetheless. The wall of biology and instinct was a perfect boundary between them.

Their immortality also buffered their relationship. After Battra left to go train his newly acquired Magic, their entire trio broke apart. Godzilla was newly enraged by the belief that his father abandoned him; he needed someplace to vent. The grind to the top of the Standing Hierarchy began there. That left Mothra alone to her devices. This became a recurring pattern. Sometimes they'd meet for a couple of real months, then they'd never see each other for the next ten to fifty thousand years.

When he and she were forced into human bodies, that's when it happened. From 2020 up until they married on 2029, neither Godzilla nor Mothra had stayed apart for longer than *several months*. This has changed both of them, for better or worse. They were no longer a Sun and Moon entering in an eclipse and leaving so soon. Their meeting was no longer on their terms, the eclipse was not theirs to decide this time.

## Analysis

**The Virgin King:**
- Making Godric a literal virgin is a powerful choice—it strips away the hyper-masculine, experienced god trope
- His libido fading due to being the last of his kind is tragic and logical biological detail
- His body simply gave up on an urge that could never be fulfilled

**The Awkward Awakening:**
- He is experiencing powerful, primal feelings for the first time in 250 million years
- He has the emotional toolkit of an adolescent in the body of a god
- This justifies his denial, confusion, and clumsy navigation of early romance

**A Friendship of Immense Distance:**
- "They were *just* friends"—the wall of biology was a natural boundary
- Relationship measured in millennia, not months—meeting for years, then not seeing each other for fifty thousand years
- The constant telepathic connection was the key—a background hum of "we are here"

**The Forced Proximity:**
- The transformation shattered the "wall of biology"—now effectively the same species
- From millennia apart to months together—unprecedented proximity
- **The Unchosen Eclipse:** They are now stuck sharing the same sky, forced into a permanent eclipse
