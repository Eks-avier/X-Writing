# They Did What
> **Source:** `Formatted_With_Thoughts - The Antitheriomorphosis - KOTM Rewrite.md` — Prompt 299

## Your Notes

"THEY DID WHAT?!"

Mark Russell's voice echoed throughout the command center of Castle Bravo. 

"IT STARTS ON WHAT DAY?!"

## Analysis

The silence that followed Mark Russell’s scream was heavy, broken only by the frantic, rhythmic clacking of Dr. Rick Stanton’s keyboard.

On the massive main screen of the Castle Bravo command center, Maria Lepidiel sat composedly in her lounge chair at the Palace, sipping tea. She looked for all the world like a woman discussing a brunch schedule, not the logistics of a planetary-scale gladiatorial event.

"Friday, Mark," she repeated, her voice calm, melodious, and utterly terrifying in its finality. "The Opening Ceremonies are at dusk. Battra insists the lighting will be 'sublime' at that hour."

"Friday..." Mark slumped into his chair, the blood draining from his face. "That is **three days** from now. You want to move eighty percent of the planet's mega-fauna to a single location in *three days*?"

"Oh, we don't have to move them," Maria corrected gently. "My brother is handling the logistics. The Gateways are already being recalibrated. I believe Scylla has already arrived; she was complaining about the humidity."

"Scylla is there?" Sam Coleman, the Head of Public Relations, looked like he was about to cry. He was clutching a tablet so hard his knuckles were white. "I just told the UN Security Council that the recent seismic activity in Antarctica was 'ice shelf settling.' You're telling me it was a giant spider-crab taking a portal to a wrestling match?"

"Technically," Rick Stanton chimed in, spinning his chair around with a grin that was entirely too wide for the situation, "it's more of a Greco-Roman tournament structure with elemental obstacle courses. And Sam? Look at these energy readings coming off Monster Island. Battra isn't just building an arena; he's terraforming a stadium. This is gonna be *awesome*."

"Rick, shut up," Mark rubbed his temples. "Maria... why? Is there a threat? Is something coming?"

"Boredom, Mark," Maria said, blowing on her tea. "Your 'consultant' is bored. And a bored King is a dangerous thing. This keeps him occupied, it lets the Wardens blow off steam, and it gives Kong a chance to... network."

"Network," Mark repeated, deadpan. "The giant ape is networking."

"He's a Sovereign now, Mark. He has a court to maintain."

Dr. Ilene Chen stepped forward, her eyes scanning the scrolling data feeds of Titan movements. "It makes sense, culturally," she said, her voice the only calm anchor in the room beside Maria's. "Historically, Titan hierarchies were established through violence. This... 'Festival of Sovereignty,' as Battra calls it, turns that violence into ritual. It’s a sign of a stabilizing civilization. It’s actually a good sign, Mark."

"A good sign," Mark muttered, standing up and pacing. "A good sign is a quiet week. A good sign is Godzilla sleeping in a hole. A global Titan fight club is a logistical apocalypse."

He turned to the main screen. "And what about the students? I saw the memo Battra's Sentinel dropped on my desk. Please tell me I misread the part about 'Inter-Divisional Combat scenarios'."

"You did not," Maria smiled, and for a second, the divine light behind her eyes flared, reminding everyone exactly *what* she was. "They need the experience. And they need to see that they are part of this world, not just observers. Don't worry, Mark. Battra has sworn on his... well, on his ego, that no permanent harm will come to them. And I will be watching."

"The insurance premiums," Sam Coleman whispered, staring into the middle distance. "The liability waivers..."

"Look," Mark said, rallying his shredding sanity. "If this is happening, we need to be there. Not just as spectators. We need containment protocols, we need medical evac on standby for the students, we need—"

"You need a suit," Maria interrupted. "Formal wear. It’s a gala, Mark. Godric insists."

The line went dead.

Mark stared at the black screen for a long moment. The command center was buzzing with activity—analysts tracking movement, military liaisons shouting into phones, Rick Stanton taking bets on the Rodan vs. Scylla odds.

"Three days," Mark whispered to himself. He looked at the ceiling, as if asking a higher power for strength. But the higher powers were currently busy building an obstacle course in the Pacific.

"Sam," Mark barked, turning back to the room. "Draft a press release. Tell the world... tell them it's a 'Monarch Joint-Training Exercise.' Tell them it's a migration pattern study. I don't care. Just make sure no civilian ships get within five hundred miles of Monster Island."

"On it," Sam squeaked.

"Rick," Mark pointed a finger. "If you bet on Godzilla losing any match, you're fired."

"Wouldn't dream of it, boss!"

Mark sighed, collapsing back into his chair. He pulled a bottle of aspirin from his drawer. "I miss the days when they just destroyed cities," he muttered. "At least the paperwork was straightforward."
