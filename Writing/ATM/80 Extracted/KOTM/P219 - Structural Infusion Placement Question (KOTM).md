## The Issue

Alright, so now, where do we put his ability to use Structural Infusion? It's not really an AA-exclusive skill, so it's a purely Krátonic ability. I think we should make a distinction for that, no?

My preliminary, original idea is that you can sheath an object OR yourself with your Krátonic Will. Coated objects gain the properties of either Penetration or Neutralization, or both in the case of Godzilla's AA.

The issue is we also established that you can *strengthen* the structural integrity of external objects by sheathing them with one's Krátonic Will. This is frankly a third, different notion from Penetration and Neutralization. Let's formally name it as Fortification!

## Caveats

- However, while a sheathed object can gain Penetration or Neutralization properties, it's not as fully effective as applying it to the Kráton's body. As we've established, Biology and Krátos are intertwined: a Kráton's Manifestation synergizes better with the Kráton's actual body as opposed to an external object, like how a snake posseses greater resistance against its own poison.
- Let's formalize the differences:
    - Penetration does NOT add attacking power, it only adds "damage piercing." An attack that deals 10 damage normally, but deals 7 damage to an armored opponent will deal 10 damage again if said attack receives the property of Penetration. This depends on the output, by the way. 100% Penetration is not the default.
    - Neutralization ADDS defensive power; its nature is innately defensive: it dissolves attacks that comes into contact with an object that received the property of Neutralization. However, it does NOT strengthen an object. 100% Neutralization is also not the default.
     - Fortification can potentially increase BOTH offensive and defensive capability. A Fortified arm can hit harder and block attacks better. However, that same fortified arm does not receive the benefit of Penetration or Neutralization. Again, 100% Fortification is not the default.

## Analysis

This is an excellent and necessary refinement. You've correctly identified the logical gap: Structural Infusion's effect (strengthening an object) is fundamentally different from Penetration (bypassing defense) and Neutralization (dissolving attacks). Creating a distinct third category, **Fortification**, is the perfect solution.

This not only solves the logical inconsistency but also dramatically enriches the entire Personal Coating system, giving Krátons a more diverse and strategic toolkit.

Let's organize these concepts into a clean, final framework.

---

### **Personal Coating (`Epichrisis`): The Three Applications of the Sovereign's Will**

Personal Coating is the Krátonic art of consciously sheathing a tangible object (oneself, a weapon, or a tool) with a focused layer of one's Aura. This act imbues the object with a specific, willed intent. There are three fundamental applications, or "edicts," that a Kráton can apply through this process.

#### **I. Penetration (`Diatresis`): The Edict of Breaching**

*   **Core Principle:** "Bypass all that defends."
*   **Function:** This application does **not** increase the raw power of an attack. Instead, it imbues the attack with the Kráton's will to **ignore or shred defenses.** It is pure "armor piercing" or "magic penetration."
*   **Mechanism:** The will-infused Aura disrupts the integrity of a target's defenses at the point of impact, whether they are physical (armor), energetic (shields), magical (wards), or biological (innate durability).
*   **MOBA Analogy:** It reduces the target's Armor/Magic Resistance, allowing the attack's base damage to land unmitigated. A 100% Penetration doesn't make you hit harder; it makes sure your full strength connects.

#### **II. Neutralization (`Exoudeterosis`): The Edict of Dissolution**

*   **Core Principle:** "Unmake all that threatens."
*   **Function:** This application **adds a layer of active, destructive defense.** It does not reinforce the sheathed object; it creates a corrosive field around it that dissolves incoming attacks.
*   **Mechanism:** The will-infused Aura is programmed with the intent to "dissolve what is not me/this object." When an attack makes contact, the Neutralization field actively works to unravel its physical or energetic structure.
*   **MOBA Analogy:** It provides a "Damage Shield" that absorbs a certain amount of incoming damage. It is a direct, flat bonus to one's defensive capabilities.

#### **III. Fortification (`Stereosis`): The Edict of Reinforcement**

*   **Core Principle:** "Strengthen and make whole."
*   **Function:** This is the true "structural infusion." This application **increases the molecular integrity and physical durability** of the object it coats. It does not dissolve attacks or bypass armor; it simply makes the object **harder, tougher, and more resilient.**
*   **Mechanism:** The will-infused Aura permeates the object at a molecular level, reinforcing the bonds between its atoms and temporarily granting it a durability derived from the Kráton's own.
*   **MOBA Analogy:** It is a direct buff to the "Armor," "Magic Resistance," and even "Attack Damage" stats of the object (or a body part). A Fortified arm can block an attack better (higher defense) and punch harder (its structure can withstand greater impact forces).

### **Strategic Integration and Limitations**

This three-pronged system creates a rich tactical landscape.

*   **The Biological Synergy Caveat:** You are absolutely right. All three applications work best on the Kráton's own body. The will synergizes most perfectly with its own biological vessel. Applying these properties to an external object is less efficient and often less potent. It's like a snake's venom being most effective when delivered by the snake's own fangs.
*   **Strategic Choices:** A Kráton must now make tactical choices.
    *   Do I **Fortify** my fist to hit harder?
    *   Or do I coat it with **Penetration** to bypass my opponent's armor?
    *   Do I **Fortify** my arm to block a blow?
    *   Or do I coat it with **Neutralization** to dissolve the incoming weapon?
*   **Atomic Amplification Revisited:** Godzilla's AA is the ultimate expression of this system because it is a masterful, simultaneous application of all three concepts on himself.
    *   He is **Fortified** (his muscles are augmented, hitting harder).
    *   He is imbued with **Penetration** (his every strike bypasses defenses).
    *   He is suffused with **Neutralization** (his cells actively dissolve incoming damage).
    This is what makes it such a difficult, master-level technique—he is maintaining three distinct, complex intents across his entire body at once.

### **The Final Placement of Structural Infusion**

With this framework, the answer is clear.

*   **Structural Infusion** is simply the formal name for the effects of **Fortification (`Stereosis`)**.
*   It is a **foundational Krátonic art,** one of the three core applications of Personal Coating, available to any Kráton.
*   Atomic Amplification does not grant him this ability; AA is a state that allows him to use a perfected, internally applied version of it in concert with the other two applications. The **Atomic Mantle**, in turn, is what allows him to project a "gentler," more controlled version of it externally.

This is a complete, logically sound, and strategically rich framework that elegantly incorporates all of your ideas. It is ready.
