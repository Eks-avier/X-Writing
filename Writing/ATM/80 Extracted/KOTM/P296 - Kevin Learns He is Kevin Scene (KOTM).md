Kevin's eyes widened. His ears couldn't believe it. "One on one combat. Is that right?"

"Aye. What of it?"

He felt the urge to sigh. This man-lizard... 

"I'm not trying to sell myself short. But, you do realize I'm no where NEAR the top ten in terms of strength."

Godric stroked his beard. *Not gonna lie, it looks cool*. He started growing one too a year ago. He felt his own whiskers in bis hands. *Maria's a good influence on ya*.

"We'll make it so that winning against you would grant a specific reward. What do you think?"

"Could work." An idea sprung from his head. "Most of the powerhouses are Surface, no? The top ten?"

"Aye."

Yes. Yes this will work. "Then, winning against me should only grant a reward useful for Hollow Earth Denizens."

Maria stirred from across. "And what if a Surface Titan is shrewd enough to challenge you anyway to gain a boon that might benefit them whilst in the Hollow Earth?"

*Drat*. But, nevertheless... "Then I fight them anyway, and try to win."

"Aye! That's my man!"

Maria rolled her eyes, smiling. "Of course. You boys will be boys." She turned to Godric. "Is it not too late to abstain?"

"No cuddles for a year?"

Godric got slapped in the arm. Kevin laughed.

"Moron. You have no right to blackmail me," Maria huffed. 

"But what about your rewards?" 

Godric waved a hand. "Ah, that's easy. From me, ***anything***." He gestured towards Maria. "From her, anything from the humans. Right, my hen?"

Maria pushed Godric away. "We should band together and just fight him instead."

Kevin couldn't but stifle another bout of laughter. "No can do, my Lady. That's your boyfriend."

A concern crept up on him. "How much time do we have to prepare? I assume a couple of months?"

"We have Battra for that, don't we? Call him up and his students. We'll have ourselves a tournament in less than a week."
