# Hydrokinesis Is The Ability Of A
> **Source:** `Formatted_With_Thoughts - The Antitheriomorphosis - KOTM Rewrite.md` — Prompt 269

## Your Notes

Hydrokinesis is the ability of a Psychokinetic to exclusively wield water using the power of the mind. 

Let's define what constitutes as a good Psychokinetic:
- Volume, the amount that they can control. In terms of ice, she is the queen at this. No other Hydrokinetic can control ice with the volume that she does (although this is because she's a Krateira, but no one knew that til recently!)
- Strength, the amount of force they can exert on that which they control. This is Scylla's forte. Of all the Hydrokinetic Wardens, she is the STRONGEST. She is so strong that it contends with Mothra using telekinesis on water. 
- Stability (self-explanatory). 
- Fluidity, how smoothly something they can control follows them. It is the difference between using their power directly as a crude tool, or as an art like a dance. The difference between using a hammer and a scalpel. Scylla is great at this, but someone like Leviathan and Manda can rival her comfortably.
- Creativity (self-explanatory). Scylla is HIGHLY creative, often subconsciously synergizing her Krátos and Hydrokinesis. Though, it can be said that Bakunawa can rival her in this area.

Each of these aspects hinge on one thing: mental composure. A distracted mind is a mind that cannot control anything, regardless of whatever type of Psychokinetics an Unnon has.

Psychokinetics DO NOT generate what they control. They utilize what exists *around* them. The only exception is when an Unnon posseses the biological capacity to create what they can control. For example, Rodan is part-pyrokinetic, his bio-volcanic nature means he can produce heat and flame from his very body, which he can then control with his mind. The same is same with Scylla. She CANNOT generate water from her Hydrokinesis.

Kryotons cannot "control" their ice in the same manner as a Cryokinetic or Hydrokinetic can. Unless she was like Ghidorah, who is a Holariston Polykraton, she is much like Godzilla. He can wield  atomic energy internally and emit it outward, but he cannot weave it into anything complex. Why? Because their biology didn't evolve for it (though unlike Scylla and the Ice Weavers, the gojira have Limitless Adaptation, so they technically could develop it—they just found it antithetical to their philosophy).

But, you do know what she can generate via willpower? What every Ice Weaver can do, but she can do in unprecedented amounts? The birthright of every Kryoton? That's right.

## Analysis

You've laid out the foundation perfectly and ended with the exact right, tantalizing question. Let's walk through it.

---

### **Scylla's Power Set: A Layered Analysis**

#### **I. The Psionic Foundation: Hydrokinesis (The Scalpel)**

This is the power she has always known, the source of her initial "freak" status. It is a purely mental, psionic ability.

*   **The Five Pillars of Psychokinesis:** You've defined the metrics perfectly.
    *   **Volume:** The *amount* of matter she can control.
    *   **Strength:** The *force* she can exert with that matter.
    *   **Stability:** The *consistency* of her control.
    *   **Fluidity:** The *grace and precision* of her control.
    *   **Creativity:** The *novelty and ingenuity* of her applications.
*   **Scylla's Psionic Profile:**
    *   She is **unmatched in Strength.** Her raw telekinetic force on water is second only to Mothra's general-purpose telekinesis. This is her signature psionic trait.
    *   Her Volume, Stability, Fluidity, and Creativity are all top-tier, rivaled only by other master Hydrokinetics like Leviathan, Manda, and Bakunawa in their respective areas of expertise.
*   **The Crucial Limitation:** As a pure Psychokinetic, she **cannot generate water.** She can only manipulate what is already present in the environment—be it in the air, on the ground, or within another object.

#### **II. The Krátonic Limitation: The Kryoton's Nature (The Hammer)**

This is the power of her species, the one she shares with every other Ice Weaver. It is a Krátonic ability, an expression of will through biology.

*   **The Gojira Parallel:** You are absolutely right. A Kryoton's control over ice is like a Gojira's control over atomic energy. It is a **projection**, not a fine-tuned manipulation. They can generate it from within and project it outward, but they cannot "weave it into anything complex" once it has left them. Their biology simply did not evolve for that level of external control. A typical Ice Weaver can produce a thread of ice and project it, but they cannot then reshape that thread into a complex sculpture mid-air.

---

### **The Great Synthesis: The Question and The Answer**

So, we have a contradiction. Her Psionics allow her to manipulate but not generate water. Her Krátos allows her to generate a substance, but not manipulate it with finesse.

And this leads to your brilliant, final question:


The answer is **ICE.**

This is the core of her hybrid genius.

1.  A normal Ice Weaver's Krátos allows them to **generate ice threads.** This is their one and only trick.
2.  A normal Hydrokinetic can **manipulate water, ice, and steam,** but cannot generate them.
3.  Scylla, the Krateira and the Hydrokinetic, has discovered the ultimate synergy. She uses her **Krátonic will**—her birthright as a Kryoton—to **generate vast, unprecedented quantities of pure, solid ice** from her own being. And then, she immediately uses her **Psionic gift**—her Hydrokinesis—to **seize control of that newly generated ice and sculpt it with the precision of a master artist.**

She is her own source of raw material *and* the master craftswoman. Where a normal Ice Weaver shoots a simple thread, Scylla can generate a glacier's worth of ice and simultaneously carve it into a thousand razor-sharp spears. Where a normal Hydrokinetic needs to find a lake to be a threat, Scylla *is* the lake.

This is why her **Volume** in ice manipulation is so utterly unmatched. Other Hydrokinetics are limited by the environment. Scylla's only limit is her own immense, Conqueror-level willpower. She is the perfect, terrifying fusion of the Hammer and the Scalpel.
