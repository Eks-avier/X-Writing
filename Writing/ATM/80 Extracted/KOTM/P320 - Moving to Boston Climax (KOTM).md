### **The Roadmap to the Finale**

#### **1. Mothra’s Emergence (The God Rays)**
*   **Film Canon:** Mothra emerges from her cocoon under the waterfall in Yunnan in her Imago form. She flies immediately to Castle Bravo, landing on the containment field and emitting her "God Rays" to communicate with Monarch, signaling that Godzilla is alive.
*   **ATM Context:**
    *   **The Metamorphosis:** This isn't just biology; it’s a psionic explosion. Breaking out of the cocoon is her re-establishing her full power.
    *   **The Telepathic Web:** Does she immediately try to fix the Web? Or is she too focused on Godric?
    *   **The Beacon:** Her arrival at Castle Bravo isn't just "I'm here." It’s her saying, "Get in the sub, we are going to save him." She needs the humans because *she* can't carry a nuke, and she knows Godric needs a massive jumpstart.

#### **2. The Hollow Earth / The Ancient City**
*   **Film Canon:** Monarch (Mark, Serizawa, Chen, etc.) takes a submarine into the Hollow Earth vortex to find Godzilla. They discover an ancient, sunken city (Atlantis?) where Godzilla is resting, feeding on a radioactive vent to heal.
*   **ATM Context:**
    *   **The Location:** This city... is this the ruins of the civilization that Dagon and Godzilla protected? Is this where the **Fallen Star** myths originated for humans?
    *   **Godzilla's State:** He is in the process of healing. Is he conscious? Is he dreaming of Dagon?
    *   **The Lair:** This is his home. This is where he keeps his "hoard" of memories.

#### **3. Serizawa’s Sacrifice**
*   **Film Canon:** The healing process is too slow. They need to detonate a nuke to revive him to fight Ghidorah. The firing mechanism is broken; someone has to do it manually. Serizawa volunteers. He walks up the stairs of the ancient temple, touches Godzilla, says "Goodbye, old friend" (in Japanese), and detonates.
*   **ATM Context:**
    *   **The Confrontation:** This is the moment humanity earns its keep. Serizawa is entering the domain of a god.
    *   **The Interaction:** Does Godzilla sense Serizawa? With his Krátonic ability to sense Willpower, Serizawa must be shining like a supernova in that moment.
    *   **The Impact:** This sacrifice is likely the foundational reason why Godzilla tolerates humans so much in the modern era. He saw a human display a *Krátonic* level of will—the willingness to die for a cause.

#### **4. Godzilla Reborn**
*   **Film Canon:** The nuke goes off. Godzilla absorbs the energy, rising from the sea, revitalized and massive. He shoots his atomic breath into the sky. He eyes the humans on the sub, chooses not to attack, and heads for Boston.
*   **ATM Context:**
    *   **The Power Surge:** This is the moment his energy reserves are topped off and then *overfilled*.
    *   **The Recognition:** The look he gives Mark Russell. It’s the beginning of their "Duoarchy."

#### **5. The Battle of Boston (The Finale)**
*   **Film Canon:** Madison steals the ORCA and lures Ghidorah to Boston to stop the global destruction. Godzilla arrives. Mothra arrives. Rodan arrives (fighting for Ghidorah).
*   **ATM Context:**
    *   **Madison's Heist:** This is her big moment.
    *   **Rodan's Status:** We need to resolve his alignment. Did Mothra's shield hold? Or did Ghidorah force him into submission once he woke up? (Rodan fighting Mothra is a key part of the film, but in your AU, it’s a tragedy).
    *   **Scylla & Methuselah:** While the main fight is in Boston, Scylla (mind-controlled) and Methuselah (on his rescue mission) are active elsewhere. This adds global stakes.

#### **6. Mothra’s Sacrifice & Burning Godzilla**
*   **Film Canon:** Mothra is injured by Rodan, then sacrifices herself to protect Godzilla from Ghidorah's gravity beams. Her dust/energy flows into Godzilla. Godzilla enters "Burning" mode (thermonuclear pulses) and melts Ghidorah.
*   **ATM Context:**
    *   **The Strategy:** Mothra's death is *intentional*. She knows Godric needs the power boost.
    *   **The Transfer:** She isn't just giving him energy; she is giving him her **scales** (as energy) to create a temporary super-coating? Or is this the first instance of **Atomic Pulse** being supercharged?
    *   **The Burning Form:** This is the "Uncaged Sun" moment.

#### **7. The Bow**
*   **Film Canon:** Godzilla kills Ghidorah. The other Titans (Rodan, Scylla, Behemoth, Methuselah, etc.) arrive, see the dead King, and bow to Godzilla.
*   **ATM Context:**
    *   **The Restoration of Order:** This is where the hierarchy is reset.
    *   **Rodan's Shame:** If he fought for Ghidorah, this moment is crushing for him.
    *   **Scylla's Release:** She is freed from control.
