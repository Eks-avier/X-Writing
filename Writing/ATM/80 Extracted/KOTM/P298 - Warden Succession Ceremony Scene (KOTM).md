# Lets Transform The Creation Of These
> **Source:** `Formatted_With_Thoughts - The Antitheriomorphosis - KOTM Rewrite.md` — Prompt 298

## Your Notes

Let's transform the creation of these Games into one, cohesive, connected, fleshed out scene. Let's set it from Kong's perspective.

## Analysis

The air in the Alpha Wing’s grand living quarters hung heavy, not with the threat of violence, but with something infinitely more dangerous: the boredom of a god.

Kevin Kent—King Kong, the Alpha Sovereign of the Hollow Earth—sat on a reinforced sofa that cost more than most houses, nursing a mixing bowl full of fruit like it was a teacup. He watched Godric pace. It wasn’t a nervous pace; it was the prowl of a tiger in a cage that had grown too small.

Godric stopped at the floor-to-ceiling window overlooking the Pacific, his silhouette imposing against the setting sun.

"A tournament," the King of Monsters rumbled. It wasn't a question. It was a decision seeking an arena.

Maria, curled up on the adjacent loveseat with a book, didn't even look up. "He's trying to say he misses hitting things, Kevin. But he’s too dignified to just start a brawl."

"Aye," Godric turned, a spark of gold flickering in his blue eyes. "But not just a brawl, my hen. A spectacle. The Wardens are getting restless. Rodan is vibrating. Scylla is freezing ocean lanes out of spite. They need focus." He paused, a grin spreading through his beard. "And I need entertainment."

Kevin swallowed a melon whole. "So... you want us to fight them?"

"One on one combat," Godric confirmed, crossing his massive arms. "If they win the qualifiers, they get a shot at the crown. Or rather, the crown holders."

Kevin set the bowl down. His ears couldn't quite believe it. "One on one combat. Is that right?"

"Aye. What of it?"

He felt the urge to sigh. This man-lizard... he ruled by being the strongest thing in existence, so he assumed everyone else could just casually take on all comers.

"I'm not trying to sell myself short, Gric," Kevin said, leaning forward, his elbows on his knees. "I can handle myself. But you do realize I'm nowhere *near* the top ten in terms of raw power output on the Surface, right? If Scylla or Rodan decides they want a piece of me, it’s not going to be a 'sparring match.' It’s going to be a demolition."

Godric stroked his beard thoughtfully. Kevin had to admit, the facial hair suited him. He’d started growing his own out a year ago, a subconscious mirroring of the man he now called a brother.

"We'll make it so that winning against you would grant a specific reward," Godric mused. "Tailor the prize to the opponent."

"Could work." An idea sprung from Kevin’s head, a tactical filter. "Most of the powerhouses are Surface Titans, right? The ones who just want glory or territory up here?"

"Aye."

"Then, winning against me should *only* grant a reward useful for Hollow Earth Denizens," Kevin declared. "Mineral rights, territory in the Deep, safe passage. That keeps the bored Surface heavyweights off my back and lets me focus on the guys I actually need to manage."

Maria finally looked up from her book, a sharp, amused glint in her sapphire eyes. "And what if a Surface Titan is shrewd enough to challenge you anyway? Perhaps they want a safe haven in the Hollow Earth? Or perhaps," she smirked, "they just want to say they beat a King."

*Drat.* She had a point.

Kevin shrugged, a massive roll of shoulders. "Then I fight them anyway. And I try to win."

"Aye! That's my man!" Godric roared with laughter, pointing a finger at Kevin.

Maria rolled her eyes, though her smile betrayed her. "Of course. You boys will be boys." She turned her gaze to her boyfriend. "Is it too late to abstain? I have duties."

Godric leaned over the back of her chair, his voice dropping to that gravelly, velvety register that Kevin tried very hard to ignore. "No cuddles for a year?"

*Thwack.*

Maria slapped his arm without looking. Kevin chuckled.

"Moron," she said affectionately. "You have no right to blackmail me. You need the cuddles more than I do."

"But what about your rewards?" Kevin asked, steering the ship back to business.

Godric waved a hand dismissively. "Ah, that's easy. From me, **anything**. A promotion, a pardon, a new territory. If they can beat me, they’ve earned it." He gestured towards Maria. "From her, anything from the humans. A law change, a sanctuary, a cease-fire."

Maria pushed Godric away as he tried to nuzzle her hair. "We should band together and just fight him instead. Save everyone the trouble."

Kevin couldn't stifle the laughter this time. "No can do, my Lady. That's your boyfriend. You picked him."

A concern crept up on him. "How much time do we have to prepare? Logistics, arenas, getting the word out... I assume a couple of months?"

"We have Battra for that, don't we?" Godric said, walking toward the kitchen. "Call him up and his students. We'll have ourselves a tournament in less than a week."

Kevin jolted. The air in the room suddenly changed. It wasn't the heavy pressure of Godric; it was static. The hairs on his arms stood up, and the taste of ozone filled his mouth.

"I heard my name."

The voice boomed from above them.

Kevin looked up. Floating near the vaulted ceiling, sitting cross-legged on absolutely nothing as if he were lounging on an invisible throne, was the Lord of the Mystic Arts. He was wearing a deep purple robe that defied gravity, looking down at them with an expression of supreme, unbothered arrogance.

*What a pompous ass,* Kevin thought fondly. *Only he would hover in a room with the three most powerful beings on Earth.*

"What's this racket all about?" Battra asked, descending slowly like a feather.

"A tournament," Godric hollered from the fridge, not bothering to look up.

"A way to assuage his boredom," Maria added, returning to her book. "As per usual."

Battra landed soundlessly, smoothing his robes. "You never do change, old friend."

Kevin shook his head. *Honestly, how do you even begin to get used to this?* To the outside world, they were gods. In here, they were a sitcom.

"You in?" Godric asked, returning with a beverage.

Kevin saw the grin spread across the sorcerer's face. It was sharp, intelligent, and just as unnerving as Godric's power.

"An excellent idea," Battra purred. "And a perfect opportunity to include my students. Shall we run a parallel tournament? Sorcery versus Psionics? It would be a perfect final exam for this year. A public demonstration of their superiority."

Godric clapped his hands once, the sound like a gunshot. "Then it's settled. My hen—" he turned to Maria. "—inform everyone. From the lowest Denizen to the Wardens. Use the Web. I'll inform Alex and Rom myself. They'll need to get the word to the Primals."

Battra flicked a finger, and a notebook materialized in the air, a pen scribbling furiously on its own. "If we wish to have this in a week, we must move with haste."

Godric raised a brow, taking a sip of his drink. "We can give you a fortnight if you need it—"

"Nonsense." Battra’s head snapped up, his eyes narrowing. "Do you take me for unproductive? Do you mistake me for a bureaucrat?"

Kevin flinched. Even *he* wouldn't cut off the Paramount like that. But the bond between the Trinity played by different rules.

"By tomorrow," Battra declared, his voice rising with theatrical flare, "the Gateways shall be recalibrated to receive a 400% increase in traffic. The physical foundations will be laid beside the Villa by sunrise."

He floated up a few inches, unable to contain his energy. "No need to call on your Wardens for labor; they would only slow me down. The next 48 hours shall be allotted for the creation of the stands, the obstacle courses, and the elemental arenas. I will personally oversee the enchantment of the spectator shields."

He turned his gaze to Kevin. "Sovereign, I will need the coordinates of your preferred Hollow Earth biomes for the environmental simulation." Then to Maria. "Sister, I require you to dampen the local weather patterns so my construction spells are not interrupted by rain."

He turned back to Godric, a smirk playing on his lips. "The rest of the week shall be spent on mental and physical preparation, for both student and Titan alike. It would also give Monarch enough time to process what we've just planned. If their primitive minds can catch up, that is."

Maria rolled her eyes, but she closed her book. "Show-off."

Godric smiled, a genuine, excited expression. "Good."

And Kevin... well. *Damn.* He leaned further into the couch, watching the whirlwind of planning begin.

"Not a moment wasted," he muttered to himself. The world was about to change again, just because the King was bored. And honestly? He couldn't wait to see it.
