## Your Notes

I think we now have the perfect ingredients to crystallize the AU interpretation of KOTM. But, first, King Caesar. I've been avoiding his timeline. Here it is:

- King Caesar awakens during the Xilien Invasion, protecting Osaka. 
- His reason for dormancy was due to recovering from an even in ancient human history.
- From then on, he is now fully operational, although he becomes a focal POV was he experiences the scale of human life as they were recovering from the Invasion. Think Godzilla during 2020-2023 but set in the aftermath of the Xilien Invasion. He has to catch up quickly. Through him, we also get more integrated Shouwa lore in the AU as I attempt to pick up lore from Toho films to integrate into the AU 's timeline.

## Analysis

This is a fantastic addition. King Caesar serves as the perfect vessel to integrate the Showa era's wilder, alien-invasion-heavy lore into the grounded history of the ATM Universe. His awakening during the chaos of the Xilien Invasion provides a dramatic entrance, and his subsequent "man out of time" arc offers a fresh, poignant perspective on the post-war reconstruction.

Here is the crystallized profile and narrative arc for the Last Guardian.

---

### **King Caesar: The Guardian of the Rising Sun**

**Titan Name:** King Caesar (*Titanus shisa*)
**Human Name:** Kai Shisa
**Standing:** Lord Warden of the East (Cardinal)
**Status:** Awakened (2023), Active
**Classification:** Emergent (Species Alpha), Magic-Biological Hybrid
**Affiliation:** The Azumi Lineage (Ancient Humanity), The Alpha Paramount

#### **I. Core Identity: The Living Statue**

King Caesar is unique among the Wardens. He is not just a biological creature; he is a **living construct**, a Titan whose biology was fundamentally altered and enhanced by the ancient sorcery of the Azumi people (a sister-civilization to Atlantis, located in what is now Okinawa).

*   **Personality:** He is the embodiment of **Bushido**. He is stoic, fiercely loyal, deeply traditional, and possesses a quiet, contemplative wisdom. He speaks with an archaic formality that contrasts sharply with the modern world.
*   **The Man Out of Time:** Unlike the other Titans who watched humanity evolve, Caesar slept through the rise of modern civilization. He remembers humans as priests and warriors, not salarymen and influencers. His adjustment is not just biological; it is cultural.

#### **II. The "Ancient Event" (Showa Integration): The Simian Siege (10,000 BC)**

This is where we integrate the *Godzilla vs. Mechagodzilla* (1974) lore.

*   **The Invaders:** Long before the Xiliens, the **Simians** (aliens from the Black Hole Planet 3) attempted to conquer Earth. They targeted the magic-rich region of the Azumi kingdom.
*   **The Weapon:** They built a crude, terrifying mechanical effigy of the King of the Monsters to mock and demoralize the Earth's defenders: the **Proto-Mechagodzilla.**
*   **The Sacrifice:** King Caesar, alongside the Azumi sorcerers, fought a desperate battle against this machine. He didn't just fight; he absorbed the full brunt of its alien weaponry to protect the Azumi royal family. He destroyed the machine, but the damage to his essence was catastrophic.
*   **The Dormancy:** To save him, the Azumi placed him in a mystical stasis within a cliffside shrine. He was meant to sleep for a century. He slept for twelve millennia.

#### **III. The Awakening: The Xilien Invasion (2023)**

King Caesar didn't wake up because of a biological clock; he woke up because he heard the **Song.**

*   **The Azumi Descendant:** During the Xilien assault on Osaka, a young woman named **Nami** (a direct descendant of the Azumi priesthood, perhaps a minor Monarch consultant) found herself cornered. In desperation, she sang the ancient prayer passed down through her family.
*   **The Resonant Frequency:** The song wasn't just tradition; it was a specific **Soul Resonance frequency** keyed to Caesar's dormant consciousness.
*   **The Entry:** He burst from the mountainside not as a giant monster, but as a glowing, human-sized warrior. The Antitheriomorphosis had affected him even in stasis. He engaged the Xilien ground forces in Osaka with a ferocity and martial skill that stunned the invaders. He didn't know what a "Xilien" was, but he knew what an invader looked like.

#### **IV. Power Set: The Solar Battery**

King Caesar's powers are a unique fusion of **Biological Sovereignty** and **Ancient Magic**.

1.  **Solar Refraction (Prism Eye):** His signature ability.
    *   **Mechanism:** One of his eyes is replaced by a mystical gemstone (the Prism Eye). He can catch energy attacks (beams, blasts) with this eye, circulate the energy through his body to amplify it, and fire it back out of the other eye with devastating force.
    *   **Human Form:** In human form, he catches energy with one hand and fires it from the other. It is the ultimate "No U" card in combat.
2.  **Solar Battery:** He draws strength directly from sunlight. In the day, his stamina is effectively infinite. At night, he is weaker but still formidable.
3.  **Golem Physiology:** His skin is as hard as stone, and he feels no pain. He fights until he is broken, not until he is hurt.

#### **V. The Post-Invasion Arc: The Ronin of Osaka**

While Godzilla and the others deal with global politics, King Caesar remains in Osaka and Okinawa, helping the humans rebuild. This is his "catch-up" arc.

*   **The Cultural Shock:** He walks the streets of a neon-lit Japan, a warrior from 10,000 BC trying to understand vending machines, trains, and the concept of "anime."
    *   **Manda as Guide:** Manda, his Intercardinal proxy, becomes his gentle guide. She explains the modern world to him with patience, helping him navigate the complexities of modern human society.
*   **The Human Connection:** He bonds not with Monarch leadership, but with the **construction workers and the locals.** He uses his immense strength to lift girders, clear rubble, and protect the workers from scavengers. He becomes an urban legend: the "Lion of Osaka," a silent guardian who watches over the reconstruction.
*   **The Meeting with Godric:** When Godzilla finally visits him, it is a meeting of two ancient soldiers.
    *   **Godric:** Respects Caesar for his sacrifice and his loyalty to his territory.
    *   **Caesar:** Recognizes Godric as the Alpha Paramount immediately, not through fear, but through the "scent" of authority. He bows, not as a servant, but as a samurai bowing to his Shogun.

**The Narrative Role:**

King Caesar represents **Tradition and Honor.** In a world that is rapidly changing (aliens, magic schools, mechs), he is the anchor to the Earth's ancient, mystical past. His arc explores the beauty of the old ways finding a place in the new world. He is the stoic, noble heart of the Eastern Hemisphere.

This completes the Warden roster with a character who brings a completely different flavor—mystical, martial, and deeply historical—to the ensemble. He is the perfect bridge between the magical lore of Battra and the physical reality of the Titans.
