# Next Is Defining The Scope These
> **Source:** `Formatted_With_Thoughts - The Antitheriomorphosis - KOTM Rewrite.md` — Prompt 293

## Your Notes

Next is defining the scope these titles encompass. The Paramount rules over everything in the World of the Titans. A Sovereign rules over a realm assigned by the Paramount. A Warden rules over a hemisphere of the Earth. How would we logically define the scope of authority for other Ranks?

## Analysis

This is the final step in making the hierarchy a functional map of the world. To define these scopes logically, we must look at **Geological and Ecological boundaries**. Titans don't care about human borders (lines on a map); they care about **Biogeographic Realms**—areas defined by plate tectonics, climate, and the flow of energy.

Following the established pattern (Planetary $\rightarrow$ Realmic $\rightarrow$ Hemispheric), here is the logical breakdown of the remaining Ranks.

---

### **The Jurisdictions of the Sapient Court**

#### **1. Alpha Paramount: Planetary Scope**
*   **Jurisdiction:** The Global Biosphere.
*   **Definition:** All matter and energy within the Earth's gravity well, spanning both the Surface and the Hollow Earth.
*   **Authority:** The "Monopoly on Violence." He sets the global laws that all other Ranks must enforce.

#### **2. Alpha Sovereign: Realmic Scope**
*   **Jurisdiction:** A Fundamental Realm (e.g., The Surface World or The Hollow Earth).
*   **Definition:** The entirety of one of the two primary layers of the planet.
*   **Authority:** Legislative and Executive. They interpret the Paramount’s laws for their specific realm and manage the Wardens within it.

#### **3. Warden Rank: Hemispheric Scope**
*   **Jurisdiction:** A Hemispheric Zonal quadrant (Cardinal/Intercardinal).
*   **Definition:** Massive, multi-continental zones (e.g., The Southern Hemisphere, The Northeast Asian Quadrant).
*   **Authority:** Administrative. They oversee the "big picture" of climate and energy flow. They are the ones who report to the Alphas if a continent-scale disaster is looming.

---

### **The "Grounded" Ranks: From Continents to Niches**

#### **4. Regional Rank: Continental / Basin Scope**
*   **Jurisdiction:** A Major Tectonic Plate or Continental Mass.
*   **Definition:** An area roughly the size of a continent (Australia, South America) or a major ocean basin (The North Atlantic).
*   **Authority:** The highest rank for "Natural Lords." They manage the overall peace between different species within a major landmass.
*   **Examples:** Behemoth (pre-Warden) ruled the Amazonian/South American mass.

#### **5. Provincial Rank: Sub-Continental / Feature Scope**
*   **Jurisdiction:** A Biogeographic Province (e.g., A massive mountain range, a sea, or a vast desert).
*   **Definition:** A large, distinct geographical feature that dictates the climate of a surrounding area (The Andes, The Mediterranean, The Gobi Desert).
*   **Authority:** "Duke-level." They manage the stability of that specific feature.
*   **Methuselah’s Context:** His original Provincial-equivalent territory covered the vast mountain and forest tracts of Central/Northern Europe.

#### **6. Municipal Rank: Ecosystem / Hub Scope**
*   **Jurisdiction:** A Macro-Ecosystem (e.g., A specific large forest, a river system, or a volcanic chain).
*   **Definition:** A self-contained ecological system or a hub of resources (The Nile Delta, The Great Barrier Reef, The Cascadia Forest).
*   **Authority:** "Mayor-level." They ensure the biological "services" of that ecosystem (cleaning water, providing food) continue without disruption.

#### **7. Territorial Rank: Site-Specific / Niche Scope**
*   **Jurisdiction:** A Micro-Ecosystem or Niche (e.g., An island, a valley, a cave system, or a trench).
*   **Definition:** A singular, well-defined location of high importance (Monster Island, Skull Island, The Mariana Trench, Mount Fuji).
*   **Authority:** "Chieftain-level." They are responsible for the immediate residents and the physical integrity of a single location.

---

### **The Non-Geographical Ranks: Functional Scope**

#### **8. Vassal Rank: Institutional / Functional Scope**
*   **Jurisdiction:** A Specific Facility, Artifact, or Process.
*   **Definition:** Their authority is not over a piece of the Earth, but over a **function** assigned by the Crown.
*   **Scope:** The boundaries of their office (e.g., The Palace, the smithy, the global portal network).
*   **Barb’s Context:** Her scope is the interior of the Monster Island Palace. Outside those walls, she has no authority.

#### **9. Denizen Rank: Resident Scope**
*   **Jurisdiction:** The Self.
*   **Definition:** No political reach. They have the right to exist within a territory and seek protection from the local ranked Titan, but they cannot issue commands.

### **Political Consequences of the "Scope" System**

1.  **The Warden-Regional Friction:** Because Wardens (Hemispheric) are appointed by the Alphas and Regionals (Continental) are "Natural Lords," there is often tension. A Regional Titan might feel they understand their continent better than a "foreign" Warden who only visits once a month.
2.  **The Importance of Municipalities:** In the Hollow Earth, Municipal Titans rule over "Oases" of energy. Because the Hollow Earth is wilder, these Municipal hubs are the equivalents of major cities, making Municipal Rank very prestigious there.
3.  **The "Gacha" Rule:** If a human enters a Territorial zone (like a specific forest), they must answer to that Protector. If they cross into another forest, the rules might change. Monarch's job is to map these "Niche" boundaries so researchers don't accidentally step from a friendly Municipality into a hostile one.
4.  **Special Positions and Scope:** This explains why **Baragon** is so vital. His scope is **Vertical**. He doesn't rule a square area; he rules the *tunnels* connecting Ranks 3 through 7. He is the elevator operator of the hierarchy.

This "spring cleaning" of scopes makes the Standing Hierarchy feel like a living, breathing geopolitical machine. It clarifies why Godzilla is bored (he’s managing a planetary scale while everyone else is dealing with valleys and rivers) and why the Betas are so busy (they have to travel between all these tiered jurisdictions). We are ready for the next phase!
