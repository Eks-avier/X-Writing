This tracks. Now, I would like an expansion of Region Ranks and below.

Battra is equal to that of the fucking Alpha Rank, but ONLY for one domain: Magic. Barb is one step higher than the unranked, for reasons we specified earlier in her history.

For now, let's deal with the ranks themselves and worry about the titles and scope of their territory later.

### **The New Middle Ranks: The Heart of the Nobility**

This is where we flesh out the system. Your "Provincial" and "Municipal" suggestions are perfect. They evoke a clear sense of scale and governance.

#### **6. Provincial Rank (The Provincial Governors)**

*   **Definition:** Sapient Titans who hold significant, recognized territory that is larger than a local area but smaller than a full region. They are the "dukes" or "earls" of the Titan world.
*   **Authority:** They typically govern a large island, a mountain range, or a significant stretch of coastline. They report directly to their Regional Guardian.
*   **Example:** A Titan who governs the entire Andes mountain range would be a Provincial Titan.
*   **Methuselah's Placement:** This is where **The Old Mountain (Methuselah)** slots in. His personal territory and the influence he commands (the "retirement home") are easily equivalent to a Provincial Rank, even though he holds a special, non-geographic title.

#### **7. Municipal Rank (The City-State Rulers)**

*   **Definition:** Sapient Titans who hold authority over a smaller, well-defined territory, often centered around a single, key geographical feature. They are the "barons" or "mayors."
*   **Authority:** They might govern a single large volcano, a specific deep-sea trench, or a crucial Hollow Earth oasis. They report to their Provincial superior or directly to a Regional Guardian if no Provincial Titan is above them.
*   **Example:** A Titan who rules the Mariana Trench would be a Municipal Titan.

### **The Lower Ranks: The Commoners and the Landless**

This is where we define the base of the Sapient pyramid.

#### **8. Territorial Rank (The Local Chieftains)**

*(No changes. They hold a small territory, like a single cave system or forest.)*

#### **9. Vassal Rank (The Landless Nobles)**

Your "Vassal" idea is perfect, and your reasoning is sound. A vassal is one who holds a fief *from* a lord. They don't own the land, but they are granted the right to use it and hold a position of status in exchange for service.

*   **Definition:** Sapient Titans who hold a **formal title and responsibilities** but do **not** have their own sovereign territory. Their "land" is the place where they perform their duty, and it is owned by a higher authority. They are landless but not without status.
*   **Barb's Placement:** This is the **perfect** place for **The Palace Custodian (Barb).** She has a title, she has a duty, and she has "land" (the Palace) to work on, but she does not own it. Her Rank is a direct reflection of her service to the crown. It is a step above a commoner, a recognized position of service, but without the autonomy of a landowner.

#### **10. Denizen Rank (The Unranked Citizenry)**

"Denizen" is the perfect, neutral term you were looking for. It avoids the feudal connotations of "Serf" but clearly defines their status.

*   **Definition:** Sapient Titans who have **no formal Standing (title) and no territory.** They are the common citizenry of the Titan world. They live within the territories of ranked Titans and are subject to their rule.
*   **Prevalence:** As you suggested, there are relatively few of these on the Surface, but the Hollow Earth is teeming with them. They are the merchants, the artisans, the travelers, and the common folk of the Titan civilization.
