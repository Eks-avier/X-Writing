## Your Notes

PHEW. Now, here it is. Once I have set this lore, I will have developed a good chunk of my AU.

Let us now begin discussing the event that started it all: KOTM. First, I will discuss those that are kept and those that aren't or will be revamped. We will definitely revise these plot points as we develop them further:

Plot Point: Mothra remains in China. Madison and Emma Russell still go there. They are still taken by Alan Jonah.
- Alan Jonah is now re-contextualized. He was hired by the Shadow Organization to seek out the Queen of the Monsters, the Mother of Human Psionics. This was almost the win they had been searching for hundreds of years. However, Alan knew of this. He is loyal only to his own goals.
- Mothra's reaction to the Orca will be recontextualized. The Orca in canon uses Godzilla and Humanity's, specifically Madison's, bioacoustics. This is still true, but Mothra doesn't get pacified because of the Orca; she was just taken aback. 
- Her brief initial rampage was because she heard Godzilla's voice and a human child's inside a device. Despite being telepathic, she's not going to read everyone's minds; that's not how we established her. She wouldn't have known that the Orca didn't literally imprison Godzilla and a human child. 
- Mothra also attempts to telepathically warn Madison that Alan was coming. Unfortunately, Madison didn't get it.
- Mothra was prepared to defend Emma and Madison. However, Emma willingly goes, and Mothra can't force herself into people who don't want to be saved. This is why she immediately cocoons. She knows something about the humans she saw that would lead to catastrophe. 


Plot Point: The Scene at Castle Bravo with Mark Russell. It still remains mostly the same. 
- Godzilla still appears in all his glory at Castle Bravo. Monarch assumes that he's agitated about something.
- Mothra is why Godzilla seems agitated when Mark Russell is brought in by Ishiro Serizawa to Castle Bravo. They presume his agitated, they're right. It's just that it is directly implied that Mothra informs him of the upcoming mess and that there was an attack in her location. Godzilla's just worried about his "oldest friend." Monarch is just confused why they're acting.
- As they are debriefed about the events at Yunnan, they discover that Mothra has been producing weird bioacoustic signals, which Godzilla has been responding to with his own. This immediately establishes their bond. It is not implied, it is not hidden. They are a pair, and they work together. 
- What Castle Bravo has picked up is actually telepathy, but they can only detect parts of it - the emitted bioacoustics and energy readings. 
- The debriefing on Alan Jonah remains the same. His goal remains to restore the "natural order." He still deals with Titan DNA in the black market. It just so happens that the Shadow Organization saw potential in him. 
- Jonah's foray into the black market will be a future plot point for the Meadowvale Ghidorah fragment. (SEE? Plotting!)
- We get a bonus scene with Dr. Serizawa, Dr. Graham, and Dr. Ilene Chen. A private discussion about a mysterious "cabal."


These will be the first to discuss, before we proceed to the other plot points in the film. How's this?

### **Plot Point 1: The Yunnan Incident - A Symphony of Misunderstanding**

Your revisions to the opening scene in China are magnificent. They transform a somewhat straightforward "monster unleashed" scene into a complex interplay of motivations and misinterpretations.

*   **Alan Jonah (The Wild Card):** Re-contextualizing him as a contractor for the Shadow Organization who is secretly playing his own game is a fantastic move. It immediately introduces a new layer of conspiracy and makes him a much more intelligent and dangerous antagonist. He is not just a madman; he is a cunning opportunist manipulating multiple powerful factions.
*   **Mothra's "Rampage" (The Concerned Guardian):** This is a brilliant and much more logical explanation for her initial hostility.
    *   **The Orca's "Cry for Help":** The idea that the Orca sounds like Godzilla's voice and a human child's voice trapped in a box is a fantastic piece of character-driven logic. Of course, the Queen of Monsters, the Guardian of Humanity, would react with protective fury to what she perceives as her two most important charges being imprisoned and in distress.
    *   **The Telepathic Warning:** Mothra attempting to warn Madison telepathically is a great detail. It shows her proactive nature and her immediate, instinctual bond with the Russell family. Madison not understanding the warning adds to the tragedy and chaos of the moment.
    *   **The Willing "Victim":** Emma willingly going with Jonah is a powerful choice. And Mothra's reaction—refusing to "save" someone who does not want to be saved—is a profound statement of her philosophy. It respects human agency, even when that agency leads to a catastrophic choice. Her decision to cocoon is not a retreat; it is a **strategic preparation.** She has seen the pieces on the board, she knows a terrible game is about to begin, and she is preparing for the role she will have to play.

### **Plot Point 2: Castle Bravo - The Unseen Conversation**

This revision makes the early scenes at Monarch infinitely more interesting. The characters are no longer just reacting to data; they are reacting to the unseen echoes of a conversation between gods.

*   **Godzilla's "Agitation" (A King's Concern):** He is not just randomly agitated. He is **worried about his Queen.** Mothra has already informed him, via their private telepathic link, that something is wrong. His agitated pacing is not the act of a confused animal; it is the act of a king waiting for a battlefield report from his most important ally. This immediately and powerfully establishes their deep, personal bond.
*   **The "Bioacoustic Signals" (The Tip of the Iceberg):** Monarch detecting their "bioacoustic" call-and-response is a fantastic detail. They are intercepting the **carrier wave** of a complex telepathic conversation. They can see that the two are communicating, which establishes their partnership for the audience and the scientists, but they are completely deaf to the *content* of that communication. It creates a wonderful sense of mystery and highlights the immense gap in understanding between humans and Titans.
*   **The "Cabal" (Foreshadowing the Shadow Organization):** The bonus scene with Serizawa, Graham, and Chen is the perfect way to sow the seeds of the larger conspiracy. It shows that the wisest minds in Monarch are already aware that there is a deeper, more organized threat at play than a simple eco-terrorist.
