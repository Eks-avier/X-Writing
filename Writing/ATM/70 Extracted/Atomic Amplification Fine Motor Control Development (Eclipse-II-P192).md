## Analysis

This is a fantastic and crucial development for Godzilla's abilities. It's an elegant solution to a real-world physics problem (leverage) that operates perfectly within your established Krátonic rules, reinforces his character, and further distinguishes him from other energy manipulators.

You have correctly identified that this would fall under the **Personal Coating** domain, specifically as an advanced application of **Object Infusion (`Endusis`)**.

Let's break this down and give it a formal place in his arsenal.

---

### **The King's Grip: Structural Infusion (`Stereosis`)**

This technique is a game-changing innovation born from Godzilla's mastery of Atomic Amplification. It is his direct, Krátonic answer to the problem of human-scale leverage, allowing him to apply his full Titan-level strength to massive objects without them collapsing under their own weight.

#### **I. Definition & Royal Analogy**

*   **Formal Name:** Structural Infusion
*   **Krátonic Term:** *Stereosis* (στερέωσις - Greek for "strengthening, making solid, foundation")
*   **The Royal Analogy:** This is **The King's Edict of Fortification.** When Godzilla applies this technique, he is extending his own sovereign durability to an object. He is issuing a silent decree to every atom of that object: **"You will act as one. You will hold. You are, for this moment, as unyielding as I am."**

#### **II. Krátonic Mechanics: An Advanced Application of `Endusis`**

*Stereosis* is an advanced technique that can only be performed while in a state of **Atomic Amplification.**

1.  **The Foundation (Contact):** As with all `Endusis` techniques, it requires direct physical contact. Godzilla must be touching the object he wishes to lift.
2.  **The Mechanism (Field Extension):** He then extends a specialized, cohesive field of his Amplified Aura to "coat" the entire object. This is not a telekinetic field that lifts the object for him. It is a **structural integrity field.**
3.  **The Effect (Reinforcement, Not Movement):** The *Stereosis* field's sole purpose is to reinforce the object's molecular structure, binding it together with atomic-level force. It temporarily grants the object a durability approaching his own. This allows him to lift a 747 by its nose wing because the field prevents the wing from ripping off under the plane's immense weight. **The lift is still a purely physical act performed by him; the field just makes the object liftable.**

#### **III. The Consequence: The Radiant Contamination ("Death Touch")**

This is the inherent and terrifying cost of the technique.

*   **Inevitable Irradiation:** The *Stereosis* field is composed of his raw, concentrated atomic energy. The object being coated is bathed in an impossibly high dose of radiation. Any living being inside or near the object would suffer from instantaneous, catastrophic radiation poisoning.
*   **Degradation:** Against non-living objects, prolonged application can cause their physical structure to degrade and decay, as if aged thousands of years in seconds.
*   **The Ultimate Skill Ceiling (Modulation):** The true mark of Godzilla's mastery is not just using this power, but using it **non-lethally.** To lift a collapsing bridge section with civilians still on it, he must modulate the energy of the field with a surgeon's precision—providing just enough structural integrity to prevent collapse, while suppressing the lethal radiation to survivable levels. This is an immense psychological and physical strain, a feat only he is capable of.

#### **IV. Limitations & Thematic Resonance**

This ability perfectly reinforces the established limitations and philosophy of the *gojira* lineage.

*   **The Limit of External Manipulation:** This is the absolute peak of their external energy control. As you said, his energy is too stubborn and explosive to be sculpted into constructs. It can be projected (Atomic Breath) or used to reinforce (Amplification/Infusion), but it cannot be given independent form. It is a blunt instrument, not a sculptor's clay.
*   **Why the Children are Different:** The children *can* create constructs precisely because their *mosura* heritage grants them a "gentler" and more "flexible" nature via their potent psionic potential (Telekinesis). They have a component in their "personality physics" that their father lacks, allowing them to coax their stubborn Gojira energy into shapes and forms.
*   **The Gojira Philosophy:** This ability is the ultimate expression of a *gojira's* approach to a problem. He doesn't want to float the plane with his mind; that's a psychic's trick. He wants to *lift it*. His power does not replace his physical action; it **enables** it. It is a direct, tangible, and overwhelmingly powerful solution to a physical problem, which is the very heart of the Krátos system.
