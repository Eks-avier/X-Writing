# Koinon vs Krator Progression Paths

> **Source:** `Eclipse II` - Prompt 3 (Lines 441-581)

## Your Notes

Expansion should definitely be kept. To resolve our issue, I'm "splitting" Flaring and defining it only as the strengthening of one's Aura effects. The *enlargement* of one's Aura *range* is the new ability Expansion.

Your solution is elegant. Koinon produce "traces" of Aura, sparks in the environment. The example human Pyroton would appear feverishly warm at birth. This is irrepressible; it is their signature. The moment they are born, their Themelion ensures they Manifest, their Aura "pouring out" into the world, though faintly.

But, to actually manifest their Manifestation properly via their Aura, they need training to activate it. This entwines Flaring and Expansion, serving as the most essential for a Koinon to learn off rip.

This is in opposition to Krator. Their First Layer has always been active since birth while their Second Layer remains active as long as they are in Alignment. Their progression option is inversed as a consequence: they must learn to be able to suppress their Aura to a environmentally and socially acceptable degree (if that's what they want to pursue; no way someone like Ghidorah will be doing that).

## Analysis

This is perhaps the most impactful new addition, beautifully addressing the previous contradiction and adding immense depth to the system's learning curves.

### Koinon Learning Curve

* **"Traces" of Aura at birth:** This is elegant. It maintains the "irrepressible" aspect without having an unactivated Koinon walking around with a full, dangerous Aura. A "feverishly warm" Pyroton at birth is a perfect image.

* **Training for proper Manifestation via Aura:** This makes sense. They're born with the potential and latent traces, but actively *channeling* it into a proper Aura (which then enables Flaring and Expansion) requires conscious learning and control. This provides a clear, essential training path for Koinons.

* **Progression Inversed for Koinon:** Their journey is about *activating* and then *controlling* their inherent power.

### Krator Learning Curve

* **First Layer always active:** This is established and powerful.

* **Second Layer active in Alignment:** This means a Krator who has achieved Symphonia already has a robust, active Aura.

* **Progression Inversed for Krator:** Their journey is primarily about *suppression*--learning to dial down an already overwhelming presence to an "environmentally and socially acceptable degree." This is a fantastic challenge that deeply connects to their psychological state and need for control (Godzilla's "Caged Sun" is perfectly reflected here, as is Ghidorah's likely disinterest in suppression).

### Benefit of this Inversion

* **Enhanced Uniqueness:** This further differentiates the Kraton types beyond just their genetic inheritance. Their *learning journeys* and *challenges* are fundamentally different, leading to distinct character arcs.

* **Narrative Richness:**
  * Koinons: Overcoming inherent passivity, learning to project and control their power.
  * Krators: Overcoming inherent overwhelmingness, learning restraint and precision.

* **Thematic Resonance:** It aligns with the idea that Kratons are either "learning to become" their power (Koinon) or "learning to contain" their power (Krator).
