# Godzilla BJJ and Sumo Wrestling Consideration

> **Source:** `Formatted_With_Thoughts - The Eclipse of the ATM AU II.md` — Prompt 189 (Lines 14763-14813)

## Your Notes

I wonder if he'd use BJJ, or Sumo, too? What do you think?

## Analysis

That's an excellent question, and exploring it reveals even more about the nuances of his fighting philosophy. He would absolutely analyze and draw principles from both BJJ and Sumo, but his adoption would be highly selective and filtered through his core doctrine. He wouldn't become a "Sumo wrestler" or a "BJJ practitioner"; he would extract their most brutal and efficient tools and integrate them into his "Gojira-Ryu."

---

### **Integration of Additional Grappling Arts**

#### **I. Sumo Wrestling: The Art of Unmovable Mass**

Godzilla would have a profound, almost spiritual respect for Sumo. It is a martial art built on principles that are deeply resonant with his own *gojira* nature.

*   **Why it Fits:** Sumo is the art of **dominating through a low center of gravity, overwhelming forward pressure, and immovable stability.** It is, in many ways, the human martial art that most closely mirrors a Titan's approach to combat.
*   **His Application:** He wouldn't participate in Sumo rituals or become a *rikishi*, but he would assimilate its core tactical principles into his stance and clinch work.
    *   **The Tachiai (The Initial Charge):** The explosive forward charge at the start of a Sumo match is a perfect human-scale expression of Godzilla's inexorable advance. He would perfect this, using his immense leg strength to generate unstoppable momentum from a dead stop, closing the distance and shattering his opponent's stance instantly.
    *   **Tsuki/Oshi (Thrusting and Pushing):** He would master the art of using open-palm strikes and relentless forward pressure to drive opponents back, denying them space and keeping them perpetually off-balance. This perfectly complements his "pressure fighting" style.
    *   **Yorikiri (Force Out):** The classic Sumo victory of gripping the opponent's belt and relentlessly driving them out of the ring is a perfect analogy for his clinch-and-dismantle strategy. He would adopt the footwork and body positioning needed to become an unstoppable wall of force.
    *   **The Stomp's Ancestor (Shiko):** The ritual leg-stomping exercise of Sumo wrestlers would be something he'd find deeply familiar. He would see it as a human-scale echo of his own signature move, a way to connect with the earth and display one's power and stability.

**Sumo's Role:** Sumo would primarily influence his **stance, forward pressure, and the mindset of being an unmovable, dominant force.**

#### **II. Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu (BJJ): The Science of Submission**

Godzilla's relationship with BJJ would be more clinical and predatory. He would view it not as a "gentle art," but as a terrifyingly efficient library of **joint destruction and incapacitation.** He would have little interest in the sport aspect (points, guard passing), but would be fascinated by its lethality.

*   **Why it Fits:** BJJ is the science of what to do when a fight goes to the ground. For Godzilla, who excels at takedowns, mastering this is the logical next step.
*   **His Application:** He would not "roll" with opponents. He would use BJJ techniques as brutal, fight-ending **finishers** after a successful takedown.
    *   **Positional Dominance:** He would master the core positions of BJJ—mount, side control, back control—not to set up a submission, but as platforms for delivering devastating, short-range strikes. His immense weight and strength would make these positions an inescapable vise.
    *   **The Predator's Submissions (Chokes):** While he can crush a windpipe with a simple grip, he would appreciate the cold, inescapable efficiency of a **Rear Naked Choke** or a **Guillotine Choke**. For him, these are not just submissions; they are the grappling equivalent of a predator's killing bite to the neck.
    *   **Joint Manipulation (The Brutal Application):** He would view joint locks like the **Kimura**, **Armbar**, and **Heel Hook** not as techniques for forcing a tap-out, but as brutally efficient methods for **dismemberment.** He's not trying to get his opponent to quit; he's trying to tear their arm off. The clinical, anatomical precision of BJJ would appeal to his analytical mind as the quickest way to permanently disable a threat.

**BJJ's Role:** BJJ would become his **ground-game finishing school.** It provides the final, lethal tools he needs to end a fight once his Sumo-inspired takedowns have brought the opponent into his world.

### **The Final, Synthesized Doctrine ("Gojira-Ryu") - Updated**

With these additions, his final style is even more complete and terrifying.

**A relentlessly advancing, pressure-based fighting style that uses a foundation of Boxing defense to close the distance, then employs the overwhelming forward drive and takedowns of Sumo Wrestling to bring an opponent into the clinch. In the clinch, he systematically dismantles them with the devastating strikes of Muay Thai. If the fight goes to the ground, he establishes dominant control and finishes it with the brutal, anatomical finality of Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu submissions, all while reserving the primal, unexpected threat of a Kaiju's bite as his ultimate wild card. This entire system is powered by the god-like physical enhancements of Atomic Amplification.**

He is a complete and total combat engine, a perfect fusion of ancient Titan instinct and the most effective, brutal principles of human martial science. He has truly become the Apex Predator Perfected.
