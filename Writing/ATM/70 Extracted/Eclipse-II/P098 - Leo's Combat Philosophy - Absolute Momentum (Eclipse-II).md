Let us now move on to Leonard Aurelius Nordson!

Leo... has no fighting style. He is nothing but brute force, leveraging his catastrophic strength, tangible or intangible, to bulldoze opponents and the environment they're in.

His CQC speaks to this principle the most. When enhanced by AA and his telekinesis, it's devastating. He doesn't need Junior's Magnifying Scales to bring an enemy to the brink. Though, naturally, Junior *can* magnify Leo.

Not to mention that his flight and speed makes him hard to track and sustain against. He's not a slow tank, he's a flying freight train. With his flight, he can build momentum; he will body slam, headbutt, and dropkick from several kilometers away.

With AA, coating with Penetration becomes easy and more devastating for his enemies as it is backed by his sheer strength. Crucially, he has mastered *Injection* first undeniably. While Junior understands the principle and Lora has a functional one, Leo's Injection is the most potent and, ironically, the most elegant—simply because he understands the underlying principle of the technique. It's a gambit, and Leo's base form fighting style is all about gambits.

His energy-based attacks are also indicative of this, but due to his base form, many of his specific mosura attacks are unavailable. Thus, he relies on the Atomic Breath often. Unfortunately, dur to the massive energy requirement of the Atomic Railgun, he can only do so one or two at a time before fatiguing. Critically, due to his speedy recovery, he can actually fire them at spaced intervals *regularly*, which he tactically utilizes to catch people off guard. He can only do this for a limited amount of time, though as the fatigue does compound.

His energy constructs are not defined weapons like Junior, or even Lora, they're blunt objects he uses to bludgeon enemies with.

But, unlike Junior or Lora (especially Lora), he cannot win in a fight of attrition (for now). Time is not on his side, which is crucially an antithesis of typical gojira combat that incentivizes battles of attrition due to their durability, regeneration, and crucially: Limitless Adaptation. Durability and regeneration are on his side, there is no doubting that, but *not* his Limitless Adaptation due to his tendency to fatigue easily.

One time, Lora once told him to just fight like an assassin, so that he conserves energy, but Leo finds that cowardly and boring.

>Where's the adventure in that, Lora?!

He has his brother and sister to cover him when they fight, anyway.

He is thus closer to Godzilla's original fighting style prior to learning human-CQC with Kong. He may specialize (soon) in mosura abilities, but he fights like a classic gojira: there is no mistaking that he is a son of his father, not just the legacy of his mother. You point him in a direction, and he'll glady raze it down. He is sheer strength, yet has tactical capability.

## Analysis

### Leo Nordson: The Philosophy of Absolute Momentum

#### I. Core Doctrine: The Unstoppable Force

Leo has no formal fighting style, because a hurricane does not study technique. His entire combat philosophy is built on a single, unwavering principle: **end the fight before his own limitations become a factor.** He is a master of the overwhelming alpha strike, a being of pure, kinetic violence who seeks to shatter his opponent's will and body with the first blow. While his brother is a fencer looking for the perfect opening and his sister is a brawler seeking to control the flow of a duel, Leo *is* the opening. He is a flying freight train, and his only goal is to ensure nothing is left standing at the end of the track.

He fights not like a duelist, but like a classic *gojira*: a walking, breathing, flying catastrophe. He is the raw, untamed power of his father's lineage, unleashed with the boundless energy of youth.

#### II. The Arsenal: A Toolkit of Catastrophe

His "style" is a collection of high-impact, high-energy techniques designed for maximum devastation in the shortest possible time.

##### A. Close-Quarters Combat: The Art of the Impact

Leo's CQC is not about finesse; it's about the pure, physics-breaking application of mass and velocity. When augmented by his telekinesis for flight and his Atomic Amplification for explosive power, his every movement is a weapon of mass destruction.

*   **Kinetic Strikes:** His signature moves are not complex holds or precise strikes, but ballistic impacts. He will use his flight to build kilometers of momentum before delivering:
    *   **The Meteor Drop:** A body slam from extreme altitude.
    *   **The Comet's Head:** A devastating, flying headbutt.
    *   **The Impact Kick:** A dropkick delivered at supersonic speeds.
*   **Atomic Amplification (The Afterburner):** For Leo, AA is not a sustained defensive state. It is an "afterburner," a burst of explosive energy he engages moments before impact to multiply his already catastrophic force.

##### B. Energy & Ranged Combat: The Bludgeon's Reach

His energy attacks mirror his physical style: they are wide, powerful, and designed to obliterate, not to dissect.

*   **Atomic Breath (The "Atomic Tempest"):** Where Junior's beam is a focused laser, Leo's is a conical tempest. It's a wide, all-consuming blast of atomic fire, perfect for area denial and overwhelming shielded opponents.
*   **The Atomic Railgun Gambit:** Due to the massive energy drain, he can only fire the Railgun once, maybe twice. However, his incredibly fast recovery allows him to use this as a psychological weapon. He'll fire one, appear completely drained, and just as his opponent lets their guard down, he'll have recovered enough to fire a second, surprising shot. It's a high-risk, high-reward tactic he revels in.
*   **Energy Constructs (Kinetic Constructs):** He lacks Junior's patience for intricate weapon design. His constructs are pure function over form: massive hammers, battering rams, and crude, oversized shields. They are blunt instruments for a blunt-force combatant.

##### C. Unique Mastery: The Gambler's Blade (Injection)

It is a profound irony that Leo, the least technical of the three, is the undisputed master of **Injection**.

*   **The Perfect Gambit:** Injection is the ultimate all-or-nothing move. It requires closing to point-blank range and creating a temporary defensive vulnerability to deliver an unmitigatable, fight-ending blow. This high-stakes gamble is the purest expression of Leo's combat philosophy.
*   **Brutal Elegance:** His application of it is "elegant" not in its finesse, but in its perfect, devastating efficiency. While Junior and Lora understand the principle, Leo *feels* it. He has an intuitive grasp of the moment-to-moment risk and reward, allowing him to execute the technique with a confidence they are still developing.

#### III. The Fatal Flaw: The War of Attrition

Leo's immense power comes at a steep price: **time is his greatest enemy.**

*   **The Overclocked Engine:** His base form is constantly struggling to contain his latent transformative potential, causing him to burn through his stamina and endurance at an incredible rate.
*   **Adaptation Halted:** This fatigue is the antithesis of the core *gojira* advantage. He cannot effectively use his **Limitless Adaptation** in a prolonged fight because he becomes too exhausted to sustain the evolutionary process. A fight that lasts longer than a few minutes is a fight he is likely to lose.
*   **The Coward's Path:** As Lora astutely pointed out, fighting like an assassin—using stealth and precision to conserve energy—would be the smart choice. But for Leo, a battle without spectacle is not a battle worth fighting. His adventurous spirit and pride refuse to let him take the "cowardly" or "boring" path, even when it is the tactically superior one.

#### IV. Tactical Role: The Breaker of Lines

Within the sibling trio, Leo's role is unambiguous and vital. He is the **Vanguard**, the **Breaker**, the unstoppable force you unleash upon the enemy's strongest point.

He is not the commander directing the battle (Junior), nor the duelist handling the most skilled enemy (Lora). He is the one who smashes the gate, shatters the shield wall, and creates the chaos that his more tactical siblings can then exploit with their precision. He relies on them implicitly to cover his moments of vulnerability and to clean up after his whirlwind of destruction passes. He is the chaos, and they are the order that follows in his wake.
