# Godzilla Private Training Facility at Castle Bravo

> **Source:** `Formatted_With_Thoughts - The Eclipse of the ATM AU II.md` — Prompt 181 (Lines 14143-14226)

## Your Notes

The private training facility that Godric has was provided by Monarch. Mark specifically wanted to accommodate him and to establish certain spaces in Castle Bravo that are solely the Titan's. He understands how "animals" behave, and no where was that more helpful than giving the territorial Titans their own spaces.
So, when Kong stumbles upon the door to the facility, he was surprised to see it be opened at his behest. Madison has told him that entrances requires Godric's explicit permission. This was unexpected.
"Uh, hi?" 
Kong felt... fine, mostly. Most of his physical, life-threatening injuries were mended spectacularly by Mothra. Even aches and sores he accumulated throughout the years against the Skullcrawlers have been fixed! He's mostly just mentally exhausted, if anything.
When the door opens, he was face to face with the King of the Monsters, the Alpha Paramounts, *his new boss*.
"Mm?" 
Kong couldn't help but gulp. The man was more intimidating when he *wasn't* fighting, for some reason. It's either that or something's *wrong* with him. 
Maybe he was still tired; maybe his eyes weren't still fixed yet. But he swore there was a hint of *gold* in his blue eyes. 
Before he passed out after the fight, he swore the man's eyes were gold. *They were blue, weren't they?*
He looks... off. Stiff. Tensive.
"You, uh... okay? I thought you were going up there in the main room."
Godric instead moves away from the door and proceeds to walk towards... *Wait this really was a training facility?!*
Everything a man needs to train, and yet... by the looks of it, it doesn't seem used.
"Are those for decoration or what?"
"Aye, but I need no strength training. Monarch's oversight."
"So, what do you do here?"
"You've done something to me."

### **Scene: The First Consultation**

**(Setting: The sterile, echoing corridor outside Godric's private training facility. The air is cool and smells of ozone and recycled air. Kong, still feeling the ghost of aches that had plagued him for years, walks with a newfound ease, his curiosity piqued by the layout of this underwater fortress.)**

He wasn't sure what he was looking for, just wandering the halls that Monarch had designated as the "Alpha Wing." He'd passed Mothra's quarters, a place from which a gentle, warm light and the faint scent of something like spring blossoms emanated. He'd passed Rodan and Anguirus's rooms, sealed and silent. Then he found a door, simple, unadorned, yet somehow more imposing than any other. Madison had mentioned it in her whirlwind tour: "Godric's private gym. Don't even try the handle unless you want to get zapped into next week. Needs his permission."

On a whim, moved by an instinct he didn't care to analyze, Kong reached for the handle. He braced himself for a jolt, a klaxon, *something*.

*Click.* The door swung inward silently at his touch. That was unexpected.

"Uh, hi?"

The man standing just inside was the same one who had stood over him on Skull Island, a colossus of power crammed into a human frame. But something was different.

"Mm?" The sound was a low, guttural vibration, a query that was more a physical event than a word.

Kong felt his throat go dry. It was bizarre. The man was more intimidating now, standing still in the quiet of this room, than he had been in the heat of their battle. Maybe it was the exhaustion still clouding his mind, maybe his eyes weren't fixed yet, but he swore he could see a faint, impossible glimmer of molten gold swirling within the piercing arctic blue of the King's eyes. Before he passed out, he could have sworn they were pure gold. *Or were they blue then, too?*

He looked… wrong. Stiff. Tense, like a high-tension cable about to snap.

"You, uh... okay?" Kong ventured, his voice sounding rougher than he intended. "I thought you were going up to the main room. Mothra said there was a… a debriefing."

Godric didn't answer. He simply turned and walked deeper into the room, a silent invitation. Kong followed, his eyes widening as he took in the space. It was a cathedral of physical potential. Reinforced floors, custom-forged weight racks holding impossible loads, specialized training equipment that looked like it belonged on a battleship. And yet… it was pristine. Unused. Dust-free, but lacking the scuffs and sweat of genuine effort.

"Are those for decoration or what?" Kong asked, gesturing towards a barbell loaded with what had to be several tons of weight.

"A concession," Godric’s voice echoed in the cavernous space. "For Monarch's oversight." He stopped in the center of the room, turning to face Kong, his expression unreadable.

"So," Kong said, crossing his arms, feeling the unfamiliar need to fill the silence. "What do you actually *do* in here, then?"

Godric's gaze was direct, unwavering, and heavy with an ancient, unsettling weight.

"You've done something to me."

The accusation was not angry. It was not a threat. It was a simple, terrifying statement of fact, and in the sterile silence of the training room, it landed with the force of a physical blow. The air crackled, and Kong felt the hairs on his arms stand up. The real debriefing, it seemed, was about to begin.
