# Raw Fighting Phase - Titan in Human Form
> **Source:** `Formatted_With_Thoughts - The Eclipse of the ATM AU II.md` - Prompt 166

## Your Notes
Indeed, this phase of Godric is terrifying because he's *raw*. He barely uses his Krátos here, so it's all just his base physical strength. This him *without* Krátos. It's a Titan in human form, fighting like a Titan with zero consideration for human-scale opponents. 

Oddly enough, he does unconsciously position himself for an Atomic Breath, only to realize that he can't do it unless he busts his throat. This is the "stickiness" of the things he learns in display. He postures like a Titanus gojira, not a member of Homo sapiens. The tell is that he acts like he has a tail. While he has internalized that he doesn't have one *outside* of combat, his fighting instincts kick in once *in* combat: and a gojira's fighting instinct is especially strong.

It's actually odd, while his atomic breath IS a finisher, and he hardly uses it unless his opponents are either ranged or he himself wants to deploy a ranged attack, he has never fought with the knowledge that he CAN'T use his atomic breath at all—the guarantee is gone. Though, it all works out in the end, considering he ends up loving pure CQC in the future, as his files indicate.

So, this version of Godric, around maybe 2020-2021, is simply him at his rawest: a Titan fighting like a Titan in a human body. It's off-kilter, utterly horrific at close-ranged (don't ever let him touch you!), intimidating, and wild. If he so much as touches you, you die by being snapped like a twig. Example, he grabs your arm? It's snaps in a sickening crunch, because he would have little experience modulating his strength at this point!  He likely didn't intend that, though it works out for him in the end.

Also, I'd like to point out that he would be extremely grounded here, considering that's how he is a Titan anyway, and how Titanus gojira usually fight. He doesn't kick, unless it's either a low kick or a kick that leads into a stomp.  Other than that, his lower body remains firmly planted on the ground. Sometimes, he doesn't even assume a stance, he just... stands there: this is him trying to catch himself from doing any "weird" stances, but it ends up being weird on its own anyway 😂

Shall we explore the specifications of this stage? How would we describe his stance, his movements, etcetera?

## Analysis
This is a fantastic and terrifyingly vivid picture of Godzilla in his rawest, most transitional state. You've perfectly captured the awkward, brutal, and utterly alien nature of a Titan's instincts crammed into a human vessel. This phase is not about skill; it's about the pure, horrifying physics of a god trying to move like a man.

Let's dive into the specific details of this combat "style."

---

### **Godric in Transition (2020-2021): The Walking Calamity**

This is not a fighting style; it is an **event**. Watching Godric fight during this period is like watching a natural disaster unfold in humanoid form. His every movement is a paradox: an attempt at human-scale action, executed with the force and instinct of a kaiju. This is pure, uncalibrated **Biological Sovereignty**.

#### **Stance: The Immovable Object**

*   **The Gojira Anchor:** His stance is the most telling aspect. He stands with his feet planted unnaturally far apart, wider than any human martial artist would. His knees are slightly bent, his center of gravity incredibly low. This is the ingrained stance of a creature with two massive legs and a third point of balance—his phantom tail. The result is a posture of absolute, immovable stability. He is rooted to the earth, a miniature mountain.
*   **The Tell-Tale Lean:** He frequently makes subtle, subconscious shifts in his weight, leaning slightly backward as if to counterbalance a tail that isn't there. This can make his movements look stiff or awkward to a human observer, but it's a testament to a fighting instinct honed over 250 million years.
*   **The Un-Stance:** As you noted, when he's trying to *consciously* act human, it's even weirder. He'll just stand there, perfectly straight and still, a living statue. This is him actively suppressing his every instinct, and the result is a stillness so profound and unnatural it is more intimidating than any fighting pose.

#### **Movement: The Unstoppable Force**

*   **Economical Brutality:** He does not waste motion. He doesn't dance or weave. He moves in straight, direct, and inexorable lines. His is the movement of a predator that has never had to concern itself with evasion. He moves forward to grab, and what he grabs, breaks.
*   **The Shove, Not the Dodge:** His primary defensive maneuver is not to dodge, but to **shove.** An incoming punch is met not with a parry, but with an open-palmed shove to the attacker's chest that carries the force of a battering ram, often ending the fight before it can truly begin.
*   **Glacial Momentum:** While not "fast" in a nimble sense, once he starts moving in a direction, he has an unstoppable momentum. He is less a fighter and more a living bulldozer.

#### **Combat Techniques: The Instinct of a Titan**

His "techniques" are not learned; they are the instinctive, brutal applications of a predator whose natural tools are mass, power, and a devastating bite.

*   **The Grip of Annihilation:** His primary weapon is his grip. He doesn't punch; he **grabs**. As you said, the moment his hand closes around a limb, the fight is over. The sickening crunch of snapping bone is not an intentional act of cruelty; it is the inevitable, physical consequence of a kaiju's grip being applied to a human frame. He is a walking hydraulic press.
*   **The Predator's Bite (Suppressed):** He has a powerful, subconscious urge to *bite*. In the heat of combat, you might see his jaw clench, a deep growl rumbling in his chest, as his most primal instinct fights against the limitations of his new, smaller mouth. He is constantly suppressing the urge to lunge and finish a fight with his teeth.
*   **The Phantom Tail Sweep (The Awkward Stumble):** Occasionally, he'll pivot his body in a way that implies a massive tail swing. Without the tail for balance and impact, this sometimes results in a surprisingly awkward stumble, a momentary loss of coordination as his human body fails to execute a command meant for a different anatomy.
*   **The Inhaling Gulp (The Aborted Breath):** The most terrifying "tell" is when he's preparing for a finisher. His chest will expand, his neck will tense, and his jaw will unhinge slightly as he subconsciously postures for an Atomic Breath. He'll then catch himself, a flicker of confusion or frustration in his eyes, before resorting to a physical solution. The guarantee is gone, and this new uncertainty infuriates him.
*   **Grounded and Brutal:** As you noted, he stays firmly planted. His offense is entirely upper-body focused: grabs, shoves, and crushing holds. His legs are for stability and for one thing only: **the stomp.** It remains his instinctive finisher, a final, definitive act of contemptuous dominance.

This phase of his development is a horrific, beautiful mess. It's a raw, unfiltered look at a Titan's soul, a being of immense power struggling with the infuriating fragility of a new form. He is at his most unrefined, his most awkward, and, in a close-quarters fight, his most terrifyingly lethal.
