### **Godzilla's Combat Doctrine: A Three-Phase Evolution**

#### **Phase 1: The Raw Titan (The Father's Echo)**
**(Time Period: Pre-Antitheriomorphosis / Immediate aftermath in early 2020)**

This is Godric's foundational, instinctual style, learned through a combination of genetic inheritance and his brief, brutal tutelage under Dagon. It is the pure, unadulterated combat philosophy of a Northern *Titanus gojira*.

*   **Core Philosophy:** **Overwhelm and Endure.** A fight is a contest of attrition. The goal is to absorb the enemy's best attacks through sheer durability and then deliver a single, decisive, overwhelming blow to end the conflict.
*   **Stance & Movement:** A low, impossibly grounded stance, prioritizing stability over agility. Movement is linear and inexorable, like a glacier. There is no weaving, no dodging—only advancing and enduring. The phantom tail is a constant, subconscious factor in his balance.
*   **Techniques:**
    *   **Brutal Grappling:** Focuses on grabs, shoves, and crushing holds.
    *   **Punishing Blows:** Heavy, clubbing strikes with claws and fists.
    *   **Primal Urges:** A suppressed but present instinct to bite and finish fights with his jaw.
    *   **Atomic Finisher:** The Atomic Breath is the ultimate one-shot tool, the checkmate move.
*   **Human Form Expression:** As the "Walking Calamity," this style is terrifyingly effective against human-scale opponents through sheer, uncalibrated overkill. Every move is a potential fatality. However, against a skilled opponent of equal power, its rigidity is a critical weakness.

#### **Phase 2: The Refined Titan (The Calibrated Engine)**
**(Time Period: Mid-2021 to the Kong fight in 2022)**

This is the style Godric develops through intense, solitary training in the Castle Bravo facility. He has accepted his human form and has systematically adapted his Titan instincts to its scale.

*   **Core Philosophy:** **Controlled Devastation.** He retains his father's core principles of durability and overwhelming force, but applies them with the precision and control of a master tactician.
*   **Stance & Movement:** The Gojira Anchor is now a perfected, conscious choice. The phantom tail tells are gone. His movement is still direct, but it is now fluid and controlled, with the ability to stop his immense momentum on a dime.
*   **Techniques:**
    *   **The Calibrated Grip:** He has mastered strength modulation. A grab is no longer an automatic bone-shatterer; it is a tool for control, leverage, and intentional, brutal punishment.
    *   **The Devastating Clinch:** His primary tactic is to close the distance, neutralize striking range, and systematically dismantle his opponent with a barrage of short, powerful blows and crushing holds.
    *   **Weaponized Durability:** He now consciously uses his durability as a tactical tool, willingly taking minor hits to create openings for devastating counters.
    *   **The Atomic Breath Feint:** He has weaponized the *memory* of his ultimate attack, using the charging posture as a psychological tool to misdirect and unbalance his opponents.

#### **Phase 3: The Contamination (The Hybrid Warrior)**
**(Time Period: Immediately following the Kong fight)**

This is the immediate, chaotic, and inelegant aftermath of his duel with Kong. His hyperthymesia has perfectly recorded and integrated Kong's techniques, but he has not yet had time to synthesize them into a cohesive whole.

*   **Core Philosophy:** **An Unstable Synthesis.** He is now a being with two conflicting combat operating systems running simultaneously. He possesses the immovable foundation of a *gojira* and the agile footwork of a *kong*, but they do not yet work in harmony.
*   **Stance & Movement:** This is where the conflict is most visible. He might start in his wide, grounded Gojira Anchor, then suddenly shift to the lighter, more mobile footwork of a boxer, creating a jarring and unpredictable rhythm. He has more options, but less certainty.
*   **Techniques:** A messy, chaotic, but brutally effective fusion.
    *   **The Grab and Weave:** He might initiate with a classic *gojira* grab, but then follow it up with a *kong*-style evasive weave that feels foreign to his own body.
    *   **Mid-Combat Innovations:** He now has access to the feints, dodges, and unorthodox attacks he learned and created during the fight.
    *   **The Psychological Quagmire:** This is his weakest and most dangerous phase mentally. His fighting style, the ultimate expression of a *gojira's* self, has been "contaminated." He feels inauthentic, a messy hybrid of himself and his rival. The victory feels hollow because he doesn't recognize the warrior who won it.

This "Contamination" phase is the crucial, painful crucible. It is the uncomfortable "in-between" stage that forces him to seek out Kong, not as a rival, but as a teacher, in order to take this chaotic mess of techniques and forge them into his final, ultimate form: **The Apex Predator Perfected.**
