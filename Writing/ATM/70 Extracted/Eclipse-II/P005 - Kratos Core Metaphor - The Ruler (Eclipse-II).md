# Kratos Core Metaphor - The Ruler

> **Source:** `Eclipse II` - Prompt 5 (Lines 781-986)

## Your Notes

As for Kratos, a Koinon has a simpler route:

They are born with the Will of the Koinon (Koinon's Will) and Resolve of the Koinon (Koinon's Resolve), which together is the "Themelion," which is in the drafts, I think.

This is innate, no matter if they have their own personal desires, and they misalign. Even if they don't find such desires at all, it will not affect the fidelity of their Manifestation.

Only the strength of their willpower can fluctuate the effects of their Manifestation, and by consequence, their Aura.

Metaphorically, they have inherited their titles; they need not take risks like Conquerors.

And for the entire Kratos system, their metaphor is the ruler: the ones destined to rule, whether through conquest or inheritance.

## Analysis

### Kratos: The Ruler (Godzilla)

* **Ontological Core ("What exists through will"):** This defines the "ruler" metaphor. A ruler's will becomes law; a Kraton's will becomes physics.

* **Inheritance vs. Conquest:**
  * **Koinon (Inheritance):** The idea that Koinons have their Themelion (Will + Resolve) as an innate, stable birthright is perfect. They have "inherited their titles" and don't face the same psychological risks as Krators. Their power is secure, if less spectacular.
  * **Krator (Conquest):** Krators must discover their *Horme* (Desire) and achieve Symphonia (Alignment). They are "conquerors" not just of territory, but of their own inner selves. Their power is immense but volatile, tied directly to their identity and purpose. This is Godzilla's journey in a nutshell--his power is tied to his trauma, his need for control, and his evolving purpose.
