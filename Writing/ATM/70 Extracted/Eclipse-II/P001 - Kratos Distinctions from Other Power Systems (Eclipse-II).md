# Kratos Distinctions from Other Power Systems

> **Source:** `Eclipse II` - Prompt 1 (Lines 3-278)

## Your Notes

To One Piece:
- *Haki* is an even more similar power system, especially the Conqueror subsystem and the Color of Supreme King Haki/Conqueror's Haki.
- The Color of Supreme King Haki is rare, genetic, and represents "kingly ambition," and that's a facet of Kratos, too. Ghidorah's destructive Desire can be reframed as a kingly ambition when considering that he was to be the King of Destruction.
- The difference is in the *genetic determinism*, which the Haki system also has, but all three have their progressions as a part of the system.
- There are no techniques for Kratos beyond Coating, then Penetration or Neutralization, because it depends on the species what they'll do with the power.
- While there are many fundamental similarities with Haki, Kratos remains distinct by doubling down on the determinism.

To JJK:
- The nature of Kratos seems similar to *Jujutsu Kaisen*'s Cursed Techniques.
- Someone like Gojo Satoru can't share his combination of Limitless and the Six Eyes trait.
- The difference in this system works because each character's power and place in the power system is a reflection of who they are.
- What doesn't exist in Kratos is a universal tier of progression (Sealed to Shikai to Bankai or Haki to Advanced Haki).
- JJK's Cursed Techniques also share this distinction with Kratos.
- Despite the similarities with JJK, the Magic power system of the ATM AU is actually supposed to reference it outright! Kratos, having parallels with it, was just a happy coincidence.

## Analysis

### Distinctions from Cited Comparisons

Your analysis effectively highlights how Kratos, despite superficial similarities, carves out its own niche:

* **Vs. Bleach (Zanpakuto/Shinigami):** While "The Blade Is Me" resonates with the Kraton-Manifestation bond, Kratos doesn't have universal tiers like Shikai/Bankai. The Kraton's connection is purely genetic and willpower-based, not through a spiritual weapon. The psychological state (Krator's Symphonia/Diaphonia) is a direct internal condition, not a partnership.

* **Vs. Avatar (Bending):** The "culture, family, individual philosophy" influences are very strong parallels. Kratos doubles down on genetics as the *foundation* (you can't *become* a Nucleon if you're not born with it), but then allows culture/philosophy to shape its *expression*. Zuko's lightning redirection is a perfect example of "progression despite limitation" that you aim for.

* **Vs. One Piece (Haki):** This is where you draw the most direct comparison and make the clearest distinctions.
    * **Conqueror's Haki:** Kratoros Thelesis (Conqueror's Will) shares its rarity, genetic basis, and "kingly ambition" (Ghidorah's Desire) with Conqueror's Haki. This is a very strong parallel.
    * **Key Distinction: Determinism & Progression:** Your absolute stance that Kratos has *no universal advanced forms* of Coating/Penetration/Neutralization, and that progression is purely about the *wielder's unique expression* within their fixed set of abilities, is what truly sets it apart from Haki's more structured advanced forms.

* **Vs. Jujutsu Kaisen (Cursed Techniques):** The parallels here are indeed strong, especially the idea of unique, fixed techniques per sorcerer (Gojo's Limitless, Fushiguro's Ten Shadows) that reflect their personality.
    * **Fixed Techniques, Unique Manifestations:** The core idea that Dagon and Godzilla, despite having the same Kratos classification, wield their power in wholly unique ways (and Junior/Leo even more so) perfectly mirrors JJK's approach.
    * **Progression Despite Limitation:** Like JJK, the creativity and innovation *within* the fixed limitations are where progression occurs. Godzilla's Atomic Amplification as a response to his human form, or Ghidorah learning to make electricity constructs, exemplifies this beautifully.
