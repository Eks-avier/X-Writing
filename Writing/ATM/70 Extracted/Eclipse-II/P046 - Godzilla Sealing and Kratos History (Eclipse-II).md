# Godzilla Sealing and Kratos History

> **Source:** `Formatted_With_Thoughts - The Eclipse of the ATM AU II.md` — Prompt 46 (Lines 2963-3035)

## Your Notes

While Godzilla ultimately wins his fight against Battra, he gets sealed by a trap placed by Battra intended for Mothra. It's meant to seal her off specifically, but due to the threat of Burning Godzilla to the entire invasion, he has no choice but to sacrifice that piece to get him off the board. This is why he allies with Gigan, even though he shows contempt towards him as a cyborg against Mothra.

Some background:

So before Krátos' conception, I had Godzilla's powers be an innate ability of himself and his species: Limitless Adaptation and Universal Energy Control. The ATM AU only had Psionics and Magic. But this felt unsatisfying and tedious because I had to think about Anguirus, Ghidorah, and other Titans. So, I decided to just develop Krátos, which is why Godzilla and Ghidorah are the first users of it. Then, I retroactively expanded it to Scylla to deepen her character as well as her species. This was such a revolutionary that I now have cleanly organized all my past and potential characters, their power sets, and mechanics. The introduction of Biological Sovereignty further solidified this.

Godzilla no longer has Universal Energy Control; it is now a product of his Pyrenikon/Eketromagneton nature and being a Conqueror. But still, you can see that despite the obsolescence of "Domination of Energy," you can see elements of Krátos in there. But the most striking is that the Titans never use their abilities in his presence! This is the First Layer of Conqueror's Aura in action! The Titans are too scared to do so. It's quite serendipitous that I can connect this old piece of lore to the new and refined one.

What isn't obsolete and is canon about this narrative sidepiece is Battra's postmortem of his fight; only the terminology is rendered outdated. If I were to rewrite this, I'd definitely insert more foreshadowing towards Krátos. This is the catalyst that sends Battra on a side-plot to dig deeper into the biology and powers of the Titanus gojira species. Yet, I honestly think these outdated terminologies work as a "theory" of Battra, a step into the classification of Krátos later on.

Now, as to the mechanics of Godzilla's nullification of Magic. He didn't understand how to do it; rather, it was something he came up with on the fly to gain an advantage. It's essentially the application of Submission into the area covered by his Aura. By saturating the entire radius of his Aura with his Manifestation, it becomes "hostile" to the ambient, background Primus Energy. His Will occupies the space where Primus Energy should be, meaning his Aura effectively pushes PE away from him. So, if Battra is within his Aura, he can't use Magic. Battra's counter was to shift energy bases constantly: by utilizing his own reserves of Primus Energy, he makes Cursed Energy, Elemental Energy, and so on multiple times. The constant transmutation of base PE provides equilibrium, pushing back against Godzilla's Aura, like a star's internal fusion fighting against gravity.

Let me know what you think of it!
