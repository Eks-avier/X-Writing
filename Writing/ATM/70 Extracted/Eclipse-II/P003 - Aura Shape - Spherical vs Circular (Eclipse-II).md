# Aura Shape - Spherical vs Circular

> **Source:** `Eclipse II` - Prompt 3 (Lines 441-581)

## Your Notes

- **Aura Shape**: Good catch on the circular versus spherical. It should be spherical, but I wonder what practical benefits do either bring.

## Analysis

Spherical (3D) is almost certainly the more practical and impressive choice for a planetary-scale power system.

**Practical Benefits (Spherical):**

* **Omnidirectional Influence:** A sphere naturally allows for influence above, below, and around the Kraton, crucial for flying beings (Ghidorah, Scylla), subterranean entities, or those in the Hollow Earth.

* **Environmental Control:** Spherical coverage makes "saturating the environment" more literal and impactful, affecting atmosphere, ground, and water equally.

* **Defense:** Provides a 360-degree protective field.

* **Intimidation:** A visible sphere of destructive energy is inherently more terrifying than a disc.

**Recommendation:** Clarify that Aura is generally a spherical (3D) field, with "radius" referring to the measurement from the Kraton's center to the edge of this sphere. A "circular area" might be used in a more casual, conversational context, or when discussing its projection onto a flat surface (e.g., "the Aura created a circular scorch mark on the ground").
