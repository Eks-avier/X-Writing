# Aura Two Axes - Intensity and Spatial Management

> **Source:** `Eclipse II` - Prompt 4 (Lines 585-778)

## Your Notes

I believe that we should have:

* Suppression
* Flaring

These control the intensity of the innate effects of one's Aura. For example, Ghidorah's Aura can either pin someone to the ground while frying them or make someone float with little to no shock. In fact, floating via anti-gravity is typically how he flies in his human vessel. He has mastered "separating" and "combining" his electrogravitational powers, inducing either electromagnetic effects on one thing or gravitational effects on another.

Then, we have:

* Expansion
* Reduction

These control the *size* of the Aura without intensifying the effects. One enlarges it, the other minimizes it.

Now, we have precise vocabulary to describe these mechanics.

## Analysis

Splitting the controls into two distinct axes--Intensity and Spatial Management--is a brilliant move. It creates a much more granular, strategic, and intuitive system.

### Axis One: Intensity Management

This axis governs the *strength and potency* of the Aura's innate effects, modulating the raw power output within its given area.

#### **1. Flaring (Anapsis - "Kindling" or "Lighting Up")**

* **Definition:** The conscious act of intensifying the innate effects of one's Aura, causing it to burn brighter, feel heavier, or become colder.
* **Mechanics:**
  * **Primary Effect:** Increases the strength of the Aura's innate properties, typically by 1.5x to 2x the baseline.
  * **Side Effect:** A slight, unintentional expansion of the Aura's radius often occurs as a consequence of the increased energy output.
* **Energy Cost:**
  * **Exponential Drain:** The stamina cost increases exponentially with the degree of intensification.
  * **Krator Efficiency:** While still costly, Kratores (Conquerors) experience a more favorable cost curve for marginal increases.

#### **2. Suppression (Kryptis - "Hiding" or "Concealment")**

* **Definition:** The conscious act of minimizing or modulating the innate effects of one's Aura, concealing its true power or refining its application.
* **Mechanics:**
  * **Primary Effect:** Reduces the strength of the Aura's properties. At high levels of mastery, this allows for the separation and independent modulation of phenomena (e.g., Ghidorah using his Aura's gravity to fly without its shocking effects).
  * **Koinon Application:** A Koinon can suppress their Aura back to the faint, passive "traces" of their manifestation.
* **Energy Cost:**
  * **Koinon Efficiency:** Suppression is nearly free for a Koinon, as it is their natural state to require activation for a full Aura.
  * **Krator Learning Curve:** For a Krator, suppression is initially very costly, requiring immense concentration. The energy cost follows a logarithmic curve (high initial cost for small gains), which then drops steeply as true mastery is achieved.

### Axis Two: Spatial Management

This axis governs the *size and reach* of the Aura's spherical field, managing its territorial influence without altering its baseline intensity.

#### **1. Expansion (Ektasis - "Expanse" or "Spreading Out")**

* **Definition:** The conscious act of enlarging the radius of one's Aura to extend territorial influence.
* **Mechanics:**
  * **Primary Effect:** Increases the volume of the spherical Aura field.
  * **Consequence - "Thinning":** As the Aura expands, its innate effects are spread over a larger area, causing them to "thin out" and become less potent at the outer edges.
* **Energy Cost:**
  * **Diminishing Returns:** The energy cost to expand the Aura's radius increases exponentially, while the rate of expansion slows.

#### **2. Reduction (Systole - "Contraction" or "Shrinking")**

* **Definition:** The conscious act of shrinking the radius of one's Aura, concentrating its influence into a smaller, more intimate space.
* **Mechanics:**
  * **Primary Effect:** Decreases the volume of the spherical Aura field.
  * **Consequence - "Condensing":** As the Aura is reduced, its latent energy is concentrated into a smaller volume, causing a slight but noticeable intensification of its innate effects.
* **Energy Cost:**
  * **Low Cost:** Reduction is the most energy-efficient of all Aura manipulations. It requires more mental focus than raw stamina.
