### **Godric Nordson: `Téchni` Profile (Post-Xilien Invasion)**

#### **III. The Primary Arsenal: Abilities Governed by Atomic Amplification**

These are the pinnacle techniques of Godzilla's human-form combat doctrine, a suite of advanced abilities that require the perfect control, stability, and power provided by the Atomic Amplification state. Each represents a profound lesson learned and a key relationship honored.

*   **The Atomic Railgun (The Pinnacle) - Trophy of Intellect**
    *   **Description:** A hyper-concentrated, pencil-thin beam of pure atomic energy, typically fired from a single, extended fingertip.
    *   **Function:** His ultimate single-target, long-range finisher. It is a weapon of absolute surgical precision, designed to pierce any known defense and end a conflict with a single, undeniable blow. The Railgun is the final word in any ranged engagement.

*   **The Atomic Stride (`Seismos`) (The King's Advance) - Trophy of Humility**
    *   **Description:** A near-instantaneous, linear burst of movement. When used with the Atomic Mantle, the Stride is almost completely silent and free of collateral damage.
    *   **Function:** His primary mobility tool for closing distances or repositioning with god-like speed. It is a direct, physics-based propulsion, not teleportation, and operates with the tactical limitation of a "King's Move"—absolute in any direction, but over a short-to-medium range.
    *   **Sub-Technique: *The Phasing Stride*** A high-skill variant where Godric engages **Atomic Resonance** simultaneously with a Stride. This allows him to vibrate his own atomic structure to pass through solid objects in his path, trading a portion of his maximum range for the ability to bypass physical barriers.

*   **The Atomic Mantle (`Aegis Atomicus`) (The Legacy) - Trophy of Love**
    *   **Description:** An externalized, skin-tight layer of his internal Neutralization field, projected outward to form a perfect, personal shield.
    *   **Function:**
        1.  **Stride Control:** It is the "silencer" and "brake" for the Atomic Stride, neutralizing the catastrophic shockwaves and radiation byproducts.
        2.  **External Allocation:** In a profound act of protection mirroring his Queen, he can impart a portion of his Mantle to an ally or object, temporarily granting them a measure of his own sovereign durability.
    *   **Application: *Structural Infusion (`Stereosis`):* The King's Grip** The Mantle allows for the perfected, "gentle" application of External Fortification. It creates a structural integrity field around a massive object, enabling him to lift it without it collapsing, while simultaneously buffering the most lethal radioactive effects.

*   **Atomic Resonance (`Pàlmos`) (The Disintegrator's Touch)**
    *   **Description:** The controlled generation of high-frequency vibrations throughout his own body or into an object he is touching.
    *   **Function:** A versatile and devastating tool. At low frequencies, it can be used for mundane tasks. In combat, it becomes a horrifying weapon, capable of shattering inanimate objects at a molecular level or inflicting catastrophic internal damage to a grappled opponent by bypassing their external armor entirely.
