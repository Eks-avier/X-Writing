### **Godric Nordson: `Téchni` Profile (Post-Xilien Invasion)**

#### **II. Foundational State: Atomic Amplification (AA)**

Atomic Amplification is the cornerstone of Godzilla's human-form combat system. It is not merely a "power-up," but a state of perfect internal harmony where his Krátonic will and his translated *gojira* physiology are brought into a single, unified, and devastatingly efficient state. It is the physical manifestation of his journey of self-mastery, a technique that transforms him from a formidable brawler into a true god of combat.

*   **Krátonic Definition: The Sovereign's Stance**
    AA is the master-level feat of simultaneously and uniformly applying all three Personal Coating Edicts across his entire body. It is a constant, sustained act of will requiring immense focus and a harmonious internal state.
    1.  **Internal Fortification:** His muscles and bones are reinforced, augmenting his physical power.
    2.  **Internal Neutralization:** His very cells are suffused with the will to dissolve incoming attacks, supercharging his defense and regeneration.
    3.  **Innate Penetration:** His entire being is imbued with the will to bypass defenses, making his every physical action a piercing attack.

*   **Mechanical Effects: The Complete Enhancement Suite**
    Activating AA engages a total-system override, elevating every aspect of his combat capability:
    *   **Offensive Power:** His musculature is augmented, providing a massive, multiplicative increase to his striking and lifting strength.
    *   **Defensive Power:** His internal Neutralization field works in concert with his innate durability and supercharged regeneration, making him nearly invulnerable to conventional harm.
    *   **Cognitive Acceleration:** His nervous system is supercharged, drastically increasing his sensory intake, processing speed, and reaction time.
    *   **Systemic Velocity:** The synergistic combination of augmented muscles and accelerated cognition grants him a level of speed, agility, and coordination that is impossible at his baseline.
    *   **Energy Control:** The harmonious state acts as a perfect "focusing lens" for his atomic energy, providing the stable foundation required to execute his most advanced and precise Krátonic techniques.

*   **Tiers of Amplification: A System of Controlled Escalation**
    Godzilla can consciously modulate the intensity of his Amplification, treating it as a scalable tool rather than a simple on/off switch.
    *   **Tier 1 (1-15%) - "Combat Flow":** A low-cost state used for training and light engagements. It perfects his senses, agility, and allows for an effortless flow between his various martial arts techniques.
    *   **Tier 2 (15-50%) - "Dominance":** His standard for serious combat against high-level threats. His physicality becomes overwhelming, enabling brutal pressure fighting and battlefield control.
    *   **Tier 3 (50-100%+) - "The King's Wrath":** Reserved for existential threats. At this level, he becomes a localized physical event, his power so immense it begins to affect the immediate environment. Post-Xilien Invasion, his mastery of efficiency allows him to sustain this tier with far greater ease.
