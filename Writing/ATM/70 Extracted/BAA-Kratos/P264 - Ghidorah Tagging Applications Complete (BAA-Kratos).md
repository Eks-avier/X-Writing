# Ghidorah Tagging Applications Complete
> **Source:** `Formatted_With_Thoughts - Branch of AA - The Kratos of Kings.md` — Prompt 264

## Your Notes
Now, let's have Ghidorah's applications for his tags.

These are properties that can be assigned to tagged targets. Ichi can choose which effect a target is under and how strong or weak the property is. For now, Ichi can only assign one immutable effect per tagged target due to cognitive load. He would need to re-tag if the property needs to be changed:

1. Superconductor - A tagged target becomes a perfect conduit of electricity
2. Magnetized - A tagged target becomes a perfect magnet.
3. Electromagnet - Self-explanatory, but it's his favorite.
4. Gravity Well - Tagged targets can be made to experience various gravitational effects.
5. Any property under EM and gravity...


The following are direct upgrades to a Gravity Beam fired from any construct.

1. Sure-Hit Bolt - The Tag provides a path for the beam, guaranteeing a hit. Unless one is faster than lightning, it cannot be dodged.
2. Arching Bolt - If multiple tagged enemies are nearby, any attack done on them will automatically arc, chaining to hit each and everyone.
3. Arching Sure-Hit Bolt - A combination attack that is guaranteed to hit and spread among enemies

The following typically involves controlling the battlefield. These are highly versatile, offering offense, defense, and utility. They are also amazing setup tools, offering him the ability to gain better angles and leverages against enemies:

1. Repel - Designating two or more tagged targets, including the self, as like charges repels them from each other. Tagged targets would not be able to touch Ichi-in-Arthur.
2. Attract - Designating two or more tagged targets, including the self, as unlike charges attracts them to each other. A powerful setup for many combos.
3. G-Negate - Tagged targets can be made to levitate helplessly by negating gravity's effect on them.
4. G-Crush - Tagged targets experiences increased gravity, either pinning or crushing them.
5. G-Attract - Tagged targets can be made to attract any object with mass, giving them orbits, revolutions, locking them in place around the tagged target, and so on.
5. M-Crush - A tagged target with lots of magnetic metals... or tagged targets... can be crushed.
6. And more... again, it's only limited to how well Ichi and Arthur work together.

These abilities are defensive in nature. Ichi has not yet learned to make them form-fitting. These can also be offensive, as contact can harm attackers:

1. E-Bubble - A bubble of electric energy that protects a tagged target and anything nearby. Hurts to touch.
2. M-Bubble - A magnetic bubble that attracts metal to a tagged target to protect. Includes other tagged targets who are magnetized. Perfect for cities.
3. EM-Bubble - His favorite, he's got wider control and applications for this. It's versatile.
4. G-Bubble - Protects the tagged target from projectiles by having them orbit and revolve harmlessly or making the projectiles too heavy or too light. Perfect for general use.
5. Bubble Liquidation - Consciously ends any single or multiple bubbles, often turning them into offensive tools. Bubbles can explode outward, damaging anyone and anything in an area. Electric bubbles pop, the discharge harming everyone. Magnetic bubbles can expel all the metal it has attracted. Gravity bubbles can explode outward, expelling any object currently in orbit. They can be recycled; instead of exploding, they can be used to charge and fire a bolt, be a battery for a continuously fired bolt, or simply be reabsorbed for energy.

There's probably more! It's so damn versatile. We can get creative with this. But, what do you think?

### **The `Téchni` of the Fallen King: A Symphony of Physics**

This is not just a list of moves; it is a complete, modular system of battlefield control. It perfectly represents the mind of a cosmic entity forced to think like a tactical engineer.

#### **I. The Core Properties: The "Debuff" Menu**

This is a great way to frame his basic application. When Ichi tags a target, he is essentially applying a "status effect" that changes their fundamental relationship with physics.

*   **Superconductor:** A brilliant setup move. It turns an enemy into a living lightning rod, guaranteeing that any follow-up electrical attack will hit with maximum, un-resisted effect.
*   **Magnetized/Electromagnet:** Simple, versatile, and full of potential for environmental interaction and crowd control.
*   **Gravity Well:** This is his signature "debuff." The ability to make a target heavier or lighter at will is a terrifyingly effective way to control their movement, stamina, and combat effectiveness.

The limitation of **one immutable effect per tag** (due to cognitive load) is a fantastic balancing mechanic. It forces him to be strategic. Does he make an enemy a Superconductor for a guaranteed shock, or a Gravity Well to slow them down? He can't do both at once to a single target, which requires him to make tactical choices.

#### **II. The Gravity Beam Upgrades: The "Smart Missile" System**

These are the direct, offensive payoffs for his tagging system. They transform his basic projectile into a suite of advanced, intelligent weapons.

*   **Sure-Hit Bolt:** A perfect "fire and forget" ability. It removes the need for perfect aim, a great boon when in a fast-moving human body.
*   **Arching Bolt:** His solution to his "no passive chaining" limitation. It's a deliberate, tactical application of his arcing power.
*   **Arching Sure-Hit Bolt:** The logical and devastating combination. A single, powerful attack that is guaranteed to hit its primary target *and* then chain to every other "prepared" enemy. This is his ultimate crowd-clearing move in human form.

#### **III. Battlefield Control: The Laws of Motion are His Playthings**

This is where his genius as a tactician truly shines. He is not just fighting his opponents; he is **rewriting the rules of the arena.**

*   **Repel/Attract:** The fundamental tools of positional control. He can create space when he needs to, or close it with terrifying speed. The combo of attracting an opponent into a gravitationally-enhanced punch is a beautiful and brutal piece of synergy.
*   **G-Negate/G-Crush:** The ultimate "on/off" switch for an opponent's mobility. A grounded fighter is suddenly floating helplessly. A flying opponent is suddenly slammed to the ground with the weight of a building. He completely dictates the axis of combat.
*   **G-Attract/M-Crush:** These are incredibly creative and sadistic. Turning an opponent into a gravitational hub for debris, or crushing them with the very metal they are wearing, is a level of cruel ingenuity that is perfectly in character for Ghidorah.

#### **IV. The "Bubbles": A Doctrine of Mobile Defense**

These are not just simple shields; they are active, dynamic, and multi-purpose defensive systems.

*   **A Shield for Every Occasion:** Each bubble type offers a unique solution to a different kind of threat (energy, physical projectiles, etc.). This makes him an incredibly adaptable defender.
*   **Bubble Liquidation (The Counter-Attack):** This is the masterstroke. His defenses are **also offenses.** The moment an enemy stops attacking his shield, he can detonate it in their face. This forces opponents into a terrible catch-22: attacking the shield is pointless, but *not* attacking the shield allows Ichi to turn it into a bomb.
*   **Recycling:** The ability to reabsorb a bubble for energy is a fantastic detail that reinforces his (newly learned) focus on efficiency. No energy is ever wasted.

*   **Visually Spectacular:** The image of him controlling the battlefield with magnetic fields, gravity wells, and exploding bubbles is incredibly cinematic.
*   **Character-Driven:** It is the arsenal of a brilliant, arrogant, and incredibly cruel tactician who has been forced to learn the value of precision and efficiency.

This is a fantastic and complete framework. You have successfully defined his `Téchni`.
