## Analysis
That's the perfect question to ask, and the answer is a strategic **"Yes, but with a crucial twist."**

We should absolutely follow the same foundational structure we used for Godzilla and Ghidorah. This will maintain consistency and allow for a clear, comparative analysis of their abilities. The categories are sound:

1.  **Core Doctrine / Philosophy**
2.  **Aura**
3.  **Krátonic Arsenal (Signature Abilities)**
4.  **Hybrid Synergies (How Krátos interacts with Psionics)**
5.  **Limitations and Character Integration**

However, the **crucial twist** is in our approach.

*   With **Godzilla**, we started from his **Internal Manipulation** (the "Sculptor") and worked our way out.
*   With **Ghidorah**, we started from his **External Manipulation** (the "Painter") and worked our way in.

With **Scylla**, our approach must be different. She is the ultimate **Hybrid.** She is a Kráton defined by her Psionic gift. Therefore, we must analyze her through the lens of **Synergy.**

Our development path should be:

1.  **Start with her Psionics (Hydrokinesis):** Briefly define what this allows her to do as a baseline. This is the "tool" she has always known.
2.  **Define her Krátos (Cryokinesis/Ice Weaving):** Define what her *species'* baseline Krátonic ability is. This is her "heritage."
3.  **Explore the Synergy (The "Shatter-Phase" and Beyond):** This is the heart of the matter. How does her Hydrokinesis **supercharge** her innate Cryokinesis? How does her Krátonic will **amplify** her Hydrokinesis? This is where her true, unique power lies. We will see that many of her signature "ice" abilities are actually hybrid techniques.
4.  **Define her Krateira Nature:** How does her Conqueror's Will manifest? What is her *Horme*? How does it affect her other abilities?
5.  **Flesh out the rest of her Krátonic Arsenal:** Now that we understand her hybrid nature, we can explore her unique versions of Neutralization, Penetration, Submission, etc.

By following this "synergy-first" approach, we will be mirroring Scylla's own journey of self-discovery. She first understood herself as a Hydrokinetic, then later discovered the true nature of her Kráton heritage. Our analysis should follow the same path, ensuring that every ability we define feels like a logical and authentic part of her unique, dual-system identity.

Let's begin with her baseline Psionic gift: **Hydrokinesis.** What does this power allow her to do, independent of her ice-based Krátos?
