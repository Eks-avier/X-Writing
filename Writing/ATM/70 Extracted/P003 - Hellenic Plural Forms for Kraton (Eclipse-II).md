# Hellenic Plural Forms for Kraton

> **Source:** `Eclipse II` - Prompt 3 (Lines 441-581)

## Your Notes

- **Plural Forms**: I've never considered the plural and singular forms of the Kraton, actually. So, I think this is a good opportunity to think about them. They should be styled in Hellenic plural forms. In that manner, we keep the thematic unity of the system. If not, then the singular and plural are one and the same, hence my use of Koinon/Krator with plural conjunctions and pronouns.

## Analysis

Adopting Hellenic plural forms would absolutely maintain thematic unity and elevate the system's aesthetic. It adds a layer of ancient, formal gravitas that aligns perfectly with the "cosmic royalty" theme.

**Recommendation:**
* **Kraton (kraton):** Singular (e.g., "A Kraton manifested their power.")
* **Kratones (kratones):** Plural (e.g., "Several Kratones gathered.")
* **Koinos Kraton (koinos kraton):** Singular Common Kraton (masculine)
* **Koinos Kratones (koinoi kratones):** Plural Common Kratons (masculine)
* **Koine Kraton (koine kraton):** Singular Common Kraton (feminine)
* **Koinai Kratones (koinai kratones):** Plural Common Kratons (feminine)
* **Krator (krator):** Singular Conqueror (masculine)
* **Kratores (kratores):** Plural Conquerors (masculine)
* **Krateira (krateira):** Singular Conqueror (feminine)
* **Krateirai (krateirai):** Plural Conquerors (feminine)

**Benefit:** This adds an immediate layer of academic and mythical depth, signaling the system's ancient origins and formal classification.
