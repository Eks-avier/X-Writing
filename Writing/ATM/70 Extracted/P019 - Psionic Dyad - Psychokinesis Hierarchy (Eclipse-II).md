# Psionic Dyad - Psychokinesis Hierarchy

> **Source:** `Formatted_With_Thoughts - The Eclipse of the ATM AU II.md` — Prompt 19 (Lines 1621-1767)

## Your Notes

I would like to introduce a change to one of the fundamental skills of Psionics:

1.  Let's find a name for these two abilities. What is this "bedrock" of Psionics?
2. I would like to change the name of *Telekinesis* into *Psychokinesis*.

The reasoning behind this change is that:

* *Psychokinesis* sounds broader than mere *Telekinesis*. Why is that?
* *Telekinesis* will instead be a subskill of *Psychokinesis*. It is the ability to move *anything* with the mind alone.

This changes several things as a consequence:

* *Constrained Telekinetics* will now also be a subskill of *Psychokinesis*. It is defined as psychokinetics whose ability to move objects, energies, or phenomena is limited by biology, e.x, most "elemental" Titans can control one or more elements like fire, water,  rock, and so on.
* The definition of *True Psychics* remain the same: Unnons who possess either *Active Telepathy* or *Telekinesis*.  However, someone like Rodan, who is a Passive Telepath, Pyrokinetic, Aerokinetic, and Lithokinetic, cannot be a True Psychic; but someone like Gerome is a True Psychic even though he lacks Telekinesis because he is an Active Telepath. By this logic, all of Keep Charlie's Psionic Division are True Psychics. Most Titans are not.

Note: I'm also considering giving Psychokinesis a 'passive' / 'inactive' / 'subtle' counterpart:

- My issue is that some telepathic abilities do require psychokinesis. Readers may question how something like mind manipulation is possible without actually, physically manipulating how one's mental faculties function in the brain.
- If someone like Gerome Marcel, who *is* an Active Telepath, but has no psychokinesis can perform mind manipulation, then how would that ability be possible in the first place without psychokinesis?

This now solidifies Scylla as a Krator (her first gift) who is a Kryoton (as is her right by heritage as an Ice Weaver) and a Hydrokinetic (her second gift).

## Analysis

### 1. Naming the Bedrock of Psionics

To give the two fundamental abilities a formal, resonant name that reflects their foundational nature, we can call them:

*   **The Psionic Dyad:** This term is technical, precise, and emphasizes that these two abilities are the core pair from which everything else originates.
*   **The Twin Faculties:** A more poetic and thematic name that subtly echoes the twin symbolism of Mothra and Battra, and the dual nature of Psionics and Magic.

For the purpose of this framework, we'll use **The Psionic Dyad**.

### 2. The New Hierarchy: Psychokinesis and its Disciplines

Your reasoning is spot-on. *Psychokinesis* (mind-movement) is the broader, more accurate term for the fundamental ability. *Telekinesis* (distant-movement) is an excellent name for a specific, powerful application of it.

This creates a much cleaner and more logical hierarchy:

| Level | Ability | Definition |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Pillar** | **Psychokinesis (PK)** | The fundamental psionic ability to influence matter and energy with the mind. |
| **Discipline** | **Telekinesis (TK)** | The **unconstrained** and versatile application of PK. The ability to move, shape, or affect any physical object or energy field directly through will. This is a "pure" expression of PK. |
| **Discipline** | **Constrained Kinesis** | The application of PK that is **biologically limited** to a specific element or phenomenon. The user's biology acts as a "filter" for the Psi Force, allowing them to manipulate only certain things (fire, water, ice, etc.). |

This change has several excellent consequences:
*   It clearly differentiates between a versatile, all-purpose power (Telekinesis) and a specialized, innate one (Constrained Kinesis).
*   It makes "elemental" Titans feel more like natural, biological wonders rather than just generic psychics.
*   It solidifies **Scylla's** classification perfectly: she is a Krator (Kryoton) whose powers are augmented by her separate, secondary gift of Constrained Kinesis (Hydrokinesis).

### 3. Redefining the "True Psychic"

With this new structure, we can create a much more precise definition that aligns perfectly with your examples.

A **True Psychic** is any Unnon who can **actively and independently project their will to influence either the environment or another sapient mind.**

This requires possessing at least one of the following:
1.  **Active Telepathy**
2.  **Telekinesis (Unconstrained PK)**

Let's test this against your characters:
*   **Gerome Marcel:** Has Active Telepathy. He can actively project his will into other minds. **He is a True Psychic.**
*   **Suki Takao:** Has Active Telepathy AND Telekinesis. **She is a True Psychic.**
*   **Rodan:** Has Passive Telepathy (receptive only) and Constrained Kinesis.
    *   His kinesis is considered a highly advanced biological function that *utilizes* the Psi Force, rather than a direct, versatile projection of his mind. It's more akin to an organ (like Barb's EMP) than a true psychic faculty. Therefore, he cannot independently project his will in a versatile way. **He is NOT a True Psychic.**
*   **The Result:** This logic holds perfectly. All of Keep Charlie's Psionic Division are True Psychics. Most "elemental" Titans, despite their power, are not, which adds a fascinating layer to the hierarchy.

### 4. Solving the Mental Manipulation Paradox

This is the most important clarification. How can Gerome, an Active Telepath without Psychokinesis, manipulate a mind if it requires physically altering the brain?

The solution is to introduce a subtle but crucial counterpart to active telepathy. Let's call it **Micro-Psychokinesis (Micro-PK)**.

*   **Definition:** Micro-PK is the inherent, subtle ability of an Active Telepath to influence the electrochemical processes of the brain at a neural level. It is **not** a separate power; it is the **mechanism by which Active Telepathy functions.**
*   **The Software vs. Hardware Analogy:**
    *   **The Brain** is the *hardware*.
    *   **Consciousness/The Mind** is the *software* running on that hardware.
    *   **Psychokinesis (Macro-PK/Telekinesis)** is like using a magnetic crane to physically move the computer. It's powerful, external, and brute-force.
    *   **Active Telepathy (via Micro-PK)** is like being a master hacker. You aren't physically moving the computer, but you are sending precise signals (via Micro-PK) to manipulate the *software* (the mind). You can rewrite code (alter memories), introduce viruses (cast illusions), or send messages (communicate).

**How this solves the paradox:**
*   Gerome doesn't have the "macro" power of Psychokinesis to lift a car, but his Active Telepathy inherently includes the "micro" ability to influence a neuron to fire or a synapse to connect.
*   This is why Mental Incursion is a telepathic discipline. It's achieved by manipulating the mind's "software" through subtle, precise "hardware" tweaks that are part of the telepathic toolkit.
*   This also explains why combining powerful Telekinesis with Telepathy is so potent. A psychic with both can manipulate the software *and* the hardware on a massive scale, making them incredibly formidable.

This new framework is robust, logical, and adds wonderful depth. It maintains Psionics as the most "stable" system while providing clear avenues for specialization, power scaling, and narrative tension.
