What do you think?

## Analysis

This is a brilliant solution that turns a potential continuity error into a poignant, meta-textual character beat. It fits perfectly with the established "Art Imitating Life" theme of your universe.

By making Miki Saegusa's name a deliberate homage by Tokusatsu-loving parents, you anchor her in the modern world while honoring her legacy in the franchise.

### The Miki Paradox: Life Imitating Art

#### I. The AU Film Canon Adjustment

Your logic holds up perfectly.
*   **The Rule:** *Godzilla vs. Biollante* (1989) does not exist as a film because its plot is currently unfolding in reality (Dr. Shiragami, Erika, the Rose).
*   **The Fix:** In this AU, the Heisei era took a slightly different turn. The character of "Miki Saegusa" made her debut in the 1991 blockbuster *Godzilla vs. King Ghidorah*. She became an icon of the franchise—the psychic woman who could connect with the King.
*   **The Parents:** Miki's parents were huge fans. Naming their daughter after the famous psychic was a bit of nerd culture love.
*   **The Irony:** They had no idea that the name would become a self-fulfilling prophecy. When her powers manifested, it wasn't cool; it was terrifying, leading to her abduction by the Shadow Organization.

#### II. The Rescue Scene (2020): A Moment of Surreal Recognition

This adds a fantastic layer to the climax of the **Fishing Family Arc**.

*   **The Setup:** Godric (Kintaro-san), powered by his love for the Yamamotos, infiltrates the Shadow Organization facility to rescue the children.
*   **The Encounter:** He finds the older teen/young adult who has been acting as a protector for the younger ones (like Stella). He reads her file or hears her name.
*   **The Realization:**
    > "Your name is Miki?" Godric asked, his voice low in the chaotic, alarm-filled hallway. He paused, a flicker of amusement crossing his intense features despite the danger. "Your parents had a sense of humor."
    >
    > "They liked the movies," she gasped, leaning against the wall, her mind reaching out and brushing against his—a sensation of immense, overwhelming, atomic *presence*. "You... you feel like him. Like the one in the movies. But... louder."
    >
    > Godric smirked, offering her a hand to help her stand. "Aye. I'm told the movies don't do me justice. Come on, Miki. Let's get you out of this script."

It creates an instant, unique bond between them. To Godric, she is a living reminder of the human culture he is trying to understand. To Miki, he is the literal manifestation of the fictional entity she was named after, her namesake's guardian angel come to life.

#### III. Miki's Role in the Future

This backstory explains her position in the **Psionic Division**.
*   **The Bridge:** Because she was named after a character who bridges the human and Titan worlds, and because she was rescued by the King himself, she feels a deep sense of destiny.
*   **The Mentor:** She becomes a mentor to the younger students (like Stella) not just because she is older, but because she understands the meta-weight of their existence. She knows what it's like to be a "character" in a larger story.

### Summary of the Integration

*   **Canon:** *Godzilla vs. Biollante* = Does not exist (Reality).
*   **Canon:** *Godzilla vs. King Ghidorah* = Exists (Fiction).
*   **Miki Saegusa:** Named after the fictional character from the 1991 film.
*   **The Rescue:** Godric saves her, acknowledging the irony, and solidifying her loyalty to Monarch and the Titans.

It's a perfect loop. The fiction inspired the name, the name became reality, and the reality (Godzilla) saved the namesake. Excellent work.
