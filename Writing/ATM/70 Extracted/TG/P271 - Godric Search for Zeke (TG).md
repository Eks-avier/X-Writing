# Godric's Search for Zeke

> **Source:** `Formatted_With_Thoughts - The _Titanus gojira_.md` — Prompt 271

## Your Notes

Here's how I envision Godric beginning his search:
- He gets them not to let anyone know where he's going or what he's doing... except for some important people. As amusing as it was personally, he doesn't want to drop bombshells again, like with the entire fiasco with Kong. He's like getting this "man urge" to disappear and do random shit again, and he's trying to control it — he's not nobody, he's the King of the Monsters.
    - He informs Maria. Naturally, she asks why. He wants to lie and downplay it, but Godric replies with "family business." Maria knows of the *zilla*, after all, she's been close friends with him for so long. She even met them herself. But, for the sake of this charade, she pretends she doesn't know and lets him. Although, before he goes, she whispers something: "You mustn't be too rough." He smiles; she smiles back. It's a game they unknowingly play, both oblivious to how the other really feels.
    - He informs his Betas next. He can afford to say he's going somewhere, so they must be cautious about Castle Bravo. They take it at face value. Simple, easy, and loyal: that's why they're his Betas.
    - He informs Mark. "Checking on something Serizawa mentioned." The two men say nothing more than that.
    - He informs Madison. Instead of facing her personally and suffering through her numerous questions and desires to accompany him, he writes her a letter. "Be back by next week, or tomorrow — who knows?" She face palms when she receives it. *He could have just texted*. Then, she sulks, 'cause she really wanted to go.
- He requests to have his pickup truck be transported to the East Coast — easy enough, Castle Bravo is in Bermuda.
- Godric has begun his search. He already hates the city. Actually, he hates any municipality. He hated Tokyo; he also disliked towns in England. He hates these concrete jungles.
- At best, this search would only take a day. A *zilla*'s scent is unmistakable. And if scent isn't reliable, then he has other senses that are just as sharp as his nose.
- However, he begins having doubts that this is a *zilla* because he remembered from Serizawa's words that the son of the "GINO" specimen could breathe a green flame. *A green flame? A zilla could not do such things*. They were incapable of the "Breath of the Gojira."
    - He theorizes that if this were a zilla, it might've been a hybridized one. If not, then this was not a *zilla*, but a mutation of another Titan species — as Serizawa alluded to in his section in the journal. This HEAT group dealt with these "mutations," which are really just Emergent and lowly ranked Titans, discreetly. He recognized some of these "mutated beasts" based on Ishiro's descriptions.
    - This "Son of GINO" intrigued him further.
- He's in NYC now, being annoyed by the buzz of... everything. Amidst all of that, though, is an undercurrent of atomic energy... somewhere. It's faint, it's distinct.... It's familiar and oddly nostalgic. There's a trace of gojira there, a similarity with his father, but a different flavor (a Southern gojira), but it feels diluted and impure (the zilla). "*This is a *zilla* that wields the gift of the Fallen Star?!*"

## Analysis

This is an exceptionally strong structure. It balances character dynamics, lore integration, and the "detective noir" vibe of Godric hunting down a lead in a city he hates.

Your handling of the **preparation phase** is perfect character work—especially the letter to Madison (which is hilariously on-brand for an ancient being trying to use modern communication methods) and the "game" with Maria.

The **investigation phase** is where the lore really shines. Godric's internal confusion about the "Green Flame" is the perfect narrative hook. It sets up Zeke not just as a target, but as a **biological anomaly** that challenges Godric's understanding of his own species' history.

### I. The Departure: Managing the Court

**1. The Queen's Whisper**
The conversation with Maria is a dance. He is trying to be vague ("Family business"), and she is letting him get away with it because she knows *exactly* what that family business is.
*   **The Whisper:** Her leaning in to whisper *"You mustn't be too rough"* is the tell. She knows he's going to find a relative. She knows that relative is "lesser." She is gently reminding him of his duty as a protector, not just a judge.
*   **The Smiles:** The exchanged smiles are the mask slipping for a second. It's intimacy disguised as professional courtesy.

**2. The Letter to Madison**
This is comedy gold.
*   **The Medium:** He writes it on physical stationary, seals it in an envelope, and leaves it on her desk.
*   **The Content:**
    > *Madison,*
    >
    > *I am departing for the East Coast. Verifying Serizawa's notes regarding the 1998 anomaly. Do not follow. I will return when the matter is settled. Perhaps a week. Perhaps tomorrow.*
    >
    > *— G.N.*
*   **The Reaction:** Madison finding this 20th-century artifact and screaming, "HE HAS A PHONE! HE HAS A $2,000 PHONE THAT I BOUGHT HIM!"

### II. The Concrete Jungle: A King in Traffic

Godric in New York City is a fantastic fish-out-of-water scenario, but not because he's confused—because he is **irritated.**

*   **The Truck:** He drives his massive, black, non-atomic pickup truck through Manhattan. It is his mobile territory, the only place in the city that smells right (like him) and is quiet enough for him to think.
*   **Sensory Overload:** To a Titan with super-senses, NYC is hell. The electrical grid hums like a swarm of insects. The sewage system smells of decay. The millions of heartbeats are a constant, thrumming background noise. He has to actively filter it all out to find the specific "flavor" he is hunting.

### III. The Trail of the Green Flame

**1. The Cognitive Dissonance**
*   **The Zilla Fact:** Godric knows *Titanus zilla*. He remembers them from the Southern Faction's territories. They are fast, they are scavengers, they dig. They do *not* breathe fire. They lost the "Favor of the Fallen Star" (Kratos) when they diluted their blood.
*   **Serizawa's Note:** The mention of the "Green Atomic Breath" gnaws at him. Serizawa was a good scientist; he wouldn't hallucinate that.
*   **The Hypothesis:** Godric assumes it must be a mutation or a chimera. Perhaps the Shadow Organization grafted Gojira cells onto a Zilla? The idea disgusts him, fueling his "hunter" mindset. He is going to put a misery out of its misery.

**2. The Scent**
He parks the truck near the docks or an industrial park in Queens/Bronx. He steps out, closing his eyes, and **inhales.**

*   **The Layering:**
    *   **Layer 1 (The Surface):** Ozone, chemical fire, the scent of the city.
    *   **Layer 2 (The Species):** Beneath that... *Zilla*. Unmistakable. Musk, fish-eater, rapid metabolism. It confirms his target.
    *   **Layer 3 (The Anomaly):** And deeper still... something that stops him in his tracks.
*   **The Realization:** It's the scent of the **Fallen Star.**
    *   It's weak compared to his own.
    *   It tastes "Southern"—a slightly different genetic marker.
    *   It tastes "diluted"—mixed with the other DNA.
    *   *But it is there.* It is the distinct, spicy, crackling signature of a bio-atomic reactor. A Zilla that has regained the favor of the Star.

**3. The Shift**
His mission changes instantly. He is no longer hunting a "mutation" to euthanize. He is hunting a **miracle.** A cousin who has achieved the impossible. The predator's aggression shifts into a burning, intense curiosity.

This structure is perfect. It moves from the interpersonal dynamics of the Palace to the sensory experience of the hunt, culminating in a revelation that challenges Godric's understanding of his own biology. It sets the stage for the confrontation with Zeke not just as a fight, but as a meeting of two impossibilities.
