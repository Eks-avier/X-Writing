## **The Antitheriomorphosis Universe: Definitive Master Timeline**

### **Era I: The Primordial & Pre-Transformation (Pre-History – 2019)**
*The foundational history that shapes the present.*

*   **Arc 0: The Saga of the Fallen Star (Ancient Pre-History)**
    *   **The Fallen Star:** The *Titanus gojira* ascend; the Kingdom of Kong rises and falls; Mother Prime wages the Bio-Cultural War.
    *   **The Last Family:** Dagon courts Astraea (Aurelia); Dagon sacrifices himself; Godzilla is orphaned.
    *   **The Trinity:** The early bond of Godzilla, Mothra, and Battra; the Divine Mandate; Battra's exile.
*   **Arc 0.5: The Modern Titan Wars (2014 – 2019)**
    *   **Events:** The re-emergence of the MUTOs; the awakening of Ghidorah; the Mass Awakening; Mothra's sacrifice; Godzilla's first (assisted) Burning Form; the crowning of the Alpha Paramount.
    *   **Status:** Godzilla is the "Weary Warden," his fighting spirit doused.

---

### **Era II: The Awakening & Adaptation (2020 – 2022)**
*The biological shock, the gathering of the court, and the first clash of kings.*

*   **Arc 1: The Antitheriomorphosis (Early 2020)**
    *   **The Event:** Global transformation. Godzilla enters **First Repose**.
    *   **Titan Story:** **The Fishing Family.** Godric washes ashore in Chiba, meets the Yamamotos, learns Japanese, and rediscovers the joy of learning. He breaks Repose to protect them.
    *   **Human Story:** *The Wards of Castle Bravo (Phase 1).* Stella and Sierra Balagtas are rescued and brought to Monarch.
*   **Arc 2: The Gathering (Mid 2020 – 2021)**
    *   **Titan Story:** Rodan (Fuji), Anguirus, and the Wardens are discovered. The "Castle Bravo Four" reunite awkwardly. Godric joins Monarch as a consultant.
    *   **Human Story:** *Monarch Files.* Mark Russell establishes the "Duoarchy."
    *   **Climax:** **The Coronation (Mid 2021).** Mothra returns/reincarnates on Monster Island. The revelation of Titan culture to Monarch.
*   **Arc 3: The Duel of Kings (2022)**
    *   **Titan Story:** Skull Island collapses. Godric seeks the Kong survivors.
    *   **The Fight:** Godric vs. Kong. The "Stack" battle. Kong becomes **Alpha Sovereign**. Godric's fighting style is "contaminated."
    *   **Human Story:** *The Wards (Phase 2).* The students witness the brutal reality of Titan combat training.

---

### **Era III: Conflict & Reconstruction (2023 – Early 2025)**
*The first great war, the birth of magic, and the building of a new order.*

*   **Arc 4: The Training Interlude (2022 – 2023)**
    *   **Titan Story:** Godric trains with Kong. Development of **Atomic Amplification (AA)**. Anguirus feels replaced.
*   **Arc 5: The Xilien Invasion (2023 – 2024)**
    *   **Titan Story:** Global war. Battra returns as antagonist with Gigan. The Wardens defend the globe.
    *   **Climax:** **The Uncaged Sun.** Godric fights Battra, enters "The Zone," and achieves **Burning Form** solo. Mothra kills Battra.
*   **Arc 6: The Headhunter Arc (2024 – 2025)**
    *   **Human/Titan Story:** Post-war reconstruction. Battra, now "leashed" and reincarnated, scours the globe for the scattered Sorcery students (Andrew, Elric, Mat, etc.).
*   **Arc 7: The Keystone Arc (Late 2024 – Early 2025)**
    *   **Titan Story:** Battra formally reintegrates. Anguirus and Kong resolve their tensions. The construction of the **Monster Island Palace**.
    *   **Human Story:** The founding of **Keep Charlie**. The Psionic and Sorcery Divisions are established.

---

### **Era IV: Domesticity & Secrets (Mid 2025 – Mid 2027)**
*The Golden Age of the Nordsons, lived in secret.*

*   **Arc 8: The Grand Opening (Spring 2025)**
    *   **Event:** The **"Soft Launch."** Monarch reveals the Palace to the world. The public knows Titans live there, but *not* that they are humans.
    *   **Human Story:** *Keep Charlie Chronicles.* The students defend the event from traffickers while the Titans pose as "human dignitaries."
*   **Arc 9: Blue House at Maple Street (Summer 2025 – Summer 2027)**
    *   **Titan Story:** Godric and Maria go undercover in Meadowvale as **Gaius & Helena Goddard**. They meet **Arthur Calloway**.
        *   **Development:** Godric develops **Atomic Stride** and **Atomic Mantle**.
        *   **Romance:** They fall in love for real, shedding the "partner" label.
    *   **Human Story:** *Meadowvale Diaries.* The perspective of Arthur and the neighbors.
    *   **Climax:** **The New York Incident (Summer 2027).** The **"Hard Launch."** Godric uses his powers publicly to save Arthur. The secret is out. Arthur becomes **Ichi's Vessel** and they make the **Blood-Vow**.
    *   **Resolution:** Godric and Maria return to Castle Bravo and become "boyfriend and girlfriend."

---

### **Era V: The Legacy Era (Late 2027 – 2029)**
*The expansion of the family and the healing of the past.*

*   **Arc 10: The Resurrection (Autumn 2027)**
    *   **Titan Story:** Immediately following the public reveal, the Bilusaludo steal Dagon's bones (Project Kiryu). Godric rampages. Dagon is resurrected in human form (**Darius Nordson**).
    *   **Theme:** Father and Son reunited. Healing the abandonment trauma.
*   **Arc 11: The Rose of the North (Early 2028)**
    *   **Titan Story:** The creation of **Biollante** (Erika Rose Nordson).
    *   **Theme:** Integration. Rose joins the family. **Rodan and Rose** begin their healing relationship.
*   **Arc 12: The Road to Marriage (Late 2028 – 2029)**
    *   **Titan Story:**
        *   Rodan and Rose break up amicably; Rodan confesses to Godric ("Clearing the Skies").
        *   Mothra and Battra fully reconcile regarding the Divine Mandate.
        *   Godric proposes (Nov 2028).
    *   **The Second Repose:** Upon learning of Maria's pregnancy, Godric's *Horme* shifts from "Survival" to "Family," causing a power drop.
    *   **Climax:** The Wedding (April 2029).
    *   **Ending:** The Birth of **Junior** and **Leo** (Dec 2029).

---

### **Era VI: The Convergence / Pacific Rim Era (2030)**
*The global crisis where the parents must trust the world to survive without them.*

*   **Arc 13: The Breach (Surface)**
    *   **Titan Story:** Precursors invade with Kaiju. Godric refuses to leave his wife and newborns.
    *   **Human Story:** The **PPDC** and **Jaegers** deploy. The **Keep Charlie** students mobilize for war. Monarch acts as the strategic bridge.
*   **Arc 14: The Hollow Throne (Below)**
    *   **Titan Story:** Skar King and Shimo rise in the Hollow Earth.
    *   **Climax:** Kong defends his kingdom alone, proving his Sovereignty.
*   **Arc 15: The Red Planet (Space)**
    *   **Titan Story:** Ichi and Arthur hunt the Precursors on the Moon and Mars.
    *   **Climax:** Ichi finds meaning, defeats the Precursors, and accidentally terraforms Mars.

---

### **Era VII: The Next Generation (2030 – Future)**
*The story of the children and the future of the lineage.*

*   **Arc 16: The Growing Years (2030 – 2045)**
    *   **Events:** Lora is born. The children manifest powers (Scales at 7, Atomic Breath at 10-12).
    *   **Key Scenes:** The "Family Pile," the smithy training, the sibling rivalries.
*   **Arc 17: The Star-Crossed Pilgrimage (Approx. 2047)**
    *   **The Mission:** Godric is poisoned by his own energy. The siblings travel to the parallel Earth to find the cure.
    *   **Junior:** Meets his tragic love, learns the burden of leadership.
    *   **Leo:** Glimpses the **Rainbow Form**.
    *   **Lora:** Sacrifices biological immortality (eternal youth) to amend the Covenant and gain **Magic**.
    *   **Resolution:** Godric is saved. The children return as adults in spirit.

This is the complete roadmap. It covers every beat, every character arc, and every major event we have designed.
