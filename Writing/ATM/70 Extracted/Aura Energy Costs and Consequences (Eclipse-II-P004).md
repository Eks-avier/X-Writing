# Aura Energy Costs and Consequences

> **Source:** `Eclipse II` - Prompt 4 (Lines 585-778)

## Your Notes

Then, we have the consequences of each effect:

Intensity Management:

* Flaring: Drains stamina faster, regardless of Kraton type, but Krator stamina drains slower due to being accustomed to the passivity of their emission. It also expands Aura slightly as a side-effect. Due to these, its energy cost increases exponentially, but Kratores experience reduced costs for marginal intensity increases.

* Suppression: Nearly free for a Koinon, as they only need to revert back to emitting traces or modulating those traces themselves. Costly for a Krator initially, but becomes efficient and less draining once mastered.
  * For a Krator, its energy cost appears to be logarithmic, but then steeply falls down upon subsequent mastery.

Spatial Management:

* Reduction: Slightly intensifies the Aura, the smaller its radius becomes.

* Expansion: Risks "thinning" the effect of the Aura.
  * I'm thinking of making the enlargement effect rise logarithmically, then slowing down the further one expands, while increasing the costs further than normal: a diminishing returns sort of mechanic.

## Analysis

### Crystallized Summary & Tactical Implications

This new, more granular system provides a rich tactical landscape:

| **Action** | **Formal Term** | **Axis** | **Primary Effect** | **Secondary Consequence** | **Cost Profile** |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Flaring** | **Anapsis** | Intensity | **Increases Potency** | Slightly expands area | Exponential Stamina Drain |
| **Suppression** | **Kryptis** | Intensity | **Decreases Potency** | None | Free (Koinon) / Steep learning curve (Krator) |
| **Expansion** | **Ektasis** | Spatial | **Increases Area** | "Thins out" effects | Exponential, diminishing returns |
| **Reduction** | **Systole** | Spatial | **Decreases Area** | "Condenses" effects | Low (requires focus) |

This framework is excellent. It's logical, balanced, and creates clear trade-offs. A Kraton can't simply have a massive, super-intense Aura without an immense cost. To dominate a large area effectively, they must master both **Expansion (Ektasis)** to reach it and **Flaring (Anapsis)** to maintain potency, a combination reserved for the most powerful and disciplined beings.

Conversely, a Kraton can use **Reduction (Systole)** to create a deceptively small but potent field for close-quarters combat.
