### Developmental Timeline: The Hybrid Growth Curve

Before diving into their specific abilities, let's establish a clear timeline for when their key powers emerge. This will provide a crucial framework for their growth.

*   **Age 0-2 (The Larval Stage):** Primarily developing basic motor skills and psionic sensitivity. Their Gojira heritage is mostly dormant, but they possess incredible resilience. Lora's volatile personality is hidden behind a demure baby facade.
*   **Age 2-4 (The Awakening):**
    *   **Age 2:** Latent atomic energy and radiation senses begin to activate. They instinctively begin to absorb ambient radiation. Basic telepathy and telekinesis (Unnon traits) become controllable.
    *   **Age 4:** **Kratoros Thelesis** becomes palpable in Junior and Leo. Their "Royal Presence" (First Layer Aura) can be felt by sensitive beings, though it is uncontrolled. Lora does *not* manifest this, confirming she is a Koinon.
*   **Age 7 (The Metamorphosis):** Their **scales manifest**. This is their metaphysical "cocooning" stage, marking the true awakening of their *mosura* abilities and cementing their core identities.
*   **Age 13 (The Threshold):** Their powers become more refined and powerful. They are now considered young adolescents in Titan terms and begin their formal training in earnest under their family's guidance.
*   **Adulthood (Variable):** Their hybrid nature means their path to full maturity is unknown. This will be a central point of discovery for them and their family.

### Layer 1: Base Physical and Cognitive Capabilities

This layer defines their inherent, non-systemic attributes. All three children share an incredibly high baseline due to their heritage, but express it in unique ways.

#### 1. Intelligence & Cognitive Style

*   **Godric Jr. "Junior" (The Strategist):**
    *   **Core Trait:** Tactical and intuitive. He is a natural strategist who sees the "whole board."
    *   **Cognitive Style:** Deeply analytical but trusts his gut feelings. He processes information rapidly and identifies patterns that others miss. His intuition is his greatest intellectual asset, allowing him to anticipate enemy moves before they are made.
    *   **Academic Parallel:** He would excel at chess, complex strategy games, and subjects that require understanding systems, like economics or political science.

*   **Leo (The Innovator):**
    *   **Core Trait:** Creative and unpredictable. He is the out-of-the-box thinker who finds solutions no one else considers.
    *   **Cognitive Style:** A perfect balance of intuition and analytical thought. His mind makes associative leaps, connecting seemingly unrelated concepts to create novel solutions. He's not an idiot; his goofiness is a byproduct of a mind that operates on a different, more chaotic wavelength.
    *   **Academic Parallel:** He would excel at physics, engineering, and the arts—subjects where creativity and a deep understanding of principles are key to innovation.

*   **Lora (The Tactician):**
    *   **Core Trait:** Relentless and pragmatic. She is the battlefield tactician focused on the immediate conflict.
    *   **Cognitive Style:** Highly logical and retentive. She absorbs and processes information with incredible speed, making her a natural for the academic rigors of magic. In combat, she is hyper-focused, analyzing enemy weaknesses in real-time to press her advantage.
    *   **Academic Parallel:** She is the top academic student, excelling in memorization-heavy and logic-based fields like history, chemistry, and, of course, the intricate art of sorcery.

#### 2. Strength & Combat Style

*   **Junior (The Fencer):**
    *   **Natural Strength:** Immense, second only to Leo's raw power.
    *   **Combat Style:** He is a master of **applied force**. He prefers precision over brute strength, using his intelligence and intuition to find the most efficient way to end a fight.
    *   **Weapon of Choice:** His preference for swords is a direct reflection of this. A sword is a tool that extends one's reach and requires skill, precision, and strategy to wield effectively. His **Magnification Scales** make a sword in his hands exponentially more lethal, rewarding his skilled approach.

*   **Leo (The Berserker):**
    *   **Natural Strength:** The highest raw physical power of the three. He is a true physical powerhouse.
    *   **Combat Style:** He is a master of **overwhelming force**. His approach is direct, powerful, and adaptable. He relies on his incredible strength and the versatile abilities of his future forms to overwhelm his opponents.

She closes the distance, gets inside their guard, and uses leverage and skill to bring them down. Her **Adhesive Scales** are the perfect tool for this, allowing her to control and immobilize her foes up close.
