# Psionics Core Metaphor - Artist and Ethicist

> **Source:** `Eclipse II` - Prompt 5 (Lines 781-986)

## Your Notes

With Psionics, the metaphor is art, music, illustration, art itself. They are intended to be illustrators, musicians, dancers, and even martial arts practitioners. Mothra is the most beautiful and talented being on Earth, Titan, or human. She is "talent unbound" in contrast to her twin brother Battra's "hard work beats talent." Within her soul is an authentic angelic being from Heaven itself. But, due to more dense lore reasons which I won't divulge here, psionics has been tainted by science, by technology. Current Psionics is finding a way to be in tune with its aesthetic side.

Psionics shouldn't just be Aesthetics, but Axiology as a whole, encompassing Ethics, too. One of the key tensions in psionic-focused narratives within this AU focuses on the ethical use of telepathy, for example, and the reclamation of Psionics as an art, rather than as a science. So, it also undergoes a linguistic-thematic evolution as the overarching narrative progresses.

Despite distinctions between Active and Passive Telepathy, there is also something called "sensitivity," much like how certain people are sensitive to sounds, smells, and alike, so too are telepaths, yet at the same time, we can only focus on one smell, one sight, one sound. Telepathy is just as much voluntary as it is involuntary.

## Analysis

### Psionics: The Artist and Ethicist (Mothra)

* **Axiological/Aesthetical Core ("What is valued/felt"):** This is a profound choice. It shifts Psionics from just "mind powers" to a system deeply concerned with values, ethics, and beauty.

* **"Talent Unbound":** Mothra, as the inheritor of the Divine Soul, embodies this. Her power is a natural, graceful extension of her being, much like a gifted musician's talent. It flows, it feels, it connects.

* **The "Tainted by Science" Conflict:** This introduces a fantastic internal conflict for the system itself. The struggle to reclaim Psionics as an art form from those who would treat it as a cold, quantifiable science provides a great narrative engine for stories centered on the Psionic Division.

* **Ethics of Telepathy:** One of the key tensions focuses on the ethical use of telepathy and the reclamation of Psionics as an art rather than a science.
