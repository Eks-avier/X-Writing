# Battra Telepathy Pragmatism

> **Source:** `Eclipse II` - Prompt 5 (Lines 781-986)

## Your Notes

In contrast, Battra cares not at all for minds whenever he uses his telepathy. In fact, if it's for the sake of knowledge, he'll gladly rip entire memories out of people's brains to satisfy his thirst. To Battra, the mind is a nearly unparalleled knowledge base, one that is worthy to explore like a public library; to be graced by the presence of a telepath such as himself is of great honor, rather than unethical intrusion. Since establishing Keep Charlie, Mothra often reminds and stresses to him to set an example, if anything else, lest she deal with him personally once more.

### Sample Scene: Battra Memory Extraction

```markdown
"Please! Let me go! I was only a glorified janitor in that facility! I never did anything to those kids."

Battra raised a finger to his chin, as if to consider.

In this stooge's mind, he sees halls, white, pristine, and sterile. He sees this creature sweep and dust.

How quaint, even government facilities meant to hold and experiment on psionic children have their little innkeepers, or whatever that term has evolved to now.

"Please... let me go."

"You will prove useful."

"PLEASE--wait... what?"

Battra placed a finger on his temple; the gesture struck him as odd, unnecessary due to the ease with which this man's mind was to enter. *Was it even closed in the first place?* Yet, perhaps his students have grown on him a bit.

Memories like those films humans make stream from this human's mind and onto his own.

*Oh, this never gets old...*

The man slumps, unconscious.

"What... what did you do to him?!" the operative assigned to follow him asked. His name was of no concern to him.

"Why, shall we not make haste? I knew your superiors said he was useless to you, but it's my chance to prove that my students could be of use to you. This man's memories are worth their weight in gold, after all."

Battra ushered his accomplice to the door. "Come now, human, my students await in Castle Bravo; surely, they'll be glad one piece of the puzzle is solved."

"That's not what I asked for--"

"Don't mind him," Battra said, giggling at his pun. "He'll only be gone for a couple of days; there were about 3 years of memories I siphoned out of him."

"So we're just gonna leave him here? To starve, to die?"

"Make haste now, *human*. We have pressing matters to attend to."
```

## Analysis

For Battra, a master of an epistemological system, the mind is simply another source of information--a "public library." He sees Mothra's restraint not as ethical, but as a "lack of foresight" and a failure to use a valuable tool. To him, refusing to gather information is a strategic error.

The scene perfectly illustrates the contrast with Mothra's approach. Where Mothra is diplomatic and seeks permission, Battra is pragmatic and rips out memories for a greater strategic purpose.
