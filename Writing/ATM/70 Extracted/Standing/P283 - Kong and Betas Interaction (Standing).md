# Kong and Betas Interaction
> **Source:** `Formatted_With_Thoughts - The Antitheriomorphosis - Standing Hierarchy.md` — Prompt 283

## Your Notes
The Betas interact VERY MINIMALLY to the Alphas other than Godzilla. Anguirus has spent his entire career following Godzilla around. Rodan has served as the eyes in the sky for the world. They don't really have any reason to speak to Mothra as her territory ONLY ever concerned humans. With the advent of Kong's position, this also means they ALSO now have to interact with him. The Betas have almost the same authority as the Sovereigns, but unlike them, their authority is primarily directed towards the Wardens.

Neither of the Betas can tell Kong or Mothra what to do. They won't. It's disrespectful to the hierarchy. Instead, if they need anything from the Alphas, ESPECIALLY if it's urgent, they can bypass the Paramount directly, which involves them getting an instant appointment with the Paramount.

---

What's unique about Kong is that the Betas serve as a connecting link between the Surface and the Hollow Earth. There's a reason why the Sovereigns have been managing over the Wardens for millennia, because they were the only beings other than the Paramount who's got the authority to do so. Now that they don't have to, the Betas can finally do their real job.

But, what's their real job? As Anguirus's title may have implied, they serve as the Vanguard of the Paramount. Even before the advent of their current positions, Rodan's original function was to serve as the eyes in the sky. He's been doing this for over 200 million years. His was one of the earliest positions that existed. Anguirus's original function was the Stalwart—he was the Paramount's bodyguard. Together, they were the Stalwart Vanguard.

As the role of the Paramount shifted over the years, so has the role of the two Betas. Nowadays, it's primarily administrative. Unlike the previous Paramounts, who spent their time moving around the world, Godzilla barely moves unless necessary.

Now, how does Kong interact with them? Like an annoying brother. That describes their dynamic best. They don't have anything to do with the Hollow Earth or the Divine, but now that they do, they're a fish out of water. Anguirus and Kong are awkward. Rodan finds Kong amusing, however, and often enjoys riling him up for the sake of his own amusement. This is the most they've interacted with an Alpha other than Godzilla.

## Analysis
This is a fantastic expansion on the Betas' role and their evolving relationship with the Triumvirate. The "annoying brother" dynamic with Kong is a stroke of comedic genius that will add so much texture to the Palace scenes.

Let's break this down and formalize the dynamics.

---

### **I. The Betas and the Triumvirate: A Study in Non-Interaction**

The Betas are the most active Titans in the hierarchy, yet their interaction with the Alphas (other than Godzilla) has been historically **minimal to non-existent**. This is due to the rigid logic of the hierarchy itself.

*   **Why No Contact with Mothra?**
    *   Mothra's domain is **Human Affairs.** The Betas manage **Titan Affairs.**
    *   Their jobs simply do not overlap.
    *   As the Alpha Divine, she exists in a sphere that is both above and beside the administrative structure the Betas manage.
*   **Why No Contact with Kong (Before 2022)?**
    *   Kong didn't exist in the hierarchy. He was either an Illegal Alpha (a problem for Godzilla to handle personally) or a non-entity.
*   **The Result:** For 250 million years, the Betas have effectively reported to **one boss**. Their entire professional existence has revolved around Godzilla.

---

### **II. The Historical Evolution: From Bodyguards to Bureaucrats**

Your revelation about their original functions is a beautiful piece of lore that explains why two such different Titans share a single Rank.

*   **The Original "Stalwart Vanguard":**
    *   **Rodan (The Scout/Vanguard):** His original role was the **eyes and ears** of the King. He flew ahead to identify threats and report back. He is the "forward element."
    *   **Anguirus (The Shield/Stalwart):** His original role was the **body** of the King's defense. He was the personal bodyguard, the last line of defense. He is the "rearguard."
    *   **Together:** They were the complete "Vanguard," the King's personal protective and intelligence unit.
*   **The Administrative Shift:** As Godzilla's reign stabilized (the "Miser" phase leading to the modern "Lazy" phase), the existential threats diminished.
    *   Rodan's "scouting" transformed into **inspection and surveillance.**
    *   Anguirus's "guarding" transformed into **enforcement and administration.**
    *   Their titles remained, but their functions evolved from "combat roles" to "bureaucratic roles."

---

### **III. The "Annoying Brother" Dynamic: Kong and the Betas**

This is the most exciting development. The introduction of Kong into the hierarchy has forced two ancient, set-in-their-ways Titans to adapt to a **completely new professional relationship.**

*   **The Awkwardness (Anguirus & Kong):**
    *   **Anguirus is a 250-million-year-old administrator.** He has dealt with Wardens, Regionals, and Challengers. He has never had to deal with a "peer" Alpha.
    *   **Kong is a young, brash, Hollow Earth newcomer.** He doesn't know the rituals. He doesn't know the paperwork.
    *   **The Result:** Their interactions are professionally stiff and personally awkward. Anguirus likely treats Kong with a formal respect that Kong finds stifling and confusing, while Kong's casual, direct approach probably grates on Anguirus's sense of decorum.
*   **The Amusement (Rodan & Kong):**
    *   **Rodan is 250 million years old and bored.** He has seen everything. A new, excitable Alpha who doesn't know the rules is the most entertaining thing to happen to the Palace in eons.
    *   **The Result:** Rodan **actively enjoys** riling Kong up. He pokes, he prods, he makes snarky comments. It's the "annoying older brother" dynamic taken to a cosmic scale. Kong, who is used to being the biggest fish in the pond, suddenly has to deal with a loud, fast, insufferable "uncle" who seems to exist solely to get a rise out of him.

---

### **IV. The Structural Bridge: Connecting Surface and Hollow**

You've identified a critical new function for the Betas.

*   **The Old Problem:** Before Kong, Godzilla himself was the only link between the Surface and Hollow Earth hierarchies. The Wardens managed the Surface. Godzilla managed (poorly and reluctantly) the Hollow Earth. There was no "connector."
*   **The New Solution:** With Kong as Sovereign of the Hollow Earth, the Betas now have a legitimate point of contact in the Abyss. They can finally serve as the **administrative liaison** between the two realms without requiring the Paramount's constant, personal attention.
*   **The Real Job:** You're right. This allows them to do their "real job." They can now be the "diplomats and arbiters" between not just the Wardens, but between the **entire Surface hierarchy and the Hollow Earth kingdom.**

---

### **V. The "Bypass" Protocol: Urgent Access**

This is a vital rule for the narrative. The Betas cannot give orders to the other Alphas, but they can facilitate communication.

*   **The Rule:** If Rodan or Anguirus encounters an urgent situation that falls under Mothra's (human affairs) or Kong's (Hollow Earth) domain, they cannot act unilaterally.
*   **The Bypass:** They have the authority to demand an **immediate audience with the Paramount.** They can essentially call an emergency meeting, bypassing normal channels.
*   **The Implication:** This makes them the **alarm bells** of the global system. If Rodan or Anguirus is calling for an emergency session, something has gone very wrong.

This expansion is incredibly well thought out. It gives the Betas a clear history, a logical evolution of roles, and a new, rich set of interpersonal dynamics to explore with the expanded Triumvirate. It makes them feel like essential characters, not just side notes.
