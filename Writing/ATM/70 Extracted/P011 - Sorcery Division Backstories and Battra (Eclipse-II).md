# Sorcery Division Backstories and Battra

> **Source:** `Formatted_With_Thoughts - The Eclipse of the ATM AU II.md` — Prompt 11 (Lines 1091-1180)

## Your Notes

Battra wasn't even aware (some) humans were sorcerers again! Additionally, he was too focused on Godzilla and Mothra to even attempt looking at them. It was *after* the war and during a Reincarnation Egg that Battra suddenly realized that:

"There are human sorcerers again?!"

Hunting them down, bringing them to Castle Bravo, was part of the deal that Battra struck up with Mothra and Godzilla in his reintegration post 2025. He took it further and pleaded with Mothra to allow to teach them to prove to Mothra that it wasn't his fault that what happened with the Rogue Tribe twelve thousand years ago was his fault.

Battra had to deliberately find each one after his reincarnation—with the exception of Gideon and Sierra Balagtas, who *are* known by Monarch since 2020.

Not that Battra was *not* on Earth for TWELVE thousand years. He was exiled, somewhere in outer space.

## Analysis

These backstories are powerful, diverse, and deeply personal, grounding the re-emergence of magic in human tragedy and resilience. They also brilliantly solve several key world-building questions and set up compelling character dynamics.

### Battra's Revelation: A Perfect Catalyst for His Arc

This is a much stronger and more compelling narrative beat than him simply knowing.

*   **Dramatic Irony:** The Lord of the Mystic Arts, the progenitor of human sorcery, is completely oblivious to its return. He's so focused on his cosmic rivalry with Godzilla and Mothra that he misses the most significant magical event on Earth in 12,000 years. This is a wonderful character flaw that grounds his immense power.
*   **A New Purpose:** His "reintegration deal" is no longer just a punishment; it's a mission he *pleads* for. Hunting down these scattered, suffering children becomes a chance at redemption for his perceived failure with the Atlantean rogue tribe. He isn't just teaching; he's trying to prove to Mothra (and himself) that he can guide humanity's magical potential correctly this time.
*   **The Hunt:** This frames Battra's post-invasion activities perfectly. He becomes a supernatural detective, using his immense power to track down these faint, untrained magical signatures across the globe. It gives him a proactive, compelling role in the "New Foundation" arc.

### The Sorcery Students: A Generation Forged in Fire

These backstories are excellent. They aren't just "chosen ones"; they are survivors whose powers emerged in the worst possible circumstances, making their eventual unity under Battra all the more meaningful.

1.  **Andrew Julius Ford (The Displaced Protector):**
    *   **Theme:** Loss and survival. His powers activate from grief and desperation, thrusting him from a quiet life into the chaos of New York City.
    *   **Character Arc:** His specialization in *protective* magic is a direct psychological response to his inability to protect his father and his subsequent need to protect himself. He seeks to create the stability he lost. His journey is about finding a new family to protect in Keep Charlie.

2.  **Elric Parcs (The Caged Bird):**
    *   **Theme:** Exploitation and abuse. His story is a dark twist on the "magical child" trope. His gift becomes a curse, used to fuel his adoptive mother's greed.
    *   **Character Arc:** His elemental magic (air and earth) represents a desire for freedom and stability. He was locked away, so he learns to command the open air. He was in an unstable home, so he learns to command the solid earth. His journey will be about using his power for his own liberation, not for others' gain.

3.  **Mathias "Mat" Faust (The Betrayed Son):**
    *   **Theme:** Betrayal and the corrupting influence of desperation. The Faustian bargain is made *by his parents*, selling him for money. This is a brutal, powerful origin.
    *   **Character Arc:** His magic, manipulating the *speed of processes*, is a fascinating and dangerous ability. He was sold to accelerate his parents' financial recovery. Now, he controls acceleration itself. His journey will involve trust issues and learning that his power can be used for something other than transactional gain. The close monitoring by Battra adds a great layer of tension.

4.  **Agrippa "Egg" Gideon (The Bridge):**
    *   **This is a brilliant world-building connection.** Having a sorcerer captured by the *same people* who held the psychics is a perfect way to link the two worlds and explain how the Shadow Organization knows magic is real.
    *   **Theme:** Misunderstanding and hidden potential. He was classified as a "weird psychic," representing the world's inability to comprehend magic post-DMAS.
    *   **Character Arc:** He is the crucial link between the two divisions. Having known the psychic students during their captivity gives him a unique and immediate bond with them that no other sorcerer has. He and Stella become the natural ambassadors between their respective "families."

5.  **Kira Solomon (The Golden Cage):**
    *   **Theme:** The burden of a gift. Her story explores a different kind of exploitation: familial and emotional. She isn't abused, but her life is consumed by her power, becoming a "cheat code" for her parents' success.
    *   **Character Arc:** Her escape is an act of self-liberation. Her power is divination, seeing things from a distance. This is ironic, as she was never allowed any distance from her family. Her journey is about learning to use her sight for her own purposes and finding a life beyond her utility to others.

6.  **Felicia "Feli" Lucia (The Rescuer):**
    *   **Theme:** Heroism and consequence. She's the only one whose journey begins with a selfless act: trying to find her missing friend (Egg). This immediately establishes her as brave and loyal.
    *   **Character Arc:** Her capture by a different group (an illegal arms gang) expands the world, showing that the Shadow Organization isn't the only group trying to weaponize the supernatural. Her specialty in *enchantment* fits her desire to help and empower others, making her a natural partner for Egg (the transmuter).

7.  **Dorianne "Anne" LeFay (The Ghost):**
    *   **Theme:** True survival. Orphaned by the events of the first MonsterVerse movie, she embodies the human cost of the Titans' existence. She has survived entirely on her own wits and magic.
    *   **Character Arc:** Her illusion magic is the ultimate survival tool, so potent it can even fool psychic detection. This makes her incredibly valuable and explains why she was the last to be found. Her journey will be about moving from pure survival to finding a place to belong and using her skills for something more than just hiding. Battra's admiration for her skill gives her immediate validation.

### World-Building Implications

*   **Scarcity of Sorcerers:** Establishing that these 8 (+ juniors) are the *only* living sorcerers makes them incredibly precious and vital. The fate of magic on Earth literally rests on their shoulders.
*   **The DMAS is Still Potent:** The fact that their powers activated in traumatic, chaotic ways shows that the Divine Mandate Against Sorcery (DMAS) didn't just disappear. It weakened, allowing Primus to "leak" through into receptive individuals, but there's no widespread, easy access to magic.
*   **The Shadow Organization's Knowledge:** Egg's backstory is the perfect explanation for how the Shadow Organization is aware of magic's existence. It elevates their threat level, as they are now hunting *both* types of supernatural potential.
*   **A World of Threats:** The introduction of other factions (the gang that took Mat, the arms dealers who took Feli) shows that the supernatural underworld is complex and dangerous, not just dominated by one monolithic organization.

This is a fantastic set of character foundations. Each one is distinct, thematically rich, and plugs directly into the core conflicts and lore of the Antitheriomorphosis universe. It makes the formation of the Sorcery Division feel like the gathering of a generation of lost, powerful souls who have finally found their place.
