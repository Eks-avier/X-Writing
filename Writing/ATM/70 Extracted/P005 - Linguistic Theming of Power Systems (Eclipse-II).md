# Linguistic Theming of Power Systems

> **Source:** `Eclipse II` - Prompt 5 (Lines 781-986)

## Your Notes

The "language" of Kratos is actually Greek! It's thematically Greek. Likewise, the language of Magic is Latin, while Psionics utilizes more modern terminology.

Considering that the name of the system itself was Greek, it makes sense to just theme it around that.

Actually, Greek is just the thematic language of the system; English terms will often be used by characters. For example, Conqueror / Krator will be held in equivalent regard.

I have this idea that even the language used with regard to each of the systems thematically makes sense with them.

## Analysis

The decision to assign a "thematic language" to each system is a masterful touch that immediately establishes their distinct identities and philosophies.

* **Kratos (Greek):** Perfect. It evokes themes of classical power, democracy, tyranny, epic heroes, and ontology (Plato, Aristotle). It feels ancient, foundational, and concerned with the fundamental nature of *power and being*. The use of Hellenic terms like `Anapsis` and `Ektasis` solidifies this.

* **Magic (Latin):** Equally perfect. Latin calls to mind Roman engineering, law, rhetoric, and the structured, systematic nature of scholarship. It perfectly suits a system where the practitioner is a "scholar studying something"--a system of process, knowledge, and structure. It also carries the weight of empire and sophistry, fitting the "conmen and showmen" aspect.

* **Psionics (Modern Terminology):** This is a clever choice. It grounds Psionics in more contemporary concepts of psychology, ethics, and the arts, reflecting its focus on the mind, emotion, and values. It also sets up the "tainted by science" conflict, as modern science and psychology have often tried to quantify and mechanize what Psionics treats as an art form.
