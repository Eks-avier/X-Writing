### The Royal Court of the Kraton: A Framework of Sovereign Power

A Kraton's Aura is not merely a field of energy; it is their **Royal Court**, the domain through which their sovereign will is expressed. A Kraton's mastery is defined by their control over this court, governed by two fundamental edicts and expressed through four primary applications of power.

#### The Two Edicts of Sovereignty (The Control Dials)

These are the two fundamental "dials" a Kraton can manipulate to manage their Royal Court.

1.  **The Edict of Dominion (Spatial Management):** This governs the *size and reach* of the Royal Court. It is the monarch's control over the boundaries of their kingdom.
    *   **Expansion (*Ektasis*):** Enlarging the court's radius, extending the monarch's influence but risking "thinning" their presence.
    *   **Reduction (*Systole*):** Shrinking the court's radius, concentrating the monarch's presence and making it more potent.

2.  **The Edict of Majesty (Intensity Management):** This governs the *potency and intensity* of the monarch's presence within their court.
    *   **Flaring (*Anapsis*):** Intensifying the monarch's presence, making the court itself an expression of their power.
    *   **Suppression (*Kryptis*):** Muting the monarch's presence, allowing for subtlety, precision, or mercy.

#### The Four Applications of Sovereign Power

These are the four specific, active techniques a Kraton can employ, representing the direct actions of the monarch within their personal and territorial domains.

| **Domain** | **Application** | **The Royal Analogy** | **Description** |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Personal** | **Neutralization** (Defensive Coating) | **The Royal Armor** | The monarch dons their personal regalia of will, a dense layer of Aura that dissolves incoming threats. It is a purely defensive act, protecting the sovereign's person. |
| **Personal** | **Penetration** (Offensive Coating) | **The Sceptered Strike** | The monarch imbues their weapon—be it a fist, a blade, or a beam of energy—with their authority, allowing it to bypass the defenses of lesser beings. |
| **Territorial** | **Flaring (*Anapsis*)** | **The Majestic Radiance** | The monarch unleashes their raw power, causing the very atmosphere of the Royal Court to become a weapon. The air burns, the ground freezes, or gravity intensifies. This is a *physical* assault on the territory. |
| **Territorial** | **Submission (*Proskynesis*)** | **The Sovereign Decree** | The monarch issues an undeniable, silent command throughout their court, targeting the very will of all within. This is a *psychological* assault on all subjects within the territory, compelling them to kneel. |

### Godzilla's Magic Nullification: The Ultimate Majestic Radiance

With this new clarity, Godzilla's feat is not Submission. It is an extreme application of **Flaring (Anapsis)**. He turned the dial of his **Edict of Majesty** to its absolute maximum, projecting his **Majestic Radiance** with such overwhelming atomic force that the fundamental energy of Magic (Primus) was physically displaced and could not exist in the same space. He made the very "air" of his Royal Court hostile to the laws of another king.

### The Koinon Lord vs. The Krator Monarch (The CPU Analogy)

This is where the distinction becomes a massive tactical advantage for a Krator.

#### The Koinon Lord (Single-Core Processing)

A Koinon is a powerful lord, but their will is a singular resource. They can project a **Sovereign Decree (Submission)**, but it requires their full concentration. They must actively shift their focus from defending themselves with their **Royal Armor (Neutralization)** to projecting their will outward. They can do one or the other effectively, but not both at the same time.

#### The Krator Monarch (Dual-Core Processing)

A Krator's power is architecturally different, thanks to their two-layered Aura.

1.  **The Royal Presence (First Layer):** This is the Krator's **innate, perpetual majesty**. It is always on, projecting a constant, low-level **Sovereign Decree (Submission)** without any conscious thought or effort. This is their divine right made manifest—the constant, unnerving pressure that forces lesser wills to bend.
2.  **The Conscious Will (Second Layer):** Because their Royal Presence handles the baseline intimidation automatically, a Krator's conscious will is completely free. They can dedicate 100% of their focus to wielding their **Royal Armor** and their **Sceptered Strike** simultaneously, even while their very presence is already crushing their opponent's spirit.

This is the ultimate advantage of a true monarch. A lord must announce their authority. A monarch's authority is felt the moment you enter their court, leaving them free to attend to the business of combat.
