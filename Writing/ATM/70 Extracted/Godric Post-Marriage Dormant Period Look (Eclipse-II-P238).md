### **Maria's Private Catalog: The "Flavors" of the Active Season King**

In the quiet moments of her own mind, Maria has a private, unspoken catalog of Godric's various presentations during his Active Season. Each is a different facet of the man she loves, and each evokes a unique, powerful, and often frustratingly compelling response from her.

#### **Flavor 1: The Regent (Clean-Shaven)**
*(No image provided, but we can extrapolate)*

*   **The Look:** This is Godric at his most controlled, most "perfect." The jawline is sharp and defined, the classical Greco-Roman ideals of his form are on full display. When paired with a crisp suit and his severe, swept-back hair, he is the very picture of a young, powerful, and untouchable monarch.
*   **Maria's Perspective:** She recognizes this as his **"Armor."** It's the face he presents to the world, a mask of flawless, regal authority. She admires the sheer power of it, but it's also the look that feels most distant to her. It is the King, not the man. It is a beautiful sculpture, but she knows the warmth that lies beneath the marble.

#### **Flavor 2: The Ambassador (Image 2 - Tuxedo, Stubble)**

*   **The Look:** The stubble is the first crack in the perfect facade. It is the hint of ruggedness beneath the refined elegance of the tuxedo. The intense, blue-eyed gaze is that of a man on high alert, assessing every variable in the room. This is the look of a king navigating the treacherous waters of human high society.
*   **Maria's Perspective:** She calls this **"The Ambassador."** It is Godric performing his diplomatic duty with devastating perfection. She is immensely proud when she sees him like this, the undisputed center of gravity in any room. The stubble is the detail she latches onto—the first sign of the "wilderness" she loves, a promise that the authentic man is still there beneath the flawless performance.

#### **Flavor 3: The Operative (Image 1 - Trench Coat, Stubble)**

*   **The Look:** The stubble is more pronounced, a clear 24-48 hours of growth. The hair is slightly more tousled, and the popped collar of the trench coat gives him an air of mystery and danger. This is not a diplomat; this is a predator on the hunt, a field agent deep in his mission. The gaze is not just assessing; it is focused, intense, and lethal.
*   **Maria's Perspective:** This is **"The Agent."** This is the look that both thrills her and fills her with a quiet dread. It is the physical manifestation of his duty as a guardian, the silent warrior she has known for eons. It's a look of profound competence and danger, a reminder that the man she loves is also the planet's most effective weapon.

#### **Flavor 4: The Patriarch (Image 4 - Suit, Full Beard)**

*   **The Look:** This is the most powerful and complex of his Active Season looks. The full, well-maintained beard combined with the sharp, tailored suit creates an aura of immense, settled authority. This is not a young prince or a field agent; this is a **King.** This is the face of a being who has ruled for a quarter of a billion years. The beard adds a gravitas and wisdom that his younger, clean-shaven face masterfully conceals.
*   **Maria's Perspective:** This is her secret favorite of the active styles, the one she calls **"The Patriarch."** Why? Because it is the closest he gets to his true, unguarded self during his "on-duty" months. It is a perfect synthesis of the formidable King (the suit and tie) and the rugged, authentic Man she fell in love with by the poolside (the full beard). It's a glimpse of the husband she has in private, now on public display. It is the look that makes maintaining her professional composure the most difficult.

It's a look of quiet contemplation and weary peace, the closest he comes to his dormant self during the active season, and a sight Maria secretly cherishes.

These "flavors" are a brilliant way to visually track his psychological state and the circumstances of the narrative. They are a testament to the depth of your character design and the profound, observant love that defines Maria's perspective.
