# Kratos Mechanics Fixing Circular Definitions

> **Source:** `Formatted_With_Thoughts - The Eclipse of the ATM AU II.md` — Prompt 47 (Lines 3037-3149)

## Your Notes

Now that we have explored how Godzilla nullifies Magic, it's time for a deep dive into the mechanics of Krátos once again.

Submission is just Neutralization applied to the Aura rather than on the body. Neutralization is just Coating oneself with one's Aura, which enables one's willpower to intercept attacks by dissolving them via the same process that enables Injection to dissolve and disable Psionics and Magic.

But if:
- Aura is Manifestation
- Penetration is Aura applied to the body or ranged attacks
- Neutralization is an Aura applied to the body

Then, shouldn't Submission be just Aura itself? It's like adding positively and negatively, we just arrived at the same exact spot on the number line!

Can we salvage this or perhaps reframe it differently?

## Analysis

Excellent question. You've correctly identified a point of logical fuzziness in the previous definitions, and your instinct is spot-on. The chain of `Submission -> Neutralization -> Coating -> Aura` does indeed become circular and confusing. This is a perfect opportunity to salvage and reframe it into something much cleaner, more intuitive, and thematically powerful.

You're right, Submission shouldn't just be "Aura itself." Aura is the passive field; Submission is an active, terrifying *weaponization* of that field. The problem isn't the concept, but the old description of its mechanism.

Let's reframe this by establishing a clearer hierarchy. A Kráton has two primary domains where they can apply their will: **Personal** (their own body and attacks) and **Territorial** (the space their Aura occupies).

### The Two Domains of Krátonic Application

#### Domain 1: Personal Application (Coating / *Epichrisis*)

This is the art of taking your Aura and applying it directly to a tangible object—primarily yourself or your attacks. It is the focused, direct application of will.

1.  **Defensive Coating -> Neutralization:**
    *   **Mechanism:** You consciously sheathe your *own body* in a dense layer of your Aura. This layer is programmed with a simple, defensive intent: "Dissolve what is not me." Your willpower intercepts incoming attacks at the point of contact, neutralizing their force and structure.
    *   **Analogy:** This is like wearing a suit of armor made of pure will. It only protects you.

2.  **Offensive Coating -> Penetration (*Diatresis*):**
    *   **Mechanism:** You consciously sheathe your *attacks* (a fist, a projectile, an energy beam) in a layer of your Aura. This layer is programmed with an offensive intent: "Pierce and bypass." Your willpower piggybacks on the attack, disrupting the target's defenses (be they physical, magical, or psionic).
    *   **Analogy:** This is like enchanting your sword. The power is focused on the weapon, not the space around you.

#### Domain 2: Territorial Application (Aura Manipulation)

This is the art of manipulating the entire passive field of your Aura. It is the broad, environmental application of will. This is where Submission finds its true, distinct home.

1.  **Physical Intensification -> Flaring (*Anapsis*):**
    *   **Mechanism:** You pour more energy into your Aura, turning up the "volume" on its *latent physical properties*. Your passive heat becomes a scorching wave; your passive gravitational pull becomes a heavy pressure. You are making the environment itself more hazardous.
    *   **Analogy:** This is like turning up the thermostat in a room to an unbearable temperature. You are changing the passive conditions of the territory.

2.  **Psychological Dominance -> Submission (*Proskynesis*):**
    *   **Mechanism:** This is **not** a defensive technique. It is a pure, active, *offensive* projection of your commanding will throughout your entire Aura. You issue a silent, irresistible command to the very will of every being within your territory: **"Kneel."** Your willpower doesn't dissolve their attacks; it directly attacks and overwhelms *their willpower*, causing the physiological and psychological collapse associated with Submission.
    *   **Analogy:** This is like a king issuing a decree that echoes in every corner of his throne room, a command so absolute that all subjects are compelled to obey. You are weaponizing the very authority of your territory.

### The Salvaged and Refined Definitions

With this new framework, the definitions become clear and distinct:

*   **Aura** is the passive, latent territorial field.
*   **Neutralization** is a **personal, defensive** technique that uses Coating to protect the body.
*   **Submission** is a **territorial, offensive** technique that weaponizes the Aura to dominate the wills of others.

The circular logic is gone. Submission is no longer confusingly derived from a defensive technique. It is now the ultimate expression of a Conqueror's territorial dominance—the active, psychological counterpart to Flaring's passive, physical pressure.

### How This Solves the Problem

This reframing provides a clean, symmetrical, and intuitive structure:

| **Domain** | **Application** | **Defensive/Passive Use** | **Offensive/Active Use** |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Personal** | **Coating (*Epichrisis*)** | **Neutralization** (Protecting Self) | **Penetration** (Enhancing Attacks) |
| **Territorial** | **Aura Manipulation** | **Flaring** (Intensifying Environment) | **Submission** (Dominating Wills) |

Now, the systems are beautifully parallel. In both domains, you have a less aggressive option (Neutralization/Flaring) and a more aggressive one (Penetration/Submission). This structure is far more robust and provides a clearer understanding of a Kráton's tactical options. They can either armor up and fight (Coating) or make the very ground their enemy stands on a weapon (Aura Manipulation).
