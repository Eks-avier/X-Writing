# Mothra Form Names Finalized - The Three Phases of the Divine Moon

It's the same aesthetic as Godric's abilities. Atomic Amplification and so on have specific names for specific abilities or techniques, but the idea of a Kratonically enhanced form is simply... "Amplified," or in Mothra's case, "Form." But which form?

1. Heavenly Form = "Ourania" (Heavenly One). This is Mothra's base state.
2. Lunar Form = "Selene" (Moon). This is Mothra in connection with the Moon, her Heavenly Soul, and Godric.
3. Eclipsed Form = "Eklipsis" (The Eclipse). This is Mothra in conjunction with both the Sun, the Moon, and herself. The Eclipse Regalia is an allusion to this, perhaps a "civilian" version of her Eclipsed Form.

## Analysis
This is the perfect naming convention. By using Ourania, Selene, and Eklipsis, you have given Mothra a personal taxonomy that is:

1. **Ancient & Divine:** Greek names evoke her mythical, almost-sacred nature.
2. **Astronomically Symbolic:** The Moon/Sun/Eclipse imagery directly connects to the Nordson marriage symbolism (Sun & Moon).
3. **Narratively Resonant:** Eklipsis being her ultimate form echoes the "Eclipsed Queen" status of her marriage.

### The Three Phases of the Divine Moon

#### Phase 1: Ourania (The Heavenly One) - Base State

This is Mothra at her resting state. Still immensely powerful, but not actively drawing on the deeper wells of her being.

- **Symbolism:** The Sky itself. Distant, beautiful, omnipresent.
- **Power Level:** Planetary-class psychic, standard mosura biology, the "everyday" goddess.

#### Phase 2: Selene (The Moon) - Lunar State

This is Mothra when she draws upon her connection to the cosmos, her **Heavenly Soul**, and her **bond with Godric**.

- **Symbolism:** The Moon in the sky. Reflective, calming, but also pulling tides and influencing the night.
- **Power Level:** This is where she accesses her deepest Psionic reserves.
- **The Nordson Connection:** Godric's Sun illuminates the Moon. Her connection to him empowers this form.

#### Phase 3: Eklipsis (The Eclipse) - Ultimate State

This is Mothra at the height of her power—a fusion of the **Solar** (Tangible, Godric's influence), the **Lunar** (Intangible, her own Soul), and her own **Divine Core**.

- **Symbolism:** The moment the Sun and Moon align perfectly. A rare, terrifying, and awe-inspiring cosmic event.
- **Power Level:** This is Mothra at her absolute limit.
- **The Regalia Connection:** The Eclipse Regalia she wears as Maria is a "civilian" version—a symbolic reminder of her ultimate form worn as elegant clothing. A hint of the goddess beneath the teacher.
