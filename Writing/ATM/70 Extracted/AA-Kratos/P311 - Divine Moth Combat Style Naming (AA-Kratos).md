## Analysis
This is an excellent question that requires balancing linguistic consistency with thematic resonance. The Titanus mosura are ancient, divine, and deeply connected to the spiritual history of Asia (and beyond).

### Naming Conventions

#### I. The Dual-Language System: Confirmed

- **Ancient Greek:** For species-specific terminology (mosura-only abilities, rituals, biology).
- **Modern English (Scientific):** For general Psionic terminology (Telekinesis, Telepathy, Neural Ignition).

#### II. Maria's Psionic Techniques

**Option A: The Muted Approach (Recommended)**
For her combat techniques, a muted, functional naming scheme that reflects her "This isn't art; this is a chore" philosophy:
- **Telekinetic Crush** (not "Godhand of the Heavens")
- **Mental Command: Sleep**
- **Reflective Barrage**

**Option B: The Divine Titles Approach**
For her rare, ultimate-tier abilities, a more formal Greek/Mythic title to reflect their apocalyptic scale:
- **Lampas** (Torch): Her God-Rays
- **Aithír** (Ether): Her ultimate psionic field

#### III. The Form Transformations

The "Heavenly," "Lunar," and "Eclipsed" forms require names that convey state-of-being rather than action. Since these forms are intrinsic to Maria rather than Psionics generally, Ancient Greek or a more formal style is appropriate.

**Proposed Naming:**
1. **Heavenly Form** = "Ouranía" (Heavenly One)
2. **Lunar Form** = "Seléne" (Moon)
3. **Eclipsed Form** = "Éklipsis" (The Eclipse)

The result: Maria's attacks feel like a goddess performing a task with divine impatience, not a warrior enjoying the art of battle.
