### **Godric Nordson: `Techni` Profile (Post-Xilien Invasion)**

#### **IV. The Primal Arsenal: Innate Gojira Abilities**

These are the foundational, biological, and instinctual weapons of his lineage. While his human form has changed their application, their devastating effectiveness remains. This is the untamed "monster" that lies beneath the disciplined martial artist, a primal force he can unleash at any moment.

*   **The Atomic Breath (`Othisi`): The Ancient Roar**
    *   **Description:** The iconic, devastating beam of pure atomic energy projected from the mouth. In human form, thanks to the stability provided by Atomic Amplification, he can fire it with the same control and power as he could in his Titan form.
    *   **Function:** His primary ranged weapon and a versatile tool. He can fire it as a quick, concussive burst or a sustained, cutting beam.
    *   **Unorthodox Application: *Atomic Propulsion*** A creative, if inefficient, mobility tool. He can use a controlled burst of his breath to launch himself through the air, a chaotic and unpredictable maneuver perfect for surprising aerial opponents.

*   **The Nuclear Pulse: The King's Decree**
    *   **Description:** An omnidirectional, explosive release of atomic energy radiating from his entire body.
    *   **Function:** His ultimate area-denial and "get off me" ability. It is an indiscriminate wave of pure force, designed to obliterate everything in his immediate vicinity. He uses this when surrounded or to counter an overwhelming swarm-type attack.

*   **The Primal Weapons: The Predator's Toolkit**
    These are the instinctual, close-quarters combat techniques that are a part of his very being. They are not learned; they are remembered. He now integrates them as pattern-breaking, unorthodox attacks within his refined martial style.
    *   **The Bite:** His most shocking and primal weapon. Used in a clinch or to disarm an opponent, it is a devastatingly effective and psychologically shattering attack that no opponent ever anticipates from a being of his refined martial prowess.
    *   **The Crocodile Death Roll:** His ultimate grappling finisher, especially in aquatic environments. A brutal, inescapable technique that combines a crushing hold with a disorienting, tearing roll.
    *   **The Phantom Tail Doctrine:** A power generation principle where he channels the immense torque of his lost tail through his human limbs, resulting in a series of "heavy" strikes with impossible weight and momentum. This includes the leg-sweeping **Takedown**, the space-clearing **Backhand**, the armor-breaking **Elbow**, and the ground-finishing **Hammer Fist**.
