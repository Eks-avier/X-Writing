## Analysis
Excellent. Now we dive into the core engine of her power. By defining her purely Kratonic abilities, we get to see the raw, unfiltered will beneath the psionic artistry.

Let's break down Scylla's application of the core Kratonic arts.

### **Scylla's Kratonics: The Soul of the Glacier**

While her Psionic Hydrokinesis provides her with finesse and versatility, her Kratonics are the source of her absolute, undeniable power. It is the will of the Krateira, the unyielding, foundational ice upon which her entire combat doctrine is built.

#### **I. The Aura of the Permafrost**

As you've perfectly described, Scylla's Aura is a fundamentally different experience from that of a standard hydrokinetic.

*   **The Difference:** A hydrokinetic's aura feels like a change in **weather**—it gets humid, misty, or chilly. Scylla's Aura feels like a change in **reality.**
*   **"The Cold Seeps into Your Bones":** This is the key. It's not just a drop in temperature; it is a **metaphysical cold.** It is the direct, chilling touch of her Conqueror's Will. It bypasses physical insulation and chills the very will of those who enter it. It's an aura that doesn't just make you shiver; it makes you despair.
*   **Submission (`Proskynesis`):** Her Sovereign Decree is not a shout, but a silent, creeping frost. Beings within her flared Aura don't just feel pressured to kneel; they feel their very life force being frozen, their limbs growing heavy and sluggish, their thoughts slowing to a crawl. It is the slow, inexorable submission of a creature freezing to death.

#### **II. The Personal Applications: The Armor and Blade of Black Ice**

Her application of Personal Coating reflects her philosophy of brutal, efficient perfection.

*   **Neutralization (The Glacial Armor):** When Scylla activates her Royal Armor, her skin takes on a faint, crystalline sheen, as if coated in a layer of black ice. Incoming attacks don't just bounce off; they seem to **lose all thermal and kinetic energy upon impact,** freezing and shattering into inert fragments. It is an absolute, energy-draining defense.
*   **Penetration (The Diamond Edge):** When she coats her ice constructs or her own limbs with her Sceptered Strike, the ice glows with a faint, internal blue light. The effect is not about brute force. It imbues her attacks with the ability to find and exploit microscopic fractures in any defense, physical or energetic. It doesn't shatter a shield; it **propagates a crack through it at a molecular level,** causing it to disintegrate. It is the very principle of ice wedging.

#### **III. The Ultimate Technique: The Double Injection**

This is a horrifying and brilliant synthesis of her Kratonic and biological abilities. It is a two-stage execution that is unique to her.

*   **Mechanism:** She performs two near-simultaneous Injection attacks, or a single Injection with a dual payload.
    1.  **The Biological Payload (The Venom):** The first part of the Writ is a payload of her species' innate venom. This is not a will-based attack, but a potent biological agent. Its purpose is to **paralyze the target, slow their regenerative abilities, and shatter their mental state with hallucinations.** It is the perfect setup.
    2.  **The Kratonic Payload (The Will):** The second part is the true Sovereign's Writ—a concentrated payload of her Neutralization-infused will. Against a target that is already paralyzed, hallucinating, and unable to heal properly, the ontological decay of the Kratonic Injection is **unstoppable and absolute.**
*   **The Unbeatable Combo:** This makes her Injection arguably one of the most lethal in existence. A normal Injection is a contest of "can you survive this before your regeneration kicks in?" Scylla's Double Injection is a contest of "can you survive this while your body is actively betraying you and your healing factor is offline?" For all but the most supremely resilient beings (like Godric), the answer is a definitive no.

This is a fantastic and complete picture of her Kratonic arsenal. It is powerful, unique, and every single ability is a perfect reflection of her cold, precise, and terrifyingly efficient character.
