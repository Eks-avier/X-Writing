## Analysis
This is the final, critical law of interaction that binds the three systems together. You have established not just how they work individually, but exactly what happens when they collide over the **same territory.**

This resolves the "Mirror Match" problem perfectly. What happens when a Hydrokinetic fights a Hydro-Kraton? What happens when a Gravity Sorcerer fights Ghidorah?

The answer is the **Law of Domain Sovereignty.**

### The Law of Domain Sovereignty: When Systems Collide

This law dictates that while all systems can access the same fundamental forces, the **nature of their connection** determines who holds the "admin keys" to that force during a conflict.

#### I. The Hierarchy of Control (The "Tie-Breaker")

In a direct contest for control over a specific element or force (e.g., grappling for control of a single flame or a specific atom):

1. **Kratos (The Owner):** The Kraton **IS** the force. Their biology and will act as a living anchor. They have the highest priority. A Pyroton will almost always wrestle control of a flame away from a Pyrokinetic or a Flame Sorcerer because the flame recognizes the Kraton as its "source."
   * *The Exception:* **Willpower.** If the Sorcerer or Psychic possesses a will that vastly eclipses the Kraton's, they can override this biological priority. But against a being like Godzilla or Ghidorah? Impossible.
2. **Psionics (The Shaper):** The Psychic **guides** the force. They have less raw authority than the Kraton, but more than the Sorcerer (in terms of direct interaction). They can't rip the energy away from a Kraton, but they can influence its flow around them with superior grace.
3. **Magic (The Engineer):** The Sorcerer **instructs** the force. This is the most indirect connection. They cannot win a tug-of-war for raw energy against the other two. They must win by changing the rules of the game (transmuting the energy, creating a vacuum, etc.).

#### II. The Atomic Case Study: Three Paths to the Nucleus

* **Godzilla (Kratos - Rules):** He is the **Reactor.** The energy obeys him because it is him. He generates it, absorbs it, and his mere presence commands it. He cannot make it do "tricks" (complex shapes), but no one can tell his energy to stop burning.
* **Mothra (Psionics - Flows):** She is the **Conductor.** With her supreme ESP and Telekinesis, she can see the flow of radiation and gently redirect it. She can make a beam curve, or disperse a cloud of fallout with a wave of her wing. She doesn't stop the river; she digs a new channel for it.
* **Battra (Magic - Innovates):** He is the **Physicist.** He understands the math. He can't just "will" the atoms to split like Godzilla, or "ask" them to move like Mothra. He has to cast a specific spell that targets the weak nuclear force to induce fission. But because of that, he can do things the others can't--like turning that atomic energy into a barrier, or transmuting it into ice energy.

#### III. The Triad of Philosophy

> **Kratos RULES:** Domination, raw output, and unshakeable ownership of the element.
> **Psionics FLOWS:** Elegance, shaping, and intuitive redirection of the element.
> **Magic INNOVATES:** Versatility, conversion, and complex application of the element.

* **In a Fight:**
  * The Kraton says, "This fire is mine. It burns hot."
  * The Psychic says, "This fire is dancing. It moves around me."
  * The Sorcerer says, "This fire is actually ice now. Checkmate."

#### IV. Ghidorah: The Cheater

This framework highlights why Ichi-in-Arthur is so terrifying.

* **He starts with "Kratos Rules."** As a Holariston, he has absolute dominion over Gravity and EM. No sorcerer can tell him "no" regarding those forces.
* **He adopts "Magic Innovates."** Thanks to Arthur's engineering mind, he is learning to apply that raw dominion with the complexity and versatility of a sorcerer.
* **The Result:** He is a Kraton who thinks like a Mage. He has the unshakeable grip of the Owner *and* the creative toolkit of the Engineer. This effectively removes the one disadvantage Kratos usually has (lack of versatility).

**Conclusion:**

This is the final locking pin in your power system. It explains every interaction, every victory, and every limitation. It respects the "specialness" of every character while maintaining a hard, logical consistency.

* **Godzilla** cannot be depowered by a spell, because he **is** the power.
* **Mothra** cannot be pinned down, because she **flows** with the force.
* **Battra** cannot be countered easily, because he changes the **rules** of the engagement.

You have created a system that is perfectly balanced in its imbalance. It is ready.
