# Junior War Fans and Eclipse Combos
> **Source:** `Formatted_With_Thoughts - AA - The Kratos of Kings.md` — Prompt 331

## Your Notes
Fun fact! Years later, Junior makes a physical copy of her war fans, which are displayed alongside the Nordson family's ceremonial swords.

Time for the combos. Mothra and Godzilla are incredibly well-rounded fighters. Offense? They have loads of those. Defense? Maria possesses the GREATEST defensive ability for FREE: her Reflective Scales. Godric is the most physically durable Titan, with the greatest regenerative factor, who can adapt limitlessly in the midst of battle. Utility? With Godric's AA, he now has greater utility than in his Titan form. He now possesses Fortification, granting him multiple ways to be airborne and lift objects larger than his human form. Mothra has a vast array of tricks due to the nature of her scales and telekinesis. Even as Maria, her bag has only gotten deeper.

We've already talked about "Scatterbreath," but there's also this new combo they found in the Xilien Invasion. It's using each other as literal weapons.
- Godric would grab Maria's arm or leg and swing her around like a bat. Because she's in a battlefield, she abandons her parrying tendency to be coated in her Reflective Scales instead. By swinging her around, she can literally serve as a bat. Godric can then throw her at an enemy, allowing her to slice them with her fans or slam into them.
- Maria would do the same, grabbing Godric by a limb and then swinging him around. Because he's in AA mode...well, how would one feel if one receives a whole Godric straight to the face while he's in AA mode?

They can also use each other as shields:
- Maria would stand behind Godric. Through his AA, he can utilize the Neutralization property while Maria charges an attack, such as a full-on beam of light (which we will discuss later).
- Godric would stand behind Maria, so she can reflect the incoming attack while Godric fires an Atomic Railgun point-blank at her, which she also scatters.

Most expect one or the other to be a sword or a shield. No. Godric and Maria have been fighting each other's sword AND shield since prehistory; their bond has only evolved due to the new abilities and skills they've gained in their new human lives. Their synergy is so good that they don't even need to communicate in a fight. They know what each other's roles are, and they understand how these roles shift in the midst of battle.

## Analysis
This is the ultimate evolution of their partnership. It moves beyond "coordination" into **total combat symbiosis.** The idea that they are interchangeably the Sword and the Shield is the perfect tactical expression of their relationship dynamic: equal, complementary, and fluid.

The image of them physically throwing one another is both hilarious and terrifyingly effective. It capitalizes on their unique physiology (super-dense Titan bodies) and their unique powers (Scales and AA) to turn their human forms into weapons of mass destruction.

Let's codify these **Combos of the Eclipse.**

### **The Dance of the Eclipse: Synergistic Combat Arts**

To an outsider, their fighting style looks like a chaotic blur. To a master, it is a flawless dance where the lead changes every second. They do not need to speak; the shift in a stance, the flare of an aura, or a telepathic impulse is all the signal they need.

#### **I. The "Living Weapon" Doctrine (Physical Projectiles)**

This relies on the fact that both of them are nigh-indestructible objects with immense mass.

**1. The Golden Guillotine (Godric swings Maria)**
*   **The Setup:** Maria coats herself in her **Reflective Scales**, becoming a diamond-hard, unyielding statue. She extends her War Fans (or just her limbs).
*   **The Execution:** Godric, using **Atomic Amplification** for maximum torque, grabs her by an arm or leg and swings her with the force of a centrifuge.
*   **The Effect:** She becomes a living buzzsaw. Her scales reflect any impact, so she takes no damage from the collision. Her fans (or limbs) slice through armor, bone, and concrete. Godric can release her to turn her into a spinning, slicing projectile, which she can then correct mid-air with Telekinesis for a second strike.

**2. The Atomic Meteor (Maria swings Godric)**
*   **The Setup:** Godric enters **Tier 2 or 3 Atomic Amplification**, turning his body into a super-dense, energy-charged battering ram. He creates a rigid **Internal Fortification.**
*   **The Execution:** Maria, using her **Telekinetic Strength enhancement**, grabs him (perhaps by the back of his jacket or arm) and spins him. Her flight allows for 3D movement, generating momentum from the air.
*   **The Effect:** She hurls him. He doesn't slice; he **obliterates.** He hits with the mass of a mountain and the explosive force of a bomb. Upon impact, he can release a **Nuclear Pulse** or a **Phantom Tail Strike** to maximize the devastation. It is a bunker-busting maneuver.

#### **II. The "Phalanx of Two" Doctrine (Defensive Swapping)**

This highlights their ability to cover each other's specific weaknesses instantly.

**1. The Mountain Stance (Godric Defends)**
*   **Scenario:** They face an overwhelming, chaotic energy attack or a massive physical blow that cannot be easily reflected (or requires absorption).
*   **The Move:** Maria ducks behind Godric. He activates **Maximum Neutralization (AA)** and potentially the **Atomic Mantle.**
*   **The Payoff:** He tanks the hit completely, his body dissolving the energy. Meanwhile, safely in his shadow, Maria charges her ultimate ranged attack (the **God Ray** / Light Beam) undisturbed. As the smoke clears, Godric steps aside, and Maria fires.

**2. The Mirror Stance (Maria Defends)**
*   **Scenario:** They face a precision beam, a magical curse, or an attack they want to turn against the enemy.
*   **The Move:** Godric steps behind Maria. She deploys a wall of **Reflective Scales** and **Fairies.**
*   **The Payoff:** She catches the enemy attack and holds it in the refractive cloud. Simultaneously, Godric fires his **Atomic Breath** or **Railgun** *into* her cloud. She mixes his beam with the enemy's attack, amplifies both, and redirects the combined, chaotic stream back at the foe. (This is the **Scatterbreath** evolved).

#### **III. The Silent Flow**

The most intimidating aspect of this is the **silence.**
*   They don't shout attack names.
*   They don't say "Switch!"
*   One moment Godric is the shield, the next he is the sword. One moment Maria is the sniper, the next she is the projectile.
*   It creates a rhythm of combat that is impossible to predict because it adapts instantly to the threat. It is the physical manifestation of 250 million years of trust.

### **The Legacy: Junior's Tribute**

The detail about Junior forging a copy of her War Fans is beautiful.

*   **Completion of the Collection:** It means the Family Heirloom collection isn't just about the "men's" weapons (Swords, Hammers, Axes). It includes the **Queen's Arsenal.**
*   **A Son's Respect:** Even though Junior favors swords, making the fans is his way of honoring his mother's warrior spirit. It acknowledges that she is just as lethal as his father.
*   **The Display:** Displaying them alongside the Greatsword and Nodachi visually cements the equality of the Nordson-Lepidiel union. The Sun's blade and the Moon's fan, side by side.

This is a perfect combat doctrine for the couple. It is romantic, brutal, tactical, and deeply emblematic of their "Two Halves of a Whole" theme.
