### **Psionics: The Complete System Profile (Current State)**

#### **I. Core Philosophy: The Axiology of Power**

Psionics is the power system of **direct mental and emotional expression.** It operates on the principle that the mind, through its interaction with the **Psi Force** (the sixth fundamental force), can directly influence the physical and metaphysical world. It is a system of intuition, empathy, and connection, where the clarity of one's feelings and the precision of one's thoughts are the ultimate measures of power.

*   **The Universal Principle:** *"The universe is a canvas, and a thought is the brush."*
*   **The Axiological System:** Among the three universal power systems, Psionics represents the **Axiological** approach—it is concerned with **Values and Feelings.**
    *   **Psionics (Axiology):** "What is valued and felt."
    *   **Magic (Epistemology):** "What is known."
    *   **Kratos (Ontology):** "What exists through will."
*   **The Artist vs. The Engineer:** Psionics is often viewed as the "art" to Magic's "science." Its mastery comes from emotional clarity and intuitive grace, not from technical memorization or structured process. The core conflict within the system is the struggle to reclaim this artistic nature from those who would treat it as a cold, exploitable science (the Shadow Organization).

#### **II. Foundational Principles & Classifications**

*   **The Non/Unnon System:** The binary classification for psionic capability.
    *   **Unnon:** Any species or individual with the biological capacity for Telepathy or Psychokinesis.
    *   **Non:** A being with no psionic capability whatsoever (e.g., *Titanus gojira*).
*   **True Psychic:** A crucial distinction. A True Psychic is an Unnon who can **actively and independently** influence the world with their mind. This requires either **Active Telepathy** or **Telekinesis**. Passive telepaths do not qualify. All of Keep Charlie's Psionic Division are True Psychics.

#### **III. The Psionic Dyad: The Two Pillars of Power**

All psionic abilities are derived from one or both of these two fundamental faculties.

1.  **Telepathy:** The ability to interact with minds.
2.  **Psychokinesis (PK):** The ability to influence matter and energy with the mind.

#### **IV. A Deep Dive into Telepathy**

##### **A. The Core Ethics: A Consent-Based System**
The entire practice of ethical telepathy, as championed by Mothra, is built on consent.
*   **Surface Thoughts:** Involuntary, general emotional/mental impressions "brushed" from other minds. Similar to reading body language. Generally considered ethically acceptable as it is passive.
*   **Deep Reading:** A voluntary, active penetration of another's mind. Requires **explicit permission** and is a profound violation if done without it.

##### **B. The Realm of Consciousness**
A non-physical realm generated by the brain's interaction with the Psi Force, where a being's consciousness resides. It acts as the "software interface" for the brain.
*   **The Mechanism of Mental Manipulation:** Active Telepaths can perform complex mental feats (illusions, memory alteration) by accessing and manipulating a target's Realm of Consciousness, bypassing the need for direct physical alteration of the brain.

##### **C. The Five Core Disciplines of Telepathy**
1.  **Mental Communication:** The exchange of thought and emotion (T2T/T2N linking, reading).
2.  **Mental Fortification:** The art of mental defense (shielding, obfuscation).
3.  **Mental Incursion:** The ethically fraught offensive branch (persuasion, illusion, memory manipulation).
4.  **Mental Projection:** Extending one's consciousness remotely (remote viewing, dreamwalking).
5.  **Extrasensory Perception (ESP):** Sensing the environment beyond normal means (empathy, psychometry).

#### **V. A Deep Dive into Psychokinesis (PK)**

*   **Psychokinesis:** The umbrella term for all mind-over-matter abilities.
*   **Discipline 1: Telekinesis (TK):** The **unconstrained, versatile** application of PK. The ability to move, shape, or affect any physical object or energy field. This is the "pure" form wielded by masters like Mothra.
*   **Discipline 2: Constrained Kinesis:** The application of PK that is **biologically limited** to a specific element or phenomenon (e.g., Pyrokinesis, Hydrokinesis, Aerokinesis). The user's biology acts as a "filter" for their power. This is the domain of Titans like Rodan and the hydrokinetic Wardens.

#### **VI. Planetary Infrastructure: The Telepathic Web**

*   **Function:** A planetary-scale telepathic communication network for Titan governance, created and primarily maintained by Mothra. It replaced the crude, vocal Alpha Calls of the past.
*   **Mechanics:** Passively powered by the psionic emissions of all telepathic beings on Earth. It is organized into **hierarchical channels** (Alpha, Beta, Warden, Common) that reflect the Titan power structure.
*   **Role in Human Development:** The constant, subtle emanations from the Web act as a catalyst, awakening latent psionic potential in the human population.

#### **VII. Cross-System Interactions & Balancing**

| Matchup | Result | Rationale |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Psionics vs. Kratos** | **Strong Advantage** | Psionics targets the will, the very fuel of Kratos, and can often bypass its purely physical defenses. |
| **Psionics vs. Magic** | **Neutral / Skill-Based** | A duel of intuition vs. knowledge. A psychic can see through a magical illusion; a sorcerer can create a ward to block telepathy. Victory depends on the practitioner's skill and strategy. |
| **Psionics vs. Biological Sovereignty** | **Advantage** | Psionics can often bypass, manipulate, or overcome purely physical defenses and abilities. |

#### **VIII. Conclusion: The Power of the Artist**

Psionics is the system of the artist, the empath, and the philosopher. It is defined by its elegance, its intuitive grace, and its profound connection to the inner world of emotion and thought. Where Kratos is about what you **are** and Magic is about what you **know**, Psionics is about what you **feel.** Its power is not found in a textbook or a bloodline, but in the clarity of one's heart and the focus of one's mind.
