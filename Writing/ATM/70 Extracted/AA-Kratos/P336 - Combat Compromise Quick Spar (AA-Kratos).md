## Analysis
This is a riotously funny and surprisingly sweet subversion of their dynamic. You've successfully flipped the script: the "primal beast" Godric is the one seeking emotional intimacy through shared activity and cuddling, while the "divine goddess" Maria is the one whose mind immediately jumps to sex because that's become her standard deflection tactic.

It highlights a tragicomic disconnect in their love languages:
*   **Godric's Language:** Combat *is* intimacy. Cuddling is the aftercare for combat.
*   **Maria's Language:** Sex is intimacy. Combat is a chore.

The fact that Godric is wearing his **Regalia**—the armor made of her silk and his scales—makes it even more poignant. He dressed up for a formal date!

Let's flesh out this scene of domestic misunderstanding.

### **Scene: The Date That Wasn't**

**(Setting: A secluded, reinforced clearing on the far side of Monster Island. Godric stands waiting, looking magnificent in his full Titan Regalia—a high-collared, sleeveless coat of woven white silk and obsidian scales that shimmers with a faint blue light. He looks like a warrior-king ready for a duel of honor.)**

Maria landed softly, the grass barely bending beneath her feet. She was wearing a simple, elegant sundress, expecting a picnic. She stopped dead when she saw him.

"Why are you fully dressed in your Regalia, Gric?"

Godric frowned, looking her up and down. "Why *aren't* you wearing yours, Mari? Are you sure you want to get that dress ruined? We're going to be moving fast."

Maria froze. Her eyes widened. "Oh. Oh, no... Godric..."

"Maria..." His shoulders slumped, the regal posture deflating instantly into a petulant slouch. "I told you. I just wanted a *real* fight. Just one. I set the perimeter, I reinforced the ground..."

"No," she said, crossing her arms, though her cheeks were heating up. "I said—"

"Don't try that with me," he interrupted, pointing a finger at her. "Did *you* forget that I *can't* forget? You literally whimpered your 'yes' to me while I was thrusting inside of you three nights ago! You agreed to the terms!"

Maria glowed a bright, mortified pink, her bioluminescence betraying her completely. "Don't just say that out loud, you idiot! Someone could hear!"

Godric sighed, a sound of profound disappointment, and dropped onto his back on the grass unceremoniously. The terrifying King of Monsters looked like a kid who had been told Christmas was cancelled.

"I even got Dad to watch over the kids for the day..." he grumbled at the sky. "And I told Alex not to bother us with duty. Do you know I had to make several pinky promises to Junior just to leave the Palace? Your son is absurdly clingy."

Guilt, sharp and sudden, pierced Maria's chest. She walked over and knelt beside him. "I'm... I'm sorry, my love... I didn't mean to go back on my word. I just thought you'd... well, that you were using the fight as an excuse to..."

Godric turned his head, his golden eyes soft and tired. He reached up to caress her face. "No, no. I get it. You hate it. Let's just... be done with this. We can just lay on the hammock after."

Maria blinked. "A hammock?" The blush returned, darker this time. "Where? You prepared a... bed? Outside?! Godric, you can't possibly mean to have sex out here in the open—"

Godric rolled his eyes so hard it looked painful. "Is sex *really* the only thing on your mind right now?!"

He gestured vaguely to a massive, reinforced hammock strung between two ancient trees nearby. "I planned it so that after we do the REAL FIGHT, we could just lie there and cuddle while we cooled down. Goodness, woman... first you think me a brute, and now sex-starved?! Didn't I just fuck your brains out last night?!"

"Gric!" She slapped his arm, scandalous delight warring with embarrassment. "Stop being a baby. You have three children. And I already said I'm sorry!"

He huffed, turning his gaze back to the clouds. The "date" was ruined. The fight wasn't happening. The misunderstanding had sucked the wind out of his sails.

"Whatever," he muttered. "Just sock me in the jaw and let's go back. I'll cook dinner for everyone later."

Maria looked at him—this powerful, ancient, ridiculous man who just wanted to wrestle and hold her. Her heart melted. She lay down on the grass beside him, resting her head on his armored chest.

"No hammock?" she asked softly.

"No," he grumbled, wrapping an arm around her anyway. "Mood's gone."

**The Aftermath:**

They stayed there for an hour, just lying in the grass. Maria eventually did sock him in the jaw—playfully—and he caught her hand and kissed the knuckles. He cooked dinner that night (whale stew), and if he was a little quieter than usual, only Dagon noticed.

And Maria, feeling the weight of his disappointed affection, promised herself she would give him his fight. Maybe next anniversary. Maybe.
