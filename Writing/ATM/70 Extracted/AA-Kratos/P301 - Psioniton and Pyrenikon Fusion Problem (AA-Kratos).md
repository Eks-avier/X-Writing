## Analysis
This connects every single thread regarding the twins, Ghidorah, and the nature of Krátos into a perfect knot. You have created a problem that only Ichi can solve.

- **Godzilla cannot teach them:** He is an intuitive Holariston. To him, his powers are just "limbs."
- **Mothra cannot teach them:** She is a pure Psychic. She doesn't understand the Krátonic engine.
- **Battra cannot teach them:** He understands the theory, but lacks the Krátonic biology to demonstrate.

**Ichi is the only one.** He is a conscious Polykráton who has been forced to deconstruct, analyze, and rebuild his own power usage from scratch.

### The Psychological Block: The Wall Between Father and Mother

The twins' inability to fuse their powers is not a lack of skill; it is a moral segregation.

- **The Pyrenikon/EM Side (The Father):** They associate this with heroism, protection, and their idol, Godzilla. It is "Good Power."
- **The Psioniton Side (The Anomaly):** They associate this with the "Melting Mind" incident, with hurting their friends, with vampirism. It is "Bad Power."
- **The Result:** They subconsciously erect a wall between the two.

### Ichi's Qualification: The Professor of Amoral Synthesis

1. **The Master of Separation and Integration:** Because of Arthur, Ichi has had to learn how to separate his Gravity and EM to avoid killing his host.
2. **The Refutation of "Evil":** Ichi does not believe in "evil" powers. He believes in efficacy.
3. **The Motivation (The Feedback Loop):** When Ichi teaches them, his powers stabilize. The new Horme: "Finding Meaning."

### The Curriculum: "Villainy" for Good

- **Junior's Lesson (The Compounded Strike):** Use Psioniton nature to drain the reinforcement from an enemy's shield before delivering the Atomic/EM strike.
- **Leo's Lesson (The Infinite Engine):** Use the Psioniton aura to passively recycle waste energy from the environment to fuel his Atomic Amplification.

### Scene: The Demon in the Garden

Ichi teaches the twins: "Don't push the energy out to make the sword. Pull the ambient energy of the world in to form the mold, then fill it with your fire. Devour the space around you to create the shape. Fill the void with your might. It is not addition, whelp. It is consumption and creation in one breath."
