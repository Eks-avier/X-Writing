# Godric Darius Human World Field Trips
> **Source:** `Formatted_With_Thoughts - The Saga of the _Titanus gojira_.md` — Prompt 266

## Your Notes
Godric and Darius must have had a lot of awkward social moments whenever they both descend from the Palace at Monster Island and visit the human world.

One of the activities Godric and Darius like is going to cities and states of every nation, if they have any. Darius got to visit Meadowvale this way, and even Andrew Ford's home state as well!

These trips serve two things, acquainting Godric's father to the human world through "field trips," which is how Madison showed Godric the human world, and father-son bonding. Before, Godric had to endure Madison's... mischievousness... now, he doesn't just have a fellow Titan with which he can comment the human world, but it's a fellow `gojira` as well. On top of that, he doesn't just have a fellow `gojira`, it's his father!

Now, this ritual continued over to Junior and Leo's birth, then to Lora's. So, it's normal for the two men to bring the kids along whenever possible. After all, the kids are also receiving their fair share of exposure to the human world. Although, Darius always jokes he actually has *4* kids with him, though nobody ever believes him because of his apparent youth. Instead being Godric's Dad takes... on a new tone.

Darius is confused, but amused. Internally, he wishes Aurelia was here to experience this all. Perhaps it wouldn't be so awkward if she were!

While Godric appears stoic and the "straight man" to Darius' mischief, you have to remember that Godric inherited his father's mischief! It's just that, he's a lot more private in his affectations. He never fails to recount this all to Maria when they return to the Palace.

Before, they can go in anonymously. They may be two, well-built, intimidating men, but at least the others could just chalk them up to being some sort of athlete. Yeah, they get weird rumors of being a couple, but that's few and far in between. *Not when they have toddlers with them, though*.

These shenanigans and rumors are how Godric and Darius are so comfortable with the rumors surrounding Junior in the future when the three children are young adults.

>Darius: I wouldn't worry 'bout it [the rumors]. The humans thought *your father* was this... What's that? A ***sugar baby*** of mine. Wild, huh?
>Junior: Papa, ***shut up***. Please.

## Analysis
This is absolute comedic gold, and it adds such a rich, lived-in texture to their relationship. It perfectly illustrates the "Man out of Time" aspect for Dagon and the "King trying to be a Man" aspect for Godric.

The idea that the world's most terrifying apex predator—a being who can tank nukes and level cities—was rumored to be a **Sugar Baby** to a boisterous man with a mustache is the kind of absurdity that fits perfectly with the "Whimsy" layer of your universe.

### **The Sociology of the "Nordson Couple"**

Why did the humans assume this? It comes down to their public energy.

1.  **Darius (The Provider Energy):** Darius is the one ordering the food, laughing with the waitress, carrying the heavy bags, and generally taking charge of the social environment. He radiates "Big Dad Energy" or, to the uninitiated, "Rich Boyfriend Energy."
2.  **Godric (The Silent Prize):** Godric stands there, looking impossibly handsome, dressed in bespoke silk suits (made by Mothra), saying very little, and letting Darius handle the logistics. To a casual observer, he looks like the "high-maintenance, beautiful partner" who is being pampered.
3.  **The "4 Kids" Joke:** When Darius points to Godric and says, "I've got four kids with me," humans don't see a father and son. They see an older-acting man making a cheeky joke about his younger-acting partner being "high maintenance."

### **The Generational Transmission of Mischief**

*   **Darius (The Showa Goofball):** He is openly playful, loud, and takes joy in confusing humans.
*   **Godric (The Private Mischief):** He appears stoic, but internally he is laughing. He recounts every ridiculous incident to Maria when they get home. He is secretly just as entertained as his father.
*   **Junior (The Mortified Heir):** Junior is the one who has to deal with the *consequences* of his grandfather's "jokes" when he's an adult. The "sugar baby" comment is designed to get a rise out of him.
