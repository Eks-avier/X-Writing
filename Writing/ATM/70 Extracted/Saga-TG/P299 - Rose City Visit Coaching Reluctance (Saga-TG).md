# Rose City Visit Coaching Reluctance
> **Source:** `Formatted_With_Thoughts - The Saga of the _Titanus gojira_.md` — Prompt 299

## Your Notes

This is a continuation of the city visit dynamics, expanding on Rose's role as the "Human Barometer" and translator of normality for the Nordson family.

## Analysis

This further establishes Rose as the crucial bridge between the Nordsons and the human world they've left behind.

### **Rose: The Human Anchor**

Rose serves as the perfect audience surrogate in these scenarios.

*   **The Psychosomatic Response:** Even though her hybrid biology can handle almost anything, her *mind* remembers being Erika. She remembers what it was like to be normal, to worry about what strangers think, to have social anxiety.

*   **The "Alien" Reminder:** She is the constant reminder that, despite their appearance, these people are *not* human. Watching her family treat human social conventions as quaint puzzles is one of the starkest reminders of the Antitheriomorphosis.

### **The Coaching Sessions: Rose vs. Titans**

Before every city visit, Rose attempts to prepare her family for human interaction.

**The Darius Problem:**
*   *Rose:* "Please do not arm-wrestle strangers."
*   *Darius:* "But what if they challenge me first?"
*   *Rose:* "They won't."
*   *Darius:* "But what if they DO?"
*   *Rose:* "You'll break their arm."
*   *Darius:* "...Intentionally?"

**The Aurelia Problem:**
*   *Rose:* "Maybe wear something... less Victorian?"
*   *Aurelia:* (looks down at her black lace mourning gown) "What is wrong with my attire?"
*   *Rose:* "It's the 21st century, not 1890."
*   *Aurelia:* "Fashion is eternal, dear."
*   *Rose:* (sighs) "At least leave the parasol."
*   *Aurelia:* "Absolutely not."

**The Godric Problem:**
*   *Rose:* "Can you please smile? Just once?"
*   *Godric:* (attempts a smile)
*   *Rose:* "...Never mind. Don't do that."
*   *Godric:* "I told you it was inefficient."

**The Leo Problem:**
*   *Rose:* "Leo, you cannot—"
*   *Leo:* (already running toward something)
*   *Rose:* "—touch that. Okay. Great."

### **Why Rose Endures**

Despite her mortification, Rose keeps going on these trips because of what she sees in Aurelia's eyes.

*   **The Invitation:** When Aurelia says, "You must bloom for all of the world to see," it isn't just a command. It's an invitation. It's Aurelia saying, "You are beautiful, you are family, and I want to show you off."

*   **The Belonging:** Rose spent her early existence as Biollante—monstrous, isolated, confused. Being dragged into a human city by a woman in a Victorian gown, surrounded by immortal beings who call her "Little Flower," is the most normal she has ever felt.

*   **The Love:** She endures the embarrassment because, for the first time in her existence, she has parents who want to take her places. Parents who argue about who gets to hold her hand crossing the street.

### **The Ultimate Scene**

The chaos of a city trip always ends the same way:

**(Setting: The end of the day. They're back on Monster Island. Rose collapses on a couch.)**

**Rose:** "That was a disaster."

**Darius:** (laughing) "I thought it went well!"

**Aurelia:** (calmly pouring tea) "The restaurant will recover."

**Rose:** "The waiter was crying."

**Godric:** "He was emotional."

**Maria:** "I thought he handled the family revelation quite gracefully."

**Leo:** (holding a souvenir he definitely wasn't supposed to touch) "Can we go again tomorrow?"

**Rose:** (staring at the ceiling) "Give me at least a month."

**Aurelia:** (placing a cup of tea in Rose's hands) "Two weeks."

**Rose:** "...Three."

**Aurelia:** (smiling) "Done."

And Rose, despite everything, smiles back. Because this is her family. And she wouldn't trade them for anything—not even a normal trip to the grocery store.
