**Astraea Outtakes (Rejected)**

1. Originally, I wanted her to be unrelated to the Northern Faction. I wanted her to be a rare case of a `gojira` from another faction surviving and journeying to another faction. The case was that she was stranded during the process of moving out of the failing Faction she was from. She was an immigrant.
2. Following that, I wanted her to be an Eastern `gojira`. You see, the Eastern `gojira` are thinner, smaller, and lighter compared to their hardy Northern brethren. However, she was also weaker. She was not as durable as the Northern `gojira`. Because I felt it was too convenient to tie her into the founding of the Four Factions by having her be related to the Eastern faction, I scrapped this as well.
3. I also wanted her to specifically be a Southerner first, actually. The Southerners looked closer to Dagon than an Easterner would, but their personalities would be starkly different.
4. I wanted her heart condition to be related to her Faction, so that their bloodline would be already special to begin with. It'd be a Chosen One situation, which doesn't fit the Nordsons.

## Analysis
These outtakes are fascinating because they reveal the **design philosophy** behind the Nordson family: they must be **ordinary people in extraordinary circumstances.** The rejection of the "Chosen One" narrative is crucial.

Your final design for Astraea serves the narrative far better because she is a **Northerner born with a debilitating local condition**, not a prophesied savior from an exotic land.

### **Astraea: A Portrait of the "Caged Star"**

Based on what we've established, here is the emerging profile:

**I. Physical Description (The "Tight" Northern Female)**

*   **Species Distinction:** She is a Northern *Titanus gojira*, but female. The Northern females, while still resilient, are generally leaner than the males, built for endurance and agility rather than raw mass.
*   **The Condition's Impact:** Her heart condition may have contributed to a slightly underdeveloped musculature. She didn't have the luxury of the heavy power-training the males undertook; her body couldn't handle the caloric expenditure.
*   **Human Stats (If Hypothetical):**
    *   **Height:** 5'3" (Short, but with a compact, intense presence).
    *   **Weight:** 60 kg (Lean, almost wiry muscle. Every gram is functional).
    *   **Build:** Tight, athletic, like a dancer or a marathon runner. No "bulk" at all.
    *   **Eyes:** The defining feature. Brilliant, piercing gold.
    *   **Coloring:** Dark, obsidian-hued scales. Darker than Dagon's blue-black.

**II. Personality (The "Hope" Engine)**

*   **The Contrast with Dagon:** Where Dagon is loud and boisterous (Showa), Astraea would have been sharp and fierce. She doesn't have the energy for boisterous play; her fire is internal.
*   **The Will to Hope:** Her defining trait is stubborn, almost irrational optimism. She was told she would die young. She was told she couldn't keep up. She refused to accept those "truths."
*   **The "Control" Paradox:** Because her body was a constant threat to her existence, she likely developed an extreme need for control over her environment. She was meticulous, planned ahead, and despised surprises.
*   **With Dagon:** He was the chaos to her order. He was the only person who could make her *laugh* about her condition, who treated her not as a fragile thing to be protected, but as a partner to be respected.
