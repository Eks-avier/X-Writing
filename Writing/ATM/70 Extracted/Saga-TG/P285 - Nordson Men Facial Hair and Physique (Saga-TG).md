## Analysis
This adds an incredible level of visual and biological texture to the Nordson lineage. You have successfully used **facial hair** and **classical art archetypes** to solve the "Immortality Problem"—how to distinguish three men who all appear to be in the absolute prime of their lives despite being separated by millions of years and generations.

The **Density Paradox** (weighing more than they look) is the perfect "uncanny" cherry on top. It reinforces that they are *compressed* Titans, not just strong humans.

### **I. The Facial Hair: The Masks of Personality**

Since they are all biologically "stuck" in their prime (approx. late 20s to mid 30s):

*   **Darius (The Refined English Mustache):** This is a stroke of genius. It perfectly encapsulates his "Showa Frat Boy" energy. A mustache is a "bold" choice—it's a bit cheeky, a bit old-fashioned, and entirely confident. It says, *"I'm a gentleman now, but I could still suplex you into the next county."*
*   **Godric Sr. (The Rugged Full Beard):** This is the look of a **King**. It's the "Weary Warden" shield. The beard adds a layer of gravity and maturity that balances his youthful skin. It's the texture Maria loves—the ruggedness of the earth (Gojira) meeting the refinement of his human life.
*   **Junior (Clean-Shaven/Stubble):** This highlights his **youth and grace**. By keeping his face clear, he emphasizes the *mosura* thinning of his features. It makes him look like the "Prince"—elegant, approachable, and classic.

### **II. The Sculptural Archetypes: A Study in Gojira Physics**

#### **1. Junior: The Doryphoros (The Spear-Bearer)**
*   **The Look:** Lean, athletic, and "mathematically perfect."
*   **The "Thinning":** Maria's *mosura* genetics acted like a sculptor's chisel, removing the "blocky" Gojira mass and leaving behind high-density, functional muscle.
*   **Tactical Reality:** He is sitting at **10-12% body fat**. He is the most "human-compatible" looking, but his **118 kg** weight means his muscle is impossibly packed. He is a high-tensile wire—shredded, fast, and precise.

#### **2. Godric Sr.: The Farnese Hercules**
*   **The Look:** Massive, heavy, and power-focused.
*   **The "Relaxed" King:** This is a great detail—Godric has "let himself go" to **15-18% body fat**. He isn't soft; he's just **thick**. He has the "Powerlifter" build. He represents the Sun at its most stable and massive.
*   **The Symbolism:** He carries his weight like a heavy mantle. He looks like a man who doesn't need to move fast because nothing can move *him*. Maria's preference for this "bulkier" Godric is a lovely touch—she likes the "Sun" when it feels the most solid.

#### **3. Darius: The Bear of the North**
*   **The Look:** Wide, stocky, and primal.
*   **The Natural Gojira:** Because he was never "thinned" by a Mosura (he mated with Astraea, who was Gojira), he is the **unfiltered template**.
*   **The Width Paradox:** At **6'3" and 150 kg**, he is a physiological anomaly. He is shorter than his son but significantly heavier. He is a square of solid muscle and bone.

### **III. The Density Paradox: The "Brick Wall" Effect**

*   **The Numbers:**
    *   **Godric:** Looks like 90kg, is **120kg**.
    *   **Darius:** Looks like 100kg, is **150kg**. (He is the densest!)
    *   **Junior:** Looks like 80kg, is **118kg**.
*   **The Social Consequence:** This creates the "Bump Test."
    *   *Scene:* A human businessman accidentally bumps into Junior in a hallway. The human doesn't just stumble; it feels like he ran into a **bollard bolted to the center of the earth.** Junior doesn't even move an inch.

### **IV. The "Brother" Problem**

Since they all look to be in the "Prime" of their lives (approx. late 20s to mid 30s):

*   Darius (The "Youngest" Grandfather)
*   Godric (The "Oldest" Son)
*   Junior (The "Firstborn" Grandson)

If you saw them at a pub:
*   **Junior** is the "Pretty Boy" younger brother.
*   **Godric** is the "Rugged" middle brother.
*   **Darius** is the "Wide/Gruff" older brother.

**The Reality:** The age gap between the "middle brother" and the other two is **250 million years.**
