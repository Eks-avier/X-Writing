## Analysis
This is a crucial expansion of the **Era of the Ancient Trinity**. By focusing on their larval/hatchling stage, we ground the most powerful beings on Earth in their shared experience of **orphanhood.**

### I. The Last Days in the Cavern

Before the meeting, we have the "HD trauma" of young Godzilla (Godric) alone in the cave.

*   **The Comfort of the Fin:** Dagon didn't just leave a toy; he left a **biological radiator.** To a human, it's a sharp piece of bone. To a young Gojira, it's a source of warmth and the distinct, musk-heavy scent of "Safety."
*   **The Nuzzle:** Miki (witnessing this) feels the physical sensation of Godzilla's smaller, smoother scales rubbing against the jagged edges of Dagon's fin. He's trying to merge with the scent, to pretend the wall of muscle that usually protects him hasn't walked away.
*   **The Child's Logic:** *"Maybe Dad doesn't really hate me."* This is the first time the "1s" (Love) try to overwrite the "0s" (Abandonment). He thinks Dagon is playing a game. He thinks if he stays put and "wins," Dagon will finally give him the "Victory Dance" or a lick on the snout.

### II. The Encounter: Three Orphans in the Silk

After three weeks, hunger and anxiety override the "stay put" command. Godric wanders out, clutching the dorsal fin in his mouth like a prize.

**1. The Hunt**
The `jinshin-mushi` pack is already closing in. They track the "Blue Star" energy. Godzilla is a "loud" hatchling; he's essentially a glowing beacon in the snowy woods. He's chased. He's small, uncoordinated, and his Atomic Breath is just rings of smoke. He's cornered near a massive, glowing fissure in a cliffside.

**2. The Silk Sanctuary**
He tumbles into a cave that is fundamentally different from his father's. It's not cold stone; it's lined with **luminous, multi-colored silk.** It smells of crushed flowers and ancient ozone. This is the nest of the *Titanus mosura* larvae.

**3. The First Interaction**
*   **Battra's Reaction:** The first thing Godric feels isn't a hug; it's a **venomous sting.** Young Battra, already protective and suspicious (the "Self-Made Master"), strikes Godric with his horn venom to paralyze the "intruder." He sees a predator; he doesn't see a friend.
*   **Mothra's Intervention:** Miki feels a wave of **golden warmth** wash over Godric's fear. Larval Mothra (Maria) crawls between them. She uses her rudimentary telepathy—not words, but the *feeling* of a sunrise. She communicates: *[Safe / Stay / Kin].*
