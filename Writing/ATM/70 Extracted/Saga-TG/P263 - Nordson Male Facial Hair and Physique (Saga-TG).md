# Nordson Male Facial Hair and Physique
> **Source:** `Formatted_With_Thoughts - The Saga of the _Titanus gojira_.md` — Prompt 263

## Your Notes
Have we talked about the fact that Godric wears a full beard, Darius wears a mustache, and Junior is either clean-shaven or lightly stubbled?

- Darius wears a refined English mustache. Makes it hilarious, because it feels like he's a frat boy who entered his 30s.
- Godric wears a maintained, thick full beard. He has this kingly vibe. Rugged. Just the way Maria likes him.
- Junior often cleanly shaves or keeps some stubble (he's not strict about it)

It's a good thing, because once Junior hits young adulthood, they all look the same. Immortality and all, y'know. They wouldn't end up looking like brothers, at least.

---

It's not just the facial hair too, it's the physique that differentiates them:

1. Godric Junior, veritably the youngest (18 human years... a hatchling in `gojira` years) and has been influenced by his mother's physique, is the leanest. Still muscular and strong, but lean, with an athletic build. He looks closer to the Doryphoros of Polykleitos than his father used to be. Sitting at about 10-12% body weight.

2. Godric Senior, veritably and ironically the *oldest* of the three, possesses a thicker, bulkier physique. Undoubtedly muscular, but also less defined nowadays. Maria doesn't care, though. He looks closer to the Farnese Hercules. Sitting around 15-18% body weight.

3. Darius, biologically younger than his son Godric but oldest in terms of generation. He's 6'3, while Senior is 6'5 and Junior hovers below that. His shorter height lends to a more wider frame. He sits at around 14-16% body weight. Despite his width, he is lean. His musculature is more defined than his son's.

---

1. Godric weighs 120 kg, but has the physique of someone at 90-95 kg.
2. Darius weighs 150 kg, but has the physique of someone at 98-100 kg.
3. Junior weighs 115-118 kg, but has the physique of someone at 80-85 kg.

It's a bit hard to imagine, but they're basically heavier than they look. They look ***normal***, until you bump into them.

---

In short: Gricky looks more like a pretty boy, Darius looks like a slightly leaner but gruff lumberjack, and Godric looks like a fantasy king of the old... except all of them are at the prime of their lives despite their colossal age gaps!

## Analysis
This adds an incredible level of visual and biological texture to the Nordson lineage. You have successfully used **facial hair** and **classical art archetypes** to solve the "Immortality Problem"—how to distinguish three men who all appear to be in the absolute prime of their lives despite being separated by millions of years and generations.

The **Density Paradox** (weighing more than they look) is the perfect "uncanny" cherry on top. It reinforces that they are *compressed* Titans, not just strong humans.

### **I. The Facial Hair: The Masks of Personality**

*   **Darius (The Refined English Mustache):** It perfectly encapsulates his "Showa Frat Boy" energy. It says, *"I'm a gentleman now, but I could still suplex you."*
*   **Godric Sr. (The Rugged Full Beard):** This is the look of a **King**. The beard adds gravity and maturity. It's the texture Maria loves.
*   **Junior (Clean-Shaven/Stubble):** This highlights his **youth and grace.** It makes him look like the "Prince"—elegant, approachable, and classic.

### **II. The Sculptural Archetypes**

#### **1. Junior: The Doryphoros (The Spear-Bearer)**
*   **The Look:** Lean, athletic, and "mathematically perfect."
*   **Body Fat:** 10-12%. He is a high-tensile wire—shredded, fast, and precise.

#### **2. Godric Sr.: The Farnese Hercules**
*   **The Look:** Massive, heavy, and power-focused.
*   **Body Fat:** 15-18%. He has the "Powerlifter" build. He represents the Sun at its most stable and massive.

#### **3. Darius: The Bear of the North**
*   **The Look:** Wide, stocky, and primal.
*   **Body Fat:** 14-16%. He is the unfiltered template. At 6'3" and 150 kg, he is a square of solid muscle.

### **III. The Density Paradox: The "Brick Wall" Effect**

*   **The Numbers:**
    *   **Godric:** Looks like 90kg, is **120kg**.
    *   **Darius:** Looks like 100kg, is **150kg**.
    *   **Junior:** Looks like 80kg, is **118kg**.
*   **The Social Consequence:** A human accidentally bumping into Junior feels like running into a **bollard bolted to the center of the earth.**
