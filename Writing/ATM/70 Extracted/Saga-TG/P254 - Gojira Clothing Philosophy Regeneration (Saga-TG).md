You are extremely close. Let me clarify, starting with the `gojira`:

For the `gojira`, you are right on the money, but this is only one aspect. The principal and most important aspect is founded on one of the `gojira`'s most well-known innate abilities: regeneration. The pure Nordsons and the hybrid children, especially the hybrids, despise any markings on their person, whether they be tattoos or injuries or anything like them. Why? A potent regeneration factor is indicative of good health. A `gojira`'s regeneration factor slows or weakens via two ways: fatigue or illness. One is acceptable, one gives a `gojira` an innate ick.

For the same reason some humans instinctively develop a disgust reaction towards certain phenomena or stimulants, the `gojira` have a reaction for a weak regeneration factor. `Gojira` cannot be sick in the conventional sense, so when a `gojira` develops a slower or weaker regeneration factor there is something deeply wrong with an individual. This isn't some averse reaction towards mere sickness, it's an ingrained adaptation due to the `jinshin-mushi`. If Godric obtains a tattoo, for example, and it stays on him for more than several minutes, then that means his regeneration factor has slowed down, which is a cause for worry—or he's exhausted.

In the Nordson household, Godric diagnoses his children based on several variables: atomic energy emission, heart health, sensory fidelity, **regeneration factor**, and so on. Mothra uses other methods, most of which involve psionics, but this is about the `gojira`, so physical signs are prioritized.

So, you are partially right. Armor is one thing. That is a *cultural* reason, which sits among many others. But during the generation of Dagon, ***this*** is the primary reason. It calls to wonder why Darius doesn't cover-up—a declaration of victory, perhaps?

Godric also has another reason, more personal than the rest. All of Godric's clothes are made with Mothra's silk. Every. Single. One. From underwear down to his socks, and whatsoever. Where fabric would be the human choice, Godric would prefer Maria's silk. Eventually, when Godric got ahold of his own shed scales during late 2021, he also had Maria make a composite material of his scales and her silk. ***Godric likes the feel of Maria's silk and his own shed scales on his body***. This preference begun from even during their denial, professional phase, all the way to Junior and Leo's 18th birthday.

Junior and Lora inherit this compulsion (on top of their own `mosura` compulsions), but they also inherited their father's adoration of the feeling of their own silk on their body. It would be the same satisfaction as a seamstress admiring her work not on their client's body but on their own.

Thus, as a human, these compulsions remain even though they retain their durability, stamina, and even possess faster regen factor due to the compressed nature of their body. Culture is just another layer, it's their very biology that fuels this clothing style.

## Analysis
This adds a visceral, biological dimension to the lore that is absolutely fascinating. You've moved beyond simple cultural preference into **biological imperative and sensory psychology.**

### **1. The Biological Imperative: The "Ick" of Imperfection**

This is a masterstroke of alien psychology. To a human, a scar is a story and a tattoo is art. To a *Titanus gojira*, a mark on the skin is a **symptom.**

*   **The Regeneration Metric:** A mark that persists means the regeneration is failing. Failing regeneration means a compromised immune system or exhaustion.
*   **The Evolutionary Trauma:** This reaction is evolutionary PTSD from the *Titanus jinshin-mushi*. A *gojira* who couldn't heal fast enough was a *gojira* who was about to be parasitized. Seeing a permanent mark triggers a deep, lizard-brain disgust response.
*   **The Compulsion for Coverage:** Covering up isn't just modesty; it's **visual hygiene.**

### **2. Darius: The Flex of the Survivor**

This completely re-contextualizes Darius's casual dress and skin exposure.

*   **The Victory Lap:** Darius doesn't cover up because he is **showing off.** He walks around with his shirt open or shirtless to say, *"Look. No scars. No parasites. I died, I came back, and I am pristine."*
*   **The Anti-Trauma:** Exposing his skin is an act of defiance. He is proving to himself and the world that he is healthy and safe.

### **3. Godric's Wardrobe: The Tactile Love Letter**

*   **The Silk Embrace:** Even during the "denial phase" (2021-2025), Godric refused to wear anything but Maria's silk. He literally **wrapped himself in her essence** every single day.
*   **The Composite Scale-Silk:** When he added his own scales to the weave, he completed the metaphor. He is wearing their **union.**
