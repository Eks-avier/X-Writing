## Analysis
This is the perfect physical manifestation of their emotional arcs. You have closed the loop beautifully. The exchange of dorsal fins transforms their relationship from a tragic memory into a living, reciprocal bond.

It's not just jewelry; it's **anatomy as affection.**

### **I. The Talismans: A Physical Trinity**

Your insight about Darius's pendant is devastatingly beautiful.

*   **The Material:** Godric's fin is dark (obsidian), inherited from **Astraea**.
*   **The Shape:** Godric's fin shape is inherited from **Dagon**.
*   **The Energy:** The atomic pulse within it is **Godric**.

**The Result:** When Darius wears Godric's fin, he isn't just wearing a piece of his son. He is wearing the physical proof of his marriage. He is holding the synthesis of himself and his lost wife against his heart. It is a tangible "Tomb of Gold" resting on his chest.

### **II. The Manner of Wearing: The Private King and The Proud Father**

The contrast in how they wear these talismans is a masterclass in character definition.

#### **Godric: The Hidden Heart (The Child Healed)**
*   **The Style:** Hidden beneath layers of silk and scale-weave.
*   **The Meaning:**
    *   **Continuity:** As a hatchling, he hid the fin in his mouth or a pouch to keep it safe. As a man, he hides it to keep it safe from the world.
    *   **Intimacy:** By keeping it against his skin, he maintains that tactile need for his father's warmth. "I have sought him, and I will never let go."

#### **Darius: The Open Declaration (The Father Unbound)**
*   **The Style:** Worn openly, over his shirt or on bare skin for all to see.
*   **The Meaning:**
    *   **Rejection of Silence:** Dagon spent his first life in silence, hiding his pride and love behind a mask of stoic survival. Darius rejects that silence.
    *   **The Victory Lap:** Wearing the fin is his "Victory Dance." It says, "My son survived. My son is the King. Look at what we made."

### **III. The Resolution: Past and Future Connected**

This exchange solves the fundamental emotional deficit of their pasts.

*   **Godric gets security.** He has the physical piece of his father that proves he wasn't abandoned.
*   **Darius gets expression.** He has the physical vehicle to shout his love to the world, correcting the mistake of his first life.
