## Analysis
This is a beautiful, aching exploration of the "Golden Timeline." You are absolutely right—because of the time disparity, **Darius and Aurelia are the teenagers of the family.**

Godric and Maria are the ancient, settled patriarch and matriarch who have weathered eons. Darius and Aurelia are the tragic young lovers who were interrupted at the very start of their story and are now frantically, joyfully trying to write the rest of the chapters.

### **The Eternal Honeymoon: The 20-Year-Olds in the Palace**

The dynamic shift is fascinating. Godric is the "Responsible Adult" because he has lived for 250 million years. Darius and Aurelia are the "Young Couple" because they are biologically and psychologically in their prime youth (20 and 30 GEY).

*   **The Public Displays of Affection (PDA):** Godric and Maria are subtle—a hand on the small of the back, a shared look. Darius and Aurelia are **magnetic.** They are constantly holding hands, leaning on each other, or sneaking kisses in the hallways. They act like they are terrified that if they let go, the other will vanish again (because that is exactly what happened).
*   **The "Catch-Up" Walks:** You nailed it. They spend hours walking the shores of Monster Island. Darius talks about the 10 years he raised Godric alone, filling in the gaps of her memory. Aurelia talks about the silence of the Fallen Star and the joy of seeing him again. They are building a shared history from the fragments of their trauma.

### **The Daughters They Never Had: Rose and Maria**

For a couple who only had one son and lost him (from their perspective) too soon, having **Rose** and **Maria** is a revelation.

*   **Rose (The Miracle):** They dote on her. Aurelia finally has someone to share the "Internal Domain" with—someone to talk to about feelings, botany, and the soft power of the family. Darius treats her like a porcelain treasure that can also punch a tank. He is the overprotective dad she never had, and she drinks it up.
*   **Maria (The Queen-Daughter):** They are in awe of her. They know she is the Alpha Divine, but to them, she is the woman who saved their boy. Aurelia likely braids Maria's hair or helps her with the silk, reveling in the simple, female bonding she missed out on.

### **Scene: The "Scandalous" Dinner**

**(Setting: The Alpha Wing Dining Room. The air is light, filled with the smell of roasted meat and laughter.)**

"You two are inseparable today," Godric noted, cutting his steak with precise, kingly motions. He gestured with his fork to where Darius had his arm draped casually over the back of Aurelia's chair, playing with a loose strand of her hair. "Should Rose and I expect another sibling soon?"

Darius choked on his drink, sputtering loudly. He turned a shade of red that rivaled the sunset. "You have the gall?! *You*?! You and Maria are at it like rabbits! The walls aren't that thick, boy! We hear—"

"**THAT'S ENOUGH, YOU TWO!**" Aurelia's voice cracked like a whip, though her face was burning a brilliant crimson. She slammed her teacup down. "I do *not* need to hear about OUR sex life, and I certainly do not need to hear about MY SON'S! Have you no sense of **SHAME**?!"

The table went silent. Junior looked scandalized. Leo looked delighted.

Darius rubbed the back of his neck, grinning sheepishly at his wife. "I suppose we can't help it, my star. The blood runs hot."

Godric smirked, taking a slow sip of his wine. "Like father, like son, ne?"

Aurelia buried her face in her hands, but she was laughing. It was the sound of a woman who had spent an eternity in silence and was finally allowed to be loud, embarrassed, and alive.

### **The Observer: Godric's Healing**

This is the most poignant part of this timeline. Godric "eavesdropping" isn't about control; it's about **learning.**

*   **Watching from the Terrace:** He stands on the balcony above, watching his parents walk on the beach below. He sees Darius scoop Aurelia up in his arms to carry her over the surf. He hears Aurelia's bright, unburdened laughter.
*   **The Inner Child Healed:** For 250 million years, his mental image of his parents was defined by **absence** (Astraea) and **grief** (Dagon). Now, he sees the truth. They weren't just tragic figures; they were *people* who were madly in love.
*   **Understanding Himself:** Seeing them helps him understand his own relationship with Maria.
    *   *Why does he love the physical affection?* Because Darius does.
    *   *Why does he crave the deep emotional connection?* Because Aurelia does.
    *   *Why does Junior watch him?* He realizes, with a start, that Junior watches him and Maria for the exact same reason he is watching his parents now: **to see what Love looks like.** To feel safe. To know that the foundation of their world is solid.
