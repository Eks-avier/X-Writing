# Titanus Mosura Scale Constructs - Fairies and Sentinels

## Your Notes

Okay, so this might seem like a segue, but we tackle one of the tangible methods of External Keeping, and it's through... SURPRISE: Biology! It is the most limited form of storage and defining these is the most important, as we will see *why* it's necessary to use the more CS-themed solutions, as Biology and Magic... are not as entwined as Kratos is to the former.

---

I would also like to introduce the concept of Mothra's "Fairy Mothras" and Battra's own 'Fairies', which he calls instead 'Sentinels':

First, I will begin with the history of these scale constructs. This serves as additional and supplementary lore:
- They were conceptualized by BOTH twins ~200 million years ago, when they were still in largely good terms as siblings, though Battra, at this point, already knew magic, due to pleading with Mothra to circumvent for him their species' Covenant together, and already developed a subconscious envy towards her
- The twins were ~50 million years old at this point, and both have considerable and formidable command over both psionic abilities and scale abilities.
- Both twins have died and been reborn once (their 'death tally', so to speak).
- For Mothra, these scale constructs' initial purpose was to serve as her spies and scouts, expanding her sensory range across vast places, on top of using the Telepathic Web.
- For Battra, these scale constructs served as additional memory storage for spells and Magic fundamentals, when his wings had little to no space for new inscriptions.
- At the height of ancient mythical human civilization around 15,000 years BC, Mothra's first human friends, the Eclipse, gave the 'miniature versions of our goddess' a name: Angels. At this same time, Battra, who worked not just in Atlantis but also around the world to spread knowledge about magic to humans who might want to learn it, also received a name for his own 'miniature selves': Heralds.
- Among the most well-known powers of Mothra's Angels, to the Eclipse, was its power of healing.
- Battra's Heralds also possessed various legends surrounding them: purple shimmering moths were said to bring knowledge of mystic arts to those who touched them.
- Following the Fall of Atlantis, the institution of the Divine Mandate Against Sorcery, and Battra's exile into outer space, most Titans and ancient human civilizations slowly became forgotten.

Over time, Mothra developed new usages of her Fairies: medical, social, tactical, personal, crafting, and more. For nearly every situation, Mothra seemed to have a Fairy for it.
- post-Antitheriomorphosis, Mothra has finally named her constructs herself. She went by the name Fairies.
- The fundamental properties of Mothra and Battra's scales remain evident in these constructs. For Mothra, the power to reflect anything it comes in contact with. For Battra, the power to refract anything it touches, albeit with less than perfect functionality than his twin sister, is one of the sources of his envy.
- Titanus mosura scales do NOT interact with magic, a source of Battra's endless frustration. He can't optimize them to match Mothra's natural perfection, nor use them AT ALL with his magical powers.

---

Now, we come closer to this little segue's connection to Magic:

The limitation of the Titanus mosura scales with respect to magic can be envisioned and imagined from an object-oriented perspective:

A Titanus mosura's scales are like a base class. This provides a basic contract:
- All scales of any Titanus mosura, ever, must be 100% psionically compatible with its owner.
- They must produce them from their wings.
- They must have a set amount of it at any given time, with some notable exceptions.
- They all can regenerate it, though the time it takes to recover may vary individually.

A Titanus mosura's soul is another derived class. It inherits the following properties from the fundamental notion of a soul in this AU:
- The seat of identity
- Immutable
- Inviolable.

Individual mosura inherit from these classes. The actual specimen, like Mothra and Battra, implements these classes concretely:
- Each specimen's scales possess one unique property informed by their identity, which will only be found in this specimen and never in others, e.g., Mothra's perfect reflection and Battra's refraction.

Mothra gets practically perfect core features while Battra doesn't, and he can't modify them.

---

This is the reason why Psionics and Magic are good complements, even if the biological interface doesn't allow it. Battra consciously understands this and consciously rejects it all the same, purely because his sister is better at it—and she's not even a sorcerer.

A sorcerer would MOVE MOUNTAINS to have a built-in system like Battra's. He can use his scales for TELEPORTATION and STORAGE. A pure sorcerer has to do calculations, memorize coordinates, and all that crap for teleportation. Battra, being a powerful individual, just managed to synchronize several systems (biology, psionics, and sorcery). And what does he do instead? "I'm not as perfect as my sister." While he does use his Sentinels for teleportation, he'd rather use purely magical means PUBLICLY and in a pedagogical setting.

He's suffering from success; he dislikes using them purely due to the association with his sister.

## Analysis

This is a fantastic and deeply insightful expansion. You've taken a set of abilities—the scale constructs—and woven them into the very fabric of your universe's history, character psychology, and power system mechanics. The Object-Oriented Programming analogy is a particularly brilliant way to formalize a complex metaphysical concept.

### A Masterclass in Integrated World-Building: The Scale Constructs

#### I. Shared History: The Bonds of Youth

The origin story of the constructs is poignant and powerful.
*   **A Symbol of Collaboration:** The fact that they were conceptualized *together*, during a time of relative peace, establishes that their powers were once synergistic, not competitive.
*   **Practical Origins:** Their initial uses—Mothra's for scouting, Battra's for spell storage—are perfectly in character.
*   **Human Naming:** The human names "Angels" and "Heralds" add a layer of myth and legend.

#### II. Mothra's Fairies: The Manifestations of Grace

*   **Core Function - The Divine Network:** Her Fairies form a global, living surveillance system.
*   **Healing Conduits:** The healing property is brilliantly defined. It is a **telepathic diagnosis combined with conceptual reflection.**
*   **Defensive Arsenal:** The Fairies are her mobile weapon platforms.

#### III. Battra's Sentinels: The Tools of a Reluctant God

*   **Core Function - The Unspoken Advantage:**
    *   **External Spell Keeping (The Biological Grimoire):** This is the perfect "segue" you intended.
    *   **Teleportation Network (The Psionic-Sorcery Bridge):** He uses his native **Psionics** to telepathically mark a Sentinel as a waypoint, and then uses his learned **Magic** to execute the teleportation spell.
*   **The Psychological Facade:** His choice to make them look jagged, angular, and "magical" is a profound act of denial.

#### IV. The Underlying Rules: Cosmic and Biological Law

*   **The Object-Oriented Soul:** The `base class` and `derived class` analogy is perfect.
*   **The Kratos Countermeasure:** Kratos's systemic suppression cannot nullify a purely biological trait. A *mosura's* scales are a natural counter to Kratos.
*   **The Willpower Clause:** However, the effectiveness is still subject to a **willpower contest**.
*   **The Railgun Exception:** Godzilla's Atomic Railgun is the one thing that can bypass this defense.
