# Psionic Abilities Spectrum - Five Disciplines

> **Source:** `Formatted_With_Thoughts - The Eclipse of the ATM AU II.md` — Prompt 18 (Lines 1488-1619)

## Your Notes

We have dealt with Kratos's mechanics, but to further develop it, we have to veer a little bit into Psionics.

---

Okay, let's discuss the spectrum of psionic abilities. This is central to the narrative because part of its overarching style is that a power system is an inherent part of a character. It, along with many classical story devices, informs their decisions, thoughts, beliefs, relationships, and how they form them, and motivations. Plus, cool shit entices readers.

Psionics defines only **two** fundamental abilities. The underlying philosophy of this system is that it is the easiest to understand and the most stable relative to the other systems. Magic is the most technically demanding, with virtually uncountable variations, schools, and styles. At the same time, Kratos is the least complex, but also the most volatile of the three and genetically determined of the two. The signature of Psionics, thus, as a system, is that it has easy-to-understand mechanics, reliability, and stability, which is why it is the first to be introduced.

All abilities in Psionics therefore derive from these: Telepathy or Telekinesis. I'm considering categorizing different psychic abilities. Currently, we have one given for telepathy: Mental Communication. This is where the distinction between Active and Passive Telepaths comes in. Mental Communication is the umbrella term for all skills and procedures covering communication via the mind.

Most of the abilities listed, especially those of the students, are examples. For example, Dreamwalking is a telepathic subskill that enables users to enter another person's dreams or subconscious. However, that's only the *act* of entering itself. Changing something in the mind of *another* person requires an advanced Psionic skill called Mental Manipulation, which is also another umbrella term. It involves having both telepathy and (at the bare minimum, even just weakly) telekinesis. This is because something changing in the mind of another person is a direct consequence of a Psychic tampering with the brain's physical architecture. However, Psychics can freely rearrange their mind, though this is constrained by the fact that it is dangerous. A psychic's power is in his brain, and if its physical makeup is incorrectly tampered with, it can be fatal.

The challenge is actually creating these categories and mapping them out hierarchically! Let's focus on telepathy first, I'm thinking maybe something like five "general categories" that map out most of what a telepath can do. For example, a telepath should be able to communicate with other telepaths or non-telepaths, hence we have Mental Communication. A telepath, who is also a telekinetic, may use such an ability to tamper with memories or even, dangerously, the physical makeup of another person, thus that falls under Mental Manipulation.

Each Psychic is advised to specialize in one area, but possess general know-how on others.

My current ideas are:

- Mental Communication
- Mental Manipulation (which can be the "offensive" branch in combat)
- A defensive branch, but that is not to say it is physical; I meant mental defense
- A branch that focuses on sensing things in the environment
- A branch that focuses on projection, which would be advanced.

Does this cleanly fit most telepathic abilities, and give way for further development and additions down the line?

## Analysis

This is an excellent way to structure and formalize the Psionic system. By defining two fundamental abilities (Telepathy and Telekinesis) and then branching them into distinct, well-defined categories of application, you're creating a system that is both easy to understand (as intended) and rich with potential for specialization and character expression.

Your proposed five categories for Telepathy are a very strong starting point.

### Level 1: The Two Fundamental Abilities

This is the core of the system. Every psionic ability is a manifestation or combination of these two.

1.  **Telepathy:** The ability to interact with minds.
2.  **Telekinesis:** The ability to interact with matter/energy using the mind.

### Level 2: The Five Core Disciplines of Telepathy

These are the five general "schools" or "branches" of application that a telepath can specialize in. They cover the full spectrum of what telepathy can achieve.

#### **1. Mental Communication (The Lingua Franca)**

*   **Core Function:** The exchange of thoughts, emotions, and concepts between minds.
*   **Philosophy:** Connection and understanding. The foundational skill for all collaborative psionic work.
*   **Core Skills:**
    *   **T2T Linking (Telepath-to-Telepath):** Standard communication. (Foundation for the Telepathic Web).
    *   **T2N Linking (Telepath-to-Non):** Advanced, requires immense precision and empathy. Mothra is the ultimate master of this.
    *   **Surface Skimming:** Involuntary/passive reading of surface thoughts.
    *   **Deep Reading:** Active, consent-based probing of memory and consciousness.
*   **Student Examples:** **Samuel Johns** is a specialist here, focusing on maintaining group links.

#### **2. Mental Fortification (The Aegis)**

*   **Core Function:** The defense of one's own mind and the minds of others from external influence.
*   **Philosophy:** Integrity and sovereignty of the self. Protecting the sanctity of the mind.
*   **Core Skills:**
    *   **Mental Shielding:** Creating passive or active barriers to prevent mental intrusion (both telepathic and magical).
    *   **Thought Obfuscation:** Actively camouflaging or hiding one's own surface thoughts from skim readers.
    *   **Interference Filtering:** The ability to create "mental boundaries" to function in a "mental soup" of other psychics.
    *   **Anchoring:** Reinforcing an ally's mind against mental attack or emotional manipulation.
*   **Student Examples:** **Isabella Vance** is the clear specialist, the team's dedicated mental defender.

#### **3. Mental Incursion (The Scalpel)**

*   **Core Function:** The active and offensive manipulation of another's mind, thoughts, and perceptions. **This is the most ethically fraught discipline.**
*   **Philosophy:** Influence and control. The "offensive" branch of telepathy.
*   **Core Skills:**
    *   **Persuasion/Suggestion:** Subtly influencing decisions and emotions (the "Jedi mind trick").
    *   **Illusion Casting:** Projecting false sensory information directly into a target's mind.
    *   **Memory Manipulation:**
        *   *Memory Extraction:* Forcibly pulling information from a mind (Battra's preferred method).
        *   *Memory Alteration/Erasure:* The most dangerous and unethical application, capable of rewriting personality.
    *   **Psychic Assault:** A direct, brute-force attack on a target's consciousness, designed to disorient, incapacitate, or shatter their will.
*   **Student Examples:** **Nathan Draper** specializes in the subtle end of this spectrum (persuasion), while **Gerome Marcel** is more on the invasive side (memory extraction).

#### **4. Mental Projection (The Surveyor)**

*   **Core Function:** Extending one's consciousness beyond the physical body to perceive and interact with the world remotely.
*   **Philosophy:** Exploration and awareness. Transcending physical limitations.
*   **Core Skills:**
    *   **Remote Viewing (Clairvoyance):** Perceiving a distant location.
    *   **Psychic Projection (Astral Projection):** Sending one's consciousness to a location as an intangible observer. Advanced users might be able to subtly interact.
    *   **Dreamwalking:** A specialized form of projection that targets the subconscious landscape of another individual's mind.
*   **Student Examples:** **Stella Balagtas** (Dreamwalking) and **Gerome Marcel** (Advanced Psychic Projection) are the primary specialists here.

#### **5. Extrasensory Perception (The Antenna)**

*   **Core Function:** Using psionic senses to gather information about the physical and psychic environment beyond the normal five senses.
*   **Philosophy:** Sensitivity and intuition. Reading the "unseen" world.
*   **Core Skills:**
    *   **Empathy:** Sensing the emotional state of others.
    *   **Psychometry:** Reading the psychic "imprint" or history of an object or location.
    *   **Precognition/Danger Sense:** Glimpses of the immediate future or detection of imminent threats.
*   **Student Examples:** **Julia Simmons** (Empathy), **Marcus Hale** (Psychometry), and **Ethan Caldwell** (Precognition) are all specialists within this discipline.

---

### Does this framework fit your needs?

**Yes, absolutely.** Here's why:

*   **Clean Categorization:** It cleanly maps every student's ability to a primary discipline, while allowing for secondary skills.
    *   **Suki Takao** is a great example of a generalist. Her "superior telepathy" would mean she has strong foundational skills in Communication, Fortification, and probably Incursion, making her a natural combat leader.
*   **Hierarchical Logic:** The structure is logical. You can't really perform Mental Incursion without some level of Mental Communication. You need Fortification to protect yourself while you Project. It all fits together.
*   **Room for Development:** This framework is beautifully expandable. You can add more granular sub-skills under each of the five disciplines as the story requires, without breaking the core structure. For example, "Technopathy" could be a very rare sub-skill of Mental Incursion or Projection that allows interaction with computer systems.
*   **Integration of Telekinesis:** The requirement of telekinesis for the more potent forms of **Mental Incursion** (like altering the brain's physical architecture) is a brilliant way to tie the two fundamental abilities together. It raises the stakes and makes true "mind control" a hybrid, high-level skill, not a common telepathic trick.
    *   This also explains why most telekinetics have passive telepathy—they need that mental "sense" to guide their physical manipulation.

### The Role of Specialization

Your idea that psychics are advised to specialize is perfect. It creates distinct roles within the team and allows for individual character expression.

*   **Samuel** is the "Comms Officer."
*   **Isabella** is the "Shield."
*   **Nathan** is the "Infiltrator."
*   **Marcus** is the "Tracker."
*   **Ethan** is the "Scout."

This structure feels robust, intuitive, and perfectly aligned with the philosophical underpinnings you've established for Psionics. It successfully makes the system "easy to understand" on the surface while providing immense depth for those who look closer.
