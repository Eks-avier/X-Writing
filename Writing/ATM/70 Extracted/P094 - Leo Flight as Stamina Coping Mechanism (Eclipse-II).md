## Analysis

### IV. Leo's Flying: A Smart Adaptation

Leo's use of flight as a stamina-saving measure is a clever and logical adaptation for his character.

*   **Playing to His Strengths:** He has a massive pool of psionic energy but poor physical stamina. By using telekinesis to fly, he's intelligently conserving his physical energy for his explosive bursts of combat. It's a sign of his underlying creativity and problem-solving skills, even if his primary motive is just to get a piggyback ride.
*   **The Endearing Habit:** The fact that he'll still choose to ride his family members, even as a teenager, is an adorable character trait that shows his playful nature and deep affection for them never truly fades.
