## Analysis

The raw, passive emanation of a Kraton's Aura can be consciously modified through four fundamental techniques. These techniques are organized along two distinct axes of control, allowing for precise management of the Aura's properties and scale. While colloquial terms like "Flaring" are common, formal classification uses specific Hellenic-derived terms to denote these actions.

### Formal Hellenic Terms

| **Action** | **Formal Term** | **Meaning** |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Flaring** | **Anapsis** (anapsis: "Kindling" or "Lighting Up") | Intensifying innate effects |
| **Suppression** | **Kryptis** (kryptis: "Hiding" or "Concealment") | Minimizing effects |
| **Expansion** | **Ektasis** (ektasis: "Expanse" or "Spreading Out") | Enlarging radius |
| **Reduction** | **Systole** (systole: "Contraction" or "Shrinking") | Decreasing radius |
| **Coating** | **Epichrisis** (epichrisis: "Anointing" or "Coating Upon") | Sheathing with Aura |
