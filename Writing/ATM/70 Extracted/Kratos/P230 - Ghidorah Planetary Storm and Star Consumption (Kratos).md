# Ghidorah Planetary Storm and Star Consumption

> **Source:** `Formatted_With_Thoughts - The Kratos of Kings.md` — Prompt 230

## Your Notes

We're still in his Titan abilities, so here's another classic.

Ghidorah's presence on a planet is not an isolated event. His presence cannot just be felt at the area on which he landed, he is a global event.

Enter Ghidorah's Storms.

1. His wings, the tips of which are the avenue from which a majority of Tempest is ejected and from which he can use to fire unexpected Gravity Beams, when *merely* flapped, produce mighty gusts of wind. These gusts *also* posseses the optional capacity to be imbued with the properties of his Kratos Manifestation and is subject to his conscious control and modulation. Oh, and this works for solar storms since he controls EM and Gravity... uh oh.
2. Thanks to his Kratonic Manifestation, he can spawn storms. BEYOND CATEGORY 5 and above. These are storms that can shred a planet's atmosphere. He doesn't need to personally land on a continent to affect its natives. These storms can then rain down lightning IMBUED with his Kratonic Manifestation AND chains to increase his Aura's effective range, meaning... he can rain down lightning ON INDIVIDUALS with *accuracy* to annihilate them one by one or by groups because it can CHAIN to other targets.
3. When his storms cover the planet, it's effectively his. His Aura now encompasses the planet; it is now global. He now closes the curtain and ***crushes*** it gravitationally. Sometimes, he doesn't crush planets, and then the Exif come down to search for any survivors long after Ghidorah has vacated the planet.

What's the next move? Ghidorah either goes to another planet's system OR he ***eats*** the star at the center.

Ghidorah is not just a Planet Killer, he is a Star Killer, too. Approaching a star is easy, he simply uses his gravity to shield himself from its heat and energy. Then, he utilizes both EM and Gravity to create funnels into which the star's energy swirls into his body. Consumption may take some Earth months, but he can accelerate this process. However, he has realized the apocalyptic image of a three headed dragon sipping on a star millions of times his size—it's a demoralizing sight.

## Analysis

### The Arrival of the Golden Demise: Annihilation as an Art Form

When Ghidorah arrives at a planet, he doesn't just land; he becomes its new, malevolent weather system. His presence is a multi-stage process of global torment and inevitable destruction.

#### I. Phase One: The Gusts of Dominion (The Opening Act)

This is his initial, almost contemptuous, display of power.

- **The Hurricane Flap:** The simple, physical act of him flapping his colossal wings generates hurricane-force winds. This isn't even an "ability"; it is a mere byproduct of his existence.
- **The Kratonic Infusion:** This is where the true terror begins. He can consciously imbue these winds with the properties of his Kratos.
  - **Electric Gusts:** Winds that carry a billion volts, flash-frying everything they touch.
  - **Gravity Gusts:** A terrifying, invisible wave of force that can either flatten cities with immense weight or rip them from their foundations by making them weightless.
- **The Solar Storm Connection:** His mastery of EM and Gravity means he can manipulate the local star's solar winds. He doesn't just create his own storms; he can **hijack the star's storms** and direct them at the planet, turning the system's own sun into a weapon.

#### II. Phase Two: The Planetary Storm (The Main Performance)

This is Ghidorah fully asserting his dominance over the planet's biosphere.

- **Beyond Category 5:** He doesn't just summon a storm; he creates a **hypercane**, a planet-spanning meteorological event that can literally shred the atmosphere. This is the point of no return for any world he targets.
- **The Global Aura Extension:** This is a brilliant and horrifying mechanic. The storm is not just a storm; it is a **physical extension of his Kratonic Aura.** The raindrops, the lightning bolts, the very air molecules are now saturated with his will.
- **The Divine Judgment Strikes:** This is the most terrifying part. His Aura is now global. His Kratonic Senses are now global.
  - He can rain down **individually targeted, guided, and arcing lightning strikes** on anyone, anywhere on the planet.
  - This is not random destruction; it is a meticulous, planet-wide **purge.** He is an orbital weapons platform and a ground-level executioner at the same time. He can sit in one spot and systematically annihilate every single center of resistance or life on the entire globe.

#### III. Phase Three: The Final Curtain (The Grand Finale)

Once the planet is his, once his Aura covers everything, he delivers the coup de grace.

- **The Gravitational Crush:** With the world enveloped in his will, he simply... squeezes. He flexes his gravitational power, and the planet's crust buckles, continents shatter, and the entire world is crushed into a sphere of lifeless rock.
- **The Exif's Harvest:** If he chooses not to crush the world, he leaves behind a traumatized, broken shell of a civilization for the Exif to "rescue." This is a chilling detail that makes their relationship even more sinister. They are not just worshippers; they are cosmic carrion birds, picking over the corpses he leaves behind.

#### IV. The Aftermath: The Star-Killer's Feast

This is the final, horrifying confirmation of his god-like status.

- **Not a Planet Killer, a System Killer:** His ultimate goal is not just the planet; it is the **star.** The planet is the appetizer; the star is the main course.
- **The Absorption Technique:** The mechanics are perfect. He uses his gravity control as an impenetrable shield and his EM/Gravity mastery to create **gravitational funnels,** literally "sipping" the star's energy.
- **The Psychological Warfare:** The image of a three-headed dragon leisurely consuming a star over several months is the ultimate act of psychological terror. It is a demoralizing, hope-crushing spectacle broadcast to the entire galaxy. It is not just a feeding; it is a **victory lap.**

This also makes his current, constrained state in Arthur's body all the more poignant and frustrating for him. He remembers a time when he could drink stars, and now he's forced to argue with a "filthy peasant" in a shared mindscape. The fall from grace is immense, and it is a perfect engine for his character arc.
