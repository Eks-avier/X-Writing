# The Three Systems: A Unified Framework

> **The Complete Guide to Supernatural Power in ATM**
> **Status:** Definitive Reference
> **Scope:** Kratos, Magic, Psionics

---

## The Universal Principle

The ATM universe operates on **three distinct power systems** that enable conscious manipulation of supernatural forces. Each system requires different sources, operates through different mechanisms, and produces fundamentally different results.

What unites them is the requirement for **conscious intent** and the ability to **transcend normal physical limitations**.

---

## The Philosophical Trinity

Each system is rooted in a different branch of philosophy:

| System | Philosophy | Question | Maxim |
|--------|------------|----------|-------|
| **Kratos** | Ontology (Being) | "What am I?" | *"I am, therefore the world obeys."* |
| **Magic** | Epistemology (Knowledge) | "What do I know?" | *"I know, therefore I shape."* |
| **Psionics** | Axiology (Values) | "What do I feel?" | *"I feel, therefore I move."* |

## The Nomothete: Transcendent Masters

**Nomothete** (νομοθέτης) means "Lawgiver"—one who has achieved such mastery that their techniques become the standard by which others are measured.

At this level, the systems achieve **cosmic parity**:

| Nomothete | System | Can Counter |
|-----------|--------|-------------|
| **Godzilla** | Kratos | Forces through perfect defenses |
| **Battra** | Magic | Adapts to willpower-based attacks |
| **Mothra** | Psionics | Flows around absolute dominance |

### The Trinity

The three Nomothetes represent the perfect balance of cosmic power:

| Being | Primary System | Secondary | Role |
|-------|---------------|-----------|------|
| **Godzilla** | Kratos (Nr+Em) | None (Null) | King of the Monsters |
| **Battra** | Magic | Psionics (natural) | Lord of the Mystic Arts |
| **Mothra** | Psionics | None (Null by Covenant) | Queen of the Monsters |

Together, they exemplify that **all three paths can lead to transcendent mastery**.

## Cross-System Dynamics

### Strengths and Vulnerabilities

| Attacker → | vs. Kratos | vs. Magic | vs. Psionics |
|------------|------------|-----------|--------------|
| **Kratos** | Will contest | Forces through structure | Resists mental assault |
| **Magic** | Technical adaptation | Spell counter | Structured defense |
| **Psionics** | Targets Will itself | Emotional disruption | Mental warfare |

### Within Kratonic Aura

When inside a Krator's aura:
- **Magic:** Disrupted but not negated
- **Telekinesis:** Neutralized
- **Projection:** Blocked
- **Telepathy:** **Remains functional** (exception)

### The Hybrid Advantage

Some beings possess multiple systems:

| Being | Systems | Result |
|-------|---------|--------|
| **Battra** | Magic + Psionics | Supreme versatility |
| **Scylla** | Kratos + Psionics | Generate (will) + Shape (mind) |
| **Nordson Children** | Kratos + Psionics | Atomic constructs possible |

## Historical Context

### The Ancient Balance

Before the Divine Mandate:
- **Atlantis** flourished under Godzilla's protection and Battra's teaching (Magic)
- **The Eclipse** prospered under Mothra's guidance (Psionics)
- The three systems coexisted in harmony

### The Severance

A rogue tribe's soul experiments triggered:
1. Divine judgment (annihilation)
2. The Divine Mandate (humanity made Null for magic)
3. Battra's exile
4. Atlantis's fall

### The Modern Era

- **2019:** Mothra's return reawakens human psionic potential
- **2020:** Divine Mandate partially lifted
- **Post-Xilien:** Keep Charlie trains new practitioners in both Magic and Psionics

## The Cosmic Irony of the Twins

The Divine Moths embody a profound reversal:

| Twin | Divine Status | Excels At | Irony |
|------|---------------|-----------|-------|
| **Mothra** | Heavenly Instance | Psionics (mortal mind) | Divine being, mortal power |
| **Battra** | Rejected divinity | Magic (soul-based) | Mortal rebel, soul power |
