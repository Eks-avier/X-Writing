## Overview

**Psionics** is the power system of **direct mental influence over reality**. Unlike Magic (structured transformation through knowledge) or Kratos (willpower made manifest), Psionics operates through the **Mind's interface with Psiforce**, the sixth fundamental force of nature.

### The Universal Principle

> *"I feel, therefore I move."*

When a psychic manifests their power, their mind directly translates emotional and mental intent into physical effects. There is no intermediary process—consciousness touches reality without transformation or structure.

### What Makes Psionics Unique

- **Direct mind-to-reality interface** - no intermediary forces
- **Intuition-based** - responds to feeling rather than technique
- **Emotion-driven** - power influenced by emotional states
- **Minimal classifications** - only telepathy and telekinesis as true forms

## Key Concepts

### Neural Ignition

The process where the brain/mind interfaces with Psiforce:

| System | Process | Energy Produced |
|--------|---------|-----------------|
| Magic | Soul Infusion | Primus Energy |
| **Psionics** | **Neural Ignition** | **Psienergy** |
| Kratos | (Direct) | (Direct) |

### True Psychics vs. Constrained PK

**True Psychics (Direct):**
- Mind can directly, actively influence reality
- No intermediary or elemental requirement
- Examples: Mothra, Battra, active telekinetics

**Constrained Psychokinesis (Indirect):**
- Requires elemental medium for expression
- Cannot generate what they control—only utilize existing environment
- Examples: Hydrokinesis, Pyrokinesis, Geokinesis

### The Five Pillars of Psychokinesis

| Pillar | Definition |
|--------|------------|
| **Volume** | Amount controllable |
| **Strength** | Force exertable |
| **Stability** | Control consistency |
| **Fluidity** | Grace and precision |
| **Creativity** | Novelty and ingenuity |

All hinge on **mental composure**—a distracted mind cannot control anything.
