## Psiforce: The Sixth Fundamental Force

### Fundamental Nature

**Psiforce** (also called **Psi Force** or **Psionic Force**) exists as the sixth fundamental force of nature—a supernatural force alongside the five natural forces (gravity, electromagnetism, strong nuclear, weak nuclear) and Primus.

### Key Properties

| Property | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| **Mind-Responsive** | Reacts directly to mental intent |
| **Emotion-Linked** | Power scales with emotional state |
| **Intuitive** | No structure required for access |
| **Universal** | Present throughout the cosmos |

### Comparison to Primus

| Aspect | Psiforce | Primus |
|--------|----------|--------|
| Interface | Mind/Brain | Soul |
| Access Process | Neural Ignition | Soul Infusion |
| Energy Produced | Psienergy | Primus Energy |
| Requires Structure | No | Yes |

## Neural Ignition

### The Process

**Neural Ignition** is the foundational process where the brain/mind interfaces with Psiforce, generating usable psionic energy.

Unlike Soul Infusion (which transforms unstable Primus into structured energy), Neural Ignition is a **direct connection**—the mind touches Psiforce and translates intent into effect.

### Comparison Across Systems

| System | Process | Energy | Interface |
|--------|---------|--------|-----------|
| **Psionics** | Neural Ignition | Psienergy | Mind/Brain |
| Magic | Soul Infusion | Primus Energy | Soul |
| Kratos | (Direct) | (Direct) | Will |

## Biological Architecture

### Genetic Determination

Psionic ability is **genetically determined**:

- Natural aptitude varies by species and individual
- Cannot be gained through training if not present
- Can be developed and refined if present
- Some species are entirely Non

### Neural Structure

The physical brain provides the interface:

- Specific neural configurations enable psionic access
- Brain development affects potential
- Physical brain damage can impair abilities
- Mental discipline enhances function

### Species Variation

Different species have varying psionic potential:

| Species | Typical Classification |
|---------|------------------------|
| Divine Moths | True Psychic (supreme) |
| Humans (pre-Mandate) | Variable Unnon |
| Humans (post-2019) | Reawakening Unnon |
| Titanus Gojira | Non (but see Nordson children) |
| Many Titans | Constrained PK |

## The True Psychic vs. Constrained Distinction

### True Psychics

**Definition:** Unnon whose mind can directly, actively, and independently influence reality without intermediaries.

**Characteristics:**
- Mind touches reality directly
- No elemental medium required
- Full telepathy and telekinesis
- Highest psionic potential

**Examples:**
- Mothra (supreme)
- Battra (as Divine Moth)
- Active human telekinetics

### Constrained Psychokinesis

**Definition:** Psionic ability that requires an elemental medium for expression.

**Characteristics:**
- Mind works through specific element
- Cannot generate what they control
- Must utilize existing environment
- Appears as "elemental magic" but is limited psionics

**Examples:**
- Hydrokinesis (water/ice) - Scylla, Leviathan, Manda
- Pyrokinesis (fire/heat) - Rodan
- Geokinesis (earth/stone) - Baragon

**Key Limitation:** CPK users **DO NOT** generate what they control—they manipulate what already exists.

## The Antitheriomorphosis Revelation

### What It Revealed

When Titans were transformed into human form, their true capabilities became apparent:

| Type | What Happened |
|------|---------------|
| Constrained PK | Gained new applications (flight without wings, weapon creation) |
| True Kratons | Discovered willpower-based manipulation |
| Pure Biological | Limited to scaling existing abilities |

### Why It Matters

The transformation stripped away biological constraints, revealing that many "elemental Titan abilities" were actually **constrained psionics** operating through specific elements—not separate magical systems.

## Emotional Dependency

### The Core Vulnerability

All psionic ability depends on **mental and emotional state**:

| State | Effect on Psionics |
|-------|-------------------|
| Calm, focused | Optimal control and power |
| Emotional turmoil | Reduced precision, potential power surge |
| Mental instability | Erratic, dangerous effects |
| Unconscious | Abilities dormant |

### Combat Implications

Psionic combat often becomes **emotional warfare**:

- Opponents try to destabilize each other mentally
- Fear, anger, and grief can be weaponized
- The calmer combatant often prevails
- "Ragebait" is a legitimate strategy

## Mothra's Reawakening (2019)

### The Event

When Mothra unfurled her wings after a thousand years of hibernation, she released a **psionic shockwave** that swept across Earth:

- Reactivated dormant human psychic potential
- Awakened latent psionic abilities worldwide
- Connected to the partial lifting of the Divine Mandate

### The Result

Humanity's psionic potential, dormant since the Divine Mandate, began reawakening:

- Some humans developed telepathic sensitivity
- Others manifested telekinetic potential
- Keep Charlie was established to train these individuals
