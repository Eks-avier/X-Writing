# Appendix A: Glossary of Terms

> **Parent:** [[Psionics, the Power of Mind]]
> **Quick Reference for All Psionic Terminology**

---

## Practitioner Classifications

| Term | Definition |
|------|------------|
| **Unnon** | Capable of psionic abilities |
| **Non** | Cannot use psionic abilities |
| **True Psychic** | Direct, unconstrained mental influence over reality |
| **Constrained PK** | Psionic ability requiring elemental medium |
| **Active Telepath** | Can initiate mental contact |
| **Passive Telepath** | Can only receive mental contact |

---

## Fundamental Concepts

| Term | Definition |
|------|------------|
| **Psiforce** | The sixth fundamental force; supernatural force interfacing with mind |
| **Psi Force** | Alternate name for Psiforce |
| **Psionic Force** | Alternate name for Psiforce |
| **Psienergy** | Usable psionic energy produced through Neural Ignition |
| **Neural Ignition** | Process where brain/mind interfaces with Psiforce |

---

## The Five Disciplines

| Term | Definition |
|------|------------|
| **Mental Communication** | Mind-to-mind contact for communication |
| **Mental Fortification** | Psychic defense and barriers |
| **Mental Incursion** | Entering and affecting other minds |
| **Mental Projection** | Extending consciousness beyond body |
| **Extrasensory Perception** | Enhanced/expanded sensory capabilities |

---

## Communication Terms

| Term | Definition |
|------|------------|
| **Telepathy** | Mind-to-mind communication |
| **Empathy** | Sensing emotions rather than thoughts |
| **Mental Speech** | Direct thought transmission |
| **Broadcast** | Projecting to all minds in range |
| **Selective Telepathy** | Choosing specific minds to contact |

---

## Defense Terms

| Term | Definition |
|------|------------|

| **Emotional Barrier** | Prevention of empathic reading |
| **Psychic Resistance** | General defense against mental attack |
