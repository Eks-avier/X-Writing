## Supreme Practitioners

### Mothra, Queen of the Monsters

**Species:** Divine Moth (Titanus mosura)
**Classification:** Supreme True Psychic
**Status:** Null for magic, Unnon for psionics

#### Unique Position

Mothra represents the **pinnacle of psionic power**:
- Unmatched telepathic range and precision
- Supreme telekinetic capability
- Perfect emotional mastery
- Divine soul enhancement

#### The Divine Paradox

Despite being the Heavenly Instance—direct divine connection—Mothra's greatest practical power is **psionic**, stemming from the mortal mind rather than the divine soul.

#### Teaching Role

At Keep Charlie, Mothra serves as a **guest teacher** for:
- Advanced intuitive concepts
- Ethical telepathy practices
- Emotional mastery techniques
- The "feel" of psionic manipulation

---

### Battra, Lord of the Mystic Arts

**Species:** Divine Moth (Titanus mosura)
**Classification:** True Psychic + Master Sorcerer
**Unique:** Only known dual practitioner at supreme level

#### The Reluctant Psychic

Battra was born with Divine Moth psychic abilities but:
- Philosophically disdains intuition-based power
- Prefers structured magical approach
- Cannot escape his natural gifts
- Now teaches both systems

#### Teaching Approach

Battra brings **structured methodology** to psionics:
- Systematic training protocols
- Efficiency optimization
- Technical understanding of Neural Ignition
- Integration with magical practice

#### The Irony

He rejected divine connection for structured magic, yet:
- His teaching strength comes from natural psychic ability
- He excels at explaining intuitive concepts technically
- He bridges the gap between systems

## Hybrid Practitioners

### Scylla Frost

**Species:** Ice Weaver (Titanus scylla)
**Classification:** Stenokakiston Krateira (Ky) + Hydrokinetic

#### The Synthesis

Scylla represents the **perfect fusion** of Kratos and Psionics:

| System | Function |
|--------|----------|
| **Kratos** | Generates ice through will |
| **Psionics** | Shapes and controls generated ice |

#### Significance

Her existence proves that:
- Systems can work together synergistically
- Limitations of one system can be overcome by another
- Hybrid approaches may exceed pure approaches
- "Constrained" doesn't mean "limited"

---

### The Nordson Children (Leo & Junior)

**Species:** Gojira hybrid
**Classification:** Holariston Polykráton Krator (Nr+Em+Ps)

#### The Third Path

Unlike their parents:
- **Godzilla:** Pure Krator, Null
- **Mothra:** Supreme Psychic, Null (for magic)
- **Leo/Junior:** Both Krator AND Psychic

#### Current Status

**Lanthaneia** - seeking their Horme (Conqueror's Desire)

Their integration of systems remains incomplete:
- Cannot fuse Pyrenikon and Psioniton perpetually
- Viewing psionics as "bad" creates separation
- Potential remains largely untapped

#### Potential

If they achieve integration:
- Atomic energy constructs (impossible for pure Gojira)
- Telekinetic enhancement of Kratonic attacks
- Psionic recycling of waste energy
- Unprecedented hybrid capabilities

## Human Practitioners

### Pre-Divine Mandate

Before Mothra's decree:
- Humans had variable psionic potential
- The Eclipse was a nation of psychics
- Mothra guided their development
- Integration with sorcery was possible

### Post-2019 Reawakening

Mothra's return released a psionic shockwave:
- Dormant abilities began reactivating
- New psychics emerging worldwide
- Keep Charlie established to train them

### Current Students

**The Psionic Division at Keep Charlie includes:**
- Students with newly awakened abilities
- Those with latent potential being developed
- Two special junior students with dual capability
- One prodigal psychic with exceptional aptitude

## Keep Charlie Curriculum

### Psionic Division

**Philosophy:** Intuition guided by structure

**Core Training:**
1. Mental Fortification (defense first)
2. Telepathic communication basics
3. Emotional mastery and stability

## The Quadrivium: Naturals vs. Technicians

### The Intuitives (Naturals)

**Mothra (The Divine):**
- Innate, effortless grace
- Pure unfiltered being
- Power without technique

**Godzilla (The Worker):**
- Instinct + physical repetition
- Hard work applied to intuition
- Not psionic, but intuitive in Kratos

### The Technicians (Architects)

**Battra (The Scholar):**
- Rigorous study + structural perfection
- Knowledge as power
- Applies structure to natural psionics

**Ghidorah (The Engineer):**
- Meticulous deconstruction
- Will through structure
- Disdains psionics philosophically

### Dynamics

| Pairing | Relationship |
|---------|--------------|
| Battra & Mothra | Resentment (her power "unearned") |
| Ghidorah & Mothra | Confounded (she represents Meaning) |

## Cross-System Considerations

### Psionics Within Kratonic Aura

| Ability | Effect |
|---------|--------|
| Telekinesis | Neutralized |
| Projection | Blocked |
| **Telepathy** | **Functional** (unless specifically jammed) |

### The Disdain Club

Battra and Ichi (Ghidorah) share philosophical disdain for psionics:
- Both prefer structured approaches
- Both view intuition as "lesser"
- Both are actually capable of psionic effects
- Neither admits the irony
