## Overview

**Constrained Psychokinesis (CPK)** refers to psionic abilities that require an **elemental medium** for expression. Unlike true telekinesis, CPK users cannot manipulate matter directly—they work through specific elements.

### The Key Limitation

> *CPK users DO NOT generate what they control—they utilize existing environment.*

A hydrokinetic cannot create water; they manipulate water that already exists. A pyrokinetic cannot create fire; they control existing flames or heat sources.

---

## True Psychics vs. Constrained PK

| Aspect | True Psychic | Constrained PK |
|--------|--------------|----------------|
| **Scope** | Any matter | Specific element only |
| **Generation** | Can create effects | Cannot create medium |
| **Dependency** | None | Requires element present |
| **Classification** | Direct manipulation | Indirect manipulation |

---

## Common CPK Types

### Hydrokinesis (Water/Ice)

**Definition:** Psychokinetic control over water in all its forms.

**Capabilities:**
- Move and shape liquid water
- Control ice and snow
- Manipulate humidity and vapor
- Create waves, currents, pressure

**Limitation:** Cannot generate water—requires existing source

**Notable Practitioners:** Scylla, Leviathan, Manda

---

### Pyrokinesis (Fire/Heat)

**Definition:** Psychokinetic control over fire and thermal energy.

**Capabilities:**
- Shape and direct flames
- Control heat intensity
- Expand or suppress fires
- Manipulate thermal currents

**Limitation:** Cannot ignite fire—requires existing flame or heat source

**Notable Practitioners:** Rodan

---

### Geokinesis (Earth/Stone)

**Definition:** Psychokinetic control over earth, stone, and minerals.

**Capabilities:**
- Move rock and soil
- Shape stone formations
- Create tremors and vibrations
- Manipulate sand and dust

**Limitation:** Cannot create earth—requires existing material

**Notable Practitioners:** Baragon

---

### Aerokinesis (Air/Wind)

**Definition:** Psychokinetic control over air currents and atmospheric pressure.

**Capabilities:**
- Generate and direct winds
- Create pressure differentials
- Manipulate sound transmission
- Control local atmosphere

**Note:** Air is ubiquitous, making this one of the least "constrained" CPK types.

## Why CPK Appears as "Elemental Magic"

### The Misconception

Many civilizations interpret CPK as separate magical systems—"fire magic," "water magic," etc. This misconception arises because:

1. Effects appear elemental
2. Practitioners seem specialized
3. No visible "mental" component
4. Different from structured sorcery

### The Truth

CPK is actually **limited psionics** operating through biological constraints:

- The ability IS psionic (Neural Ignition based)
- The limitation is biological (specific element only)
- The expression resembles magic (element manipulation)
- The mechanics are psionic (mind-to-matter through medium)

## Cross-System Combat

### Kratos vs. Psionics

**Disadvantage for Kratons:** Psionics targets Will—the fuel of Kratos.

**However:**
- Immense mental fortitude can resist
- Kratonic Submission can disrupt brain-to-Psiforce connection
- Extreme Flaring creates hostile environment

### The Telepathy Exception

Within Kratonic Aura:
- Telekinesis neutralized
- Projection blocked
- **Telepathy remains functional** (unless specifically jammed)

This makes telepathic communication the most reliable cross-system capability.
