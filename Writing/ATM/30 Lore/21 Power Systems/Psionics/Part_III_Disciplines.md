# Part III: The Five Disciplines

> **Parent:** [[Psionics, the Power of Mind]]
> **The Core Categories of Psionic Ability**

---

## Overview

All psionic abilities fall into **five primary disciplines**, each representing a different application of the mind's interface with Psiforce.

| Discipline | Focus |
|------------|-------|
| **Mental Communication** | Mind-to-mind contact |
| **Mental Fortification** | Psychic defense |
| **Mental Incursion** | Entering other minds |
| **Mental Projection** | Extending consciousness |
| **Extrasensory Perception** | Enhanced sensing |

Additionally, **Telekinesis** (mind-to-matter manipulation) operates as the primary physical expression of psionic power.

---

## Discipline 1: Mental Communication

### Definition

The ability to establish direct mind-to-mind contact for the purpose of communication.

### Sub-Categories

| Type | Function |
|------|----------|
| **Telepathy (Active)** | Can initiate mental contact |
| **Telepathy (Passive)** | Can only receive, not initiate |
| **Empathy** | Sensing emotions rather than thoughts |
| **Mental Speech** | Direct thought transmission |

### Telepathy Classification

| Type | Capability |
|------|------------|
| **Active Telepath** | Can initiate contact with any receptive mind |
| **Passive Telepath** | Can only receive contact initiated by others |
| **Selective** | Can choose who to contact |
| **Broadcast** | Projects to all minds in range |

### Ethical Considerations

Telepathy carries unique moral weight:
- Contact without consent is considered violation
- Reading private thoughts is intrusion
- Communication telepathy is generally acceptable
- Deep mind-reading requires strong justification

---

## Discipline 2: Mental Fortification

### Definition

The ability to defend one's mind against psionic intrusion and external mental influence.

### Applications

| Application | Function |
|-------------|----------|
| **Mental Shields** | Blocking telepathic intrusion |
| **Thought Guards** | Protecting specific thoughts/memories |
| **Emotional Barriers** | Preventing empathic reading |
| **Psychic Resistance** | General defense against mental attack |

### Importance

Mental Fortification is often the **first discipline taught** because:
- Protects the developing psychic
- Provides foundation for other abilities
- Essential for maintaining mental integrity
- Prevents accidental thought leakage

---

## Discipline 3: Mental Incursion

### Definition

The ability to enter, explore, and potentially influence other minds.

### Applications

| Application | Function |
|-------------|----------|
| **Mind Reading** | Accessing surface thoughts |
| **Memory Diving** | Exploring memories |
| **Mental Influence** | Affecting thoughts/emotions |
| **Mind Control** | Dominating another's will |

### Ethical Spectrum

Mental Incursion exists on a spectrum from acceptable to forbidden:

| Level | Description | Ethics |
|-------|-------------|--------|
| Surface Reading | Sensing immediate thoughts | Contextual |
| Deep Reading | Accessing hidden thoughts | Questionable |
| Memory Access | Exploring past experiences | Rarely justified |
| Influence | Subtly affecting decisions | Problematic |
| Control | Overriding free will | Forbidden |

### The Mothra Standard

Mothra, as the supreme psychic and moral authority, uses Mental Incursion only under extreme circumstances—establishing the ethical standard for psychic practitioners.

---

## Discipline 4: Mental Projection

### Definition

The ability to extend one's consciousness beyond the physical body.

### Applications

| Application | Function |
|-------------|----------|
| **Astral Projection** | Separating consciousness from body |
| **Remote Viewing** | Perceiving distant locations |
| **Dream Walking** | Entering others' dreams |
| **Consciousness Transfer** | Moving awareness to other forms |

### Risks

Mental Projection carries unique dangers:
- Body vulnerability during projection
- Potential for consciousness loss
- Risk of encountering hostile entities
- Difficulty returning under stress

---

## Discipline 5: Extrasensory Perception

### Definition

Enhanced or expanded sensory capabilities beyond normal physical limits.

### Applications

| Application | Function |
|-------------|----------|
| **Clairvoyance** | Seeing beyond normal sight |
| **Clairaudience** | Hearing beyond normal range |
| **Psychometry** | Reading objects/locations |
| **Precognition** | Sensing future possibilities |
| **Danger Sense** | Intuitive threat awareness |

### Passive Nature

ESP abilities often function **passively**:
- Operating without conscious activation
- Providing constant background information
- Alerting to significant events
- Enhancing situational awareness

---

## Telekinesis: The Physical Discipline

### Definition

The ability of mind to directly manipulate matter without physical contact.

### The Five Pillars

All telekinetic ability is measured across five dimensions:

| Pillar | Definition |
|--------|------------|
| **Volume** | Amount of matter controllable |
| **Strength** | Force exertable |
| **Stability** | Consistency of control |
| **Fluidity** | Grace and precision of movement |
| **Creativity** | Novelty and ingenuity of application |

### The Critical Dependency

All five pillars hinge on **mental composure**:

> *"A distracted mind cannot control anything."*

Emotional turmoil, mental fatigue, and psychological stress directly impair all telekinetic metrics.

### Applications

| Application | Description |
|-------------|-------------|
| **Object Manipulation** | Moving/lifting physical items |
| **Flight** | Self-levitation |
| **Force Generation** | Creating kinetic impact |
| **Fine Manipulation** | Precise, delicate control |
| **Shield Construction** | Telekinetic barriers |

---

## Discipline Interaction

### Synergies

Different disciplines complement each other:

| Combination | Synergy |
|-------------|---------|
| Communication + Fortification | Secure mental conversation |
| Incursion + ESP | Enhanced mind exploration |
| Projection + Communication | Long-distance telepathy |
| Telekinesis + ESP | Precise remote manipulation |

### Within Kratonic Aura

Psionic abilities behave differently within a Krator's aura:

| Ability | Within Aura |
|---------|-------------|
| Telekinesis | Neutralized |
| Projection | Blocked |
| **Telepathy** | **Remains functional** (unless specifically jammed) |

This exception makes telepathy the most reliable psionic ability against Kratonic opponents.
