## Battra, Lord of the Mystic Arts

### The Greatest Sorcerer in the Universe

**Species:** Divine Moth (Titanus mosura)
**Classification:** Dual practitioner (Magic + Psionics)
**Achievement:** Circumvented the Divine Moth Covenant

### Origin Story

Battra and Mothra emerged from a shared egg as the last of their species. From birth, Battra felt inferior—Mothra had inherited the soul of the Heavenly Instance, making her the "golden child" of their lineage.

The Divine Moth Covenant with Heaven traded magical aptitude for immortality through reincarnation. Battra **modified** this Covenant, regaining Soul Infusion capabilities while maintaining Soul Resonance access—achieving what should be impossible.

### Unique Capabilities

| Capability | Description |
|------------|-------------|
| **Raw Primus Manipulation** | Can halt Soul Infusion to work with unstable Primus |
| **Direct PE Perception** | Sees Primus Energy beneath the Bases |
| **Perfect Optimization** | No trade-offs between spell aspects |
| **98.999986% Clarity** | Near-perfect Primus Ignition perception |
| **0.000001% Excess Rate** | Virtually no energy leakage |
| **1000+ Spell Capacity** | Far beyond normal limits (deliberately underutilized) |
| **Silent Casting Mastery** | 100% effectiveness without verbal components |

### Teaching Philosophy

Battra emphasizes:
- **Fundamental understanding** over standardization
- **Patient development** over quick results
- **Utility-based versatility** over specialized power
- **Personal expression** over mimicry

His goal: *"Earth will not just be the home to the Greatest Sorcerer, it will be the Greatest Home of the Greatest Sorcerers!"*

## Mothra, Divine Instance

### The Paradox of the Divine Teacher

**Species:** Divine Moth (Titanus mosura)
**Classification:** Null (by Covenant), Divine Instance
**Specialty:** Psionics teaching

### The Cosmic Irony

Despite being the Divine Instance—the Heavenly authority on Earth—Mothra is **Null**. She cannot perform Soul Infusion or conventional magic.

Yet she possesses extraordinary capabilities through her divine soul:
- **Divine Mandates** - restructuring reality through Soul Resonance
- **Supreme Psionics** - unmatched mental abilities
- **Soul Agency** - awareness and control even when bodiless

### The Divine Mandate Against Sorcery

When a rogue human tribe violated soul sanctity, Mothra—possessed by divine grief—responded:

1. **Annihilated** the tribe completely (erased from history)
2. **Issued** the Divine Mandate, disabling human Primus perception
3. **Exiled** Battra, making Earth invisible to him

The Mandate rendered humanity Null for thousands of years, causing the fall of Atlantis and the loss of ancient magical knowledge.

### Teaching at Keep Charlie

Despite the history with her brother, Mothra serves as a **guest teacher** for advanced psionic concepts—demonstrating intuitive approaches that complement Battra's structured methodology.

## The Twin Dynamic

### The Original Teachers

Before the Divine Mandate:
- **Godzilla** guarded and guided Atlantis
- **Mothra** guided and guarded the Eclipse
- **Battra** ensured both nations knew the best ways to use sorcery

### The Severed Relationship

The transgression of the rogue tribe shattered this balance:
- Mothra blamed Battra for everything
- Battra was exiled from Earth entirely
- The twins were separated for thousands of years

### Reconciliation (Ongoing)

The lifting of the Divine Mandate and establishment of Keep Charlie represents tentative reconciliation. Both theories suggest the partial lifting is a **test** for Battra—if he proves himself as a responsible teacher, Mothra may fully restore human magical potential.

## Keep Charlie

### The Institution

A mystical educational facility housed within **Castle Bravo** (Monarch's mobile fortress), established by Battra following the Xilien Invasion.

### Structure

| Division | Focus | Lead Teacher |
|----------|-------|--------------|
| **Sorcery Division** | Magic, spellcasting | Battra |
| **Psionic Division** | Mental abilities | Battra (Mothra guest) |
| **Junior Division** | Young students | Various |

### The Current Students

**Sorcery Division:**
- Limited to 10 young human sorcerers
- Carefully selected for aptitude and character
- Training in all four arts of the Fourfold Doctrine

**Special Cases:**
- **Two Junior Students** - possess both psychic and sorcerous abilities
- **One Prodigal Psychic** - exceptional psionic aptitude

### Curriculum Philosophy

Keep Charlie's approach differs from most magical institutions:
- **Visualization first** - foundation for both systems
- **Understanding over memorization** - grasp principles
- **Personal development** - authentic expression
- **Integration awareness** - understanding how systems interact

## Historical Context

### The Golden Age

**Atlantis** - greatest human nation, flourished under Godzilla's protection and Battra's teaching

**The Eclipse** - Mothra's nation, masters of psionics, allies of Atlantis

Both enjoyed unprecedented prosperity through the three Titans' cooperation.

### The Fall

The rogue tribe's soul experiments triggered:
1. Divine judgment—complete annihilation
2. The Divine Mandate—humanity made Null
3. Atlantis's fall—couldn't maintain magical infrastructure
4. Loss of magical knowledge—thousands of years of development gone

### The Modern Era

**2019:** Mothra awakens, releases psionic shockwave reactivating human potential
**2020:** Divine Mandate partially lifted
**Post-Xilien Invasion:** Keep Charlie established
