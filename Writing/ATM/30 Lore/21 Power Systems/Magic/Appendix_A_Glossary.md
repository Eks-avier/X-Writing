# Appendix A: Glossary of Terms

> **Parent:** [[Magic, the Power of Knowledge]]
> **Quick Reference for All Magical Terminology**

---

## Practitioner Classifications

| Term | Definition |
|------|------------|
| **Unnul** | Capable of perceiving and manipulating Primus |
| **Null** | Cannot perceive or manipulate Primus |
| **Sorcerer** | General practitioner of magic |

---

## Fundamental Concepts

| Term | Definition |
|------|------------|
| **Primus** | The fifth fundamental force of nature; raw magical energy |
| **Primus Force** | Primus in its natural, unstable state |
| **Structured Primus** | Primus stabilized through Soul Infusion |
| **Primus Energy (PE)** | Usable magical energy (same as Structured Primus) |
| **Soul** | Immutable, inviolable, eternal essence of sentient beings |
| **Soul Infusion** | Process by which soul transforms Primus into PE |
| **Soul Resonance** | Multi-directional interaction between souls |

---

## Soul Mechanics

| Term | Definition |
|------|------------|
| **Contact Phase** | Soul's energy field touches raw Primus |
| **Harmonization Phase** | Soul creates vibration patterns in Primus |
| **Imprint Phase** | Patterns solidify into structured energy |
| **Identity Signature** | Unique transformation pattern of each soul |
| **Horizontal Pathway** | Soul-to-Soul resonance (all beings) |
| **Vertical Pathway** | Soul-to-Primus manipulation (Unnul only) |

---

## Energy Systems

| Term | Definition |
|------|------------|
| **Reserves** | PE stored within a sorcerer's body |
| **Running Reserves** | Current PE amount at any time |
| **Primus Potential** | Maximum Reserve capacity |
| **Recovery Rate** | Speed of Reserve replenishment |
| **Excess** | PE that escapes and transforms into other energy |
| **Leakage** | Visible magical radiation from abundant Excess |
| **Signature** | Unique Excess pattern identifying a sorcerer |

---

## The Bases (Energy Types)

| Term | Definition |
|------|------------|
| **Cursed Energy (CE)** | Energy from negative emotions/thoughts |
| **Blessed Energy (BE)** | Energy from positive emotions/thoughts |
| **Elemental Energy (EE)** | Energy for classical element manipulation |
| **Physical Energies (PHYSE)** | Real-world energy types (thermal, etc.) |

---

## The Fourfold Doctrine

| Term | Definition |
|------|------------|
| **Art of Spell Casting (ASC)** | Transforming Primus into effects |
| **Art of Commitments (AOC)** | Binding magical structures |
| **Art of Spell Optimization (ASO)** | Refining performance |
| **Art of Spell Keeping (ASK)** | Storing and organizing spells |

---

## Casting Terms

| Term | Definition |
|------|------------|
| **Primus Ignition** | Gathering and converting Primus to PE |
| **Visualization** | Mental construction of desired effects |
| **Articulation** | Verbal/physical/written components |
| **Execution** | Final manifestation into reality |
| **Incantation** | Full-length verbal spell |
| **Chant** | Shortened, optimized verbal spell |
| **Silent Casting** | Magic without verbal components |
| **Gesture** | Physical movement enhancing spell |

---

## Spell Aspects

| Term | Definition |
|------|------------|
| **Speed** | How quickly a spell can be cast |
| **Power** | Raw intensity of effect |
| **Efficiency** | Energy cost relative to effect |
| **Stability** | Resistance to disruption |
| **Complexity** | Sophistication of effect |

---

## Casting Paradigms

| Term | Definition |
|------|------------|
| **Techniques** | Object-oriented, encapsulated frameworks |
| **Traditionals** | Sequential, step-by-step casting |
| **Imperatives** | Intent-driven, outcome-focused |
| **Conditionals** | Trigger-based, automated |
| **Responsives** | Self-adjusting, adaptive |
| **Compositionals** | Layered, synergistic |

---

## Commitments

| Term | Definition |
|------|------------|
| **Commitment** | Binding structure shaping Primus through sacrifice |
| **Contract** | Technical, time-bound enhancement framework |
| **Promise** | Conviction-based declaration |
| **Oath** | Identity-binding allegiance |
| **Vow** | Soul-etched permanent bond |
| **Covenant** | Divine agreement with Heaven |
| **Self-Commitment** | Personal commitment (requires Soul Infusion) |
| **Mutual Commitment** | Agreement between parties |
| **Implicit Commitment** | Unconsciously formed through practice |
| **Explicit Commitment** | Consciously established with clear terms |

## Spell Keeping

| Term | Definition |
|------|------------|
| **Engram** | Complete experiential record of a spell |
| **Repertoire** | A sorcerer's collection of learned spells |
| **Specs** | Foundation spells integral to identity |
| **Dueling Spells** | Combat spells (Offensive/Defensive) |
| **Utilities** | Quality of life spells |
| **Overload** | Death from storing too many spells |
| **Spell Dependency** | Spells serving as foundations for others |
| **Spell Collapse** | Failure when foundation spell is lost |
| **BuYR's Remorse** | Regret over Specs choices |
