# Part IV: The Art of Commitments

> **Parent:** [[Magic, the Power of Knowledge]]
> **The Binding Architecture of Magic**

---

## Overview

**Commitments** are binding magical structures that shape Primus through sacrifices and promises. They represent one pillar of the Fourfold Doctrine and uniquely bridge the gap between magical and non-magical beings through Soul Resonance.

> *"A Commitment is a binding magical structure that shapes Primus through sacrifices and promises. Breaking a Commitment causes Primus to mutate unpredictably, often with catastrophic consequences."*

---

## Fundamental Mechanics

### Core Properties

| Property | Function |
|----------|----------|
| **Soul Interaction** | All operate through Soul Resonance |
| **Sacrifice Requirement** | Concrete sacrifices proportional to power |
| **Divine Oversight** | Heaven witnesses and enforces |
| **Consequence Mechanism** | Predictable severity, unpredictable manifestation |
| **Pattern Stability** | Creates persistent patterns in Primus |

### The Dual Pathway System

#### Horizontal Pathway (Soul-to-Soul)

- Direct resonance between souls
- Heaven serves as witness
- **Available to all beings with souls, including Null**
- Basis for Mutual Commitments

#### Vertical Pathway (Soul-to-Primus)

- Direct soul manipulation of Primus via Soul Infusion
- **Limited to Unnul beings**
- Required for Self-Commitments

## The Five Commitment Types

### 1. Contracts: Technical Frameworks

**Core Mechanics:**
- Shape and stabilize Primus like spell-enhancement frameworks
- Breaking collapses structured magic instantly
- Time-bound with defined beginning and end points

| Aspect | Detail |
|--------|--------|
| Enhancement | 20-50% improvement |
| Duration | Temporary, renewable |
| Null Access | **No** (requires Soul Infusion) |
| Self-Breaking | Loss of bonus effect |
| Mutual-Breaking | As dictated by contract terms |

**Applications:**
- Targeted spell enhancement
- Experimental technique stabilization
- Flexible magical development

> *Example: "I commit to practicing fire manipulation for four hours daily for the next month. In exchange, my fire spells will maintain stability for 30% longer during this period."*

---

### 2. Promises: The Power of Conviction

**Core Mechanics:**
- Based on **conviction** rather than technical precision
- Anyone can make, including Null beings
- Breaking always results in a Curse

| Aspect | Detail |
|--------|--------|
| Enhancement | None (shapes character, not magic) |
| Duration | Until fulfilled or broken |
| Null Access | **Yes** (conviction-based) |
| Self-Breaking | Temporary curse |
| Mutual-Breaking | Permanent curse |

**Unique Properties:**
- A Null's Promise can unintentionally generate magic if conviction is absolute
- Long-lived Null Promises can last generations
- Sorcerers fear fully committed Nulls making Promises

> *Example: "I promise to never use my knowledge to harm an innocent. Should I break this promise, may my power turn against me."*

---

### 3. Oaths: Binding Duty and Identity

**Core Mechanics:**
- Connect individual identity to larger cause or group
- Breaking severs identity connection with severe consequences
- Shape social connections rather than enhance magic

| Aspect | Detail |
|--------|--------|
| Enhancement | None (shapes character, not magic) |
| Duration | Until identity changes or broken |
| Null Access | **Partial** (Mutual only) |
| Self-Breaking | Oathbreaker Status (disables magic and new Commitments) |
| Mutual-Breaking | Collective loss of identity and power |

**Oathbreaker Status:**
- Removes ability to use magic
- Prevents forming new Commitments
- Only removed through redemption or intervention

> *Example: "I swear on my name and power that I shall uphold the principles of Keep Charlie, protecting its knowledge and students with my life and magic."*

---

### 4. Vows: Soul-Etched Permanence

**Core Mechanics:**
- Directly bind to the soul, persisting beyond death
- The strongest personal Commitments
- Can never be undone, only replaced

| Aspect | Detail |
|--------|--------|
| Enhancement | 75-120% improvement |
| Duration | **Eternal** |
| Null Access | **Partial** (Mutual only, requires soul strength) |
| Self-Breaking | Punishment proportional to soul strength |
| Mutual-Breaking | **Guaranteed, unpredictable death** |

**The Element of Permanence:**
- Become integral to sorcerer's identity
- Perfect for establishing foundational technique rules
- Used by masters for most refined techniques

> *Example: "I vow to surrender independent use of Convergence and Coordination in exchange for perfect integration within the Singularity Technique."*

---

### 5. Covenants: The Divine Agreement

**Core Mechanics:**
- Formed only between Mortal and Divine
- Cannot be broken—attempting results in ultimate damnation
- Represent divine-level agreements reshaping fundamental existence

| Aspect | Detail |
|--------|--------|
| Enhancement | **Unlimited** |
| Duration | Eternal, immutable |
| Null Access | **Partial** (can participate if Divine initiates) |
| Breaking | **Impossible** |

**Known Covenant:**
The Divine Moths accepted a Covenant with Heaven, trading their magical aptitude for true immortality through reincarnation.

> *Example: The Titanus mosura Covenant—"In exchange for surrendering our ability to perform Soul Infusion, we gain perfect immortality through reincarnation, our souls eternally returning to new bodies."*

## Case Study: The Godzilla-Mothra Vow

The most powerful Vow in existence, between two **Null beings**:

### Why It Works

Despite being Null, their Vow is unbreakable due to:

1. **Unprecedented Soul Strength** - both possess immense souls
2. **Perfect Harmonic Compatibility** - natural resonance
3. **Divine Interest** - Mothra's divine connection
4. **Absolute Conviction** - unwavering commitment
5. **Explicit Divine Invocation** - formally presented before Heaven

### The Terms

| Domain | Authority |
|--------|-----------|
| **Titan Affairs** | Godzilla rules; Mothra cannot object |
| **Human Affairs** | Mothra rules; Godzilla cannot override |
| **2020 Amendment** | Neither acts for/against humanity without mutual consent |

### Enforcement

The Vow manifests through verbal consent:
- *"I consent!"* - One agrees, other must decide
- *"I do not consent!"* - One blocks the other
- *"We consent!"* - Both agree, action proceeds

When violated, they are **physically prevented** from acting—"chained in place."

## Implicit vs. Explicit Commitments

### Explicit Commitments

Consciously formed with clearly defined terms:
- Deliberate sacrifice articulation
- Specific consequence awareness
- Formal acknowledgment

### Implicit Commitments

Unconsciously formed through practice:
- Shortened chants create implicit Contracts
- Full incantations act as implicit Vows
- Trade effort for effect without recognition

> *Example: When a sorcerer uses a shortened chant instead of a full incantation, they implicitly form a Contract trading reduced casting time for reduced effect.*

---

## Technique Development

### Contracts as Flexible Implementation

- Provide "glue" for spell frameworks
- Handle variable parameters
- Allow evolution and improvement
- Perfect for experimentation

### Vows as Permanent Foundations

- Establish absolute rules (e.g., "cuts anything")
- Create permanent sacrifices for maximum efficiency
- Become integral to identity
- Used by masters for refined techniques

### Layered Implementation

Advanced techniques often use both:
- **Foundation Vow** establishes permanent sacrifice
- **Implementation Contracts** determine situational function
