## Primus: The Fifth Fundamental Force

### Fundamental Nature

**Primus** exists as the fifth fundamental force of nature, alongside gravity, electromagnetism, strong nuclear force, and weak nuclear force. Unlike the stable physical forces, Primus exists in a **perpetually unstable state**, continuously seeking transformation.

### Key Properties

| Property | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| **Omnipresence** | Permeates the entire cosmos |
| **Instability** | Constantly seeks transformation |
| **Responsiveness** | Reacts to soul contact |
| **Structurability** | Can be shaped into stable patterns |

### The Transformation Principle

Raw Primus is **unusable**. When it contacts a sorcerer's soul, it involuntarily transforms into **Structured Primus** (also called **Primus Energy** or **PE**)—its stable, usable form.

**Exception:** Battra alone can halt this transformation, enabling manipulation of Primus in its raw state.

## The Soul: Fundamental Essence

### Core Properties

The soul is **immutable, inviolable, and eternal**:

| Property | Meaning |
|----------|---------|
| **Immutability** | Cannot be fundamentally changed |
| **Inviolability** | Protected from external manipulation |
| **Eternity** | Exists beyond physical death |
| **Identity Foundation** | Houses complete essence of being |
| **Primus Interface** | Enables magical manipulation |

### The Soul's Imperceptibility

A profound paradox of magic: **sorcerers cannot perceive their own souls**.

- Cannot be seen, measured, or directly observed
- Work with and through souls without experiencing them
- Like painting with an invisible brush, knowing only by results

**Exceptions:**
- **Divine Moths** - awareness after death (no agency)
- **Mothra** - full agency via Heavenly Instance
- **Battra** - marginally greater awareness (slight qualitative sense)

## Soul Infusion: The Transformation Process

### The Three Phases

When a sorcerer initiates spellcasting:

**1. Contact Phase**
- Soul's energy field touches raw Primus
- Occurs at the moment of Primus Ignition

**2. Harmonization Phase**
- Soul's unique frequency creates vibration patterns in Primus
- Patterns reflect the soul's individual nature

**3. Imprint Phase**
- Vibrations solidify into structured patterns
- Patterns mirror the soul's identity

The entire process occurs in **microseconds**.

### Identity Signatures

Every soul produces a **unique transformation pattern**:

- No two sorcerers structure Primus identically
- Creates an "identity signature" in the structured energy
- Explains why same spells vary between casters
- Signatures evolve with experience and emotion

## Soul Resonance: The Horizontal Pathway

### Core Concept

**Soul Resonance** is multi-directional interaction between souls that creates patterns Primus naturally conforms to—**without requiring direct manipulation**.

This explains how **Null beings** can participate in Commitments.

### The Resonance Spectrum

1. **Passive Resonance** - natural between souls in proximity
2. **Conscious Resonance** - deliberately established
3. **Structured Resonance** - formalized patterns
4. **Divine Resonance** - connecting mortal to divine

### The Dual Pathways

| Pathway | Mechanism | Accessibility |
|---------|-----------|---------------|
| **Horizontal** (Soul-to-Soul) | Direct resonance, Heaven as witness | All beings with souls |
| **Vertical** (Soul-to-Primus) | Direct manipulation via Soul Infusion | Unnul beings only |

## Primus Energy Systems

### The Energy Hierarchy

```
                    RAW PRIMUS (unstable)
                         ↓
                  SOUL INFUSION
                         ↓
               PRIMUS ENERGY (PE)
                         ↓
    ┌──────────┬──────────┬──────────┬──────────┐
    ↓          ↓          ↓          ↓          ↓
 Cursed    Blessed   Elemental  Physical   (Others)
 Energy    Energy    Energy     Energy
```

### The Bases (Apparent Foundation)

Most civilizations recognize four fundamental energy types:

**1. Cursed Energy (CE)**
- Source: Negative emotions, thoughts, actions
- Applications: Entropy magic, curses, disruption

**2. Blessed Energy (BE)**
- Source: Positive emotions, thoughts, actions
- Applications: Healing, harmony, protection

**3. Elemental Energy (EE)**
- Purpose: Manipulating classical elements
- Applications: Fire, Water, Earth, Air, Aether

**4. Physical Energies (PHYSE)**
- Scope: All real-world energy types
- Applications: Direct physics-based magic

### The Hidden Foundation

**Primus Energy** exists beneath the Bases, but most civilizations lack the knowledge to perceive it directly. Only advanced practitioners like Battra understand PE's existence.

## Energy Storage & Management

### Reserves System

| Term | Definition |
|------|------------|
| **Reserves** | PE stored within sorcerer's body |
| **Running Reserves** | Current PE amount at any time |
| **Primus Potential** | Maximum Reserve capacity |
| **Recovery Rate** | Speed of Reserve replenishment |

### The Excess System

**Excess** = PE that escapes the body and transforms into other energy types.

| Aspect | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Leakage** | Visible magical "radiation" when abundant |
| **Signatures** | Unique patterns identifying individuals |
| **Detection** | Primary method of sensing magical activity |
| **Overload Risk** | Dangerous excess from overstoring |

## Null Classification

### What Makes a Being Null

**Null beings** cannot perform Soul Infusion—their souls fundamentally lack the capacity to structure Primus. When Null souls contact Primus, no transformation occurs.

### Notable Null Beings

- **Titanus Gojira** (Godzilla's species) - inherently Null
- **Humans** (post-Divine Mandate) - forcibly made Null
- **Divine Moths** (by Covenant) - traded magical aptitude

### Null Capabilities

Despite being Null:
- Can participate in **Mutual Commitments** via Soul Resonance
- Strong souls can create powerful binding agreements
- Example: The Godzilla-Mothra Vow

## The Soul's Optimization Function

### Perfect Memory

The soul contains **everything a person was and is**, without conscious memory's limitations. During spellcasting, the soul automatically:

- Tightens spell structure
- Fills in gaps
- Corrects errors
- Contributes accumulated knowledge

### Skill Level Effects

| Level | Soul Contribution |
|-------|-------------------|
| Novice | None beyond basic function |
| Intermediate | Minor enhancements |
| Master | Significant power boosts |
| Battra | Conscious collaboration with soul |
