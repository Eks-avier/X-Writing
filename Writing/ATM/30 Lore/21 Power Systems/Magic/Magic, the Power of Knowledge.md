## Overview

**Magic** (also called **Sorcery**) is the power system of **structured transformation through understanding**. Unlike Kratos (willpower made manifest) or Psionics (mind directly affecting reality), Magic operates through the **Soul's interaction with Primus**, the fifth fundamental force of nature.

### The Universal Principle

> *"I know, therefore I shape."*

When a sorcerer casts a spell, their soul transforms the inherently unstable Primus into structured magical energy. This transformation is **personal and unique**—each soul shapes Primus according to its individual nature, making magic an inherently personal expression of knowledge and technique.

### What Makes Magic Unique

- **Requires Soul Infusion** - the soul transforms Primus into usable energy
- **Structure-Dependent** - success depends on knowledge, optimization, and technique
- **Infinite Subdivisions** - schools, specializations, and approaches abound
- **Enhancement Through Commitment** - Contracts, Vows, and Oaths amplify effects

## Key Concepts

### Soul Infusion

The automatic, involuntary process by which a soul transforms raw Primus into Structured Primus:

1. **Contact Phase** - soul touches Primus
2. **Harmonization Phase** - soul creates vibration patterns
3. **Imprint Phase** - patterns solidify into structured energy

### Soul Resonance

Multi-directional interaction between souls that creates patterns Primus naturally conforms to:

- **Horizontal Pathway** (Soul-to-Soul) - enables Commitments for all beings
- **Vertical Pathway** (Soul-to-Primus) - requires Soul Infusion capability

### The Divine Mandate Against Sorcery

Mothra's divine decree that rendered humanity Null for thousands of years:

- Issued after a rogue tribe violated the sanctity of souls
- Disabled human perception of Primus
- Caused the fall of Atlantis
- Recently lifted, enabling human magic to return
