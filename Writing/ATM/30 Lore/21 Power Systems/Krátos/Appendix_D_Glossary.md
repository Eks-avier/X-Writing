| Term | Greek | Meaning |
|------|-------|---------|
| **Krátos** | κράτος | Power/strength; the power system of willpower made manifest |

## Quick Reference: Most Common Terms

**For Practitioners:**
- Kráton = can use Kratos
- Koinon = standard user
- Krator = Conqueror

**For Techniques:**
- Epichrisis = Coating
- Stereosis = Fortification
- Diatresis = Penetration
- Anapsis = Flaring
- Proskynesis = Submission

**For States:**
- Symphonia = aligned (full power)
- Diaphonia = misaligned (weakened)
- Kataklisis = in repose (minimal power)
- Lanthaneia = latent (awaiting Horme)
