## The Psionic System (Brief)

### Core Distinction from Kratos

| Aspect | Kratos | Psionics |
|--------|--------|----------|
| Philosophy | Ontology (Being) | Axiology (Feeling/Values) |
| Source | Willpower | Emotional/Mental state |
| Expression | Direct physical manifestation | Mind-mediated effects |
| Stability | Requires psychological alignment | Requires emotional stability |

### The Five Disciplines

1. **Mental Communication:** Telepathy and empathy
2. **Mental Fortification:** Psychic defense and barriers
3. **Mental Incursion:** Entering/affecting other minds
4. **Mental Projection:** Astral projection and remote viewing
5. **Extrasensory Perception:** Enhanced/expanded senses

## True Psychics vs. Constrained PK

### True Psychics (Direct)

**Definition:** Unnon whose mind can directly, actively, and independently influence reality without intermediaries.

**Examples:**
- Active Telepathy (mind touches mind)
- Telekinesis (mind touches matter directly)
- Mothra and Battra (ultimate examples)

### Constrained Psychokinesis (Indirect)

**Definition:** Requires elemental medium for expression.

**Examples:**
- Hydrokinesis (Scylla, Leviathan, Manda)
- Pyrokinesis (Rodan)
- Geokinesis (Baragon)

**Key Limitation:** CPK users DO NOT generate what they control - they utilize existing environment.

## The Kratos-Psionics Hybrid

### How Scylla Does It

1. **Krátonic Generation:** Uses Conqueror's Will to generate vast ice quantities
2. **Psionic Shaping:** Uses Hydrokinesis to control/sculpt the generated ice
3. **Result:** Unprecedented creative external manipulation

**Why It Works:** She IS the lake (generation), then controls it (manipulation).

### How Nordson Children Do It

1. **Krátonic Generation:** Generate atomic energy with Gojira will
2. **Psionic Shaping:** Use Telekinesis to form into constructs
3. **Result:** Atomic energy constructs (impossible for pure Gojira)

**Why It Works:** "Their mother's grace gives shape to their father's will."

## Cross-System Combat

### Kratos vs. Psionics

**Disadvantage for Kratons:** Psionics targets Will - the fuel of Kratos.

**However:**
- Immense mental fortitude can resist
- Kratonic Submission can disrupt Brain-to-Psiforce connection
- Extreme Flaring creates hostile environment

### The Telepathy Exception

Within Kratonic Aura:
- Telekinesis neutralized
- Projection blocked
- **Telepathy remains functional** (unless specifically jammed)

## Aging and the Three Systems

### Kratos: War Against Entropy
- Physical vessel power
- Time is existential threat
- Only solution: biological immortality

### Psionics: Transcendence of Form
- Mind power, pure and simple
- Time is **irrelevant**
- Age = advantage (calmer, stabler mind)

### Magic: Accumulation of Wisdom
- Knowledge power
- Time is double-edged (mind gains, body weakens)
