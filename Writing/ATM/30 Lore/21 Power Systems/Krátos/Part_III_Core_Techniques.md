## The Two Domains Framework

**Source:** Eclipse-II P047 (Fixed circular definitions)

Kratonic techniques divide into two distinct domains:

| Domain | Application | Target | Effect |
|--------|-------------|--------|--------|
| **Personal** | Coating (Epichrisis) | Self/Objects | Enhancement |
| **Territorial** | Aura Manipulation | Environment | Control |

This separation is critical - techniques don't bleed into each other.

---

## Personal Coating (Epichrisis)

**Greek:** ἐπίχρισις - "Anointing/coating upon"

The application of one's Aura for direct enhancement of self or objects.

### 1. Fortification (Stereosis)

**Greek:** στερέωσις - "Making solid/firm"

**Function:** Increasing molecular integrity, durability, and resilience.

**Two Applications:**

**Internal Fortification (Sovereign's Vessel):**
- Strengthening one's own body
- Augments muscles, bones, tissues
- "Engine" behind enhanced physical states
- *Godzilla's mastery: Atomic Amplification*

**External Fortification (Sovereign's Touch):**
- Reinforcing external objects through contact
- Making rock hard as diamond
- "Structural Infusion" = common name for this effect
- *Solves the leverage problem for massive objects*

### 2. Neutralization (Exoudeterosis)

**Greek:** ἐξουδετέρωσις - "Making null/neutralizing"

**Function:** Creating a field that dissolves incoming attacks and energy.

**Mechanism:**
- Aura acts as reactive defense
- Incoming force dispersed upon contact
- Effectiveness scales with willpower vs. attack strength

**Effectiveness Spectrum:**
| Will Comparison | Effect |
|-----------------|--------|
| Overwhelming | Complete nullification |
| Superior | Significant reduction |
| Contested | Partial protection |
| Inferior | Minimal protection |

**The Royal Armor:**
- Defensive coating applied to entire body
- Complements biological defenses
- Can be trained for selective application

### 3. Penetration (Diatresis)

**Greek:** διάτρησις - "Piercing through"

**Function:** Imbuing attacks with will to bypass defenses.

**Mechanism:**
- Willpower injected into attack
- Bypasses natural immunities and resistances
- Effectiveness depends on relative willpower

**Effectiveness Spectrum:**
| Will Comparison | Effect |
|-----------------|--------|
| Overwhelming | Complete bypass |
| Superior | Significant penetration |
| Contested | Partial bypass |
| Inferior | Minimal penetration |

**The Sceptered Strike:**
- Offensive coating for attacks
- Koinon: Must consciously apply
- Krator: Automatic, can train for selective use

## Territorial Aura Manipulation

### 4. Flaring (Anapsis)

**Greek:** ἄναψις - "Kindling/lighting up"

**Function:** Intensifying the physical effects within one's Aura.

**Mechanism:**
- Turns up heat, pressure, cold, or radiation within territory
- Physical environmental change, not psychological
- Affects ALL within Aura indiscriminately

**Advanced Application - Magic Displacement:**
- Extreme Flaring can physically displace Primus Force
- Creates "dead zone" for magic
- *This is how Godzilla nullifies spells*
- Not Submission - pure brute-force physics

### 5. Submission (Proskynesis)

**Greek:** προσκύνησις - "Forced prostration/bowing"

**Function:** Attacking opponents' willpower directly within your domain.

**Mechanism:**
- Projects dominating will through Aura
- Targets the "will to resist"
- Psychological AND physiological effects

**Effect Spectrum:**
| Intensity | Effect |
|-----------|--------|
| Mild | Intimidation, uneasiness |
| Moderate | Nausea, weakness, confusion |
| Severe | Collapse, unconsciousness |
| Extreme | Will cessation (including will to exist) |

**Spatial Mechanics:**
- Full effect only within Aura
- Proximity increases intensity
- Exit provides immediate relief
- Severe damage may linger

**Cross-System Disruption:**
- Disrupts Soul-to-Primus connection (Magic fails)
- Interferes with Brain-to-Psiforce interaction (Psionics blocked)
- Cannot affect soul or brain directly

### 6. Expansion (Ektasis)

**Greek:** ἔκτασις - "Spreading out"

**Function:** Purely enlarging Aura radius.

**Distinct from Flaring:**
- Flaring = same area, more intense
- Expansion = more area, same intensity
- Can be combined for both effects

### 7. Suppression (Kryptis)

**Greek:** κρύπτις - "Hiding/concealment"

**Function:** Reducing Aura intensity or range.

**Application:**
- Stealth and concealment
- Reducing collateral damage
- Social situations requiring discretion
- Krator training allows 30-50% minimum

## The Ultimate Sanction: Injection

**Greek:** (Sovereign's Writ)

**Source:** Eclipse-II P032

### Definition

The **hybrid technique** combining Penetration and Neutralization to deliver a devastating internal attack.

### Mechanism

**Two-Step Process:**
1. **Penetration** bypasses target defenses
2. **Neutralization** is injected directly into target

**Result:** Foreign willpower agent destabilizing internal structures from within.

### True Damage Concept

Injection inflicts **ontological decay**:
- Dissolves target from inside out
- Cannot be mitigated by external defenses
- Only counters: avoidance or regeneration faster than decay

### The Vulnerability Gap

Using Neutralization offensively creates a defensive opening:
- Must sacrifice portion of active Neutralization
- Creates temporary vulnerability window
- Gap duration scales with injection intensity

**Strategic Calculation:**
- Is destroying the target worth the exposure?
- High-risk, high-reward technique
- Used when victory is paramount

### Cross-System Applications

**Against Magic:**
- Destabilizes Primus Energy structures
- Causes spell collapse from within

**Against Psionics:**
- Disrupts Psienergy flow
- Technique failure and backlash

**Against Living Beings:**
- Internal Submission effects
- Organ failure, system shutdown

## Contesting (Combat Between Equals)

**Greek:** Antistasis (ἀντίστασις) - "Resistance between equals"

### When Submission Fails

When neither combatant can establish willpower dominance:
- All skills become relevant
- Technique, strategy, experience matter
- Highest stakes combat in the system

### The 98% Rule

Submission achieves decisive victory in 98% of Kratonic combat because willpower disparities are usually clear.

The remaining 2% - true Contesting - is where legends are made.
