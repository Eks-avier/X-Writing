## Overview

**Krátos** (Greek: κράτος, meaning "power/strength") is the power system of **absolute control through pure willpower**. Unlike other supernatural systems that manipulate external forces, Krátos operates on the principle that **willpower manifests directly as physical phenomena**.

### The Universal Principle

> *"Reality has no choice but to comply with a being of sufficient willpower."*

When a Kráton manifests their power, they do not control external forces—their willpower **literally becomes** the force itself. A Nucleon's will explodes like atomic energy; a Pyroton's determination burns like flame; an Electromagneton's conviction strikes like lightning.

### What Makes Krátos Unique

- **No intermediary** between intent and effect
- **Personality directly shapes** manifestation characteristics
- **Genetic determinism** - what you're born with is what you can use
- **Willpower as currency** - everything scales with determination

## Key Concepts

### The Genetic Lock

One's Kratonic classification is **absolute and permanent**:
- Cannot be lost through disuse, injury, or disease
- Cannot be gained through training or bestowal
- Sole exception: direct intervention by Heaven

### The Horme Requirement (Krators Only)

**Horme** (ὁρμή) = The Conqueror's Desire

A primal, instinctive drive that powers all decisions:
- Must be personally discovered
- Forms core of existence
- Requires perpetual psychological alignment

### The Sculptor vs. Painter Philosophy

The defining framework for understanding different Kratonic approaches:

**Sculptor (Godzilla):** *"I AM the weapon."*
- Power focused inward
- Body IS the art
- Self-refinement philosophy

**Painter (Ghidorah):** *"I FORGE the weapon."*
- Power focused outward
- Creates tools and constructs
- Environmental projection philosophy

### Fatum Victor (Conqueror of Fate)

The meta-narrative truth: Krators are born with an existential need to **shatter the chains of their own predetermined destiny**.

- **Godzilla** conquers solitude
- **Ghidorah** conquers meaninglessness
- **Scylla** conquers collectivism
