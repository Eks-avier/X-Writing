## The Two Edicts

The Royal Court Framework organizes Kratonic techniques into two fundamental "Edicts" - the dials that control all manifestation.

### Edict of Dominion (Spatial Management)

**Controls:** Aura SIZE

| Technique | Effect |
|-----------|--------|
| **Expansion** (Ektasis) | Increase territory |
| **Reduction** (Systole) | Decrease territory |

**Trade-offs:**
- Larger territory = thinner concentration
- Smaller territory = denser power
- Expansion costs stamina
- Reduction can conserve energy

### Edict of Majesty (Intensity Management)

**Controls:** Aura INTENSITY

| Technique | Effect |
|-----------|--------|
| **Flaring** (Anapsis) | Increase intensity |
| **Suppression** (Kryptis) | Decrease intensity |

**Trade-offs:**
- Higher intensity = faster stamina drain
- Lower intensity = reduced effectiveness
- Flaring announces presence
- Suppression enables stealth

## The Royal Court Mechanic

### Allocation Points System

Every Kráton has a finite "budget" of willpower that can be distributed across:

1. **Personal Coating** (Fortification, Neutralization, Penetration)
2. **Territorial Control** (Flaring, Submission, Expansion)
3. **Active Techniques** (Manifestation-specific abilities)

**The Trade-off Principle:**
- Investing in defense (Neutralization) means less offense (Penetration)
- Investing in territory (Expansion) means less personal enhancement
- No Kráton can maximize everything simultaneously

### Koinon Allocation (Single-Core)

Standard practitioners must **consciously choose** allocation:
- Like a single-core processor
- Can focus on offense OR defense OR territory
- Switching takes conscious effort
- Most effective when specialized

### Krator Allocation (Dual-Core)

Conquerors have a fundamental advantage:
- First Layer handles Submission automatically
- Conscious mind freed for combat
- Like dual-core processing
- Can attack AND dominate territory simultaneously

**This is why Krators are so dangerous** - they're always doing two things at once.

## Personal vs. Territorial Domain

### The Critical Separation

**Source:** Eclipse-II P047

Early system drafts had circular logic problems. The fix: cleanly separate domains.

**Personal Domain:**
- Coating techniques (Epichrisis)
- Applied to SELF and ATTACKS
- Requires contact or direct projection
- Neutralization, Penetration, Fortification

**Territorial Domain:**
- Aura manipulation techniques
- Applied to ENVIRONMENT
- Affects everything within Aura
- Flaring, Submission, Expansion

**Key Rule:** Submission is NOT derived from Neutralization. They are parallel techniques in different domains.

## The Dual-Layer Aura System

### First Layer: Royal Presence

**Nature:** Passive, unconscious, always-on

| Aspect | Detail |
|--------|--------|
| Activation | Birth |
| Control | None possible |
| Effect | Fear, dread, unease |
| Persistence | Even during Repose |
| Range | Personal proximity |

**The Mark:** Other beings feel "wrongness" - a primal recognition of predator. This is why Conqueror children are so unsettling even before manifesting.

### Second Layer: Sovereign Domain

**Nature:** Active, conscious, alignment-dependent

| Aspect | Detail |
|--------|--------|
| Activation | Symphonia required |
| Control | Full conscious control |
| Effect | Active power manifestation |
| Persistence | Ceases during Repose |
| Range | Defined Aura radius |

**The Domain:** This is where actual Kratonic techniques operate. Everything from Flaring to Submission to Injection requires Second Layer function.

### State Dependencies

| State | First Layer | Second Layer |
|-------|-------------|--------------|
| Symphonia | Active | Active (full power) |
| Diaphonia | Active | Weakened |
| Repose | Active | Disabled |
| Lanthaneia | Active | Unavailable |

## Efficiency and the Privilege Tax

### The Cost of Power

Krators experience passive energy drain from enhanced capabilities:

**Combat Tax:**
- Becomes apparent during confrontations
- Dual-core processing costs energy
- More powerful = more expensive to run

**Biological Adaptation:**
- Species biology evolves to handle baseline costs
- Gojira naturally sustain nuclear reactions
- Ice Weavers sustain cryo-generation

**Conditioning Effect:**
- Regular use builds efficiency
- Like physical training
- Disuse leads to "atrophy"

### Learning Efficiency

A major part of Kratonic growth is learning to:
- Minimize wasteful allocation
- Switch between modes smoothly
- Sustain optimal states longer
- Recover faster between exertions

*Godzilla's post-Battra efficiency* is legendary - he learned to operate at 100% Amplification with sustainable cost.

## Strategic Implications

### Combat Positioning

**Close Range:** Submission strongest, Personal Coating most effective
**Mid Range:** Balance of options, most tactical depth
**Long Range:** Territory expansion necessary, Injection effectiveness drops

### Resource Management

**Burst Strategy:** Maximum Flaring for quick victory, risk exhaustion
**Attrition Strategy:** Sustainable output, wait for opponent's mistake
**Control Strategy:** Expanded territory, suppress opponent options

### The Opening Move

Most Kratonic combat is decided by initial Aura clash:
- Stronger will establishes dominance
- Weaker will must adapt or flee
- Equal wills enter Contesting
