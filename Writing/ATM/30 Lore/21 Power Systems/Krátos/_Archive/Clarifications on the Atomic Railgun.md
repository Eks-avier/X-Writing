Let's circle back to the Atomic Railgun.

When it is used, I envision these different effects at different points of the attack:
1. The charge-up produces an aura that's lethal to organic life (concentrating atomic energy in one spot)
2. The release of attack; a thin beam slices through the air. 
3. A shockwave occurs at the area around Godric beginning from his finger. 
4. A massive shockwave occurs along the line the beam traveled in.

We've seemed to be alluding that the Atomic Railgun is a stealth weapon. It isn't 😂

It's far more destructive than an atomic breath. And while he can certainly use it more deliberately now post-invasion, it still has massive environmental damage if it is used. He's currently finding a way to minimize the damage while keeping the output of the Railgun high enough to be lethal. In other words, he's practicing how to modulate it.

Which is no matter, really. He's found so much fun using his physical prowess and Atomic Amplification, which he can now use with supreme ease post-invasion (since he had it turned on the entire event 😂)

The thing about the "flow state" or the "zone state" is that it occurs for him just as much as it occurs for regular people, he can't just enter it willy-nilly: he has to *burn away* his mental state. In essence, he has to lock in first 😂

So, in conclusion: 
- Godric reserves the Railgun for kills or dire situations 
- It is *not* a stealth tool
- He would much rather use his Amplification + hand-to-hand
- He'd prefer his trusty atomic breath, which we should talk about it next (the ol' reliable), if he needs a ranged attack, which is increasingly getting rare as he becomes more proficient in close quarters combat
- The minimum a Railgun can be fired while still being lethal is 25%.
- The minimum charge time is 5 seconds, down from 10
