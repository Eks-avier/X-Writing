## Aura: The Sovereign's Territory

### Definition

**Aura** (Αὔρα) is the passive emission of a Kráton's manifestation in a spherical field around them. It represents their **territorial domain** - the space where their will pervades.

### Latent State

Aura exists as a defined spherical field around the Kráton:
- **Radius** determined by willpower strength
- **Shape** is spherical (three-dimensional), not circular
- **Content** reflects the Kráton's specific phenomena

### Irrepressibility

Aura **cannot be completely turned off**, even through conscious effort:
- Production continues as long as the "will to act" exists
- Can only be reduced to personal thresholds
- Suppression requires constant willpower expenditure
- Complete cessation only occurs at death

### Two-Layer Structure (Krator Only)

**First Layer - Royal Presence:**
- Always active from birth
- Creates fear, uneasiness, dread in others
- Cannot be trained away
- Functions even during Repose

**Second Layer - Sovereign Domain:**
- Requires Will-Desire Alignment (Symphonia)
- Active manifestation of power
- Can be suppressed with training (to 30-50% minimum)
- Disabled during Repose

## Willpower as Currency

### The Universal Mediator

Willpower is the **fundamental driver** of all Kratonic expression:
- Determines Aura size and intensity
- Powers all techniques
- Measures combat potential
- Scales with physical and mental conditioning

### The Willpower Equation

**Power Output = Willpower × Physical Fitness × Psychological State**

For Krators, add: **× Horme Alignment**

### Will-Sensing

All Krátons possess the universal ability to:
- **Detect** willpower in sentient beings
- **Gauge** determination and strength
- **Range:** ~2x their Aura radius
- **Ancient beings** (Godzilla, Ghidorah) have colossal ranges

### Will-Perception

Beyond detection, Krátons can:
- Process and interpret will-sensing data
- Recognize other Krátons across species
- Distinguish Conquerors from standard practitioners

## Kráton vs. Akráton

### Absolute Classification

- **KRÁTON** (κράτων): Capable of Krátos
- **AKRÁTON** (ἀκράτων): Incapable of Krátos

This classification is **permanent and unchangeable**:
- Cannot be lost through disuse, injury, or disease
- Cannot be gained through training or bestowal
- Sole exception: direct intervention by Heaven itself

### Distribution Levels

| Level | Can Vary? | Description |
|-------|-----------|-------------|
| SPECIES | Yes | Entire species can be either Kráton or Akráton |
| LINEAGE | Yes | Lineages within species can differ |
| INDIVIDUAL | Yes | Individuals within lineages can differ |

## Koinon vs. Krator

### The Standard Practitioner: Koinon

**Greek:** KOINOS KRÁTON (κοῖνος κράτων) - "Common Wielder"

**Foundation:** Themelion (θεμέλιον) = Will + Resolve

**Characteristics:**
- **Guaranteed baseline:** Power never completely fails
- **Variable strength:** Can be strong or weak
- **No maintenance:** No complex psychological requirements
- **Simple progression:** Direct willpower-to-manifestation conversion

**Aura Control:**
- Must consciously activate (Flare) their Aura
- Manual control of all techniques
- Consistent function regardless of emotional state

### The Conqueror: Krator

**Greek:** KRÁTOR (κράτωρ) - "Ruler/Conqueror"

**Foundation:** Kratoros Thelesis (κράτορος θέλησις) + Horme (ὁρμή)

**Characteristics:**
- **Rare genetic variant:** Enhanced baseline capabilities
- **World-altering power:** But volatile and conditional
- **Psychological dependency:** Requires Horme alignment
- **Instinctive recognition:** Between fellow Conquerors

**Aura Control:**
- First Layer always active (subconscious)
- Second Layer requires conscious maintenance
- Automatic Penetration on all attacks
- Can learn selective suppression

### The Horme Requirement

**Horme** (ὁρμή) = The Conqueror's Desire

This is the primal, instinctive drive that powers all decisions:
- **Not inherited:** Must be personally discovered
- **Identity-defining:** Forms core of existence
- **Primal quality:** Operates below conscious thought
- **Perpetual focus:** Maintained across millions of years

**Known Horme:**
| Conqueror | Horme |
|-----------|-------|
| Godzilla | Survival and protection (evolved to family) |
| Ghidorah | Eternal domination and destruction |
| Scylla | Liberation and universal recognition |
| Dagon | Protection and happiness of his son |

## Conqueror States

### Lanthaneia (Latency)

**Greek:** Λανθάνεια - "Lying hidden"

- **Condition:** Kratoros Thelesis present, Horme undiscovered
- **Power:** First-layer Aura active, but no phenomena control
- **Status:** "Uncrowned royalty"
- **Examples:** Junior and Leo currently

Can temporarily manifest through strong childish desires, but unstable.

### Symphonia (Alignment)

**Greek:** Συμφωνία - "Harmony"

- **Condition:** Perfect harmony between Will and Desire
- **Power:** Full manifestation capability
- **Status:** True Conqueror in full glory

Requires sustained psychological coherence with core identity.

### Diaphonia (Misalignment)

**Greek:** Διαφωνία - "Discord"

- **Trigger:** Will-Desire conflict from identity changes
- **Effect:** Gradual power reduction (10-98%)
- **Duration:** Months to years
- **Recovery:** Renewed commitment or new Horme

### Kataklisis (Repose)

**Greek:** Κατάκλισις - "Gradual decline to rest"

- **Trigger:** Complete Will-Desire contradiction
- **Effect:** Second Layer Aura ceases entirely
- **Status:** Genetic potential preserved, active power lost
- **Recovery:** Requires new Horme crystallization

## The Triad of Sovereignty

**Source:** Eclipse-II P023

A Kráton's power output is a **multiplicative product** of three pillars. Weakness in one compromises the entire system.

**Formula:** Power = (Lineage) × (Will) × (Vessel)

### Pillar I: Royal Lineage

**Concept:** "The Divine Right to Rule"

- Unalterable genetic foundation
- Determines Genetic Lock classification
- **Question:** "Are you permitted to rule?"

### Pillar II: Sovereign Will

**Concept:** "The Conviction to Rule"

- Core willpower (Thelesis)
- Psychological coherence
- Krator alignment with Horme
- **Question:** "Do you have the conviction to rule?"

### Pillar III: Royal Vessel

**Concept:** "The Strength to Rule"

- Physical fitness and health
- Power efficiency and output
- Stamina and recovery
- **Question:** "Do you have the strength to rule?"

## The Pure Being Philosophy

**Source:** Eclipse-II P021

### Krátos as Ontology

Among the three systems, Krátos represents **Pure Being** - the most visceral, immediate, here-and-now power.

- Magic requires study, preparation, incantation
- Psionics requires emotional attunement, mental focus
- Krátos requires only **existence with sufficient will**

### The Immediacy Principle

When a Kráton wills something:
- Their will **is** the manifestation
- No intermediate step between intent and effect
- Reality doesn't "obey" - it has no choice

### Personality Physics

Because will and power are identical:
- Psychological traits directly shape energy
- Stubborn personality = stubborn energy
- Changing your power requires changing your self

This creates the deepest integration of character and capability in any power system.
