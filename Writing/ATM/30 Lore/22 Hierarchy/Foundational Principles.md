# Foundational Principles

> **Parent:** [[The Titan Hierarchy]]
> **Status:** Authoritative Reference
> **Sources:** Standing P291-P294

The Titan Hierarchy is not a simple power ranking. It is a sophisticated geopolitical framework built on **three independent axes** and governed by strict succession rules that ensure meritocracy over bloodline.

---

## I. The Three-Axis Classification System

Every Titan is classified along **three independent dimensions**. These axes do not determine each other.

### Axis 1: Lineage (Biological Impact)

Classifies species by their **planetary-scale influence**.

| Classification | Definition | Examples |
|----------------|------------|----------|
| **Ascendant** | Species capable of **global, extinction-level** impact | Titanus gojira, Titanus mosura, Titanus leviathan |
| **Emergent** | Species with **regional or local** influence | Ice Weavers, Titanus rodan, Titanus kong |

**Key Principle:** Lineage is biological destiny, not political destiny. An Ascendant can be a Denizen. An Emergent can be an Alpha.

**See:** [[The Lineage System]], [[Ascendant Classification]]

---

### Axis 2: Sapience (Cognitive Capacity)

Classifies beings by their **capacity for reasoning and governance**.

| Classification | Cognitive Capability | Political Participation |
|----------------|---------------------|------------------------|
| **Sapient** | Full self-awareness, abstract thought, political reasoning | Court of the Sapient - **Any rank** |
| **Semi-Sapient** | Limited reasoning, instinct-dominant | Court of the Sapient - **Capped at Territorial** |
| **Non-Sapient** | Instinct-driven, no political capacity | Court of the Primal - Ecological classification only |

**The Sapience Ceiling:**
- Semi-Sapients can hold: Territorial, Vassal, Denizen
- Semi-Sapients CANNOT hold: Municipal and above
- This is a **cognitive** limitation, not a power limitation

A Semi-Sapient may be *stronger* than a Regional Guardian but cannot *govern* a region because governance requires:
- Complex political negotiation
- Multi-species diplomacy
- Long-term strategic planning
- Ethical judgment beyond territorial defense

---

### Axis 3: Standing (Political Position)

Classifies beings by their **earned position** within the hierarchy.

This is the only axis that is **mutable** and **non-inheritable**. Standing must be earned through merit, combat, or appointment.

**See:** [[The Standing System]]

---

## II. The Two Courts Framework

The hierarchy divides into two parallel systems based on **Sapience**.

### The Court of the Sapient

**Membership:** All Sapient AND Semi-Sapient beings
**Nature:** Political, administrative, military chain of command
**Governance:** Active participation through titles and territorial mandates

| Rank | Sapient | Semi-Sapient |
|------|---------|--------------|
| Alpha | Yes | No |
| Beta | Yes | No |
| Warden | Yes | No |
| Regional | Yes | No |
| Provincial | Yes | No |
| Municipal | Yes | No |
| **Territorial** | Yes | **Yes (ceiling)** |
| Vassal | Yes | Yes |
| Denizen | Yes | Yes |

### The Court of the Primal

**Membership:** All Non-Sapient beings
**Nature:** Ecological, not political
**Governance:** **Managed** by Sapient hierarchy, not self-governing

| Classification | Definition | Management |
|----------------|------------|------------|
| **Ascendant Primals** | World-Enders (extinction-level threat) | Highest priority monitoring |
| **Apex Primals** | Regional apex predators | Managed by Wardens |
| **Species Alphas (Primal)** | Pack/herd leaders | Controlled via alpha-targeting |
| **Common Primals** | Ecosystem backbone | Protected for biome health |

**Key Distinction:** Primal classifications are ecological roles, not political ranks. An Ascendant Primal has no vote, no territory, no Standing.

**See:** [[Ascendant Primals]]

## III. Rank vs. Standing

This distinction is fundamental to the hierarchy.

### Rank = The Box

**Definition:** The **administrative category** a Titan occupies.

- Defines scope of authority
- Determines chain of command
- Examples: Alpha Rank, Beta Rank, Warden Rank

### Standing = The Title

**Definition:** The **specific position** within that rank.

- The identity of your office
- May be **unique** (Alpha Paramount) or **categorical** (Cardinal Warden of the North)
- Examples: Alpha Paramount, Lord of the Skies, Palace Custodian

### Key Rules

| Rule | Explanation |
|------|-------------|
| All Standings require a Rank | But not all Ranks require a Standing (Denizens have none) |
| Colloquial names ≠ Standings | "King of the Monsters" is not Godzilla's Standing |
| Standings can be unique or categorical | One Alpha Paramount; multiple Cardinal Wardens |

## IV. Key Succession Rules

The hierarchy is **meritocratic**, not hereditary.

### Rule 1: "Dead Man's Shoes"

**Principle:** Standing is earned, never inherited. Only death frees a title.

- Alpha Paramount passes **only upon death** of the predecessor
- Children of the Paramount are **Denizens** until they earn their own Standing
- There is no "Crown Prince"—only potential

**Example:** Godric Junior cannot "be" Godzilla while Godric lives. Junior is a Denizen despite being an Ascendant hybrid of two royal bloodlines.

### Rule 2: Standing is Positional, Not Familial

Your title defines your authority. Your bloodline does not.

| Being | Lineage | Rank | Why |
|-------|---------|------|-----|
| Junior | Ascendant (Gojira/Mosura) | Denizen | No title earned yet |
| Leo | Ascendant (Gojira/Mosura) | Denizen | No title earned yet |
| Rose | Ascendant (Gojira/Plant) | Denizen | No interest in politics |
| Dagon | Ascendant (Gojira) | Provincial | Appointed Lord Protector |

### Rule 3: Methods of Earning Standing

1. **Alpha Appointment** - The Paramount assigns a title
2. **Combat Victory** - Defeating the current holder
3. **Merit Achievement** - Demonstrating capability
4. **Death of Predecessor** - "Dead Man's Shoes" for unique titles

**Note:** Not all titles are challengeable. Service positions (Palace Custodian, Lord of Mystic Arts) require specific expertise.

## V. The "Powerless Elite" Paradox

Ascendant Denizens create fascinating social tension:

| Dimension | Reality |
|-----------|---------|
| **Legal** | A Territorial Titan outranks Junior |
| **Social** | No one would dare give Junior an order |
| **Practical** | Junior has **influence** without **authority** |

**The Protection:** Being a Denizen actually **protects** the Nordson children. With no Standing to vie for, they cannot be challenged through ritual combat.

---

## VI. Thematic Implications

### Meritocracy Over Bloodline

The Nordson children could be the most powerful beings on the planet, but they hold no authority until they earn it. This makes them **freer** than human royals—no obligation to rule.

### Chain of Command, Not Caste

The hierarchy is **functional**, not moral. A Denizen is not "lesser" as a being—they simply have no administrative role.

### Titan Civilization is a Nation-State

- Court of the Sapient = Government
- Court of the Primal = Wildlife management
- Monarch's role = Diplomatic liaisons + scientific study
