## Overview

The **Titan Hierarchy** is the political and biological framework governing all Titan life on Earth. Unlike human governments built on social contracts, the Titan Hierarchy emerges from the intersection of **raw power**, **cognitive capacity**, and **territorial influence**.

### The Three-Axis Classification

Every Titan is classified along three independent axes:

| Axis | Question | Categories |
|------|----------|------------|
| **Lineage** | *"Can your species change the world?"* | Ascendant / Emergent |
| **Sapience** | *"Can you reason and govern?"* | Sapient / Semi-Sapient / Non-Sapient |
| **Standing** | *"What is your political position?"* | Alpha → Denizen (or Primal track) |

These axes are **independent**. A being can be:
- Ascendant but Non-Sapient (Shimo)
- Emergent but Alpha-ranked (Kong)
- Sapient but Denizen (the Nordson children)

## The Two Courts

The Hierarchy divides into two parallel structures based on **Sapience**:

### Court of the Sapient

The **political hierarchy** for beings capable of governance, negotiation, and abstract reasoning.

| Rank | Scope | Key Standings |
|------|-------|---------------|
| **Alpha** | Planetary/Realmic | Paramount, Divine, Sovereign |
| **Beta** | Global (Executive) | Lord of the Skies, Stalwart Vanguard |
| **Warden** | Hemispheric | Cardinal, Intercardinal |
| **Regional** | Continental/Basin | Guardian of [Territory] |
| **Provincial** | Sub-Continental | Lord Protector, Old Mountain |
| **Municipal** | Ecosystem/Hub | Lord/Mayor of [Location] |
| **Territorial** | Local Niche | Protector of [Location] |
| **Vassal** | Institutional | Palace Custodian, Liminal Sentinel |
| **Denizen** | Self | (Unranked citizenry) |

**See:** [[The Standing System]] for complete rank definitions

### Court of the Primal

The **ecological classification** for beings operating on instinct rather than reason.

| Classification | Scope | Examples |
|----------------|-------|----------|
| **Ascendant Primals** | World-ending | Shimo, Jormungandr |
| **Apex Primals** | Regional domination | Camazotz, Yamata no Orochi |
| **Species Alphas** | Pack leadership | Alpha Sker Buffalo |
| **Common Primals** | Ecosystem backbone | Leafwings, Skullcrawlers |

**See:** [[Ascendant Primals]] for detailed analysis

## The Sapience Ceiling

**Semi-Sapient** beings occupy a unique position:

- They participate in the **Court of the Sapient**
- They are **capped at Territorial rank** and below
- This limitation is **cognitive, not power-based**

A Semi-Sapient Titan may possess strength exceeding a Regional Guardian, but cannot *govern* a region because governance requires:
- Political reasoning and negotiation
- Complex communication with chain of command
- Judgment calls beyond instinctual response

## Quick Reference

### Key Rules

1. **Standing is earned, not inherited** ("Dead Man's Shoes")
2. **Rank ≠ Standing** (Rank = category, Standing = title)
3. **Lineage ≠ Rank** (Ascendants can be Denizens; Emergents can be Alphas)
4. **Sapience determines Court** (Semi-Sapients cap at Territorial)
5. **Special Standings slot in at variable levels**
