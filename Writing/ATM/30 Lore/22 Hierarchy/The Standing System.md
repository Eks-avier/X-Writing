# The Standing System

> **Parent:** [[The Titan Hierarchy]]
> **Status:** Authoritative Reference
> **Sources:** Standing P280-P294

The Standing System is the political hierarchy governing all Sapient and Semi-Sapient Titans. It operates through a **two-axis framework**: Rank (administrative category) and Standing (specific title).

---

## The Complete Rank Structure

### Court of the Sapient

| # | Rank | Scope | Key Standings |
|---|------|-------|---------------|
| 1 | **Alpha** | Planetary/Realmic | Paramount, Divine, Sovereign |
| 2 | **Beta** | Global (Executive) | Lord of the Skies, Stalwart Vanguard |
| 3 | **Warden** | Hemispheric | Cardinal, Intercardinal |
| 4 | **Regional** | Continental/Basin | Guardian of [Territory] |
| 5 | **Provincial** | Major Feature | Lord of [Feature] |
| 6 | **Municipal** | Ecosystem/Hub | Mayor of [Location] |
| 7 | **Territorial** | Local Niche | Protector of [Location] |
| 8 | **Vassal** | Institutional | Service titles |
| 9 | **Denizen** | Self | (None) |

## Rank 1: Alpha (The Global Sovereigns)

**Scope:** Planetary to Realmic
**Nature:** The Triumvirate - three complementary authorities

| Standing | Titan | Domain | Authority |
|----------|-------|--------|-----------|
| **Alpha Paramount** | Godzilla | Global Biosphere | Final word; "Monopoly on Violence" |
| **Alpha Divine** | Mothra | Human Ethics/Spirit | Power of Veto (metaphysical) |
| **Alpha Sovereign** | Kong | Hollow Earth | Power of Challenge (political) |

### Triumvirate Dynamics

- **Paramount:** Can override all, but respects the others
- **Divine:** Absolute veto on human affairs; does not manage Titan territories
- **Sovereign:** Must convince the Paramount; greater autonomy than any Warden

**See:** [[The Alpha Tier]]

---

## Rank 2: Beta (The Global Lieutenants)

**Scope:** Global (jurisdiction-agnostic)
**Nature:** Direct extensions of the Paramount's will

| Standing | Titan | Function | Style |
|----------|-------|----------|-------|
| **Lord of the Skies** | Rodan | Surveillance, Rapid Response | Speed, visibility |
| **Stalwart Vanguard** | Anguirus | Enforcement, Administration | Deliberation, stability |

### Check-and-Balance

- Rodan (speed) and Anguirus (deliberation) mutually check each other
- Together they provide balanced executive action
- **Anguirus speaks for the Betas** in formal settings

### Key Functions

- Inter-hemispheric arbitration when Wardens disagree
- Authority to reassign jurisdiction during crises
- Annual Warden reviews during Godzilla's November hibernation

**See:** [[The Beta Tier]]

## Rank 4: Regional (The Natural Lords)

**Scope:** Continental/Ocean Basin (10-30M sq km)
**Standing:** "Guardian of [Territory]"

**Key Features:**
- Highest rank achievable through **natural strength alone**
- No Alpha appointment required
- Govern local ecosystems: mountain ranges, forests, ocean trenches

---

## Rank 5: Provincial (The Provincial Governors)

**Scope:** Major geographic feature (1-5M sq km)
**Standing:** "Lord of [Feature]" or special titles

**Examples:**
- Mountain ranges (The Andes, The Alps)
- Seas (The Mediterranean)
- Vast deserts (The Gobi)

**Current Provincial Standings:**

| Standing | Titan | Territory |
|----------|-------|-----------|
| Old Mountain | Methuselah | Castle Bravo II, European history |
| Lord Protector | Dagon | Monster Island (the capital) |

---

## Rank 6: Municipal (The Ecosystem Rulers)

**Scope:** Macro-ecosystem/Hub (100k-500k sq km)
**Standing:** "Mayor/Lord of [Ecosystem]"

**Examples:**
- The Nile Delta
- The Great Barrier Reef
- Hollow Earth oases

**Note:** Very prestigious in Hollow Earth where Municipal hubs function as major cities.

---

## Rank 7: Territorial (The Local Chieftains)

**Scope:** Local niche/site (1k-50k sq km)
**Standing:** "Protector of [Location]"

**Examples:**
- Monster Island
- Skull Island
- The Mariana Trench
- Mount Fuji

**Critical:** This is the **ceiling for Semi-Sapient beings**.

---

## Rank 8: Vassal (The Landless Nobles)

**Scope:** Institutional/Functional (not geographic)
**Nature:** Formal title and duty, but no sovereign territory

**Definition:** Titans who hold a position of status in exchange for service. Their "land" is the place where they perform their duty, owned by a higher authority.

**Current Vassal Standings:**

| Standing | Titan | Scope |
|----------|-------|-------|
| Palace Custodian | Barb | Palace interior |
| Liminal Sentinel | Baragon | Vertical tunnels between realms |

---

## Rank 9: Denizen (The Unranked Citizenry)

**Scope:** Self only
**Standing:** None

**Definition:** Sapient Titans with no formal title or territory. They are citizens but not office-holders.

## Special Standings

Positions outside standard ranks for unique planetary-scale needs.

| Standing | Titan | Rank-Equivalent | Domain |
|----------|-------|-----------------|--------|
| **Lord of the Mystic Arts** | Battra | Alpha (Magic only) | Sorcery, Bulwark, Portals |
| **Liminal Sentinel** | Baragon | Warden | Vertical tunnels, border security |
| **Old Mountain** | Methuselah | Provincial | Castle Bravo II, archives |
| **Lord Protector** | Dagon | Provincial | Monster Island capital |
| **Palace Custodian** | Barb | Vassal | Palace interior |

### Variable Authority

Special Standings "slot in" at different levels depending on their domain:
- Battra has Alpha-equivalent authority **only for magic**
- Baragon has Warden-equivalent authority **only for liminal tunnels**
- Outside their domain, they have no special authority

## The Primal Track

Non-Sapient beings are classified ecologically, not politically.

| Classification | Definition | Management |
|----------------|------------|------------|
| **Ascendant Primals** | Global extinction-level threat | Highest priority monitoring |
| **Apex Primals** | Regional apex predators | Managed by Wardens |
| **Species Alphas (Primal)** | Pack/herd leaders | Controlled via alpha-targeting |
| **Common Primals** | Ecosystem backbone | Protected for biome health |

**Key Distinction:** These are ecological roles, not political ranks. Primals have no vote, no territory, no Standing.

**See:** [[Ascendant Primals]]

## Key Rules

### Colloquial Names ≠ Standings

- "King of the Monsters" is a human epithet for Godzilla
- His **official Standing** is Alpha Paramount
- "Queen of the Monsters" is colloquial; her Standing is Alpha Divine

### Unique vs Categorical Standings

- **Unique:** Only one holder (Alpha Paramount, Lord of the Skies)
- **Categorical:** Multiple holders (Cardinal Warden of the [Direction])

### Species Alpha ≠ Formal Standing

A "Species Alpha" is a **social reality**, not a political Standing. It denotes leadership within one's own kind, separate from the hierarchy.

- Godzilla is both Species Alpha and Alpha Paramount
- Scylla is both Species Alpha (Ice Weavers) and Cardinal Warden
