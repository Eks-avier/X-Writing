# Special Standings

> **Parent:** [[The Titan Hierarchy]], [[The Standing System]]
> **Nature:** Administrative Outliers
> **Sources:** Standing P286-P294

Special Standings exist outside the standard Rank structure to address unique, planetary-scale needs that traditional Warden/Beta positions cannot handle. These are **Custom Offices** created by Godzilla to solve specific systemic problems—Titans with "cheat code" abilities that don't fit hemispheric governance.

---

## The Philosophy of Special Positions

Most Titans fit into "boxes" (Ranks). However, Special Positions represent Godzilla's recognition that the world is too complex for Ranks alone. These are **ad-hoc appointments** for individuals possessing unique biological or Kratonic traits too useful to waste on standard territorial roles.

**Key Principle:** Special Standings have **variable authority** that "slots in" at different Rank-equivalencies depending on their domain. Outside their domain, they have no special authority.

## Liminal Sentinel

**Holder:** Baragon
**Rank-Equivalency:** Warden-Level
**Created:** ~4000 years after Warden establishment

### The Problem

Who rules the **verticality**? Godzilla governs Surface. Wardens govern hemispheres. But who monitors the Gateways between realms? Who watches the gravity-inversion zones where Surface and Hollow Earth meet?

### The Solution

**Baragon** - the ultimate "Grounded" character whose species evolved in the crushing dark between realms.

| Aspect | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Domain** | Gateways, tunnels, transition zones |
| **Authority** | Command Regional Titans to block/open gateways |
| **Reporting** | Dual - to both Alpha Paramount AND Alpha Sovereign |
| **Function** | Border patrol of the Titan world |

### Historical Significance

Before Kong's 2022 promotion, Baragon effectively held Kong's position—the only legitimate connection between Surface and Hollow Earth governance.

**Why Created:** Godzilla was burning out. With Rodan/Anguirus acting as proxies, they couldn't monitor tunnels. Mothra wouldn't manage Titans (her Vow). Baragon was Godzilla's first true **delegation of global security**.

### Unique Abilities

| Ability | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Earth-Sense** | Lithokinetic sight - "sees" through stone via vibration |
| **Partial Blindness** | Limited conventional sight, compensated by geomancy |
| **Grounded Movement** | Uses feet and palms as sensory organs |
| **Fire Manipulation** | Control of subterranean heat near magma chambers |

### Relationship with Kong

Baragon is a **Royalist** - his species flourished under the first King of Kongs. When Skar King rose, Baragon's kind were hunted as too useful to the Royal House. He sees Kong's Sovereignty not as replacement but as **Restoration**.

**Current Role:** Kong's advisor/"vizier" - explains unwritten Hollow Earth rules that surface-dwelling Godzilla would never know.

## Lord of the Mystic Arts

**Holder:** Battra
**Rank-Equivalency:** Alpha (Magic domain ONLY) / Lowest (Titan affairs)
**Created:** Post-Xilien Invasion (2024+)

### Historical Context

**Before Exile:** Battra was a Regional Titan for millions of years—by choice. He refused Godzilla's Sovereign offer twice:

| Era | Offer | Refusal Reason |
|-----|-------|----------------|
| Early Permian | First Sovereign offer | "Nepotistic" - wanted to earn position through sorcery |
| Early Mesozoic | Divine-equivalent option | Avoided being "Mothra's shadow" |

He stayed Regional out of **Spiteful Integrity** - better to be a big fish in a small pond than a second-tier god in Maria's ocean.

### The Paradoxical Standing

Post-2024 reinstatement required penance. Battra negotiated his magical expertise into position:

| Dimension | Reality |
|-----------|---------|
| **Rank (Titan Affairs)** | Bottom of hierarchy - must defer to everyone, even Barb |
| **Standing (Magic Affairs)** | Supreme authority - Emperor of Sorcery |

**The Humiliation:** He has no "Standing" in the Titan world. Cannot command a Regional Titan to move a rock.

**The Power:** Absolute authority over the Intangible systems. Father of Human Magic - chooses who learns, defines curriculum, serves as the "Great Filter."

### The Sibling Conflict

Mothra agreed to this title because it forces Battra into a **Mirror Dynamic**:

- **Mothra (Alpha Divine):** Authority over Human Ethics (Axiology)
- **Battra (Lord of Mystic Arts):** Authority over Human Knowledge (Epistemology)

Now they constantly clash in the human world. If Battra teaches a spell (Knowledge) that Mothra deems unethical (Axiology), they conflict. He is her twin again, whether he likes it or not.

### Infrastructure Role

Battra's actual work is the **Lifeblood of Monarch**:
- The Bulwark (planetary defense)
- Web architecture
- Portal networks
- Hollow Earth Gateways
- Castle Bravo II magical enhancements (movement, wards, shields, invisibility)

> *"If Battra goes on strike, Mark can't even get to work."*

## Old Mountain

**Holder:** Methuselah
**Rank-Equivalency:** Provincial (Special Status)
**Age:** 268 million years (oldest living Titan)

### Historical Role

| Era | Position | Note |
|-----|----------|------|
| Early Permian | Born | Predates Godzilla by 18 million years |
| Late Permian | Advisor | Mentored young Godzilla in Titan politics |
| Post-Cenozoic | First Northern Warden | Godzilla's first choice |
| +7,000 years | Resigned | Recommended Leviathan as replacement |
| Modern Era | Regional Titan | Maintained "retirement home" territories |
| 2024+ | Old Mountain | Castle Bravo II maintenance |

### The Mentor of Kings

Methuselah is the only being who can say to Godzilla: *"I remember when you were just a hatchling with the weight of the world on your shoulders, Godric."*

**What He Taught:** Not how to rule (each Paramount must solve that alone) but **Titan Sociology** - the tendencies, histories, and rivalries of every species.

### Current Functions

| Function | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| **Physical Maintenance** | Structural integrity of Castle Bravo II |
| **Waste Processing** | Phytokinetic transmutation - feeds trash to plants |
| **Living Anchor** | Wills stone and soil to hold when stressed |
| **Archive** | Living repository of 268 million years of history |

### The Munich Model

His Germany Outpost recreates his ancient "retirement home" territories for humans:
- Elderly scientists continue working instead of retiring
- Residents experience longevity and quality of life
- Families can visit
- Monarch retains "Grandmaster" talent decades longer

### The Garden Trio

Methuselah works closely with:
- **Behemoth** (Warden of West) - vast American flora
- **Biollante** (hybrid) - scientific innovation, triple-nature flowers

Together they curate the Palace gardens - likely the **Eighth Wonder of the World**.

## Palace Custodian

**Holder:** Barb (Titanus jinshin-mushi)
**Rank-Equivalency:** Lowest Possible (Vassal)
**Created:** Palace opening (2025)

### The Necessity of Protection

A sapient Titan without recognized Standing is **legally vulnerable** - an unmanaged variable. For Barb, daughter of Mother Prime (the being who orchestrated Gojira extinction), existing without a title was a death sentence.

**The Problem:** High-ranking Titans (Scylla, Anguirus) would view killing Barb as honorable "public service."

**The Solution:** The lowest possible Standing that still grants legal protection.

### The Legal Shield

| Aspect | Effect |
|--------|--------|
| **Standing** | Places her in hierarchy, not outside it |
| **Location** | Palace - far from other Titans |
| **Protection** | Harming her now violates Palace Sanctity |
| **Satisfaction** | Other Titans see her humiliated, subservient |

To harm the Custodian is no longer "killing a MUTO" - it is "damaging the King's property" and "disobeying a direct Alpha decree."

### The Debt of Truth

Barb told Godzilla what she knew of his father's final moments. This information - that Dagon didn't abandon him - was priceless.

**The Mutual Exchange:**
- Godzilla/Dagon owe her the **Truth**
- She owes them her **Life**
- She maintains the Palace not because she must, but to repay a 250-million-year debt

### Dagon's Perspective

When Dagon (Darius) was resurrected, seeing Barb in his son's home was a shock but not unwelcome. He remembers the young parasite who listened to his dying words.

> *"He was only a bit shocked that she wasn't hunted down for the 250 million years she existed, but he was glad she survived to tell the truth of everything anyway."*

## Thematic Significance

The Special Standings demonstrate that Godzilla isn't just a brawler - he is an **Organizational Genius**. He identified four Titans with unique, non-duplicable skills and placed them where they maximize planetary efficiency.

**The Pattern:** Each addresses a systemic gap:
- Baragon → Vertical blind spot
- Battra → Magical infrastructure
- Methuselah → Physical maintenance and institutional memory
- Barb → Palace sanctity and a debt of truth
