# CLAUDE.md - Godric's Foundational Nature

## Overview

This folder documents Godric Nordson's personal relationship with his **Holariston Polykrator** nature—his dual-force foundation as both Pyrenikon (nuclear) and Elektromagneton (electromagnetic) wielder. These are not techniques; they are the **ontological bedrock** from which all his abilities derive.
