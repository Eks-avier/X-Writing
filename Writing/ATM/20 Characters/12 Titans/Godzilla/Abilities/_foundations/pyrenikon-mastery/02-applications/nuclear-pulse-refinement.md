# Nuclear Pulse Refinement: Godric's Controlled Devastation

## Summary

The Nuclear Pulse is universal to all *Titanus gojira*—an omnidirectional release of atomic force for emergency situations. What distinguishes Godric is his development of **controlled deployment**, transforming a desperation move into a tactical tool.

---

## Species Baseline

> *For universal Nuclear Pulse mechanics, see [[Pyrenikon#The Nuclear Pulse]] and [[Elektromagneton#The Carrier Wave Principle]].*

The standard gojira Nuclear Pulse:
- Triggered by overwhelming threat or damage
- Indiscriminate—affects everything in radius
- Single-burst deployment (no sustain)
- Significant energy cost
- No radius control

Most gojira treated the Pulse as their "panic button"—devastating but crude.

---

## The EM Carrier Wave

Godric now understands the mechanism his species never did:

| Component | Role |
|-----------|------|
| **Elektromagneton** | Creates expanding magnetic "bubble" |
| **Pyrenikon** | The payload riding the bubble outward |

The EM field is the **delivery system**; the atomic energy is the **destructive force**. This understanding enabled conscious refinement.

---

## Godric's Refinement

### Radius Control

After his [[elektromagneton-awakening/CLAUDE|Elektromagneton awakening]], Godric developed the ability to consciously shape the carrier wave:

| Capability | Application |
|------------|-------------|
| **Tight Radius** | Focused blast for single-target devastation |
| **Standard Radius** | Balanced area denial |
| **Extended Radius** | Wide-area clearing (higher energy cost) |
| **Directional Bias** | Concentrated in chosen direction |

### Output Modulation

Beyond radius, Godric controls intensity:
- Quick "push" pulses for spacing
- Sustained waves forming solid-like barriers
- Full-power detonation for maximum effect

### Tactical Integration

The Pulse became a tactical tool rather than emergency measure:

| Scenario | Refined Application |
|----------|---------------------|
| **Swarm Counter** | Controlled radius to avoid ally damage |
| **Grapple Escape** | Minimum-power push for spacing |
| **Barrier Creation** | Constant waves forming temporary walls |
| **Area Denial** | Sustained output preventing approach |

---

## The Battra Development

During the [[Battra Profile|Battra]] fight in the [[Xilien Invasion Arc|Xilien Invasion]], Godric's Pulse refinement accelerated dramatically:

> *"Controlling the radius of the Nuclear Pulse"*
> *"Emitting constant waves to form solid-like barriers"*
> — AA-Kratos P320

This wasn't gradual improvement—it was forced development under extreme pressure, his [[Limitless Adaptation]] responding to combat demands.

---

## Limitations

Even refined, the Nuclear Pulse carries costs:

- **Energy Expenditure:** Still significant compared to directed attacks
- **Recovery Window:** Brief gap after high-output pulses
- **Ally Consideration:** Must communicate deployment to allies
- **Environmental Impact:** Causes collateral damage regardless of refinement

These limitations make the Pulse a specialized tool, not a primary weapon.
