# CLAUDE.md - Elektromagneton Awakening

## Overview

This folder documents Godric Nordson's personal **awakening** to his [[Elektromagneton]] nature—the hidden half of his power that remained unconscious for 250 million years until recent events forced its revelation.

Unlike his [[pyrenikon-mastery/CLAUDE|Pyrenikon]], which was always conscious, Godric's Elektromagneton journey is about **discovery and integration**—learning that a part of himself existed that he never knew about, and learning to wield it consciously.

## Key Narrative

**The Arc:** Godric possessed Elektromagneton capabilities for 250 million years without knowing it. His journey is not about gaining new power, but about **discovering power he always had**—and learning to wield it consciously.

**Central Revelation:** The enemy taught him strength. [[Barb Profile|Barb]], a turncoat MUTO, explained how gojira EM fields work—knowledge her species evolved to exploit. She pointed the King toward his hidden potential.

**The Crucible:** The fight against [[Battra Profile|Battra]] during the Xilien Invasion forced rapid development. Under pressure, Godric's [[Limitless Adaptation]] integrated his EM nature with his combat abilities.

**The Achievement:** Symphonia—perfect harmony between Pyrenikon and Elektromagneton. Visual marker: Aura without sparks.
