# Rodan Comparison

The Atomic Stride was developed, in part, to address a specific grievance: **Godric was slower than Rodan.**

## The Speed Hierarchy Problem

In a contest of pure speed, agility, and mobility—no teleportation or enhancement "hacks"—Rodan reigns supreme as the **Lord of the Skies**.

This created an uncomfortable truth:
- Godric's mobility ranked **B-** compared to his peers
- Among the Trio (Battra, Godzilla, Mothra), he was the slowest
- Rodan could outmaneuver him in any open engagement

## Settling the Grievance

The Stride doesn't make Godric faster than Rodan. That would miss the point.

What it does:

| Before Stride | After Stride |
|---------------|--------------|
| Clearly slower than all flyers | Competitive burst mobility |
| No answer to aerial repositioning | Vertical Stride option |
| Ground-bound only | 3D movement capability |
| B- mobility rating | Potentially S-tier in bursts |

## The Friendly Competition

This isn't hostility—it's **peer competition**:
- Rodan remains the uncontested master of sustained flight
- Godric concedes that domain willingly
- The Stride gives him an answer, not a victory

## The Rankings Post-Stride

| Context | Godric's Standing |
|---------|-------------------|
| Among the Trio | No longer the slowest (still grounded) |
| Among the Quintet | Fastest of the land walkers |
| Against Rodan specifically | Can challenge in bursts |

The Stride brings his mobility "up to speed" with his other S-tier stats—at least in short engagements.
