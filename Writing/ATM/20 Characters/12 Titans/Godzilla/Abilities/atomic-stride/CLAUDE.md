# CLAUDE.md - Atomic Stride

## Overview

This folder documents the **Atomic Stride** — Godric's near-instantaneous linear burst mobility technique. It represents the **Trophy of Humility**, honoring Kong's martial philosophy.

> **Related Ability:** [[atomic-mantle/CLAUDE|Atomic Mantle]]
> The Mantle was developed to solve Stride's collateral damage issues.
> Together they form the mobility+protection synergy of Godric's arsenal.

## Core Concept

> **Trophy of Humility** — *Per AA-Kratos P280*
>
> The Atomic Stride honors Kong's influence—the humility to accept that raw power isn't enough, that mobility and positioning matter. Godric learned to *move* like a martial artist rather than simply *overwhelm* like a Titan.
