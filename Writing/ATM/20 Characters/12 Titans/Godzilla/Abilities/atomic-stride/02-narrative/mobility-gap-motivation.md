# Mobility Gap Motivation

## The B- Problem

If all of Godric's stats are S or S+, his mobility as a human is about **B or "B-"**.

Why? Because **Rodan skews the ranking.**

## The Speed Hierarchy

In a contest of pure speed, agility, and mobility—where no teleportation and similar "hacks" are used—the rankings are clear:

| Tier | Titan | Notes |
|------|-------|-------|
| **Supreme** | Rodan | Uncontested Lord of the Skies |
| **Enhanced** | Mothra, Battra | Can enhance flight beyond natural means |
| **Standard** | Godzilla (pre-Stride) | B- rating due to Rodan comparison |

Mothra and Battra are exempt from pure speed rankings because they can enhance their flight through their respective abilities. But in raw, unaugmented mobility, **Rodan reigns supreme**.

## The Transformation Gap

Possessing a human body granted Godric mobility at base that simply didn't exist for him as a Titan:

> If Titan Godzilla were a tank, Human Godzilla is a tank with the mobility and maneuverability of a motorcycle.

But even with this baseline upgrade, he remained the slowest among his peers in pure movement capability.

## Atomic Stride: The Equalizer

With Atomic Stride, Godric can finally catch up:

| Context | Pre-Stride | Post-Stride |
|---------|------------|-------------|
| Among the Trio (Battra, Godzilla, Mothra) | Slowest | No longer slowest (though still grounded) |
| Among the Quintet (with Kong, Rodan) | Below average | Fastest of the land walkers |
| Overall mobility stat | B- | Potentially S-tier |

The Stride might finally bring his mobility up to speed with his other stats.
