# Atomic Resonance (Pàlmos)

The ability to create high-frequency vibrations throughout the body or into an object in contact. It serves as the foundation for the [[phasing-stride|Phasing Stride]] variant.

## Mechanism

Atomic Resonance allows Godric to vibrate at controlled frequencies:
- **Low frequencies:** Mundane utility applications
- **High frequencies:** Combat applications
- **Precise frequencies:** Phasing capability

## Applications

### Mundane Uses

At low frequencies, Resonance can be used for practical, non-combat purposes—though Godric's definition of "mundane" still involves considerable energy output.

### Combat Applications

At combat frequencies, Atomic Resonance becomes a horrifying weapon:

| Application | Effect |
|-------------|--------|
| **Internal Disruption** | Shattering internal structures of a target |
| **Armor Bypass** | Inflicting internal damage without penetrating external defenses |
| **Structural Destruction** | Breaking apart objects or environments |

### Phasing Stride Integration

When combined with Atomic Stride:
- Vibration allows passage through solid matter
- Trades maximum Stride distance for intangibility
- Requires simultaneous maintenance of both abilities

## The Physics

The vibration creates a state where Godric's physical form can slip between the molecular structure of solid matter—not true intangibility in a supernatural sense, but an application of extreme physics achievable only through a gojira's energy mastery.
