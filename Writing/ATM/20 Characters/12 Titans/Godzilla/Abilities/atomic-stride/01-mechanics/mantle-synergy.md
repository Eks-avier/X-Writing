# Mantle Synergy

The **Atomic Mantle** is the solution to the Stride's destructive side effects. Without it, every Stride is a weapon of mass destruction. With it, the Stride becomes a precise, silent tactical tool.

## The Visualization

The Mantle acts like a **net** around Godric's body during a Stride. It *catches* everything that would otherwise cause collateral damage:
- Excess energy
- Displaced air
- The minutest sound
- Shockwave artifacts
- Radiation leakage

## Kratonic Mechanism

From a Kratonic perspective, "catching" means **Neutralizing**. The Mantle:
1. Captures the shockwave at each stage of the ability
2. Dissolves harmful artifacts before they can propagate
3. Contains the lethal radiation that accompanies all gojira abilities

## The Radiation Buffer

Like all of Godric's abilities, the Stride is **highly radioactive**. Besides shockwaves, it leaves lethal radiation everywhere.

The Mantle serves as a containment field:
- Being *within* the Mantle is safe
- *Touching* it is not
- This has practically no difference for daily life—his mere presence is already radioactive

## Result: Silent Stride

When Mantle and Stride work together:

| Attribute | Without Mantle | With Mantle |
|-----------|----------------|-------------|
| **Sound** | Catastrophic sonic booms | Near-silent |
| **Shockwaves** | Lethal to surroundings | Neutralized |
| **Radiation** | Lethal trail | Contained |
| **Precision** | Chaotic | Controlled |
