# Mothra Inspiration

The Atomic Mantle is directly inspired by [[Mothra Profile|Maria/Mothra]]'s application of her mosura scales.

## Maria's Scale Capabilities

Maria can protect others through her scales in multiple ways:

| Method | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Telekinetic Protection** | Shielding people at a distance |
| **Scale Barrier** | Manipulating scales as an impenetrable, reflective wall |
| **Personal Shielding** | Extending scale protection to individuals |

## The Parallel

Godric's Mantle mirrors these capabilities within gojira limits:

| Maria's Scales | Godric's Mantle |
|----------------|-----------------|
| Protect at distance | Protect at skin-contact range |
| Reflective barrier | Neutralization field |
| Share protection with others | External Allocation |

## The Homage

The Mantle doesn't just function like Maria's scales—it was consciously developed **as tribute to her**:

> "This application of the Atomic Mantle is similar to Mothra's own application of her mosura scales. She can either protect people telekinetically or through manipulating her scales to act as an impenetrable, reflective barrier against attacks."

Every use acknowledges: *This is what you taught me. This honors you.*

## The Poetry

> "Oh, the poetry of it all! Just as Godric has begun to truly be in love with Maria, he develops an ability honoring her."

The timing of the Mantle's development coincides with the deepening of their relationship. The ability is romantic in the purest sense—a declaration of love expressed through capability.
