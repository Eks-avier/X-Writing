# CLAUDE.md - Atomic Mantle

## Overview

This folder documents the **Atomic Mantle** — Godric's externalized Neutralization field that provides form-fitting protection. It represents the **Trophy of Love**, honoring Maria's influence.

> **Related Ability:** [[atomic-stride/CLAUDE|Atomic Stride]]
> The Mantle was developed to solve Stride's collateral damage issues.
> Together they form the mobility+protection synergy of Godric's arsenal.

## Core Concept

> **Trophy of Love** — *Per AA-Kratos P280*
>
> The Atomic Mantle honors Maria—protection externalized, a shield that can be shared. Just as she extends her scales to protect others, Godric learned to extend his sovereign defense. It is love made manifest in Kratonic form.

## Atomic Amplification Clarifications

The ability does *not* exist as an external amplification. By its very principles, it is an *internal* enhancement.

Visually, if animated: Godzilla's body glows blue, but the blue comes from *within*. Sparks of energy explode *out* from the body itself.

But actually, the fewer "sparks" there are, the more refined the AA. This is the visual cue of mastery. The lightning, the sparks—these are all excess energy being unleashed. While it's a spectacle to see a la Super Saiyan, it's not the point. **The more normal he looks in AA, the more frightening it is.**

> *Per [[elektromagneton-nature#5. Aura Sparks: The Language of Mastery|Elektromagneton Nature]]: The sparks are his Elektromagneton side "acting out of sync"—EM leakage caused by imperfect integration of his dual natures. When he achieves Symphonia, both the blue glow (Pyrenikon) and the EM field work in perfect harmony, eliminating the sparks entirely.*
