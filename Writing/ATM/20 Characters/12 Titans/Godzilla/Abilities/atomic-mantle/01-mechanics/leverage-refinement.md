# Leverage Refinement

A quality-of-life upgrade to Godric's object manipulation capabilities, using the Mantle to simplify what was previously a complex calculation.

## The Previous Problem

Godric's ability to infuse objects for leverage (lifting, moving, manipulating massive objects) required careful calculation:
- How much power is precisely needed?
- What is the decay threshold for this material?
- Will the object survive the infusion?
- Are there living beings inside that could be harmed?

Every application required real-time calculation to avoid destroying what he was trying to manipulate.

## The Mantle Solution

Instead of precise power calculation, he can now:
1. Infuse a *portion* of the Mantle onto an object
2. Lift the object safely
3. Any living beings within the object are protected from radiation

The Mantle's Neutralization layer acts as a **buffer** between his energy and the object/occupants.

## Benefits

| Before Mantle | After Mantle |
|---------------|--------------|
| Complex power calculations | Simplified application |
| Risk of object decay | Protected structural integrity |
| Radiation exposure for occupants | Shielded living beings |
| Hesitation in urgent situations | Confident immediate action |

## Important Caveat

> This does **not** remove the danger of radiation contamination. It only **increases the threshold** before the ability becomes unsafe.

The Mantle buys margin for error, not immunity. Excessive power output will still overwhelm the protection.

## Practical Examples

- Lifting a car with people inside without irradiating them
- Moving debris during rescue operations
- Manipulating equipment that would normally decay under his touch
- Emergency evacuations where he must carry vulnerable beings
