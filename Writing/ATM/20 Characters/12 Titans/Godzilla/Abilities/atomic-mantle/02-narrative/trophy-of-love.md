# Trophy of Love

> **Trophy Framework:** Each ability is not a random power-up, but a *trophy*, a hard-won memento of a pivotal relationship and a crucial stage in personal growth.
> — *Per AA-Kratos P200*

---

## The Designation

The Atomic Mantle is the **Trophy of Love**, honoring Maria's influence on Godric's development.

## Maria's Lesson

The Mantle is similar to Mothra's own application of her mosura scales:
- She can protect people telekinetically
- She can manipulate her scales as an impenetrable, reflective barrier
- Her protection is **shared**, not hoarded

Godric learned to **externalize** protection—to extend his shield to others rather than keeping all defense for himself.

## The Poetry

> "Just as Godric has begun to truly be in love with Maria, he develops an ability honoring her."

The timing is not coincidental. As his feelings for Maria deepened, his abilities evolved to reflect what she represents to him: **protection extended to those he loves**.

## Love Made Manifest

The Mantle is love expressed through Kratonic form:
- Protection externalized
- A shield that can be **shared**
- Defense as an act of **care**, not just survival

She extends her scales to protect others. He learned to extend his sovereign defense. The parallel is deliberate, acknowledged, and treasured.

## A Homage

The Mantle doesn't just function like Maria's scales—it was consciously developed **in honor of her**. Every use is a quiet acknowledgment: *You taught me this. You inspired this. This is for you.*
