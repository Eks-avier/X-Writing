# Knowing Thyself

## The Integration Project

Godzilla sought ways to integrate his atomic energy into the martial arts demonstrated to him by [[Kong]] in their fight. However, many limitations first had to be acknowledged.

## Species Limitations

### Atomic Breath Issues

*Titanus gojira* were masters of **projecting** energies out of their bodies. The most prominent example—the Atomic Breath—they could use with primal grace. It was their ultimate and most signature weapon.

However, the human form constrained Godzilla's control over this ability:
- **Range** — Inconsistent
- **Output** — Inconsistent; too strong becomes painful
- **Manifestation** — Inconsistent; sometimes cone, sometimes beam
- **Comfort** — Sustaining it strains him; general use has become uncomfortable

### Internal Manipulation

*Titanus gojira* cannot manipulate their energy internally or externally. Their only method of internal manipulation is for *preparation* of the Atomic Breath:
- They can coalesce energy necessary for the Atomic Breath
- They can build it up according to whatever output they desire
- This typically changes how the energy ray manifests
- Traditionally used in two modes: quick burst or sustained beam

### External Manipulation

They *seemingly* possess no method to manipulate energies externally:
- Cannot steer Atomic Breath direction once fired
- Cannot create energy constructs

This stands in contrast to other Titan types:
- *Titanus mosura* can telekinetically shape their scales
- Elemental Titans control fire, water, air, earth
- [[Rodan]] possesses aerokinesis and lithokinesis
- The Wardens are often masters of specific elements (Leviathan, Scylla, Manda, Bakunawa, Amhuluk with water control)

### Sensing Abilities

What *Titanus gojira* can do—and have excelled at—is sensing any form of nuclear energy and radiation. This ability improved steadily across the ages:
- Godzilla figured out how to expand his senses to other energy types
- This led to expanding his "energy palate" (crucial as radiation levels waned)
- As a human, these senses were dulled initially:
  - After one month: functional levels reclaimed
  - After six months: back to normal
  - By early 2021: expanded beyond previous capability (more precise, though shorter range)

## The "Limitation" Paradox

These issues on internal and external control were said to be inherent limitations of the species' control over atomic energy—a curious belief for the species most known to adapt and evolve through anything limitlessly.

Monarch believes this resulted from the Atomic Breath and sensing abilities being "enough" for the majority of their use cases.

This also contradicts what Monarch now understands about Godzilla's atomic energy manipulation: it is the **domination of energy**, the absolute subjugation of any and all forms of energy. They can sense, absorb, disperse, convert, nullify, and manipulate energy in whatever form or from whatever source.

> *This working understanding was posited by [[Battra]] himself after reincarnation from his death at the hands of his sister during the tail end of the [[Xilien Invasion]].*

## The Crucial Foundation

Understanding these limitations proved crucial in knowing Godzilla's current predicament—and how to move past it.
