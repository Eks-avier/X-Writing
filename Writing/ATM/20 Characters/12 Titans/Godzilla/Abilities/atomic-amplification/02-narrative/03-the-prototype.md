# The Prototype

## The Draft Phase

Prior to finalizing the Atomic Amplification, Godzilla and [[Kong]] created a sort of "draft" of the ability which would serve as the foundation—and also prove how dangerous it is when misused.

With some helpful lessons from books and resources about human anatomy from [[Madison Russell|Madison]], the duo ended up with a way to actualize the ability.

## The Kong-Godzilla Fight (2022)

Their collaboration was born from conflict. When Kong and Godzilla fought in 2022, Kong had done exploring across the human world since his transformation.

### Kong's Preparation

Kong was turned into a human at the same time large swathes of the Titan population did—in 2020. His experience as a bipedal Titan helped make the transition to human form nearly effortless. His only problems were learning human speech and dealing with the perspective shift from being a giant monster to a tiny human.

Kong's "disappearance" alarmed Monarch operatives at Skull Island—superseding even the island's imminent sinking after one of Ghidorah's generated storms merged with the perpetual storm cell. It became an ongoing mystery until Jia eventually revealed that Kong had left months ago, hitching a ride on Monarch vehicles with help from her and Dr. Ilene Andrews.

Jia urged him to explore the world beyond, to finally escape the island that had become a prison. Kong promised to come back—not until after he sought a way to prevent Skull Island from sinking. Jia, knowing this was impossible, allowed her father-figure to hold onto that hope.

Kong would circle the world:
- Picked up on human fighting styles, learning and integrating them
- Little difference between Titan and human form made martial arts incredibly intuitive
- Thanks to unparalleled physical prowess, he excelled quickly
- Also gained experience wielding a variety of weapons

### The Battle

Kong eventually returned to a Skull Island barely holding on. In his travels, he encountered [[Manda]], the Guardian of Mu and Northeastern Warden, who explained there was nothing to be done. The island had degraded too much. Residents had migrated to the Hollow Earth. Kong despaired that memories of his childhood and parents would sink into the ocean's depths.

Luckily, Monarch had moved the immaculately preserved remains of Kong's mother and father to Castle Bravo. Godzilla, now somewhat used to his human body, was surprised to know remnants of Kong's species still remained. As far as he was concerned, he dealt with the last of them after Skar King attempted to invade the surface world.

Godzilla gave history on the species that wouldn't have been possible had he not been human:
- The Royal House of Kong were the rightful rulers—stronger, smarter, aging very slowly
- They would take almost a millennia to die, remaining in prime state far longer
- During a civil war, the Royal House was deposed by the Skar King
- The Royal Line was supposedly utterly annihilated—but continued on Skull Island

Curious, Godzilla physically swam in search of the island. He found it barely there, parts having already sunken.

### The Confrontation

Kong, sensing a dangerous being in his dying territory, moved to get Jia out of the way. She and the doctors boarded one of the last Monarch vessels. Kong would defend what was left—if he died, at least he would die a warrior, a captain sinking with his ship.

He encountered Godzilla:
- At first, fascinated—Godzilla thought the Castle Bravo bones were the last of the Kongs
- Before him stood not just a living giant ape but the true heir to their species' throne
- Kong responded with aggression, demanding to know who this being was
- Godzilla, weary from his fight with Ghidorah and still adjusting, attempted to diffuse
- Kong suddenly realized this was the fabled *King of the Monsters*—the one who urged Skar King to eliminate their family
- All explanations fell on enraged deaf ears

Godzilla was loathe to fight again, still spiritually exhausted from Ghidorah. But something about Kong compelled him.

### The Fight

Godzilla purposefully limited himself to physical might:
- Unsure if using abilities would be wise given the island's fragility
- A voice urged him: if he lost here, no one would know; if he won, it would be hollow
- But the determination in Kong's onyx eyes compelled him to accept

Kong's unprecedented fighting style confused Godzilla. They were relative in strength, but Kong was faster and more agile. He weaved between attacks, unleashed precise hits, crafted tools mid-fight.

For the first time since Ghidorah, a fire within Godzilla's spirit was lit again. This was all new. He had holes in his body, gashes, wounds—but could not help feeling a build-up within. Sheer joy overflowed.

Godzilla began countering Kong by the second:
- Became more mobile, more fluid
- Kong found himself on the back foot
- Each strike hit harder despite compounding injuries
- Movements became harder to read
- Feints, dodges, unorthodox attacks were being used against him
- Kong realized Godzilla was learning as the fight went on—and despite injuries, kept going with sheer joy

### The Outcome

At the end, the one with the most endurance won. After a particularly powerful jab to the stomach, Kong lurched over. Godzilla stood atop Kong's broken body. Unbroken and unbent, Kong waited for the final hit.

But it never came. Godzilla lent him a hand.

He saw fire he had rarely seen in millions of years—reminding him of himself. This fire never wavered, even facing defeat. It harkened back to his younger self, alone in the northern tundras while his father searched the South. It brought back countless battles across eons. It brought back Ghidorah in 2019.

It wasn't subordination in Kong's eyes. It wasn't Ghidorah's cold, calculated malice. It wasn't the grace and dignity in Mothra's sapphire eyes that compelled him to make her remain by his side forever. The vision before him was something achingly familiar—fire that Kong's species should have no right having, that never had any indication of possessing.

Other Titans would consider them merely intelligent pests. But the fire spoke of something beyond violence and the need to conquer. He saw pure, unfiltered willpower in Kong.

Kong, barely conscious, was recognized as Alpha Sovereign amidst the blood, sweat, and destruction. A new brotherhood would be formed, born from kindred connection.

## The Prototype Technique

The question of how to visualize controlling one's own circulatory system was difficult. Godzilla was eager to move forward; Kong was stuck too.

Godzilla, perhaps from sheer intuition, attempted to "charge"—basing the idea from the Atomic Breath preparation stage:
- Imagine charging the Atomic Breath
- But instead of going through his throat, direct it towards a limb and into a hand
- Surprisingly, this worked!

The duo called this the prototype.

### Observing Anguirus

Meanwhile, Godzilla continued observing [[Anguirus]] from afar:
- Watched how his friend utilized captured energy
- Noticed that in areas requiring reinforcement, Anguirus would coalesce energy most in that area
- This tracked with his current usage

### Testing Hypotheses

Godzilla tested his hypotheses with Kong:
- Visualize atomic energy travelling from heart to chest
- Just before it goes to throat, "nudge" it away and "instruct" it to go into a limb
- The energy begrudgingly "obeys"
- The process is uncomfortable and slow, painful at higher outputs
- But it works
- Veins of energy manifest across his arm; hand glows blue
- It is *hot* and *painful*—regeneration working overtime

It was, however, a success. He kept using it, starting at base output and incrementally raising it.

### Adaptation and Failure

Eventually, his body "got used to it":
- Hurt less—still noticeable, but *less*
- Direction speed improved—still sluggish, but improved
- Felt less hot

But when he used it to punch something: a massive explosion detonated. His arm went along with it—a cauterized stump that slowly grew back.

There was progress, and that was what mattered.

### The Plateau

Unfortunately, progress plateaued over a few weeks:
- The sensation got less painful and uncomfortable as his body adapted
- However, nothing could be done about limbs combusting in a rain of embers and blood
- [[Madison Russell|Madison]] had lurched over vomiting so much she probably lost count
- She was probably completely used to it by then
