# Anguirus: The Shadow of Achievement

## Overshadowed Pride

As Godzilla's mastery of Atomic Amplification grew, [[Anguirus]]'s pride in inspiring the technique was increasingly overshadowed.

### Evolution Beyond Origin
The technique had evolved far beyond its original inspiration:
- What began as an adaptation of his absorption ability had transformed into something entirely different
- The final version incorporated more of [[Kong]]'s philosophical approach than Anguirus's mechanics
- Even the name—Atomic Amplification—reflected this shift away from pure absorption

### The Impossible Position
Anguirus found himself in an impossible position:
- He could hardly criticize an achievement that made his King more powerful
- His millions of years of loyalty prevented direct expression of his concerns
- Yet he couldn't ignore the significance of what was happening

### Subtle Manifestations
His discomfort manifested in subtle ways:
- He volunteered less frequently for joint training sessions
- His usual tactical suggestions became increasingly rare
- He developed a habit of examining damage to training facilities after Godzilla and Kong's sessions, as if searching for evidence of instability in their approach

## The Formal Demonstration

The formal demonstration of the completed technique proved particularly difficult.

### The Setting
Godzilla arranged a controlled demonstration for his inner circle:
- [[Mothra Profile|Mothra]], [[Rodan]], and Anguirus were invited to observe the perfected technique
- Kong attended as both participant and co-developer
- The setting was a specialized Monarch testing facility reinforced to withstand Titan-level energy

### Flawless Execution
The demonstration was flawless:
- Godzilla activated the enhancement with minimal visible effort
- The uniform blue aura indicated perfect energy distribution
- Physical tests confirmed enhancement levels far beyond previous capabilities
- Not a single instability occurred throughout the demonstration

### Telling Reactions
The reactions were telling:
- **Mothra** observed with scholarly interest, immediately recognizing the technique's significance
- **Rodan** responded with characteristic enthusiasm, practically demanding a demonstration of combat speed
- **Anguirus** offered appropriate congratulations, his military bearing perfectly maintained
- Yet Godzilla, with his enhanced senses, detected the subtle increase in Anguirus's heart rate, the microscopic tension in his posture

## The Aftermath

The aftermath revealed the true depth of Anguirus's emotional conflict.

### Madison's Question
[[Madison Russell]], documenting the session, innocently triggered the issue:

> "So this is basically an adaptation of Anguirus's absorption ability?" she asked.

Before Anguirus could respond, Kong answered:

> "It started there, but evolved into something that works with Godzilla's unique energy. The distribution principle is entirely different."

### Unintentional Dismissal
The technical explanation was accurate but unintentionally dismissive:
- Kong meant no disrespect—he was simply clarifying the technique's evolution
- To Anguirus, however, it felt like his contribution was being minimized
- The fact that Kong was explaining *his* ability to a human felt particularly galling

### Godzilla's Blind Spot
Godzilla missed these nuances entirely:
- His focus remained on the technical aspects of the achievement
- The significance of Kong answering for Anguirus didn't register
- To Godzilla, the development represented pure progress without emotional complications

## The Midnight Sessions

That night, Monarch security footage recorded an unusual incident.

### The First Session
At approximately 2:30 AM, Anguirus entered the now-empty training facility:
- The footage showed him standing motionless in the center for several minutes
- Then, with methodical precision, he began a solo combat routine
- The exercise was executed with perfect military discipline, devoid of flashiness

### Intensification
As the routine progressed, Anguirus's movements intensified:
- His damage absorption visibly activated, creating his characteristic reinforcement pattern
- The energy signature was entirely different from Godzilla's uniform aura—concentrated, targeted, pragmatic
- With each impact, the reinforced floor took more damage than it had during the earlier demonstration

### The Final Strike
The session ended with a single, devastating strike:
- Anguirus channeled absorbed energy into a perfectly executed blow
- The reinforced floor—designed to withstand Titan-level force—shattered completely
- He stood motionless for a moment, surveying the destruction, then methodically cleaned up the debris and left

## The Silent Ritual

This nocturnal pattern would become Anguirus's silent ritual.

### Documented Repetition
Security logs would later reveal dozens of similar sessions:
- Always alone, always late at night
- Always ending with systematic destruction of training equipment
- Always meticulously cleaned up afterward, with damaged equipment properly requisitioned for replacement

### Unacknowledged
No one mentioned these incidents—not security personnel, not Monarch officials, not even Mothra, who almost certainly knew:
- There was something too personal, too private about this expression of emotion
- The military precision with which he conducted even these emotional releases made them seem like an extension of duty
- It was as if acknowledging them would somehow diminish the dignity of Godzilla's oldest companion

### The Composed Exterior
Anguirus himself never spoke of his nocturnal activities:
- By day, he continued to serve as the perfect Stalwart Vanguard
- His tactical advice remained impeccable, his loyalty unquestioned
- Only the growing frequency of replacement requisitions for training equipment hinted at the conflict beneath his composed exterior
