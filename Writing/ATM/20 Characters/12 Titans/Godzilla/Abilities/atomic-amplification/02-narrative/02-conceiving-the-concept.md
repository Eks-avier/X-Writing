# Conceiving the Concept

## After Understanding Limitations

Once Godzilla understood clearly the limitations of his species, conceiving the concept was now in order.

## Failed Approaches

### Energy Constructs

Godzilla and [[Kong]] went over many ideas for the integration project. First, they figured energy constructs.

Kong, a master of creating weapons from the environment on the spot, offered this idea. But despite the considerable boost Godzilla gained from his [[burning-form|Burning Form]] post-Ghidorah, he could not muster any sort of aptitude for it:
- Any attempts simply overheated his limbs
- At worst, it caused a miniature explosion
- Additionally, fighting with weapons isn't Godzilla's modus, even if he's considering a change in fighting style

In the end, it was a disaster.

## The Anguirus Inspiration

Then one day, Godzilla's first breakthrough into the Atomic Amplification came in the form of the one and only [[Anguirus]] and his damage absorption ability.

### Anguirus's Signature Ability

The signature ability of *Titanus angilas* is simple: they can absorb any form of damage that comes in contact with them physically. Underneath this remarkable defensive capability is the hidden option to utilize the damage absorbed as physical empowerment.

This allows Anguirus to literally use an opponent's momentum against them. It also fits his fighting style: "A strong defense is a good offense."

Unfortunately, it's often overlooked due to its lack of flashiness unlike the element-manipulating Titans. To those who are aware and know how it works, it is truly a formidable ability fit for the one and only Stalwart Vanguard, whose hardiness could only be matched by his unfailing loyalty to his greatest friend the King of the Monsters.

### The Epiphany

Anguirus's empowerment ability is what inspired Godzilla:

> *If he cannot manipulate energy externally, then perhaps he can explore controlling his internal flow of energy. Instead of relying on absorbing energy from external sources, he would utilize his flow of energy to empower him!*

## An Homage to Brotherhood

The Atomic Amplification is an homage to Godzilla's first brother-in-arms:
- A callback to a relationship that began all the way from the Mesozoic Era
- A manifestation of his appreciation for his best friend in the form of the one thing Godzilla loves: fighting

### The Seeds of Jealousy

It is a shame, though, that it ended up serving as a catalyst for Anguirus's jealousy towards Kong. At this point in time, Anguirus had noticed the newly minted King of the Hollow Earth, the first Alpha Sovereign since Mothra herself, and the only one now since the title of Alpha Divine was formalized many eons ago.

To Anguirus, this ability would only serve as the representation of his impending replacement at the hands of Kong.

## Moving Forward

Now armed with the intuition of what he now calls the Atomic Amplification, Godzilla begins finding ways to feasibly manifest it:
- *Titanus gojira* cannot utilize their internal flow of energy to empower themselves
- He must now go beyond the perceived limitations of his species and himself to achieve this

Kong made absolutely certain if he really can't create energy constructs—and Godzilla simply violently exploded an arm in his face.
