# Combination Attacks

## The Core Principle: "Bring Them Down to My World"

His goal is never to *stay* in the air. The sky is a temporary, hostile environment—a launching point and a repositioning space, but never the arena. Every aerial combo is designed with two outcomes:

1. **Annihilate the target mid-air**
2. **Incapacitate and force them to plummet,** where the true fight begins on his terms

---

## The Combinations

### Stride > Foothold > Attack (The "Air-Brake" Assault)

His bread-and-butter aerial ambush. Stride to an optimal position above or behind an enemy, then use a Foothold to kill momentum and create a stable platform for a devastating physical strike or point-blank Atomic Breath.

### Jump > Foothold > Stride (The "Two-Stage Rocket")

His solution for high-altitude targets. A physics-based combo extending effective range—the initial jump and foothold provide altitude to bring distant targets within Stride range.

### Propulsion > Stride (The "Chaos Gambit")

Pure psychological warfare. The loud, highly visible Atomic Breath propulsion makes him look clumsy and desperate. It's a feint. The enemy focuses on the obvious "rocket," leaving them vulnerable to the silent, instantaneous Stride to their flank.

### Charge > Stride > Fire (The "Point-Blank Execution")

His most terrifying anti-air finisher. Begin the obvious, visible charge-up for Railgun or Atomic Breath, forcing the enemy to focus on that threat. At the last second, Stride directly into their face and unleash the blast. There is no time to react. The panic makes their movements predictable, guaranteeing a hit.

---

## The Ultimate Anti-Air Platform

Aerial opponents have two choices:
1. Stay at a distance and risk being sniped by a perfectly aimed Atomic Railgun
2. Close the distance to attack and be dragged down into his domain, where his CQC, grappling, and Primal Arsenal make him unbeatable

It's a perfect tactical catch-22.
