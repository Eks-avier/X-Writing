# Atomic Propulsion (Othisi) — The Goofball's Rocket

**Mechanism:** A controlled, sustained burst of his Atomic Breath, used as a rocket engine to propel himself through the air.

**The Unorthodox Weapon:** Its primary strength is its sheer, unpredictable absurdity. No sane opponent would ever expect a fighter of his caliber to suddenly use his ultimate ranged weapon as a simple movement tool. It is the ultimate pattern-break.

## Characteristics

| Attribute | Rating |
|-----------|--------|
| **Cost** | Highest energy consumption (consumes more than distance justifies) |
| **Visibility** | Extremely visible and loud |
| **Stealth** | Worst option for stealth |
| **Fun Factor** | His favorite to use—an expression of his inner Dagon |

## Tactical Use

- **Psychological warfare:** Makes him appear clumsy or desperate
- **Pattern-breaking:** Catches opponents off-guard
- **Emergency repositioning:** Useful in a pinch despite inefficiency

> *"It may look like a gag, and it is—he does have fun using it—but it is a powerful tool to catch an enemy off guard. Sometimes, that's all you need."*
