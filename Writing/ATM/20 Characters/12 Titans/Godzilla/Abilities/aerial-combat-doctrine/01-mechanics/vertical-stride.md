# Vertical Stride (Seismos) — The King's Gambit

**Mechanism:** His perfected **Atomic Stride**, aimed vertically or diagonally.

**The Ultimate Repositioning Tool:** This is his fastest and most efficient way to change elevation or cover short-to-medium distance in the air. It is a silent, instantaneous "blink" from one point to another.

## Characteristics

| Attribute | Rating |
|-----------|--------|
| **Cost** | Slightly more than Foothold, far less than Propulsion |
| **Speed** | Fastest option (instantaneous) |
| **Stealth** | Most stealthy |
| **Range** | Limited to Stride's maximum distance (~100-120m at 100% AA, typically less) |
| **Intimidation** | Highest psychological impact |

## The Burden of Calculation

He is not a Psychic who can "feel" the destination, nor a Sorcerer who can "target" it with a spell. He is a being of physics. He has to **aim.** It's a calculation of power output, trajectory, and distance—every Stride is a feat of immense, high-speed mental processing. A slight miscalculation could see him materialize a hundred feet above or below his intended target.

## The Intimidation Factor

He uses it sparingly. Its very rarity makes it a terrifying psychological weapon. When he does use it, it signifies that he is being absolutely serious. It's not a playful rocket burst; it is the silent, deadly advance of a king.

## Kratonic Terminology

| Term | Greek | Application |
|------|-------|-------------|

## Prerequisite

All aerial mobility options require **Atomic Amplification** and proper energy allocation. They are simply too precise to perform raw.
