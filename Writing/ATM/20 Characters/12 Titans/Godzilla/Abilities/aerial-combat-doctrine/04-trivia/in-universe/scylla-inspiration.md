# Scylla: The Mechanical Inspiration ("The What")

The Air Foothold is actually **the technique Scylla uses to fly.** For her, it's supremely effortless—she freezes the moisture in the air, then moves it with her hydrokinesis, granting her actual sustained flight.

---

## The Observation

Godzilla, with his analytical mind, would have observed Scylla's flight with immense curiosity. He saw her create solid, tangible platforms from seemingly intangible air.

This planted the seed: *"It is possible to make the air solid."*

---

## The Reverse-Engineering

He cannot freeze moisture like she can. But he understood the *principle*: applying a Kratonic effect to the atmosphere to create a load-bearing surface.

He translated this into his own language—instead of cryokinesis, a pinpoint application of **Structural Infusion (Stereosis)**.

Same *what* (solid platform in air) through a different *how*.

---

## The Limitation

| Aspect | Scylla | Godric |
|--------|--------|--------|
| **Duration** | Sustained | Temporary |
| **Mobility** | Mobile platforms | Fixed position |
| **Method** | Cryokinesis + Hydrokinesis | Stereosis |
| **Specialty** | Controlled elemental manipulation | Brute-force state change |

His is temporary and requires contact; hers is sustained and mobile. This highlights the difference between their Kratonic specialties.

---

## Timeline

He achieved this unconsciously during the Battra fight. Watching Scylla made him understand the principles from another perspective.
