# Ghidorah's Elegant Disdain

For comparison: Ghidorah (and thus Arthur by way of Ichi) can also create air footholds.

But when you already control gravity, why bother with an inelegant method?

He can simply negate gravity on himself—his flight is closer to psionics and magic in its innate elegance and simplicity.

---

## The Kratonic Contrast

This highlights the fundamental difference between their Kratos:

| Aspect | Godzilla | Ghidorah |
|--------|----------|----------|
| **Approach** | Forceful application of physics | Rewriting the laws of physics themselves |
| **Effort** | Calculated, deliberate | Effortless, innate |
| **Style** | Blue-collar mastery | Golden arrogance |
| **Philosophy** | Work within rules | Change the rules |

---

## The Character Implication

Ghidorah *could* use footholds. He simply considers them beneath him.

Godric *must* use footholds—and through discipline and creativity, turns this limitation into a unique fighting style.

This mirrors their broader dynamic: the working-class king versus the aristocratic usurper.
