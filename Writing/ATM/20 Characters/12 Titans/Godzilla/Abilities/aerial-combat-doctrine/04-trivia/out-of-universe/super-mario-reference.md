# The "Super Mario" Rule

**The foothold only exists for a couple of seconds, or it vanishes the moment he leaves it.**

This is not a construct; it is a temporary state change.

---

## The Reference

The timing and behavior mirrors the **vanishing platform** mechanic from Super Mario games—platforms that disappear shortly after being touched, requiring precise timing and continuous movement.

---

## The Narrative Purpose

This "Super Mario Rule" reinforces a fundamental limit of Godric's pure Kratonic system: **his inability to create independent external objects.**

Unlike his children (who can overcome this through their Psionic nature as they mature), this is a **permanent** characteristic for Godric. He cannot give his will an independent form.

---

## The Thematic Fit

The reference works because:
1. **Gameplay logic** → Clear, understandable limitation
2. **Kinetic energy** → Forces dynamic movement, not static hovering
3. **Character truth** → Reflects his "visitor, not conqueror" philosophy
