# Rodan: The Tactical Inspiration ("The How")

Rodan no longer has wings post-Antitheriomorphosis. He utilizes his aerokinesis to reposition himself quickly in the air—powerful, instantaneous bursts of propulsion to pivot and dodge. His flight is a series of controlled, tactical repositions.

---

## The Application

Godzilla takes this *tactical philosophy* and applies it to his newfound foothold ability.

He doesn't think of it as "building stairs in the sky." He thinks of it as **creating a temporary point of leverage** to execute a Rodan-style pivot or launch.

The foothold is not a platform to stand on; it is a **wall to kick off of.**

---

## The Love Letter

This is a beautiful, unspoken tribute. Godzilla, the grounded king, incorporates the signature technique of the Lord of the Skies into his own arsenal—a profound sign of respect for Rodan's mastery of his domain.
