# The Iconic Meta-Reference

The Atomic Propulsion technique is described as "the iconic meta-reference"—a deliberate callback to one of Godzilla's most famous (and memeable) moments in franchise history.

---

## The Reference

In *Godzilla vs. Hedorah* (1971), Godzilla famously used his atomic breath aimed downward to propel himself through the air, effectively flying by rocket propulsion.

This scene has become one of the most iconic and frequently referenced moments in kaiju cinema—simultaneously ridiculous and awesome.

---

## The ATM Interpretation

Rather than ignoring or retconning this moment, ATM **embraces** it:

| Aspect | Franchise Original | ATM Version |
|--------|-------------------|-------------|
| **Tone** | Comedic/Absurd | Comedic **and** tactical |
| **Purpose** | Emergency travel | Psychological warfare |
| **In-universe reaction** | N/A | "Expression of his inner Dagon" |

The technique becomes a **character moment**: Godric's playful side, his willingness to look foolish for tactical advantage, and his connection to his father's spirit.

---

## The Subversion

What looks like a gag is actually a sophisticated combat tool:
- Makes him appear desperate (lowering enemy guard)
- Unpredictable pattern-break (no one expects the rocket)
- Sets up the "Chaos Gambit" combo (Propulsion → Stride flank attack)
