# The Banter with Maria

When he refuses to be floated by Maria from Skull Island, it's simultaneously out of **pride**, **respect**, and the need to **tease her** (in their denial phase, of course).

---

## The Layers

| Surface Reading | Deeper Meaning |
|-----------------|----------------|
| Stubbornness | Pride in his own capabilities |
| Playful refusal | Flirtatious teasing during denial phase |
| "I can do it myself" | Respect for aerial domain sovereignty |

---

## Post-Transformation Growth

Even after learning to be more of his authentic self in his human form, he **doesn't regret** disliking flight.

His options are to *visit* the sky, not conquer it—because it has already been conquered by his peers. This isn't stubbornness; it's a consistent expression of his values.

---

## The Character Moment

This small interaction reveals the complexity beneath Godric's surface:
- What looks like alpha male posturing is actually **layered honor**
- What looks like flirtation is actually **mutual respect**
- What looks like limitation is actually **choice**
