# Trophy Framework: A Love Letter

> **Trophy Framework:** This doctrine is a **love letter to Rodan and Scylla** — two Titans whose unique mastery of their domains inspired Godric's approach to aerial combat. The techniques honor their sovereignty while enabling him to operate as a "visitor" rather than a conqueror of the sky.

---

## The Significance

The Aerial Combat Doctrine exemplifies the **Trophy Narrative Framework** — each ability is not a random power-up, but a *trophy*, a hard-won memento of a pivotal relationship and a crucial stage in personal growth.

Unlike techniques representing romantic bonds (Atomic Mantle for Maria) or rivalries (Atomic Stride for Kong), this doctrine honors **peer respect**:

| Titan | What Godric Learned | Trophy Type |
|-------|---------------------|-------------|
| **Scylla** | The mechanical *possibility* of air platforms | Inspiration of Technique |
| **Rodan** | The tactical *philosophy* of aerial repositioning | Inspiration of Strategy |

---

## The "Visitor" Philosophy

The techniques deliberately limit Godric's aerial capability. He can *visit* the sky, not conquer it—because it has already been conquered by his peers.

This self-imposed limitation is not weakness but **honor**: acknowledgment that the Lord of the Skies and other aerial Titans have earned their domains through mastery he chooses not to replicate.
