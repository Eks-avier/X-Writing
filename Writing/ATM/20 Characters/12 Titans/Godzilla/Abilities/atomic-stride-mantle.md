# Atomic Stride & Atomic Mantle

> **Trophy of Humility** (Stride) / **Trophy of Love** (Mantle) — *Per AA-Kratos P280*
>
> The **Atomic Stride** honors Kong's influence—the humility to accept that raw power isn't enough, that mobility and positioning matter. Godric learned to *move* like a martial artist rather than simply *overwhelm* like a Titan.
>
> The **Atomic Mantle** honors Maria—protection externalized, a shield that can be shared. Just as she extends her scales to protect others, he learned to extend his sovereign defense. It is love made manifest in Kratonic form.

---

Enter the Asymmetrical Permeable Shield or the APS.

## Atomic Amplification Clarifications

The ability does *not* exist as an external amplification. By its very principles, it is an *internal* enhancement.

Visually, if we imagine this is all animated: Godzilla's body glows blue, but the blue comes from *within*. Sparks of energy explode *out* from the body itself.

But, actually, the less “sparks” there are, the more refined the AA. This is the visual cue of mastery. The lightning, the sparks, these are all excess energy being unleashed by the body. So, while it is a spectacle to see it a la Super Saiyan, it's not actually the point. The more looks normal in AA, the more frightening it is.

> *Per [[elektromagneton-nature#5. Aura Sparks: The Language of Mastery|Elektromagneton Nature]]: The sparks are his Elektromagneton side “acting out of sync”—EM leakage caused by imperfect integration of his dual natures. When he achieves Symphonia, both the blue glow (Pyrenikon) and the EM field work in perfect harmony, eliminating the sparks entirely.*

## Definition

* **Mechanically**: The skill takes a portion of the defensive aspect granted by AA and elevates as a layer *on* the skin of an Antitheriomorphosized *Titanus gojira*.
* **Krátonically**: A *portion* of the Neutralization aspect of AA defending the body *from the inside* is brought out and placed *onto* the skin. The Penetration aspect remains *within* the body.
* **[[elektromagneton-nature|Elektromagneton]] Foundation**: The Mantle's form-fitting structure is possible because of the EM “wireframe” that gives shape to the Neutralization layer. Per the [[elektromagneton-nature#The Atomic Mantle: EM Framework|Sculptor metaphor]]: the EM field provides the invisible lines, and the atomic energy fills them in.
* **Clarifications**:
	* The entire Neutralization layer of AA is not *completely* brought out from within the body; only about a quarter or a half of it is, depending on the situation at hand. For more demanding challenges, additional layers may be introduced.
		* This is so that the *internal organs* of the body remain enhanced and defended by AA, as a gojira would be sacrificing internal protection for external defense.
	* It is **form-fitting**. It does not *hover* over the skin; it sits right on top of it.
	* It remains *highly* radioactive at higher outputs.

## Stride Synergy

* **Visualization**: The reason why APS is the solution to the issues of the Atomic Stride is that I imagine it acting like a net. It *catches* everything that may *cause* a sonic boom: excess energy, displaced air, the minutest sound, and so on.
	* **Krátonic Perspective**: And by catching such artifacts, I meant it Neutralizes them. Whenever he does a Stride, it captures the shock wave as well as any other harmful artifacts at each stage of the ability.
* **Radiation Buffer**: Another thing about the Atomic Stride: as with all of Godzilla's abilities, it is VERY radioactive. Besides the shock waves, it leaves lethal radiation everywhere. The APS also serves to contain this.
	* **Caveat**: Being *within* the APS is safe, but *touching* it is not. This has practically no difference for Godzilla's daily life as he already has to contend with the fact that his mere presence is already radioactive.

## The Atomic Mantle

The APS is *not* just a supplement for the Stride. Its conception was caused *by* the Stride, but making it usable is not its sole purpose.

* **Reallocated Defenses**: While it may seem like he has an FOURTH LAYER of Armor, it isn't. It's just his AA Neutralization sitting on top of his skin. The overall defensive capability of AA is just reallocated. Part of it remains inside his system, part of it now sits on his skin. 7 and 3 still make 10.
* **External Allocation**: This is the *other* ability of the Mantle. It's a “buffing ability,” as they say. By imparting a *portion* of the Mantle itself onto an object or a person, he can grant them the same protection he would get from the defensive aspect of AA.
	* **Leverage Refinement**: His ability to infuse objects for leverage gets a QoL upgrade. Before, he would have to quickly calculate how much of his power is precisely needed in order to lift a massive object without inducing decay onto the object. He can instead use infuse a *portion* of the Mantle onto an object in order to lift it and living beings that might be within that object without worrying about radiation contamination.
		* **Caveat**: This does *not* remove the danger of radiation contamination. It only *increases* the threshold before the ability becoming unsafe.
* **The Zenith of a Pure *Titanus gojira*'s External Energy Manipulation**: The Atomic Mantle is the utmost limit of his, and any other pure gojira like Dagon, external manipulation. It brings one of the major motifs of this specific Titan species' to full circle. The protection of territories, whether external for males and internal for females, is the basest instinct of a *Titanus gojira*, hence why it's called the Atomic Mantle! (Hey, that's the name! Now I don't have to rely on a stark reference).
* **A Homage to the Woman of his Life**: This application of the Atomic Mantle is similar to Mothra's own application of her mosura scales. She can either protect people telekinetically or through manipulating her scales to act as an impenetrable, reflective barrier against attacks.
	* **The Poetry**: Oh, the poetry of it all! Just as Godric has begun to truly be in love with Maria, he develops an ability honoring her.

### The Capstone of Atomic Amplification

It is now complete, each of his abilities have a source that is from his closest friends:

* The damage absorption of Anguirus and the martial prowess of Kong (AA)
* The speed of Rodan and the scales of Mothra and Battra (Stride and APS)
* Battra as the one who pushed him to unlock all of this via instinct.

Notice how AA was developed *before* Battra while Stride and Mantle was developed *after* their fight. Yet, **all** of them where used in the fight anyway. Battra was the one being who pushed him in his human body to discover his utmost potential. The period after it is him chasing that high.

You can say that the Mantle is foreshadowing of his Horme evolving, from survival of himself to survival of his family.

*Oh wow, I didn't expect any of this!*

## Retrospective and Notes

It's amusing that the Stride is defined by its flaws more than its usefulness. That's intentional on my part.

Godzilla isn't really known to be mobile, but possessing a human body granted him the mobility *at base* that simply didn't exist for him as a Titan. If Titan Godzilla were a tank, Human Godzilla is a tank with the mobility and maneuverability of a motorcycle.

With the advent of Atomic Stride, his innate mobility in a human body is *further* increased. If all of Godzilla's stats are S or S+, his mobility as a human is about B or “B-”. Why? Because Rodan skews the ranking. Mothra and Battra are exempt because they can enhance their flight beyond natural means through their respective abilities. But, in a contest of pure speed, agility, mobility, and where no teleportation and such hacks are used, Rodan reigns supreme. With Atomic Stride, Godzilla can finally catch up.

* Among the Trio of Battra, Godzilla, and Mothra, he is no longer the slowest one, though he is still grounded.
* Among the Quintet (with Kong), he is now the fastest of the land walkers.

With the Atomic Stride, it might get it up to speed with his other stats!
