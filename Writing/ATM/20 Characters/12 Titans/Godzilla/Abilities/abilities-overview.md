# The Suite

> [!info] Quick Reference
> This document provides brief definitions of Godric's core abilities. For detailed development histories and mechanics, see the individual ability files linked below.

## Atomic Amplification

Mechanically, it is a uniform distribution of atomic energy throughout a *Titanus gojira*'s body, serving as a holistic physical enhancement that provides increased offense, defense, speed, agility, cognitive capability, and energy control. The intensity of this state is variable, allowing it to be modulated and scaled depending on the situation at hand.

Krátonically, it is uniform distribution of the internal applications of the *Edicts of Coating* (**Penetration**, **Neutralization**, and **Fortification**) throughout the body.

**See:** [[Atomic Amplification|Full Development History]]

## Atomic Breath

The classic ability of any *Titanus gojira* specimen.

**See:** [[atomic_breath_evolution|Atomic Breath Evolution]]

## Atomic Railgun

Descriptively, it is a hyper-concentrated, pencil-thin beam of atomic energy, typically fired from a single, extended fingertip. It has replaced his Atomic Breath as his ultimate finisher, erasing its intended target and everything in its path whether up-close or at a distance.

### Red Spiral Railgun

A variant of the Atomic Railgun used during his Burning Form. Its strength and range is incredibly enhanced.

**See:** [[atomic_railgun_development|Atomic Railgun Development]]

## Atomic Stride

A near-instantaneous, linear burst of movement. When used in conjunction with the Atomic Mantle, the Stride is almost completely silent and free of collateral damage.

It is his primary mobility tool for closing distances or repositioning.

Crucially, it is **not** teleportation. It is simply direct, physical propulsion at an extreme speed.

It can be used in any direction in three dimensional space, though it is tactically limited by a short-to-medium range.

### Phasing Stride

A variant of the Atomic Stride that enables the ability to phase through solid objects in the path of the stride.

It must be utilized with another ability, which is the Atomic Resonance.

By vibrating his physical body, he trades a portion of his Stride's maximum range for the ability to bypass physical barriers.

**See:** [[Atomic Stride & Atomic Mantle|Atomic Stride & Atomic Mantle]]

## Atomic Mantle

By projecting outward a portion of his internal Neutralization property afforded to him by Amplification, he achieves a skin-tight layer of protection.

The Mantle's primary purpose is to act as a silencer and brake for the Atomic Stride, neutralizing its destructive shockwaves and radiation byproducts. Its secondary purpose is to reallocate a portion of the Mantle to an ally or object, granting an external layer of protection to the target at the expense of the user's own protection.

**See:** [[Atomic Stride & Atomic Mantle|Atomic Stride & Atomic Mantle]]

## Atomic Resonance

It allows for the creation of high-frequency vibrations throughout the body or into an object in contact. At low frequencies, it can be used for mundane tasks. In combat, it can be an effectively horrifying weapon, capable of shattering internal objects, inflicting internal damage, or bypassing external armor.

When used together with an Atomic Stride, it can enable the user to phase through physical barriers at the expense of the Stride's maximum distance.
