*   **Manifestations of Grief: The Heavy Air:** During this initial phase (roughly 2020-2022), when he was still processing Dagon's story and prior to his fight with Kong, Godric's distress occasionally manifested physically. Unintentionally, the air around him would become palpably **“heavy,”** making it difficult to breathe and move through. His eyes would sharpen, and his jaw would tighten, a physical expression of his internal turmoil. This was a direct result of his radiation emissions fluctuating with his emotional state, causing a localized physics distortion field.
    *   **Titan Reactions:** Other Titans, instinctively aware, would avoid his general direction. Anguirus and Rodan would assume grave countenances, understanding the weight of his unexpressed grief.
    *   **Human Reactions:** Humans, lacking the Titan's understanding, would often panic, misinterpreting the sensation as Godric being angry or disappointed with something specific.
    *   **Mothra's Uniqueness:** Only Mothra remained unaffected by this phenomenon, her presence acting as a grounding force. She often sought to remain in his vicinity, intuitively sensing his need.
*   **Healing Trajectory:** This behavior lightened significantly with [[Kong, King of the Hollow Earth|Kong's]] introduction and their subsequent rigorous combat training, as it provided an outlet for his intensity. It further dissipated as his relationship with Mothra deepened into romance, and completely subsided with Dagon's eventual resurrection. In their current presence, the air around both father and son feels noticeably lighter, symbolizing the peace they've found.
