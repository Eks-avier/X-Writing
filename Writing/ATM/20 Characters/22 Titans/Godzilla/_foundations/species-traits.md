# Core Gojiran Traits

> [!info] Scope
> These traits apply to **all *Titanus gojira*** — not just Godric. For individual character details, see the appropriate character profile.

## Ascendant Classification

*Titanus gojira* is classified as an **Ascendant Lineage** — one of the rare Titan species whose biological complexity matches *Titanus mosura*. This designation indicates:

- **Genetic Compatibility**: Potential for viable offspring between Ascendant species
- **Superior Sapience**: Full cognitive equivalence to human intelligence
- **Evolutionary Refinement**: 300+ million years of development

For full documentation: [[Writing/ATM/30 Lore/33 Species/Titanus Gojira/Titanus Gojira|Titanus Gojira Species Encyclopedia]]

## Limitless Adaptation

The defining trait of *Titanus gojira* — unprecedented biological evolution within a single lifespan:

- **Cellular Reprogramming**: Cells alter structure in response to challenges
- **Tissue Transmutation**: Organs transform to develop new capabilities
- **Challenge Response**: Greatest adaptation from greatest challenges
- **Offspring Transmission**: Successful adaptations can pass to descendants

Unlike conventional evolution across generations, gojiran bodies actively rewrite their own biological code through experience. Power derives not from inheritance but from challenges overcome.

For full mechanics: [[Writing/ATM/30 Lore/33 Species/Titanus Gojira/1_Biology/Limitless Adaptation|Limitless Adaptation (Full)]]

## Krátos Mastery

All *Titanus gojira* possess exceptional affinity for [[Writing/ATM/30 Lore/31 Power Systems/Krátos/Krátos, the Power of Will|Krátos]] — the power system derived from willpower and physical prowess:

- **Species Baseline**: Minimum Archon-tier aptitude
- **Natural Affinity**: Krátos manifests instinctively from birth
- **Krátonic Aptitude Ceiling**: Kráton (Conqueror/Archon, Nomothete)

> [!note] Supernatural Limitation
> *Titanus gojira* are **null** for magic and **non** for psionics — they cannot perceive or wield either system. Krátos is their exclusive supernatural domain.
>
> See [[Writing/ATM/30 Lore/31 Power Systems/The Three Systems|The Three Systems]] for how Krátos, Magic, and Psionics interrelate.

For power system details: [[Writing/ATM/30 Lore/31 Power Systems/Krátos/Krátos, the Power of Will|Krátos Documentation]]

## Holariston Dual-Nature

The internal energy dynamics of *Titanus gojira* operate through two complementary forces:

| Force | Nature | Expression |
|-------|--------|------------|
| **Pyrenikon** | Primal fire, wild power | Heat, nuclear force, ancestral |
| **Elektromagneton** | Ordered lightning, refined control | Electromagnetism, precision |

Mastery of both forces — achieving "Holariston" (wholeness) — represents the pinnacle of gojiran development.

For biological details: [[Writing/ATM/30 Lore/33 Species/Titanus Gojira/1_Biology/Domination of Energy|Domination of Energy]]
