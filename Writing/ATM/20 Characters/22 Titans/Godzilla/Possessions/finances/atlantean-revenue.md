# Financial Independence: The Atlantean Revenue Stream

Godric maintains complete financial independence from Monarch through a unique and historically significant method.

---

## The Revenue Source

### Atlantean Artifacts

**Method of Acquisition:** Using perfect memory of his time as the region's "ward," he personally retrieves priceless, authenticated artifacts from the sunken city of Atlantis

**Dating:** 12,000-14,000 years old

**Authentication:** Incontestable (he knows exactly where things are and when they're from)

**Historical Significance:** First physical proof of the mythological civilization

---

## How the System Works

### Retrieval Process

**Godric's Role:**
1. Recalls artifact locations from 12,000+ years ago
2. Dives to retrieve (comfortable at extreme ocean depths)
3. Brings artifacts to Monarch

**Monarch's Role:**
1. Authenticates artifacts (always genuine)
2. Arranges loans to museums worldwide
3. Negotiates compensation on his behalf
4. Deposits revenue to his accounts

### The Paradigm-Shifting Discovery

**Impact:** The artifacts provide the first physical proof of Atlantis

**Result:** Shatters previous archaeological assumptions

**Irony:** They waved treasures in front of his snout thinking he wouldn't see or remember them

---

## Financial Outcome

### Complete Independence

**Revenue Generated:** Sufficient to fund "several generations"

**Coverage:** All personal expenses without reliance on Monarch budget

**Investments:** Managed portfolio (Madison handles details)

**Spending:** Minimal (few material needs or wants)

---

## The Historical Irony

### Godric's Quote

> "They would wave treasures in front of my snout thinking I wouldn't see or remember them. How odd that now they are the ones forgotten."

### The Context

**12,000 Years Ago:** Atlanteans displayed treasures as offerings/tributes

**Their Assumption:** The monster wouldn't comprehend or remember

**The Reality:** Perfect hyperthymestic memory recorded everything

**The Result:** Now he retrieves what they thought he ignored, and they are extinct while he endures

---

## Why This Matters

### Sovereignty Through Independence

**Financial dependence = vulnerability**

**Self-funding = autonomy**

**Monarch can't control him through budget** because he doesn't need them financially

### The Perfect Memory Advantage

**Only he can do this**—no one else knows where Atlantis's artifacts are

**His memory = archaeological map** more detailed than any human could create

**The irony:** His "simple animal nature" (as they saw it) was recording their entire civilization

## The Museum Partnership

### How Loans Work

**Artifacts Never Sold:** Remain Godric's property

**Museums Pay:** Loan fees for display rights

**Renewable Contracts:** Museums compete for access

**Cultural Impact:** Atlantean exhibits draw millions of visitors

**His Reputation:** The being who proved Atlantis real

### The Last Laugh

**Atlanteans:** Advanced civilization that viewed him as beast

**Godric:** "Simple beast" who remembers their every detail

**Outcome:** He prospers from their memory while they're forgotten

**The Message:** Underestimating ancient beings is always a mistake

**Traditional Wealth:** Accumulate material goods
