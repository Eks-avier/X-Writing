#### A. Personal Territories (Castle Bravo)

In response to the unique needs of the transformed Titans, Monarch established several dedicated facilities within Castle Bravo exclusively for Godric's use. He treats these spaces with the same meticulous care as any other territory under his command.

| Territory                   | Description & Purpose                                                                                                                                                             | Key Features & Access Protocols                                                                                                                                                                   |
| :-------------------------- | :-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Private Pool**            | A large, Olympic-sized swimming facility designed for his primary form of recreation and exercise. It is a space of solitude and connection to his aquatic nature.                         | Features a large, reinforced underwater hatch providing direct access to the open ocean for his nighttime excursions. Access is restricted to Godric alone; all others require his explicit permission. |
| **Private Training Facility** | A specialized gym connected to the pool, designed to help him calibrate his titanic strength within his human form. He uses it primarily for strength modulation practice via weightlifting. | Constructed with reinforced materials intended to withstand his power, though still insufficient for him to use his full strength. Access is limited to Maria, Alexios, Roman, Mark, and Madison by personal invitation only. |
| **Private Quarters**        | A slightly enlarged standard quarters with a large, ocean-facing window. It serves as his space for rest, contemplation, and solitude, especially during his dormant cycle.              | Maintained with spartan minimalism. It contains only his wardrobe and a small collection of significant personal artifacts. Its cleanliness and order are a direct reflection of his mental state.       |
| **Command Center Claims**   | Though a shared space, Godric has established subtle, unspoken territorial claims. Other personnel instinctively avoid his preferred stations and observation windows.               | These markers are recognized by all Monarch staff and fellow Titans, who defer to his claims without any verbal command ever being issued.                                                      |

#### B. Significant Possessions & Artifacts

Godric owns very few personal items. Each one is either a direct link to his history or a tool with a specific, practical purpose.

| Possession / Artifact        | Description                                                                                                                                                                                | Status & Significance                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| :--------------------------- | :----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Eiji Serizawa's Watch**    | A pocket watch, its hands frozen at the time of the Hiroshima bombing. Entrusted to Mark by Ishiro Serizawa just before Ishiro's own sacrifice.                                                  | Non-functional. Godric keeps it as a powerful memento of sacrifice and the interconnected history of his existence with the Serizawa family. He is debating whether to make it functional again.                            |
| **The Serizawa Notebooks**   | Several leather-bound notebooks containing the personal thoughts and decades of research on Godzilla by both Ishiro and his father, Eiji. Passed down from Vivienne Graham to Mark, then to Godric. | Actively read. He occasionally muses on their theories about him, finding humor and a strange sense of connection to the men who dedicated their lives to understanding him.                                                       |
| **Dagon's Dorsal Fin**       | The tip of a dorsal fin taken from his father's skeleton (the one discovered in 1999). It is housed in a special pendant that he wears for significant Titan matters.                         | A sacred artifact. It is the physical representation of his paternal lineage and serves as the Northern Faction's contribution to the forging of his obsidian crown.                                                               |
| **The Pickup Truck**         | A powerful, unembellished, black pickup truck. He declined to have it run on atomic energy, preferring to experience vehicle ownership authentically.                                         | **A Tool for Sovereignty.** It is his primary means of achieving true solitude. He uses it for his recreational stargazing, creating a personal, mobile territory completely independent of Monarch's direct oversight. |
| **Modern Technology**        | A smartphone and tablet, both top-of-the-line models purchased by Madison. A plain, black leather wallet. A collection of IDs for various countries and Monarch facilities.                 | **Largely Ignored.** He rarely, if ever, uses the phone and tablet, which are almost always in Madison's possession. His wallet is usually empty, as Monarch handles his finances. The IDs are a formality he rarely needs. |

#### C. Financial Independence: The Atlantean Revenue Stream

Godric maintains complete financial independence from Monarch through a unique and historically significant method.

| Revenue Source             | Method of Acquisition                                                                                                                                                           | Impact & Outcome                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| :------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | :---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Atlantean Artifacts**    | Using his perfect memory of his time as the region's “ward,” he personally retrieves priceless, authenticated artifacts from the sunken city of Atlantis, dated between 12,000-14,000 years old. | **Paradigm-Shifting Discovery:** The artifacts provide the first physical proof of the mythological civilization, shattering previous archaeological assumptions.                                                 |
| **Monarch Partnership**    | He transfers the artifacts to Monarch, who then arranges for them to be loaned to museums worldwide. Monarch negotiates generous compensation on his behalf.                        | **Complete Financial Independence:** The revenue generated is sufficient to fund “several generations,” covering all personal expenses and allowing him to operate without any reliance on Monarch's budget. |
