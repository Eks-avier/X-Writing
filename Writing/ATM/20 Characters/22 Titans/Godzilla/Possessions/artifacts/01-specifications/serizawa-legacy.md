# The Serizawa Legacy: Watch and Notebooks

## Eiji Serizawa's Watch

### Description

A pocket watch, its hands frozen at the time of the Hiroshima bombing. Entrusted to Mark by Ishiro Serizawa just before Ishiro's own sacrifice.

### Status

**Functional State:** Non-functional (hands frozen)

**Current Decision:** Godric is debating whether to make it functional again

**Why Debate Exists:**
- Making it functional = practical use
- Leaving it frozen = memorial to specific moment
- Restoration vs. preservation tension

### Significance

**Memorial Function:**
- Powerful memento of sacrifice
- Links to Serizawa family history
- Represents interconnected history of his existence with the Serizawas

**Three Generations:**
1. Eiji Serizawa - Hiroshima survivor, original owner
2. Ishiro Serizawa - Inherited watch, sacrificed self for Godzilla
3. Mark Russell - Received watch, passed to Godric

**Symbolic Weight:** The frozen time represents the moment that changed everything—for Japan, for the Serizawas, for Godzilla

### Storage

**Location:** Private quarters, Castle Bravo

**Display:** Kept in protective case, visible but secured

**Handling:** Rarely touched, viewed more than handled

## The Serizawa Notebooks

### Description

Several leather-bound notebooks containing the personal thoughts and decades of research on Godzilla by both Ishiro and his father, Eiji. Passed down from Vivienne Graham to Mark, then to Godric.

### Status

**Actively read:** Godric occasionally muses on their theories about him

**Contents:**
- Behavioral observations
- Power estimates (often hilariously low)
- Ecological impact theories
- Personal reflections on Godzilla's nature
- Father-son correspondence about "the King"

### Godric's Reaction

**Finds humor:** Human attempts to understand him from outside

**Finds connection:** Strange sense of intimacy with men who dedicated lives to studying him

**Quotes from Godric:** "They would speculate about my diet. I was eating nuclear waste and they thought it was fish."

### The Paradox

**They never met him as himself**—knew him only as towering monster

**Yet they understood something true**—the role he played, the weight he carried

**Result:** Godric feels odd kinship with them despite never speaking

### Storage

**Location:** Private quarters, Castle Bravo

**Organization:** Chronological by date written

**Preservation:** Climate-controlled environment (notebooks are 60+ years old)

## The Transfer Chain

### Passage Through Hands

| Person | Role | Action |
|--------|------|--------|
| **Eiji Serizawa** | Original owner (watch), researcher (notebooks) | Created legacy |
| **Ishiro Serizawa** | Inheritor, continued research | Passed watch to Mark before sacrifice |
| **Vivienne Graham** | Monarch archivist | Preserved notebooks, passed to Mark |
| **Mark Russell** | Monarch director | Recognized Godric should have them |
| **Godric Nordson** | Subject of research | Final inheritor, lives the legacy |

**The Full Circle:** The subject of study now possesses the studies

---

## Why Mark Gave Them to Godric

**Reason 1: Rightful Owner**
- These artifacts concern Godzilla directly
- Godric is Godzilla
- He should possess what was written about him

**Reason 2: Memorial**
- Ishiro died saving Godzilla
- Godzilla (Godric) should remember that sacrifice
- Physical objects anchor memory

**Reason 3: Connection**
- Mark understands Godric values human connections
- The Serizawas represent humans who saw him as more than weapon
- Artifacts foster that humanity
