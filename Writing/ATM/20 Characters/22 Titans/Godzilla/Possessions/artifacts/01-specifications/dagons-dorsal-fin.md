# Dagon's Dorsal Fin: The Sacred Pendant

## Description

The tip of a dorsal fin taken from his father's skeleton (the one discovered in 1999). It is housed in a special pendant that he wears for significant Titan matters.

---

## Physical Specifications

### The Fin Fragment

**Source:** Dagon's skeleton (Philippines, 1999 discovery)

**Size:** Approximately 3 inches long, 1 inch wide

**Condition:** Fossilized but retaining energetic signature

**Properties:**
- Still contains trace atomic energy
- Recognizes Godric's signature (family resonance)
- Indestructible (dorsal fin material)

### The Pendant Housing

**Material:** Platinum casing (Maria's design)

**Size:** Fits against chest, beneath shirt

**Security:** Cannot be removed except by Godric or Maria

**Design:** Simple, elegant, befitting sacred object

---

## Status and Significance

### Sacred Artifact

**This is not jewelry**—this is reliquary

**Religious Weight:**
- Physical representation of paternal lineage
- Last tangible piece of father
- Connection to 250 million years of *Titanus gojira* history

### The Northern Faction Contribution

**Context:** When Godric forges his obsidian crown (symbolic Alpha Paramount authority), each faction contributes something

**Northern Faction's Gift:** Tip of Dagon's dorsal fin

**Meaning:** The North's contribution = family, continuity, unbroken line

**Why This Matters:** Crown incorporates father's essence—Godric rules with Dagon's blessing (symbolically)

---

## Usage Protocol

### When Worn

**Significant Titan Matters Only:**
- Titan Council sessions
- Territorial disputes
- Coronation ceremonies
- Inter-species diplomatic events

**NOT Worn For:**
- Daily activities
- Human-facing events
- Private family time
- Casual Titan interactions

### When Not Worn

**Storage:** Private quarters, Castle Bravo

**Location:** Special case, temperature-controlled

**Why Too Precious for Daily Carry:** Risk of loss or damage unacceptable

**Exception:** Will wear under clothing for significant personal moments (no one sees it, but he knows it's there)

---

## The 1999 Discovery Context

### How the Fin Was Acquired

**Discovery:** Monarch found Dagon's skeleton in Philippines (1999)

**Godric's Knowledge:** Didn't learn of discovery until 2014 (stayed isolated)

**Retrieval:** After transformation (2019), he requested permission to take one fin tip

**Monarch's Response:** Granted immediately (recognized sacred nature)

### The Emotional Weight

**This is all he has** of his father physically

**Dagon died 250 million years ago** but skeleton preserved

**The pendant = bridge** across geological time

## The Pendant Exception

As documented in [[../../Lifestyle/clothing/02-narrative/accessories|Accessories]], the pendant is explicitly NOT an "accessory"—it's a sacred artifact.

**Distinction from accessories:**
- Cufflinks = functional/power indicator
- Ties = social necessity (hated)
- Watch/notebooks = memorial objects
- **Pendant = family soul**

### Why He Keeps It In Quarters

**Public Risk:** Loss or theft unthinkable

**Private Sanctity:** Too precious for external exposure

**Spiritual Center:** Having it nearby (even when not worn) provides comfort

**Symbolism:** Father's presence in his personal domain
