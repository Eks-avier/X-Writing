## Key Concepts

### The 06:00 Absolute Law
The day starts at 06:00 sharp—non-negotiable household law. Even Godric and Maria (who linger longest) respect this structure.

### Domestic Sovereignty
> *"They aren't pretending to be royal; they are simply living at the scale of power they possess."*

The fusion of royal court protocols with self-sufficient homesteading—no staff, no modern appliances, just biological power and family labor.

### The Breakfast Rotation
Each family member cooks on their assigned day, expressing their personality through food:
- Monday (Godric): Minimalist efficiency
- Tuesday (Maria): High Tea elegance
- Wednesday (Darius): The Feast
- Thursday (Rose): Experimental fusion
- Friday (Junior): Precise macros
- Saturday (Lora): Spicy energy
- Sunday (Leo): Chaotic creativity

### The Technology of Titans
Living without modern appliances as the "ultimate flex":
- Godric/Darius's hands = induction burner
- Telekinesis = blender
- Scylla's Ever-Ice = refrigerator
- Maria's glow = lights
