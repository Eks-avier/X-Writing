# Daily Schedule: The 06:00-22:00 Routine

> *"They aren't pretending to be royal; they are simply living at the scale of power they possess."*

The Nordson family's daily routine on [[../../../../60 Concepts/Monster Island|Monster Island]] operates with military precision, yet accommodates both rugged homesteading and royal court life.

---

## Morning Block (06:00 - 12:00)

### 06:00 - The Bell

The Nordson day starts at **06:00 sharp**. This is absolute law in the household.

**Wake-Up Response Pattern:**
| Family Member | Response |
|---------------|----------|
| Junior, Leo, Lora | Up instantly—biological clocks set to predatory precision |
| Darius & Rose | Moving immediately |
| Godric & Maria | *Lingering*—one indulgence they allow themselves |

**Note:** The irony of Godric and Maria skimp the most at this, considering their *activities* during the night.

### 06:30 - Breakfast (Rotating Chef System)

Each family member cooks on their assigned day.

### 07:00 - The Split

**Godric & Darius** (The Atomic Smoker):
- Stand on the shore
- Exhale controlled streams of blue atomic vapor
- Pure biological release and bonding ritual

**Junior** (The Morning Concert):
- Plays **Bach's Cello Suite No. 1** in the Palace Living Room
- **Vivaldi Protocol**: On first day of new season, entire family performs *Four Seasons*

### 08:00 - 11:00 - Division of Labor

| Group | Activity |
|-------|----------|
| **Godric Sr. & Jr.** | Hunting, territory patrol (hiking), sparring |
| **Lora & Rose** | Gardens—produce checking, harvesting, climbing fruit trees |
| **Leo** | Bouncing around island, playing with fauna, swimming |
| **Darius & Maria** | Managing stock, conversing, Palace maintenance |

### 11:00 - 12:00 - The Convergence

Junior returns from hunting. Family reconvenes for lunch.

**Lunch:** Maria is always the cook. Leo arrives at the latest possible time.

---

## Afternoon Block (12:30 - 18:30)

### 12:30 - 14:30 - The Quiet Industry

**Individual Disciplines:**
| Child | Activity |
|-------|----------|
| **Junior** | Retires to forge—cleaning blades, checking damages, brainstorming |
| **Leo** | Patches ruined clothes in living room (perfectly) |
| **Lora** | Lounges, reading sorcery books from Uncle Barth |

**Adults:** Free time. Monarch contacts (Mark/Madison) usually during this window—resolved in under an hour.

### 14:00 - The Reconvene

If Junior is quick, all three siblings in living room by 14:00. Uncle [[../../../Battra, the Lord of the Mystic Arts|Bartholomew]] might visit.

### 15:00 - The Mozart Truce

Leo (violin) or Lora (piano) perform **Mozart concertos** together.

**Significance:** The only time where Leo and Lora aren't bickering. The Hurricane and the Volcano become one musical thought.

### 15:30 - 17:00 - The Titan Games

Godric, Darius, Junior, Leo, and Lora go outside for sports.

**Darius chooses the game.** This is where he shines as the "Fun Grandpa."

### 17:00 - 18:00 - Various Activities

**Rose:** Follows Bartholomew to Dormitory to teach [[../../../../60 Concepts/Keep Charlie|Keep Charlie]] students.

**Maria and Godric:** Maria beckons her husband to come "play" with her. **We know how this ends.**

### 18:30 - Dinner

---

## Evening Block (19:00 - 22:00)

### 19:00 - Dishes

**The Rule:** After dinner, the children clean. (Maria covers everything at lunch.)

| Child | Style |
|-------|-------|
| Junior | Surgical precision (Efficiency) |
| Leo | Telekinetically dries plates to show off (Creativity) |
| Lora | Organizes cupboards (Order) |

Godric and Maria watch from the doorway, leaning against each other.

### 20:00 - The Nocturnal Symphony

**The Parents (The Abyss and the Aether):**
- Godric on **Double Bass**, Maria on **Violin**
- Play *Handel/Halvorsen's Passacaglia* or *Ravel's Pavane*
- The room vibrates at the frequency of the universe—the sound of the **Eclipse**

**The Twins (The Future Melody):**
- Junior (Cello) and Leo (Violin)
- Play *Dvorak's Humoresque* or Romantic pieces
- Junior's cello adds Heisei gravity to Leo's Showa playfulness

### 21:00 - The Unwinding

**The Family Pile:**
- Godric acts as "Central Heat Source"
- Junior and Leo lean against his sides
- Maria and Lora sit together, finishing crochet or discussing books

**Board Games:** Godric is a monster at Chess. Darius tells "Son of an Iceberg" stories.

### 22:00 - Lights Out

**"So Long, Farewell":** When the day has been eventful, one family member starts the *Sound of Music* melody as they retire. Playful meta-reference to their "Goddard" days in Meadowvale.

**Final Goodnights:**
- Darius gives hearty back-slaps to grandsons
- Rose leaves gentle floral scent in their rooms
- Bartholomew telepathically pokes them one last time

### 22:00+ - The Master Suite

The children sleep. Godric and Maria finally have the world to themselves. They shed the "Sovereign" masks. They are two souls who waited 250 million years to finally be **This Close**.
