# Education Philosophy: The Academy of the Palace

> *"No amount of human education, even from the most prestigious institutions, could ever satisfy them."*

The Nordson children do **not** attend school. They don't need to. Traditional education would be a **sensory and psychological prison** for beings who possess superhuman capabilities.

---

## I. The Fishbowl Problem

For Junior, Leo, and Lora, a human school isn't just "easy"—it's **claustrophobic**.

### Sensory Overload
They can hear the heartbeats of everyone in the building, the hum of the electrical grid, and the microscopic shifting of the foundation. A classroom is a chaotic storm of data they must manually filter.

### Physical Restraint
Every moment with "Normals" is a high-stakes exercise in **Modulation**:

| Child | Challenge |
|-------|-----------|
| **Junior** | Must not accidentally magnify the force of a door handle |
| **Lora** | Must ensure her "sticky" aura doesn't bind classmates to desks |
| **Leo** | Must fight the urge to launch through the roof when bored |

### Cognitive Gap
They don't learn; they **assimilate**. Subjects humans spend years on, they master in weeks. Spending a year on a subject understood in an afternoon is temporal torture.

Even **Lora**, the weakest of the three in physical strength, can lift a house comfortably on one hand.

---

## II. The Faculty of the Palace

The children have the most over-qualified teaching staff in history. Their curriculum isn't about passing tests—it's about **Sovereignty**.

### The Instructors

| Teacher | Subject | Focus |
|---------|---------|-------|
| **Aunt Rose** | Mundane Subjects | Math, Science, Literature, Human History—the "Human Link" |
| **Maria** | Ethics & Morality | The "Why"—ensuring power is directed by moral compass |
| **Godric** | Titan Hierarchy, Duty, Responsibility | The "How"—frontiersman training |
| **Darius** | *Titanus gojira* History | What he remembers of their species |
| **Uncle Barth** | Magic (Lora only), Human History | The "Hidden Truths"—real history humans got wrong |
| **Kong** | Swordsmanship (Junior, initially) | Foundation that Junior later transcended |

### Rose: The Human Link

She was **Erika Shiragami** in her first life. Teaching allows her to reconnect with that identity. She ensures the children understand human art, history, and science—the reason they can pass as humans at all.

### Maria: The Divine Ethicist

She teaches that a King's first duty is to the smallest of his subjects. Power without conscience is merely destruction.

### Godric & Darius: Lords of the Wild

They take the children on **Frontier Treks** where the only lesson is survival and dominance. They teach how to read the land, understand the hierarchy of the wild, and carry the absolute weight of one's word.

---

## III. The Keep Charlie Connection

The [[60 Concepts/Keep Charlie|Keep Charlie]] students are the **only peers** the Nordson children can have.

### The Peer Problem

If the students of Keep Charlie—who are effectively superhuman—cannot find peers among elite humans, then what of the sons and daughter of the two most powerful entities on Earth?

### The Feedback Loop

| Nordsons Provide | Students Provide |
|------------------|------------------|
| Help Keep Charlie feel less like aliens | Help children experience human culture |
| Connection to absolute power | Connection to human normalcy |
| Protection and belonging | Social grounding |

### The Slowed Generation

The Keep Charlie students (now in their 30s by 2045) are the only humans who can "take it":
- A high-level telekinetic like Suki can spar with Leo without dying
- A sorcerer like Andrew can discuss the abstract geometry of power with Junior

**Proximity Effect:** Years of living near the "Sun" (Godzilla) and "Moon" (Mothra) has saturated their cells with high-density energy. In 2045, you have 35-year-old humans who look 25, hanging out with 16-year-old Titans who look 20.

---

## IV. The 2045 Field Trip

The Nordson trio has visited schools—usually as field trips with Keep Charlie students. At age 16, the results were predictable:

**The Visual Impact:**
Due to their [[uncanny-perfection|Uncanny Perfection]], the student body went into collective shock. They looked like "Old Money" royalty from a country that doesn't exist.

**The Social Dynamics:**

| Child | Behavior |
|-------|----------|
| **Junior** | Stood in the library, looking at books with nostalgic pity for how "thin" the knowledge was |
| **Leo** | Got in trouble for trying to "improve" the gym equipment |
| **Lora** | Center of every social orbit—terrified them by being more articulate, faster, and more intimidating than everyone combined |

**The Lesson:** The trip reminded them **why** they don't go to school. They see the fragility of human teens and it reinforces their duty to protect that world—even if they can never truly be part of it.

---

## V. Junior's Observed Mastery

The fact that Junior moved past [[Kong|Kong's]] teaching to "observed mastery" is testament to his [[godric_hyperthymesia|hyperthymesia]].

- He didn't just practice; he **archived** every sword style humanity ever recorded
- He watched footage of Kendo masters, HEMA practitioners, ancient Atlantean guards
- By 16, he was a **synthesis of every blade-user to ever live**

Kong likely sparred with him one day and realized: *"I have nothing left to teach. He isn't following my lead anymore; he's writing his own."*

---

## VI. Thematic Significance

The Nordsons are a **True Diaspora**—a civilization of five (plus extended family) living in a world that is their "Territory" but no longer their "Home."

The Keep Charlie students are the **Ambassadors**—the only ones who can walk in both worlds, ensuring that the Princes and Princess of the Monsters don't become the "Aloof Gods" that Mothra and Battra were before 2020.
