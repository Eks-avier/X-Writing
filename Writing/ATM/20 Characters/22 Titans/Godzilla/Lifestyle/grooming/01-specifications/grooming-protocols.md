# Grooming Protocols: The Daily Regimen

Godric approaches personal grooming with the same methodical precision he applies to global territorial management. It is a domain to be conquered and perfected.

---

## The Hyperthymesia Advantage in Skill Acquisition

Godric's perfect memory allowed him to achieve complete mastery over his own grooming with astonishing speed.

### Learning Process

**Phase 1: Observation (Sessions 1-5)**
- Silently recorded every snip of scissors during mandatory Monarch barber sessions
- Perfect memory captured every pass of the razor
- Absorbed angle, pressure, technique without conscious effort

**Phase 2: Mastery (Session 6+)**
- Rendered barbers obsolete after just five sessions
- Demonstration of drive for absolute self-sufficiency
- Never required external grooming services again

### The Cognitive Engine

> **Corpus Source:** BSaga-TG P369

> [!quote] The Perfect Record
> "He doesn't just 'remember'; he **relives.** Every sensation, every heartbreak, every second of 250 million years is accessible in 4K resolution with full sensory playback."

**Skill Acquisition Pattern:**
- Godric doesn't "learn" grooming techniques—he **records** them
- Five sessions → complete muscle memory replication
- Mirror avoidance compensated by proprioceptive precision

---

## Daily Grooming Protocol

### Facial Hair: The Clean-Shaven Standard

**Frequency:** Daily during Active Season (December-August)

**Equipment:**
- Classic straight razor
- Specialized titanium-alloyed blade (conventional steel dulls instantly)
- No shaving cream (regeneration handles micro-cuts)

**Technique:**
- Achieves perfect precision through proprioception and touch alone
- Often performs without mirror
- Blade angle memorized from recorded barber sessions

**Seasonal Exception:** During Dormant Season (September-November), grooming relaxes—stubble appears more frequently.

### Head Hair: The 4-6 Week Cycle

**Style:** Short, practical, long enough to sweep back but short enough to stay out of eyes during activity

**Frequency:** Every 4-6 weeks

**Technique:**
- Self-administered cuts
- Scissors technique replicated from Monarch barbers
- Perfect consistency through hyperthymestic muscle memory
- No mirror required—proprioception sufficient

**Maintenance:** Daily brushing/styling takes under 30 seconds

### Body Hair: The Strategic Surrender

**Initial Attempt (Months 1-2 post-transformation):**
- Complete body shaving attempted
- Goal: Maximum grooming control

**Outcome:**
- Deemed "colossal waste of time and resources"
- Hair's rapid growth and resistance made daily shaving impractical
- Regeneration meant micro-cuts healed before shaving completed

**Current Protocol:**
- Maintains natural state
- Rare but pragmatic concession to his own biology
- One of few domains where he accepted biological limitation

## Tools and Equipment

### The Titanium Razor

**Specifications:**
- Custom-forged titanium alloy blade
- Only material that maintains edge against his hair's density
- Conventional steel dulls after 2-3 passes

**Maintenance:**
- Sharpening every 2 weeks
- Blade replacement every 6 months
- Stored in private quarters

### The Scissors

**Type:** Professional barber scissors (high-carbon steel)

**Replacement:** Every 3 months (dulling from hair density)

**Storage:** Medicine cabinet in private quarters

### The Absence of Product

**No Hair Product:** Natural texture maintained

**No Shaving Cream:** Regeneration handles micro-cuts

**No Cologne:** Natural scent sufficient (see [[../../../Psychology/godric-scent-profile|Scent Profile]])

---

## Temporal Patterns

### Active Season (December-August)

**Standard:** Daily facial shaving, haircuts every 4 weeks

**Reasoning:** Public-facing role requires consistent presentation

**Result:** "Impeccable presentation" as default state

### Dormant Season (September-November)

**Relaxed:** Shaving 3-4x per week, haircuts every 6 weeks

**Reasoning:** Reduced public appearances during dormancy

**Result:** Stubble and slightly longer hair—ironically more attractive to Maria (see [[../03-thematic/self-mastery#The Grooming Paradox|The Grooming Paradox]])

---

## The Mirror Avoidance Phenomenon

Godric rarely uses mirrors for grooming.

**Why:**
- [[../../../Psychology/mirror-avoidance-syndrome|Mirror Avoidance Syndrome]]—dislikes seeing his reflection
- Perfect proprioception makes mirrors unnecessary
- Hyperthymesia provides complete mental map of his face

**Exception:** Haircuts occasionally require mirror for back-of-head verification
