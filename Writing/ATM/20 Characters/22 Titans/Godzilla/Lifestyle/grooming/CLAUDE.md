## Key Concepts

### The Five-Session Mastery
Godric achieved complete grooming independence after just five Monarch barber sessions through hyperthymestic recording—observing once, replaying perfectly, mastering completely.

### The Grooming Paradox
**His Goal:** Impeccable presentation through relentless grooming
**Maria's Response:** Most attracted when presentation "lapses" (stubble, unkempt hair)
**The Irony:** Territorial instinct to eliminate disorder removes what she finds captivating

### The Body Hair Surrender
One of the few domains where Godric accepted biological limitation—attempted complete body shaving for two months before deeming it impractical. Rare pragmatism overriding perfectionism.

### Mirror-Free Technique
Performs grooming through proprioception and hyperthymestic body mapping—mirrors unnecessary and psychologically avoided (see [[../../Psychology/mirror-avoidance-syndrome|Mirror Avoidance Syndrome]]).
