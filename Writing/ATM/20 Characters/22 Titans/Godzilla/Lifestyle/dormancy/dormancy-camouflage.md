#### 2. The Winter Camouflage Strategy

Godric leverages human seasonal behaviors to mask the physical changes of his dormancy. As his cycle coincides with autumn and early winter, he adopts a “winter camouflage” strategy.
*   **Layering:** He makes his undershirt a standard part of his daily attire and begins regularly incorporating his waistcoat or a sweater.
*   **Performance:** He engages in a calculated performance of “feeling cold,” such as gravitating towards heat sources or making token hand-warming gestures, to provide a plausible, human explanation for his increased layering and bulk.

#### 3. The “Hibernation Misunderstanding” as Operational Cover

Most Monarch personnel misinterpret his dormancy as animalistic hibernation, an error he deliberately allows to persist.
*   **The Misconception:** Staff assume he is sleeping deeply, with a slowed metabolism, to conserve energy through the winter.
*   **The Reality:** He is often at his most mentally active during this period, and his metabolism is actively processing conventional food. It is a period of planning, not unconsciousness.
*   **Strategic Advantage:** This misunderstanding provides him with the perfect operational cover. It ensures he is disturbed less frequently, giving him uninterrupted time for contemplation and the formulation of long-term strategies for managing the planet and the Titan hierarchy. He finds no reason to expend the energy to correct a misconception that serves his purposes so well.
