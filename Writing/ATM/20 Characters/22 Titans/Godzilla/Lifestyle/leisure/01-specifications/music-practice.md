# Music Practice: Daily Performances

## Godric's Instrument: The Double Bass

**Chosen Instrument:** Double Bass (upright bass)

**Why This Instrument:**
- Represents "The Abyss"—deep, resonant, foundational
- Complements Maria's violin ("The Aether")
- Requires immense physical control (perfect for his strength)
- Creates frequency that "vibrates at the frequency of the universe"

**Mastery Level:** Professional (through hyperthymestic observation)

---

## Daily Performance Schedule

### 20:00 - The Nocturnal Symphony (The Parents)

**Configuration:** Godric (Double Bass) + Maria (Violin)

**Standard Repertoire:**
- Handel/Halvorsen's *Passacaglia*
- Ravel's *Pavane*
- Other Romantic/Baroque duets

**Significance:**
- "The sound of the Eclipse"
- The Abyss (tangible/earth/depth) meets The Aether (intangible/sky/height)
- Perfect musical representation of their cosmic complementarity

**Duration:** 30-45 minutes

**Audience:** Family (occasionally Keep Charlie students visit)

## Learning Method: Hyperthymestic Observation

**How Godric Learned Bass:**
1. Watched professional bassist performances (video/live)
2. Perfect memory recorded finger placement, bow technique, posture
3. Replayed recordings mentally with full sensory detail
4. Achieved mastery without formal instruction

**Time to Competence:** ~3 months from first observation to professional level

**Same Method Applied To:**
- Maria's violin style (to complement her playing)
- Specific pieces in repertoire
- Musical theory through observation

## The Eclipse Sound

### What Makes Their Duet Unique

**Godric's Bass:**
- Represents earth, depth, gravity, the tangible
- Provides foundation and structure
- Resonates at frequencies that affect physical space

**Maria's Violin:**
- Represents sky, height, grace, the intangible
- Provides melody and emotional expression
- Resonates at frequencies that affect emotional state

**Together:**
- Create "the frequency of the universe"
- Room physically vibrates
- Listeners report feeling the music in their bones
- Perfect synthesis of opposing forces

## Equipment

### The Custom Bass

**Specifications:**
- Reinforced construction (normal bass would shatter under his grip)
- Scale-silk strings (conventional strings snap)
- Weighted to accommodate his strength
- Junior-crafted hardware

**Maintenance:** Weekly tuning, monthly string replacement

### The Bow

**Material:** Composite of conventional horsehair and Maria's silk

**Why:** Standard bow breaks after 2-3 uses

**Result:** Produces unique tonal quality—deeper resonance than conventional
