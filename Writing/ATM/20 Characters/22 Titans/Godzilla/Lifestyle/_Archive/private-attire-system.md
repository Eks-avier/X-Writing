#### 5. Private Attire: The Reductive Relaxation System

Godric's approach to leisure wear is one of **reductive relaxation**. He does not own “comfort clothing” like sweatpants or loungewear, as he views such garments as inefficient and purposeless. For him, comfort is achieved not through substitution but through the systematic removal of his public-facing “armor” based on the projected duration and security status of his downtime.

##### A. The Philosophy of Absence & The Loungewear Impossibility

Dedicated loungewear fundamentally contradicts Godric's mindset. Every moment is an opportunity for territorial management or readiness, making clothing designed for “doing nothing” a philosophical impossibility. Relaxation is achieved through varying his state of readiness, not by changing into a different category of costume.

##### B. The Progressive Reduction Levels

His private attire is determined by a simple, logical decision tree:

| Level     | Configuration                                | Trigger                                       | Resulting Attire                                                                                                                                                                             |
| :-------- | :------------------------------------------- | :-------------------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **One**   | Minimal Adjustment                           | Downtime under 3 hours.                       | **Bare Chest or Undershirt.** He removes only his dress shirt, retaining his pants and socks. His state depends on whether he was wearing an undershirt that day as part of his “winter camouflage.” |
| **Two**   | Extended Comfort                             | Downtime over 3 hours, standard alert status. | **T-Shirt/Henley & Underwear.** Dress shirt and pants are removed. He will don one of his few T-shirts or henleys.                                                                         |
| **Three** | Maximum Reduction / The Swimming Exception   | Extended downtime, low alert or swimming.     | **Underwear or Swimming Trunks Only.** The final level of reduction. Swimming trunks are his sole specialized leisure garment, a necessary compromise for his preferred recreational activity. |
