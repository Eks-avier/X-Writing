# Leisure Activities

> *"The truck is the only place where the Domestic Fantasy meets the Open Road."*

Despite their cosmic significance, the Nordsons engage in surprisingly "mundane" leisure activities—though always at their own scale.

---

## I. The Chariot of the North

**Godric's pickup truck** is the only piece of human technology that exists in proximity to The Palace. It has become the **Mobile Palace**.

### Driving Status

| Child | Status |
|-------|--------|
| **Junior** | Earned driver's license at 16 (Madison helped expedite) |
| **Leo** | Can drive, prefers not to—argues they can fly anyway |
| **Lora** | Can drive, just for the giggles |

### Sibling Trip Configuration

| Position | Occupant | Notes |
|----------|----------|-------|
| Driver | Junior | The "Prince Regent"—responsible, follows rules |
| Shotgun | Leo | Does zero navigation, controls "music" |
| Back Seat | Lora | Claims the "Throne," sprawls, demands climate control |

**The Irony:** They could fly to their destination in seconds, but choose the truck because **the journey is the bonding time**.

### Nuclear Family Configuration

| Position | Occupant | Notes |
|----------|----------|-------|
| Driver | Godric | Has the earth's electromagnetic field as GPS |
| Passenger | Maria | Hand on his neck/shoulder, keeping connection |
| Back Seat | Junior (middle), Leo, Lora | Junior is the DMZ between Hurricane and Volcano |
| Truck Bed | Leo (when exiled) | Prefers it—wind in his hair, closer to flying |

**When Just Junior and Lora:** Lora sleeps on Junior's shoulder—showing she trusts him implicitly despite her prickliness.

### Full Clan Configuration

**The Cabin (Sanctuary of Grace):**
- Godric (Driver), Maria, **Lora, and Aunt Rose**
- The "Two Roses" claim the back seat
- Quiet conversation, warmth, safety

**The Truck Bed (The Northern Expedition):**
- **Darius, Junior, and Leo**
- Pure Showa energy
- Darius leans against the cab, wind in his mustache, pointing out landmarks: *"Back in the Permian, that mountain was three feet to the left!"*
- Leo vibrating with excitement, trying to catch bugs at 60mph

**Technical Note:** Godric uses **Structural Infusion** on the suspension. Three male Nordsons (~400kg of ultra-dense Titan muscle) would otherwise drag the bumper on asphalt.

## II. Outdoor Games

The Titan Games occur daily (15:30-17:00) but represent their primary leisure activity.

### The Sports

| Activity | Notes |
|----------|-------|
| **Rugby** | Godric Sr. is immovable wall; Darius is tackling machine; Leo tries to jump *over* them |
| **Baseball** | Godric batting hits the ball into the stratosphere |
| **Football** | Standard chaos |
| **Volleyball** | Requires reinforced equipment |
| **Wrestling** | Father-son bonding |
| **Sparring** | Training disguised as play |

### The Equipment

Junior forges all equipment:
- Normal sports gear would vaporize
- Balls made of **woven scale-silk and weighted cores**
- Heavy enough to kill a normal human, perfect for Titans

**Darius always chooses the game.** This is where he shines as the "Fun Grandpa."

Music permeates Nordson leisure life.

### Daily Performances

|------|--------------|--------|
| 07:00 | Junior (Cello) | Bach's Cello Suite No. 1 |

On the first day of each season, the entire family performs **Vivaldi's Four Seasons** together.

## V. Swimming

Godric and Darius frequently swim in the morning hours, particularly during their bonding time (07:00-08:00).

For Godric, swimming is:
- Connection to his natural environment
- **[[seasonal-biology|Seasonal Biology]]** expression—cold water soothes his internal reactor
- The sole recognized "downtime" activity
- Territorial maintenance—ocean connection preserves global awareness

**The "Sneaking Out":** Madison covers for Godric when he sneaks out to swim. Even the King needs unauthorized breaks.

## VI. Winter Activities

As [[Northern Faction|Northern Gojira]], winter is a **homecoming**.

### Winter Leisure

| Activity | Participants |
|----------|--------------|
| **Shirtless Blizzard Walking** | Godric, Darius, Leo |
| **Snow Games** | The whole family |
| **Cold Region Vacations** | Siberia, Greenland, Andes |

**The Winter Migration:** [[Battra, the Lord of the Mystic Arts|Battra]] regularly teleports [[Monster Island]] to winter regions so the family can enjoy their seasonal preference.

**Mark's Rule:** Monarch doesn't cover expenses if "incidents" occur during winter vacations. (There have been incidents—leveled forests, broken glaciers.)

## VII. The Silent Phones

The children have smartphones but **never use them** inside the Palace.

| Human Teen Need | Nordson Alternative |
|-----------------|---------------------|
| Look at screen | Talk to a legendary King |
| Watch movie | Play concerto with brother |
| Browse social media | Fly with mother |

The phones are purely **Functional Tools**—for calling Aunt Madison about human idioms or Miki for psychic check-ins.
