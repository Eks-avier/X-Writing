# Daily Routines: The Regal Homestead

> *"They aren't pretending to be royal; they are simply living at the scale of power they possess."*

The Nordson family's daily routine on [[Monster Island]] represents a fusion of **rugged homesteading** and **royal court life**. To a modern human, it would seem completely alien—yet to them, it's simply Tuesday.

---

## I. Morning (06:00 - 12:00)

### 06:00 - The Bell

The Nordson day starts at **06:00 sharp**. This is absolute law in the household.

**The Wake-Up Dynamic:**
| Family Member | Response |
|---------------|----------|
| Junior, Leo, Lora | Up instantly—biological clocks set to predatory precision |
| Darius & Rose | Moving immediately |
| Godric & Maria | *Lingering*—one indulgence they allow themselves |

The irony: Godric and Maria skimp the most at this, considering their *activities* during the night.

### 06:30 - Breakfast Rotation

A tradition where each family member cooks on their assigned day:

| Day | Chef | Style |
|-----|------|-------|
| Monday | **Godric** | Minimalist, high protein, seared meat—efficient fuel |
| Tuesday | **Maria** | Delicate pastries, fruits, complex teas—High Tea breakfast |
| Wednesday | **Darius** | "The Feast"—massive portions, whole roasted game |
| Thursday | **Rose** | Experimental fusion, floral infusions |
| Friday | **Junior** | Precise, balanced macros, perfectly uniform cuts |
| Saturday | **Lora** | Spicy, energizing |
| Sunday | **Leo** | Chaos—pancakes shaped like monsters, burnt edges |

### 07:00 - The Split

**The Atomic Smoker (Godric & Darius):**
Father and son stand on the shore. They don't use cigarettes—they inhale deeply and exhale thin, controlled streams of **blue atomic vapor**. It looks like smoke but smells of ozone. Pure biological release and bonding.

**The Morning Concert (Junior):**
Junior plays the first piece—usually **Bach's Cello Suite No. 1**—in the Palace's Living Room.

**The Vivaldi Protocol:** On the first day of a new season, the whole family picks up **Vivaldi's Four Seasons** to celebrate. Spring for Spring, Winter for Winter.

### 08:00 - 11:00 - Division of Labor

| Group | Activity |
|-------|----------|
| **Godric Sr. & Jr.** | Hunting, territory "patrol" (hiking), sparring |
| **Lora & Rose** | Gardens—checking produce, harvesting, climbing fruit trees |
| **Leo** | Bouncing around the island, playing with fauna, swimming |
| **Darius & Maria** | Managing stock, conversing, Palace maintenance |

**The "Domestic Patriarch" Dynamic:** Despite the *gojira* male typically being outside, Darius stays inside. Why? For 10 HEY (Human Equivalent Years), he played both parental roles in the Late Permian.

### 11:00-12:00 - The Convergence

Junior returns from hunting. If he has no forge project, he's back by 11:30. The family reconvenes for lunch.

**Lunch:** Maria is always the cook. Leo arrives at the latest possible time.

---

## II. Afternoon (12:30 - 18:30)

### 12:30 - 14:30 - The Quiet Industry

**Individual Disciplines:**
| Child | Activity |
|-------|----------|
| **Junior** | Retires to his forge—cleaning blades, checking for damages, brainstorming projects |
| **Leo** | Patches up ruined clothes in the living room (perfectly) |
| **Lora** | Lounges, reading sorcery books from Uncle Barth |

**The Adults:** Free time. This is when Monarch (via Mark or Madison) usually contacts them. Affairs are typically resolved in under an hour.

### 14:00 - The Reconvene

If Junior is quick, all three siblings are in the living room by 14:00. Uncle [[Battra, the Lord of the Mystic Arts|Bartholomew]] might visit to chat, tease, or entertain.

### 15:00 - The Mozart Truce

**Leo and Lora's Duet:**
A solo by Leo on **violin** or Lora on **piano**, playing something from **Mozart concertos**.

This is the only time where both Leo and Lora aren't bickering like petulant children. The Hurricane and the Volcano become two halves of the same musical thought.

### 15:30 - 17:00 - The Titan Games

The "Jock" energy peaks. Godric, Darius, Junior, Leo, and Lora go outside for outdoor games.

**The Sports:**
- Football, rugby, baseball, volleyball
- Wrestling, sparring, catch
- All equipment made by Junior (normal sports gear would vaporize)

**Darius chooses the game.** This is where he shines as the "Fun Grandpa."

### 17:00 - 18:00 - Various

**Rose:** Follows Bartholomew to the Dormitory to teach mundane subjects to [[60 Concepts/Keep Charlie|Keep Charlie]] students.

**Maria and Godric:** Maria beckons her husband to come "play" with her. She tracks him from the Palace terrace. **We know how this ends.**

### 18:30 - Dinner

| Family Member | State |
|--------------|-------|
| Godric | Tired, but perfectly content |
| Maria | Glowing (literally and figuratively) |
| Darius & Leo | Tired and happy |
| Lora | Pumped up and refreshed |
| Rose | Finished grading papers |
| Bartholomew | Visiting for dinner |

---

## III. Evening (19:00 - 22:00)

### 19:00 - Dishes

**The Rule:** After dinner, the children clean. (Maria covers everything at lunch.)

| Child | Style |
|-------|-------|
| Junior | Surgical precision (Efficiency) |
| Leo | Telekinetically dries plates to show off (Creativity) |
| Lora | Organizes cupboards (Order) |

Godric and Maria watch from the doorway, leaning against each other.

### 20:00 - The Nocturnal Symphony

**The Parents (The Abyss and the Aether):**
Godric on **Double Bass**, Maria on **Violin**. They play *Handel/Halvorsen's Passacaglia* or *Ravel's Pavane*.

The room vibrates at the frequency of the universe. It is the sound of the **Eclipse**.

**The Twins (The Future Melody):**
Junior (Cello) and Leo (Violin). They play *Dvorak's Humoresque* or something Romantic. Junior's cello adds Heisei gravity to Leo's Showa playfulness on the violin.

### 21:00 - The Unwinding

**The Family Pile:** The family settles onto massive, silk-lined sofas.
- Godric acts as the "Central Heat Source"
- Junior and Leo lean against his sides
- Maria and Lora sit together, finishing crochet or discussing books

**Board Games:** Godric is a monster at Chess. Darius tells stories of "The Son of an Iceberg" days.

### 22:00 - Lights Out

**"So Long, Farewell":** When the day has been eventful, one family member starts the *Sound of Music* melody as they retire. A playful meta-reference to their "Goddard" days in Meadowvale.

**Final Goodnights:** Flurry of hugs and kisses.
- Darius gives hearty back-slaps to grandsons
- Rose leaves a gentle floral scent in their rooms
- Bartholomew telepathically pokes them one last time

### 22:00+ - The Master Suite

The children sleep. Godric and Maria finally have the world to themselves. They shed the "Sovereign" masks. They are two souls who waited 250 million years to finally be **This Close**.

---

## IV. The Technology of Titans

The Nordsons live without modern appliances—not from limitation, but as the **ultimate flex**.

| "Appliance" | Solution |
|-------------|----------|
| **Refrigerator** | Walk-in pantry with Scylla's Ever-Ice (never melts) |
| **Stove** | Godric or Darius's hand *is* the induction burner |
| **Blender** | Telekinesis spinning the fruit |
| **Lights** | Bioluminescent moss from Rose; Maria glowing softly |
| **AC** | Battra's spatial magic or Scylla's cooling totem |

The children **have smartphones** (how else would Madison contact them?). They never use them.

Why browse social media when you can fly with your mother?
