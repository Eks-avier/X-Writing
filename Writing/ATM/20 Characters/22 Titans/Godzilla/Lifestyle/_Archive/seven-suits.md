#### 4. Public Formal Attire: The Diplomatic Arsenal

For high-stakes diplomatic or social events where his daily uniform is insufficient, Godric maintains a small, strategic collection of seven suits. His selection is not based on fashion but on a methodical assessment of the event's requirements, deploying each suit to achieve a specific psychological or presentational effect.
