# Accessories: Symbols and Statements

Godric's use of accessories is minimal to the point of near-absence, making the few items he does possess incredibly significant. They are not decorations but focal points of power, relational dynamics, and personal history.

---

## Cufflinks: Integrated Power

He possesses two pairs of cufflinks, both forged from his shed scales and designed to integrate seamlessly with his French cuff shirts. They are the most overt link to his Titan nature in his formal wear.

### Formal Wear Set

**Construction:** Matte obsidian black scales

**Special Property:** Contains microscopic fragments of dorsal fins

**Effect:** Emit subtle, pulsing blue glow when he channels atomic power

**Usage:** Standard formal events, dress shirts

### Titan Regalia Set

**Construction:** Enhanced design with intricate patterns

**Special Property:** More intricate design matches ceremonial uniform aesthetic

**Effect:** More pronounced glow, clearer indicator of energy state

**Usage:** Titan Council sessions, high-stakes diplomatic events

---

## Ties: The Reluctant Necessity

Godric fundamentally **hates** wearing ties. He views them as restrictive and functionally useless. Their use is a rare concession, requiring significant negotiation from Maria or Madison for only the most critical diplomatic events where protocol is absolute. His collection is, therefore, brutally spartan.

### The Standard Collection

**Total:** 6 ties, all solid-colored

**Breakdown:**
- 3 Black
- 1 Charcoal Gray
- 1 Navy Blue
- 1 Golden (special—see below)

### The Golden Tie: Unspoken Symbol

**The Sixth Tie:** A single, beautiful golden tie, handwoven by Maria from her finest silk.

**Creation Intent:** Specifically crafted to complement his true golden eye color—a feature she is one of the few beings to have ever seen.

**Symbolic Significance:**
- Physical manifestation of their intimacy
- Her knowledge of his authentic self
- Everything they will not—or cannot—say to each other

**Current Status:** Remains in specially made case, untouched and unworn

**Thematic Weight:** The tie waits for a moment of authenticity that may never arrive. It exists as potential—the promise of a day when he shows his true self publicly.

### Why He Hates Ties

**Restriction:** Feels constrictive around throat—vulnerable area

**Function:** Serves no practical purpose (unlike all other clothing)

**Symbolism:** Represents submission to human social norms

**Concession:** Only wears when Maria or Madison negotiate it as absolutely necessary

---

## The Minimal Philosophy

### Why So Few Accessories

**Godric's Principle:** Accessories are decorative, not functional

**His Exceptions:**
- Cufflinks integrate with clothing practically (French cuffs require them)
- Cufflinks serve power indication function (energy glow)
- Ties only when protocol demands

**What He Doesn't Wear:**
- Watches (has perfect internal chronometer)
- Rings (except wedding band—see [[../../Possessions/artifacts|Artifacts]])
- Bracelets (no function)
- Necklaces (except Dagon's pendant—sacred, not decorative)
- Pocket squares (purely decorative)
- Tie clips (hates ties in first place)

**Category:** Sacred artifact, not accessory

**Status:** Too precious for daily external carry

**Location:** Permanently in private quarters when not worn for significant Titan matters

**Significance:** Represents heart of his emotional territory, protected even when armor-philosophy relaxes

## Maria's Influence

### The Silent Communication

**The Golden Tie:** Created before their relationship became explicit

**The Message:** "I see your true self, even when no one else does"

**His Response:** Kept in case, honored but unworn

**The Tension:** He cannot wear it publicly without revealing golden eyes—the ultimate vulnerability

### Her Negotiations

**Maria can convince him to wear ties** when no one else can

**Her Method:** Not command, but request as partner

**Success Rate:** ~80% when she asks

**Why It Works:** He values her judgment on human cultural navigation
