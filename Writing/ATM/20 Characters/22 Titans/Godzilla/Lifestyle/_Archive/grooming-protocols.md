#### 1. Grooming & Maintenance: The Mastery of Self

He approaches personal grooming with the same methodical precision he applies to global territorial management. It is a domain to be conquered and perfected.

*   **a. The Hyperthymesia Advantage in Skill Acquisition:** Godric's perfect memory allowed him to achieve complete mastery over his own grooming with astonishing speed. During his initial mandatory sessions with Monarch barbers, he silently recorded every snip of the scissors and every pass of the razor. After just five sessions, he had absorbed enough information to render the barbers obsolete, a demonstration of his drive for absolute self-sufficiency.
*   **b. The Daily Grooming Protocol:**
    *   **Facial Hair:** He maintains a clean-shaven appearance daily during his Active Season. He uses a classic straight razor with a specialized titanium-alloyed blade, achieving perfect precision through proprioception and touch alone, often without a mirror.
    *   **Head Hair:** He cuts his own hair every 4-6 weeks, maintaining a short, practical style that is long enough to sweep back but short enough to stay out of his eyes during activity.
    *   **Body Hair: The Strategic Surrender:** He initially attempted complete body shaving for two months before deeming the effort a colossal waste of time and resources due to his hair's rapid growth and resistance. He now maintains a natural state, a rare but pragmatic concession to his own biology.

Godric's grooming mastery exemplifies the cognitive advantage documented in [[P369 - Godric Hyperthymesia Unique Cognitive Memory (BSaga-TG)|BSaga-TG P369]]:

**Skill Acquisition Pattern:**
- Godric doesn't "learn" grooming techniques—he **records** them
- Five barber sessions → complete muscle memory replication
- Mirror avoidance compensated by proprioceptive precision

### Thematic Connection

The grooming protocols connect to the [[grooming-paradox|Grooming Paradox]]: Maria is most attracted to him when his "impeccable presentation" lapses (stubble, unkempt hair), yet his territorial instincts drive him to eliminate any perceived "disorder"—inadvertently removing what she finds captivating.
