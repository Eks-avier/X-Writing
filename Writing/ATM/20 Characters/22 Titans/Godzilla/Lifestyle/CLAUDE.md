## Key Concepts

### Domestic Sovereignty
> *"They aren't pretending to be royal; they are simply living at the scale of power they possess."*

The Nordson family lifestyle fuses royal court protocols with self-sufficient homesteading—no staff, no modern appliances, just biological power and family labor.

### The 06:00 Absolute Law
The day starts at 06:00 sharp—non-negotiable household law demonstrating discipline as territorial management applied to time itself.

### The Technology of Titans
Living without modern appliances as the "ultimate flex":
- Godric/Darius's hands = induction burner
- Telekinesis = blender
- Scylla's Ever-Ice = refrigerator
- Maria's glow = lights
- Bioluminescent moss = ambient lighting

### The Hyperthymesia Advantage
Perfect memory enabling five-session grooming mastery—doesn't "learn" techniques, **records** them with full sensory fidelity.

### The Analog Lifestyle
> *"Why browse social media when you can fly with your mother?"*

Children have smartphones but never use them for entertainment—reality superior to digital consumption.

### The Seven Suits
Strategic formal wardrobe for human diplomatic events. **See [[../Appearance/clothing/formal-suits/CLAUDE|Appearance/clothing/formal-suits/]]** for complete documentation.
