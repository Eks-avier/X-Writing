## The Dual-Force Mechanism

The Atomic Railgun is not merely a "compressed Atomic Breath"—it is a **physics-based hybrid technique**:

| Component | Force | Function |
|-----------|-------|----------|
| **The Bullet** | Pyrenikon (Nuclear) | Compressed atomic energy payload |
| **The Barrel** | Elektromagneton (EM) | Magnetic rails that accelerate the payload |

This is why the technique required Godric to consciously engage his hidden EM nature—pure Pyrenikon compression simply explodes.
