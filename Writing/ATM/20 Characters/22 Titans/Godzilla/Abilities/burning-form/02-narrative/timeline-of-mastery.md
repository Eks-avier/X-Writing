# The Taming of the Sun: Timeline of Mastery

The Burning Form is not a technique that was "learned" in a single moment. It is an ongoing journey of control that began in 2019 and continues into Era VII.

---

## 2019: The Assisted Burn (Era V)

### The Context

The [[Battle of Boston]] against [[Ghidorah]]. Godric was near death, outmatched by an opponent who could regenerate from any damage he dealt.

### The Catalysts

The first true, sustained activation required **two external catalysts**:

| Catalyst | Function |
|----------|----------|
| **Serizawa's Nuke** | External energy to jump-start the process to absolute peak |
| **[[Mothra, the Queen of the Monsters\|Mothra's]] Essence** | Calming, psionic energy acting as **temporary external regulator** |

### The Achievement

Without both catalysts, Godric would have exploded—possibly cratering approximately half of the Earth. Mothra's sacrifice provided the control he did not yet possess.

### The Significance

- First sustained Burning Form in recorded gojira history
- Proved the state was achievable (not merely theoretical)
- Demonstrated his body COULD handle it—with help
- Set the foundation for future self-regulated attempts

---

## The Battra Duel: The First Solo Flight (Era VI)

### The Context

During the [[Xilien Invasion]], Godric faced [[Battra, the Lord of the Mystic Arts|Battra]] in 3v1 combat (Battra plus two magical constructs).

### The Breakthrough

The Zone—the "uncaging of the sun"—was Godric achieving the Burning Form **on his own, without external aid**:

| Aspect | Detail |
|--------|--------|
| **Catalyst** | Intense intellectual and physical pressure |
| **Foundation** | Mastery of Atomic Amplification's efficiency principles |
| **Achievement** | Achieved delicate balance himself—pushing heart to limit while using will and adaptation to regulate output |

### The Result

A shorter, less powerful burn than 2019, but it was **his**:

- No external energy source required
- No psionic regulation from Mothra
- Pure self-control through Kratonic mastery
- Proof that he could do it alone

### The Significance

Per AA-Kratos P310:
> *"The very fact that he unlocks Burning Form all on his own in their fight, which is a symbol of his freedom, is a testament to this."*

This was not just a combat achievement—it was **ontological vindication**.

---

## 2030 and Beyond: The Ongoing Risk (Era VII)

### Current Status

Even well into the [[60 Concepts/Antitheriomorphosis|Antitheriomorphosis]] era, the Burning Form is never "safe":

| Reality | Implication |
|---------|-------------|
| **Always a risk** | Meltdown/detonation remains possible |
| **Always a last resort** | Not used casually |
| **Constantly improving** | Each use teaches the body further |

### The Ongoing Dialogue

Godric is in a constant, lifelong dialogue with the raging star inside his own heart:

- Each successful activation teaches his body to adapt further
- The balance becomes more natural over time
- The burn duration extends incrementally
- The recovery time shortens
- But it is **never routine**

### The Asymptotic Curve

Control approaches mastery but never reaches it:

```
Time →
Control: ████████████████████░░░░░ (approaching but never reaching 100%)
```

This is the nature of the Burning Form—it is power that must be **actively maintained**, not passively possessed.
