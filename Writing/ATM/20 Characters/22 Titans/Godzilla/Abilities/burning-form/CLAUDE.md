## Why Godric Succeeded

Godric is the **perfect storm**—the one gojira in history born with the perfect Engine who then spent his entire life unintentionally building the perfect Radiator:

| Requirement | Source |
|-------------|--------|
| **The Engine** | [[Astraea]]'s "flawed" heart—naturally overproduces energy |
| **The Radiator** | 250 million years of combat + hyper-regeneration + human experience |

He didn't achieve the Burning Form through talent or destiny. He achieved it through **biology meeting circumstance meeting will**.
