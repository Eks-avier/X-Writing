# Hidden Elektromagneton Nature

## The Chisel He Doesn't Know He's Holding

Godric Nordson is a **Holariston Polykrator**—a being whose Krátos manifests as two fundamental forces. While his [[Writing/ATM/20 Characters/22 Titans/Godzilla/_species-reference/Pyrenikon|Pyrenikon]] (nuclear) nature has always been consciously acknowledged and trained, his [[Writing/ATM/20 Characters/22 Titans/Godzilla/_species-reference/Elektromagneton|Elektromagneton]] (electromagnetic) nature remains unconscious and culturally ignored.

He is the sculptor who knows only about the clay, unaware that he holds a chisel in his other hand.

### What He Actually IS

***Titanus gojira* are dual-force beings**—every member of the species manifests both:

| Force | Character | Godric's Awareness |
|-------|-----------|-------------------|
| **Pyrenikon** (Nuclear) | "The Wild"—chaotic, explosive, expansive | ✅ Fully conscious, actively trained |
| **Elektromagneton** (EM) | "The Order"—structured, precise, containing | ❌ Unconscious, passively used |

This dual nature is **species-universal**. It's not a hidden power Godric uniquely possesses—it's what ALL gojira are. The difference lies entirely in awareness and application.

> *"Despite the proto-gojira and their eventual descendants in the Four Factions not even being aware of the ability, it existed."*
> — AA-Kratos P302

### The Sculptor Metaphor

The relationship between his two natures follows what Kratonic theory calls the **Sculptor Metaphor**:

| Element | Force | Function | Godric's Status |
|---------|-------|----------|----------------|
| **Clay** | Pyrenikon (Nuclear) | Raw material—explosive potential | Consciously mastered |
| **Chisel** | Elektromagneton (EM) | Shaping tool—provides structure | Unconsciously held |
| **Sculpture** | Synthesis | Focused application—breath, techniques | Incomplete without both |

When Godric projects atomic breath, uses Nuclear Pulse, or coats himself in atomic energy, he's using BOTH forces:
- His **Pyrenikon** provides the destructive payload
- His **Elektromagneton** provides the delivery system and containment

He just doesn't know it. To him, it all feels like "atomic power."

### What His Elektromagneton Nature Enables

His unconscious Elektromagneton nature currently provides:

**Passive Applications (Species-Universal, Automatic):**
- **The Sixth Sense**: Perceiving Earth's magnetic field for navigation
- **Lightning Rod**: Absorbing and grounding electrical attacks as superconductor
- **Magnetization**: Becoming electromagnet after absorbing massive electrical discharge
- **Carrier Wave**: Creating EM fields that shape and contain his nuclear projections

**Hidden Potential (Possible When Conscious):**
- **Controlled Pulse Radius**: Shaping EM carrier wave to specific distances
- **Barrier Emission**: Constant EM waves forming solid-like barriers
- **Active Magnetization**: Deliberate magnetic field generation for tactical advantage
- **Energy Shaping**: Fine control over external atomic energy forms
- **Railgun Acceleration**: EM fields accelerating particles to relativistic speeds

> *For complete species-level documentation, see [[Writing/ATM/20 Characters/22 Titans/Godzilla/_species-reference/Elektromagneton|Elektromagneton]].*

### The "Universal Energy Control" Misconception

**Battra's Outdated Theory:**

When Battra first confronted Godric and witnessed these capabilities, he had no framework to understand what he was seeing. He called it **"Domination of Energy"** or "universal energy control"—his working theory before Krátos was formally classified.

> *"Godzilla no longer has Universal Energy Control; it is now a product of his Pyrenikon/Elektromagneton nature and being a Conqueror."*
> — AA-Kratos P2947

What Battra THOUGHT was "universal energy control" was actually:
1. **Pyrenikon applications**: Fission, fusion, atomic breath, nuclear pulse, radiation absorption
2. **Elektromagneton applications**: EM field generation, electrical absorption, magnetic effects, energy shaping
3. **Synthesis**: Pyrenikon payload delivered via Elektromagneton containment

The "energy types" Battra observed weren't independent abilities—they were different applications of the two fundamental forces Godric embodies.

### Why He Doesn't Know

**Cultural Blindspot:**

For 250 million years, *Titanus gojira* celebrated their Pyrenikon nature while treating Elektromagneton as "secondary, passive ability." Why bother with invisible forces when you embody literal star-fire?

> *"It's a muscle imbalance. Humans are born with muscles, of course. But when we neglect to train a specific set of them, they weaken, atrophy, or develop bad habits."*
> — AA-Kratos P317

**No Framework:**

Without modern physics, gojira had no way to conceptualize EM as a separate force. To them, magnetic navigation and electrical absorption were just "things gojira can do," not evidence of a whole other aspect of their being.

**Adequate Performance:**

They won fights without understanding the mechanism. Conscious Pyrenikon mastery combined with unconscious Elektromagneton use was sufficient for 250 million years—until Mother Prime discovered how to target the EM half directly.

### The Limitation's Consequence

**"You Cannot BE What You Do Not KNOW":**

> *"Krátos thrives when a Kráton is aware of their Manifestation. How can you **be** if you do not **know** yourself?"*
> — AA-Kratos P317

Without understanding his Elektromagneton nature, Godric cannot:
- Consciously integrate both halves of his power
- Develop techniques that require active EM manipulation
- Achieve true Symphonia (harmony between dual natures)
- Access the full potential of being a Holariston Polykrator

He's been sculpting with one hand tied behind his back—except the hand isn't tied. He just doesn't know it's there.

### The Mother Prime Lesson

[[Mother Prime]] discovered what the gojira themselves had missed: their electromagnetic manifestation ENABLES their atomic projection.

She invented the EMP ability specifically to shatter gojira EM containment fields, making their atomic energy chaotic inside their own bodies. By targeting the "chisel," she rendered the "clay" useless.

This demonstrated the fundamental truth Godric has yet to learn: both forces are aspects of the same unified Will, and mastery requires conscious integration of BOTH.

### Until 2025

Until the confrontation with Battra forces him to access electromagnetic capabilities he doesn't understand, Godric will continue to believe:
- His power is purely "atomic/nuclear"
- The various energy effects are just "natural extensions"
- Other abilities (navigation, electrical absorption) are unrelated traits

The sculptor will keep trying to shape clay with his bare hands, unaware that the chisel capable of precise work has been in his grasp the entire time.
