# CLAUDE.md - Godric Energy Limitations

## Overview

**The One-Handed Sculptor** — Godric Nordson is a Holariston Polykrator (dual-force wielder), but psychological barriers and species-traditional thinking prevent him from consciously integrating both aspects of his nature. He sculpts with only the clay (Pyrenikon) while remaining unaware of the chisel (Elektromagneton) in his other hand.

This folder documents:
- His hidden Elektromagneton nature (the unconscious "Order" to his conscious "Wild")
- The micromanagement weakness (sensory switching lag from psychological need for control)
- Pyrenikon/Elektromagneton applications (nuclear and electromagnetic force wielding)
- Physical enhancement block (separation of "body" vs "power")
- The 2025 revelation (discovering the chisel—his Elektromagneton nature)

**Core Paradox:** A being who IS both nuclear and electromagnetic force has spent 250 million years consciously developing only half of his dual nature.

**Historical Note:** What Battra initially theorized as "Domination of Energy" or "universal energy control" in his combat analysis was actually his first attempt to understand the Holariston Polykrator phenomenon before Krátos was formally classified. This outdated terminology appears in narrative sidepieces as the birth of the theory that would later become the formal Krátos system.

## Thematic Significance

**The One-Handed Sculptor** represents one of Godric's defining internal conflicts:
- **External perception:** Unstoppable force, apex predator, King of Monsters
- **Internal reality:** Using only half his dual nature—the visible clay (Pyrenikon) without the invisible chisel (Elektromagneton)
- **Character arc:** Discovering and accepting his Elektromagneton nature to achieve true synthesis
- **Resolution path:** 2025 Battra battle forces recognition + Atomic Amplification emerges from synthesis
- **Irony:** He IS a complete sculptor; he just doesn't know he's holding the chisel

This limitation makes him **more interesting** as a character—his greatest weakness isn't lacking power, but lacking self-knowledge. The ambidextrous warrior has been fighting one-handed for 250 million years.

**Battra's Role:** In the original confrontation, Battra called what he witnessed "Domination of Energy" because he was observing a Holariston Polykrator without having the formal framework to classify it. His analysis was the first step toward understanding Krátos itself.
