# Restraint Philosophy

The Atomic Stride represents a tension between gojira directness and the elegant systems Godric has learned to build. It is power choosing precision over spectacle.

## Gojira Nature vs. Learned Refinement

| Gojira Instinct | Stride Reality |
|-----------------|----------------|
| Overwhelm with force | Navigate with precision |
| Destroy obstacles | Move around them |
| Maximum output | Calculated efficiency |
| Spectacle of power | Silent efficiency |

The Stride is fundamentally *un-gojira* in its philosophy. A pure gojira would simply blast through barriers, not develop techniques to bypass them quietly.

## Why Restraint Matters

Godric could solve most mobility problems through brute force:
- Blast through walls instead of phasing
- Destroy pursuit instead of evading
- Simply *be* where he needs to be through overwhelming presence

He **chooses** not to. The Stride represents deliberate self-limitation—a recognition that subtle power is often more effective than obvious power.

## The Mantle as Restraint

The [[Writing/ATM/20 Characters/22 Titans/Godzilla/Abilities/atomic-mantle/CLAUDE|Atomic Mantle]] makes this philosophy concrete:
- It doesn't *add* power—it *contains* existing power
- The Stride becomes useful by being *less* destructive
- Growth came through learning to restrain, not expand

This inverts the typical power fantasy where abilities grow through becoming more destructive. The Stride grew through becoming more *controlled*.

## Connection to Kong's Philosophy

This restraint philosophy ties directly to the [[trophy-of-humility|Trophy of Humility]]:
- Kong fights with technique, not just power
- Martial arts require restraint and precision
- The best warriors know when *not* to attack

Godric honors Kong by learning that the most powerful move is sometimes the smallest one.
