# Stride Physics

What was intended for subtle contexts has turned into a weapon of mass destruction—typical for Godric. The following issues plagued this ability during development.

## Distance Calculations

| Attribute | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| **Maximum Range** | ~100-120m at 100% AA |
| **Typical Combat Use** | Less than maximum |
| **Minimum Effective** | ~10m (below this, standard movement suffices) |

The Stride is a **calculation of power output, trajectory, and distance**—every use is a feat of immense, high-speed mental processing. A slight miscalculation could see him materialize a hundred feet above or below his intended target.

He is not a Psychic who can "feel" the destination, nor a Sorcerer who can "target" it with a spell. He is a being of physics. **He has to aim.**

## The Three Core Issues

### Issue #1 — Triplet Shockwaves (Primary Concern)

Three shockwaves are generated during a Stride:

| Stage | Shockwave Source | Lethality Ranking |
|-------|------------------|-------------------|
| Step 2 | Sudden leg enhancement | 3rd (lowest) |
| Step 3 | Powerful launch from point A | 1st (highest) |
| Step 4 | Sudden arrival at point B | 2nd |

All three shockwaves have enough force to launch people outward violently at weakest, and **vaporize surroundings** at strongest. Due to the near-instantaneous nature, all three seem to occur simultaneously.

**Source:** The first shockwave mirrors his original issues with Atomic Amplification—the boost to enhanced legs is far too sudden. This requires a holistic, uniform approach.

### Issue #2 — Lack of Brakes (Secondary Concern)

He has no way to brake momentum. He will **not stop** unless he crashes into something—terrible for whoever is on the receiving end.

- He *does* halt at the destination, but missing is far more likely
- Over-amplified legs send him careening for kilometers
- Without braking, precision landing is impossible

**Solution:** Maria's specialized scale barriers (see [[training-progression|Training Progression]])

### Issue #3 — Incorrect Location (Tertiary Concern)

He does not consistently land on the desired point B:

| Error Type | Cause |
|------------|-------|
| **Undershoot** | Insufficient leg boost; ability fails or lands short |
| **Overshoot** | Excessive boost; becomes uncontrolled projectile |

This issue is indirectly addressed by fixing Issue #1.

## The Mantle Solution

The [[Writing/ATM/20 Characters/22 Titans/Godzilla/Abilities/atomic-mantle/CLAUDE|Atomic Mantle]] was developed specifically to address all three issues:
- **Shockwaves:** Neutralized by the Mantle's form-fitting energy layer
- **Radiation:** Contained by the Mantle acting as a buffer
- **Braking:** Neutralization catches momentum artifacts
