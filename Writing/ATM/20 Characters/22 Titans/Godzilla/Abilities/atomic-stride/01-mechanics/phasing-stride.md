# Phasing Stride

A variant of the Atomic Stride that enables the ability to **phase through solid objects** in the path of the Stride.

## Mechanism

The Phasing Stride must be utilized with another ability: **Atomic Resonance** (Pàlmos).

By vibrating his physical body at specific frequencies, Godric trades a portion of his Stride's maximum range for the ability to bypass physical barriers.

## The Trade-Off

| Attribute | Standard Stride | Phasing Stride |
|-----------|-----------------|----------------|
| **Maximum Range** | ~100-120m | Reduced |
| **Obstacle Interaction** | Must avoid or impact | Passes through |
| **Energy Cost** | Standard | Higher (dual ability) |
| **Complexity** | High | Very High |

The distance reduction is significant. The energy required to maintain both the Stride's propulsion and the Resonance's vibration limits how far he can travel while phased.

## Applications

- **Tactical entry:** Phasing through walls, doors, barriers
- **Escape:** Bypassing traps or encirclements
- **Ambush:** Appearing from unexpected angles
- **Defense:** Passing through attacks in transit

## Inspiration

The Phasing Stride was conceived as a potential application in [[atomic-stride-development|Godric's development notes]]:

> "Potential to phase through objects à la Maria or Bartholomew?"

This references Maria's ability to phase through matter via her scales, and potentially Battra's spatial manipulation capabilities.
