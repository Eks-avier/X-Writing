# Core Mechanics

A near-instantaneous, linear burst of movement. When used in conjunction with the [[Writing/ATM/20 Characters/22 Titans/Godzilla/Abilities/atomic-mantle/CLAUDE|Atomic Mantle]], the Stride is almost completely silent and free of collateral damage.

## Fundamental Nature

**Crucially, it is NOT teleportation.** It is simply direct, physical propulsion at an extreme speed. Godric travels through all the intervening space—he just does it so fast it appears instantaneous.

## Directional Capability

The Stride can be used in any direction in three-dimensional space:
- **Horizontal:** Standard ground-level repositioning
- **Vertical:** Rising straight up (see [[vertical-stride|Seismos]])
- **Diagonal:** Any angle between horizontal and vertical
- **Downward:** Tactical descents

This 3D capability makes it his primary mobility tool for closing distances or repositioning in any combat scenario.

## Activation Sequence

Per [[atomic-stride-development|Godric's development notes]]:

1. Activate [[atomic-amplification|Atomic Amplification]] at approximately half-strength
   - As proficiency increases, AA output requirement lowers
2. Channel additional energy into legs via Fortification (Stereosis)
3. Launch from point A
4. Arrive at point B

## Range Limitations

The Stride is tactically limited to **short-to-medium range**:
- Maximum distance: ~100-120m at 100% AA output
- Typical combat use: Much less than maximum
- Trade-offs exist with variants (see [[phasing-stride|Phasing Stride]])

## Energy Cost

The technique requires sustained Atomic Amplification as a foundation. It is too precise to perform raw—the energy calculations and physical control demands are simply too high without AA's comprehensive enhancement.
