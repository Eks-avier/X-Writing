# Development Journey

The Atomic Stride's development represents a transition from catastrophic chaos to surgical precision—a journey made possible by the [[Writing/ATM/20 Characters/22 Titans/Godzilla/Abilities/atomic-mantle/CLAUDE|Atomic Mantle]].

## Pre-Mantle Era: Chaos

Before the Mantle, every Stride was a weapon of mass destruction:

| Problem | Effect |
|---------|--------|
| **Triplet Shockwaves** | Vaporized surroundings at high output |
| **Lack of Brakes** | Uncontrolled projectile motion |
| **Precision Issues** | Inconsistent landing locations |
| **Radiation Trail** | Lethal contamination everywhere |

The technique was *usable* but impractical for anything other than deliberate destruction. Using it near allies or civilians was unthinkable.

## The Battra Catalyst

Notice how Atomic Amplification was developed *before* Battra while Stride and Mantle were developed *after* their fight. Yet **all** of them were used in the fight anyway.

Battra was the being who pushed Godric in his human body to discover his utmost potential. The period after their confrontation is him **chasing that high**—refining and mastering what instinct had unlocked.

## Maria's Training Barrier

The breakthrough came through Maria's specialized scale barriers:
- Designed to bounce *only him* off
- Absorbed kinetic energy without reflection
- Prevented him from becoming a catastrophic projectile

Without this training tool, controlled practice would have been impossible. See: [[training-progression|Training Progression]]

## Post-Mantle Era: Mastery

With the Mantle developed, the Stride became what it was always meant to be:

| Attribute | Pre-Mantle | Post-Mantle |
|-----------|------------|-------------|
| **Sound** | Catastrophic | Near-silent |
| **Collateral** | Devastating | Minimal |
| **Precision** | Unreliable | Surgical |
| **Usability** | Combat-only | Universal |

## The Visual Evolution

The before/after is stark:
- **Pre-Mantle:** Explosions of displaced air, blue energy trailing, radiation wake
- **Post-Mantle:** A blink—he's simply *there*, with only a faint shimmer marking his passage
