# Training Progression

Maria's scale barriers served as essential "training wheels" for Atomic Stride development—a controlled environment that made refinement possible.

## The Problem

Without a controlled training environment, practicing the Stride was impossible:
- Each attempt created lethal shockwaves
- Over-amplified launches sent him careening for kilometers
- There was no way to safely "miss" a landing
- Any practice near civilization risked catastrophe

## Maria's Solution

Maria created a specialized version of her scales around their backyard with specific properties:

| Property | Purpose |
|----------|---------|
| **Bounce Only Him** | Catches his momentum without harming others |
| **No Reflection** | Absorbs kinetic energy rather than redirecting it |
| **Selective Targeting** | Only responds to Godric specifically |

## The Critical Caveat

If Maria's scales **fully reflected** his momentum instead of absorbing it:

> He would turn into a catastrophic projectile that would demolish the house and everything around their property, including the neighbors in proximity.

The barrier had to be precisely calibrated to catch him without becoming a launching mechanism.

## Training Benefits

With the scale barrier in place, Godric could:
- Practice varying energy outputs safely
- Learn his distance-to-power ratios
- Experience "failed" landings without consequences
- Iterate rapidly on refinements

## The Deeper Meaning

Maria's contribution to the Stride's development foreshadows the [[Writing/ATM/20 Characters/22 Titans/Godzilla/Abilities/atomic-mantle/CLAUDE|Atomic Mantle]] itself:
- Both involve her protective nature enabling his growth
- Both represent love expressed through practical support
- Both show the partnership underlying his development

Her scales protected him while he learned. The Mantle he developed would later protect *her* and others.
