## Kratonic Terminology

| English Term | Greek Term | Application |
|--------------|------------|-------------|
| **Personal Coating** | Epichrisis (ἐπίχρισις) | Willpower directly on body |
| **Fortification** | Stereosis (στερέωσις) | Augments body to god-like degree |
| **Neutralization** | Exoudeterosis (ἐξουδετέρωσις) | Suffuses cells with defensive will |
| **Penetration** | Diatresis (διάτρησις) | Actions bypass defenses |
| **Flaring** | Anapsis (ἄναψις) | Intensifies Aura effects |
| **Submission** | Proskynesis (προσκύνησις) | Will domination through Aura |
| **Expansion** | Ektasis (ἔκτασις) | Enlarges Aura radius |
| **Suppression** | Kryptis (κρυπτής) | Conceals Aura presence |
| **Injection** | Sovereign's Writ | Ultimate sanction (Penetration + Neutralization) |

**Key Concepts:**
- **Horme** (ὁρμή) = Conqueror's Desire; specific driving obsession
- **Thelesis** (θέλησις) = Sovereign Will; raw willpower strength
- **Symphonia** (συμφωνία) = Aligned state when Will and Desire harmonize
- **Diaphonia** (διαφωνία) = Misaligned state when Will and Desire conflict
- **Repose** = Broken state when Will contradicts Desire
