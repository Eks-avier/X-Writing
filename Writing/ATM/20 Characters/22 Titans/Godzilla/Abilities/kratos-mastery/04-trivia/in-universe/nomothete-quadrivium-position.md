# Position in the Nomothete Quadrivium

The four strongest beings across all power systems form the **Nomothete Quadrivium**:

| Position | Being | System | Classification |
|----------|-------|--------|----------------|
| **The Tangible Intuitive** | Godzilla | Krátos | The Worker |
| **The Intangible Intuitive** | [[Writing/ATM/20 Characters/22 Titans/Mothra, the Queen of the Monsters\|Mothra]] | Psionics | The Divine |

## Why Godric is "The Worker"

* **Hard Work Applied to Intuition**
* Relies on instinct honed through physical repetition
* 250+ million years of accumulated muscle memory
* Hyperthymesia ensures nothing is ever truly forgotten

---

## Why Battra Respects Godric

Both dedicate themselves to honing Will (Pillar II) and Vessel (Pillar III). Despite different systems, they recognize fellow sovereigns in discipline and effort.
