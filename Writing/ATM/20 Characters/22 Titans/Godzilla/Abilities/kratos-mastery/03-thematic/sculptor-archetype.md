# The Sculptor Archetype

Godric exemplifies the **Sculptor** philosophy—one of the [[Writing/ATM/30 Lore/31 Power Systems/Krátos/Part_VI_Character_Applications|Four Kratonic Archetypes]].

---

## Core Philosophy

*"I AM the weapon."*

Power as self-refinement. The Sculptor shapes themselves into the perfect instrument for any challenge.

---

## Characteristics

* Power focused inward
* Body IS the art
* Limited control once power leaves body
* Intimate, personal, profoundly integrated
