# Signature Arsenal: The Four Trophies

Each of Godric's signature techniques represents a milestone in his journey—a "trophy" earned through growth.

---

## [[Writing/ATM/20 Characters/22 Titans/Godzilla/Abilities/atomic-amplification/CLAUDE|Atomic Amplification]] — Master of Internal Enhancement

The simultaneous, sustained application of all THREE Personal Coating Edicts across his entire body.

**Components:**
1. **Internal Fortification (Stereosis)** — Augments musculature and physical power
2. **Internal Neutralization (Exoudeterosis)** — Suffuses cells with will; supercharges defense/regeneration
3. **Innate Penetration (Diatresis)** — Every action imbued with will to bypass defenses

**Tiers of Amplification:**

| Tier | Range | Name | Use Case |
|------|-------|------|----------|
| 1 | 1-15% | Combat Flow | Enhanced senses, agility, martial flow |
| 2 | 15-50% | Dominance | Standard serious combat; overwhelming physicality |
| 3 | 50-100%+ | The King's Wrath | Existential threats; affects environment |

## Atomic Railgun — Trophy of Intellect

The ultimate synthesis of Pyrenikon (nuclear force as bullet) + [[elektromagneton-hidden-half|Elektromagneton]] (electromagnetic force as barrel).

* **Difficulty:** Extremely challenging to master; requires perfect coordination of both forces per [[elektromagneton-hidden-half#The Muscle Imbalance Problem|the muscle imbalance]]
* **Power:** Sufficient to cleave through Xilien mothership hull
* **Burning Form Variant:** Red Spiral Railgun
* **See:** [[Writing/ATM/20 Characters/22 Titans/Godzilla/Abilities/atomic-railgun/CLAUDE|atomic-railgun/]] for full documentation

---

## Atomic Stride (Seismos) — Trophy of Humility

Short-range displacement through molecular vibration. Born from need to close distance against Kong.

* **The King's Advance:** Visual anchors mark where he will be, not where he is
* **Phasing Application:** Can achieve near-intangibility through extreme vibration
* **See:** [[Writing/ATM/20 Characters/22 Titans/Godzilla/Abilities/atomic-stride/CLAUDE|atomic-stride/]] for full documentation

---

## Atomic Mantle (Aegis Atomicus) — Trophy of Love

Visual manifestation of [[elektromagneton-hidden-half|Elektromagneton nature]] within AA. The EM field provides the [[Writing/ATM/20 Characters/22 Titans/Godzilla/Abilities/_foundations/elektromagneton-awakening/03-applications/mantle-framework|invisible wireframe]] that gives shape to the Neutralization layer.

* **The King's Protection:** Triple-layer defense (innate durability + Neutralization + Mantle)
* **Named for:** Those he loves and protects
* **See:** [[Writing/ATM/20 Characters/22 Titans/Godzilla/Abilities/atomic-mantle/CLAUDE|atomic-mantle/]] for full documentation
