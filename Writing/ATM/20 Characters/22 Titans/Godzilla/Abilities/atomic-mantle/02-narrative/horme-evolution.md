# Horme Evolution

The Atomic Mantle foreshadows a profound shift in Godric's **Horme** — his fundamental survival drive.

## What is Horme?

In Kratonic philosophy, **Horme** (ὁρμή) represents the primal survival instinct that underlies all will-based power. It is the deepest drive: the imperative to *persist*.

## The Evolution

| Stage | Horme Focus | Expression |
|-------|-------------|------------|
| **Original** | Survival of self | Raw power, territorial defense |
| **Evolving** | Survival of family | Extended protection, shared defense |

The Mantle represents this shift made tangible. His protection is no longer hoarded—it can be **shared**.

## The Significance

This is not merely tactical expansion. It represents a fundamental change in *what Godric is protecting*:

- Before: "I will survive"
- After: "**We** will survive"

The External Allocation capability—giving his protection to others—is the physical manifestation of a Horme that now encompasses more than one being.

## Connection to Maria

Maria's influence is central to this evolution:
- She taught him that protection can be extended
- She showed him that his power can shield others
- She gave him something beyond himself to protect

The Mantle is the *result* of his Horme expanding to include her. The ability to protect her (and others) emerged because protecting them became as fundamental as protecting himself.

## Narrative Implication

> "You can say that the Mantle is foreshadowing of his Horme evolving, from survival of himself to survival of his family."

This evolution continues beyond the Mantle. As his family grows—Maria, Madison, his found family of Titans—so too will the expressions of his protective drive.
