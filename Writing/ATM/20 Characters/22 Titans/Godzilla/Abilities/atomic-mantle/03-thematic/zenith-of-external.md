# Zenith of External Manipulation

The Atomic Mantle represents the **utmost limit** of a pure *Titanus gojira*'s external energy manipulation.

## The Limit Defined

For Godric, and any other pure gojira like Dagon, the Mantle is the ceiling:
- They can bring their internal energy **to** the surface
- They can project it **onto** objects and others
- They **cannot** go further without hybrid traits or external systems

## Why This Is the Limit

Pure gojira energy manipulation is fundamentally **internal**:
- Atomic Amplification enhances the body from within
- The Atomic Breath projects internal energy outward
- The Nuclear Pulse radiates energy from the core

The Mantle is the furthest externalization possible while still remaining connected to the body. It sits *on* the skin—the boundary between internal and external.

## What Would Exceed It

True external manipulation—controlling energy completely divorced from the body—would require:
- Hybrid physiology (like the MUTOs' EMP)
- Magical training (external energy systems)
- Psionic capability (mind-over-matter)

The Mantle reaches the edge of what pure biology can achieve.

## The Thematic Significance

This limitation is **meaningful**, not disappointing:
- It defines what it means to be a pure gojira
- It shows that even the Alpha Paramount has boundaries
- It makes the Mantle's achievement more impressive

Reaching the ceiling of your species' capability is not failure—it is mastery.

## Comparison to Maria

Maria's scale manipulation exceeds what the Mantle can do:
- Her scales can operate fully independent of her body
- She can create barriers at a distance
- Her protection is not limited to skin-contact range

The Mantle **honors** her capability rather than **matching** it. Godric acknowledges that her way of protection is superior; his homage is doing what he can within gojira limits.
