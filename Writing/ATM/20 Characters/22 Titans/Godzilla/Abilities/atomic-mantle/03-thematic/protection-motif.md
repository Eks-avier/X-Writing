# Protection Motif

The Atomic Mantle brings one of the major motifs of the *Titanus gojira* species full circle: **the protection of territories**.

## The Gojira Instinct

Protection is the basest instinct of a *Titanus gojira*:

| Sex | Primary Instinct |
|-----|------------------|
| **Male** | External territory defense |
| **Female** | Internal territory defense (nest, young) |

This instinct predates consciousness, civilization, or relationship. It is **biological** before it is philosophical.

## Why "Mantle"

The name "Atomic Mantle" directly references this territorial protection:
- A mantle is a covering, a protective layer
- It evokes geological mantles—the protective layer around a planet's core
- It suggests sovereignty—a ruler's mantle of office

The ability is named for what it *means*, not just what it does.

## From Territory to People

Godric's development of the Mantle represents the evolution of this instinct:

| Original Form | Evolved Form |
|---------------|--------------|
| Defend geographic territory | Defend people as "territory" |
| Protect space | Protect relationships |
| Instinctual reaction | Conscious choice |

The people he loves have become his "territory" in the deepest gojira sense. Protecting them is as fundamental as protecting any geographic domain.

## The Species-Level Significance

The Mantle's External Allocation capability—extending protection to others—takes the gojira territorial instinct and makes it **transferable**. This is unprecedented:

> Traditional gojira protection is **anchored**: defend this place, this space.
> The Mantle makes protection **mobile**: defend this person, wherever they are.
