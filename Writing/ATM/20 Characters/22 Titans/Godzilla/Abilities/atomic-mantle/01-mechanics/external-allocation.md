# External Allocation

The Mantle's secondary purpose: a **"buffing ability"** that grants others the same protection Godric receives from AA's defensive aspect.

## Mechanism

By imparting a *portion* of the Mantle itself onto an object or person, Godric can grant them an external layer of protection.

| Target Type | Application |
|-------------|-------------|
| **Person** | Grants AA-level Neutralization defense |
| **Object** | Protects structural integrity |
| **Vehicle** | Shields occupants within |

## The Trade-Off

This protection comes at a cost:
- The allocated portion is **removed** from Godric's own Mantle
- His personal protection decreases proportionally
- More targets = less individual protection each

The math remains constant: protection allocated to others is subtracted from his own.

## Applications

### Protecting Allies
In combat, Godric can extend protection to vulnerable allies:
- Humans who would otherwise be collateral damage
- Weaker Titans facing overwhelming attacks
- Anyone within range who needs immediate shielding

### Protecting Civilians
During larger conflicts, entire groups can receive partial protection:
- Reduced individual shield strength
- Still significantly more than no protection
- Allows civilian evacuation under fire

### Protecting Maria
The most personal application—extending his shield to encompass Maria in dangerous situations. This mirrors and honors her own protective nature.

## Limitations

- Range is limited to his Mantle's extension capability
- Cannot grant full AA-equivalent protection without leaving himself vulnerable
- Duration requires sustained concentration
- Multiple targets dilute individual protection

## Thematic Significance

External Allocation represents the evolution of gojira protection instinct:
- Natural gojira defend **territory**
- Godric has learned to defend **people**
- The Mantle makes this instinct **tangible**
