# Stride Synergy

The Atomic Mantle's conception was caused *by* the [[Writing/ATM/20 Characters/22 Titans/Godzilla/Abilities/atomic-stride/CLAUDE|Atomic Stride]], but serving as its supplement is not its sole purpose.

## Why the Mantle Was Created

The Stride had three critical problems (see [[stride-physics|Stride Physics]]):
1. Triplet shockwaves at launch, transit, and arrival
2. No braking mechanism
3. Lethal radiation trail

The Mantle solves all three.

## The Visualization

The Mantle acts like a **net** around Godric's body during a Stride. It *catches* everything that would cause collateral damage:

| Artifact | Mantle Response |
|----------|-----------------|
| Excess energy | Absorbed |
| Displaced air | Neutralized |
| Sound waves | Dampened |
| Shockwaves | Captured |
| Radiation | Contained |

## Kratonic Mechanism

From a Kratonic perspective, "catching" these artifacts means **Neutralizing** them:
- The Mantle captures the shockwave at each stage of the ability
- It dissolves harmful artifacts before propagation
- It contains radiation that would otherwise trail the Stride

## The Radiation Buffer

Being *within* the Mantle is safe, but *touching* it is not.

**Caveat:** This has practically no difference for Godric's daily life, as his mere presence is already radioactive. The Mantle simply prevents the *additional* radiation spike that would accompany an unshielded Stride.

## Result: Enabling Relationship

The Mantle doesn't just fix the Stride—it **enables** it:

| Without Mantle | With Mantle |
|----------------|-------------|
| Weapon of mass destruction | Tactical mobility tool |
| Combat-only application | Universal use |
| Devastating collateral | Minimal footprint |
| Obvious energy signature | Near-silent |
