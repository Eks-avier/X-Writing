# Core Mechanics

The Atomic Mantle is an **Asymmetrical Permeable Shield (APS)** — a portion of Godric's internal Neutralization field brought out and placed onto the skin.

## Fundamental Definition

| Perspective | Definition |
|-------------|------------|
| **Mechanical** | A skill that takes a portion of AA's defensive aspect and elevates it as a layer *on* the skin |
| **Kratonic** | A portion of the Neutralization aspect—which normally defends the body *from the inside*—is brought out and placed *onto* the skin |

The Penetration aspect remains within the body. The Mantle externalizes only the defensive component.

## Form-Fitting Structure

The Mantle is **form-fitting**:
- It does not *hover* over the skin
- It sits right on top of it
- It conforms to his body's contours precisely

This precision is possible because of the **Elektromagneton "wireframe"** that gives shape to the Neutralization layer. Per the [[elektromagneton-nature#The Atomic Mantle: EM Framework|Sculptor metaphor]]: the EM field provides the invisible lines, and the atomic energy fills them in.

## Reallocated Defenses

While it may seem like he has a **fourth layer of armor**, this is not the case:

> It's just his AA Neutralization sitting on top of his skin. The overall defensive capability of AA is just reallocated. Part of it remains inside his system, part of it now sits on his skin. **7 and 3 still make 10.**

The total defensive power is unchanged—only its distribution is altered.

## Partial Externalization

The entire Neutralization layer is **not** completely brought out:
- Only about a quarter to half is externalized
- This varies depending on the situation
- For demanding challenges, additional layers may be introduced

**Reason:** Internal organs must remain enhanced and defended by AA. Externalizing the full Neutralization layer would sacrifice internal protection for external defense.

## Radiation Note

The Mantle remains **highly radioactive** at higher outputs. While it solves the Stride's radiation leakage problem by containing it, the Mantle itself is still dangerous to touch.
