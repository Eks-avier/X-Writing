# Uniform Distribution: The Breakthrough

## Forced Reevaluation

The plateau eventually forced Godzilla to reevaluate his entire approach. [[Kong]]'s martial teachings had set the foundation, but his understanding remained incomplete.

Like many breakthroughs in Godzilla's life, this one occurred during solitary reflection. He needed to understand what made [[Anguirus]]'s technique fundamentally different from his own attempts. The answer, when it came, was elegantly simple.

## The Epiphany

Godzilla's epiphany came during a rare shared quiet moment with Anguirus. They sat atop a clifftop overlooking the Pacific, discussing the challenges of human form.

Anguirus spoke of adaptation rather than resistance:

> "The way I see it, this form isn't about limitations but redistribution. Our power didn't diminish—it just needs different pathways."

The word echoed in Godzilla's mind long after the conversation ended: ***Redistribution***.

Perhaps the error wasn't in directing energy to a limb, but in *concentrating* it there.

## Renewed Observation

For days afterward, Godzilla observed Anguirus with renewed focus. He noticed something he'd overlooked before—Anguirus never reinforced just one area:
- When absorbing impact, the energy dispersed throughout his entire body
- The distribution was near-perfect in its uniformity
- No single point bore the full force of enhancement

By contrast, Godzilla had been forcing concentrated energy into specific pathways:
- The result was like trying to force a tsunami through a garden hose
- His body wasn't rejecting the energy—it was being overwhelmed by its concentration

## The New Approach

The solution would require an entirely different mindset:
- Rather than directing energy in a linear path, he needed to create a *circulatory system* for it
- Energy should flow throughout his body simultaneously, reinforcing it uniformly
- This would require significantly more control, but potentially eliminate the destructive results

## Collaboration with Kong

Godzilla approached Kong with his new theory.

> "I've been thinking about what you taught me about breath control in combat," Godzilla began.
>
> Kong nodded, recognizing where this was heading.
>
> "The way you control oxygen flow to sustain power throughout a fight," Godzilla continued. "What if I applied that principle to energy circulation instead?"

Kong immediately grasped the concept:
- "A continuous circuit rather than a concentrated burst"
- "Like blood moving through your body—constant flow, not a single direction"
- "If successful, you'd avoid pressure buildup entirely"

Together, they outlined a new approach:
- The goal would be constant, measured flow throughout his entire body
- Kong suggested using meditative breathing as the foundation
- This would create a rhythm that the energy could follow—balanced and predictable

## The Pathway System

The key to uniform distribution would be the pathway system itself.

### Human Anatomy Advantages
Human anatomy provided unexpected advantages:
- The circulatory and nervous systems created natural pathways for energy flow
- By visualizing these systems as energy conduits, Godzilla could achieve total-body enhancement
- [[Madison Russell|Madison]]'s anatomy books proved unexpectedly valuable

### Kong's Perspective
Kong contributed vital perspective on body mechanics:

> "Think of it like water flowing through a river system, not a dam breaking. The energy should support your movements, not drive them."

This subtle distinction would become crucial to the technique's refinement.
