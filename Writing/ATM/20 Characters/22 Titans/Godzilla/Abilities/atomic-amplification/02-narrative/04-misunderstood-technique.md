# The Misunderstood Technique

## Incomplete Understanding

Godzilla's initial observations of [[Anguirus]] had led him to an incomplete understanding.

What he had first witnessed wasn't Anguirus's basic technique but an advanced application:
- He observed the "compounding" effect—Anguirus layering additional absorbed energy onto already enhanced areas
- This layering created the visible concentration that Godzilla attempted to replicate
- It represented an advanced technique built upon a foundation Godzilla hadn't yet recognized

## The Fundamental Truth

The fundamental truth was more elegant than Godzilla initially realized.

Anguirus's primary technique involved **uniform distribution** throughout his entire body. This served two synergistic purposes:
1. It prevented overloading any single part of his body, allowing greater total absorption
2. It created efficient, balanced physical enhancement across his entire form

Only *after* establishing this foundation could Anguirus perform the compounding technique.

## The Explanation for Failure

This misunderstanding explained Godzilla's initial failures:
- He attempted to jump directly to the advanced application without establishing the foundation
- It was like trying to build a tower without first laying the groundwork
- The catastrophic results were inevitable given this approach

## Anguirus's Revelation

As Godzilla finally grasped the true mechanics, Anguirus offered rare insight into his technique's development.

> "It took me millennia to refine," he admitted during a private discussion. "The distribution came first, out of pure necessity. No single part of me could handle the full force of what I absorbed. The compounding was something I discovered much later, after I'd mastered the basics."

## Human Form Adaptation

For Anguirus, the adaptation to human form had been relatively straightforward:
- His technique had always been about internal management rather than external projection
- The principles remained constant regardless of physical scale
- His challenges had been more psychological than technical—adjusting to human social dynamics proved far more difficult than adapting his abilities

## The Crucial Insight

This conversation reinforced a crucial insight for Godzilla:
- The foundation had to be perfected before advanced applications could be attempted
- His impatience had been the primary obstacle to progress
- True mastery would require patience—a quality not naturally abundant in his nature
