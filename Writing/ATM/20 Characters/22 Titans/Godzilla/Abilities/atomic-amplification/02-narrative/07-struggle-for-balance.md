# The Struggle for Balance

## Initial Improvements

The first attempts at uniform distribution showed immediate improvement but revealed new challenges.

### Progress
No explosions occurred, which was significant progress:
- The catastrophic limb failures ceased entirely
- This alone represented a breakthrough

### New Issues
However, new issues arose immediately:
- Maintaining conscious control of energy flow across his entire body required immense focus
- The moment his concentration wavered, energy would pool dangerously in specific areas
- Sustaining the flow for more than a few minutes left him mentally exhausted

### Physical Discomfort
Physical discomfort remained significant:
- Though less acute than before, energy circulation felt like being constantly submerged in scalding water
- His regeneration was constantly engaged, creating a peculiar equilibrium where damage and healing occurred simultaneously
- This balance was precarious at best

## The Mental Challenge

The most challenging aspect proved to be mental rather than physical.

Godzilla's lifelong experience involved channeling energy outward, not containing it internally:
- This created persistent psychological resistance
- His instincts constantly fought the new pattern

[[Kong]] recognized this cognitive barrier immediately:

> "Your mind is still trying to make a weapon, not a tool. You need to stop thinking of the energy as something separate from yourself."

This conceptual shift would prove more difficult than any physical challenge.

### Adapting Meditation
The solution required adapting meditation techniques:
- Traditional martial arts breathing exercises provided a foundation
- Kong guided Godzilla through visualization exercises borrowed from ancient practices
- Progress came in frustratingly small increments

## Anguirus's Growing Unease

[[Anguirus]] observed these training sessions with increasing unease.

### Shifting Pride
His initial pride that Godzilla would adapt his technique shifted gradually:
- At first, he offered occasional suggestions based on his own experience
- These proved less applicable than expected due to fundamental differences between their energy systems
- Kong's insights consistently proved more relevant—a fact that didn't escape Anguirus's notice

### Accumulating Exclusion
Small moments of exclusion began to accumulate:
- Kong and Godzilla developed a training shorthand that Anguirus wasn't part of
- They referred to concepts from their previous combat that Anguirus hadn't witnessed
- Technical discussions increasingly referenced Kong's martial philosophy rather than Anguirus's absorption mechanics

### The Telling Change
Most tellingly, Anguirus noticed a change in Godzilla's approach to adaptation:
- For millions of years, Godzilla had embodied the brute-force philosophy of their species
- This new, methodical approach felt foreign—more akin to Kong's patient analytical style
- The significance wasn't lost on Anguirus: Godzilla was evolving in ways that reflected Kong's influence, not his

## Technical Refinement

Training sessions became increasingly technical as Godzilla refined his approach.

### Microscopic Control
The focus shifted to microscopic control rather than broad concepts:
- Godzilla worked to identify the precise activation threshold for uniform distribution
- He experimented with various percentages of his total energy reserves
- The goal was to find the perfect balance between enhancement and sustainability

### The Central Challenge
Balance emerged as the central challenge:
- Too little energy provided negligible enhancement
- Too much energy caused the old problems of overheating and pain
- The window between these extremes was frustratingly narrow

### Diminishing Returns
Progress followed a pattern of diminishing returns:
- Initial gains came quickly as obvious issues were addressed
- Fine-tuning required exponentially more effort for smaller improvements
- Months of training yielded increasingly subtle refinements

## The Setback

A significant setback occurred during a session witnessed by Anguirus.

### The Attempt
After weeks of steady progress, Godzilla attempted to push beyond his established threshold:
- Kong had suggested that mental barriers were likely limiting his potential
- Godzilla believed he could safely increase energy flow by approximately 10%
- The results proved otherwise

### The Failure
The failure was dramatic though not catastrophic:
- Energy synchronization collapsed almost instantly
- The familiar blue glow turned dangerously vibrant
- While no explosion occurred, the energy discharge melted the reinforced training floor

### Different Reactions
The incident affected the three Titans in markedly different ways:
- **Godzilla** responded with scientific curiosity, immediately analyzing what went wrong
- **Kong** approached it as an informative data point requiring tactical adjustment
- **Anguirus** saw it as confirmation of his growing concerns—Godzilla was being pushed in dangerous directions under Kong's guidance
