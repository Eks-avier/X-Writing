# Tiers of Amplification

> *Per AA-Kratos P279: The post-Xilien refinement of the technique.*

Godric can consciously modulate the intensity of his Amplification, treating it as a scalable tool rather than a simple on/off switch. Post-[[Xilien Invasion]], his mastery of efficiency allows him to sustain higher tiers with far greater ease.

---

## Tier 1 (1-15%): Combat Flow

A low-cost state used for training and light engagements.

**Characteristics:**
- Senses perfected beyond baseline
- Agility reaches optimal levels
- Effortless flow between martial arts techniques
- Sustainable for extended periods without strain

**Typical Use:** Sparring sessions, casual intimidation, routine patrols

---

## Tier 2 (15-50%): Dominance

His standard for serious combat against high-level threats.

**Characteristics:**
- Physicality becomes overwhelming
- Enables brutal pressure fighting
- Battlefield control becomes possible
- The uniform blue aura becomes clearly visible

**Typical Use:** Fights against Beta-tier opponents, Titan conflicts, defensive operations

---

## Tier 3 (50-100%+): The King's Wrath

Reserved for existential threats.

**Characteristics:**
- He becomes a localized physical event
- Power so immense it affects the immediate environment
- Other Titans' energy-based abilities may be suppressed
- Reality itself seems to bend around his presence

**Typical Use:** Ghidorah-level threats, Xilien invasion, defense of [[Maria Lepidiel|Maria]]

---

## Related Mechanics

The tier system reflects post-Xilien refinement. During initial development, Godric could only maintain approximately 40% capacity before destabilization. The breakthrough to full mastery came through mental model shift—treating energy as embodiment rather than control.
