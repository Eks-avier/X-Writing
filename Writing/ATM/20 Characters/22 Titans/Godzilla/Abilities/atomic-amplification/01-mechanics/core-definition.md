# Core Definition

> *For detailed Kratonic mechanics, see [[kratos-mastery#Atomic Amplification|Krátos Mastery]].*

## The Uniform Distribution Principle

Atomic Amplification is the **uniform distribution** of atomic energy throughout the entire body, creating balanced physical enhancement across all systems simultaneously.

Unlike concentrated energy projection (Atomic Breath) or localized reinforcement, AA requires the wielder to maintain energy circulation like a second circulatory system—constant, measured, and evenly distributed.

## Kratonic Definition

Per BAA-Kratos-P210:

> "The simultaneous and sustained application of both Personal Coating techniques… a master-level feat requiring a Kraton to maintain two opposing intents across their entire body at once: Neutralization (The Royal Armor) and Penetration (The Sceptered Strike)."

The technique integrates three Kratonic edicts:

| Edict | Greek Term | Function |
|-------|------------|----------|
| **Fortification** | Stereosis | Reinforcing structural integrity — the "Sovereign's Vessel" |
| **Neutralization** | Exoudeterosis | Dissolving incoming attacks — the "Royal Armor" |
| **Penetration** | Diatresis | Bypassing defenses — the "Sceptered Strike" |

## Physical Enhancement

When Amplified, Godric receives comprehensive enhancement:

- **Strength** — Dramatically increased striking and lifting power
- **Defense** — Physical resistance boosted across entire body
- **Senses** — Perfected beyond baseline human/Titan levels
- **Reaction Speed** — Accelerated processing and response
- **Agility** — Enhanced coordination and movement speed
- **Cognitive Function** — Tactical decision-making accelerated

## Species-Breaking Achievement

The *Titanus gojira* species traditionally possessed only two methods of energy manipulation:
1. Projecting energy outward (Atomic Breath)
2. Sensing radiation (detection abilities)

Internal energy manipulation for self-enhancement was considered impossible—a mental barrier reinforced across millions of years. Atomic Amplification represents Godric breaking through this species-wide limitation.
