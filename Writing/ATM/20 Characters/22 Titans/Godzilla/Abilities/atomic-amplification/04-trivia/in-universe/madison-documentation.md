# Madison Documentation

## The Human Witness

[[Madison Russell]] served as both resource provider and documentarian throughout Atomic Amplification's development.

## Contributions

### Anatomy Resources
With helpful lessons from books and resources about human anatomy, Madison enabled Godzilla and Kong to understand the human body's natural pathways:
- Circulatory and nervous systems as energy conduits
- Visualization techniques for total-body enhancement
- The "river system" metaphor for energy flow

### Field Documentation
Madison witnessed the development process firsthand—including the explosive failures:
- She "lurched over vomiting" so many times during prototype testing she "probably lost count"
- She was "probably completely used to it by then"

### Titan Combat Theory
Witnessing the final version, Madison created detailed records:
- Analysis included both technical aspects and philosophical underpinnings
- These records formed the foundation of what Monarch classified as **"Titan Combat Theory"**

## The Question That Triggered Tension

At the formal demonstration, Madison's innocent question exposed Anguirus's growing discomfort:

> "So this is basically an adaptation of Anguirus's absorption ability?"

Before Anguirus could respond, [[Kong]] answered:

> "It started there, but evolved into something that works with Godzilla's unique energy. The distribution principle is entirely different."

The technically accurate explanation unintentionally minimized Anguirus's contribution—a moment that crystallized his jealousy.
