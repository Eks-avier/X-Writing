# The Predator's Patience

## The Hunter of the Skies

He doesn't fight *in* the air; he **hunts** from the ground. His limited aerial abilities are not for dogfights but for creating inescapable traps and delivering decisive, killing blows. His approach is that of a patient, intelligent, ground-based apex predator picking birds out of the sky.

---

## The Hunting Process

His hunting method is not just a fight; it's a **process:**

### 1. The Observation Phase

Begin completely grounded, reinforcing the enemy's perception of him as a slow, terrestrial threat. Use this time to **gather data**—with hyperthymesia and enhanced senses, he meticulously documents flight patterns, speed, turning radius, tells.

### 2. The Lull

Let them get comfortable. Let them believe they have the advantage of the sky, that they are safe in their domain.

### 3. The Strike

Once he has a complete predictive model in his head, he strikes. He doesn't react to where they are; he Strides to **where they are going to be.** His attack is not an interception; it is an **inevitability.**

| Factor | Application |
