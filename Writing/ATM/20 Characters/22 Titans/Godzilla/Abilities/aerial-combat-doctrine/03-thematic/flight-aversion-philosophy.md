# Flight Aversion Philosophy

## The Psychology of Domain: "A Realm He Shouldn't Trespass"

Godric doesn't fear flight—he **honors the sovereignty of others.**

---

## The Kratonic Manifestation of Will

> "He IS his power, and HE does NOT want to fly. Therefore, he *won't* fly."

All of Godric's airborne options are temporary:
- Footholds fade through duration or lack of contact
- Breath propulsion is wildly inefficient
- Stride has set distance and requires aim

These limits are his **subconscious preventing him from stepping into a realm he does not belong to.** Through his Limitless Adaptation, he could probably find a way to propel himself indefinitely—but he doesn't, and he won't.

This has been cemented into his Will, and thus is how his Kratos expresses itself. His Horme was unchanged, but his Will to act on it **excludes flight.**

---

## Honor, Not Fear

This aversion is out of a sense of honor. While he has those three options, he'd prefer to shoot enemies down first with an Atomic Breath or, if intending to finish, an Atomic Railgun.

It's aim practice, it's familiar, and it respects the boundary between earth, sea, and sky.

**The Statement to Enemies:** *"You will engage with me on my terms, not yours."*

---

## The Deeper Truth

His aerial techniques are **deliberately limited** because his Will has decided the sky is not his domain to conquer. This is not a failure of power but a **success of character**—his Kratos accurately reflects who he chooses to be.
