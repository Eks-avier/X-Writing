# Domain Respect: The Weight of History

He has lived for 250 million years. He witnessed the rise and fall of dynasties in every domain.

---

## The *Titanus mosura* Era

Before he was born, the rulers of the sky were the mosura. Their mix of natural flight with telekinetic assistance made them the most graceful, yet deceptively lethal, fliers.

Though he has never seen other mosura besides Mothra and Battra, he is convinced their kin would have been just as elegant.

---

## The *Titanus radon* Succession

He personally witnessed the rise of the radon during the Mesozoic. Where Mothra and Battra flew with elegant grace, the radon flew with powerful eagerness—crude, loud, destructive, but magnificent. Agile, maneuverable, and FAST.

This is why Rodan's chosen epithet, the one Godric personally made "official," is **Lord of the Skies.**

---

## The Pattern of Respect

| Domain | Historical Rulers | Godric's Position |
|--------|-------------------|-------------------|
| **Sky** | Mosura (ancient), Radon (Mesozoic-present) | Visitor, not conqueror |
| **Sea** | Gojira | Sovereign |
| **Land** | Contested, but Gojira dominant | Sovereign |

---

## The Implication

When Godric limits his aerial capabilities, he's not showing weakness—he's **acknowledging history.** He has seen what true masters of the sky look like across geological time, and he chooses not to diminish their legacy by claiming a domain he didn't earn.
