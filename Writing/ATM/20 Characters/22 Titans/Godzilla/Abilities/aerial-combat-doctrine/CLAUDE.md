## Core Concept

> This doctrine is a **love letter to Rodan and Scylla** — two Titans whose unique mastery of their domains inspired Godric's approach to aerial combat. The techniques honor their sovereignty while enabling him to operate as a "visitor" rather than a conqueror of the sky.
