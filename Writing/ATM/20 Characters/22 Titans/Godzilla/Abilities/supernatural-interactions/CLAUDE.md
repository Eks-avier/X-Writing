# CLAUDE.md - Godric's Supernatural Interactions

## Overview

Godric represents a unique anomaly in supernatural systems: a being **completely incapable** of perceiving or wielding psychic and magical forces, yet possessing adaptations that make him their greatest counter. This folder documents the profound difference between **inherent capability** and **evolved resistance** — the paradox of supernatural immunity.

**Core Paradox:** Godric is defined by what he lacks, yet becomes what others fear most.

## Kratonic Terminology

| Term | Greek | Application in This Document |
|------|-------|------------------------------|
| **Flaring** | Anapsis (ἄναψις) | Physical displacement of Primus Force (magic nullification) |
| **Submission** | Proskynesis (προσκύνησις) | Disruption of Soul-to-Primus and Brain-to-Psiforce connections |
| **Aura** | — | The territorial field where Kratonic effects manifest |
| **Neutralization** | Exoudeterosis (ἐξουδετέρωσις) | Dissolution of incoming supernatural attacks |
| **Penetration** | Diatresis (διάτρησις) | Bypassing magical/psionic defenses |

> **Note:** Godric's supernatural nullification operates through **brute-force Flaring** — his Manifestation saturates space, physically displacing ambient Primus Energy. This is NOT magical perception or manipulation; it is pure Kratonic pressure making the environment "hostile" to supernatural forces. His **Null** status (unable to perceive Primus) paradoxically enables this — he cannot be targeted by effects he cannot perceive.
