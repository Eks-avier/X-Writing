# Current Mental Defenses

## Resistance Capabilities

The result of the [[divine-moth-protection-project|Divine Moth Protection Project]]:

| Capability | Description |
|------------|-------------|
| **Passive Scraping Immunity** | Mothra and Battra cannot casually read his thoughts |
| **Active Probe Resistance** | Direct telepathic attempts require painful effort from both parties |
| **Memory Protection** | Hyperthymesia remains secure despite external mental pressure |
| **Universal Application** | If the top two telepaths on Earth cannot breach his defenses, no one can |

---

## The Pain Mechanism

When telepaths attempt forced mental contact:

| Participant | Experience |
|-------------|------------|
| **Godric** | Mental pressure and instinctive resistance activation |
| **Telepath** | Severe psychic feedback and potential injury |

### Effects

| Aspect | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Mutual Deterrent** | Both parties discouraged from non-consensual contact |
| **Emergency Override** | Only extreme circumstances justify forced penetration |
| **Cost-Benefit Calculus** | Any telepath must weigh the pain against the potential gain |

---

## Practical Applications

### What This Means in Combat

* **Psychic Warfare Immunity**: Direct mental attacks fail or backfire
* **Information Security**: Strategic plans cannot be extracted
* **Communication Hub**: Can serve as telepathic relay point for allies
* **Psychological Warfare Resistance**: Cannot be demoralized through mental manipulation

### What This Means for Relationships

* **Voluntary Connection Only**: Telepathic communication requires his consent
* **Privacy Preserved**: His inner thoughts remain his own
* **Trust Required**: Linking minds is a conscious choice, not passive vulnerability
* **[[telepathic-link-sustainability|Sustainable Links]]**: Can maintain connections without draining his psychic allies

---

## Limitations

Despite these defenses:

* Barriers require active maintenance during extreme pressure
* Sufficient psychic force could theoretically breach them (none exists on Earth currently)
* Emotional states can affect barrier strength
* Voluntary lowering for trusted contacts creates temporary vulnerability
