## Theoretical Basis

These developments would follow the established [[adaptation-under-pressure|adaptation under pressure]] pattern:

1. **Encounter novel threat** → Survival pressure
2. **Identify limitation** → Problem definition
3. **Develop countermeasure** → Adaptation process
4. **Integrate capability** → New baseline

Each potential evolution listed above represents a logical response to a specific type of supernatural threat Godric might encounter.
