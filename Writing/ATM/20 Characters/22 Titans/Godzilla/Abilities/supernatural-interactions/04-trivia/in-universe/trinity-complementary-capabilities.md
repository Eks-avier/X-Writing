## Combined Effectiveness

### Complete Coverage

No supernatural system goes uncountered by the trio:

| Threat Type | Primary Counter | Backup |
|-------------|-----------------|--------|
| **Psychic Attack** | Godric (immunity) | Mothra (counter-telepathy) |
| **Magical Attack** | Godric (suppression) | Battra (counter-magic) |
| **Physical Attack** | Godric (combat) | All (combined force) |
| **Information Warfare** | Mothra (network) | Godric (memory protection) |
| **Reality Manipulation** | Battra (expertise) | Godric (anchoring) |

### Mutual Protection

Each member covers the others' inherent vulnerabilities:

| Member | Vulnerability | Protected By |
|--------|---------------|--------------|
| **Godric** | Psychic exposure | Mothra/Battra (barrier project) |
| **Mothra** | Physical combat | Godric (direct defense) |
| **Battra** | Primus suppression | Mothra (psychic coordination) |

### Amplified Power

Godric's suppression enables allies' abilities to dominate unopposed:

* Enemy sorcerers lose ambient Primus access
* Battra retains full capability (internal reserves + source-cycling)
* Net result: magical superiority in Godric's presence

---

## Strategic Flexibility

The Trinity offers multiple approaches to any supernatural threat:

1. **Godric-led**: Suppression + physical engagement
2. **Mothra-led**: Psychic coordination + support
3. **Battra-led**: Magical assault + reality manipulation
4. **Combined**: All three synchronized for overwhelming force
