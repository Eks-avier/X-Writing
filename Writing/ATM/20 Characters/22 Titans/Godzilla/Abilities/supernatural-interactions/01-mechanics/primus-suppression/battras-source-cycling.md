# Battra's Source-Cycling Strategy

## The Problem

During their confrontation, [[Battra, the Lord of the Mystic Arts|Battra]] discovered that Godric's [[nullification-mechanics|Primus Suppression]] could cripple standard magical practice.

### Standard Magical Practice

| Aspect | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Single Source** | Most sorcerers draw from one familiar Primus reservoir |
| **Efficiency Focus** | Optimizing connection to preferred magical source |
| **Deep Attunement** | Stronger bond enables more powerful spellcasting |
| **Vulnerability** | Suppression of primary source cripples magical capability |

---

## Battra's Innovation

### The Source-Cycling Strategy

| Aspect | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Multiple Reservoirs** | Maintains connections to dozens of Primus sources |
| **Rapid Switching** | Constantly cycles through different magical wellsprings |
| **Suppression Evasion** | "Shakes off" Godric's influence by changing sources |
| **Sustained Capability** | Maintains full magical power despite nullification field |

### Why This Works

| Factor | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Overwhelming Targets** | Godric cannot suppress all sources simultaneously |
| **Adaptation Speed** | Faster source-switching than suppression activation |
| **Magical Virtuosity** | Requires master-level skill to maintain multiple connections |
| **Energy Cost** | Extremely taxing but enables continued magical warfare |

---

## The Cost

Battra estimated requiring **200 times** normal effort to match Godric's efficiency while employing this strategy:

* Constant mental multitasking
* Managing puppets, spells, sources, and tactics simultaneously
* Energy management during continuous cycling
* Maintaining combat effectiveness while adapting

This represents the extreme measures required to counter Godric's suppression—a testament to how effective his nullification truly is.

---

## Implications

Source-cycling demonstrates:

1. Godric's suppression *can* be countered, but only by master-level sorcerers
2. The counter requires enormous effort and skill investment
3. Lesser magic users have no viable response
4. Even Battra was pushed to his limits
