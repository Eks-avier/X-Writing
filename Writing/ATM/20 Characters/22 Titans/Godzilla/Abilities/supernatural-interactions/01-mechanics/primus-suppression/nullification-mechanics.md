# Nullification Mechanics

## Environmental Primus Suppression

### Standard Magical Environment

| Aspect | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Ambient Primus** | Background magical energy available for casting |
| **Sorcerer Access** | Magic users tap into environmental Primus reserves |
| **Spell Fuel** | Ambient energy supplements personal magical capacity |
| **Renewable Resource** | Environment naturally replenishes magical energy |

### Godric's Suppression Field

| Parameter | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| **Area Denial** | Prevents access to ambient Primus in his vicinity |
| **Range Limitation** | Effect extends approximately 50-meter radius |
| **Selective Interference** | Only affects external magical energy access |
| **Personal Reserves** | Sorcerers retain their internally stored Primus |

---

## Kratonic Mechanism

This suppression operates through **brute-force Flaring** (Anapsis):

> Godric's Manifestation saturates space, physically displacing ambient Primus Energy. This is NOT magical perception or manipulation; it is pure Kratonic pressure making the environment "hostile" to supernatural forces.

### Why His Null Status Enables This

His **Null** status (unable to perceive Primus) paradoxically enhances this ability:

* Cannot be targeted by effects he cannot perceive
* No magical "fingerprint" for sorcerers to counter
* Pure physical force application, not magical technique
* Immune to magical feedback or backlash

---

## Practical Effect

Within Godric's suppression field:

| Effect | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Reduced Casting** | Sorcerers limited to internal reserves only |
| **Spell Weakening** | Spells that draw on ambient energy diminish |
| **Extended Combat** | Forces magical opponents into attrition |
| **Tactical Control** | Creates "dead zones" for magical support |

---

## Limitations

* Doesn't affect internally stored Primus
* Master sorcerers can adapt (see [[battras-source-cycling]])
* Requires active maintenance
* Energy cost for sustained suppression
