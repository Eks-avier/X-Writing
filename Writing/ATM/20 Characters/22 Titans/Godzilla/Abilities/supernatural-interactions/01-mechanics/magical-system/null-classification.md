# Null Classification

## Fundamental Limitations

### The Definition of "Null"

| Limitation | Description |
|------------|-------------|
| **Soul Incompatibility** | Cannot convert Primus into usable magical energy |
| **Perception Blindness** | Cannot see magical preparations, effects, or energies |
| **Universal Restriction** | No sorcerer can alter Null status |
| **Species-Wide Trait** | All *Titanus gojira* share this limitation |

---

## The Magic-Reality Interface

### What Godric Cannot Perceive

From a sorcerer's perspective, a Battra portal appears as:

> *"A ring forms in the air, crackling with energy. Patterns and particles protrude from the border of the ring, purple in color as his energy tends to be. Then, another part of the world can be seen through the ring: a portal to New York City from Tokyo."*

### What Godric Actually Experiences

From his perspective, the same portal appears as:

> *"Battra does a circular motion in the air and within seconds he could see a circular image of the city of New York in his midst."*

### The Critical Distinction

| Aspect | Visibility to Godric |
|--------|---------------------|
| **Magical Process** | Completely invisible |
| **Physical Effects** | Fully observable and interactable |
| **Tactical Implication** | Can use magical effects without understanding their nature |

---

## Implications

Being Null means Godric operates in a fundamentally different reality than magic users:

* **Cause and Effect Inverted**: He sees effects without causes
* **No Warning Signs**: No "sparks" or preparation indicators
* **Physical Framework**: Must interpret everything through physical observation
* **Compensatory Evolution**: Led to [[inverse-sensing-evolution|Inverse Sensing Evolution]]
