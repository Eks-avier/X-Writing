# Combat Applications

## Predictive Defense

Using [[inverse-sensing-evolution|Inverse Sensing]], Godric employs several defensive tactics:

| Tactic | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Pre-Manifestation Warning** | Detects magical attacks before they fully form |
| **Positioning Advantage** | Can begin evasive action while spell is "invisible" |
| **Counter-Timing** | Times physical attacks to interrupt magical preparation |
| **Environmental Awareness** | Senses reality alterations across entire battlefield |

---

## Effect Interaction

Despite being [[null-classification|Null]], Godric can interact with magical effects once they manifest physically:

| Approach | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| **Physical Counters** | Uses conventional force against magical effects |
| **Energy Disruption** | Applies atomic energy to interfere with magical constructs |
| **Environmental Manipulation** | Alters battlefield to counter magical advantages |
| **Adaptive Tactics** | Learns magical patterns through observing their physical results |

---

## Battlefield Applications

### Anti-Magic Operations

| Capability | Description |
|------------|-------------|
| **[[nullification-mechanics\|Suppression Field]]** | Denies magical support to enemy forces |
| **Effect Interaction** | Can physically counter magical constructs and spells |
| **Predictive Defense** | Pre-manifestation sensing enables proactive countermeasures |
| **Adaptive Evolution** | Develops new capabilities when facing unknown magical systems |

### Integration with Physical Combat

Godric's anti-magic capabilities combine with his physical prowess:

* Closes distance before spells complete
* Uses terrain created by magical effects against the caster
* Forces sorcerers into physical confrontation where they're disadvantaged
* Treats magical attacks as physical hazards to be navigated

---

## Limitation

> These tactics require **time and observation**. Against an unknown magical system or opponent, Godric starts at a significant disadvantage until he can learn the patterns.
