# The Fifth Fundamental Force

## Universal Force Hierarchy

| Rank | Force | Domain |
|------|-------|--------|
| 1 | **Strong Nuclear Force** | Controls atomic nucleus binding |
| 2 | **Electromagnetic Force** | Governs electrical and magnetic phenomena |
| 3 | **Weak Nuclear Force** | Manages radioactive decay |
| 4 | **Gravitational Force** | Creates attraction between masses |
| 5 | **Primus Force** | Powers all magical phenomena |

---

## Godric's Command Scope

| Force | Level of Control |
|-------|-----------------|
| **Strong Nuclear** | Full Control |
| **Electromagnetic** | Full Control |
| **Weak Nuclear** | Full Control |
| **Gravitational** | Partial Access (still locked—Ghidorah's specialty) |
| **Primus** | Absolute Blindness (cannot perceive or manipulate) |

### The Paradox

Despite having **no access** to Primus, Godric possesses:

* **[[nullification-mechanics|Suppression Capability]]**: Can prevent others from accessing ambient Primus
* **[[inverse-sensing-evolution|Effect Detection]]**: Can sense magical results through physical reality changes
* **Combat Dominance**: Effectively counters magic without using it

This represents the ultimate expression of [[adaptation-under-pressure|Adaptation Under Pressure]]: mastering opposition to a force he cannot even perceive.
