## The Species Truth

This theme connects directly to *Titanus gojira* biology:

> **[[Limitless Adaptation]]**: The species' defining trait is the ability to adapt to any challenge given sufficient pressure and time.

The supernatural interaction system demonstrates this trait pushed to its absolute limit—adapting to forces the species cannot even *perceive*.
