## The Phantom Tail Doctrine

Unique to this folder is the **Phantom Tail Doctrine**—a power generation principle that exists *only* because of Godric's human transformation:

- Channels the torque of his "lost" tail through human limbs
- Creates strikes with "impossible" weight and momentum
- Represents his most elegant adaptation to human form
- Only meaningful because his Titan form had a tail to remember

This technique could not exist at the species level because it requires the specific circumstance of losing one's Titan form.

## Trophy Assignment

Per the AA-Kratos P200 Trophy Framework, the Primal Arsenal represents:

> **No single trophy** — These are not learned from others but inherited. They represent Godric's *essential nature* rather than relationships that shaped him.

However, their integration into his human form carries thematic weight:
- **Statement of identity**: "I'm still me"
- **Acceptance of duality**: Martial artist AND monster
- **Pattern-breaking**: Opponents cannot prepare for both
