# Tactical Functions

> *"His ultimate area-denial and 'get off me' ability."*
> — AA-Kratos P281

The Nuclear Pulse serves specific tactical purposes in gojira combat—it is not a primary offensive tool but a **situational response** to particular battlefield conditions.

---

## Primary Applications

### Swarm Counter
**Scenario:** Multiple weaker opponents surrounding the gojira

The pulse obliterates numerous enemies simultaneously, eliminating the swarm's numerical advantage in a single action.

### Grapple Escape
**Scenario:** Opponent has achieved clinch or restraint position

The omnidirectional force creates instant separation, breaking holds that would otherwise require complex grappling exchanges to escape.

### Area Denial
**Scenario:** Need to clear immediate vicinity of all threats

The pulse establishes a temporary exclusion zone, allowing the gojira to reposition or prepare for subsequent action.

### Defensive Reset
**Scenario:** Overwhelmed by multiple attack vectors

When facing pressure from multiple directions, the pulse clears all threats simultaneously, allowing the gojira to regain tactical initiative.

---

## Inherent Limitations

The Nuclear Pulse's power comes with significant constraints:

| Limitation | Description |
|------------|-------------|
| **Indiscriminate** | Cannot target specific enemies—affects everything in radius |
| **Energy Cost** | Significant expenditure compared to directed attacks |
| **Ally Danger** | Must ensure allies are clear before activation |
| **Cooldown** | Requires momentary recovery before subsequent high-output techniques |

These limitations make the pulse a **tactical choice** rather than a default action.

---

## Strategic Philosophy

The Nuclear Pulse embodies a specific gojira tactical philosophy:

> *"When surrounded, don't fight outward sequentially—eliminate all threats simultaneously."*

This reflects the species' preference for **decisive action** over prolonged engagement. Rather than addressing threats one by one, the pulse resolves multiple problems in a single moment.

---

## Energy Economics

From a resource management perspective:

| Approach | Energy Cost | Time Required | Risk |
|----------|-------------|---------------|------|
| **Sequential elimination** | Low per target | High total time | Exposure during extended engagement |
| **Nuclear Pulse** | High single expenditure | Instant | Energy depletion but immediate resolution |

The pulse trades **energy reserves** for **time and certainty**—a worthwhile exchange when the alternative is prolonged vulnerability.
