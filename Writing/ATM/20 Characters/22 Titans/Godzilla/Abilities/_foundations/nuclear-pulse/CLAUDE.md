## The EM Carrier Principle

The Nuclear Pulse demonstrates the [[Holariston Dual-Nature|Holariston]] nature in action:

| Component | Force | Function |
|-----------|-------|----------|
| **Payload** | Pyrenikon (Nuclear) | Destructive atomic energy |
| **Delivery** | Elektromagneton (EM) | Expanding magnetic bubble carrying the energy |

Without EM fields, atomic energy released omnidirectionally would simply detonate the gojira themselves. The EM "carrier wave" allows force projection in all directions simultaneously.
