# The EM Carrier Wave

> *"How does he project atomic force in all directions without detonating himself? The answer lies in his hidden Elektromagneton nature."*
> — AA-Kratos P281

The Nuclear Pulse is an omnidirectional release of atomic energy—but the mechanism that makes it possible is **electromagnetic**, not nuclear.

---

## The Fundamental Problem

Atomic energy released in all directions simultaneously should, logically, destroy the gojira emitting it. The energy has no "preferred direction"—it would expand equally inward and outward.

The solution lies in the [[Holariston Dual-Nature|Holariston nature]] that all gojira possess.

---

## The Solution: EM Bubble

When a gojira initiates a Nuclear Pulse:

**Stage 1: EM Field Generation**
The gojira generates a momentary electromagnetic field that begins at their body's surface and expands rapidly outward.

**Stage 2: Energy Loading**
Atomic energy is released from the body but is immediately "caught" by the expanding EM field.

**Stage 3: Outward Propagation**
The EM bubble carries the atomic energy outward—the field serves as a **delivery vehicle** for the destructive payload.

**Stage 4: Impact Dissipation**
When the bubble encounters resistance (enemy, terrain, etc.), the EM structure collapses and releases its atomic payload at the point of contact.

---

## The Dual-Force Requirement

| Component | Force | Function |
|-----------|-------|----------|
| **The Payload** | Pyrenikon (Nuclear) | Raw destructive force |
| **The Delivery** | Elektromagneton (EM) | Expanding carrier that directs force outward |

This is why the Nuclear Pulse is a **Kratonic** technique rather than a simple biological release—it requires the coordinated application of both force types.

---

## Self-Protection Mechanism

The EM field serves a crucial protective function:

- **Outward bias** — The field is generated to expand outward, not inward
- **Origin point exclusion** — The gojira's body serves as the "anchor" of the field, exempt from its effects
- **Directional force** — All energy is pushed away from the origin rather than through it

This is an instinctive application of what Kratonic theory terms **Neutralization (Exoudeterosis)**—the dissolution of harmful energy before it can affect the wielder.

---

## Energy Cost

The Nuclear Pulse is energy-intensive compared to directed attacks:

| Factor | Impact |
|--------|--------|
| **EM field generation** | Requires energy to create the carrier |
| **Full-radius emission** | No focus means energy spreads across entire sphere |
| **Self-protection** | Additional energy required for Neutralization |

This is why the pulse is typically reserved for emergencies rather than used as a primary attack.

---

## Thematic Significance

The Nuclear Pulse demonstrates a fundamental truth about gojira power: even their most "chaotic" abilities rely on the **hidden structure** of their Elektromagneton nature. The Wild (Pyrenikon) is always contained and directed by The Order (Elektromagneton).
