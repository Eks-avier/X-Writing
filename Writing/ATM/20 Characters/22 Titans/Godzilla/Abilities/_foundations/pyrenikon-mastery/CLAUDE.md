# CLAUDE.md - Pyrenikon Mastery

## Overview

This folder documents Godric Nordson's personal relationship with his **Pyrenikon** (nuclear) nature—the half of his dual power that has always been conscious, trained, and celebrated.

Unlike his [[Writing/ATM/20 Characters/22 Titans/Godzilla/Abilities/_foundations/elektromagneton-awakening/CLAUDE|Elektromagneton]], which required awakening, Godric's Pyrenikon journey is about **inheritance and elevation**—taking what he was born with and refining it to heights no gojira imagined possible.

## Key Narrative

**The Arc:** Godric was born with exceptional Pyrenikon potential—his mother's overactive reactor—and spent 250 million years forging himself into a vessel capable of containing it. His Pyrenikon story is not about discovering something new, but about elevating something familiar to unprecedented heights.

**Central Tension:** The [[Pyrenikon]] is "The Wild"—chaotic, explosive, demanding release. Godric's journey is about containing that wildness without extinguishing it, channeling destruction into precision.
