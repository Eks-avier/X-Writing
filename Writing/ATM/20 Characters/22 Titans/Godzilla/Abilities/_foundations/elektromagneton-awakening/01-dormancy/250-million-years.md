# 250 Million Years: Godric's Unconscious Elektromagneton

> *"Despite the proto-gojira and their eventual descendants in the Four Factions not even being aware of the ability, it existed."*
> — AA-Kratos P302

## Summary

For 250 million years—longer than his entire species existed—Godric used his [[Elektromagneton]] nature constantly without knowing it. He navigated by magnetic sense, shrugged off electrical attacks, shaped his atomic breath through EM fields, and never once understood *how* he was doing any of it.

This document explores the pre-awakening period: how the hidden power operated, and why he never discovered it.

---

## The Unconscious Applications

### The Sixth Sense

Godric always knew where he was going. He could navigate the [[Hollow Earth]] without landmarks, traverse oceans with perfect directional sense, and "home in" on threats across vast distances.

He thought this was instinct. It was electromagnetic perception.

> *"He perceives the Earth's magnetic field lines as clearly as a road map."*
> — AA-Kratos P316

**What was really happening:**
- His body detected Earth's magnetic field
- He sensed EM signatures of other beings
- He tracked energy patterns across distances
- His territorial awareness operated through EM mapping

### The Lightning Rod

Godric was never hurt by electrical attacks (except [[Ghidorah Profile|Ghidorah's]] Kratonic-coated lightning). Lightning strikes, bioelectrical attacks, energy discharges—all were absorbed harmlessly.

He thought he was simply "tough." His EM fields were grounding everything.

**What was really happening:**
- Dorsal fins and scales acted as superconductors
- Incoming electricity was channeled and grounded
- Energy was absorbed rather than resisted
- He could even temporarily magnetize from absorbed charge

### The Shaping Function

Most critically: every time Godric used his [[Pyrenikon]] powers, his Elektromagneton was silently at work.

| Pyrenikon Ability | Hidden EM Function |
|-------------------|-------------------|
| Atomic Breath | EM "barrel" focusing explosion into beam |
| Nuclear Pulse | EM bubble carrying payload outward |
| Energy Coating | EM wireframe holding atomic energy in place |

Without understanding this, he simply did it instinctively—250 million years of muscle memory operating beneath conscious awareness.

---

## Why He Never Knew

### The Cognitive Barrier

> *"Without human science to distinguish and identify them, they have no framework to understand. How can beings from the Late Permian even know any of that anyway?"*
> — AA-Kratos P302

Godric lacked the conceptual vocabulary to recognize electromagnetic force as a separate phenomenon:
- No physics framework
- No way to distinguish "magnetic sense" from "instinct"
- No reason to question abilities that simply worked
- Pyrenikon was visible; Elektromagneton was not

### The Cultural Reinforcement

Gojira culture celebrated [[Pyrenikon]] and ignored everything else:
- Atomic breath was their identity
- Nuclear pulse was their desperation move
- "Invisible" abilities received no attention
- No one taught young gojira about EM

Godric learned what his culture taught. His culture didn't know half of what he was.

### The "You Cannot BE" Principle

> *"Krátos thrives when a Kráton is aware of their Manifestation. How can you **be** if you do not **know** yourself?"*
> — AA-Kratos P317

This principle explains the limitation: Godric could not consciously develop what he did not know existed. His EM powers remained at "unconscious competence"—functional but unrefined, like a muscle never deliberately trained.

---

## The Muscle Imbalance

> *"It's a muscle imbalance. Humans are born with muscles, of course. But when we neglect to train a specific set of them, they weaken, atrophy, or develop bad habits."*
> — AA-Kratos P317

The comparison is apt: Godric was like a naturally ambidextrous person trained only to use one hand. His EM "hand" was strong—it had to be, supporting his Pyrenikon for 250 million years—but it was clumsy, operating purely on instinct rather than intention.

**What this meant:**
- He could only improve *preexisting* applications
- Better senses, better defenses, stronger pulses
- But no *new* applications
- No conscious shaping or control
- No techniques like the Railgun

---

## The Hidden Potential

Despite his ignorance, Godric's EM nature was always there, always active:

| Potential | Dormant State |
|-----------|---------------|
| Controlled Pulse Radius | Only instinctive radius |
| Barrier Emission | Only incidental resistance |
| Deliberate Magnetization | Only reactive absorption |
| Railgun Acceleration | Completely inaccessible |
| Energy Shaping | Only unconscious defaults |

All of these waited beneath the surface, needing only awareness to unlock.

---

## The Irony

The deepest irony: Godric's enemies knew his Elektromagneton better than he did.

[[Mother Prime]] invented the MUTO EMP specifically to disrupt gojira EM fields—she understood the architecture of his power better than any gojira. Her parasitic children evolved to target what the gojira themselves never acknowledged.

The species nearly went extinct from an attack on a power they didn't know they had.

---

## The End of Dormancy

This 250-million-year period ended when [[Barb Profile|Barb]]—a turncoat MUTO—explained how gojira EM fields actually work.

> *See [[barbs-revelation]] for the awakening catalyst.*

The enemy taught him strength. The parasite's knowledge, turned against its original purpose, finally showed Godric the half of himself he'd never known.
