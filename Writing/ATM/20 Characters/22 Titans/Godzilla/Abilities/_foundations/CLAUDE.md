1. **Personal Foundations**: Godric Nordson's personal relationship with his **Holariston Polykrator** nature—his dual-force foundation as both Pyrenikon (nuclear) and Elektromagneton (electromagnetic) wielder

## Content Guidelines

### What Belongs in Species-Level Folders

- Universal mechanics all gojira share
- Cultural patterns across the species
- Abilities any gojira could theoretically use
- Biological capabilities from evolutionary heritage

### What Belongs in Personal Foundations

- Godric's personal history with each nature
- His awakening arcs and breakthrough moments
- Character development tied to his dual nature
- Thematic analysis of what each force represents for him
- How his nature differs from other gojira (due to lineage, experience, etc.)

### What Belongs in Technique Layer (Abilities)

- Specific techniques like Atomic Railgun, Stride, Mantle
- Combat applications and tactics
- Kratonic terminology and mechanics
- Technical breakdowns
- Godric's personal use of species-level abilities
