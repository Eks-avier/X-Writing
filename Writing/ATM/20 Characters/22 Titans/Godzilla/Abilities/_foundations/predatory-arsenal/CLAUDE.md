## Predatory Philosophy

These are not learned techniques—they are **instincts**. Every gojira carries within them the hunting behaviors of their aquatic predator ancestors:

- **Bite** as primary close-combat weapon
- **Death Roll** as grappling finisher
- **Territorial aggression** driving combat behavior

The refinement of these instincts through Kratonic discipline is a character-level achievement. The instincts themselves are universal to the species.
