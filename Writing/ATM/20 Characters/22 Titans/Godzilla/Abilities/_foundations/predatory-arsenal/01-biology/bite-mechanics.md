# Bite Mechanics

> *"His most shocking and primal weapon."*

The gojira bite is not a technique—it is a **biological weapon** inherited from millions of years of apex predator evolution.

---

## Anatomical Foundation

Gojira jaws represent peak predatory engineering:

| Feature | Function |
|---------|----------|
| **Jaw structure** | Massive crushing force capacity |
| **Tooth design** | Evolved for gripping, tearing, and crushing |
| **Neck musculature** | Supports head movement and bite power |
| **Sensory integration** | Precision targeting for vital areas |

These are not trained muscles or developed skills. They are **species-standard equipment**.

---

## Combat Applications

The bite serves multiple tactical functions:

### Primary Attack
Direct damage through crushing force. Against other Titans, this can breach armor, sever limbs, or target vulnerable areas.

### Clinch Control
When grappling range closes to nothing, the bite becomes the only remaining weapon. Arms may be pinned, but the head remains free.

### Weapon Disarmament
Gojira jaws can bite through weapons, artificial limbs, or restraints that would otherwise require complex escape techniques.

### Grip Establishment
Securing a bite creates anchor point for subsequent techniques (such as the [[death-roll|Death Roll]]).

---

## Instinctual Nature

The bite is not taught—it is **remembered**:

- Young gojira use biting instinctively before any training
- Combat situations trigger bite response without conscious decision
- The behavior is hardwired into species neurology

This makes the bite fundamentally different from Kratonic techniques, which require development. The bite simply *is*.

---

## Psychological Impact

The bite carries enormous psychological weight in Titan combat:

- Other Titans recognize it as apex predator behavior
- Being bitten by a gojira triggers instinctive prey response
- The sheer **primality** of the attack can shatter opponent composure

A gojira using their bite is not fighting—they are **hunting**.
