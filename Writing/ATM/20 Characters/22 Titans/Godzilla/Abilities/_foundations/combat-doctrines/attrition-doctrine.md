# The Attrition Doctrine

> *"The longer it goes, the more I win."*

The Attrition Doctrine is the gojira's advantage against the entire rest of the Titan world—the certainty that time is always on their side.

---

## The Four Pillars

Gojira attrition advantage rests on four biological pillars:

| Pillar | Function |
|--------|----------|
| **Durability** | Tank incredible damage without structural failure |
| **Regeneration** | Rapidly repair what damage does occur |
| **Endurance** | Vast energy reserves sustaining extended combat |
| **[[Limitless Adaptation]]** | The true killer—actively learning and evolving counters |

Each pillar reinforces the others. Together, they create a combat dynamic where gojira *improve* as fights continue.

---

## Why Other Titans Fear Gojira

The Attrition Doctrine creates a terrifying equation for opponents:

**Insufficient firepower** → **Cannot shut down quickly** → **Tide inevitably turns**

Other Titans understand this mathematics:
- Early advantage means nothing if it cannot be pressed to victory
- Every passing moment allows gojira regeneration and adaptation
- The window for victory shrinks constantly
- Eventually, the gojira will win simply by surviving

This is why gojira encounters often end in flight rather than fight—experienced Titans know that unless victory is certain and immediate, defeat is inevitable.

---

## Limitless Adaptation: The Ultimate Factor

The fourth pillar—[[Limitless Adaptation]]—is what transforms attrition from "outlasting" to "winning":

| Phase | Effect |
|-------|--------|
| **Early combat** | Gojira takes damage, learns opponent patterns |
| **Mid combat** | Adaptation begins countering successful tactics |
| **Late combat** | Opponent's effective strategies become useless |

The longer combat continues, the more **specifically optimized** the gojira becomes against that particular opponent. Victory becomes mathematical certainty.

---

## Strategic Implications

The Attrition Doctrine shapes gojira tactical approach:

### Patience Over Aggression
There is no urgency to end fights quickly (unless [[one-shot-doctrine|One-Shot]] opportunity presents). Time is an ally.

### Damage Acceptance
Taking damage is acceptable if it leads to information gathering. Every hit teaches the gojira something.

### Energy Conservation
Efficiency matters more than immediate output. Resources must last.

### Certainty Over Risk
Risky decisive actions are unnecessary. Patient inevitability is preferred.

---

## Interaction with One-Shot

The Attrition Doctrine works **alongside** the [[one-shot-doctrine|One-Shot Doctrine]]:

1. **First:** Attempt immediate victory if opportunity exists
2. **If that fails:** Shift to attrition mentality
3. **As attrition progresses:** One-shot opportunities may emerge through adaptation
4. **Eventually:** Victory becomes inevitable either way

The doctrines are sequential stages, not competing philosophies.

---

## Thematic Significance

The Attrition Doctrine reflects the gojira as **forces of nature** rather than merely powerful creatures:

- Nature does not rush
- Time is always on nature's side
- Eventually, everything yields
- Patience and inevitability trump force

This philosophy positions gojira as elemental presences—beings that cannot be outwaited because they *are* the weight of time itself.
