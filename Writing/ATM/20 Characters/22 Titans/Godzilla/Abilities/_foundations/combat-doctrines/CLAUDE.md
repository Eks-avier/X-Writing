## The Two Universal Doctrines

All gojira combat is built on two complementary principles:

| Doctrine | Philosophy | When Applied |
|----------|------------|--------------|
| **One-Shot** | End fights immediately if possible | Against vulnerable targets |
| **Attrition** | Outlast opponents through endurance | Against durable targets |

These are not mutually exclusive—gojira seamlessly transition between them based on tactical assessment.
