## Overview

This folder documents the **Burning Form** as a species-level *potential* of *Titanus gojira*—the theoretical ultimate state that any gojira could achieve, but which 99.9% fail to survive.

## The Two Requirements

Any gojira could *theoretically* attain the Burning Form thanks to [[Limitless Adaptation]]. However:

| Requirement | Challenge |
|-------------|-----------|
| **The Engine** | A heart capable of generating excessive energy (achievable through desperation) |
| **The Radiator** | A body capable of *regulating* that energy (nearly impossible) |

The first is easy—any adult gojira can push their reactor past safety limits through supreme effort. The second is what kills them.
