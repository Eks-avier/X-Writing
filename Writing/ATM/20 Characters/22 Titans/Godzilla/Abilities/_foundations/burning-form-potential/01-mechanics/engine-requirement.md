# The Engine Requirement

> *"Any adult gojira can, in a moment of supreme desperation, push their internal reactor past its safety limits."*

The first requirement for the Burning Form is deceptively simple: **a heart capable of generating excessive energy**.

---

## The "Easy" Part

Every gojira possesses an internal nuclear reactor. Under normal circumstances, this reactor operates within biological safety limits—producing enough energy for daily function and combat without threatening the body's integrity.

In moments of supreme desperation, any adult gojira can override these limits:

- **Willpower surge** pushes reactor output beyond normal thresholds
- **Energy production** increases dramatically, flooding the body
- **Burning state** begins as excess energy manifests as heat and light

This is the **Engine** of the Burning Form—the heart generating more energy than the body was designed to contain.

---

## Why This Is Achievable

The Engine requirement is met through:

| Factor | Contribution |
|--------|--------------|
| **Desperation** | Extreme circumstances triggering override instinct |
| **Willpower** | Conscious choice to push past safety limits |
| **Kratonic focus** | Concentrated intent amplifying reactor output |

Any gojira facing extinction-level threat can access this. The *will to survive* is powerful enough to break normal biological constraints.

---

## The Problem

Achieving the Engine is not the barrier to the Burning Form. The problem is what happens *next*.

When a gojira pushes their reactor past safety limits:
- Body temperature rises catastrophically
- Internal tissues begin to break down
- Radiation floods systems never meant to handle such levels
- Self-destruction becomes inevitable

Without the [[radiator-requirement|Radiator]] to regulate this energy, the Engine simply destroys its own vessel.

---

## Thematic Significance

The Engine requirement demonstrates a core gojira truth: **willpower can override biology**. The species' capacity to push their reactors beyond design limits represents the Kratonic principle in its purest form—will shaping physical reality.

But will alone is not enough. Power without control is simply destruction.
