# Regeneration & Clothing Compulsion

> *"They don't dress like humans; they dress according to 250-million-year-old Titan biology."*

The gojira preference for covering their skin is not mere cultural modesty—it is a **biological imperative** rooted in one of their most famous abilities: regeneration.

---

## I. The Biological Imperative

### The "Ick" of Imperfection

To a human, a scar is a story and a tattoo is art. To a *Titanus gojira*, a mark on the skin is a **symptom**.

**The Regeneration Metric:**
- A potent regeneration factor indicates good health
- A *gojira* cannot be "sick" in the conventional sense
- Therefore, a mark that persists means regeneration is failing
- Failing regeneration signals: exhaustion OR something deeply wrong

**The Evolutionary Trauma:**
- This reaction is evolutionary PTSD from the *Titanus jinshin-mushi* parasites
- A gojira who couldn't heal fast enough was about to be parasitized
- Seeing a permanent mark triggers lizard-brain disgust: *"That creature is sick. That creature is prey."*

### The Compulsion for Coverage

Covering up isn't just modesty—it's **visual hygiene**.

- By keeping skin covered, they present a uniform, unblemished surface
- Projects absolute health and vitality to the world
- A form of biological reassurance

---

## II. Godric's Diagnostic Protocols

When Godric looks at his children, he isn't just checking happiness—he is scanning their skin for cellular refresh rate.

**Health Indicators He Monitors:**
| Variable | What It Indicates |
|----------|------------------|
| Atomic energy emission | Power levels |
| Heart health | Core vitality |
| Sensory fidelity | Awareness/alertness |
| **Regeneration factor** | Overall systemic health |

If Godric obtained a tattoo and it stayed for more than several minutes, that would be cause for worry—or evidence of exhaustion.

[[Mothra, the Queen of the Monsters|Maria]] uses psionic methods for similar diagnostics, but gojira prioritize physical signs.

---

## III. The Tactile Love Letter

### Godric's Wardrobe: Wrapped in Her Essence

The most romantic detail of Godric's biology: his clothing preference is a subconscious confession of love spanning decades.

**The Composition:**
- **Every single piece** of Godric's clothing is made from Maria's silk
- From underwear to socks—wherever fabric would be the human choice
- Later (late 2021): added composite material of his own shed scales + her silk

**The Emotional Truth:**
- Even during the "denial phase" (2021-2025), he refused anything but her silk
- He literally **wrapped himself in her essence** every single day
- Craved the texture of her power against his skin
- A way to be held by her, even when standing apart in a briefing room

**The Scale-Silk Composite:**
- Softness of her silk + hardness of his scales
- Her grace + his strength
- He feels "right" only when encased in the material reality of their bond
- A sensory addiction born of love

## IV. Family Manifestations

### Darius: The Victory Flex

[[Dagon|Darius]] doesn't cover up—he's **showing off**.

- Walks around shirtless or with shirt open
- A declaration: *"Look. No scars. No parasites. I died, I came back, and I am pristine."*
- Spent his first life terrified of parasites
- Now exposing skin is defiance—proving he is healthy and safe
- The ultimate "flex" of a healthy Northern male

### The Children

| Child | Response to Compulsion |
|-------|----------------------|
| **Junior** | Double compulsion (gojira + mosura); wears the most layers |
| **Lora** | Double compulsion; revels in wearing her own silk |
| **Leo** | The outlier—doesn't care about the "ick"; relies on raw regeneration |

**Junior and Lora's Additional Layer:**
- Inherited father's adoration of feeling their own silk on their bodies
- Same satisfaction as a seamstress admiring her work on her own body
- Self-soothing: their silk smells like them, feels like them
- A feedback loop of biological security

---

## V. The Mosura Factor

### The Silk Cocoon Instinct

Mosura are surrounded by silk through their entire lifecycle:
- As larvae: wrapped
- During metamorphosis: pupa
- As imago: producing

In human form, this manifests as psychological aversion to nakedness—being without clothes triggers a crisis of exposure.

**Maria's Wardrobe:**
- Also wears scale-silk composite identical to Godric's
- Contains both his scales and her silk

**The "Professional Phase" Agony:**
- 2021-2025 was emotionally agonizing for Maria
- Seeing Godric wrapped entirely in HER essence while denying their feelings
- A form of intimacy torture neither would acknowledge

**Now: The Symphonia of the Skin**
- Married, they achieve biological marriage through wearing each other
- The final evolution of the clothing compulsion

### Hybrid Double-Compulsion

**Junior and Lora** have two overlapping instincts:
1. **Mosura "Wrap" Instinct:** Safety/cocoon sensation
2. **Gojira "Standard" Instinct:** Perfect/unmarked skin

This creates the most comprehensively-covered Nordsons, layering both compulsions.
