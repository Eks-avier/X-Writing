# Seasonal Biology

> *"Their humanity is a performance, but their nature is absolute."*

As a [[Northern Faction|Northern Gojira]], Godric's seasonal preferences are diametrically opposed to human norms. Winter is not a time to bundle up—it is a **homecoming**.

---

## I. The Call of the Cold

### The Northern Exception

This exception applies *only* because the Nordsons are Northern gojira. Southern, Western, or Eastern gojira would not share this reaction to winter (though they may have similar preferences for other seasons).

**Biological Logic:**
- Gojira bodies run hot (internal nuclear reactors)
- Cold acts as a perfect natural cooling system
- Snow and ice don't bite—they **soothe**
- Feels like a cool drink of water on a hot day

**Psychological Logic:**
- Cold air feels "clean" to Northern gojira
- Reminds them of the Permian eras, of [[Dagon]]'s cave
- An echo of a time before humid modern warmth

---

## II. The Winter Preference

### Godric: The Tortured Strategist

During his "Winter Camouflage" phase at Monarch, Godric was physically miserable. Wearing sweaters and coats to sell the "hibernation" lie while overheating internally—wanting nothing more than to stand shirtless in the snow.

**At Monarch:**
- Layers of heavy clothing = sensory nightmare
- Sitting by heaters and pretending to shiver
- No wonder his winter grumpiness was legendary

**At [[Monster Island]]:**
- Finally free to enjoy winter properly
- Shirtless in blizzards, steam rising off skin
- The one time he allows complete physical comfort
- Craves the feeling of snow on human skin, cold breeze

## III. Family Manifestations

| Family Member | Winter Response |
|--------------|-----------------|
| **Godric** | Shirtless in blizzards; finds it deeply peaceful |
| **[[Dagon\|Darius]]** | Shirtless, shorts—treats blizzards like beach days |
| **[[Leonard Nordson\|Leo]]** | Takes it too far; forbidden from appearing nude around guests |
| **[[Godric Nordson Jr.\|Junior]]** | Lighter clothing, unbuttoned shirts, rolled sleeves |
| **[[Lora Nordson\|Lora]]** | Sleeveless dresses, exposed neck and back—maintains elegance |
| **[[Rose Nordson\|Rose]]** | Finds it hilariously absurd (remembers being human) |

### The "Polar Bear" Phenomenon

**Darius:** The quintessential "Snow Dog." Walking around with his shirt open or completely shirtless while snow piles on his shoulders. A declaration: *"This is the good life."*

**Leo:** The "Husky" who refuses to come inside. [[Mothra, the Queen of the Monsters|Maria]] has explicitly forbidden him from appearing in his "birthday suit" around guests—including the Betas, Wardens, and [[Kong]]. He genuinely doesn't understand the problem.

### The Elegant Thaw

**Junior and Lora** have more refined responses:
- Cold air provides sensory feedback like physical pressure
- Feels like being "held" or "contained"—substituting their clothing compulsion
- Junior: Unbuttoning is equivalent to a human taking off their shoes
- Lora: Backless dresses in snowstorms make her look even more untouchable

## IV. Strategic Implications

### Monster Island Migration

[[Monster Island]] and [[Castle Bravo]] must remain parallel on the x-axis (opposite hemispheres) for strategic coverage. However, they can move north/south to chase seasons.

**[[Battra, the Lord of the Mystic Arts|Battra]]'s Role:**
- Regularly requested to migrate the island to winter regions
- Allows the Nordson family to enjoy their seasonal preference
- Could technically chase eternal winter through hemisphere-hopping

**Luxury Vacations:**
When strategic duties prevent migration:
- Family takes trips to colder regions (Siberia, Greenland, Andes)
- [[Mark Russell]] explicitly forbids Monarch from covering expenses if "incidents" occur
- (Suggesting there have been destructive incidents—leveled forests, broken glaciers)

**Natural Conditions Preferred:**
Godric refuses to ask [[Scylla]] or other Hydrokinetic Wardens to artificially create winter. He wants:
- Authentic atmospheric pressure
- Genuine snowfall
- Real storm fronts

## V. Rose: The Human Barometer

Rose (formerly Erika) serves as the perfect audience surrogate:
- Her hybrid biology can handle the cold (has Gojira cells)
- But her *mind* remembers being human—snow equals freezing
- Watching her family lounge half-naked in sub-zero temperatures triggers phantom shivers
- A stark reminder that despite their appearance, these people are *not* human

## VI. The Lepidiel Factor

### Mosura Temperature Regulation

Normal mosura are dormant in winter, active in spring/summer. [[Mothra, the Queen of the Monsters|Mothra]] and [[Battra, the Lord of the Mystic Arts|Battra]] don't know this—they grew up chasing Godric through blizzards.

**Scale Adaptations:**
| Being | Scale Type | Function |
|-------|-----------|----------|
| Mothra | Reflection | Perfect homeostasis; body heat reflects inward |
| Battra | Refraction | Bends cold around him; less efficient but effective |

### Hybrid Children's Curated Experience

The children use their mother's gifts to experience their father's nature more deeply:

| Child | Method |
|-------|--------|
| **Junior** | Magnifies air's chill to feel it intensely (satisfies gojira craving while wearing layers) |
| **Lora** | Makes cold "stick" longer (sustained chill while staying warm in motion) |
| **Leo** | No scales yet; must tank cold naturally—future Rainbow Form may enable heat-splitting |
