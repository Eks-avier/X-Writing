#### 5. Key Proportional Analyses and Adaptations

*   **a. The Combat-Optimized Arm:** Godric's upper arm to forearm circumference ratio (**1.2361:1**) represents a perfect $2/\phi$ relationship. This is not a classical artistic proportion but an evolutionary adaptation for combat, creating an ideal balance between the explosive power of his Titan heritage and the manipulative precision required by a human form.
*   **b. Titan-to-Human Mass Distribution Inversion:** His Titan form's power was centered in the lower body (legs, tail). His human form inverts this, concentrating mass and power in the upper body (torso, shoulders, arms). This shift from a locomotive base to a manipulative upper body symbolizes his transformation from primordial force to conscious protector.

#### 6. Mathematical Essence and Artistic Context

*   **a. Mathematical Embodiment of Character:** The recurring Golden Ratios throughout his physique create a mathematical self-reference, mirroring his timeless nature. The near-perfection of his visible features represents his vigilant, superhuman persona, while the deliberate “Titan Echo” deviations in his core measurements reflect his authentic, mortal power and vulnerability.
*   **b. Classical and Modern Artistic References:** His physique evokes proportions found in classical masterpieces like Polykleitos' *Doryphoros* and the *Farnese Hercules*, but with a functional ruggedness more akin to modern depictions like Daniel Craig in “Casino Royale” or Henry Cavill in “Man of Steel.”
