#### 1. Foundational Principles of Measurement

*   **a. Mathematical Precision Standards:** All measurements adhere to a hybrid precision approach. Physical measurements are presented to 2 decimal places for anatomical accuracy, while mathematical ratios and deviations are calculated to 4 decimal places to document the degree of harmony.
*   **b. Key Mathematical Ratios & Derivatives:** Godric's proportions are built upon the Golden Ratio and other classical constants.
    *   **True Golden Ratio ($\phi$):** $1.6180$
    *   **Reciprocal of $\phi$ ($1/\phi$):** $0.6180$
    *   **Classical Harmony Constant:** $2/3 \approx 0.6667$
    *   **Combat-Optimized Arm Ratio ($2/\phi$):** $1.2361$
    *   **Square Root of $\phi$ ($\sqrt{\phi}$):** $1.2720$
    *   **Square Root of 2 Reciprocal ($1/\sqrt{2}$):** $0.7071$
    *   **Other $\phi$ Derivatives Used:** $1/\phi^2 = 0.3820$, $1/\phi^3 = 0.2361$

#### 2. The Mathematical Framework of Godric's Physique

Godric's body presents a deliberate mathematical pattern, creating a gradient of perfection from his most visible features to his core. His face exhibits the highest degree of mathematical harmony, representing his vigilant guardian persona. His limbs possess near-perfect proportions, while his core contains deliberate deviations that echo his true Titan nature.

### The Horror of the Hyper-Real

The mathematical precision documented above serves a narrative purpose: **Perfection is Alien.** Humans are defined by flaws—asymmetry, scars, slouching. By making Godric mathematically perfect ($\phi$ ratios, 98.7% symmetry), the design makes him visually stunning but instinctually terrifying.

**The "Statue" Effect:** Looking at Godric shouldn't feel like looking at a man; it should feel like looking at Michelangelo's *David* if it suddenly started walking. This triggers a primal "This is not one of us" alarm in the human brain, explaining the [[uncanny-perfection|Proximity Effect]].

### Thematic Significance

The mathematical framework represents:
- **Near-perfect visible features:** His vigilant guardian persona (the mask)
- **Deliberate #TitanEcho deviations:** His authentic, mortal power (the truth beneath)
