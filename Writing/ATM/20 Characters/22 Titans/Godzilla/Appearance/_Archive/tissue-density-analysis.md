## Corpus Enhancement

### Biological Origin: The Fallen Star

The tissue density anomalies documented above are a direct consequence of the *Titanus gojira* species' exposure to the [[The Fallen Star|Fallen Star]]:

> [!quote] Adaptation Through Bombardment
> "Thanks to their exposure to the Fallen Star, which bombarded them constantly with lethal radiation and electromagnetic waves, their *Limitless Adaptation* mutated their body to, first survive, then ***thrive*** in its presence."
> — [[P389 - Gojira Biological Immortality Fallen Star (BSaga-TG)|BSaga-TG P389]]

### Biological Perks from Density

The enhanced tissue density grants:
- **Unparalleled durability** — bodies rapidly adapted to energy bombardment
- **Full-body EM sensors** — scattered throughout the body, causing [[godric_sensory_processing|synesthesia]]
- **Biological immortality** — perpetual self-replenishment via radiation (caloric intake as fallback)

### The Synesthetic Curse

> [!important] Godric's Unique Burden
> For a normal Gojira, these sensors create a "high-fidelity HUD." For Godric, combined with [[godric_hyperthymesia|hyperthymesia]], it means he remembers the *taste* of the air and the *color* of the wind from 250 million years ago.
