# Nordson - Body Hair Profile

- **Coverage Areas**: Breast, sternum, and medial end of clavicle with terminal hair
- **Pattern Type**: Most common masculine pattern (57% of males)
- **Pectoral Regions**: Moderate density across chest muscles
- **Sternal Coverage**: Central coverage connecting the pectoral regions
- **Upper Sternum Node**: Hair above points up, hair below points down
- **Infraclavicular Extension**: Extends to collarbone area for complete masculine coverage
- **Visual Effect**: Creates classic masculine “shield” pattern emphasizing chest architecture

### Abdominal Hair: Acuminate Pattern

- **Pattern Type**: Inverted V-pattern extending upward from pubic area
- **Occurrence**: Found in approximately 55% of males
- **Shape**: Characterized by tapered, inverted 'V' configuration
- **Base Width**: Moderate spread at pubic region
- **Taper Point**: Extends to navel level or slightly above
- **Symmetry**: Perfect bilateral balance reflecting his 98.7% body symmetry
- **Connection**: Links with sternal coverage creating unified topography

- **“Root System”**: Pubic area providing broad foundation base
- **Visual Flow**: Creates natural pathway for eye movement
- **Continuity**: Connected pattern from chest to pubic region

## Regional Distribution

### Arms

- **Forearms**: Moderate to dense coverage from mid-forearm to wrist, tapering toward hands
- **Upper Arms**: Light to moderate coverage, less dense than forearms
- **Directionality**: Hair grows downward on upper arms, shifts toward wrist on forearms
- **Density**: Visually apparent but not overwhelming, classical masculine coverage

### Underarms

- **Coverage**: Standard dense coverage appropriate for enhanced testosterone levels
