## Key Concepts

### The Tree Metaphor
- **Chest hair** = leaves/crown
- **Sternal/abdominal line** = trunk
- **Pubic region** = root system
- **Symbolism**: Grounded, organic strength and natural development

### The Attendance Check Phenomenon
Post-dormancy appearance revealing peak vulnerability and attractiveness, creating recurring narrative crucible for character and relationship development.

### The Misinterpretation Tragedy
Maria's avoidance due to overwhelming attraction → Godric interprets as discomfort → increases grooming frequency → reduces her appreciation opportunities. Perfect mutual misunderstanding.

### The Proximity-Authenticity Motif
- **Distance** = museum-specimen perfection
- **Intimacy** = natural textures and imperfections
- **Trust indicator**: Who witnesses ungroomed states

### Seasonal Cycles
- **Active Season (Dec-Aug)**: Precise grooming, controlled presentation
- **Dormant Season (Sep-Nov)**: Natural growth, authentic self-revelation
