# Head Hair Profile

## Basic Properties

### Color & Texture

- **Color**: Pure black (correlating to his dorsal fins, inherited from his mother)
- **Texture**: Soft to the touch yet resistant to cutting with conventional tools
- **Wave Pattern**: Ever so slightly wavy, developing natural body and movement
- **Response to Activity**: Develops loose and tousled appearance during physical work

### Current Length & Style

- **Length**: 2.5-3 inches on top
- **Fringe Length**: When pulled down, reaches slightly more than half his forehead
- **Default Styling**: Swept backwards, parted naturally (slightly off-center)
- **Cut Structure**:
    - Gradually shorter toward back and sides
    - Tapered from 1.5 inches at temples to 0.5 inches above ears
    - Graduated from crown length to 1 inch at nape
    - Strategic point-cutting with shears to enhance natural wave

### Movement Characteristics

- **Controlled State**: Precisely swept back with disciplined lines
- **Activity Response**: Individual strands fall across forehead during physical exertion
- **Strand Behavior**: Falls in graceful tendrils that dance and dangle rather than move stiffly
- **Wind Response**: Natural wave enhances movement, creating dynamic visual appeal

## Seasonal Variations

### Active Season (December-August)

- **Appearance**: More precisely styled, sleeker presentation
- **Maintenance**: Regular cutting every 4-6 weeks
- **Texture**: Crisper definition, more angular sweep
- **Light Interaction**: Individual strands catch light like dark silk

### Dormant Season (September-November)

- **Appearance**: More tousled quality from reduced grooming attention
- **Growth Pattern**: Develops fuller body and enhanced wave at the ends
- **Texture**: Becomes more tactile-looking and naturally windswept
- **Maintenance**: Delayed cuts allow slight length increase

## Cutting Resistance

- **Tool Limitations**: Resistant to conventional cutting tools
- **Electronic Interference**: Electric tools malfunction in his proximity
- **Required Tools**: Must be cut with scissors, shears, or straight razors
- **Blade Durability**: Specialized tools required due to hair's enhanced structure

## Determination Metric

- **Cutting Trigger**: Determines need for haircut when strands falling on forehead reach his eyes
- **Personal Standard**: Maintains length at few centimeters longer than buzz cut
- **Visual Preference**: Enough length to style but short enough for practical functionality
