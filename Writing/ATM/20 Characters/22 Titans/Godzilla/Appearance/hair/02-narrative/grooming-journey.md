# Hair Grooming Journey

## Initial Long Hair Phase

### Discovery State

- **Length**: Flowing mane reaching to his neck
- **Duration**: Maintained throughout time in Japan without cutting
- **Appearance**: Absolutely fabulous despite being unmanaged
- **Challenge**: Difficult to maintain without proper tools and accommodations
- **Decision Point**: Cut short when Monarch developed necessary tools

## Professional Barber Phase

### Monarch's Security Protocol

- **Hiring Process**: Strict internal selection from Monarch personnel only
- **Security Motivation**: Maintaining secrecy of Antitheriomorphosized Titans
- **Personnel Source**: Current Castle Bravo staff or seconded personnel from other outposts
- **Risk Management**: Avoided external contractors requiring NDAs and surveillance
- **Tool Limitations**: Scissors-only configuration due to electronic interference

### Barber Selection Challenges

- **Equipment Stress**: Barbers required specialized scissors due to hair resistance
- **Predator Presence**: Godric's gaze triggered prey instincts in barbers during sessions
- **Psychological Pressure**: Intense scrutiny created deep nervousness in service providers
- **Mark's Concerns**: Worried scissors-only requirement would deter qualified volunteers
- **Technical Reality**: Shielded electronics still unreliable in Godric's proximity

## Learning Phase (Five Appointments)

### Observation Methodology

- **Hyperthymesia Application**: Perfect memory recording every movement and technique
- **Intense Scrutiny**: Gaze tracking barber's every action with predatory focus
- **Strategic Patience**: Opted to "gather intel" despite theoretical proficiency after first session
- **Information Absorption**: Complete technical understanding achieved through observation
- **Barber Impact**: Subjects deeply unnerved by his unwavering attention

### Skill Acquisition Timeline

- **Session 1**: Absorbed enough information for theoretical execution
- **Sessions 2-4**: Continued observation for comprehensive technique mastery
- **Session 5**: Achieved complete proficiency in all cutting methods
- **Final Assessment**: Could replicate existing style, refine it, or adopt new cuts at will
- **Barber Recognition**: Gave single praise to barber for providing learning opportunity

## Self-Sufficiency Achievement

### Technical Mastery

- **Complete Proficiency**: Perfect replication of any observed technique
- **Style Versatility**: Ability to maintain current cut or experiment with new styles
- **Precision Level**: Superhuman hand-eye coordination enabling flawless execution
- **Tool Requirements**: Operates exclusively with scissors, shears, and straight razors
- **Madison's Observation**: Could excel as Castle Bravo's resident barber if desired

### Personal Philosophy

- **Alpha Imperative**: Compulsion to master every domain of existence
- **Self-Sufficiency Drive**: Eliminating external dependencies in personal care
- **Territorial Control**: Managing personal grooming as extension of territorial management
- **Efficiency Achievement**: Streamlined process removing scheduling and personnel needs

## Body Hair Maintenance Nightmare

### The Practical Horror

- **Blade Destruction**: Requires 2+ specialized blade replacements per full-body session
- **Time Investment**: Full body grooming becomes 2-3 hour ordeal
- **Tool Limitations**: Conventional razors completely ineffective
- **Electronic Failure**: Electric tools malfunction despite shielding attempts
- **Cost Factor**: Specialized blades expensive with frequent replacement needs

### Psychological Impact

- **Efficiency Violation**: Process contradicts his optimization principles
- **Territorial Disorder**: Body hair represents "untidiness" in personal domain
- **Practical Futility**: Enhanced regrowth rate makes effort feel pointless
- **Resource Waste**: Significant time and material investment for temporary results

### Strategic Surrender

- **Practical Acceptance**: Eventually embraces natural state as more efficient
- **Seasonal Compromise**: Only attempts removal during active periods when growth slower
- **Facial Hair Focus**: Maintains daily facial shaving as manageable routine
- **Body Hair Tolerance**: Accepts natural coverage as biological reality

## Current Grooming Protocol

### Daily Facial Maintenance

- **Tool Selection**: Straight razor or high-quality safety razor
- **Technique**: Single-pass precision utilizing superhuman coordination
- **Duration**: 8-12 minutes for complete removal
- **Precision Level**: Achieves surgical smoothness without irritation

### Post-Dormancy Routine

- **Preparation**: Hot water application to soften extended growth
- **Multi-Pass Necessity**: Requires 2-3 carefully executed passes
- **Tool Stress**: Often damages conventional razors during process
- **Duration**: 20-25 minutes for complete beard removal
- **Transformation**: Visible change from rugged to refined appearance

### Hair Cutting Schedule

- **Frequency**: Every 4-6 weeks during active season
- **Seasonal Adjustment**: Delayed cuts during dormancy allowing natural growth
- **Self-Execution**: Complete independence from external assistance
- **Style Consistency**: Maintains preferred length and structure through precise technique
