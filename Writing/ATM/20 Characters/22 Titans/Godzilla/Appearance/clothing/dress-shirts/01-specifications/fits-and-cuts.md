# Dress Shirts: Fits and Cuts

## Overall Aesthetic Specifications

| Element | Specification |
|---------|---------------|
| **Fit** | Precisely tailored to cling to physique while accommodating combat mobility |
| **Opacity** | Completely opaque, creating strategic mystery about form beneath |
| **Length** | Extended cut for 195.97cm height ensuring permanent tucking |
| **Lines** | Clean, unbroken visual sweep from shoulder to waist emphasizing mathematical proportions |

---

## Collar Configuration

**Type**: Spread collar creating wide frame around neck and upper chest

**Presentation**: Top button permanently undone, creating triangular window of visibility

**Effects**:
- Emphasizes jawline and shoulder width
- Provides calculated glimpse of throat and upper chest
- Creates only visible indication of physique beyond hands and face

**Strategic Value**: The triangular window represents controlled revelation—the precise amount of exposure Godric permits observers. Maximum concealment with strategic glimpse.

---

## Cuff Design

### Standard Variants
- **Type**: Barrel cuffs for practical daily wear
- **Philosophy**: Never rolled regardless of activity—maintains coverage as species dominance signal

### Formal Variants
- **Type**: French cuffs requiring cufflink integration
- **Engineering**: Maria's design ensures wrist flexibility during combat despite doubled fabric

### Coverage Philosophy
Sleeves are never rolled because exposed forearms would reveal the perfection of his form more directly than strategic concealment allows. Maximum coverage signals Alpha Paramount status while the collar window provides controlled revelation.

---

## Pocket Philosophy

**Configuration**: Completely pocketless design

**Rationale**:
- Maintains unbroken visual lines
- Prevents bulk disruption of silhouette
- Creates uninterrupted sweep emphasizing V-shaped torso taper

**Alignment**: Consistent with minimal pocket preference in tactical pants (three-pocket maximum)

---

## Structure/Drape Ratios by Variant

| Variant | Ratio | Purpose |
|---------|-------|---------|
| **Standard** | 45/55 | Form emphasis while enabling function |
| **Formal** | 40/60 | Sophisticated hang and movement |
| **Seasonal** | Adaptive | Accommodates dormant physique changes |

The drape ratio determines how fabric hangs versus how it maintains structure. Higher drape numbers create more elegant movement; higher structure numbers create sharper silhouettes.

---

## Seasonal Accommodation

**Active Season (December-August)**:
- Physique: Leaner, more angular frame (120kg, 10-12% body fat)
- Fit Preference: Standard variants providing optimal presentation
- Rotation: Regular weekly patterns of black/white selection

**Dormant Season (September-November)**:
- Physique: Bulkier, softer musculature (129kg, 15-18% body fat)
- Fit Options: Choice between tighter regular variants or dedicated seasonal shirts
- Selection Process: Pragmatic proximity-based choice rather than deliberate aesthetic decision

**Transition Periods**: No ceremonial consideration—purely practical selection of first available shirt regardless of optimal fit.
