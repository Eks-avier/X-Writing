# Dress Shirts: Color Palette

## Distribution Overview

| Color | Percentage | Reasoning |
|-------|------------|-----------|
| **Black** | 40% | Represents dark scale coloration as Titan |
| **White** | 40% | Clean, formal color providing sophisticated appearance |
| **Charcoal Gray** | 10% | Professional neutrality |
| **Navy Blue** | 9% | Formal alternative maintaining conservative approach |
| **Forest Green** | 1% | Single shirt representing extreme color tolerance |

---

## Primary Palette (80% Black and White)

### Black (40%)
- **Titan Connection**: Direct reference to his dark scale coloration
- **Authority**: Creates commanding, absorbing presence
- **Coordination**: Perfect harmony with 100% black pants collection
- **Symbolism**: The obsidian king in human form

### White (40%)
- **Formal Standard**: Clean, sophisticated appearance
- **Contrast**: Provides visual variety while maintaining conservatism
- **Reliability**: Appropriate for any formal or semi-formal occasion
- **Symbolism**: The brightness within darkness—nuclear fire contained

---

## Secondary Options (20%)

### Charcoal Gray (10%)
- **Purpose**: Professional neutrality
- **Usage**: When black feels too severe, white too bright
- **Effect**: Softer authority projection

### Navy Blue (9%)
- **Purpose**: Formal alternative
- **Connection**: Echoes atomic blue of his power signature
- **Usage**: Variation without departure from conservative approach

### Forest Green (1%)
**The Green Shirt Significance**:
- **Quantity**: One shirt only
- **Origin**: Part of three-shirt set gifted by Dr. Stanton
- **Status**: Remade with Maria's silk but rarely worn
- **Symbolism**: Represents his absolute limit for color experimentation
- **Meaning**: The single concession to human gift-giving customs

The green shirt exists as proof that Godric *can* accept aesthetic suggestions—he simply chooses not to. Its rarity makes it notable when worn.

---

## Weekly Selection Patterns

**Pre-Planning Ritual**: Every Friday, Godric selects the coming week's shirts (typically one black, one white rotation)

**Daily Selection**: Proximity-based choice rather than deliberate aesthetic consideration

**Seasonal Adjustment**: None—color selection remains constant regardless of active/dormant physique changes

---

## Coordination with Overall Wardrobe

| Garment | Color Coordination |
|---------|-------------------|
| **Pants** | 100% black—always harmonizes |
| **Belts** | Black leather—seamless integration |
| **Footwear** | Black boots/shoes—unbroken line |
| **Suits** | Black, charcoal, navy available for formal occasions |

The result: Near-monochromatic presentation creating unified silhouette from collar to footwear.
