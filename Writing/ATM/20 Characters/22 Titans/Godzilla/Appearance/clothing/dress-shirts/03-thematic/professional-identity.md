# Dress Shirts: Professional Identity and Thematic Significance

## Territorial Management Extension

Each dress shirt represents controlled territory—perfectly maintained, strategically presented, and serving multiple functional purposes while projecting unwavering authority.

**Domain Equivalence**:
- Dress shirts receive same precision as Castle Bravo maintenance
- Personal presentation managed like territorial boundaries
- Every garment choice reflects broader management capabilities

---

## Clothing as Armor: The Gojira Protocol of Coverage

> **Corpus Source:** Saga-TG-P275 (DEFINITIVE)

For Godric, the refusal to show skin stems from Northern Gojira martial culture:

**The Concept**: To a *Titanus gojira*, skin is a shield—primary defense against a hostile world. In Titan form, they're encased in impenetrable hide and scales.

**The Human Vulnerability**: Transformed into human, that natural armor becomes soft, vulnerable skin. Subconsciously, this feels wrong, unsafe—like walking into battle naked.

**Clothing as New Scales**: Dress shirts function as replacement for lost durability:
- High-collar configuration = regalia and armor
- Maximum coverage = Alpha Paramount status display
- Strategic concealment = species dominance signal

**The Dagon Pendant Factor**: The specific refusal to go shirtless intensifies around the pendant—that fragment of Dagon represents his "heart." A Northern Gojira male protects the heart of his territory. Exposing the pendant means exposing grief and love, which are private.

## Control and Revelation

### The Strategic Exposure System

**Triangular Collar Window**: Only visible indication of physique beyond hands and face—precisely calibrated exposure.

**Opaque Fabric**: Forces observers to imagine rather than observe—psychological tension through concealment.

**Never-Rolled Sleeves**: Maximum arm coverage as species dominance signal.

**Overall Effect**: Perfect metaphor for his broader approach to relationships and authority—precisely controlling how much of himself others are permitted to observe.
