# Dress Shirts: Variants by Scenario

## Collection Overview

**Total Shirts**: 20 (approximate)

| Category | Count | Percentage |
|----------|-------|------------|
| **Standard** | 10 | 50% |
| **Formal** | 6 | 30% |
| **Seasonal** | 4 | 20% |

---

## Standard Variants (50% of Collection)

**Primary Configuration**:
- **Structure/Drape Ratio**: 45/55 emphasizing form while enabling function
- **Usage**: Daily wear, general activities, standard combat readiness
- **Colors**: 5 black, 4 white, 1 gray/navy/green distribution
- **Features**: Barrel cuffs, spread collar, scale buttons
- **Combat Enhancement**: Invisible stress-point reinforcement

**Selection Logic**: First available based on proximity rather than deliberate choice

---

## Formal Variants (30% of Collection)

**Enhanced Configuration**:
- **Structure/Drape Ratio**: 40/60 creating sophisticated hang and movement
- **Fabric Treatment**: Micro-jacquard weaving creating subtle luster
- **Hardware**: French cuffs requiring cufflink integration
- **Usage**: Diplomatic events, formal occasions, ceremonial functions

**Visual Effects**:
- Light-catching facets creating liquid-like depth under artificial lighting
- Enhanced drape creating elegant cascade effects during purposeful motion

---

## Seasonal Adjustments (20% of Collection)

**Anticipatory Design**:
- **Creation Context**: Maria's preemptive measurement during Godric's first dormant transition
- **Fit Adaptation**: Accommodates bulkier dormant period physique (129kg vs 120kg)
- **Colors**: 2 black, 2 white maintaining core palette

**Usage Reality**:
- Available but not prioritized
- Selection based on proximity rather than deliberate seasonal choice
- Standard variants' flexibility often makes these unnecessary
- Others notice and prefer specific configurations though Godric remains indifferent

---

## Scenario-Variant Matrix

| Scenario | Primary Variant | Rationale |
|----------|-----------------|-----------|
| **Daily Monarch work** | Standard | Combat readiness, practical |
| **Human diplomatic events** | Formal | Enhanced presentation |
| **Titan Council sessions** | Formal with Regalia | Authority display |
| **Private quarters** | None - shirt removed | Reduction philosophy |
| **Emergency response** | Whatever's nearest | Function over form |
| **Dormant season** | Standard or Seasonal | Proximity-based |

---

## Combat Readiness Across All Variants

All dress shirts maintain combat capability regardless of variant type:

**Standard Variant Combat Features**:
- Invisible stress-point reinforcement
- Full range of motion preserved
- Blast-resistant silk construction
- Scale buttons as energy indicators

**Formal Variant Combat Adaptation**:
- French cuff flexibility maintained
- Enhanced drape doesn't impede movement
- Same protective properties as standard

**Seamless Transition**: Godric can move from diplomatic conversation to physical confrontation without changing clothes—a point of incredulity among Monarch personnel.
