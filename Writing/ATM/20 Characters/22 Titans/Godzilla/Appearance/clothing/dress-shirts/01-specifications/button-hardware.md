# Dress Shirts: Button and Hardware Specifications

## Button Evolution

### Phase I: Solidified Silk Construction (Mid-2020)

Maria's original button design:
- **Material**: Solidified silk forming smooth, lustrous discs
- **Appearance**: Pearl-like quality with subtle light-catching properties
- **Strength**: Substantial enough for his strength
- **Aesthetic**: Refined, minimal, undecorated

### Phase II: Scale Integration (Late 2020-Present)

Current button construction incorporating Godric's shed scales:
- **Material**: Titan scales shaped and polished
- **Appearance**: Natural organic patterns unique to each button
- **Texture**: Slightly smooth yet undeniably scaly
- **Identity**: Literal integration of Titan nature into human presentation

---

## Atomic Energy Response

**Visual Indicator**: Buttons exhibit subtle pulsing illumination during power use

| Power Level | Button Response |
|-------------|-----------------|
| **Dormant** | No visible glow |
| **Mild activation** | Faint blue pulse |
| **Moderate use** | Rhythmic pulsing |
| **High output** | Intense blue glow |
| **Combat intensity** | Continuous luminescence |

**Symbolic Function**: Warning lights suggesting contained power—observers receive visual confirmation of energy state without direct exposure.

**Practical Note**: High-energy output can compromise silk integrity, making scale buttons both aesthetic and functional as energy indicators.

## Material Efficiency Philosophy

**Conservation Principle**: Buttons represent minimal scale usage, preserving material for higher-priority applications:

1. **Titan Regalia** - Maximum scale concentration
2. **Ceremonial accessories** - Moderate usage
3. **Daily wear buttons** - Minimal usage
4. **Future integration projects** - Reserved material

This hierarchy ensures the most symbolically significant garments receive priority while daily wear benefits from scale presence without depleting reserves.
