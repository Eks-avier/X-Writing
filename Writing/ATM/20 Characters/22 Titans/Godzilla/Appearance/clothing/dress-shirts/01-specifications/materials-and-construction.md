# Dress Shirts: Materials and Construction

## Material Evolution Phases

### Phase I: Conventional Foundation (Early 2020)

High-end dress shirts purchased by Monarch upper management to introduce Western-style formal wear. These served as Godric's template for preferred style and established his immediate gravitation toward this clothing type.

### Phase II: Maria's Silk Revolution (Mid-2020)

Complete recreation by [[Mothra, the Queen of the Monsters|Maria]] using her divine moth silk, achieved through close personal measurement and observation of his movement patterns. This transformation elevated ordinary clothing into genuine protective gear.

### Phase III: Scale Integration (Late 2020)

Incorporation of Godric's shed scales into button construction, creating literal integration of his Titan identity into his human presentation.

## The Biological Imperative

> **Corpus Source:** Saga-TG-P277 (DEFINITIVE)

The silk clothing represents far more than fabric—it embodies the Mosura biological imperative:

**The Perpetual Cocoon**: For a *Titanus mosura*, silk is the cradle of existence. From larva (surrounded by silk) to metamorphosis (encased in pupa) to imago (continually producing it), a mosura is never "unwrapped."

**The Psychological Translation**: In human form, this translates into intense aversion to nakedness. To be without clothing is to be without the "nest"—triggering instinctual vulnerability.

**The Romantic Agony**: During the Professional Phase (2021-2025), Maria experienced torment seeing Godric wrapped entirely in her essence every day. Her silk on him represented a constant "embrace" she couldn't acknowledge. Post-marriage, this transforms from agony into "Symphonia of the Skin":
- **Maria's Attire**: Scale-silk composite—shielded by Godric's discarded protection
- **Godric's Attire**: Wrapped in Maria's cradle
- **The Symbolism**: Ultimate biological union—never truly apart

## Construction Philosophy

**Maria's Design Specifications:**
- **Fit Precision**: Tailored to cling to his physique while accommodating combat mobility
- **Opacity Standard**: Completely opaque, creating strategic mystery
- **Length Engineering**: Extended cut for his 195.97cm height ensuring permanent tucking
- **Line Integrity**: Clean, unbroken visual sweep from shoulder to waist

**Material Transcendence**: Unlike conventional tailoring where complex engineering compensates for material limitations, Maria's method allows material perfection to enable focus on pure aesthetics and comfort.
