---

## Phase I: Conventional Foundation (Early 2020)

**Context**: Post-Antitheriomorphosis, Godric requires human clothing for public integration.

**Acquisition**: Monarch upper management purchases high-end dress shirts to introduce Western-style formal wear.

**Key Outcomes**:
- Established immediate gravitation toward dress shirt style
- Created template for preferred formality level
- Revealed his preference for maximum coverage
- Demonstrated natural adaptation to formal presentation

**Character Insight**: Even with arbitrary initial options, Godric's instincts drew him toward authoritative, covering garments—the Gojira "armor" instinct translating immediately.

---

## Phase II: Maria's Silk Revolution (Mid-2020)

**Catalyst**: Conventional materials proved inadequate for Titan physiology and lifestyle.

**Process**: Maria undertook comprehensive personal measurement and observation of his movement patterns—sessions of mutual internal agony where both maintained professional composure while experiencing deeper emotional responses.

**Transformation Scope**:
- Complete recreation of entire wardrobe in divine moth silk
- Elevation from ordinary clothing to genuine protective gear
- Introduction of self-repairing, blast-resistant properties
- Establishment of unconscious intimate bond through material exchange

**The Romantic Subtext** (Corpus: Saga-TG-P277):
> During the Professional Phase, Maria saw Godric wrapped entirely in her essence every day. Because he refused to wear anything but her silk, he was effectively walking around in a permanent "embrace" from her.

This phase marks the beginning of "wearing each other"—a biological intimacy neither consciously acknowledged.

---

## Phase III: Scale Integration (Late 2020)

**Innovation**: Incorporation of Godric's shed scales into button construction.

**Significance**:
- Literal integration of Titan identity into human presentation
- Partnership manifest—his protection within her creation
- Scale buttons as energy indicators
- First step toward full scale-silk fusion (later achieved in Titan Regalia)

**Material Balance**: Buttons use minimal scale quantity, preserving material for higher-priority applications while maintaining Titan identity presence.

---

## Phase IV: Stable System (Present)

**Current State**: Fully operational wardrobe system requiring only maintenance.

**Characteristics**:
- 20 shirts across three variant categories
- Established weekly selection patterns
- Repair protocols through Maria
- Gradual scale integration into accessories

**Future Trajectory**: System stable but open to evolution through:
- Madison's humanization influence (potential color expansion)
- Relationship deepening (post-confession changes)
- Narrative demands (situation-specific adaptations)

## Relationship Dynamics Through Evolution

### The Measurement Sessions

**Surface Level**: Professional tailoring appointments with small talk and laughter

**Reality**: Mutual torment beneath composed exteriors
- Maria: Close physical proximity to the man she denied loving
- Godric: Intimate attention from the woman he denied desiring
- Both: Perfect results despite emotional complexity

**Lasting Impact**: Sessions established unconscious intimacy patterns that later evolved into explicit relationship.

### The Material Exchange Symbolism

**Pre-Marriage**: Unacknowledged wearing of each other's essence
- His scales in her clothing (strength protecting beauty)
- Her silk in his clothing (grace enabling power)

**Post-Marriage** (Corpus: Saga-TG-P277):
> They have achieved the ultimate biological union. He provides the "armor" for her grace, and she provides the "vessel" for his power. They are never truly apart because they are always wearing a part of the other.
