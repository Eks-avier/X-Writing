# Dress Shirts: Maintenance Protocols

## Weekly Routine

### Laundry Schedule

**Frequency**: Every Friday regardless of visible need

**Process**: Effortless due to silk's stain-resistant properties—conventional dirt cannot adhere to Maria's divine silk.

**Ironing**:
- Performed every Friday after laundry
- Personal handling maintaining perfect presentation standards
- Military-level precision in execution

### Planning Ritual

**When**: Friday evening post-laundry

**Action**: Pre-week outfit selection (typically one black, one white shirt rotation)

**Philosophy**: Decision elimination through advance planning reduces daily cognitive load

---

## Damage and Repair

### Frequency
Extremely rare due to material durability—divine silk survives conditions that would destroy conventional fabric.

### Process
1. Return damaged item to Maria for inspection
2. 10-minute session (most time spent on examination rather than actual repair)
3. Return of restored garment

### Relationship Significance
Repair sessions serve as legitimate excuse for close physical proximity between Maria and Godric—structured opportunities for intimate interaction within appropriate professional boundaries.

**The Unspoken Dynamic**: Neither acknowledges that damage inspection requires far less time than sessions typically last. The "repair" ritual became an unconscious relationship maintenance practice long before explicit acknowledgment.

---

## Self-Maintenance Philosophy

### Approach
Complete self-sufficiency in routine care—Godric handles all standard maintenance personally.

### Standards
- Military-level precision in presentation
- No acceptable deviation from perfect condition
- Immediate attention to any imperfection

### Territory Management Extension
Clothing care represents extension of personal domain control:
- Same meticulous attention as Castle Bravo maintenance
- Same precision as Monster Island Palace management
- Body as managed territory requiring proper upkeep

---

## Storage Organization

**Location**: Private quarters within Monster Island Palace

**System**:
- Clothing organized by function and frequency
- Black and white variants grouped separately
- Formal variants stored with enhanced protection
- Seasonal variants accessible but not prioritized

**Rapid Deployment**: Emergency response capability maintained—full dress in under 90 seconds from any starting state.

---

## Quality Control Indicators

### Visual Inspection Points

| Element | Standard |
|---------|----------|
| **Collar** | Crisp, no softening |
| **Cuffs** | Sharp edges, no fraying |
| **Buttons** | Secure, no loosening |
| **Seams** | Invisible stress points |
| **Overall** | Perfect drape, no wrinkles |

### Replacement Triggers
- Rare: Material integrity compromise
- Never: Simple wear from use (silk doesn't degrade normally)
- Occasional: Scale button replacement with fresh material
