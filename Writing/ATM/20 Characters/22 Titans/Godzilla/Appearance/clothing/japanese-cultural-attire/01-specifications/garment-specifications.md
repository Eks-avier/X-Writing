# Japanese Cultural Attire: Garment Specifications

## The Three-Component Harmony

Japanese ceremonial attire follows traditional structure adapted for Godric's unique physiology.

---

## Haori: The Eastern Shadow of Authority

| Specification | Detail |
|---------------|--------|
| **Length** | Mid-calf rather than traditional mid-thigh |
| **Material** | Exclusively Maria's silk (no scale integration) |
| **Structure** | Broader shoulders, extended length for unique physique |
| **Color** | Deep obsidian black with subtle fabric weight |

**Design Rationale**:
- Extended length echoes frock coat authority from Western regalia
- Creates commanding silhouette while respecting traditional aesthetics
- Visual authority through adaptation rather than deviation

---

## Kimono: Core Expression of Identity

| Specification | Detail |
|---------------|--------|
| **Pattern** | Dorsal fin translation—subtle interlocking along lower hem and sleeve edges |
| **Proportions** | Modified for broader shoulders and height requirements |
| **Color** | Dark charcoal gray providing contrast with haori |
| **Integration** | Connects visually to waistcoat design while maintaining cultural authenticity |

**Design Rationale**:
- Dorsal fin pattern maintains Titan identity within traditional form
- Proportional adaptation accommodates his unique frame
- Cultural respect through structure preservation despite modifications

---

## Hakama: Functional Foundation

| Specification | Detail |
|---------------|--------|
| **Type** | Umanori (divided style) |
| **Structure** | Seven traditional pleats proportionally adjusted |
| **Material** | Weight and color matched to haori |
| **Function** | Movement range and combat readiness prioritized |

**Design Rationale**:
- Umanori selection prioritizes movement over formal appearance
- Traditional pleat structure maintained despite size adjustment
- Prevents material pooling while maintaining proper traditional appearance

---

## The Material Challenge

### Pure Silk Limitation

These are the **first garments without scale integration**:

| Implication | Effect |
|-------------|--------|
| **Combat Capability** | Functional but limited compared to scale-silk fusion |
| **Energy Restriction** | Cannot use full atomic output without material compromise |
| **Trust Demonstration** | Complete reliance on Maria's craftsmanship |
| **Future Priority** | Immediate designation for next batch of shed scales |

### Symbolic Significance

**Dependency Acceptance**: Rare acknowledgment of requiring external support

**Relationship Trust**: Faith in Maria's protection without his material contribution

**Value Recognition**: Despite initial reluctance, priority designation for scale integration

**Partnership Evolution**: Creates another unspoken bond through material vulnerability

| Aspect | Expression |
