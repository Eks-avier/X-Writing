# Titan Regalia: Combat-Ceremonial Specifications

## Design Philosophy

Godric's Titan Regalia transcends the typical ceremonial/functional divide—simultaneously serving as formal representation of Alpha Paramount status and maximum combat capability.

### The Subtle Integration Principle

| Aspect | Expression |
|--------|------------|
| **Scale Pattern** | Microscopic reproduction of Titan scale texture |
| **Dorsal Fin Cartography** | Waistcoat pattern suggesting Pangea continental arrangement |
| **Understated Power** | Appears exceptional from distance, otherworldly upon inspection |
| **Recognition Reward** | Observers earn discovery through attention |

### Visual Progression

| Distance | Perception |
|----------|------------|
| **Far** | High-quality formal attire |
| **Focused** | Unusual textures and patterns become apparent |
| **Close** | Unmistakable Titan references revealed |
| **Complete** | "This is Godzilla" achieved through accumulated evidence |

## Component Specifications

### Elevated Collar Design

- **Dimensional Shift**: 1.5cm taller than standard dress shirt collars
- **Authority Framing**: Immediate visual distinction and presence enhancement
- **Protective Function**: Additional throat protection during combat
- **Energy Integration**: Highest concentration of dorsal fin fragments
- **Symbolic Weight**: References protective neck ridge of Titan form

### Frock Coat Architecture

- **Length Significance**: Below-knee extension creates expanded territorial presence
- **Movement Language**: Distinctive weight and flow unlike conventional fabric
- **Combat Preparation**: Vents enable seamless leg movement during conflict
- **Material Distribution**: Strategic scale placement for protection and mobility
- **Visual Authority**: Imposing silhouette suggesting greater mass

### Waistcoat Pattern

- **Design Element**: Dorsal fin pattern suggesting ancient continental arrangement
- **Construction**: Scale-silk composite at maximum concentration
- **Visual Effect**: Pattern visible only upon close inspection
- **Symbolic Meaning**: Contains his territory mapped onto his body

### Vambrace Innovation

**Organic Attachment System**: Energy signature recognition mechanism

| Process | Function |
|---------|----------|
| **Recognition** | Responds only to Godric's unique atomic energy pattern |
| **Attachment** | Simple sliding motion over forearm triggers recognition |
| **Combat Integration** | Elevated energy during conflict strengthens connection |
| **Automatic Security** | Intensifies under stress, loosens during calm |
| **Natural Release** | Post-combat energy reduction enables easy removal |

**Technical Integration**:
- Scale Memory: Contains microscopic dorsal fin fragments for energy routing
- Silk Matrix: Maria's silk distributes connection across entire surface
- Energy Channeling: Enhances precision of directed atomic attacks
- Visual Feedback: Subtle blue glow indicates successful attachment
- Combat Enhancement: Wrist reinforcement and impact distribution
