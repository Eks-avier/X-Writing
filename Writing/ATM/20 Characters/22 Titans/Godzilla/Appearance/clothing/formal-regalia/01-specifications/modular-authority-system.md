# Titan Regalia: Modular Authority System

## Occasion Hierarchy

The regalia operates on precise calibration of presentation to situation significance.

---

## Level Specifications

### Level 1: Standard Human Interaction

**Configuration**: No Regalia—regular formal attire only

**Purpose**: Maximum concealment of Titan nature

**Usage**: Human-facing diplomatic events where Titan identity should remain subtle

**Appearance**: Distinguished businessman, nothing more

---

### Level 2: Minor Titan Business

**Configuration**: Waistcoat only

**Purpose**: Subtle acknowledgment of Titan matters

**Usage**: Routine status meetings with Wardens

**Visual Effect**: Single regalia element signals Titan context without full authority display

---

### Level 3: Formal Titan Gatherings

**Configuration**: Waistcoat + Coat

**Purpose**: Clear authority signal without combat readiness

**Usage**:
- Seasonal territorial reviews
- Diplomatic negotiations
- Standard Council sessions

**Visual Effect**: Commanding presence, clearly Alpha Paramount, but not preparing for conflict

---

### Level 4: Critical Council Sessions

**Configuration**: Near-Complete Regalia (all elements except vambraces)

**Purpose**: Full ceremonial authority with combat preparation

**Usage**:
- Dispute resolution
- Major territorial decisions
- Situations with potential for escalation

**Visual Effect**: Maximum authority display, ready to transition to combat if needed

---

### Level 5: Combat Readiness

**Configuration**: Complete Regalia including vambraces

**Purpose**: Maximum authority and combat capability

**Usage**:
- Titan challenges
- External threat response
- Situations where combat is expected or imminent

**Visual Effect**: Full Alpha Paramount war regalia—ceremony and combat unified

---

## Selection Matrix

| Situation | Level | Configuration |
|-----------|-------|---------------|
| Human press conference | 1 | No regalia |
| Warden status report | 2 | Waistcoat |
| Quarterly territorial review | 3 | Waistcoat + Coat |
| Standing dispute arbitration | 4 | Near-complete |
| Response to territorial challenge | 5 | Complete |
| External kaiju threat | 5 | Complete |

---

## Transition Protocols

### Escalation

If situation escalates during event:
- Level 1→2: Add waistcoat (prepared in advance)
- Level 2→3: Add coat (available nearby)
- Level 3→4: Already wearing; internal shift
- Level 4→5: Attach vambraces (carried or summoned)

### De-escalation

If tension decreases:
- Remove vambraces first (energy recognition releases easily at calm)
- Coat removal signals deliberate de-escalation
- Waistcoat removal signals return to human-facing mode

---

## Symbolic Significance

The modular system demonstrates that Godric's authority exists independently of display level:

**At Level 1**: He is still Alpha Paramount, merely concealed
**At Level 5**: He is not *more* Alpha Paramount, merely revealed

The regalia calibrates *visibility* of authority, not *existence* of authority.
