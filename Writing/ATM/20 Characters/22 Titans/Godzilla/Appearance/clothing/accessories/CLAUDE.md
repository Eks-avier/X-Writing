## Key Concepts

### The Minimal Philosophy

**Godric's Principle:** Accessories are decorative, not functional

**His Exceptions:**
- Cufflinks integrate with clothing practically (French cuffs require them)
- Cufflinks serve power indication function (energy glow)
- Ties only when protocol demands (hates them)

### The Scale-Forged Cufflinks

| Set | Construction | Usage |
|-----|--------------|-------|
| **Formal Wear Set** | Matte obsidian black scales with dorsal fin fragments | Standard formal events, dress shirts |
| **Titan Regalia Set** | Enhanced design with intricate patterns | Titan Council sessions, high-stakes events |

Both emit subtle pulsing blue glow when channeling atomic power.

### The Golden Tie

Maria's handwoven gift matching his golden eye color. Unworn symbol of unspoken intimacy - he cannot wear it publicly without revealing his golden eyes (the ultimate vulnerability/trust marker).
