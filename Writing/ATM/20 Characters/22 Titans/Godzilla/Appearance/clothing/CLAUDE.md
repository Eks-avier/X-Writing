## Key Concepts

### The Protocol of Coverage

> **Source:** Saga-TG-P275

For a *Titanus gojira*, skin is a shield. In human form, that natural armor becomes soft, vulnerable skin—feeling wrong, unsafe. Clothing functions as replacement scales:
- Maximum coverage signals Alpha Paramount status
- Strategic concealment (collar window) provides controlled revelation
- Never-rolled sleeves = species dominance signal

### The Biological Imperative

> **Source:** Saga-TG-P277

Maria's silk carries the Mosura cocoon instinct—the perpetual need to be wrapped. Godric walking in her silk represents her constant "embrace." Post-marriage revelation:
> "They are never truly apart because they are always wearing a part of the other."

### The Material Transcendence Principle

Maria's divine silk enables material perfection over design complexity. The silk provides solutions rather than requiring them—bulletproof, blast-resistant, self-repairing, supremely flexible.
