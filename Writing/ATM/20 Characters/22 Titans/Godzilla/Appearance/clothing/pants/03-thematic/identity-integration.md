## Pre-Marriage Understanding

During the Professional Phase (2021-2025), neither consciously acknowledged what the clothing represented:

**What Godric Wore**: Maria's silk wrapping his form every day

**What Maria Saw**: Her essence embracing the man she denied loving

**The Torment**: Both experienced agony while maintaining professional composure

**The Irony**: Perfect romantic bonding neither recognized

## Post-Marriage Revelation

> **Corpus Source:** Saga-TG-P277 (DEFINITIVE)

The ultimate biological union achieved:

**His Contribution**: Provides the "armor" for her grace through scale integration
