## The Measurement Sessions: Relationship Catalyst

### Surface Level
Professional tailoring appointments with small talk and appropriate distance

### Reality (Corpus: Saga-TG-P277)

**Mutual Internal Agony**: Both experienced torment while maintaining professional composure

**Maria's Experience**: Close physical proximity to the man she denied loving

**Godric's Experience**: Intimate attention from the woman he denied desiring

**Neither Knew**: Dramatic irony—perfect measurement results despite emotional complexity

### Lasting Impact

The sessions established patterns of structured intimacy that evolved into explicit relationship. Every pair of pants carries this origin story of professional partnership concealing growing love.
