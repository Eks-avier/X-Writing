# Pants: Materials and Construction

## Material Evolution Phases

### Phase I: Conventional Foundation (Early 2020)

Standard tactical pants modified to achieve formal appearance:
- Clean lines without tactical bulk
- Minimal external features
- Form-fitting but mobile cut
- Professional appearance

These established his preference through pocket reduction and tailoring adjustments.

### Phase II: Maria's Silk Integration (Mid-2020)

Complete reconstruction using divine moth silk, achieved through comprehensive personal measurement and observation of his movement patterns.

| Property | Capability |

## Maria's Design Specifications

**Primary Objectives**:
- **Sleek appearance**: Visual cleanliness and geometric precision
- **Combat functionality**: Unimpeded movement during physical confrontation
- **Durability emphasis**: Pants must survive his strength and activities
- **Comfort priority**: Extended wear without restriction
- **Drape optimization**: Sufficient drape without appearing baggy

**The Measurement Sessions** (Corpus: Saga-TG-P277):
Both Godric and Maria experienced mutual internal agony during measurement sessions—maintaining professional composure and small talk while experiencing deeper emotional responses. Neither realized the other's struggle. Perfect measurement results emerged despite the emotional complexity.

---

## Construction Philosophy

### Material Transcendence

Unlike conventional tailoring where complex engineering compensates for material limitations, Maria's method achieves:
- Material perfection allowing focus on pure aesthetics
- Design efficiency through capability inversion
- Technical specifications secondary to material capabilities
- The material provides solutions rather than requiring them

### Combat Integration

**Primary Considerations**:
- Grounded fighting style: Enhanced durability for ground-based combat
- Flexibility emphasis: Unmatched comfort and movement range
- Staying power: Pants remain intact during extreme physical stress
- Post-Kong adaptation: Potential future modifications for evolved techniques

## Seasonal Accommodation

**Single-Solution Engineering**: Maria's construction accommodates both seasonal states:

| Season | Physique | Adaptation |
|--------|----------|------------|
| **Active** (Dec-Aug) | 120kg, 10-12% body fat | Standard fit |
| **Dormant** (Sep-Nov) | 129kg, 15-18% body fat | Same garments stretch appropriately |

**Result**: No separate seasonal variants required—redundancy elimination through superior engineering.
