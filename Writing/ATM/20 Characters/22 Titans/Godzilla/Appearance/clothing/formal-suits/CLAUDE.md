# CLAUDE.md - Formal Suits

## Overview

This folder documents Godric's **seven-suit diplomatic arsenal** - conventional human formal wear for high-stakes diplomatic and social events. Unlike [[../formal-regalia/CLAUDE|Titan Regalia]] (ceremonial wear for Titan Council), these suits are designed to blend with human formal wear while projecting strategic psychological effects.

## Key Concepts

### The Seven Suits Philosophy

Strategic collection where suit selection is **methodical assessment, not fashion preference**. Each suit deploys specific psychological effects:

| Designation | Purpose |
|-------------|---------|
| **The Workhorse** | Standard Monarch functions - authority without attention |
| **The Statesman** | Government functions - maximum gravitas |
| **The Academic** | Scientific settings - intellectual without intimidation |
| **The Cultural Envoy** | Arts galas - contemporary sophistication |
| **The Power Play** | Corporate events - financial intimidation |
| **The Unused Gift** | Mark's gift - beautiful but no strategic use case |
| **The Backup** | Emergency formal - reliable fallback |

### Distinction from Titan Regalia

| Aspect | Seven Suits | Titan Regalia |
|--------|-------------|---------------|
| **Audience** | Human observers | Titan hierarchy |
| **Scale Integration** | Minimal (buttons only) | Maximum (vambraces, waistcoat) |
| **Authority Signal** | Subtle, cultured | Overt, ceremonial |
| **Purpose** | Human camouflage | Species declaration |
