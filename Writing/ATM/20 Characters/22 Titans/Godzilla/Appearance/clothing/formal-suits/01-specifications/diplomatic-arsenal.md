# The Seven Suits: Diplomatic Arsenal

For high-stakes diplomatic or social events where daily uniform is insufficient, Godric maintains a strategic collection of seven formal suits. Selection is not fashion-based but methodical assessment of event requirements—deploying each suit for specific psychological or presentational effect.

---

## The Seven Suits System

| Suit Description | Designation | Primary Usage & Rationale | Madison's Assessment |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| **Single-Breasted Black** (British, Tailored) | *The Workhorse* | Standard Monarch diplomatic functions (embassy receptions, military ceremonies). Maximum authority with minimal attention-drawing. | "Safe choice, but still makes everyone else look underdressed." |
| **Double-Breasted Navy Blue** (American, Classical) | *The Statesman* | High-level government functions (state dinners, international summits). Projects immense gravitas appropriate for diplomatic significance. | "He looks like he should be signing peace treaties, not just attending them." |
| **Single-Breasted Charcoal Gray** (British, Classical) | *The Academic* | Scientific symposiums and research presentations. Sophisticated and intellectual without being overpowering. | "Finally, one that doesn't make him look like he conquered Europe." |
| **Single-Breasted Navy Blue** (Italian, Modern) | *The Cultural Envoy* | Arts galas and cultural events (museum openings, opera premieres). Modern cut suggests contemporary sophistication, quietly influenced by Maria. | "Good for events where creativity is valued over authority." |
| **Double-Breasted Black** (British, Modern) | *The Power Play* | Corporate events and financial summits. Projects undeniable business authority and financial acumen, used to intimidate powerful humans. | "Only when we need to intimidate billionaires." |
| **Double-Breasted Forest Green** (American, Modern) | *The Unused Gift* | Christmas gift from [[../../../../_Relationships/mark-russell|Mark]] that remains unworn. Appreciated but deemed too distinctive for stealth and too casual for high-level events. | "It's beautiful, but where do you wear forest green to blend in?" |
| **Single-Breasted Black** (American, Classical) | *The Backup* | Emergency formal situations or when primary suit is being maintained. Reliable fallback option with broad applicability. | "My insurance policy for when Maria is making 'modifications'." |

## Selection Matrix

### By Event Type

| Event Type | Preferred Suit | Rationale |
|-----------|----------------|-----------|
| **Monarch diplomatic function** | The Workhorse | Standard authority presentation |
| **State dinner** | The Statesman | Maximum governmental gravitas |
| **Scientific conference** | The Academic | Intellectual without intimidation |
| **Museum gala** | The Cultural Envoy | Contemporary sophistication |
| **Corporate summit** | The Power Play | Financial authority projection |
| **Emergency formal** | The Backup | Reliable fallback |

### By Desired Effect

| Desired Psychological Effect | Suit Choice |
|------------------------------|-------------|
| **Blend in (relatively)** | The Workhorse or Academic |
| **Dominate room** | The Statesman or Power Play |
| **Appear cultured** | The Cultural Envoy |
| **Emergency readiness** | The Backup |

## Material and Construction

**All Suits:**
- Made from Maria's divine silk
- Combat-ready construction
- Self-repairing properties
- Bulletproof, blast-resistant

**Distinction from Titan Regalia:**
- These are conventional human suit designs
- No scale integration (except buttons/cufflinks)
- No elevated collars or frock coats
- Designed to blend with human formal wear

---

## The Forest Green Exception

### Why It Remains Unworn

**Mark's Intent:** Thoughtful gift, beautiful color, well-crafted

**Godric's Assessment:**
- Too distinctive for diplomatic stealth
- Too casual for high-level formal events
- No clear use case in his strategic wardrobe

**Current Status:** Hangs in wardrobe, appreciated but unused

**Thematic Significance:** Even gifts from valued allies must serve function to warrant use

---

## Madison's Role

### Fashion Advisor and Diplomat

**She provides strategic guidance:**
- Which suit for which event
- Cultural appropriateness
- Human perception management

**She manages expectations:**
- "The Academic" when he wants to intimidate scientists
- "The Statesman" for actual intimidation needs
- "The Workhorse" as safe default
