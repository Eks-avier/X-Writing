## Key Concepts

### Progressive Reduction Levels

| Level | Duration | Configuration |
|-------|----------|---------------|
| 1 | Under 3 hours | Shirt removal only |
| 2 | 3+ hours, standard alert | Shirt + pants removed |
| 3 | Extended, low alert | Underwear only |

### The Loungewear Impossibility

Dedicated loungewear implies purposeless time—a concept alien to Godric. Relaxation is achieved through task variation, not costume change.

### Trust Indicator

Reduction level correlates directly with trust level:
- Level 1: Trusted associates
- Level 2: Inner circle
- Level 3: Maria alone

## The Swimming Exception

**Sole specialized leisure garment**: All black swimming trunks
- Water connection echoing Titan environment
- Swimming = sole recognized "downtime"
- Ocean connection preserves global territorial awareness
