# Maria and Godric's Favorite Body Parts

> **Source:** Eclipse-I P252
> **Context:** Character-driven touchstones for which physical metrics matter narratively

## A Private Language of Love and Devotion

These rankings are not just about physical attraction; they are a form of **intimate communication**, a map of their shared desires and the specific ways they appreciate each other's physical forms. These hierarchies reveal what each character values, how they perceive each other, and the secret, unspoken language of their physical relationship.

## Maria's Hierarchy: The Appreciation of the King

Her list is a beautiful progression from the soulful to the sensual, a perfect reflection of her journey with him.

### 1. Eyes (The Soul)

**His true gold eyes are number one.**

This is perfect. It signifies that what she loves most is his **authentic self**, the vulnerable man beneath the King's armor. It is the ultimate testament to the depth of her love.

**Significance:**
- The gold represents his unguarded, true nature
- Visibility requires proximity and trust (distance shows blue)
- First thing she notices, last thing she sees before sleep
- Window to 250 million years of memory and experience

### 2. Hair (The Authenticity)

**The beard, specifically. The dormant mane.**

This is the symbol of his unguarded, authentic state, the look associated with all their most pivotal romantic moments. It is the physical manifestation of the man she fell in love with.

**Significance:**
- Dormant season grooming = relaxed, vulnerable Godric
- Falling strands phenomenon (her secret torment during active season)
- Tactile fascination—wants to run fingers through it
- See: [[../../hair/02-narrative/maria-reactions|Maria's Hair Reactions]] for complete analysis

### 3. His Back (The Unseen Power)

**Constantly gets stunned when he turns around, especially when naked/half-naked.**

This is a brilliant and very specific choice. The back is a symbol of immense, foundational strength that a person doesn't see themselves. It represents the power he carries without having to display it. Her being "stunned" by it is her being in awe of his quiet, unassuming might.

**The Shared Secret (The Marks):**
- Godric actively uses Atomic Amplification to **slow down his regeneration** and preserve the marks she leaves on him
- Temporary "tattoos" saying "I belong to you"
- Private message visible only in intimate moments
- See: [[../01-specifications/regional-specifications|Regional Specifications]] for back measurements

### 4. Phallus (The Pleasure)

**Her growing confidence and appreciation.**

This marks her own sexual awakening and their developing intimacy. It moves from a source of awkward comedy to a source of genuine pleasure and connection.

**Evolution:**
- Early relationship: Source of nervous humor
- Mid-relationship: Appreciation of his dedication to her pleasure
- Current: Confident, mutual enjoyment
- Note: "She has gotten much better at pleasuring him"

### 5. Neck (The Claim)

**Marks. She leaves marks.**

Leaving marks on his neck is a primal, possessive act, another way for her to mark her territory. Him, again, using his power to **keep those marks fresh** (AA-mediated regeneration control) is a beautiful act of willing submission to her claim.

**Significance:**
- Public visibility (unlike back marks)
- Godric wears the sign of her ownership willingly
- Slowed regeneration = conscious choice to display her claim
- Requires conscious AA control to maintain against natural healing

### 6. Arms & Hands (The Tools of a Craftsman)

**Self-explanatory and classic, but perfect.**

They are the tools he uses to build, to fight, to create... and to pleasure her.

**Measurements (Active Season):**
- Upper arm circumference: 43.18 cm (17.00 in)
- Forearm circumference: 34.93 cm (13.75 in)
- Hand length: 22.08 cm (8.69 in)
- Perfect 2/φ ratio (combat-optimized)

**See:** [[../01-specifications/regional-specifications|Regional Specifications]] for complete arm measurements

## Godric's Hierarchy: The Worship of the Queen

His list is equally telling, a mix of poetic admiration and primal, masculine appreciation.

### 1. Hair (The Surrender)

**The "river of moonlight." His kryptonite.**

It is the ultimate symbol of her vulnerability and her trust in him. For a being of his control, seeing her let go is the most captivating sight in the universe.

**Significance:**
- Represents her authentic, unguarded self
- When unbound = vulnerability and trust
- Silver catching light = "moonlight" imagery
- His hands in her hair = intimate privilege
- See: [[../../../Mothra/Appearance/hair/|Maria's Hair Profile]] for complete specifications

### 2. Eyes (The Complementary Soul)

**Her sapphires to his gold.**

It's a recognition of their cosmic, archetypal pairing—the moon to his sun. It is a profound, almost spiritual attraction.

**Symbolism:**
- Sapphire blue (Maria) ↔ Gold (Godric)
- Moon ↔ Sun
- Intangible ↔ Tangible
- Reflected light ↔ Self-luminous
- See: [[../../Symbolism/sun-moon-motif|Sun-Moon Motif]]

### 3. Vagina (The Object of Worship)

**This being so high on his list reinforces his core dynamic with her.**

His greatest joy is in serving and pleasing her. Her pleasure is his mission.

**Philosophy:**
- Service-oriented sexuality
- Her satisfaction as primary goal
- "Knows how to pleasure her in ways that brings Heaven on Earth"
- Dedication to her comfort and enjoyment

### 4. Waist (The Anchor Point)

**"His hands are always around them."**

This is a beautiful, physical representation of his role. He is her **anchor**, grounding her to the tangible world, and his hands instinctively find that center of gravity.

**Measurements (Maria):**
- Waist: Core of her physical form
- Natural resting place for his hands
- Grounding touch during flight/telekinetic moments
- Physical connection point in public settings

### 5. Neck (The Intimate Space)

**His love for kissing her neck, and her willing permission.**

A sign of their deep comfort and trust. The neck is vulnerable—allowing access demonstrates emotional intimacy.

**Dynamics:**
- Maria doesn't hesitate to give permission
- Godric leaves marks there too
- Visible claim (like her marks on him)
- Intimate gesture in public = declaration of bond

### 6. Boobs ("Balanced")

**His description of them as "balanced" is hysterical and perfectly Godric.**

It's an analytical, almost architectural appreciation that is completely devoid of crude human language. It is a scientist admiring perfect form.

**Translation:**
- **Human:** "Nice rack"
- **Godric:** "Balanced proportions, optimal distribution, aesthetically harmonious"
- Mathematical appreciation, not crude objectification
- Reflects his analytical mind applied to aesthetic beauty

### 7. Legs (The Warrior's Appreciation)

**He loves them aesthetically, but what he truly *admires* is their power.**

This is a perfect reflection of his character. He is attracted not just to her beauty, but to her **strength**. Her wanting him to leave marks there is a fantastic detail—it's a sign of her claiming and owning her own warrior nature within their intimacy.

**Significance:**
- Aesthetic beauty + functional power
- "She can kick really hard" = warrior admiration
- Marks on legs = claiming her strength
- Dual appreciation (form + function)

## Thematic Analysis

### From Soul to Sensual

**Maria's List:**
- Begins with **soul** (eyes)
- Progresses through **identity** (hair)
- Moves to **power** (back)
- Ends with **sensuality** (phallus, neck, arms)

**Godric's List:**
- Begins with **surrender** (hair)
- Moves through **cosmic bond** (eyes)
- Addresses **service** (vagina)
- Balances **anchor/intimacy** (waist, neck)
- Ends with **form/function** (boobs, legs)

### Complementary Perspectives

**Maria appreciates:**
- His hidden strength (back)
- His authentic vulnerability (hair, eyes)
- His physical capability (arms, phallus)

**Godric appreciates:**
- Her willingness to be vulnerable (hair, neck)
- Her inherent power (eyes, legs)
- Her cosmic significance (eyes = moon imagery)

### The Marks: A Bilateral Claim

Both leave marks. Both use their powers to **preserve** those marks.

**Maria → Godric:**
- Back (private)
- Neck (public)
- Uses AA to slow regeneration

**Godric → Maria:**
- Neck (public)
- Legs (private/semi-private)
- Her divine healing also slowed (willing choice)

**Shared Meaning:** Physical manifestation of "I belong to you." Temporary tattoos declaring mutual ownership.
