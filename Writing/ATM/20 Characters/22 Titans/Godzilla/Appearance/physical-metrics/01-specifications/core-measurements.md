# Core Measurements

> **Temporal Context:** Era VII (Antitheriomorphosis, post-2025)

## Primary Specifications

| Metric | Active Season | Dormant Season |
|--------|---------------|----------------|
| **Height** | 195.97 cm (6' 5.15") | Same |
| **Weight** | 120.00 kg (264.55 lbs) | 129.00 kg (284.40 lbs) |

### Height Specification

**Definitive Measurement:** 195.97 cm (6' 5.15")

This places Godric as the tallest member of the Nordson family, matched only by his eldest son Junior (6' 5" / 195.5 cm). The height represents the peak physical expression of the Northern *gojira* lineage.

> [!quote] Family Context
> "The unmovable standard. The peak of the Northern Gojira lineage."
> — *Eclipse-II P093*

## Seasonal Periods

| Season | Period | Characteristics |
|--------|--------|-----------------|
| **Active** | December-August | Leaner, more defined musculature |
| **Dormant** | September-November | Increased mass, softer definition |

### Active Season (December-August)

- **Duration:** 9 months
- **Physical State:** Peak combat readiness
- **Body Composition:** Lower body fat (10-12%), maximum muscular definition
- **Visual Appearance:** Sharper angles, more pronounced vascularity
- **Metabolic State:** Enhanced energy expenditure, increased caloric needs

### Dormant Season (September-November)

- **Duration:** 3 months
- **Physical State:** Recovery and regeneration
- **Body Composition:** Higher body fat (15-18%), reduced muscular definition
- **Mass Increase:** +9 kg (~7.5% increase from active baseline)
- **Visual Appearance:** Softer contours, reduced vascularity, enhanced rugged aesthetic
- **Metabolic State:** Reduced energy output, efficient nutrient storage

## Perpetual Readiness State

Even at rest, Godric's muscles remain partially engaged—a biological artifact of his eternal vigilance:

| Measurement | Expansion (Flexed) | Season |
|-------------|-------------------|--------|
| Upper Arm | +6.00% | Active |
| Chest | +3.35% | Active |

This state of perpetual readiness parallels his constantly blue eyes: just as his true gold is masked by atomic energy, his capacity for true relaxation is veiled by constant, controlled tension.

### Biological Implications

- **Never Fully Relaxed:** His baseline "resting" state is what most humans achieve under conscious flexing
- **Combat Response Time:** Zero preparation needed—already primed for action
- **Energy Expenditure:** Higher basal metabolic rate than measurement suggests
- **Titan Echo:** Direct carryover from centuries of territorial vigilance
