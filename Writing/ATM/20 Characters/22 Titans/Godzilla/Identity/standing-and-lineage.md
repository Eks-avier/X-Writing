## Hierarchical Standing

> [!tip] Reference Framework
> For complete hierarchy documentation, see [[The Titan Hierarchy]]. For standing definitions, see [[The Standing System]].

### Alpha Paramount

The supreme authority over all Titans—the highest position in the global [[The Titan Hierarchy|Titan hierarchy]]. This standing represents:

- **Absolute dominion** over all Titan-kind
- **Final arbiter** in territorial and behavioral disputes
- **De facto sovereignty** recognized by all sapient and non-sapient Titans

He has held this position for approximately 250 million years, making it the longest continuous reign in Earth's biological history.

#### Mother's Identity

**Gojira Name:** Golden-Eyed Dark-Finned Daughter of the Blue-Eyed Gray-Finned Son

**Titan Name:** Astraea — "Star-maiden." A direct connection to the [[The Saga of the Fallen Star|Fallen Star]], the source of *Titanus gojira* power. In Greek mythology, Astraea was the goddess of justice who left Earth and was placed among the stars as Virgo — a tragic resonance with her own story.

**Human Name:** Aurelia Nordson — "The golden one." A loving tribute to her most memorable feature: her golden eyes. This name was born from quiet conversation between Dagon (Darius) and Godric, sparked by Leo's question about his middle name "Aurelius."

> [!note] Memorial Names
> Unlike Dagon/Godzilla who received their Titan names from Monarch, Astraea and Aurelia are **memorial names** — given to her by her husband and son in loving remembrance, not labels assigned by institutions.

Her father possessed the "Blue-Eyed Gray-Finned" coloration — uncommon but not rare, roughly equivalent to being blonde among humans.

### Unique Genetic Position

Godric represents a genetic synthesis:
- **Maternal coloration** (obsidian black, golden eyes) — exceptionally rare
- **Paternal physique** (broader structure, dorsal fin blueprint)
- **Combined result** — Taller, slightly less broad than Dagon would have been; unique color palette

> [!note] A Study in Contrasts
> Dagon embodied typical Northern traits: dark gray scales, snow-white dorsal fins, glacial ice eyes. Godric's maternal inheritance makes him a striking departure from this paternal template.

## The Last Northerner

As the sole surviving member of the Northern Faction, Godric carries:
- The complete genetic legacy of his lineage
- The cultural memory of a destroyed faction
- The responsibility of representing Northern Faction values

His human name "Nordson" directly acknowledges this heritage.
