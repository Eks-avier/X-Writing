# Core Identification

> [!info] Temporal Context
> **Eras:** All (I-VII)
> **Valid From:** ~250 million years ago (emergence as Alpha Paramount)

## Basic Identification

- **Titan Name:** Godzilla / Gojira
  - He answers to both equally
- **Human Name:** Godric Nordson
  - Chosen post-[[Antitheriomorphosis]], blending Anglo-Saxon "Godric" (power of God/ruler of God) with "Nordson," representing his [[#Northern Faction]] heritage and identity as "The Last Son of the North"
- **Species:** *Titanus gojira*
  - An [[The Lineage System|Ascendant Lineage]], unique for its [[The Pinnacle of Limitless Adaptation|Limitless Adaptation]] and mastery over [[Krátos, the Power of Will|Krátos]]
- **Lineage:** Ascendant / Superior (see [[The Lineage System]])
- **Sapience:** Sapient / Minded
- **Standing:** [[The Standing System|Alpha Paramount]]
  - The supreme authority over the [[The Titan Hierarchy|Titan collective]] on the Surface and the Hollow Earth, held for approximately 250 million years (see [[Master_Timeline.md|Master Timeline]])
