# Names and Titles

> [!info] Temporal Context
> **Eras:** All (I-VII)
> Human name adopted in Era VII post-[[Antitheriomorphosis]]
> See [[Master_Timeline.md|Master Timeline]] for era definitions and event sequencing.

## Titan Names

- **Godzilla** — Primary designation used by Monarch and global institutions
- **Gojira** (ゴジラ) — Japanese designation; he answers to both equally

## Human Name (Era VII)

**Godric Nordson**

The name chosen post-Antitheriomorphosis carries deliberate meaning:

- **Godric** — Anglo-Saxon origin meaning "power of God" or "ruler of God"
- **Nordson** — "Son of the North," representing his Northern Faction heritage and identity as "The Last Son of the North"

## Naming Philosophy

The layered naming reflects his multifaceted identity:
- **Titan names** connect him to his 250-million-year legacy
- **Human name** anchors his transformed existence while honoring heritage
- **Titles** express different aspects of his role and authority

### Antitheriomorphosis Naming Convention

All Antitheriomorphosized Titans follow a **same-letter principle** — Titan and human names must share the same starting letter:

| Titan Name | Human Name |
|------------|------------|
| Godzilla/Gojira | Godric |
| Dagon | Darius |
| Mothra | Maria |
| Battra | Bartholomew |
| Rodan | Roman |
| Anguirus | Alexios |
| Kong | Kevin |

This convention was established post-transformation to maintain identity continuity while adapting to human society.

### Godric/Darius Name Synergy

The father-son names "Godric" and "Darius" share a remarkable structural synergy:

- Both are **6 letters** long
- From 'D' to 'G', count three letters: E, F, **G**
- Both end in **vowel-consonant** pairings (-ic, -us)
- The letter 'r' appears in different positions — Darius's 'r' at the front, Godric's 'r' near the end — symbolizing the father preceding the son
- The letter 'd' follows the same pattern — Darius's 'd' at the front, Godric's 'd' at center — representing the father who has been at the center of his son's life

> [!quote] Source: Saga-TG P241
> "Godric and Darius are such perfect names for each of them AND for each other. I don't think this level of synergy could be replicated!"

### Leo's Aurelius Connection

Leo's middle name **Aurelius** ("the golden one") is a deliberate tribute to his paternal grandmother, [[Astraea (Aurelia Nordson)|Aurelia Nordson]]. Though Leo did not inherit the golden eyes passed from Aurelia → Godric → Junior, his father gave him this name to declare:

> [!quote] Source: Saga-TG P219
> "They are both gold in Godric's eyes."

This ensures Leo never feels "less than" his twin brother — both are equally part of the golden legacy. The name creates a direct, matrilineal connection across three generations:
- **Aurelia** (grandmother) → **Godric** (golden eyes) → **Junior** (golden eyes) → **Leo** (Aurelius)

## Gojiran Names (Energy-Language)

In the gojiran tongue (the energy-based communication system of *Titanus gojira*), individuals are identified by descriptive epithets:

| Individual | Gojiran Name |
|------------|--------------|
| Godric | Golden-Eyed Dark-Finned Son of the Blue-Eyed Gray-Finned Son |
| [[Astraea (Aurelia Nordson)\|Astraea/Aurelia]] | Golden-Eyed Dark-Finned Daughter of the Blue-Eyed Gray-Finned Son |
| [[Dagon]] | Son of an Iceberg (nickname; birth name common like "John Smith") |

Dagon's birth name was so common among Northern Faction males — possessing typical faction features — that his nickname became his preferred identifier. He only recognized "Dagon" (a name given by Monarch after the fish god) following his resurrection.
