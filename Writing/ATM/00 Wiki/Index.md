# Antitheriomorphosis

> *In the year 2020, a mysterious phenomenon transformed every Titan that had ever touched Earth into human form. They retained their godlike powers, their ancient memories, their cosmic responsibilities—but now they walk among us, hidden in plain sight.*

---

## The Universe

**Antitheriomorphosis** is a sprawling narrative universe where the great Titans of Earth—Godzilla, Mothra, Rodan, Kong, and dozens more—have been permanently transformed into human bodies while keeping all their abilities intact.

Only one being was immune: [[Battra, the Lord of the Mystic Arts]], whose mastery of sorcery shielded him from the transformation. Yet even he chooses to wear a human form—he doesn't want to be the odd one out.

What follows is a story of adaptation, identity, and the collision of ancient power with modern humanity.

## Featured Content

### The Alpha Paramount
[[Godzilla Profile|Godric Nordson]], the King of the Monsters, now walks the Earth as a man of impossible perfection—6'5" of Greco-Roman beauty, golden-streaked black hair, and eyes that glow atomic blue when his power surges. Behind the composed exterior lies 250 million years of memory and the weight of a crown he never asked for.

### The Three Fundamental Forces
The universe operates on three interconnected power systems:
- **[[Krátos, the Power of Will]]** — Raw willpower made manifest
- **[[Overview of Magic|Sorcery]]** — Manipulation of Primus through the soul
- **[[Psionics]]** — The psychic arts, reawakened in humanity

### The Divine Moths
[[Mothra, the Queen of the Monsters|Maria]] and [[Battra, the Lord of the Mystic Arts|Battra]]—twin moths bound by a 250-million-year bond, now navigating human form together. One is the Queen of Monsters, beloved by all. The other is the exiled Lord of Mystic Arts, seeking redemption.
