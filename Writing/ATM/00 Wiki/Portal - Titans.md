# Portal: The Titans

> *250 million years of history. Infinite power. Human bodies.*

---

## Overview

The Titans are Earth's original rulers—beings of immense power who have shaped the planet's history since before the dinosaurs. In 2020, the phenomenon known as **Antitheriomorphosis** transformed them all into permanent human form, though they retained every ounce of their cosmic abilities.

Now they navigate a world built for mortals, maintaining their ancient hierarchies, honoring their ancestral bonds, and adapting to lives that require grocery shopping and human clothing.
