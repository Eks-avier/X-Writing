# Portal: The Story

> *From ancient history to the end of the world—and beyond.*

---

## Overview

The Antitheriomorphosis narrative spans millions of years of history, but its main story begins in 2020 with the mysterious transformation that gave the universe its name. The narrative is structured as a series of **arcs**, each following the format:

> **Antitheriomorphosis: [Arc Title]**

The story builds toward an eventual crossover with the Pacific Rim universe, as alien factions converge and ancient threats awaken.

## Story Themes

The narrative explores five core themes:

1. **Adaptation vs. Stagnation** — The necessity of evolution
2. **Identity Beyond Roles** — Characters discovering themselves beyond cosmic functions
3. **Human/Titan Dynamics** — Power dynamics between species
4. **The Nature of Authority** — Rulership, governance, and consent
5. **Magic and Science** — Integration of magical systems with scientific understanding
