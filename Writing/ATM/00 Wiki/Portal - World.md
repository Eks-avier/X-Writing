# Portal: World & Lore

> *A world where ancient hierarchies meet modern civilization.*

---

## Overview

The world of Antitheriomorphosis operates on systems older than humanity—Titan hierarchies that have governed Earth for hundreds of millions of years. Now, with Titans in human form and humanity's supernatural potential reawakening, these ancient structures are adapting to unprecedented circumstances.

## Organizations

### Titan Governance
The Titans maintain their own governmental structure:
- The Alpha Paramount (Godzilla) at the apex
- The Queen (Mothra) as co-ruler
- Cardinals and Wardens governing regions
- The Hollow Earth King (Kong) with autonomous authority
