# Portal: The Humans

> *Mortals standing in the shadow of gods—and some are beginning to cast shadows of their own.*

---

## Overview

Humanity in the Antitheriomorphosis universe is experiencing an awakening. For millennia, the **Divine Mandate Against Sorcery** stripped humans of their magical potential, rendering them "Null" in the eyes of the cosmos. But with Mothra's return in 2019 and the weakening of the Mandate, psychic abilities are resurging across the population.

Now humans work alongside Titans through organizations like **Monarch**, train in the mystic arts at **Keep Charlie**, and navigate a world where their neighbors might be disguised kaiju.

## Human Organizations

### Monarch
The scientific organization dedicated to studying and working alongside Titans. After Antitheriomorphosis, their role expanded from observation to active collaboration.

### Keep Charlie
Located within [[Castle Bravo]], this facility trains humans in:
- ESP and psychic abilities
- Sorcery and magical arts
- Titan liaison protocols

---

## Human-Titan Relations

The transformation of Titans into human form has created unprecedented opportunities—and tensions:

- **Integration**: Titans now navigate human society, requiring human allies and advisors
- **Training**: Keep Charlie prepares humans to work with supernatural forces
- **Tension**: Not all humans accept Titan authority; not all Titans respect human agency
