# Portal: Characters

> *Titans who became human. Humans who stand beside gods.*

---

## Overview

The cast of Antitheriomorphosis spans two fundamentally different groups: the **Titans**—ancient beings of immense power now trapped in human bodies—and the **Humans** who work alongside them, study them, or oppose them.

Despite the transformation, Titans retain their full abilities, their memories spanning millions of years, and their complex political hierarchies. Humans, meanwhile, are experiencing a reawakening of their own—the Divine Mandate that once stripped them of magical potential is weakening, and psychic abilities are emerging across the population.
