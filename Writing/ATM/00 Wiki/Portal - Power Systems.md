# Portal: Power Systems

> *Three forces shape reality: Will, Magic, and Mind.*

---

## Overview

The Antitheriomorphosis universe operates on three interconnected fundamental power systems. These aren't merely "magic" in the generic sense—they are distinct forces with their own rules, limitations, and practitioners.

Notably, the magic system draws heavily from **programming metaphors**: procedural magic mirrors sequential programming, object-oriented approaches use encapsulation, and spell optimization functions like code refactoring.
