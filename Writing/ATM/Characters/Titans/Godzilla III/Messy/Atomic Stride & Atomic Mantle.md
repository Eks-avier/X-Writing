Enter the Asymmetrical Permeable Shield or the APS: 

- Mechan

- Mechanically, it is a layer of the defensive portion of AA *on* an Antitheriomorphosized Titanus gojira. AA reinforces the body from the inside, APS simply takes the defensive portion and *on* to the skin.
- Krátonically, it the Neutralization aspect of AA is brought from within and placed on top of the skin. The Penetration aspect remains inside the body.

The reason why APS is the solution to this is because I imagine it acting like a net. It's a form-fitting field, but it *catches* everything that may cause a sonic boom: excess energy, displaced air, the most minutest sound, and so on. And by catch them, I meant it Neutralizes them.

When he does a Stride, it captures the shockwave and any other harmful artifacts at each stage. 

Another thing about the Atomic Stride: as with all of Godzilla's abilities, they are VERY radioactive. Besides the shockwaves, it leaves lethal radiation everywhere. The APS also serves to contain this. 

It's funny, the Stride is defined by its flaws more than its usefulness! That's intentional on my part. Godzilla isn't really known to be mobile. Being a human made him a "tank with a motorcycle's mobility," giving him this ability further increases that.

If all of Godzilla's stats are S or S+, his mobility is about B or "B-". With the Atomic Stride, it might get it up to speed with his other stats!
