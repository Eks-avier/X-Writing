# Atomic Stride & Atomic Mantle

Enter the Asymmetrical Permeable Shield or the APS.

## Definition

* **Mechanically**: The skill takes a portion of the defensive aspect granted by AA and elevates as a layer *on* the skin of an Antitheriomorphosized *Titanus gojira*.
* **Krátonically**: A *portion* of the Neutralization aspect of AA defending the body *from the inside* is brought out and placed *onto* the skin. The Penetration aspect remains *within* the body.
* **Clarification**:
	* The entire Neutralization layer of AA is not *completely* brought out from within the body, only about a quarter or a half of it is, depending on the situation at hand. For more demanding challenges, more of the layer may be brought out.
		* This is so that the *internal organs* of the body remain enhanced and defended by AA, as a gojira would be sacrificing internal protection for external defense.
	* It is **form-fitting**. It does not *hover* over the skin, it sits right on top of it.

---

The reason why APS is the solution to this is because I imagine it acting like a net. It *catches* everything that may cause a sonic boom: excess energy, displaced air, the minutest sound, and so on. And by catching them, I meant it Neutralizes them.

---

## Stride Synergy

When he does a Stride, it captures the shockwave and any other harmful artifacts at each stage.

Another thing about the Atomic Stride: as with all of Godzilla's abilities, they are VERY radioactive. Besides the shockwaves, it leaves lethal radiation everywhere. The APS also serves to contain this.

It's funny, the Stride is defined by its flaws more than its usefulness! That's intentional on my part. Godzilla isn't really known to be mobile. Being a human made him a “tank with a motorcycle's mobility,” giving him this ability further increases that.

If all of Godzilla's stats are S or S+, his mobility is about B or “B-”. With the Atomic Stride, it might get it up to speed with his other stats!
