# Atomic Stride & Atomic Mantle

Enter the Asymmetrical Permeable Shield or the APS.

## Definition

* **Mechanically**: The skill takes a portion of the defensive aspect granted by AA and elevates as a layer *on* the skin of an Antitheriomorphosized *Titanus gojira*.
* **Krátonically**: A *portion* of the Neutralization aspect of AA defending the body *from the inside* is brought out and placed *onto* the skin. The Penetration aspect remains *within* the body.
* **Clarifications**:
	* The entire Neutralization layer of AA is not *completely* brought out from within the body; only about a quarter or a half of it is, depending on the situation at hand. For more demanding challenges, additional layers may be introduced.
		* This is so that the *internal organs* of the body remain enhanced and defended by AA, as a gojira would be sacrificing internal protection for external defense.
	* It is **form-fitting**. It does not *hover* over the skin; it sits right on top of it.
	* It remains *highly* radioactive at higher outputs.

## Stride Synergy

- **Visualization**: The reason why APS is the solution to the issues of the Atomic Stride is that I imagine it acting like a net. It *catches* everything that may *cause* a sonic boom: excess energy, displaced air, the minutest sound, and so on. 
	- **Krátonic Perspective**: And by catching such artifacts, I meant it Neutralizes them. Whenever he does a Stride, it captures the shock wave as well as any other harmful artifacts at each stage of the ability.
- **Radiation Buffer**: Another thing about the Atomic Stride: as with all of Godzilla's abilities, it is VERY radioactive. Besides the shock waves, it leaves lethal radiation everywhere. The APS also serves to contain this.
	- **Caveat**: Being *within* the ability is safe, but *touching* it is not. This has practically no difference for Godzilla's daily life as he already has to contend with the fact that his mere presence is already radioactive.

## Retrospective and Notes

It's amusing that the Stride is defined by its flaws more than its usefulness. That's intentional on my part. 

Godzilla isn't really known to be mobile, but possessing a human body granted him the mobility *at base* that simply didn't exist for him as a Titan. If Titan Godzilla were a tank, Human Godzilla is a tank with the mobility and maneuverability of a motorcycle.

With the advent of Atomic Stride, his innate mobility in a human body is *further* increased. If all of Godzilla's stats are S or S+, his mobility as a human is about B or “B-”. Why? Because Rodan skews the ranking. Mothra and Battra are exempt because they can enhance their flight beyond natural means through their respective abilities. But, in a contest of pure speed, agility, mobility, and where no teleportation and such hacks are used, Rodan reigns supreme. With Atomic Stride, Godzilla can finally catch up.

- Among the Trio of Battra, Godzilla, and Mothra, he is no longer the slowest one, though he is still grounded.
- Among the Quintet (with Kong), he is now the fastest of the land walkers. 

With the Atomic Stride, it might get it up to speed with his other stats!
