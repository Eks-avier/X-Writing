# Medieval Titans AU Concept Development

## Core Concept
A grounded Medieval AU reimagining the Titans as humans in a feudal world, focusing on the forbidden romance between Princess Maria of the Eclipse Kingdom (Mothra) and Godric of the North (Godzilla), the last survivor of a fallen kingdom.

## Key Characters

### Maria Lepidiel (Mothra)
- **Position**: Princess and heir to the Eclipse Kingdom
- **Background**: Selected as heir over her twin brother in the mysterious succession trial
- **Abilities**: Skilled in combat (trained by her father), diplomatic, adaptive
- **Character**: Compassionate, determined, curious about the world beyond palace walls
- **Defining Relationship**: Deep bond with her father, King Leo

### Godric (Godzilla)
- **Position**: The Last Northerner, huntsman and master weaponsmith
- **Background**: Sole survivor of the fallen Northern Kingdom
- **Abilities**: Unparalleled hunting prowess, exceptional strength, master craftsmanship
- **Character**: Solemn, reserved, carries himself with quiet authority
- **Defining Trait**: His cultural heritage manifests through his craftsmanship rather than physical markings

### King Leo Lepidiel (Previous Divine Instance)
- **Position**: Ruler of the Eclipse Kingdom, "The Luminous King"
- **Appearance**: Silver-white hair, intense blue eyes with an almost luminous quality
- **Character**: Wise, spiritual, deeply connected to ancient Eclipse traditions
- **Role**: Devoted father who trained Maria personally in combat and governance
- **Current Status**: Devastated by Maria's disappearance, refusing to believe she's dead

### Bartholomew Lepidiel (Battra)
- **Position**: Twin brother to Maria, failed successor
- **Character Development**: Resentful after losing the succession trial
- **Current Path**: Delving into forbidden knowledge, secretly seeking magical artifacts
- **Motivation**: To prove the succession trial wrong through alternative sources of power

### Soichiro Serizawa (The Collector)
- **Position**: The Last Easterner, collector of ancient artifacts
- **Character**: Scholarly, precise, with perfect recall of his vast collection
- **Role**: Employs Godric to retrieve artifacts, serves as father figure
- **Special Connection**: Shares bond with Godric as the last representatives of the Four Factions

### The Zilla Clan
- **Background**: Descendants of mixed Southern Faction blood
- **Appearance**: Bear traditional Southern tattoos/scarification
- **Role**: Serve Soichiro, assist in artifact collection
- **Cultural Position**: Preserve diluted versions of ancient traditions

## World Building Elements

### The Four Factions (Former Titan Races)
- **The North** (Godzilla's lineage): Pragmatic craftsmen, valued strength and endurance
- **The East** (Serizawa's lineage): Knowledge-preservers with precision and methodical minds
- **The South** (Connected to Zillas): Expressive warriors who recorded history on their bodies
- **The West** (Extinct): No surviving pure-blooded members

### The Eclipse Kingdom (Mothra's Domain)
- **Governance**: Rule determined by mysterious succession trials rather than birth order
- **Cultural Traits**: Strong spiritual connection to celestial powers
- **Social Structure**: Traditional monarchy with mystical elements

## Plot Development

### Initial Meeting
- Maria sacrifices herself to kidnappers to save her companions
- Godric, on a hunting mission, witnesses her courage and intervenes
- Rather than returning to her kingdom, Maria offers to help Godric find artifacts in Eclipse territory

### Their Journey Together
- They adopt disguises and cover stories to travel undetected
- Maria poses as Godric's apprentice, learning real crafting skills
- They dodge Eclipse search parties while hunting artifacts
- Bartholomew unknowingly employs Soichiro to find magical artifacts, creating a connection between all characters

### Romance Development
1. **Early Attraction** (Weeks 1-2)
   - Mutual respect develops through observing each other's capabilities
   - Small moments of connection emerge through shared tasks

2. **Deepening Connection** (Weeks 3-5)
   - Teaching moments create physical proximity
   - Vulnerabilities are gradually revealed
   - Fighting alongside each other builds trust

3. **Acknowledged Feelings** (Weeks 6-8)
   - Tension builds until it can no longer be ignored
   - Confession occurs after a significant shared experience
   - First kiss marks transition to acknowledged romance

### The Choice
- Maria eventually decides to return home, driven by guilt over her father's grief
- The farewell scene captures their impossible situation:
  - Godric refuses to join her, believing he doesn't belong in her world
  - Maria struggles to leave behind their connection
  - The possibility of pregnancy is raised but dismissed
  - Their parting is bittersweet, both knowing their worlds cannot merge

## Key Themes
- Duty versus personal desire
- Cultural preservation and legacy
- The weight of being the last of one's kind
- Family bonds (both blood and chosen)
- Class divisions and their impact on relationships
- The search for belonging when between worlds

## Potential Future Developments
- Maria's return to the Eclipse Kingdom
- Bartholomew's continuing descent into forbidden knowledge
- Possible reunion between Maria and Godric under different circumstances
- The revelation of connections between artifacts being collected
- The ultimate fate of the Four Factions' legacies

## Character Dynamics
- **Maria & King Leo**: Loving father-daughter bond tested by her disappearance
- **Maria & Bartholomew**: Strained twin relationship fractured by succession trial
- **Godric & Soichiro**: Mutual respect built on shared experience as last of their kinds
- **Bartholomew & Soichiro**: Unwitting connection through artifact collection
- **Godric & Zilla Clan**: Respected leadership role despite different heritages
