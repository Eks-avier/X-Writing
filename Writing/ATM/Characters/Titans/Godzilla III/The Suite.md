# The Suite

## Atomic Amplification

Mechanically, it is a uniform distribution of atomic energy throughout a *Titanus gojira*'s body, serving as a holistic physical enhancement that provides increased offense, defense, speed, agility, cognitive capability, and energy control. The intensity of this state is variable, allowing it to be modulated and scaled depending on the situation at hand.

Krátonically, it is uniform distribution of the internal applications of the *Edicts of Coating* (**Penetration**, **Neutralization**, and **Fortification**) throughout the body.
