# Mothra's Reflective Scales: Complete Power System Analysis

## Core Mechanics

### Perfect Reflection Principle
- **Scope**: Can reflect *any* force—physical, energy, magical, or psychic—back to its source
- **Amplification**: Reflected forces can compound, potentially returning with greater intensity than the original attack
- **Universality**: Unlike typical "energy reflection," this works on conceptual forces like telekinetic attacks, magical effects, or even abstract influences
- **Divine Enhancement**: Result of inheriting the Heavenly Instance's soul at birth

### Perpetual Defense System
- **Always Active**: Scales remain in "active mode" constantly, providing 24/7 protection
- **Adjustable Radius**: Can maintain protection at skin level or expand into force-field zones
- **Invisible Mode**: Natural state has scales adhering invisibly to her skin
- **Unlimited Reuse**: Individual scales don't degrade from use, unlike other Divine Moths

## Physical Manifestation & Control

### Scale Forms
- **Dormant State**: Invisible golden dust particles on skin
- **Active Deployment**: Visible as luminescent golden particles
- **Construct Mode**: Shape into soft, approachable moth-like forms
- **Sensory Extensions**: Each scale functions as omnisensory organ (sight, hearing, smell, taste, touch)

### Advanced Applications
- **Global Network**: Single scale anywhere provides awareness of surroundings
- **Teleportation**: At 5/7 soul fragments, can teleport to any scale's location
- **Intangibility**: At 4/7 fragments (limited duration), 6/7 (unlimited)
- **Perfect Stealth**: Complete invisibility through precise light reflection

## Physical Implications & Mechanics

### The Recursive Force Feedback
When someone exerts force against Maria's scales, they experience:

1. **Gentle pressure** → feels like pushing against a wall
2. **Moderate force** → feels like pushing against your own hand, but heavier
3. **Significant force** → immediate launch backward with amplified return force

### The Sensation Experience
- **Third Law Plus**: Normal physics expects equal and opposite reaction
- **Scale Reality**: Attacker feels both the object's resistance AND their own force reflected back
- **Amplification Effect**: Returned force is heavier/stronger than applied force
- **Psychological Impact**: Deeply unsettling sensation of fighting yourself but losing

## Tactical Applications

### Defensive Uses
- **Personal Protection**: Invisible scales providing constant protection
- **Area Defense**: Expanding protective zones around allies
- **Containment**: Enemy attacks reflected back, preventing escape
- **Selective Permeability**: Allowing friendly attacks out while blocking incoming threats

### Envelope Mechanics
- **Telepathic Coordination**: Links with allies to allow their attacks through while maintaining protection
- **Intuitive Filtering**: Uses experience and instinct to predict ally attack patterns
- **Human Weapon Recognition**: Understands modern weaponry through Monarch knowledge
- **Emergency Protocols**: Consent-based telepathic links for fine-tuned coordination

## Soul Fragment Progression

### Current State (4/7 Fragments)
- **Effortless Reflection**: No energy cost for reflecting most attacks
- **Scale Regeneration**: Limited but sufficient for most encounters
- **Breakthrough Threshold**: Only entities in her power class can challenge her

### Future Capabilities
- **5/7 Fragments**: Unlimited scale regeneration, teleportation to any scale
- **6/7 Fragments**: Unlimited intangibility
- **7/7 Fragments**: Unknown enhancement

## Limitations & Vulnerabilities

### Known Weaknesses
- **Atomic Railgun**: Godric's technique pierces rather than reflects (disperses the scales)
- **Omnidirectional Attacks**: Requires proper configuration to handle attacks from all vectors
- **Electromagnetic Disruption**: Specific frequencies can temporarily break psionic connection
- **Concentration Requirements**: Complex manipulations need conscious attention
- **Throughput Issues**: Only affects entities at or above her power level

### Energy Economics
- **Below 4/7 Fragments**: Limited scales, breakable, used as last resort
- **Current Level**: Completely effortless against most threats
- **High-Level Threats**: Continuous scale breaking can create cumulative stress
- **Power Class Threshold**: Clear line between "no threat" and "actual challenge"

## Species Context & Inheritance

### Divine Moth Variants
- **Standard Divine Moths**: Various scale properties (elemental, spatial, temporal, etc.)
- **Royal Family**: Enhanced capabilities beyond species norm
- **Soul Inheritors**: Exponentially increased power (Mothra's perfect reflection)
- **Battra's Alternative**: Would have had perfect refraction instead of reflection

### Unique Properties
- **Perfect Reflection**: Appears to be Mothra's alone among Divine Moths
- **Divine Soul Enhancement**: All inheritors experience extraordinary scale strengthening
- **Incarnational Consistency**: Scale properties remain identical across all reincarnations
- **Identity Expression**: Scale abilities directly reflect core personality and nature
