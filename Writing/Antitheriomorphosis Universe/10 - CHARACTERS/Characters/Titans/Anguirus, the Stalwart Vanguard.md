# Anguirus, the Stalwart Vanguard

## Basic Information

- **Titan Name**: Anguirus
- **Human Name**: Alexios Leonidas Peltast
- **Position**: Stalwart Vanguard (Beta Titan)
- **Species**: *Titanus angilas*
- **Height**: 5'10” (177.8 cm)
- **Build**: Broad, horizontal emphasis - shorter but wider than Godric
- **Age**: Approximately 200+ million years old
- **Eyes**: Emerald green (symbolizing loyalty, patience, wisdom)

## Physical Appearance

### Ethnic Heritage

- **Base Ethnicity**: Greek with subtle Northern European influences
- **Skin Tone**: Mediterranean olive, lighter than Roman's
- **Facial Structure**: Strong, symmetrical features with classical proportions
- **Hair**: Dark brown, almost black, kept meticulously neat
- **Build**: Broad, solid frame built for endurance rather than speed
- **Cultural References**: His bearing evokes ancient Greek hoplites—disciplined, steady, immovable

### Distinctive Physical Traits

- **Golden Ratio Proportions**: His physique exhibits explicit golden ratio relationships, complementing Godric's own mathematical perfection
- **Horizontal Emphasis**: Wider than Godric but shorter, creating a visual impression of unmovable stability
- **Dense Musculature**: Incredibly compact and dense muscle structure suggesting immovable strength
- **Perfect Posture**: Military bearing maintained at all times
- **Ground Connection**: Always appears firmly planted with lower center of gravity

## Core Ability: Damage Absorption

### Mechanism

- **Passive Absorption**: Constantly active throughout his entire body, absorbing physical damage
- **Processing System**: Damage gets “processed” rather than causing harm - brief shimmer indicates activation
- **Energy Redistribution**: Can utilize absorbed damage as physical empowerment
- **Uniform Distribution**: Keeps absorbed energy evenly distributed to prevent overload
- **Regeneration Factor**: Superior to most Titans but inferior to Godric's

### Practical Applications

- **Enhanced Strikes**: After absorbing damage, counterattacks carry amplified force
- **Tactical Positioning**: Deliberately absorbs attacks meant for others
- **Environmental Interaction**: Can walk across unstable surfaces without disturbing them
- **Training Method**: Subjects himself to controlled damage to build absorption capacity
- **Combat Endurance**: Continues functioning after damage that would incapacitate others

### Limitations

- **Overwhelming Force**: Cannot sustain attacks like the Atomic Railgun
- **Gradual Buildup**: Must manage absorbed energy carefully to prevent dangerous accumulation
- **Processing Time**: Brief vulnerability during the absorption/processing moment

## Border Collie Nature

Anguirus embodies the loyal, protective, and routine-driven characteristics of a border collie:

### Behavioral Traits

- **Herding Instinct**: Naturally positions himself to “contain” threats and protect his charge
- **Routine Comfort**: Finds security in established patterns and schedules
- **Problem Prevention**: Instinctively identifies and addresses issues before they emerge
- **Territorial Patrolling**: Compulsively checks boundaries and secures perimeters
- **Protective Positioning**: Always places himself between threats and Godric

### The “Wagging” Behaviors

- **Heel Pivot**: Subtle back-and-forth weight shifting when pleased (standing position)
- **Forearm Tap**: Rhythmic finger tapping against opposite forearm (arms crossed)
- **Thigh Tap**: Nearly imperceptible finger tapping against outer thigh (formal stance)
- **Emotional Intensity Scale**: Thigh tap (controlled) → Forearm tap (moderate) → Heel pivot (genuine excitement)
- **Self-Consciousness**: Stops immediately when noticed, becomes embarrassed

## Communication System

### Species Heritage (*Titanus angilas*)

- **Primary Method**: Tail positioning and movement patterns
- **Minimal Vocalization**: Rarely uses sound, preferring physical indicators
- **Honest Communication**: Body language that reliably indicates emotional and alert status

### Human Form Translation

- **Finger Tapping**: Replaces tail communication with various tapping patterns
- **Posture Integration**: Whole-body positioning conveys complex information
- **Pointing Behavior**: Unconscious rigid pointing when something requires attention
- **Weight Distribution**: How he balances reveals his alert status to other Titans
- **Military Bearing**: Formal posture masks emotional communication underneath

## The Three-Name Identity

#### Significance

- **Only Titan with Three Names**: Reflects his formal, structured approach to everything
- **Military Precision**: Like a full rank and title, standing apart from others
- **Usage Patterns**:
  - Everyday: “Alex Peltast”
  - Formal Court: “Alexios Leonidas Peltast”
  - Casual: “Alex”
  - The Quartet's Nickname: "Al"

#### Symbolic Meaning

- **Alexios**: “Defender” - his role protecting Godric
- **Leonidas**: “Lion-like” - honoring Spartan warrior traditions
- **Peltast**: “Shield-bearer” - reflecting his carapace and protective function

### Parental Background & Psychology

#### Family History

- **Stable Upbringing**: Had parents throughout his formative years
- **Natural Death**: Parents died from disease during Mesozoic-Cenozoic transition
- **Good Farewell**: Left on good terms, making their loss sad but processable
- **Human-Relatable Experience**: Most “normal” parental experience of the quartet

#### Impact on Character

- **Emotional Security**: Stable foundation allows him to be the “reliable one”
- **Processing Stability**: Healthy relationship with loss and change
- **Support Role**: Natural ability to provide stability for others
- **Kong Disconnect**: Cannot understand the unique bond between Godric and Kong over shared early parental loss

### The Kong Crisis

#### The Existential Question

**Core Anxiety**: *“What did I miss in millions of years that Kong discovered in months?”*

#### Manifestations

- **Professional Displacement**: Kong fills the same tactical advisor role
- **Rapid Connection**: Godric's instant bond with Kong threatens Alexios's unique position
- **Skill Overlap**: Both provide combat advice and physical protection
- **Pack Dynamics**: From a border collie perspective, Kong is another working dog performing the same job

#### Coping Behaviors

- **Midnight Training**: Intensified solo sessions destroying training equipment
- **Surveillance**: Obsessively documenting Kong-Godric interactions
- **Skills Acquisition**: Attempting to learn Kong's fighting techniques
- **Hovering Presence**: Finding reasons to be nearby during their interactions

### Relationships

#### With Godric (His Person)

- **Shadow Function**: Anticipates Godric's needs before they're expressed
- **Complementary Skills**: Handles details Godric considers beneath notice
- **Physical Brotherhood**: Their bodies create visual harmony representing their relationship
- **Unspoken Communication**: Can interpret Godric's minimal expressions after millennia together
- **Protective Instinct**: Still positions himself to shield Godric despite human form

#### With Maria (Fellow Organized Soul)

- **Quiet Comfort**: Work together with minimal verbal communication needed
- **Mutual Recognition**: Both understand the challenges of supporting Godric
- **Shared Values**: Appreciate precision, preparation, and duty
- **Historical Wariness**: Initial tension from both viewing the other as competition
- **Evolved Partnership**: Now function as complementary support team

#### With Roman (Cat to His Dog)

- **Constant Irritation**: Roman seems to exist to disrupt Alexios's order
- **Crisis Unity**: Despite bickering, coordinate perfectly in emergencies
- **Opposite Approaches**: Methodical planning vs. improvisational chaos
- **Shared History**: Occasional references to past events only they understand

### Personal Organization

- **Minimalist Décor**: Everything has practical purpose
- **Display Wall**: Weapons collected over millennia (only decoration)
- **Maintenance Station**: Dedicated equipment cleaning area
- **No-Fly Zone**: Roman strictly forbidden from entering
- **Sound Dampening**: Enhanced acoustic insulation (likely to block Roman)

#### Daily Routines

- **0500 Hours**: Precise daily routine, never varies
- **Physical Conditioning**: Exercises that would kill humans
- **Security Assessments**: Daily reports to Godric regardless of requests
- **Equipment Maintenance**: Meticulous care of all possessions
- **Contingency Planning**: Develops plans for absurdly unlikely scenarios

### Combat Role & Tactical Philosophy

#### Fighting Style

- **Immovable Defense**: Uses absorption to become an unbreachable wall
- **Damage Conversion**: Transforms absorbed attacks into devastating counterstrikes
- **Tactical Positioning**: Draws fire away from teammates
- **Endurance Combat**: Outlasts opponents through superior damage tolerance
- **Precision Strikes**: Enhanced attacks using stored absorbed energy

#### Military Approach

- **Preparation Over Reaction**: Extensive planning for every contingency
- **Detail Management**: Handles logistics and tactical minutiae
- **Risk Assessment**: Constantly evaluates threats and defensive positions
- **Protocol Development**: Creates systematic responses to various scenarios
- **Backup Planning**: Always has multiple contingency options ready

### Distinctive Mannerisms

#### Unconscious Behaviors

- **The Point**: Rigid finger pointing when something requires attention
- **Circle Check**: Complete room perimeter inspection upon entering
- **Freeze-Crouch**: Slight crouch when detecting potential threats
- **Head Tilt**: When confused, before catching himself

#### Professional Habits

- **Tactical Analysis**: Views everything through security assessment lens
- **Resource Management**: Obsessed with storage solutions and preparation
- **Information Processing**: Maintains comprehensive records of everything
- **Status Reporting**: Regular updates on all relevant situations

### Character Arc Potential

#### Current Challenges

- **Identity Crisis**: Questioning his value in light of Kong's rapid integration
- **Emotional Suppression**: Processing jealousy through physical displacement
- **Role Redefinition**: Finding ways to complement rather than compete with Kong
- **Self-Worth**: Recognizing his unique contributions beyond tactical advice

#### Growth Opportunities

- **Accepting Change**: Learning that Kong's presence doesn't diminish his value
- **Emotional Expression**: Gradually accepting his “wagging” tells as natural
- **Pack Expansion**: Embracing the larger family structure
- **Historical Advantage**: Using his millennia of experience as unique asset

The Stalwart Vanguard represents loyalty made manifest—a being whose every action stems from unwavering dedication to his chosen family, even when that dedication creates internal conflict he struggles to express.
