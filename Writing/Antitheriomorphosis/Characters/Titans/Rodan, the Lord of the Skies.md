# Rodan: The Lord of the Skies

## Basic Information

- **Titan Name**: Rodan
- **Human Name**: Roman Volcario
- **Position**: Lord of the Skies (Beta Titan)
- **Species**: *Titanus radon*
- **Height**: 6'0” (183 cm)
- **Build**: Lean but muscle-packed, built for explosive movement and speed
- **Age**: Approximately 180+ million years old
- **Eyes**: Amber/topaz - communicating contained fire and energy

## Physical Appearance

### Ethnic Heritage

- **Base Ethnicity**: Spanish with strong Italian influences
- **Skin Tone**: Deep olive with perpetual sun-kissed quality that glows from within
- **Facial Structure**: Sharp, expressive features with prominent cheekbones
- **Hair**: Auburn with natural highlights that catch light like embers
- **Build**: Lean and wiry, suggesting constant motion and energy
- **Cultural References**: Movements evoke flamenco dancers—precise, passionate, flowing

### Distinctive Physical Traits

- **Perpetual Tan**: Maintains deep bronze coloration regardless of season
- **Temperature Aura**: Body runs noticeably warmer than normal humans
- **Windswept Effect**: Hair appears permanently tousled as if caught in wind
- **Restless Posture**: Never truly still, even when standing motionless
- **Expressive Hands**: Constantly moving, tracing invisible patterns
- **Aerodynamic Grace**: Every movement suggests potential for flight

### Signature Style

- **Aesthetic**: Cross between motorcyclist and pilot
- **Color Palette**: Reds, oranges, browns predominate
- **Signature Piece**: Red scarf made from Maria's silk
- **Accessories**: Goggles (aviation-style)
- **Clothing Style**: Loose, lightweight layers that create movement
- **Occasional Variations**: Green for Alexios, black for Godric (business wear only)

## Core Abilities: Aerokinesis & Pyrokinesis

### Volcanic Nature

- **Internal Heat**: Body temperature fluctuates with emotional state
- **Pressure Release**: Requires periodic emotional/energy discharge
- **Environmental Impact**: Rooms warm noticeably when he enters
- **Thermal Communication**: Temperature changes convey emotional information
- **Heat Signature**: Leaves subtle heat marks on surfaces touched for extended periods

### Aerokinetic Control

- **Unconscious Manifestation**: Creates air currents when emotional
- **Flight Capability**: Achieved human-form flight through aerokinesis (first to do so)
- **Precision Challenge**: Struggles with fine control after losing wings
- **Environmental Interaction**: Papers flutter in his wake
- **Hair Styling**: Uses aerokinesis for effortless windswept appearance

### Pyrokinetic Abilities

- **Heat Generation**: Can warm objects and spaces through direct contact
- **Temperature Control**: Modulates internal heat for communication
- **Cooking Disasters**: Lacks fine control, tends to create explosions
- **Coordinated Communication**: Combined with aerokinesis for aerial Titan communication
