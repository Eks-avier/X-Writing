# Godric Nordson's Dress Shirts: Complete Specifications

## Philosophy and Approach

Godric treats his dress shirts as extensions of territorial management—strategic presentations of authority that function as both psychological and literal armor. His approach evolved from initial adaptation to human clothing into a sophisticated system of controlled revelation and tactical readiness.

**Core Principles:**
- Simple, functional, form-fitting design enabling combat mobility
- Strategic concealment with calculated revelation (triangular collar window)
- Consistent projection of Alpha Paramount status regardless of circumstances
- Integration of Titan identity through materials and construction

## Material Evolution

### Phase I: Conventional Foundation (Early 2020)

High-end dress shirts purchased by Monarch upper management to introduce Western-style formal wear. These served as Godric's template for preferred style and established his immediate gravitation toward this clothing type.

### Phase II: Maria's Silk Revolution (Mid-2020)

Complete recreation by [[Mothra, the Queen of the Monsters|Maria]] using her divine moth silk, achieved through close personal measurement and observation of his movement patterns. This transformation elevated ordinary clothing into genuine protective gear.

**Maria's Silk Properties:**
- Bulletproof and projectile resistant
- Fireproof and blast resistant
- Waterproof with stain resistance
- Supremely flexible with shape memory
- Self-repairing capabilities through Maria's maintenance
- Limited atomic energy channeling capacity
- Enhanced durability without weight penalty

### Phase III: Scale Integration (Late 2020)

Incorporation of Godric's shed scales into button construction, creating literal integration of his Titan identity into his human presentation.

## Visual Specifications

### Overall Aesthetic

- **Fit:** Precisely tailored by Maria to cling to his physique while accommodating combat mobility
- **Opacity:** Completely opaque, creating strategic mystery about his form beneath
- **Length:** Extended cut to accommodate his 195.97cm height and ensure permanent tucking
- **Lines:** Clean, unbroken visual sweep from shoulder to waist emphasizing his mathematical proportions

### Collar Configuration

- **Type:** Spread collar creating wide frame around neck and upper chest
- **Presentation:** Top button permanently undone, creating triangular window of visibility
- **Effect:** Emphasizes jawline and shoulder width while providing calculated glimpse of throat and upper chest
- **Strategic Value:** Only visible indication of his physique beyond hands and face

### Cuff Design

- **Standard Variants:** Barrel cuffs for practical daily wear
- **Formal Variants:** French cuffs requiring cufflink integration
- **Philosophy:** Never rolled regardless of activity—maintains coverage as species dominance signal
- **Engineering:** Maria's design ensures wrist flexibility during combat despite doubled fabric in French cuffs

### Pocket Philosophy

- **Configuration:** Completely pocketless design
- **Rationale:** Maintains unbroken visual lines and prevents bulk disruption
- **Effect:** Creates uninterrupted sweep emphasizing V-shaped torso taper
- **Consistency:** Aligns with minimal pocket preference in tactical pants

## Maintenance and Care Protocols

### Weekly Routine

- **Laundry Schedule:** Every Friday regardless of visible need
- **Process:** Effortless due to silk's stain-resistant properties
- **Planning Ritual:** Pre-week outfit selection (typically one black, one white shirt)
- **Ironing:**
	- Done every Friday after laundry.
	- Personal handling maintaining perfect presentation standards.

### Damage and Repair

- **Frequency:** Extremely rare due to material durability
- **Process:** Return to Maria for inspection and repair
- **Duration:** 10-minute sessions with most time spent on examination rather than actual repair
- **Significance:** Serves as legitimate excuse for close physical proximity between Maria and Godric

### Self-Maintenance Philosophy

- **Approach:** Complete self-sufficiency in routine care
- **Standards:** Military-level precision in maintenance and presentation
- **Territory Management:** Views clothing care as extension of personal domain control
