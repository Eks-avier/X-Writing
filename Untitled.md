# Untitled

I've been thinking about further expanding the Coating mechanics, while clarifying some notions:

- Aura enables Coating.
    - Aura alone is inert.
        - It is the defined area in which Kratons exert their influence.
            - For example, a Pyroton's Aura is a circular field of fire with a radius of 25 meters.
                - The number is entirely dependent on the willpower of the Kraton.
                    - Stronger wills create larger Auras.   
    - There are two ways to activate Aura
        - Flaring, which is the act of intensifying one's Aura.
        - Coating, which is the act of “clothing,” “sheathing,” or “cladding” oneself with one's Aura.
            
- Coating has two types.
    - Offensive Coating
    - Defensive Coating

- Offensive Coating is imbuing one's Aura into one's attacks.
	- Offensive Coating carrie

	- As with _Titanus gojira_'s iconic Atomic Breaths or Ghidorah's equally iconic _Gravity Beams_, Coating can extend _from_ the body and _into_ attacks.
	    - A Pyroton can pick up a random bow and arrow and _infuse_ it with their Aura, thereby applying Coating.
	    - Ghidorah's _Gravity Beams_ are electric energy attacks that take the shape of lightning and are typically shot from one or all of his three mouths.
	        - He can apply Coating on one or all of his _Gravity Beams thanks to his mastery_.   
	    - The examples above show that Coating is not just limited to body application.
	        - Kratons can _imbue_ them into pre-existing objects.
	        - Projectiles and energy attacks that were shot from a Kraton's physical body can have Coating applied to them regardless of their distance from the Kraton.
	            
	- The application of Coating onto a pre-existing physical object should be a new skill under Coating.
	    - It needs both an English and a Greek name.
	    - It hinges on a Kraton _physically touching_ an object.
	        - It cannot be applied from a distance.    
	    - The greater the distance from the Kraton, the “imbued" object's Coating degrades.
	        - Stronger Kratons like Ghidorah and Godzilla can have their attacks go on for several kilometers, and because their attacks can cause environmental damage upfront, they often do not need to be imbued.  
	            - When Ghidorah electrocutes a human to death or disintegrates them, they die because they received hundreds of thousands of volts; whether or not his attack was applied Coating is irrelevant.   
	                - But when Ghidorah tries to fire one of his _Gravity Beams_ at Godzilla, the Coating actually matters because Godzilla is immune to high-voltage electricity.
