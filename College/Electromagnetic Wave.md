# Electromagnetic Wave

## EM Waves

- The result of **vibrations** between the electric and magnetic fields.
- They are **oscillating** magnetic and electric fields.
- An EM wave does not require a medium through which it can propagate.
	- They can travel through a vacuum or a material substance.
	- They can be produced in situations that do not involve a wire antenna.
- Any accelerating electric charge emits an electromagnetic wave, regardless of its position.
- **Speed Of an EM Wave in a Vacuum**: $c = 3.00 \times 10^{8} \, \frac{\text{m}}{s}$
- All EM Waves have wavelength and frequency, related by: $c = f\lambda$
	- Where:
		1. $c$ is the *speed* of an EM wave in a vacuum.
		2. $f$ is the *frequency*.
		3. $\lambda$ is the *wavelength*.
- Electromagnetic Spectrum:
	- Visible light is from $4 \times^{14}$ to $7.9 \times 10^{14}\text{ Hz}$
