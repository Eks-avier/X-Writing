For a **loop of wire**:
$$
B = \frac{\mu_{o}I}{2\pi r}
$$
Where:
- $\mu = 4\pi \times 10^{-7} \frac{Tm}{A}$; it is called the **permeability of space**. 
- $r$ is the **distance** from the wire where the mag. field is determined.
- $I$ is **current**

For problems that ask for the **center of circular loop**:
$$
B = \frac{N\mu_{o}I}{2R}
$$
Where:
- $N$ is the number of **turns**.

---

For a **solenoid**:
$$
B = \mu_{o}nI
$$
Where:
- $n$ is the number of **turns** per unit length.

$$
B = \frac{\mu_{o}IN}{l}
$$
Where:
- $l$ is the unit length.
- $N$ is the number of turns.
